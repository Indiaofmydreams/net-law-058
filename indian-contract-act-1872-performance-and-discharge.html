10 practice questions with answers

1Under Section 37, the legal representatives of a promisor are bound by the promise:

  1. Never
  2. Only if the estate is solvent
  3. Only with consent
  4. Unless a contrary intention appears from the contract
Show answer

Answer: D. Unless a contrary intention appears from the contract

Report this question

2Under Section 38, if a valid tender of performance is refused, the promisor:

  1. Loses all rights under the contract
  2. Is discharged completely
  3. Is not responsible for non-performance and does not lose his rights under the contract
  4. Is liable to pay damages
Show answer

Answer: C. Is not responsible for non-performance and does not lose his rights under the contract

Report this question

3Under Section 39, when a party refuses to perform wholly, the promisee:

  1. May put an end to the contract unless he has signified acquiescence
  2. Must continue
  3. Must sue for specific performance
  4. Must refund
Show answer

Answer: A. May put an end to the contract unless he has signified acquiescence

Report this question

4Under Section 41, if the promisee accepts performance from a third person, he:

  1. May enforce it against the promisor
  2. Cannot afterwards enforce it against the promisor
  3. May sue the third person
  4. May claim double
Show answer

Answer: B. Cannot afterwards enforce it against the promisor

Report this question

5Under Section 46, where no time is specified and no application is needed, performance must be within:

  1. Three days
  2. A reasonable time
  3. A month
  4. A year
Show answer

Answer: B. A reasonable time

Report this question

6Under Section 55, if time is of the essence and the promisor fails to perform at the fixed time, the contract becomes:

  1. Void
  2. Valid
  3. Voidable at the option of the promisee
  4. Illegal
Show answer

Answer: C. Voidable at the option of the promisee

Report this question

7Under Sections 59-61, a payment is first appropriated according to:

  1. The debtor's intimation
  2. The creditor's wishes
  3. Court order
  4. The age of the debt only
Show answer

Answer: A. The debtor's intimation

Report this question

8Novation under Section 62 means:

  1. Remission of performance
  2. Extension of time
  3. Substitution of a new contract for the old one
  4. Alteration of terms only
Show answer

Answer: C. Substitution of a new contract for the old one

Report this question

9Under Section 63, a promisee may:

  1. Not remit performance
  2. Only extend time
  3. Only rescind
  4. Dispense with or remit performance, extend time, or accept any satisfaction
Show answer

Answer: D. Dispense with or remit performance, extend time, or accept any satisfaction

Report this question

10Under Section 52, where the order of performance of reciprocal promises is not expressly fixed, performance occurs:

  1. Simultaneously
  2. Alphabetically
  3. At the promisee's choice
  4. In the order the nature of the transaction requires
Show answer

Answer: D. In the order the nature of the transaction requires
