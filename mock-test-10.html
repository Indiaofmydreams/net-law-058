1Regarding environmental agreements:
I. The Montreal Protocol targets ozone-depleting substances
II. The Kyoto Protocol set binding emission targets for developed countries
III. The Paris Agreement was adopted in 1997

Which of the statements is/are correct?

  1. I and II only
  2. I only
  3. II and III only
  4. I, II and III
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. I and II only

The Paris Agreement was adopted in 2015.

3Which of the following is a circular, two-way model of communication?

  1. Osgood–Schramm model
  2. Lasswell's formula
  3. Aristotle's model
  4. Shannon–Weaver model
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Osgood–Schramm model

It shows both parties encoding and decoding continuously.

4A can finish a work in 12 days and B in 24 days. Working together, they finish it in (days):

  1. 6
  2. 10
  3. 8
  4. 9

Answer: C. 8

1/12 + 1/24 = 1/8.
