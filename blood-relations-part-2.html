About this question set

This set has 21 multiple-choice questions on Blood Relations from the UGC NET Paper 1 syllabus, unit 5, Mathematical Reasoning and Aptitude. Try each question first, then tap Show answer to check the key and read the explanation. 21 of the 21 questions carry a written explanation.

For the concepts and case law behind these questions, read the Mathematical Reasoning notes. Once you are comfortable, test yourself under exam conditions with a full-length mock test.

21 practice questions with answers

1A is B's father. B is C's sister. C is D's mother. How is A related to D?

  1. Uncle
  2. Father
  3. Maternal grandfather
  4. Paternal grandfather
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Maternal grandfather

A is the father of C, and C is D's mother, so A is D's mother's father.

Report this question

2Pointing to a man, a woman says, "He is the son of my father's only son." How is the man related to the woman?

  1. Nephew
  2. Brother
  3. Cousin
  4. Uncle
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Nephew

The father's only son is the woman's brother, and his son is her nephew.

Report this question

3X's mother is Y's sister. Z is the son of Y. How is Z related to X?

  1. Nephew
  2. Cousin
  3. Brother
  4. Uncle
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Cousin

Y is X's maternal aunt, and her son Z is X's cousin.

Report this question

4A is the mother of B. B is the brother of C. D is the husband of C. How is A related to D?

  1. Mother
  2. Aunt
  3. Sister-in-law
  4. Mother-in-law
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Mother-in-law

A is the mother of C, and D is C's husband, so A is D's mother-in-law.

Report this question

5Pointing to a lady, a man says, "She is the wife of the only son of my mother." How is the lady related to the man?

  1. Mother
  2. Sister
  3. Wife
  4. Daughter
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Wife

The only son of the man's mother is the man himself, so the lady is his wife.

Report this question

6Pointing to a man, a woman says, "He is the son of my mother's mother's only daughter." How is the man related to the woman?

  1. Brother
  2. Uncle
  3. Cousin
  4. Nephew
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Brother

The mother's mother's only daughter is the woman's mother, so her son is the woman's brother.

Report this question

7In a code, 'A + B' means A is the son of B, 'A − B' means A is the daughter of B and 'A × B' means A is the brother of B. Then 'P × Q − R' means P is the ___ of R.

  1. Brother
  2. Son
  3. Daughter
  4. Father
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Son

P is the brother of Q, and Q is the daughter of R, so P is R's son.

Report this question

8Statement I: The son of one's mother's sister is a maternal cousin.
Statement II: One's mother's sister is called a maternal aunt.

  1. I true, II false
  2. I false, II true
  3. Both false
  4. Both true
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Both true

Both are standard relationship terms.

Report this question

9Assertion (A): One's mother's brother's wife is addressed as an aunt.
Reason (R): She is married to one's maternal uncle.

  1. Both true, and R is the correct explanation of A
  2. Both false
  3. A false, R true
  4. A true, R false
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Both true, and R is the correct explanation of A

She becomes an aunt by marriage to the maternal uncle.

  2. a-iv, b-iii, c-i, d-ii
  3. a-iv, b-i, c-iii, d-ii
  4. a-ii, b-i, c-iii, d-iv

11Arrange in order of generation, from oldest to youngest: 1. Mother 2. Great-grandmother 3. Daughter 4. Grandmother

  1. 3, 1, 4, 2
  2. 2, 4, 1, 3
  3. 2, 1, 4, 3
  4. 4, 2, 1, 3
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. 2, 4, 1, 3

Great-grandmother, grandmother, mother, daughter.

12Pointing to a photograph, a woman says, "He is the brother of my father's son." How is he related to her?

  1. Nephew
  2. Cousin
  3. Uncle
  4. Brother
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Brother

Her father's son is her brother, and his brother is also her brother.

13A is the mother of B. C is the daughter of B. D is the husband of A. How is D related to C?

  1. Father
  2. Grandfather
  3. Great-grandfather
  4. Uncle
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Grandfather

D is B's father, and B is C's parent, so D is C's grandfather.

14P is the sister of Q. Q is the brother of R. S is the mother of R. How is S related to P?

  1. Mother
  2. Grandmother
  3. Sister
  4. Aunt
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Mother

P, Q and R are siblings, so S is the mother of all three.

15Pointing to a lady, Ramesh says, "Her mother is the only daughter of my mother's mother." How is the lady related to Ramesh?

  1. Aunt
  2. Niece
  3. Sister
  4. Cousin
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Sister

The only daughter of Ramesh's mother's mother is his mother, so the lady is his mother's daughter, which makes her his sister.

16Pointing to a woman, a man says, "She is the wife of my mother's only brother." How is she related to him?

  1. Cousin
  2. Maternal aunt (mother's brother's wife)
  3. Mother
  4. Sister
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Maternal aunt (mother's brother's wife)

The mother's brother is the maternal uncle, and his wife is the aunt by marriage.

Report this question

17X is the father of Y. Y is the brother of Z. Z is the daughter of W. How is X related to W?

  1. Father
  2. Brother
  3. Son
  4. Husband
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Husband

Y and Z are siblings, and X is the father of Y while W is the parent of Z, so W is the mother and X is her husband.

18Introducing a man, a woman says, "His mother is the only sister of my father." How is the man related to the woman?

  1. Cousin
  2. Uncle
  3. Nephew
  4. Brother
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Cousin

The father's sister's son is a paternal cousin.

19A and B are brothers. C is B's father. D is C's mother. E is A's son. How is D related to E?

  1. Grandmother
  2. Mother
  3. Great-grandmother
  4. Aunt
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Great-grandmother

D is C's mother, and C is A's father, so D is A's grandmother and E's great-grandmother.

20Statement I: One's father's brother is called a paternal uncle.
Statement II: One's mother's brother is called a paternal uncle.

  1. Both false
  2. I true, II false
  3. Both true
  4. I false, II true
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. I true, II false

The mother's brother is the maternal uncle.
