About this question set

10 practice questions with answers

1Which philosopher defined education as "the complete development of the individuality of the child so that he can make an original contribution to human life"?

  1. Herbert Spencer
  2. John Dewey
  3. Mahatma Gandhi
  4. Rabindranath Tagore
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Rabindranath Tagore

Concept: Tagore = complete development + original contribution. His Shantiniketan embodied this — nature, creativity, freedom. Frequently tested alongside Dewey and Gandhi.

Report this question

2Mahatma Gandhi defined education as:

  1. Preparation for life in society
  2. The all-round drawing out of the best in child and man — body, mind, and spirit
  3. Transmission of cultural heritage
  4. Memorisation of scriptures
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. The all-round drawing out of the best in child and man — body, mind, and spirit

Concept: Gandhi = all-round development = Nai Talim (Basic Education). Craft-centred, mother-tongue medium, integrated. "Drawing out" = education from Latin "educare" = to bring out.

Report this question

3The Latin root "Educare" means:

  1. To train physically
  2. To draw out or bring out from within
  3. To fill in
  4. To examine
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. To draw out or bring out from within

Concept: Educare = draw out. This root supports child-centred, constructivist education — the idea that knowledge exists within the child and education reveals it. Contrast with "banking education."

Report this question

4Aristotle defined education as:

  1. The creation of a sound mind in a sound body
  2. Filling an empty vessel
  3. Transmission of moral values only
  4. Memorisation of facts
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. The creation of a sound mind in a sound body

Concept: Aristotle = "mens sana in corpore sano" = intellectual + physical development. Holistic ancient Greek view. Often paired with Plato's definition in NET questions.

Report this question

5Herbert Spencer's definition emphasises education as:

  1. Political socialisation
  2. Complete living — preparation for complete living
  3. Artistic development
  4. Religious instruction
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Complete living — preparation for complete living

Concept: Spencer (1860) = education = preparation for complete living in all its aspects — health, occupation, family, social. Utilitarian, scientific view of education.

Report this question

6"Education is not preparation for life; education is life itself." This is the view of:

  1. John Dewey
  2. Plato
  3. Froebel
  4. Rousseau
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. John Dewey

Direct memory: PYQ. Dewey's most famous statement. Education = continuous growth, continuous reconstruction of experience. Not a means to life — IS life.

Report this question

7Which of the following views of education is MOST consistent with Behaviourism?

  1. Education draws out the child's innate potential
  2. Education is continuous reconstruction
  3. Education is the free, unguided pursuit of the learner's own interests
  4. Education is shaping behaviour through systematic conditioning and reinforcement
Show answer

Answer: D. Education is shaping behaviour through systematic conditioning and reinforcement

Report this question

8Rousseau's view of education is best described as:

  1. Examination-focused
  2. Teacher-centred and discipline-focused
  3. Vocational and craft-based
  4. Nature-based — child is naturally good; society corrupts; education should follow nature
Show answer

Answer: D. Nature-based — child is naturally good; society corrupts; education should follow nature

Report this question

9"Education is the manifestation of divine perfection already in man." Who said this?

  1. Swami Vivekananda
  2. Sri Aurobindo
  3. Rabindranath Tagore
  4. Mahatma Gandhi
Show answer

Answer: A. Swami Vivekananda

10Which definition treats education as a "social process" through which the community transmits its acquired powers and aims to ensure its own existence and growth?

  1. Dewey
  2. Plato
  3. Froebel
  4. Spencer
