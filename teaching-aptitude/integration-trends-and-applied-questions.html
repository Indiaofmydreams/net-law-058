20 practice questions with answers

1The integration of MOOCs into formal higher education in India is facilitated by:

  1. AICTE's autonomous college guidelines
  2. RTE Act
  3. NAAC accreditation criteria only
  4. UGC's credit transfer framework linking SWAYAM to university degree programmes
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. UGC's credit transfer framework linking SWAYAM to university degree programmes

Concept: UGC's 2016 and subsequent circulars = formal bridge between informal MOOC learning (SWAYAM) and formal degree programmes. Credit transfer = legitimisation of MOOCs.

Report this question

2"Open and Distance Learning" (ODL) and MOOCs are similar in that both:

  1. Have very high completion rates
  2. Provide flexible, accessible education to learners separated from the traditional classroom
  3. Require physical attendance
  4. Are limited to undergraduate level
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Provide flexible, accessible education to learners separated from the traditional classroom

Concept: ODL = traditional distance education (IGNOU model). MOOCs = digital-era ODL. Both share the philosophy of breaking geographic and time barriers to education.

Report this question

3Which of the following is a correct distinction between SWAYAM and IGNOU?

  1. SWAYAM is for school students only; IGNOU is for postgraduates only
  2. SWAYAM offers free online courses for supplementary/credit learning; IGNOU is a full-fledged distance university granting degrees
  3. IGNOU offers only short courses; SWAYAM offers full degrees
  4. Both are identical in their approach
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. SWAYAM offers free online courses for supplementary/credit learning; IGNOU is a full-fledged distance university granting degrees

Concept: SWAYAM ≠ degree-granting. IGNOU = full degrees, diplomas, certificates through distance mode. SWAYAM = supplementary + credit transfer. Key distinction.

Report this question

4MOOCs are considered most suitable for which type of learning according to adult learning principles?

  1. Physical skill development
  2. Group-based mandatory learning
  3. Self-directed, problem-centred, flexible learning — consistent with andragogy
  4. Formal, structured, teacher-directed learning
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Self-directed, problem-centred, flexible learning — consistent with andragogy

Concept: MOOCs align perfectly with Knowles' andragogy — self-direction, flexibility, learning at own pace, choosing relevant courses = adult learning principles in action.

Report this question

5Which of the following statements about MOOCs in India is CORRECT as per recent trends?

  1. MOOCs are being increasingly integrated into formal education through credit transfer, blended models, and NEP 2020 implementation
  2. MOOCs in India are only for engineering students
  3. MOOCs are declining in India due to lack of interest
  4. MOOCs have completely replaced traditional universities
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. MOOCs are being increasingly integrated into formal education through credit transfer, blended models, and NEP 2020 implementation

Concept: Post-NEP 2020, SWAYAM + ABC + blended learning = official policy. MOOCs not replacing but SUPPLEMENTING traditional education. Integration is the current trend.

Report this question

6The concept of "micro-credentials" in the context of MOOCs refers to:

  1. Credentials for students below 18 years
  2. Credentials given only to microbiologists
  3. Very small printed certificates
  4. Short, focused credentials for specific skills or competencies — stackable toward larger qualifications
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Short, focused credentials for specific skills or competencies — stackable toward larger qualifications

Concept: Micro-credentials = 21st-century trend. Short courses + certificates that can stack into diplomas or degrees. edX MicroMasters, Coursera Specialisations = examples. NEP 2020 envisions this.

Report this question

7Post-COVID education in India has seen which of the following trends related to MOOCs?

  1. Complete abandonment of MOOCs
  2. Replacement of all teachers by AI
  3. Massive increase in SWAYAM enrolment and acceleration of blended learning adoption
  4. Return exclusively to offline education
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Massive increase in SWAYAM enrolment and acceleration of blended learning adoption

Concept: COVID = digital education accelerant. SWAYAM enrolment jumped dramatically. Blended learning went from optional to necessary. This shift became permanent.

Report this question

8"Adaptive learning" in the context of MOOCs means:

  1. The platform adjusts content difficulty, pace, and feedback based on individual learner performance data
  2. Adapting paper-based content to digital format
  3. Students adapting to new classrooms
  4. Teachers adapting to student behaviour
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. The platform adjusts content difficulty, pace, and feedback based on individual learner performance data

Concept: AI-driven adaptive learning = personalised MOOC. Platform tracks performance → adjusts next lesson difficulty → provides targeted feedback. The future of MOOCs.

9"Learning Analytics" in MOOCs refers to:

  1. Mathematical analysis taught as a MOOC course
  2. Using data generated by learners' online activities to understand, predict, and improve learning
  3. Analytics of library books borrowed
  4. Teacher performance analysis
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Using data generated by learners' online activities to understand, predict, and improve learning

Concept: Learning Analytics = big data applied to education. MOOC platforms generate enormous data (clicks, time spent, quiz scores, forum activity) → used to identify at-risk students, improve content, personalise learning.

10Which of the following global MOOC platforms is non-profit?

  1. Coursera
  2. edX (original model — now acquired by 2U Inc.)
  3. Udemy
  4. Udacity
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. edX (original model — now acquired by 2U Inc.)

Concept: edX was originally launched as non-profit (MIT + Harvard). Coursera, Udemy, Udacity = for-profit. edX was later acquired by 2U Inc. but maintained open access courses.

11The SWAYAM Plus initiative extends SWAYAM to:

  1. Industry-relevant courses and professional upskilling in collaboration with industry partners
  2. Only international students
  3. Providing food to students
  4. Primary school education
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Industry-relevant courses and professional upskilling in collaboration with industry partners

Concept: SWAYAM Plus = industry-focused extension. Bridges academia-industry gap. Skill development + employment readiness = key purpose.

12A professor at IIT Bombay develops a MOOC course on thermodynamics for SWAYAM. The course has 50,000 enrolled students. Which pedagogical challenge is MOST significant?

  1. Creating PDFs
  2. Providing meaningful, individualised feedback and maintaining engagement across 50,000 students
  3. Delivering video lectures
  4. Recording the lectures
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Providing meaningful, individualised feedback and maintaining engagement across 50,000 students

Concept: Scale is MOOC's biggest pedagogical challenge. 50,000 students = impossible for individual feedback. Solutions: peer assessment, automated grading, AI feedback. Engagement = completion rate problem.

13Which of the following correctly identifies the role of NPTEL in India's online education ecosystem?

  1. NPTEL (IIT/IISc) creates high-quality STEM video lecture content, which is now integrated into SWAYAM
  2. NPTEL is the funding body for SWAYAM
  3. NPTEL offers courses only for management students
  4. NPTEL and SWAYAM are competing platforms
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. NPTEL (IIT/IISc) creates high-quality STEM video lecture content, which is now integrated into SWAYAM

Concept: NPTEL predates SWAYAM (started 2003). It was the precursor to SWAYAM — its content was the foundation. Now NPTEL = national coordinator for engineering/science courses on SWAYAM.

Report this question

14MOOCs differ from traditional e-learning in that:

  1. Traditional e-learning uses videos; MOOCs use only text
  2. MOOCs require payment; e-learning is free
  3. There is no difference
  4. MOOCs are massive in scale, open in access, and often involve peer interaction; traditional e-learning is usually closed, small-scale, and institution-specific
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. MOOCs are massive in scale, open in access, and often involve peer interaction; traditional e-learning is usually closed, small-scale, and institution-specific

Concept: The MASSIVE + OPEN combination distinguishes MOOCs. Traditional LMS-based e-learning (Moodle for a single college) = closed, enrolled students only, not open access.

15In the context of SWAYAM, what does "active learning" in the platform's full name refer to?

  1. Teaching others actively
  2. Learner's active engagement through quizzes, discussions, and assignments — not passive video watching alone
  3. Learning while running
  4. Physical activity during courses
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Learner's active engagement through quizzes, discussions, and assignments — not passive video watching alone

Concept: "Active-Learning" in SWAYAM's name = the 4-quadrant model ensures learners DO things (take quizzes, join discussions, submit assignments) not just watch videos. Active ≠ passive consumption.

16The phrase "anytime, anywhere learning" is associated with:

  1. Traditional face-to-face classroom
  2. Apprenticeship model
  3. MOOCs and online learning platforms like SWAYAM
  4. Laboratory-based learning
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. MOOCs and online learning platforms like SWAYAM

Concept: Flexibility of time and place = defining feature of MOOCs. Learner studies at midnight or from a village or from abroad = "anytime, anywhere."

17A university wishes to introduce a blended learning programme using SWAYAM. The BEST approach would be:

  1. Allow students to watch SWAYAM instead of attending examinations
  2. Replace all lectures with SWAYAM videos and cancel all physical classes
  3. Use SWAYAM content for foundational/theoretical knowledge delivery, and use classroom time for discussion, problem-solving, and application
  4. Make SWAYAM optional with no credit recognition
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Use SWAYAM content for foundational/theoretical knowledge delivery, and use classroom time for discussion, problem-solving, and application

Concept: This is the flipped classroom + blended learning model. SWAYAM = content delivery (homework/pre-class). Classroom = HOTS, interaction, application. Best pedagogical use of SWAYAM.

18Which of the following is the MOST accurate criticism of MOOCs from a social justice perspective?

  1. MOOCs are too inexpensive
  2. MOOCs promote traditional education too strongly
  3. MOOCs do not have enough STEM content
  4. MOOCs benefit already-educated, digitally literate individuals most — potentially deepening inequality rather than reducing it
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. MOOCs benefit already-educated, digitally literate individuals most — potentially deepening inequality rather than reducing it

Concept: Research shows MOOC users globally are predominantly already-educated adults with degrees. The truly marginalised lack devices, internet, digital literacy. This "participation paradox" is a major critique.

19The full ecosystem of India's digital education as envisioned under NEP 2020 includes:

  1. SWAYAM + SWAYAMPRABHA + DIKSHA + ABC + National Digital Library + Virtual Labs
  2. SWAYAM only
  3. Only IIT video lectures
  4. Only IGNOU distance programmes
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. SWAYAM + SWAYAMPRABHA + DIKSHA + ABC + National Digital Library + Virtual Labs

Concept: NEP 2020 envisions an integrated ecosystem:
SWAYAM = MOOCs / SWAYAMPRABHA = DTH / DIKSHA = school teacher training / ABC = credit banking / NDLI = digital library / Virtual Labs = online lab simulations. All together.

20A UGC NET aspirant correctly identifies all the following about SWAYAM EXCEPT:

  1. Certification requires only completing video lectures without any examination
  2. Credit transfer is possible up to 40% of degree credits
  3. It was launched in 2017
  4. It uses a 4-quadrant content model
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Certification requires only completing video lectures without any examination

Concept: This is INCORRECT. Certification = mandatory proctored examination at a designated centre. Watching videos alone = no certificate. This is the most common misconception about SWAYAM.
