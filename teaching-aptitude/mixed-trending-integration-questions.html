20 practice questions with answers

1NEP 2020 replaced which earlier education policy?

  1. NPE 2010
  2. NPE 1968
  3. NPE 2005
  4. NPE 1986 (amended 1992)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. NPE 1986 (amended 1992)

Concept: NEP 2020 (National Education Policy 2020) replaced NPE 1986. Key features: multidisciplinary, flexible, OBE, mother tongue instruction, 5+3+3+4 school structure.

2Which of the following is a key feature of NEP 2020 for higher education?

  1. Abolition of research in universities
  2. Multidisciplinary education with flexible credit system
  3. Single-subject specialisation from year 1
  4. Only technical education to be funded
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Multidisciplinary education with flexible credit system

Concept: NEP 2020 = multidisciplinary + Academic Bank of Credits (ABC) + multiple entry-exit + internationalisation.

Report this question

3The Academic Bank of Credits (ABC) under NEP 2020 allows students to:

  1. Store and transfer academic credits across institutions and programs
  2. Take loans from banks for education
  3. Skip examinations with credit points
  4. Receive scholarships automatically
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Store and transfer academic credits across institutions and programs

Concept: ABC = digital credit repository. Like a bank account for credits. Students can pause, transfer, and resume education. Huge flexibility.

Report this question

4Which of the following theorists is associated with Connectivism as a learning theory for the digital age?

  1. George Siemens
  2. Piaget
  3. Vygotsky
  4. Skinner
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. George Siemens

Concept: Siemens (2004) — Connectivism = knowledge distributed across networks. Learning = connecting nodes. Most relevant for 21st-century digital learning.

Report this question

5According to constructivist theory, the role of the teacher is that of a:

  1. Information transmitter
  2. Examiner
  3. Facilitator and co-explorer
  4. Director and controller
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Facilitator and co-explorer

Concept: Constructivism (Piaget, Vygotsky, Bruner) = learner builds knowledge. Teacher facilitates, scaffolds, provokes thinking. NOT a transmitter.

Report this question

6Inclusive education means:

  1. Education that includes all learners — including those with disabilities — in regular classrooms
  2. Education exclusively for differently-abled students in special schools
  3. Education for gifted students only
  4. Education for economically weaker sections only
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Education that includes all learners — including those with disabilities — in regular classrooms

Concept: Salamanca Statement (1994) + RTE Act 2009 (India) = inclusive education = all children, regular classrooms, appropriate support.

Report this question

7The digital divide in education refers to:

  1. Gap in access to technology and internet between different socioeconomic groups
  2. Difference in digital design skills
  3. Variation in use of social media
  4. Difference in handwriting between urban and rural students
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Gap in access to technology and internet between different socioeconomic groups

Concept: Digital divide = equity issue in online education. SWAYAMPRABHA (DTH) was launched partly to address this — those without internet can still access education.

Report this question

8Which of the following is the CORRECT sequence for the Problem-Solving method (Dewey)?

  1. Hypothesis → Problem → Data → Solution
  2. Identify problem → Define problem → Collect data → Formulate hypothesis → Test hypothesis → Conclusion
  3. Data → Hypothesis → Problem → Test
  4. Conclusion → Hypothesis → Problem
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Identify problem → Define problem → Collect data → Formulate hypothesis → Test hypothesis → Conclusion

Mnemonic: ID-C-FT-C → "I Don't Care For That Conclusion" (until I test it)
Identify → Define → Collect → Formulate → Test → Conclude

Report this question

9Which of the following statements correctly represents the relationship between Formative and Summative Evaluation?

  1. Summative always occurs before formative
  2. Formative provides feedback during learning; summative judges at the end
  3. Formative is always more important than summative
  4. Formative and summative are mutually exclusive and cannot co-exist
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Formative provides feedback during learning; summative judges at the end

Concept: Both are necessary. Formative = ongoing improvement. Summative = final certification. They are complementary, not competing.

Report this question

10Which of the following factors affecting teaching is related to PHYSICAL ENVIRONMENT?

  1. Curriculum design
  2. Learner motivation
  3. Teacher's subject knowledge
  4. Classroom lighting and ventilation
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Classroom lighting and ventilation

Concept: Physical/institutional factors = furniture, lighting, space, acoustics, temperature. These affect learner attention and comfort.

Report this question

11A teacher who gives frequent short quizzes, asks questions during class, and adjusts explanation based on student responses is practising:

  1. Formative evaluation
  2. Summative evaluation
  3. Placement evaluation
  4. Norm-referenced evaluation
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Formative evaluation

Concept: All these = ongoing, feedback-driven, adjustment-oriented = Formative. Classic applied scenario question in NET.

Report this question

12"Teaching is successful only if learning has occurred" — this view is held by:

  1. Humanists
  2. Constructivists
  3. Cognitivists
  4. Behaviourists
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Behaviourists

Concept: Behaviourists (Skinner, Thorndike) = no learning = no teaching. Constructivists say teaching is the provision of conditions — learning depends on the learner's active engagement.

Report this question

13Which of the following is a characteristic of the Seminar method?

  1. Individual drill and practice exercises
  2. Teacher lectures for the full session
  3. Students passively receive information
  4. A student or group presents a paper and the group critically discusses it
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. A student or group presents a paper and the group critically discusses it

Concept: Seminar = student-led, critical discussion, research-based. Most appropriate for postgraduate education.

Report this question

14The term "hidden curriculum" refers to:

  1. The syllabus that is not officially published
  2. Implicit values, norms, and messages conveyed through school culture and routine
  3. Extra-curricular activities
  4. Online curriculum not visible to students
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Implicit values, norms, and messages conveyed through school culture and routine

Concept: Hidden curriculum (Philip Jackson) = what students learn beyond formal content — punctuality, obedience, hierarchy. Very important trending concept.

Report this question

15Which of the following best describes "Mastery Learning" (Bloom)?

  1. Given enough time and appropriate instruction, most students can achieve mastery
  2. Mastery learning ignores individual differences
  3. Only gifted students can master content
  4. Mastery is demonstrated only through final exams
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Given enough time and appropriate instruction, most students can achieve mastery

Concept: Bloom's Mastery Learning (1971) = diagnostic pre-test → teach → formative test → corrective instruction → post-test. All students CAN achieve if conditions are right.

Report this question

16Which of the following is a key principle of Outcome-Based Education (OBE)?

  1. All students must learn at the same pace
  2. Learning outcomes are clearly defined and all activities are aligned to achieve them
  3. Content coverage is the primary measure of success
  4. Assessment is only done at the end of the program
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Learning outcomes are clearly defined and all activities are aligned to achieve them

Concept: OBE = constructive alignment (Biggs) — outcomes → teaching strategies → assessment must all align. This is the cornerstone of NAAC, NBA, and NEP 2020.

Report this question

17The teacher who says "Let me think about that — I'm not sure" models which quality important in higher education?

  1. Avoidance of responsibility
  2. Intellectual humility and honesty
  3. Intellectual arrogance
  4. Incompetence
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Intellectual humility and honesty

Concept: Higher education values epistemic humility. Teacher as co-learner (constructivist view). Admitting uncertainty models good academic behaviour.

Report this question

18Which of the following pairs of teaching methods is BOTH learner-centred?

  1. Lecture and Demonstration
  2. Lecture and Recitation
  3. Recitation and Demonstration
  4. Project method and Problem-solving method
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Project method and Problem-solving method

Concept: Both require active student engagement, inquiry, and self-direction. Lecture/Demonstration/Recitation = teacher-centred.

Report this question

19A researcher studying why students repeatedly fail a particular mathematical concept and then designing remedial instruction is practising:

  1. Formative evaluation
  2. Placement evaluation
  3. Summative evaluation
  4. Diagnostic evaluation followed by remediation
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Diagnostic evaluation followed by remediation

Concept: Finding WHY = Diagnostic. Designing remedial = corrective action. This is the fullest application of diagnostic evaluation.

Report this question

20According to UGC NET trends, the MOST important single factor for effective teaching in higher education is:

  1. Integration of content knowledge, pedagogical skill, and genuine care for learners
  2. Use of technology in every class
  3. Attractive personality of the teacher
  4. Strict discipline
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Integration of content knowledge, pedagogical skill, and genuine care for learners

Concept: Shulman's PCK + humanistic teacher qualities + learner-centred approach = the gold standard answer for "best teacher" questions in NET. Technology and personality are supplementary, not primary.
