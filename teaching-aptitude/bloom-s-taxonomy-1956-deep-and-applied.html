About this question set

This set has 15 multiple-choice questions on Bloom's Taxonomy 1956: Deep & Applied from the UGC NET Paper 1 syllabus, unit 1, Teaching Aptitude. 15 practice questions with answers

1Bloom's Taxonomy of Educational Objectives (1956) was a collaborative work. The cognitive domain was published in:

  1. 1956
  2. 1964
  3. 1972
  4. 1948
Show answer

Answer: A. 1956

Report this question

2The full title of Bloom's 1956 work is:

  1. Taxonomy of Educational Objectives: The Classification of Educational Goals, Handbook I: Cognitive Domain
  2. Taxonomy of Learning Outcomes
  3. Handbook of Educational Psychology
  4. Classification of Instructional Objectives
Show answer

Answer: A. Taxonomy of Educational Objectives: The Classification of Educational Goals, Handbook I: Cognitive Domain

3A student can define "photosynthesis" perfectly but cannot explain why plants wilt without sunlight. This student is operating at which level?

  1. Knowledge
  2. Application
  3. Analysis
  4. Comprehension
Show answer

Answer: A. Knowledge

4A student explains in their own words how a nuclear reactor works after reading about it. This represents which level of original Bloom's taxonomy?

  1. Application
  2. Knowledge
  3. Comprehension
  4. Synthesis

12Bloom's original taxonomy uses "nouns" to label levels. Anderson's revision changed these to "verbs" because:

  1. Nouns were too scientific
  2. Verbs better represent the active cognitive processes learners perform
  3. Verbs are easier to spell
  4. The revision was purely cosmetic

Answer: B. Verbs better represent the active cognitive processes learners perform

13A NET aspirant memorises all definitions from study material but cannot apply concepts to new questions. According to Bloom, this student has mastered only:

  1. Knowledge
  2. Application
  3. Comprehension
  4. Analysis

14Which of the following is a CORRECTLY matched pair from Bloom's original taxonomy?

  2. Synthesis — Breaking down into parts
