About this question set

This set has 15 multiple-choice questions on John Dewey from the UGC NET Paper 1 syllabus, unit 1, Teaching Aptitude. Try each question first, then tap Show answer to check the key. This set is an answer-key drill: check each answer, then revisit the notes for any you miss.

For the concepts and case law behind these questions, read the Teaching Aptitude notes. Once you are comfortable, test yourself under exam conditions with a full-length mock test.

15 practice questions with answers

1Dewey's philosophical position is best described as:

  1. Pragmatism / Experimentalism
  2. Idealism
  3. Existentialism
  4. Realism
Show answer

Answer: A. Pragmatism / Experimentalism

Report this question

2Dewey's concept of "Learning by Doing" is most directly implemented through:

  1. Dictation
  2. Rote memorisation
  3. Project method and problem-solving activities
  4. Lecture method
Show answer

Answer: C. Project method and problem-solving activities

Report this question

3Dewey believed the school should be:

  1. Focused only on academic subjects
  2. Separate from real life and society
  3. A miniature society — a simplified and purified version of the larger social environment
  4. A place of strict discipline and rote learning
Show answer

Answer: C. A miniature society — a simplified and purified version of the larger social environment

Report this question

4Dewey's five steps of "Reflective Thinking" begin with:

  1. Drawing conclusions
  2. Forming a hypothesis
  3. Felt difficulty — a problem or obstacle in experience
  4. Testing a hypothesis
Show answer

Answer: C. Felt difficulty — a problem or obstacle in experience

Report this question

5The correct sequence of Dewey's five steps of Reflective Thinking is:

  1. Hypothesis → Problem → Data → Test → Conclusion
  2. Question → Answer → Practice → Evaluation → Feedback
  3. Observation → Theory → Experiment → Conclusion → Application
  4. Felt difficulty → Definition of problem → Suggestion of hypothesis → Reasoning → Testing
Show answer

Answer: D. Felt difficulty → Definition of problem → Suggestion of hypothesis → Reasoning → Testing

Report this question

6Dewey's concept of "experience" in education means:

  1. Attending lectures
  2. Reading textbooks thoroughly
  3. The active interaction between the organism and its environment — the basis of all genuine learning
  4. Years of teaching service
Show answer

Answer: C. The active interaction between the organism and its environment — the basis of all genuine learning

Report this question

7According to Dewey, which of the following is the best criterion for selecting curriculum content?

  1. The extent to which content connects to the child's present experience and has social utility
  2. Examination requirements
  3. Traditional cultural values only
  4. Government mandate
Show answer

Answer: A. The extent to which content connects to the child's present experience and has social utility

Report this question

8Dewey's "Instrumentalism" refers to:

  1. The idea that knowledge and ideas are instruments or tools for solving problems — their value is in their usefulness
  2. Teaching music instruments
  3. Instrumental conditioning
  4. Using audio-visual instruments in teaching
Show answer

Answer: A. The idea that knowledge and ideas are instruments or tools for solving problems — their value is in their usefulness

Report this question

9Dewey established the "Laboratory School" at which university?

  1. Columbia
  2. Harvard
  3. Stanford
  4. University of Chicago
Show answer

Answer: D. University of Chicago

Report this question

10Dewey's view that "education is a social process" means:

  1. Social studies is the most important subject
  2. Education occurs through participation in shared social activities and is inherently democratic
  3. Education should be conducted in large social groups
  4. Education is the government's responsibility alone
Show answer

Answer: B. Education occurs through participation in shared social activities and is inherently democratic

Report this question

11Which of the following teaching practices is MOST inconsistent with Dewey's philosophy?

  1. Community problem-solving activities
  2. Project-based learning
  3. Teacher-dictated notes for student memorisation
  4. Cooperative group work
Show answer

Answer: C. Teacher-dictated notes for student memorisation

Report this question

12Dewey's concept of "growth" as the aim of education implies:

  1. Physical growth of children
  2. Growth in examination scores
  3. Continuous, open-ended development — the capacity for further experience and learning, with no fixed terminal end
  4. Growth of the institution
Show answer

Answer: C. Continuous, open-ended development — the capacity for further experience and learning, with no fixed terminal end

Report this question

13A teacher designs a lesson where students identify a community water problem, research it, propose solutions, and present to local officials. This most reflects:

  1. Skinner's operant conditioning
  2. Dewey's pragmatist, problem-solving, democratic education philosophy
  3. Herbart's formal steps
  4. Bloom's memory level objectives
Show answer

Answer: B. Dewey's pragmatist, problem-solving, democratic education philosophy

Report this question

14Dewey's criticism of traditional education was that it:

  1. Was too child-centred
  2. Was passive, adult-imposed, disconnected from students' lives, and focused on remote future preparation rather than present living
  3. Gave too much freedom to students
  4. Used too many activities
Show answer

Answer: B. Was passive, adult-imposed, disconnected from students' lives, and focused on remote future preparation rather than present living

Report this question

15The book in which Dewey most comprehensively expressed his educational philosophy is:

  1. Republic
  2. Emile
  3. Pedagogy of the Oppressed
  4. Democracy and Education (1916)
Show answer

Answer: D. Democracy and Education (1916)
