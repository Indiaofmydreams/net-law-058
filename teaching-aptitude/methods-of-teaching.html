About this question set

This set has 20 multiple-choice questions on Methods of Teaching from the UGC NET Paper 1 syllabus, unit 1, Teaching Aptitude. Try each question first, then tap Show answer to check the key and read the explanation. 20 of the 20 questions carry a written explanation.

For the concepts and case law behind these questions, read the Teaching Aptitude notes. Once you are comfortable, test yourself under exam conditions with a full-length mock test.

20 practice questions with answers

1The Project Method of teaching was developed by:

  1. John Dewey
  2. Ralph Tyler
  3. William H. Kilpatrick
  4. H.C. Morrison
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. William H. Kilpatrick

Concept: Kilpatrick developed project method in 1918, based on Dewey's philosophy. PYQ — appeared multiple times.

Report this question

2The Problem-Solving method of teaching is based on the philosophy of:

  1. Skinner
  2. Bloom
  3. John Dewey
  4. Herbart
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. John Dewey

Concept: Dewey's reflective thinking = 5 steps: Identify problem → Define → Collect data → Hypothesise → Test. Problem-solving = Dewey.

Report this question

3Which teaching method is most suitable for developing critical thinking at the university level?

  1. Demonstration method
  2. Drill and practice
  3. Lecture method
  4. Seminar method
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Seminar method

Concept: Seminar = student-led discussion, presentation, critique. Develops research, communication, critical analysis — perfect for higher education.

Report this question

4The lecture method is best suited for:

  1. Developing emotional intelligence
  2. Transmitting large amounts of information to large groups efficiently
  3. Promoting individual inquiry
  4. Developing practical skills
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Transmitting large amounts of information to large groups efficiently

Concept: Lecture = most efficient information delivery. Its limitation = passive learner, only Memory level.

Report this question

5Which of the following is a LEARNER-CENTRED method?

  1. Demonstration
  2. Recitation
  3. Problem-solving
  4. Lecture
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Problem-solving

Concept: Learner-centred = student is active. Lecture/Demonstration/Recitation = teacher active, student passive.

6SWAYAM stands for:

  1. Smart Web Aided Youth Academic Modules
  2. Student Web and Youth Access Management
  3. Study Webs of Active-Learning for Young Aspiring Minds
  4. Systematised Web Access for Youth and Management
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Study Webs of Active-Learning for Young Aspiring Minds

Concept: PYQ. Government of India platform for free MOOCs. Full form has been directly asked in NET.

Report this question

7SWAYAMPRABHA is:

  1. A scholarship scheme
  2. A DTH TV channel for educational content
  3. A mobile application for students
  4. An examination platform
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. A DTH TV channel for educational content

Concept: SWAYAMPRABHA = 32+ DTH channels broadcasting educational content 24×7. Complements SWAYAM. Bridges digital divide.

Report this question

8MOOCs stands for:

  1. Modern Online Open Courses
  2. Managed Open Online Coaching
  3. Multiple Online Organised Curriculum
  4. Massive Open Online Courses
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Massive Open Online Courses

Concept: PYQ. Massive = unlimited enrolment. Open = no entry barrier. Key features: video lectures, quizzes, discussion forums, certificates.

Report this question

9The concept of cMOOC (Connectivist MOOC) was developed by:

  1. Malcolm Knowles
  2. John Dewey
  3. George Siemens
  4. Stephen Krashen
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. George Siemens

Concept: Siemens' Connectivism (2004) = learning through networks. cMOOC = knowledge co-created. xMOOC = content-delivery (Coursera model).

Report this question

10Blended learning is best described as:

  1. Teaching in multiple languages
  2. Teaching only through technology
  3. A combination of face-to-face and online learning
  4. Traditional classroom teaching only
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. A combination of face-to-face and online learning

Concept: Blended/Hybrid = best of both worlds. Post-COVID, this is the most tested emerging pedagogy in UGC NET.

Report this question

11The Case Study method originated in:

  1. Harvard Law and Business Schools
  2. IIT Delhi
  3. Medical colleges
  4. Oxford University
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Harvard Law and Business Schools

Concept: Case study = real-world complex scenarios. Used in law, medicine, management. Harvard pioneered this method.

Report this question

12Which of the following methods is based on Socratic dialogue?

  1. Project method
  2. Question-answer / Discussion method
  3. Demonstration method
  4. Lecture method
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Question-answer / Discussion method

Concept: Socrates used questioning (maieutics = midwifery of ideas) to draw out knowledge. Discussion method is the modern descendant.

Report this question

13In which method does the teacher simulate real-life situations for learners to act out?

  1. Tutorial
  2. Lecture
  3. Role Play / Simulation
  4. Seminar
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Role Play / Simulation

Concept: Simulation/Role play = experiential learning. Develops empathy, decision-making, communication. Used in teacher training, management, medical education.

Report this question

14Tutorial method is characterised by:

  1. One-way communication from teacher
  2. Large group instruction
  3. Examination-focused drilling
  4. Small group personalised interaction
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Small group personalised interaction

Concept: Tutorial = small group (3–8 students typically) + direct teacher interaction + personalised feedback. Common in UK university system.

Report this question

15Which of the following correctly differentiates off-line and on-line methods?

  1. Offline requires physical presence; online requires internet
  2. Offline methods cannot promote higher-order thinking
  3. Online is only for higher education
  4. Offline is inferior; online is superior
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Offline requires physical presence; online requires internet

Concept: The key distinction is modality. Quality of thinking depends on HOW the method is used, not the medium itself.

Report this question

16NPTEL is associated with:

  1. National Policy on Technology Enhanced Learning
  2. National Platform for Technical Education Learning
  3. National Programme on Technology Enhanced Learning — IIT/IISc platform
  4. National Portal for Teacher Education Links
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. National Programme on Technology Enhanced Learning — IIT/IISc platform

Concept: NPTEL is a joint initiative of IITs and IISc for free online courses, especially STEM. Feeds into SWAYAM.

Report this question

17Which of the following best describes the "Flipped Classroom" model?

  1. Content is delivered at home (video); class time used for application and discussion
  2. Students physically rotate classrooms
  3. Students teach the teacher
  4. Teaching is done in reverse order of the syllabus
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Content is delivered at home (video); class time used for application and discussion

Concept: Flipped = flip homework and lecture. At home = watch lecture. In class = practise, discuss, problem-solve. Promotes HOTS in class.

Report this question

18The method most appropriate for teaching psychomotor skills is:

  1. Lecture
  2. Demonstration followed by practice
  3. Seminar
  4. Discussion
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Demonstration followed by practice

Concept: Psychomotor = motor skills. Learn by seeing (demonstration) + doing (practice). Imitation → Manipulation in Dave's taxonomy.

Report this question

19Cooperative learning is based on which principle?

  1. Teacher-centred transmission
  2. Individual competition
  3. Silent individual study
  4. Students learning together in structured groups towards a common goal
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Students learning together in structured groups towards a common goal

Concept: Cooperative learning (Johnson & Johnson) = positive interdependence + individual accountability + face-to-face interaction. Based on Vygotsky's social learning.

Report this question

20Which method of teaching is most aligned with Constructivism?

  1. Drill and practice
  2. Inquiry-based learning
  3. Lecture method
  4. Rote memorisation
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Inquiry-based learning

Concept: Constructivism = learner builds knowledge through experience and inquiry. Teacher = guide, not transmitter. Piaget + Vygotsky = constructivism.
