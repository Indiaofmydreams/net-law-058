This set has 14 multiple-choice questions on Edgar Dale: Cone of Experience from the UGC NET Paper 1 syllabus, unit 1, Teaching Aptitude. Try each question first, then tap Show answer to check the key. This set is an answer-key drill: check each answer, then revisit the notes for any you miss.

14 practice questions with answers

1Edgar Dale's "Cone of Experience" was first published in his book:

  1. Teaching and Learning
  2. Learning by Doing
  3. Audio-Visual Methods in Teaching (1946)
  4. Taxonomy of Teaching Aids
Show answer

Answer: C. Audio-Visual Methods in Teaching (1946)

2How many levels does Dale's Cone of Experience have?

  1. 9
  2. 13
  3. 7
  4. 11
Show answer

Answer: D. 11

Report this question

3In Dale's Cone, which type of experience sits at the BASE (most concrete, most effective for learning)?

  1. Direct purposeful experiences
  2. Visual symbols
  3. Demonstrations
  4. Television
Show answer

Answer: A. Direct purposeful experiences

Report this question

4In Dale's Cone, "Verbal symbols" placed at the TOP represent:

  1. Most entertaining experiences
  2. Best learning experiences
  3. Most abstract, least direct learning experiences
  4. Most concrete experiences
Show answer

Answer: C. Most abstract, least direct learning experiences

Report this question

5According to Dale's Cone, which of the following learning experiences is MORE concrete than watching a film but LESS concrete than a field trip?

  1. Verbal lecture
  2. Exhibits and museums
  3. Reading a textbook
  4. Radio broadcast
Show answer

Answer: B. Exhibits and museums

Report this question

6The order from BASE to TOP of Dale's Cone (most concrete to most abstract) is:

  1. Verbal → Visual → Television → Field trips → Direct experience
  2. Verbal → Television → Direct experience → Demonstrations
  3. Visual symbols → Field trips → Television → Direct experience
  4. Direct experience → Contrived experience → Dramatised experience → Demonstrations → Field trips → Exhibits → Television → Still pictures → Visual symbols → Verbal symbols
Show answer

Answer: D. Direct experience → Contrived experience → Dramatised experience → Demonstrations → Field trips → Exhibits → Television → Still pictures → Visual symbols → Verbal symbols

7"Contrived experiences" in Dale's Cone (second from bottom) include:

  1. Watching documentaries
  2. Field visits to real locations
  3. Reading descriptions in books
  4. Models, mock-ups, simulations, and specimens that substitute for real experience
Show answer

Answer: D. Models, mock-ups, simulations, and specimens that substitute for real experience

8A science teacher uses a 3D model of the human heart instead of a real heart for teaching. According to Dale's Cone, this is a:

  1. Visual symbol
  2. Direct experience
  3. Contrived experience
  4. Dramatised experience
Show answer

Answer: C. Contrived experience

9A history teacher has students enact the signing of the Indian Constitution. According to Dale's Cone, this is:

  1. Dramatised experience / role play
  2. Demonstration
  3. Contrived experience
  4. Direct experience

Answer: A. Dramatised experience / role play

  1. 50%
  2. 30%
  3. 90%

11According to Dale's research, learners tend to remember approximately what percentage of what they SAY AND DO?

  1. 10%
  2. 90%
  3. 30%
  4. 50%

12The key educational principle of Dale's Cone is:

  1. Verbal instruction is most reliable
  2. Abstract learning is most efficient
  3. The more concrete and direct the learning experience, the greater the comprehension and retention
  4. Technology always improves learning
Show answer

Answer: C. The more concrete and direct the learning experience, the greater the comprehension and retention

13A medical college replaces cadaver dissection (unavailable) with a 3D digital anatomy simulation. According to Dale's Cone, this moves the learning experience from:

  1. Field trips to television
  2. Verbal symbols to visual symbols
  3. Direct experience to contrived/simulated experience
  4. Exhibits to demonstrations
Show answer

Answer: C. Direct experience to contrived/simulated experience

Report this question

14The criticism of Dale's Cone is that:

  1. It recommends too much direct experience
  2. It oversimplifies learning by implying a strict hierarchy — in reality, abstract symbols (language, text) can be highly effective for advanced learners
  3. It ignores technology
  4. It is too concrete in its recommendations
Show answer

Answer: B. It oversimplifies learning by implying a strict hierarchy — in reality, abstract symbols (language, text) can be highly effective for advanced learners
