25 practice questions with answers

1"Pedagogical Content Knowledge" (PCK) as a teacher-related factor means:

  1. Knowing the subject matter AND knowing how to make it understandable to learners
  2. Knowledge of classroom furniture arrangement
  3. Administrative knowledge of school policy
  4. Physical fitness of the teacher
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Knowing the subject matter AND knowing how to make it understandable to learners

Concept: Shulman (1986) — PCK = intersection of content knowledge + pedagogical knowledge. The most important professional knowledge a teacher must have.

2Which of the following is a LEARNER-RELATED factor that most significantly affects teaching effectiveness?

  1. Quality of textbooks
  2. Classroom size
  3. Learner's prior knowledge and existing conceptual framework
  4. Teacher's academic qualifications
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Learner's prior knowledge and existing conceptual framework

Concept: Ausubel — "The most important factor influencing learning is what the learner already knows. Ascertain this and teach accordingly." Prior knowledge = foundation of new learning.

3David Ausubel's statement "Find out what the learner already knows and teach accordingly" emphasises which factor affecting teaching?

  1. Support material factor
  2. Teacher's communication skill
  3. Institutional factor
  4. Learner's prior knowledge (learner-related factor)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Learner's prior knowledge (learner-related factor)

Concept: This is one of the most quoted lines in educational psychology and has appeared directly in UGC NET. Prior knowledge = most crucial learner variable.

4The "hidden curriculum" of an institution — its culture, values, and unspoken rules — is an example of which factor affecting teaching?

  1. Learner factor
  2. Institutional / Environmental factor
  3. Teacher factor
  4. Support material factor
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Institutional / Environmental factor

Concept: Hidden curriculum = institutional culture. What the institution implicitly values shapes what and how teaching occurs, even beyond the formal syllabus.

5A teacher who modifies her explanation mid-lesson based on student confusion is demonstrating:

  1. Memory level teaching
  2. Poor lesson planning
  3. Dependence on textbook
  4. Adaptive teaching — a key teacher-related quality
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Adaptive teaching — a key teacher-related quality

Concept: Adaptive/Responsive teaching = real-time adjustment based on formative feedback. Highest expression of teacher skill. Requires both content knowledge and learner awareness.

Report this question

6Which of the following support materials is MOST effective for developing abstract scientific concepts, according to Dale's Cone?

  1. Verbal descriptions alone
  2. Direct hands-on laboratory experiments
  3. Radio broadcasts
  4. Written text
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Direct hands-on laboratory experiments

Concept: Dale's Cone = more concrete = more effective. Lab experiments = direct experience = base of cone = highest retention. Verbal = top = most abstract = least effective.

Report this question

7The "Matthew Effect" in education refers to:

  1. Students who start with more knowledge tend to learn more, widening the gap with weaker students
  2. Teachers with more experience teach worse
  3. The more students study, the less they remember
  4. The effect of class size on performance
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Students who start with more knowledge tend to learn more, widening the gap with weaker students

Concept: "The rich get richer" in learning. Named after Biblical Matthew 25:29. Strong foundational knowledge accelerates further learning. Directly affects teaching — weak prior knowledge students fall further behind without intervention.

Report this question

8Which institutional factor MOST directly affects the quality of teaching?

  1. Academic leadership, professional development opportunities, and institutional culture of excellence
  2. The colour of classroom walls
  3. The location of the institution
  4. The number of canteens on campus
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Academic leadership, professional development opportunities, and institutional culture of excellence

Concept: Institutional quality = leadership vision + teacher growth opportunities + culture. These systemic factors shape teaching more than physical infrastructure alone.

Report this question

9"Noise pollution" near a school or college is an example of which factor affecting teaching?

  1. Curriculum factor
  2. Physical environment factor
  3. Teacher factor
  4. Learner factor
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Physical environment factor

Concept: External noise = physical environment = distraction = reduced attention = impaired teaching effectiveness. Environment includes both classroom interior and surrounding context.

10The factor of "class size" primarily affects teaching because:

  1. Larger classes make individualised attention, formative feedback, and interactive methods more difficult
  2. Smaller classes always mean poorer social learning
  3. Larger classes = automatically better learning
  4. Class size has no effect on teaching quality
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Larger classes make individualised attention, formative feedback, and interactive methods more difficult

Concept: Large classes force teachers toward lecture method (Memory level). Small classes enable discussion, tutorials, personalised feedback (Understanding/Reflective levels).

Report this question

11Which of the following teacher characteristics is associated with "withitness" (Kounin)?

  1. Teacher's subject knowledge
  2. Teacher's physical presence
  3. Teacher's ability to be simultaneously aware of multiple classroom events
  4. Teacher's examination marking speed
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Teacher's ability to be simultaneously aware of multiple classroom events

Concept: Kounin's "withitness" (1970) = teacher's eyes-in-the-back-of-the-head quality. Awareness of all classroom happenings simultaneously = effective classroom management.

Report this question

12The "Pygmalion Effect" or "Rosenthal Effect" in teaching refers to:

  1. Students rising or falling to meet teacher expectations — teacher expectations become self-fulfilling prophecies
  2. Teachers performing better when observed
  3. The effect of classroom decoration on student performance
  4. Students performing poorly because teachers set too many assignments
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Students rising or falling to meet teacher expectations — teacher expectations become self-fulfilling prophecies

Concept: Rosenthal & Jacobson (1968) — when teachers were told certain students were "bloomers" (randomly selected), those students actually improved more. Expectation → teacher behaviour → student outcome. Very important concept for NET.

Report this question

13Which of the following learner factors is MOST difficult for a teacher to directly modify?

  1. Attendance
  2. Note-taking skills
  3. Classroom study habits
  4. Socioeconomic background and home environment
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Socioeconomic background and home environment

Concept: Teacher can influence study skills, habits, and motivation. But SES and home environment are external — teacher must work around them, not directly change them. Policy-level interventions are needed.

14"Teacher burnout" as a factor affecting teaching quality is caused by:

  1. Having too many resources
  2. Teaching too few classes
  3. Excessive salary
  4. Chronic occupational stress — emotional exhaustion, depersonalisation, reduced personal accomplishment
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Chronic occupational stress — emotional exhaustion, depersonalisation, reduced personal accomplishment

Concept: Maslach's Burnout Inventory (1981) — three components: emotional exhaustion + depersonalisation + reduced efficacy. Burned-out teachers deliver poorer quality instruction.

15The instructional facility that MOST improves access to current information for learners is:

  1. Individual study carrels
  2. Smart classroom with internet connectivity
  3. Large lecture hall
  4. Traditional library with only print books
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Smart classroom with internet connectivity

Concept: Internet + smart board = real-time access to current knowledge, multimedia, simulations. Directly upgrades teaching from static to dynamic.

16Which of the following is a SUPPORT MATERIAL factor rather than a teacher factor?

  1. Teacher's communication skill
  2. Teacher's enthusiasm
  3. Quality and availability of laboratory equipment
  4. Teacher's knowledge of learner psychology
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Quality and availability of laboratory equipment

Concept: Equipment, library, digital resources = support material factors. These are external to the teacher but affect what and how effectively teaching can occur.

Report this question

17"Psychological safety" in the classroom — where students feel safe to ask questions, make mistakes, and express opinions — is classified under which factor affecting teaching?

  1. Learning environment factor
  2. Institutional factor
  3. Teacher factor
  4. Learner factor
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Learning environment factor

Concept: Psychological climate = learning environment. Amy Edmondson's "psychological safety" is now recognised as the most critical environmental factor for learning and creativity.

Report this question

18When a teacher uses examples, analogies, and stories relevant to the cultural background of learners, this addresses which factor?

  1. Institutional factor
  2. Support material factor
  3. Teacher's research interest
  4. Learner's sociocultural background — making content culturally relevant
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Learner's sociocultural background — making content culturally relevant

Concept: Culturally Responsive Teaching (Gloria Ladson-Billings) = connecting curriculum to students' lived cultural experience. Addresses diversity as a learner factor.

Report this question

19The factor of "time-on-task" in teaching refers to:

  1. The actual amount of time students spend actively engaged with learning content
  2. Total length of the academic calendar
  3. Teacher's preparation time
  4. Duration of examination
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. The actual amount of time students spend actively engaged with learning content

Concept: Carroll's Model of Learning (1963) — degree of learning = time spent/time needed. More time actively engaged = more learning. Distractions, off-task behaviour reduce effective learning time.

Report this question

20Which of the following is the MOST important learner-related factor in adult education specifically?

  1. Physical age of the learner
  2. The learner's handwriting speed
  3. The score on intelligence tests
  4. The learner's voluntariness and intrinsic motivation for enrolling
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. The learner's voluntariness and intrinsic motivation for enrolling

Concept: Adults typically choose to learn. Voluntary participation + internal motivation = strongest predictor of adult learning success. Forced adult learning (compliance training) has very poor outcomes.

Report this question

21"Attention" as a learner factor is affected by:

  1. Only the teacher's speaking voice
  2. Exclusively by the difficulty level of content
  3. Interest, relevance, emotional state, stimulus novelty, and physical comfort
  4. Only the physical seating arrangement
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Interest, relevance, emotional state, stimulus novelty, and physical comfort

Concept: Attention is multidetermined. Teachers can enhance attention through: relevance (connection to learner's life), novelty (stimulus variation), and a comfortable physical environment.

22Which of the following correctly lists all five major categories of factors affecting teaching?

  1. Content, Method, Assessment, Teacher, Student
  2. Teacher, Learner, Support Material, Instructional Facilities, Learning Environment and Institution
  3. Teacher, Learner, Curriculum, Support Material, Evaluation
  4. Teacher, Parent, Community, Government, Media
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Teacher, Learner, Support Material, Instructional Facilities, Learning Environment and Institution

Concept: Direct from UGC NET syllabus for Unit I. These exact five categories are stated in the official syllabus. Knowing them by name is important.

23"Transfer of training" from classroom to real-life application is MOST supported by which combination of factors?

  1. Meaningful learning at Understanding/Reflective level + real-world examples + authentic assessment
  2. Strict attendance policy + large library
  3. Teacher's academic qualifications alone
  4. Memorisation of textbook content + frequent testing
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Meaningful learning at Understanding/Reflective level + real-world examples + authentic assessment

Concept: Transfer = applying learning in new contexts. Requires meaningful learning (not rote), examples from real life, and assessment that tests application — multiple factors working together.

Report this question

24Which statement BEST summarises the interaction between teacher factors and learner factors in effective teaching?

  1. Learner factors alone determine learning outcomes
  2. Institutional factors override all others
  3. Effective teaching emerges from the dynamic interaction between teacher qualities and learner characteristics — neither alone is sufficient
  4. Teacher factors alone determine teaching quality
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Effective teaching emerges from the dynamic interaction between teacher qualities and learner characteristics — neither alone is sufficient

Concept: Teaching is a transactional process. Best teacher + unmotivated learner = limited success. Motivated learner + poor teacher = partial success. Optimal = both factors aligned.

Report this question

25A college that provides teachers with regular workshops on new pedagogical methods, research support, sabbatical leave, and peer observation programmes is addressing which factor affecting teaching?

  1. Learner factor
  2. Physical environment factor
  3. Support material factor
  4. Institutional factor — teacher professional development
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Institutional factor — teacher professional development

Concept: Institutional support for teacher growth = the most sustainable way to improve teaching quality system-wide. Individual teacher effort matters, but institutional structures determine whether that effort is sustained and rewarded.
