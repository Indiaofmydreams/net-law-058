About this question set

This set has 17 multiple-choice questions on SWAYAM: Comprehensive from the UGC NET Paper 1 syllabus, unit 1, Teaching Aptitude. Try each question first, then tap Show answer to check the key and read the explanation. 17 of the 17 questions carry a written explanation.

For the concepts and case law behind these questions, read the Teaching Aptitude notes. Once you are comfortable, test yourself under exam conditions with a full-length mock test.

17 practice questions with answers

1SWAYAM was launched by which Government of India ministry?

  1. Ministry of Finance
  2. Ministry of Electronics and IT
  3. Ministry of Human Resource Development (now Ministry of Education)
  4. Ministry of Science and Technology
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Ministry of Human Resource Development (now Ministry of Education)

Concept: MoE (formerly MHRD) launched SWAYAM. Renamed MoHRD to MoE in 2020 under NEP 2020. Both names acceptable in answer.

Report this question

2SWAYAM was launched in the year:

  1. 2015
  2. 2017
  3. 2014
  4. 2019
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. 2017

Direct memory: SWAYAM was officially launched on July 9, 2017. Prime Minister Narendra Modi inaugurated it.

Report this question

3SWAYAM courses are delivered through:

  1. Physical classroom sessions only
  2. Television broadcasts only
  3. Radio programmes
  4. Online video lectures, reading material, self-assessment tests, and discussion forums
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Online video lectures, reading material, self-assessment tests, and discussion forums

Concept: SWAYAM = 4-quadrant approach:
Q1 = Video lectures
Q2 = Reading material (e-books, PDF)
Q3 = Self-assessment (MCQs, assignments)
Q4 = Discussion forum (doubt-clearing)

Report this question

4The "4-quadrant approach" of SWAYAM includes which four components?

  1. Video lecture, Reading material, Self-assessment, Discussion forum
  2. Video, Audio, Text, Image
  3. Online, Offline, Hybrid, Blended
  4. Lecture, Tutorial, Lab, Project
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Video lecture, Reading material, Self-assessment, Discussion forum

Direct memory: 4 quadrants is one of the most frequently asked SWAYAM facts in UGC NET.
Mnemonic: V-R-S-D → "Very Reliable Study Device"
Video → Reading → Self-assessment → Discussion

Report this question

5SWAYAM courses are available for which levels of education?

  1. Only postgraduate level
  2. Only professional courses
  3. Only higher education (UG and PG)
  4. Class 9 to postgraduate level — school, undergraduate, and postgraduate
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Class 9 to postgraduate level — school, undergraduate, and postgraduate

Concept: SWAYAM covers Class 9 onwards to postgraduate. Not primary level. Covers school board curricula, UG, PG, and professional courses.

Report this question

6SWAYAM courses are offered by which institutions?

  1. Only central universities
  2. IITs, IIMs, central universities, NITs, and other reputed institutions — coordinated by 9 National Coordinators
  3. Only IGNOU
  4. Only IITs
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. IITs, IIMs, central universities, NITs, and other reputed institutions — coordinated by 9 National Coordinators

Concept: 9 National Coordinators manage different subject areas on SWAYAM. Different institutions handle different disciplines.

Report this question

7The credit transfer policy for SWAYAM allows students to:

  1. Get salary increments based on SWAYAM certificates
  2. Get academic credits for SWAYAM courses that count toward their university degree
  3. Skip examinations at their home university
  4. Transfer credits to foreign universities only
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Get academic credits for SWAYAM courses that count toward their university degree

Concept: UGC circular (2016) — universities must allow credit transfer for SWAYAM courses. Students can earn up to 40% credits through SWAYAM. This is the most important UGC-level implication.

Report this question

8The maximum percentage of credits a student can earn through SWAYAM toward their degree (as per UGC guidelines) is:

  1. 20%
  2. 40%
  3. 10%
  4. 60%
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. 40%

Direct memory: UGC recommendation = up to 40% of total credits can come from SWAYAM courses. This figure has appeared in NET questions.

Report this question

9SWAYAM courses are available at what cost?

  1. Free only for BPL students
  2. Very high fee — comparable to private universities
  3. Free of cost for learning; a nominal fee for the proctored examination
  4. Moderate fee
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Free of cost for learning; a nominal fee for the proctored examination

Concept: Watching lectures, reading material, and discussion = FREE. To get a certificate, student must appear in a proctored end-term exam = nominal registration fee.

Report this question

10SWAYAM certificates are awarded after:

  1. Completing video lectures only
  2. Submitting an assignment
  3. Peer evaluation by other learners
  4. Appearing in and passing a proctored examination conducted at designated centres
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Appearing in and passing a proctored examination conducted at designated centres

Concept: The final certification requires a physical proctored exam to ensure authenticity. This differentiates SWAYAM from other open online learning — it has credibility through examination.

Report this question

11Who coordinates the SWAYAM platform at the national level?

  1. IGNOU alone
  2. Ministry of Education with multiple national coordinators including UGC, AICTE, NPTEL, CEC
  3. NCERT
  4. UGC
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Ministry of Education with multiple national coordinators including UGC, AICTE, NPTEL, CEC

Concept: 9 National Coordinators on SWAYAM:
- NPTEL = Engineering, Basic Sciences
- UGC = Post-graduation
- CEC = Undergraduate
- NCERT = School (9–12)
- NIOS = School (open)
- IGNOU = Out-of-school
- IIMB = Management
- AICTE = Technical
- NH-E = Law

Report this question

12CEC in the context of SWAYAM stands for:

  1. Centre for E-learning Content
  2. Consortium for Educational Communication
  3. Council for Education and Curriculum
  4. Central Examination Council
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Consortium for Educational Communication

Concept: CEC = national coordinator for undergraduate courses on SWAYAM. CEC was established by UGC for educational broadcasting and now manages UG content on SWAYAM.

Report this question

13SWAYAM is implemented using which software platform?

  1. WordPress
  2. Moodle (Open edX platform)
  3. Google Classroom
  4. Blackboard
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Moodle (Open edX platform)

Concept: SWAYAM runs on Open edX — the open-source platform also used by edX globally. Microsoft provided technical support for initial implementation.

Report this question

14Which of the following best describes the primary objective of SWAYAM?

  1. To earn revenue for the government through paid courses
  2. To replace all university education with online education
  3. To reduce the number of teachers needed in schools
  4. To provide free, high-quality education to every citizen, especially the digitally and geographically disadvantaged
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. To provide free, high-quality education to every citizen, especially the digitally and geographically disadvantaged

Concept: SWAYAM's mission = bridge the quality gap. Students in remote areas, small colleges, and underserved communities get the SAME quality content as top-tier institution students.

Report this question

15SWAYAM is India's response to which global movement?

  1. Massive Open Online Course (MOOC) movement
  2. Digital India Programme only
  3. Right to Education Act
  4. Open Source Software movement
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Massive Open Online Course (MOOC) movement

Concept: SWAYAM = India's own national MOOC platform. It is India's answer to Coursera, edX, etc. — built on Indian needs, in Indian languages, with Indian content.

Report this question

16SWAYAM courses in regional Indian languages are important because:

  1. Regional language courses earn more credits
  2. English content is unavailable online
  3. They address the language barrier that prevents many students from accessing quality online education
  4. UGC mandates all courses in regional languages only
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. They address the language barrier that prevents many students from accessing quality online education

Concept: Many Indian students struggle with English-medium content. SWAYAM courses in Hindi and regional languages dramatically expand accessibility — critical for equity.

Report this question

17The discussion forum on SWAYAM serves which pedagogical purpose?

  1. Doubt-clearing, peer interaction, and collaborative learning — compensating for the absence of face-to-face teacher interaction
  2. Entertainment for students
  3. Examination preparation only
  4. Administrative communication
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Doubt-clearing, peer interaction, and collaborative learning — compensating for the absence of face-to-face teacher interaction

Concept: Discussion forum = Q4 of 4-quadrant approach. Enables social learning (Vygotsky) in the otherwise isolated online environment.
