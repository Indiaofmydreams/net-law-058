15 practice questions with answers

1The 'Fiction Theory' of corporate personality is associated with:

  1. Gierke
  2. Savigny
  3. Ihering
  4. Brinz
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Savigny

Savigny's Fiction Theory: a corporation is a mere legal fiction; only humans can truly be legal persons.

Report this question

2The 'Realist Theory' of corporate personality is associated with:

  1. Savigny
  2. Salmond
  3. Gierke
  4. Kelsen
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Gierke

Gierke's Organic/Realist Theory: a corporation has real existence as a social organism independent of its members.

Report this question

3'Bracket Theory' of corporate personality is given by:

  1. Savigny
  2. Gierke
  3. Duguit
  4. Ihering
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Ihering

Ihering's Bracket Theory: members are the real persons; the corporation is just a bracket around them.

Report this question

4A 'corporation sole' consists of:

  1. A group of individuals
  2. A single person and their successors in an office
  3. A company with limited liability
  4. A public authority with statutory powers
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. A single person and their successors in an office

A corporation sole is a single person holding an office and their successors, e.g., the Crown, Bishop of a diocese.

Report this question

5The legal position of an unborn child in law is:

  1. Has full legal personality from conception
  2. Has legal personality only after birth but rights vest from conception for their benefit
  3. Has no legal rights whatsoever
  4. Has legal rights only after 6 months in the womb
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Has legal personality only after birth but rights vest from conception for their benefit

Maxim: 'Nasciturus pro iam nato habetur' — the unborn is treated as already born for its benefit.

Report this question

6Which Indian river was granted legal personality by the Uttarakhand High Court?

  1. Yamuna only
  2. Ganga only
  3. Both Ganga and Yamuna
  4. Narmada
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Both Ganga and Yamuna

In Mohd. Salim v. State of Uttarakhand (2017), both Ganga and Yamuna were granted legal personhood (later stayed by Supreme Court).

Report this question

7Dead persons in law:

  1. Retain full legal personality
  2. Lose legal personality upon death but certain interests are protected
  3. Retain personality for 30 years after death
  4. Have personality only if they left a will
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Lose legal personality upon death but certain interests are protected

Death extinguishes legal personality, but interests in property and reputation receive some post-mortem protection.

Report this question

8The 'Concession Theory' of corporate personality holds that:

  1. Corporations have real existence independent of State
  2. Personality is a fiction
  3. All members collectively form the legal person
  4. The State grants personality and can take it away
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. The State grants personality and can take it away

Under the Concession Theory, the State grants corporate personality — it is not inherent.

Report this question

9The maxim 'Nasciturus pro iam nato habetur' protects the rights of:

  1. Unborn children
  2. Deceased persons
  3. Mentally unsound persons
  4. Corporations
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Unborn children

This maxim means the unborn is treated as already born for the purposes of protecting its interests.

Report this question

10'Corpus' in the context of a corporation means:

  1. The physical assets of the company
  2. The charter of incorporation
  3. The body of members forming the corporation
  4. The registered office
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. The body of members forming the corporation

Corpus = the body — in a corporation aggregate, it refers to the body of members.

Report this question

11The 'piercing of the corporate veil' doctrine means:

  1. Increasing a company's liability
  2. Dissolving a company by court order
  3. Looking behind the corporate entity to hold members/directors personally liable
  4. Registering a company
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Looking behind the corporate entity to hold members/directors personally liable

Courts pierce the veil in cases of fraud, sham, or evasion of legal obligations.

Report this question

12The principle of separate corporate personality was established in:

  1. Salomon v. Salomon & Co. Ltd.
  2. Rylands v. Fletcher
  3. Donoghue v. Stevenson
  4. Ashby v. White
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Salomon v. Salomon & Co. Ltd.

Salomon v. Salomon (1897): a company is a distinct legal person separate from its shareholders.

Report this question

13A minor's legal personality is:

  1. Complete in all respects
  2. Non-existent
  3. Limited — certain rights and duties are restricted due to age
  4. Same as that of an adult in all civil matters
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Limited — certain rights and duties are restricted due to age

Minors have legal personality but with limitations — they cannot contract, vote, etc.

Report this question

14'Purpose theory' of corporate personality (Zweckvermögen theory) is given by:

  1. Savigny
  2. Gierke
  3. Ihering
  4. Brinz
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Brinz

Brinz: a corporation is property dedicated to a purpose — no real person exists behind it.

Report this question

15In India, a temple or a mosque can be a:

  1. Natural person
  2. Juristic/legal person
  3. Quasi-person
  4. None of the above
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Juristic/legal person

Hindu idols and religious endowments have been recognised as juristic persons in India capable of holding property.
