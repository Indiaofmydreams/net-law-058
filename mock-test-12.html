1A television channel asks viewers to phone in to vote on an issue. The sample is most affected by:

  1. stratification bias
  2. self-selection bias
  3. systematic sampling error
  4. non-response in a probability sample
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. self-selection bias

Respondents choose to participate, so they may differ from the population.

3Read the passage and answer:
Standardised testing is often defended as an impartial measure because every candidate answers the same questions under the same conditions. Yet identical conditions do not guarantee equal opportunity: candidates differ in access to preparation, familiarity with test formats and even the language in which questions are framed. A test may thus be perfectly consistent in measuring something while measuring something other than what its designers intend. Reliability, in other words, is no proof of validity; and a score that is stable from one sitting to the next may simply be a stable record of advantage.

Which of the following is NOT supported by the passage?

  1. Language of framing can matter
  2. Equal conditions do not ensure equal opportunity
  3. A stable score may record advantage
  4. Standardised tests ought to be abolished
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Standardised tests ought to be abolished

The author criticises a defence of testing but never calls for abolition.

4Data: Revenue (₹ crore), 2019–2022: 120, 150, 180, 210. Expenditure (₹ crore): 100, 110, 150, 168. (Profit = Revenue − Expenditure.)

The revenue in 2022 is higher than the four-year average revenue by about:

  1. 22.5%
  2. 27.3%
  3. 25%
  4. 30%
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. 27.3%

Average 165; (210 − 165)/165 × 100 = 27.3%.

5In McLuhan's classification of media, which of the following is a 'cool' medium?

  1. Print
  2. Photograph
  3. Radio
  4. Television
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Television

McLuhan treated radio, photograph and print as hot media and television as cool.
