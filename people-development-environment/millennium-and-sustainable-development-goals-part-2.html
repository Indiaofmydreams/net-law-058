About this question set

This set has 25 multiple-choice questions on Millennium and Sustainable Development Goals from the UGC NET Paper 1 syllabus, unit 9, People, Development and Environment. Try each question first, then tap Show answer to check the key. This set is an answer-key drill: check each answer, then revisit the notes for any you miss.

For the concepts and case law behind these questions, read the People, Development & Environment notes. Once you are comfortable, test yourself under exam conditions with a full-length mock test.
