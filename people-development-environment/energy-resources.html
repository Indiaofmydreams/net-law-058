10 practice questions with answers

1A source of renewable energy which can be developed with minimum cost is:

  1. Tidal power
  2. Wind mills
  3. Geothermal energy
  4. Biomass power
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Biomass power

Biomass power can be developed with minimum cost as it uses agricultural waste, wood, and organic materials that are readily and cheaply available, requiring less infrastructure than tidal, wind, or geothermal.

2Among the following fuels, which is the most environmentally friendly?

  1. Biogas
  2. CNG
  3. Hydrogen
  4. Ethanol
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Hydrogen

Hydrogen is the most environmentally friendly fuel as it produces only water (H2O) when burned. It has zero carbon emissions at point of use, making it the cleanest combustion fuel.

3As per India's renewable energy targets, the percentage share of solar and wind energy combined in total power from renewable sources by 2022 was about:

  1. ~50%
  2. ~57%
  3. ~75%
  4. ~91.43%
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. ~91.43%

In India's 2022 renewable target of 175 GW, solar (~100 GW) and wind (~60 GW) together accounted for ~91.43% of the total, as per government projections.

4The percentage share of solar energy in India's total renewable energy target (2022) was approximately:

  1. ~47.5%
  2. ~57.1%
  3. ~65.5%
  4. ~75%
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. ~57.1%

Solar energy was targeted to contribute ~100 GW out of 175 GW total renewable capacity, amounting to approximately 57.1% of India's 2022 renewable target.

5India's target for additional carbon sink (in CO2 equivalent) through forest and tree cover by 2030 is:

  1. 1 to 1.5 billion tonnes
  2. 1.5 to 2 billion tonnes
  3. 2.5 to 3 billion tonnes
  4. 3.5 to 4 billion tonnes
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. 2.5 to 3 billion tonnes

As part of India's NDC under the Paris Agreement, India committed to creating an additional carbon sink of 2.5 to 3 billion tonnes of CO2 equivalent through forest and tree cover by 2030.

6In terms of per capita energy consumption (kWh/year), the correct descending sequence is:

  1. Brazil > Russia > China > India
  2. Russia > China > India > Brazil
  3. Russia > China > Brazil > India
  4. China > Russia > Brazil > India
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Russia > China > Brazil > India

Russia has the highest per capita energy consumption, followed by China, then Brazil, with India having the lowest per capita consumption among these four countries.

7In terms of total CO2 emissions from a country, the correct descending sequence is:

  1. USA > China > India > Russia
  2. China > USA > Russia > India
  3. China > USA > India > Russia
  4. USA > China > Russia > India
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. China > USA > India > Russia

China is the world's largest CO2 emitter, followed by the USA, then India, then Russia in terms of total national CO2 emissions.

8India's contribution to total global carbon dioxide emissions is approximately:

  1. ~3%
  2. ~6%
  3. ~10%
  4. ~15%
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. ~6%

India contributes approximately 6% of global CO2 emissions, making it the third-largest emitter in absolute terms, though its per capita emissions are among the lowest of major economies.

9How much carbon (in billion tonnes) is being added to the atmosphere annually by burning of fossil fuels?

  1. 2–6 billion tonnes
  2. 4–6 billion tonnes
  3. 6–9 billion tonnes
  4. 9–12 billion tonnes
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. 6–9 billion tonnes

Burning of fossil fuels (coal, oil, natural gas) adds approximately 6–9 billion tonnes of carbon to the atmosphere annually, as established in climate science literature referenced in UGC NET PYQs.

Report this question

10Which of the following is NOT a renewable energy source?

  1. Tidal energy
  2. Geothermal energy
  3. Nuclear energy
  4. Biomass energy
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Nuclear energy

Nuclear energy uses uranium/thorium which are finite (non-renewable) resources, even though nuclear is sometimes classified as low-carbon. Tidal, geothermal, and biomass are renewable energy sources.
