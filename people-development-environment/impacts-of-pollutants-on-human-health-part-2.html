About this question set

This set has 25 multiple-choice questions on Impacts of Pollutants on Human Health from the UGC NET Paper 1 syllabus, unit 9, People, Development and Environment. Try each question first, then tap Show answer to check the key. This set is an answer-key drill: check each answer, then revisit the notes for any you miss.

For the concepts and case law behind these questions, read the People, Development & Environment notes. Once you are comfortable, test yourself under exam conditions with a full-length mock test.

25 practice questions with answers

1Children living near heavy traffic and exposed to nitrogen dioxide are at greater risk of:

  1. Scurvy
  2. Hearing loss only
  3. Respiratory infections and reduced lung growth
  4. Rickets
Show answer

Answer: C. Respiratory infections and reduced lung growth

2Household air pollution from burning solid fuels is mainly linked to:

  1. Skin cancer
  2. Acute respiratory infection in children and COPD in adults
  3. Anaemia only
  4. Hearing loss
Show answer

Answer: B. Acute respiratory infection in children and COPD in adults

3Carbon monoxide is called a "silent killer" because it is:

  1. Colourless and odourless

Answer: A. Colourless and odourless
