About this question set

This set has 21 multiple-choice questions on People, Development & Environment from the UGC NET Paper 1 syllabus, unit 9, People, Development and Environment. Try each question first, then tap Show answer to check the key and read the explanation. 21 of the 21 questions carry a written explanation.

For the concepts and case law behind these questions, read the People, Development & Environment notes. Once you are comfortable, test yourself under exam conditions with a full-length mock test.

21 practice questions with answers

1The number of Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) is:

  1. 8
  2. 12
  3. 17
  4. 20
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. 17

Seventeen goals adopted by the UN.

Report this question

2The SDGs were adopted in:

  1. 2000
  2. 2010
  3. 2015
  4. 2020
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. 2015

Agenda 2030 was adopted in 2015.

Report this question

3The ozone layer is found in the:

  1. Exosphere
  2. Mesosphere
  3. Troposphere
  4. Stratosphere
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Stratosphere

It absorbs harmful UV radiation.

Report this question

4Acid rain is mainly caused by:

  1. Oxygen
  2. Helium
  3. Argon
  4. Sulphur dioxide and nitrogen oxides
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Sulphur dioxide and nitrogen oxides

These form acids in the atmosphere.

Report this question

5A major greenhouse gas is:

  1. Carbon dioxide
  2. Oxygen
  3. Nitrogen
  4. Argon
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Carbon dioxide

CO2 traps heat.

Report this question

6The Montreal Protocol deals with:

  1. Whaling
  2. Ozone-depleting substances
  3. Wetlands
  4. Biodiversity
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Ozone-depleting substances

Adopted in 1987.

Report this question

7Which is a renewable energy source?

  1. Petroleum
  2. Coal
  3. Natural gas
  4. Solar energy
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Solar energy

Solar energy is continuously replenished.

Report this question

8The Paris Agreement aims to limit warming to well below:

  1. 1°C
  2. 2°C
  3. 3°C
  4. 5°C
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. 2°C

Above pre-industrial levels, pursuing 1.5°C.

Report this question

9Consider the statements: 1. Anthropogenic activities contribute to climate change. 2. Biomass is a non-renewable resource.

  1. Only statement 1 is correct
  2. Only statement 2 is correct
  3. Both statements are correct
  4. Neither statement is correct
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Only statement 1 is correct

Biomass is renewable.

Report this question

10Assertion (A): Deforestation increases atmospheric carbon dioxide.
Reason (R): Trees absorb carbon dioxide during photosynthesis.

  1. Both A and R are true, and R is the correct explanation of A
  2. Both A and R are true, but R is not the correct explanation of A
  3. A is true, but R is false
  4. A is false, but R is true
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Both A and R are true, and R is the correct explanation of A

R explains A.

Report this question

11A primary air pollutant is:

  1. Photochemical smog
  2. Peroxyacetyl nitrate
  3. Ground-level ozone
  4. Carbon monoxide
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Carbon monoxide

Emitted directly; the others form in the air.

Report this question

12Biochemical oxygen demand (BOD) indicates:

  1. Noise level
  2. Air quality
  3. Soil fertility
  4. Organic pollution in water
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Organic pollution in water

Higher BOD means more organic pollution.

Report this question

13Noise pollution is measured in:

  1. Decibels
  2. Pascals
  3. Hertz only
  4. Lux
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Decibels

dB scale.

14The Kyoto Protocol deals with:

  1. Ozone layer
  2. Wetlands
  3. Reducing greenhouse gas emissions
  4. Whaling
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Reducing greenhouse gas emissions

Adopted in 1997.

  1. Stockholm
  2. Rio de Janeiro
  3. Kyoto
  4. Paris

UNCED.

  1. 5 June
  2. 22 April
  3. 16 September
  4. 22 March

Since 1974.

  2. CFCs

  4. Oxygen

  3. Biomass
