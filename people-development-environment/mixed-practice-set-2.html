10Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) are formally known as:

  1. Sustainable Living For All: 2022 Agenda
  2. Transforming Our World: The 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development
  3. Life Of Dignity For All: 2022 Agenda
  4. One Planet, One People: 2030 Agenda
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Transforming Our World: The 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development

The SDGs, adopted in September 2015 by all 193 UN member states, are formally titled 'Transforming Our World: The 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development'.

15Which of the following most accurately distinguishes MDGs from SDGs?

  1. MDGs applied to all countries; SDGs applied only to developing nations
  2. MDGs were developed through multi-stakeholder consultations; SDGs were top-down
  3. MDGs targeted developing countries and were top-down; SDGs are universal and multi-stakeholder
  4. MDGs focused on environment; SDGs focused only on poverty reduction
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. MDGs targeted developing countries and were top-down; SDGs are universal and multi-stakeholder

MDGs (8 goals, 2000-2015) targeted only developing countries and were designed top-down. SDGs (17 goals, 2015-2030) are universal, applicable to all 193 nations, and developed through broader multi-stakeholder consultations.

16How many Millennium Development Goals (MDGs) were there?

  1. 6
  2. 8
  3. 10
  4. 17
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. 8

There were 8 MDGs with a deadline of 2015. They were replaced by 17 SDGs in 2015 with a 2030 deadline.

17Which of the following is the largest source of water pollution in major rivers of India?

  1. Agricultural run-off
  2. Religious practices
  3. Unregulated small scale industries
  4. Untreated sewage
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Untreated sewage

Untreated sewage is the largest source of water pollution in India's major rivers, as a significant portion of municipal wastewater is discharged without adequate treatment.

Report this question

18The arsenic pollution in groundwater in Bangladesh and parts of India is due to:

  1. Industrial waste disposal
  2. Thermal power plants
  3. Natural geological sources
  4. Agricultural practices
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Natural geological sources

Arsenic contamination of groundwater in Bangladesh and West Bengal (India) is primarily due to natural geological sources — arsenic leaches from alluvial sediments into aquifers, not from industrial activity.

Report this question

19Which of the following is NOT a water-borne disease?

  1. Typhoid
  2. Hepatitis A
  3. Cholera
  4. Dengue
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Dengue

Dengue is a vector-borne disease transmitted by the Aedes mosquito, not through contaminated water. Typhoid, Hepatitis A, and Cholera are all water-borne diseases.

  1. India
  2. USA
  3. European Union
  4. China

Answer: C. European Union

Among the given options, the European Union has the highest per capita water use. This is a PYQ-specific data point from NTA question papers.

21Minamata disease is caused by:

  1. Excess fluoride in water
  2. Mercury poisoning through contaminated fish
  3. Cadmium accumulation in bones
  4. Lead poisoning from paints
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Mercury poisoning through contaminated fish

Minamata disease is a neurological illness caused by severe mercury poisoning. It was first discovered in Minamata, Japan in 1956, caused by industrial wastewater containing methylmercury discharged into the bay.
