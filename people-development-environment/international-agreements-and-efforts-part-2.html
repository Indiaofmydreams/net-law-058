About this question set

This set has 25 multiple-choice questions on International Agreements and Efforts from the UGC NET Paper 1 syllabus, unit 9, People, Development and Environment. Try each question first, then tap Show answer to check the key. This set is an answer-key drill: check each answer, then revisit the notes for any you miss.

For the concepts and case law behind these questions, read the People, Development & Environment notes. Once you are comfortable, test yourself under exam conditions with a full-length mock test.

1The Kunming-Montreal Global Biodiversity Framework (2022) includes the "30 by 30" goal, which aims to protect:

  1. 30% reduction in plastic only
  2. 30% of the planet's land and sea by 2030
  3. 30 national parks only
  4. 30 species by 2030
