About this question set

This set has 25 multiple-choice questions on Millennium and Sustainable Development Goals from the UGC NET Paper 1 syllabus, unit 9, People, Development and Environment. Try each question first, then tap Show answer to check the key. This set is an answer-key drill: check each answer, then revisit the notes for any you miss.

For the concepts and case law behind these questions, read the People, Development & Environment notes. Once you are comfortable, test yourself under exam conditions with a full-length mock test.

25 practice questions with answers

1Sustainable development, as defined in the Brundtland Report, means meeting the needs of the present:

  1. Without compromising the ability of future generations to meet their own needs
  2. By maximizing economic growth only
  3. By conserving only forests
  4. By limiting population growth only
Show answer

Answer: A. Without compromising the ability of future generations to meet their own needs

Report this question

2The three pillars of sustainable development are:

  1. Economic, social and environmental
  2. Economic, political and technological
  3. Social, cultural and spiritual
  4. Political, social and cultural
Show answer

Answer: A. Economic, social and environmental
