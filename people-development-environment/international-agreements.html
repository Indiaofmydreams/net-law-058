9 practice questions with answers

1The Montreal Protocol (1987) primarily targets:

  1. Carbon dioxide emissions
  2. Ozone-depleting substances like CFCs
  3. Hydrofluorocarbons (HFCs)
  4. Methane from agriculture
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Ozone-depleting substances like CFCs

The Montreal Protocol (1987) is an international treaty designed to protect the ozone layer by phasing out production and consumption of ozone-depleting substances, primarily CFCs.

Report this question

2In which country was the recent international agreement on phasing out Hydrofluorocarbons (HFCs) signed?

  1. Morocco
  2. South Africa
  3. Algeria
  4. Rwanda
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Rwanda

The Kigali Amendment to the Montreal Protocol, which aims to phase out HFCs, was signed in Kigali, Rwanda in 2016.

Report this question

3The Kyoto Protocol set binding emission reduction targets for:

  1. All UN member states
  2. Only developing nations
  3. Only developed (Annex I) countries
  4. G20 nations only
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Only developed (Annex I) countries

The Kyoto Protocol (1997) was the first legally binding climate treaty but set targets only for developed (Annex I) countries. This was a key difference from the Paris Agreement which applies to all nations.

Report this question

4The Paris Agreement aims to limit global temperature rise to:

  1. 1°C above pre-industrial levels
  2. 1.5°C above pre-industrial levels only
  3. Well below 2°C, with efforts towards 1.5°C
  4. 2.5°C above pre-industrial levels
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Well below 2°C, with efforts towards 1.5°C

The Paris Agreement (COP21, 2015) aims to hold global temperature increase to well below 2°C above pre-industrial levels, while pursuing efforts to limit the increase to 1.5°C.

Report this question

5The Convention on Biological Diversity (CBD) was signed at:

  1. Kyoto, Japan (1997)
  2. Paris, France (2015)
  3. Rio de Janeiro, Brazil (1992)
  4. Montreal, Canada (1987)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Rio de Janeiro, Brazil (1992)

The CBD was signed at the Rio Earth Summit (UNCED) in 1992. Its three objectives are: conservation of biological diversity, sustainable use, and fair sharing of benefits from genetic resources.

6The International Solar Alliance (ISA) was proposed by India at:

  1. Rio+20 Summit, 2012
  2. COP21, Paris, 2015
  3. COP26, Glasgow, 2021
  4. Kyoto Protocol Conference, 1997
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. COP21, Paris, 2015

India proposed the International Solar Alliance (ISA) at COP21 in Paris in 2015. The ISA was officially launched in January 2016 and is headquartered in Gurugram, Haryana, India.

Report this question

7The Nagoya Protocol (2010) is related to:

  1. Ozone layer protection
  2. Climate change mitigation
  3. Access and benefit sharing of genetic resources under CBD
  4. Reduction of greenhouse gases
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Access and benefit sharing of genetic resources under CBD

The Nagoya Protocol (2010) is a supplementary agreement to the Convention on Biological Diversity, providing a framework for access to genetic resources and the fair and equitable sharing of benefits.

Report this question

8The Rio Earth Summit (1992) produced which of the following documents/agreements? (i) Agenda 21 (ii) Rio Declaration (iii) UNFCCC (iv) Paris Agreement

  1. (i) and (ii) only
  2. (i), (ii) and (iii)
  3. (i), (ii), (iii) and (iv)
  4. (ii), (iii) and (iv)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. (i), (ii) and (iii)

The 1992 Rio Earth Summit produced Agenda 21, the Rio Declaration on Environment and Development, the Convention on Biological Diversity, and the UNFCCC. The Paris Agreement came later in 2015.

Report this question

9The UNFCCC (United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change) was adopted in:

  1. 1987
  2. 1992
  3. 1997
  4. 2005
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. 1992

UNFCCC was adopted at the Rio Earth Summit in 1992 and entered into force in 1994. It is the parent treaty under which both the Kyoto Protocol (1997) and the Paris Agreement (2015) were negotiated.
