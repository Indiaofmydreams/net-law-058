8 practice questions with answers

1Which of the following phenomena is NOT a natural hazard?

  1. Wildfire
  2. Lightning
  3. Landslide
  4. Chemical contamination
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Chemical contamination

Chemical contamination is an anthropogenic (man-made) hazard, not a natural one. Wildfires (when lightning-caused), lightning, and landslides can all occur naturally.

2Which of the following natural hazards is NOT hydro-meteorological?

  1. Snow avalanche
  2. Sea erosion
  3. Tropical cyclone
  4. Tsunami
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Tsunami

Tsunami is a geophysical hazard caused by seismic activity (earthquakes, underwater landslides), not by weather or water cycle processes. Snow avalanche, sea erosion, and tropical cyclones are hydro-meteorological.

Report this question

3A meteorological hazard among the following is:

  1. Avalanche
  2. Coastal erosion
  3. Landslide
  4. Blizzard
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Blizzard

A blizzard is a severe snowstorm with strong winds — a purely meteorological hazard. Avalanche and landslide are geomorphological; coastal erosion is a hydrological hazard.

4The occurrence of natural hazards is affected by: (a) Land use changes (b) Drainage and construction (c) Ozone depletion (d) Climate change

  1. (a), (b) and (c)
  2. (a), (b) and (d)
  3. (b), (c) and (d)
  4. (a), (c) and (d)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. (a), (b) and (d)

Natural hazard occurrence is affected by land use changes, drainage/construction activities, and climate change. Ozone depletion alone does not directly affect natural hazard frequency.

5An earthquake of magnitude in the range of 6.0–6.9 on the Richter Scale is considered:

  1. Moderate
  2. Strong
  3. Major
  4. Great
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Strong

Richter Scale classification: <2.5 micro; 2.5–5.4 minor; 5.5–6.0 moderate; 6.0–6.9 Strong; 7.0–7.9 Major; 8.0+ Great.

6Two earthquakes A and B have magnitudes 5 and 6 respectively on the Richter Scale. The ratio of energies released EB/EA is approximately:

  1. ~8
  2. ~16
  3. ~32
  4. ~64
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. ~32

Each unit increase on the Richter Scale corresponds to approximately 31.6 (≈32) times more energy released. So magnitude 6 releases ~32 times more energy than magnitude 5.

7World Meteorological Organisation's (WMO) objective was to reduce deaths from hydro-meteorological disasters over the decade 2010–2019 (compared to 1994–2003) by:

  1. 25%
  2. 50%
  3. 75%
  4. 80%
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. 50%

The WMO set an objective to reduce deaths from hydro-meteorological disasters by 50% over the decade 2010–2019 compared to the reference decade 1994–2003.

Report this question

8Which of the following is the correct sequence of steps in Disaster Management?

  1. Response → Mitigation → Preparedness → Recovery
  2. Mitigation → Preparedness → Response → Recovery
  3. Preparedness → Response → Mitigation → Recovery
  4. Recovery → Mitigation → Preparedness → Response
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Mitigation → Preparedness → Response → Recovery

The Disaster Management Cycle follows: Mitigation (reducing risk) → Preparedness (planning/training) → Response (during disaster) → Recovery (after disaster). This is the internationally accepted framework.
