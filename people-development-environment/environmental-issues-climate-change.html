About this question set

This set has 15 multiple-choice questions on Environmental Issues: Climate Change from the UGC NET Paper 1 syllabus, unit 9, People, Development and Environment. Try each question first, then tap Show answer to check the key. This set is an answer-key drill: check each answer, then revisit the notes for any you miss.

15 practice questions with answers

1The United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change (UNFCCC) was adopted in:

  1. 1997
  2. 2015
  3. 1992
  4. 1988
Show answer

Answer: C. 1992

Report this question

2Climate differs from weather in that climate is:

  1. Long-term average of weather patterns
  2. Short-term atmospheric condition
  3. Day-to-day temperature only
  4. Only rainfall
Show answer

Answer: A. Long-term average of weather patterns

Report this question

3Melting of polar ice reduces Earth's albedo, which:

  1. Increases reflection of sunlight
  2. Increases absorption of solar radiation
  3. Cools the planet
  4. Reduces ocean temperature
Show answer

Answer: B. Increases absorption of solar radiation

Report this question

4El Niño is characterized by:

  1. Strengthening of trade winds
  2. Heavy rainfall over Australia always
  3. Cooling of the central Pacific
  4. Unusual warming of the eastern and central equatorial Pacific
Show answer

Answer: D. Unusual warming of the eastern and central equatorial Pacific

Report this question

5Thawing permafrost is a concern because it:

  1. Releases methane and carbon dioxide
  2. Absorbs carbon dioxide
  3. Increases albedo
  4. Reduces sea level
Show answer

Answer: A. Releases methane and carbon dioxide

Report this question

6Climate change is expected to increase the spread of:

  1. Only non-communicable diseases
  2. Only genetic diseases
  3. Vector-borne diseases such as malaria and dengue
  4. Only nutritional deficiencies
Show answer

Answer: C. Vector-borne diseases such as malaria and dengue

Report this question

7Rising frequency of heat waves mainly increases:

  1. Winter rainfall
  2. Crop yields in all regions
  3. Glacier mass
  4. Heat stress and mortality among vulnerable groups
Show answer

Answer: D. Heat stress and mortality among vulnerable groups

Report this question

8Which of the following is highly vulnerable to sea level rise?

  1. The Deccan Plateau
  2. Low-lying islands such as Tuvalu and the Sundarbans delta
  3. The Thar Desert
  4. The Aravalli range
Show answer

Answer: B. Low-lying islands such as Tuvalu and the Sundarbans delta

Report this question

9People displaced by sea level rise and extreme climate events are often termed:

  1. Seasonal workers
  2. Economic migrants only
  3. Climate refugees or climate-displaced persons
  4. Tourists
Show answer

Answer: C. Climate refugees or climate-displaced persons

Report this question

10The Himalayan region is often called the "Third Pole" because it:

  1. Holds the largest ice reserves outside the polar regions
  2. Lies at the South Pole
  3. Has no glaciers
  4. Has the coldest climate on Earth
Show answer

Answer: A. Holds the largest ice reserves outside the polar regions

Report this question

11Natural carbon sinks include:

  1. Landfills
  2. Coal mines
  3. Cement plants
  4. Forests and oceans
Show answer

Answer: D. Forests and oceans

  1. 2015

  1. Absorb all infrared radiation
