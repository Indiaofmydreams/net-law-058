This set has 25 multiple-choice questions on The Environment (Protection) Act, 1986 from the UGC NET Paper 1 syllabus, unit 9, People, Development and Environment. 1The Environment (Protection) Act was enacted in:

  1. 1974
  2. 1991
  3. 1986
  4. 1981
Show answer

Answer: C. 1986

2The Environment (Protection) Act was enacted to implement the decisions of the:

  1. Johannesburg Summit, 2002
  2. Rio Summit, 1992
  3. Kyoto Conference, 1997
  4. Stockholm Conference, 1972
Show answer

Answer: D. Stockholm Conference, 1972

3Parliament enacted the Environment (Protection) Act under which Article of the Constitution?

  1. Article 370
  2. Article 253
  3. Article 21
  4. Article 14
Show answer

Answer: B. Article 253

4The Environment (Protection) Act extends to:

  1. Whole of India
  2. Only Union Territories
  3. Only urban areas
  4. Only industrial states

Answer: A. Whole of India

5The Environment (Protection) Act, 1986 is often described as an:

  1. International treaty
  2. Amendment to the Water Act
  3. Judicial order
  4. Umbrella legislation

6The Environment (Protection) Act contains how many sections?

  1. 40
  2. 26
  3. 60
  4. 12

7The Act defines "environment" as including:

  1. Only forests
  2. Only the atmosphere
  3. Water, air and land and their interrelationship with living beings and property
  4. Only air and water
Show answer

Answer: C. Water, air and land and their interrelationship with living beings and property

Report this question

8An "environmental pollutant" under the Act is any solid, liquid or gaseous substance:

  1. Present in a concentration that is or may be injurious to the environment
  2. That is biodegradable only
  3. That is radioactive only
  4. Present in a laboratory only
Show answer

Answer: A. Present in a concentration that is or may be injurious to the environment

Report this question

9A "hazardous substance" under the Act is one that is liable to cause harm to:

  1. Only industrial machinery
  2. Only human beings
  3. Humans, other living creatures, plants, micro-organisms, property or the environment
  4. Only water bodies
Show answer

Answer: C. Humans, other living creatures, plants, micro-organisms, property or the environment

Report this question

10Section 3 of the Act deals with:

  1. Powers of the Central Government to take measures to protect and improve the environment
  2. Offences by companies
  3. Penalties
  4. Bar on civil courts

11Section 5 empowers the Central Government to:

  2. Issue directions, including closure or regulation of an industry

  4. Constitute courts
