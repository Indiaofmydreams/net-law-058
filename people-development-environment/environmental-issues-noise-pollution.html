About this question set

This set has 10 multiple-choice questions on Environmental Issues: Noise Pollution from the UGC NET Paper 1 syllabus, unit 9, People, Development and Environment. Try each question first, then tap Show answer to check the key. This set is an answer-key drill: check each answer, then revisit the notes for any you miss.

For the concepts and case law behind these questions, read the People, Development & Environment notes. Once you are comfortable, test yourself under exam conditions with a full-length mock test.

10 practice questions with answers

1As per CPCB standards in India, the ambient noise limit for residential areas during daytime is:

  1. 55 dB(A)
  2. 45 dB(A)
  3. 65 dB(A)
  4. 75 dB(A)
Show answer

Answer: A. 55 dB(A)

Report this question

2The Noise Pollution (Regulation and Control) Rules, 2000 were framed under the:

  1. Water Act, 1974
  2. Air Act, 1981
  3. Environment (Protection) Act, 1986
  4. Factories Act, 1948
Show answer

Answer: C. Environment (Protection) Act, 1986

3Under the Noise Rules, "night time" is defined as:

  1. 8 p.m. to 8 a.m.
  2. 9 p.m. to 5 a.m.
  3. 6 p.m. to 6 a.m.
  4. 10 p.m. to 6 a.m.
Show answer

Answer: D. 10 p.m. to 6 a.m.

4A silence zone is declared up to what distance around hospitals, courts and educational institutions?

  1. 500 m
  2. 100 m
  3. 250 m
  4. 50 m

5Prolonged exposure to loud noise can cause:

  1. Goitre
  2. Night blindness
  3. Scurvy
  4. Noise-induced hearing loss

  1. 100 times
