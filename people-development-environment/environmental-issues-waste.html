15 practice questions with answers

1The Solid Waste Management Rules, 2016 require segregation of waste at source into:

  1. Biodegradable and e-waste only
  2. Plastic and metal only
  3. Wet, dry and domestic hazardous waste
  4. Organic and inorganic only
Show answer

Answer: C. Wet, dry and domestic hazardous waste

Report this question

2Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) makes which party responsible for managing post-consumer waste?

  1. Producers
  2. Retailers only
  3. Local residents
  4. Municipal workers
Show answer

Answer: A. Producers
