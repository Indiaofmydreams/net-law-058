About this question set

This set has 17 multiple-choice questions on Specific Contracts — Agency from the UGC NET Law (058) syllabus, unit 6, Commercial Law. Try each question first, then tap Show answer to check the key and read the explanation. 17 of the 17 questions carry a written explanation.

For the concepts and case law behind these questions, read the Commercial Law notes. Once you are comfortable, test yourself under exam conditions with a full-length mock test.

17 practice questions with answers

1Under Section 183, any person may employ an agent who is:

  1. Of the age of majority and of sound mind
  2. A major only
  3. A resident of India only
  4. Of sound mind only
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Of the age of majority and of sound mind

Both majority and sound mind are needed.

Report this question

2Under Section 187, an authority is said to be implied when:

  1. It is given by words spoken or written
  2. It is given in a registered deed
  3. It is to be inferred from the circumstances of the case, and things spoken or written, or the ordinary course of dealings
  4. It is given by the court
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. It is to be inferred from the circumstances of the case, and things spoken or written, or the ordinary course of dealings

Express authority is the one given by words spoken or written.

Report this question

3Under Section 193, where an agent appoints a sub-agent without authority:

  1. The principal is bound by the sub-agent's acts
  2. The sub-agent is responsible to the principal
  3. The agent is not responsible
  4. The agent stands as principal to the sub-agent and is responsible for his acts to both the principal and third persons; the principal is not bound
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. The agent stands as principal to the sub-agent and is responsible for his acts to both the principal and third persons; the principal is not bound

The principal is not represented by such a sub-agent.

Report this question

4Under Section 194, a person named by an agent who had authority to name another to act for the principal is:

  1. An agent of the principal for that part of the business entrusted to him
  2. A sub-agent
  3. An agent of the agent
  4. A trustee
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. An agent of the principal for that part of the business entrusted to him

A 'substituted agent', not a sub-agent.

Report this question

5Under Section 198, no valid ratification can be made by a person whose knowledge of the facts of the case is:

  1. Full
  2. Second-hand
  3. Written
  4. Materially defective
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Materially defective

Ratification needs full knowledge of material facts.

Report this question

6Under Section 199, a person who ratifies an unauthorised act done on his behalf:

  1. Ratifies the whole of the transaction of which such act formed a part
  2. Ratifies only the profitable part
  3. Ratifies nothing
  4. May choose the portion to ratify
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Ratifies the whole of the transaction of which such act formed a part

Ratification cannot be selective.

Report this question

7Under Section 203, the principal may revoke the authority given to the agent:

  1. At any time before the authority has been exercised so as to bind the principal
  2. Only with the agent's consent
  3. Never
  4. Only through the court
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. At any time before the authority has been exercised so as to bind the principal

Section 202 is the exception for agency coupled with an interest.

Report this question

8Under Section 205, where an agency is for a fixed period and the principal revokes without sufficient cause:

  1. The agent must pay compensation to the principal
  2. The principal must pay compensation to the agent
  3. No compensation is payable
  4. The agency continues
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. The principal must pay compensation to the agent

The agent must likewise compensate the principal for renouncing without sufficient cause.

Report this question

9Under Section 209, when an agency is terminated by the principal's death or unsoundness of mind, the agent must:

  1. Abandon the business
  2. Keep all profits
  3. Hand the business to a competitor
  4. Take all steps reasonably practicable on behalf of the principal's representatives to protect and preserve the interests entrusted to him
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Take all steps reasonably practicable on behalf of the principal's representatives to protect and preserve the interests entrusted to him

Post-termination duty of care.

Report this question

10Under Section 211, if an agent does not conduct the principal's business according to the principal's directions or, absent directions, the custom of the business, and loss results:

  1. The agent must make compensation, and if profit accrues, he must account for it to the principal
  2. The principal bears the loss
  3. The loss is shared equally
  4. The agent is not liable
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. The agent must make compensation, and if profit accrues, he must account for it to the principal

Both limbs apply.

Report this question

11Under Section 212, an agent is bound to conduct the business with:

  1. The skill and diligence ordinarily possessed by persons carrying on such business, and to compensate the principal for direct consequences of his neglect or want of skill
  2. Extraordinary skill
  3. No special skill
  4. The skill of an expert
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. The skill and diligence ordinarily possessed by persons carrying on such business, and to compensate the principal for direct consequences of his neglect or want of skill

Standard is that of the ordinary person in that business.

Report this question

12In Kelner v. Baxter, promoters signed a contract on behalf of a company not yet incorporated. It was held that:

  1. The company was bound after incorporation by ratification
  2. The promoters were personally liable, as the company could not ratify a contract made before it existed
  3. The contract was valid against the company
  4. The contract was void for uncertainty
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. The promoters were personally liable, as the company could not ratify a contract made before it existed

Ratification requires a principal in existence at the time of the act.

Report this question

13Under Section 215, a principal may repudiate a transaction where an agent dealt on his own account without the principal's consent, if:

  1. A material fact was dishonestly concealed, or the agent's dealings were disadvantageous to the principal
  2. The agent earned a profit
  3. The agent is a relative
  4. The transaction was in writing
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. A material fact was dishonestly concealed, or the agent's dealings were disadvantageous to the principal

Section 216 gives the alternative remedy of claiming the benefit.

Report this question

14A del credere agent is one who:

  1. Works without commission
  2. Acts only as a broker
  3. Is a gratuitous agent
  4. For an extra commission, guarantees the performance of the contract by the third party
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. For an extra commission, guarantees the performance of the contract by the third party

Answerable to the principal if the buyer defaults.

Report this question

15Under Section 226, contracts entered into through an agent, and obligations arising from acts done by an agent:

  1. Bind only the agent
  2. Can be enforced, and have the same legal consequences, as if made or done by the principal in person
  3. Are always voidable
  4. Bind neither
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Can be enforced, and have the same legal consequences, as if made or done by the principal in person

Basic principle of agency.

Report this question

16Under Section 228, where an agent exceeds his authority and the part within authority cannot be separated from the part beyond it, the principal:

  1. Is bound by the whole
  2. Is not bound by the transaction
  3. Is bound by half
  4. Is bound only if the agent is solvent
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Is not bound by the transaction

Under s.227, where separable, the principal is bound only by the authorised part.

Report this question

17Under Section 234, a person who contracts with an agent and induces the agent to act on the belief that only the principal will be held liable:

  1. Can later hold the agent liable
  2. Can hold both liable
  3. Cannot afterwards hold the agent liable
  4. Can hold the agent liable only for half
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Cannot afterwards hold the agent liable

The same applies in reverse where he induces the principal to believe that only the agent will be liable.
