Quick answers
  • Questions: 75 compulsory MCQs, law only.
  • Marks: 300; +4 right, -1 wrong, 0 blank.
  • Time: 90 minutes in 2026 (72 seconds a question).
  • Mode and language: computer-based; English and Hindi.

CUET PG LLM looks simple on paper: 75 questions, one mark scheme. The strategy, though, is shaped by one number: a wrong answer costs a quarter of what a right answer earns. This guide answers the pattern questions in one place. The full exam context is in our CUET PG LLM guide.

How many questions are there in the CUET PG LLM paper?

The Law (LL.M.) paper has 75 compulsory multiple-choice questions, all on law subjects. There is no General Awareness, English or aptitude section. The test is computer-based and every question is available in English and Hindi.

Earlier cycles carried a General Test part and 100 questions. The paper was cut to 75 law questions, and the 2026 paper had no general awareness questions at all, according to candidate reports.

What is the marking scheme for CUET PG LLM?

Each correct answer earns 4 marks and each wrong answer costs 1 mark, for a maximum of 300. Unattempted questions score zero. The paper therefore rewards accuracy, and blind guessing is not free.

OutcomeMarks
Correct answer+4
Wrong answer-1
Unattempted0

Worked example. You attempt 60, get 48 right and 12 wrong: 48 x 4 - 12 = 180 out of 300. If you had attempted all 75 and got 55 right, your score would be 220 - 20 = 200. Try your own numbers in our score planner.

How long is the CUET PG LLM exam?

The 2026 LL.M. papers were 90 minutes, about 72 seconds per question. Some older articles quote 105 minutes from earlier cycles. NTA's 2027 Information Bulletin is the only authority on duration.

If you see two durations on different sites, the difference is usually a different year. Compare the year in the article with the paper you are preparing for.

Is there a General Awareness section in CUET PG LLM?

No. Since the paper moved to 75 questions, the LL.M. paper has been entirely on law. The 2026 paper had no general awareness questions. The three-year LL.B. paper (COQP11) is the one with general test sections.

Do not confuse the two. LL.B. (COQP11) tests language, general awareness, reasoning and quantitative skills; LL.M. (COQP14) tests law.

Is there negative marking in CUET PG LLM?

Yes. One mark is deducted for every wrong answer. Four wrong answers cancel one right answer. Unanswered questions carry no penalty, so a blank is better than a wild guess but worse than an informed one.

The expected value of a guess depends on how many options you can remove.

Options leftChance of being rightExpected marks per attempt
425%+0.25
333%+0.67
250%+1.50

How many questions should I attempt in CUET PG LLM?

Answer every question you can confirm or reduce to two options, and leave the rest. Break-even accuracy is 20 percent, but a 60 to 70 question attempt at high accuracy usually beats attempting all 75 with guesses.

A simple three-pass method: answer sure questions first, return to two-option questions, then leave pure guesses blank.

Is CUET PG LLM computer-based, and which languages are allowed?

It is a computer-based test at NTA-allotted centres, and each question is shown in English and Hindi. Reach the centre early, carry the admit card and photo ID, and practise reading on a screen: statement-based questions are easy to misread when you cannot underline.

Verify before you rely on it

Pattern details here follow the 2026 papers and reports. The 2027 Information Bulletin is not out yet. Confirm every detail on exams.nta.nic.in/cuet-pg.
