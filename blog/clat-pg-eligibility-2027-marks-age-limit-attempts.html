Quick answers
  • Eligibility: LLB (3-year or 5-year integrated) with 50% marks (45% for SC/ST).
  • Age limit: none. Attempts: no limit.
  • Final-year students can apply, and must show proof of completion at admission.
  • PG in CLAT means postgraduate: the entrance for LL.M. admission.

CLAT PG draws questions from every LLB graduate planning an LL.M., a research career or a public-sector legal job. Before you spend months preparing, you should know whether you qualify, how many times you can try and how PG differs from UG. This guide answers the most-searched questions directly, based on the notification as reported. The Consortium of NLUs is the final authority, so confirm the exact wording on consortiumofnlus.ac.in.

What is CLAT PG eligibility?

To apply for CLAT PG you need an LLB degree, either the 3-year course or the 5-year integrated course, from a recognised university, with a minimum percentage of marks. Here is the summary:

CriterionRequirement (as reported)
QualificationLLB (3-year) or 5-year integrated law degree, or an equivalent degree from a recognised institution
Minimum marks50% for General, OBC, EWS and PwD; 45% for SC and ST
Final-year studentsCan apply; must produce proof of completing the degree at the time of admission and counselling
Age limitNo upper age limit
Number of attemptsNo restriction
NationalityIndian nationals; NRI and other categories have extra document requirements
Documents can decide your seat

If your degree cannot be verified, admission can be cancelled even after you earn a CLAT PG rank. Keep your mark sheets, category certificate (if applicable), domicile proof and ID ready before counselling. Domicile seat rules differ by NLU.

What is PG in CLAT?

PG means postgraduate. The Common Law Admission Test has two papers, one for undergraduate admission and one for postgraduate admission. CLAT PG is the postgraduate paper: it is the entrance for the LL.M. (Master of Laws) programmes of the participating National Law Universities.

The test is 120 passage-based multiple choice questions in 2 hours with 0.25 negative marking. Besides the NLUs, some other institutions and public-sector recruiters also accept CLAT PG scores, each with their own cut-offs. For the exam pattern see our CLAT PG 2027 pattern and last-week plan, and see the NLU page for where the score is used.

Which is better, CLAT UG or PG?

Neither is better. They are not rivals, because they are taken at different points in a law career.

CLAT UGCLAT PG
Who takes itStudents after Class 12LLB graduates (and final-year LLB students)
What it leads toFive-year integrated law degree at NLUs (BA LLB, etc.)LL.M. at NLUs
Based onEnglish, current affairs, legal reasoning, logical reasoning, quantitative techniquesCore law subjects through passages
Eligibility focusClass 12 marksLLB marks

If you have not finished Class 12, UG is your route. If you hold or are completing an LLB, PG is the one that matters. You do not need to "choose" between them unless you are deciding whether to start a law degree or continue it with a master's.

How many attempts are allowed for CLAT PG?

There is no limit on the number of attempts according to the reports on the notification, and no upper age limit. If you do not get the NLU or rank you wanted, you can appear again in the next cycle. Many successful candidates attempt more than once and improve through mock analysis rather than more reading.

The most useful thing you can do after an attempt is build an error log: which questions you missed because you misread, did not know the law or fell for a trap. Our 5-step method for passage questions helps with the first and third.

What should you do next?

  1. Confirm your LLB percentage against the 50% or 45% requirement.
  2. Read the official notification for your year, especially for category and domicile rules.
  3. Learn the subject-wise syllabus and start timed practice on the practice arena and mock tests.
  4. Understand what scores mean in our guide to good CLAT PG scores.

Frequently asked questions

What is CLAT PG eligibility?

You need an LLB degree (3-year, or 5-year integrated) from a recognised university with at least 50 percent marks, or 45 percent for SC and ST candidates. Final-year students can apply and must show proof of completing the degree at admission and counselling. There is no upper age limit.

What is PG in CLAT?

PG stands for postgraduate. CLAT PG is the entrance test conducted by the Consortium of National Law Universities for admission to the LL.M. programmes of the participating NLUs, and its score is also used by some other institutions and public-sector recruiters.

Which is better, CLAT UG or PG?

Neither is better, because they serve different stages. CLAT UG is for admission to a five-year integrated law degree after Class 12. CLAT PG is for an LL.M. after you complete an LLB. You only choose between them if you are at the point of entering one or the other.

How many attempts for CLAT PG?

There is no limit on the number of attempts, according to the eligibility reports on the notification. You can appear every year as long as you meet the criteria.

Is there an age limit for CLAT PG?

No. There is no upper age limit for CLAT PG as reported from the notification.
