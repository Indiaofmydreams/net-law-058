Quick summary
  • CLAT PG 2027 is on Sunday, 6 December 2026, 2:00 pm to 4:00 pm.
  • 120 passage-based MCQs, 1 mark each, 0.25 negative marking, pen and paper (OMR).
  • That is about one minute per question, including reading the passage.
  • Registration has closed. Download your admit card from the Consortium website.

CLAT PG is the single entrance for the LLM programmes of the National Law Universities, and many other universities and public-sector recruiters also use its score. With the exam about two weeks away, the aim now is not new books. It is a clean pattern, a sound attempt strategy and steady revision. Here is the confirmed pattern and a plan you can start today.

Why the "one minute a question" rule matters

Because every question rests on a passage, reading time is the real cost. Two hours for 120 questions leaves no spare minutes for slow reading. In practice, a good attempt looks like this:

  • First pass (about 75 minutes): answer everything you can solve confidently. Skip anything that needs a long calculation of doctrine or a second reading.
  • Second pass (about 35 minutes): return to the skipped questions and eliminate options.
  • Last 10 minutes: bubble carefully and check that the OMR matches your paper. Do not leave this to the final minute.

The negative-marking maths: when to guess

A wrong answer costs 0.25 and a right answer earns 1. So a guess has a positive expected value when your chance of being right is above 20 percent.

Options you can rule outChance of a correct guessExpected marks per guess
None (blind guess)25%+0.06
One option33%+0.17
Two options50%+0.38

The lesson: do not guess blindly in bulk, but do guess when you can honestly remove at least one option. Random guessing on 30 questions gains almost nothing and adds risk.

Last-week and last-two-weeks plan

DaysFocus
21 to 24 NovDownload the admit card and check every detail. Take one full 120-question mock under a 2-hour timer and review every wrong or guessed answer.
25 to 28 NovRevise Constitutional Law, Jurisprudence and the new criminal laws (BNS, BNSS, BSA). Do 40 passage questions a day, timed.
29 Nov to 2 DecRevise Contract, Torts, Family, Property and Company Law. Read the landmark cases once more. One full mock every second day.
3 to 4 DecLight revision of your error log, case names and key principles. Fix your travel plan and documents.

Exam-day checklist

  • Printed admit card and the photo ID named on it.
  • Only the stationery and items allowed in your admit card instructions.
  • Reach early, and note the route the day before.
  • Fill the OMR sheet carefully, one bubble per question, as the instructions state.
  • Do not discuss answers with others before the paper ends.

Frequently asked questions

When is CLAT PG 2027?

CLAT PG 2027 is scheduled for Sunday, 6 December 2026, from 2:00 pm to 4:00 pm, as reported from the Consortium of NLUs notification. Confirm your centre, reporting time and instructions on your admit card.

How many questions are there in CLAT PG and is there negative marking?

The paper has 120 passage-based multiple choice questions of 1 mark each, with 0.25 marks deducted for each wrong answer and no deduction for unattempted questions.

Is CLAT PG online or offline?

It is a pen-and-paper test on an OMR sheet at the allotted centre, according to the notification as reported.

When will the CLAT PG 2027 result come?

We have not seen an official date for the answer key or result, so we do not state one. Watch consortiumofnlus.ac.in.

Can I still register for CLAT PG 2027?

Registration has closed. Reports put the last date at 7 November 2026 after an extension. If you registered, the next step is the admit card.
