Quick answers
  • Main names: DU, BHU, BBAU and several central universities.
  • Not CUET PG: NLUs (CLAT PG), NLU Delhi (AILET), Jamia (own test, as reported).
  • Counselling: by each university, not NTA.
  • Scale: about 54 universities offered LL.M. among roughly 142 participants in 2026 (reported).

Choosing where to apply matters as much as the score. CUET PG LL.M. admissions are spread over many universities, each with its own seats, eligibility and dates. This guide answers the questions about participation and process. Use our searchable universities list to shortlist, and confirm each entry on the university's own page, because participation changes every cycle.

Which universities accept CUET PG LLM scores?

Participating universities include the University of Delhi, Banaras Hindu University, Babasaheb Bhimrao Ambedkar University, central universities such as South Bihar, Haryana, Punjab and Kashmir, Tripura University, Pondicherry University and several private universities. The list changes every year.

Does Delhi University admit to LL.M. through CUET PG?

Yes. Delhi University admits to its LL.M. on the CUET PG score through its own CSAS PG portal, with seats across its law centres. Apply on DU's portal after the result; NTA does not allot seats.

DU's Faculty of Law runs the Campus Law Centre and Law Centre-I and II. Seat numbers, eligibility and category-wise cut-offs change each year, so read DU's current bulletin.

Does BHU admit to LL.M. through CUET PG?

Yes. BHU's Faculty of Law has accepted CUET PG scores for LL.M. and runs its own application portal and rounds after the result. It is one of the most sought-after central university options, so its cut-off is usually higher than at newer central universities.

Do NLUs and NLU Delhi accept CUET PG for LL.M.?

No. National Law Universities admit to LL.M. through CLAT PG, and NLU Delhi through AILET. A CUET PG score does not substitute for either, so aim at NLUs only if you also prepare for those exams.

Jamia Millia Islamia has been reported to use its own entrance test for LL.M. Read our guides on CLAT PG and AILET.

Is there centralised counselling for CUET PG LLM?

No. NTA publishes the scorecard and each university runs its own application, merit list and counselling. You must apply separately on every university's portal and track their dates.

Make a calendar with every university's form, document upload and fee dates the week the result is declared. Missing one portal is the commonest way to lose a seat.

How many universities should I apply to?

Apply to a mix of five to eight: two or three reach options (DU, BHU), two or three realistic central or state options and one or two safer private ones. LL.M. intake per university is small, so a single application is risky.

Verify before you rely on it

Participation of any university in a given year is decided by that university and listed by NTA. The 2027 Information Bulletin is not out yet. Confirm every detail on exams.nta.nic.in/cuet-pg.
