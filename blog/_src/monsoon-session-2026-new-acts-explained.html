Every year a few Acts show up in exam questions, and the Monsoon Session is usually the richest source. This guide lists the 2026 Acts in one place, explains what each changes in plain words and tells you where to read more. Dates are the reported dates of assent, so check the Gazette for commencement.

Which Acts were passed in the Monsoon Session 2026?

Reports list eleven Acts that received assent between 31 July and 17 August 2026, including amendments on public examinations, tribunals, birth and death registration, banking evidence, MSMEs, mineral law and taxation.

What do the constitutional and public law Acts do?

The Supreme Court (Number of Judges) Amendment Act raises the Court's sanctioned strength, the Kerala (Alteration of Name) Act renames the State, and the National Honour Act amends Section 3 on insults to national symbols.

The Judges Act received assent on 11 August, is deemed in force from 16 May 2026 and repeals the ordinance that first raised the strength. The Kerala Act works under Article 3 and the First Schedule, because a change of name needs Parliament's law; see our post on other 2026 legislative changes for context. The Tribunals Reforms Act deals with the structure and service conditions of tribunals, a subject on which the Supreme Court has repeatedly set limits.

What do the criminal law and governance Acts do?

The Public Examinations Amendment Act raises penalties and creates Special Task Forces and Special Fast Track Courts. The Births and Deaths Amendment Act sets stricter rules for late registration, with magistrate orders after one and two years.

The exam law sets a target of about two months for investigation and three months from chargesheet for trial. It builds on the 2024 Public Examinations Act and responds to repeated paper leaks. Because registration of births and deaths underpins identity records, voter lists and inheritance, the tighter rules on delay are important for citizens as well as students.

What do the commercial and tax Acts do?

The Bankers' Books Evidence Act updates how banking records are proved in court, the MSME Act tightens payment disputes, the MMDR Act limits State mineral taxes, the NCDC Act amends the cooperative finance body, and the Taxation Act amends three tax and payments laws.

The Taxation and Other Laws (Amendment) Act amends the Payment and Settlement Systems Act, 2007, the Income-tax Act, 2025 and the Finance Act, 2026. For details of two of these, read our guides to the MSMED Amendment and the MMDR Amendment. Together they show a theme: faster enforcement and clearer Central control over national economic rules.

Which Bills are still pending?

The FCRA Amendment Bill and the Viksit Bharat Shiksha Adhishthan Bill were sent to Joint Parliamentary Committees, and the Indian Statistical Institute Bill, 2026 was introduced on 24 July 2026 and is pending.

A Bill is not law until both Houses pass it and the President gives assent. A common exam trap is to call a Bill an Act. A Joint Committee examines the Bill and reports, after which Parliament may take it up in a later session. The status can change, so check the Parliament website for the latest.

How can this be asked in UGC NET Law, CLAT PG or AIBE?

Expect questions on the process of making a law, Article 3, tribunals, ordinances and recent Acts in current-affairs sections.
