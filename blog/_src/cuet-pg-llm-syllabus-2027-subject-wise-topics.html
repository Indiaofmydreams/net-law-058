Quick answers
  • Paper: Law (LL.M.), code COQP14, 75 questions.
  • Subjects: about 30 areas of the LL.B. curriculum.
  • Core: Constitution, Criminal Law, Contract, Torts, Jurisprudence, Hindu Law.
  • No weightage is published by NTA; tiers below are editorial.

The CUET PG LLM syllabus is wide rather than deep: it follows the LL.B. curriculum, so any law graduate has seen most of it. The task is organising 30-plus subjects into a plan. This guide groups the questions students ask most and sorts the subjects into core, regular and occasional. Our syllabus page breaks every subject into topics with a tick-off tracker.

What is the syllabus of CUET PG LLM?

The Law (LL.M.) paper covers about 30 law subjects from the LL.B. curriculum, led by Constitutional Law, Contract, Torts, Criminal Law, Jurisprudence and Family Law, and extending to Property, Company, Administrative, Environmental, International, Evidence, CPC, IPR and Taxation.

Which subjects are most important for CUET PG LLM?

Constitutional Law is consistently the largest block, followed by Criminal Law (substantive and procedure), Contract, Torts, Jurisprudence and Hindu Law. These core areas supply roughly half the paper in editorial estimates. NTA publishes no official weightage.

What should I study in the Constitution of India for CUET PG LLM?

Study fundamental rights (Arts 12 to 35), Directive Principles, the executive, Parliament, the judiciary, federalism, amendments and the Basic Structure doctrine, emergency provisions, and the landmark cases for each. Expect direct questions on Article numbers, amendment numbers and case names.

  • Art 14 to 18 and reservation cases (Indra Sawhney, Davinder Singh 2024).
  • Art 19 to 22, and Art 21's expansion (Maneka Gandhi, Puttaswamy).
  • Writs, Art 32 and Art 226; PIL.
  • Art 368 and Kesavananda Bharati; Art 352 and 356; S.R. Bommai.

Are the BNS, BNSS and BSA in the CUET PG LLM syllabus?

Recent papers still used IPC, CrPC and Evidence Act terms, but the three new codes replaced them from 1 July 2024. Learn the key sections of both sets so you can answer a question in either numbering.

Are Hindu law and Muslim law both in the CUET PG LLM syllabus?

Yes. Family law appears as Hindu Law (marriage, divorce, adoption, succession, joint family) and Muslim Law (marriage, mehr, divorce, inheritance, wills, wakf), with the Indian Succession Act. Leading cases such as Shayara Bano, Shah Bano, Vineeta Sharma and Shilpa Sailesh come up often.

Is there a General Awareness or current affairs section in CUET PG LLM?

No. The LL.M. paper is entirely on law. Current developments appear only as law questions, for example a new Act or a landmark judgment, so a short last-month revision is enough.

Track the Labour Codes, the Mediation Act 2023, the DPDP Act 2023, the new criminal codes and the Income-tax Act 2025, along with recent Supreme Court constitution-bench rulings.

How should I study 30 subjects without burning out?

Aim for 90 percent in the core subjects, 70 percent in the regular ones and the basics in the occasional ones. Use a tracker, take one subject-wise test a week, and keep a one-page section and case sheet per subject for the final fortnight.
