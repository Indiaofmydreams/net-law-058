Quick answer
  • Choose one all-unit guide: commonly cited are Trueman's, KVS Madaan (Pearson) and Arihant.
  • For fast-changing units use free official sources: the NEP 2020 document, NCERT for environment and computer basics.
  • Add R.S. Aggarwal if reasoning is weak.
  • Spend the rest of your time on previous year papers, not a second guide.

Paper 1 is a breadth exam, so the best book is the one you finish, revise and test yourself against. This list reflects titles that preparation guides commonly recommend. It is not a ranking or an endorsement, and editions change, so check the edition year before you buy.

Which is the best book for UGC NET Paper 1?

Three all-unit guides come up most often in preparation guides: Trueman's UGC NET Paper 1, KVS Madaan's NTA UGC NET Paper 1 (Pearson) and Arihant's UGC NET Paper 1 guide, the last described as covering all ten units. They differ in layout and depth, so look inside each in a shop or sample and choose the one whose explanations you can follow. Buying two guides rarely helps.

What about previous year compilations?

A printed compilation of solved papers, commonly from publishers such as Arihant and Kiran Prakashan, is useful if you like to write and mark answers. The real papers are free on the PYQ page. Whichever you choose, read our analysis of four papers so you know what to look for.

How to avoid overbuying

  1. Pick one guide and set a finish date.
  2. Use free official sources for NEP, ICT and environment.
  3. Spend the saved money and time on timed practice. The 30-day plan shows how to schedule it.
