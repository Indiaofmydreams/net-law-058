The RBI Legal Officer post is among the most prestigious regulator jobs for law graduates. It is not tied to CLAT PG, but many LLM aspirants target it as a second route. Here is what is reported.

What is the RBI Grade B Legal Officer post?

It is an officer entry in the Reserve Bank of India's Legal stream (Grade B, direct recruitment), recruited through RBI's own exam, as seen in the 2026 non-CSG notice reported. The Legal Officer handles legal advice, regulations and litigation for the RBI.

Work includes drafting regulations, advising departments, representing the Bank in courts and handling matters under banking and payment laws.

What is the eligibility?

A law degree (commonly LLB) with minimum marks as per the notice, and an age limit with relaxation, as reported. Because eligibility and vacancies change every year, check the RBI notification for the cut-off date and marks.

Experience is usually not needed at this entry level.

What is the exam pattern?

Usually Phase I (objective: general awareness, English, quantitative aptitude, reasoning), Phase II (descriptive paper in the stream's subject, with law-related papers) and an interview. Check the notice for current format and weightage.

What does the post pay?

Grade B officers receive a basic pay in the pay scale reported with annual increments and allowances. Gross monthly emoluments are often reported near ₹1.5 lakh or more depending on city. Treat this as approximate and verify in the notice.

Benefits include housing or allowance, medical cover and a retirement scheme as per rules.

How should I prepare?

Build general awareness and banking basics along with law subjects such as banking, contract, company and constitutional law. Practise descriptive answers and keep up with RBI circulars and recent regulatory changes.

Study the commercial law unit and read about the IBC amendments. Other regulators are in our SEBI and NABARD guide.
