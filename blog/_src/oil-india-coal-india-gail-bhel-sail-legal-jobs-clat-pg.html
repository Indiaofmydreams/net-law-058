The big four names get the most attention, but many other central PSUs hire law graduates occasionally. This guide covers the second line of companies and how to approach them.

Which other PSUs have used CLAT PG for legal posts?

Besides POWERGRID, NTPC, IOCL and ONGC, reports list Oil India, Coal India, GAIL, BHEL, SAIL and some other central PSUs in various cycles. Participation changes each year, so confirm in the CLAT Consortium's list and each company's notice.

The main PSU guide explains why the list moves.

What posts and scales do they offer?

Most offer an executive entry such as Law Officer, Manager (Law) or Assistant Legal Adviser at a scale equal to E-1 or E-2 or its company equivalent, with pay in a range of roughly ₹40,000 to ₹1,60,000 basic. Treat it as approximate.

What kind of work do they do?

Coal and mining PSUs handle land acquisition, mining leases, environment and labour issues; gas companies handle pipelines, contracts and regulation; engineering PSUs handle large supply contracts, arbitration and service law.

Postings can be at remote mines or plants, which suits those open to a field career but may not suit others.

How can I track these notices?

Check each company's careers page, the CLAT PG website's PSU section, and employment news. Set a reminder for December to March and keep documents scanned and ready in the required format.

Fake job notices circulate on social media. Apply only through the company's official portal.

Should I prepare differently for each PSU?

Core preparation is the same: contract, arbitration, labour, service and constitutional law. Add a day of reading on the company's business and sector law before the interview.
