Quick answers
  • CLAT PG is out of 120 marks: +1 for a right answer, -0.25 for a wrong one.
  • 60 = 50%, 77 = 64%, 40 = 33%.
  • There is no pass mark. What matters is your rank and your target NLU.
  • About 9 to 10 candidates appear for each LL.M. seat, but far more compete for the top NLUs.

Few questions get more searches after a mock test than "is my score good?". The honest answer is that a CLAT PG score only means something as a rank, and ranks change with the paper's difficulty. This guide explains what 60, 77 and 40 mean, how negative marking affects them, and what to use instead of guessing.

How CLAT PG scoring works

The paper has 120 questions, 1 mark for each correct answer and a 0.25 deduction for each wrong one. So the same score can come from very different attempts:

ScorePercent of 120Example attempts
6050%60 right, 0 wrong; or 64 right, 16 wrong; or 72 right, 48 wrong
7764%77 right, 0 wrong; or 80 right, 12 wrong; or 85 right, 32 wrong
4033%40 right, 0 wrong; or 44 right, 16 wrong; or 50 right, 40 wrong

Notice that the third example for 60 needs 72 correct answers, 12 more than the first. Accuracy matters as much as attempts. Read our negative-marking maths to see when a guess pays.

Is 60 a good score in CLAT PG?

60 out of 120 is exactly half the marks. Whether it is "good" depends on three things you can check:

  • The NLU you want. For the most sought-after NLUs, Careers360's compilation of CLAT 2026 round-one general-category closing ranks puts them roughly between rank 87 and rank 450. A score of 60 would need to land inside the rank for the university you target, so look up the official allotment lists.
  • Your category. General, OBC, EWS, SC, ST and PwD each have different closing ranks.
  • The paper's difficulty. A hard paper lowers every score and moves ranks, so 60 can be strong one year and average the next.

Coaching sites quote different "good score" benchmarks, from about 70 to 90, which is itself a sign that no single number is reliable. Convert your mock scores to a rank estimate and compare with the target NLU's closing rank instead.

Is 77 a good score in CLAT?

77 out of 120 is about 64 percent, which is a strong result in most CLAT papers. Note two things before you celebrate or worry:

  • UG and PG are different papers. The two papers have separate rank lists and separate counselling, so a score from one cannot be read against the other's cut-offs.
  • Rank, not score, decides the seat. If the paper was easy, many candidates will score 77 or more.

Check the allotment lists on the Consortium website for the paper and year you mean.

Is 40 marks good in CLAT?

40 out of 120 is about 33 percent. For NLU admission it is unlikely to be competitive: it is a third of the marks and well below the 70-plus benchmarks that preparation sites quote, and we have no verified allotment data showing seats at this level. But a CLAT PG score is also used by some other institutions and by public-sector recruiters, each with their own cut-offs, so a lower score is not worthless. If you are around 40 in mocks, the fastest gains come from accuracy and passage speed, not new content.

Is CLAT PG easy to crack?

The paper is predictable, because it tests core LLB subjects through passages, so with a structured plan it is crackable. But it is not easy, because of competition. The Consortium reported 17,335 applicants for the PG programme in the CLAT 2026 cycle, and about 92 percent sat the exam, roughly 16,000 candidates. Published coverage puts LL.M. seats across the NLUs at around 1,500 to 1,750.

Is CLAT tougher than IIT?

There is no official way to rank two different exams, and the claim usually comes from comparing the pressure each puts on candidates. A fairer view:

CLAT PGIIT entrance (JEE)
Main skill testedReading speed, legal reasoning and recallMathematics, physics and chemistry problem solving
Time pressureAbout one minute per question including the passageLong, multi-step problems
Where the difficulty liesPassages, close options, negative marking, few seats at top NLUsDepth of concepts and calculation

Which is "tougher" depends on your strengths. A strong reader with a law background may find CLAT PG natural, while a candidate weak in law will find it demanding. Choose by your goal, not by which sounds harder.

How to find out if your score is good

  1. Take timed mock tests and note your score and accuracy.
  2. Pick three to five target NLUs from the NLU page.
  3. From the official allotment lists, note each NLU's closing rank for your category for the last three years.
  4. Aim above the worst closing rank you find, with backups, and train toward that score.
