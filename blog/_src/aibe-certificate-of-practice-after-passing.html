Clearing AIBE is only half of the process. The Certificate of Practice (COP) is what lets an enrolled advocate appear in courts, and the steps to get it are not obvious from the result alone. This guide pulls together what is reported about who gets it and how it reaches you. The Bar Council of India and your State Bar Council are the final authority, so treat the details as a guide and confirm them.

What is the AIBE Certificate of Practice?

The COP is issued by the Bar Council of India to advocates who clear the AIBE. It carries details such as your name, enrolment number and enrolment date. Reports say an advocate needs it to practise in Indian courts and tribunals, and that without it you are treated as non-practising. The requirement applies to law graduates from 2009-10 onward. Some categories, such as advocates already on the non-practising list, advocates-on-record and senior advocates, are reported to be outside the rule.

Who gets the Certificate of Practice?

  • Candidates who score at least the passing mark: 45 percent for General and OBC, 40 percent for SC, ST and PwD, as reported. See the pattern guide.
  • Candidates whose State Bar Council enrolment certificate was uploaded at registration, as reported.
  • Not everyone must take the exam: some advocates are exempt under the Bar Council's rules.

How is the certificate issued and collected?

  1. Result. After the result, the BCI processes qualified candidates.
  2. Dispatch. The BCI issues the certificate and sends it to State Bar Councils. Successful candidates are reported to be informed by registered post.
  3. Collection. You collect the physical certificate from your State Bar Council.
  4. Online copy. An online version can be viewed through the AIBE portal's COP service, and details can be corrected until a notified deadline.

Is the online COP valid?

Reports quote the BCI as clarifying that the online copy holds no validity and exists to let you check and correct your details. It is non-printable and non-downloadable as reported. Use it to catch errors in your name or enrolment number early, because a wrong detail on the certificate can cause problems later.

Does it expire, and what if you fail?

We could not find a stated validity period or renewal rule, so ask your State Bar Council and check the BCI rules. If you do not pass, you can generally try again in later sittings, and a guide reports that provisional enrolment is valid for about two years, so check the time window that applies to you. See eligibility and registration for the application side, and sharpen your next attempt with the syllabus guide.

What should you do after the result?

  1. Check your result and score on the official AIBE portal.
  2. Verify your details in the online COP view and correct errors before the deadline.
  3. Contact your State Bar Council about collection and any documents it asks for.
  4. Keep your enrolment papers safe.
