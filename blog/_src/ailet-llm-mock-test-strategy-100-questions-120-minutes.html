Quick answers

  • Skip rule: attempt only if two options can be removed for a legal reason.

AILET LL.M. is a 100-question paper with 0.25 negative marking and about a minute and twelve seconds per question. It rewards a clear plan: what to attempt first, when to skip and where your clock should be at each checkpoint. This guide gives a two-pass method, a branch-wise attempt order, checkpoints and a review routine for your mocks.

How should I attempt a 100-question AILET LL.M. paper in 120 minutes?

Use two passes: in about 60 to 65 minutes, answer every question you can do in under a minute; in the next 35 to 40 minutes, return to the longer ones and the two-option cases; keep 10 to 15 minutes to check the OMR. That gives about 72 seconds per question on average.

AILET LL.M. is 100 multiple-choice questions in 120 minutes with 0.25 negative marking, written on 13 December 2026 from 2 pm to 4 pm in pen and paper mode, as set out in our AILET exam guide. The questions are standalone, so unlike passage exams you can attempt them in any order and skip freely.

What is the negative marking rule in AILET and when should I skip?

AILET deducts 0.25 for each wrong answer, so the break-even accuracy is 20 percent. Skip a question when you cannot remove at least two options for a specific legal reason. A two-option guess is worth about 0.38 marks on average; a blind guess barely 0.06, and swings widely.

The arithmetic is in our negative marking guide. In a 100-mark paper each wrong answer you avoid saves 0.25 and each one you turn into a right answer gains 1.25 marks, so a careful skip is never a loss. Use your mocks to count how many of your wrong answers came from blind guesses; if it is more than a third, tighten your rule.

Which AILET branches should I attempt first in a mock?

Start with the branches you are strongest in, usually Constitutional Law, Criminal Law and Contract, because these questions are fast and give early momentum. Leave the branches you are least sure about, such as International Law or Labour Law, for the second pass. Since the paper is not sectioned, the order is entirely yours.

What pacing checkpoints should I use in an AILET mock?

Aim for 25 questions by minute 30, 50 by minute 60, 75 by minute 90 and all 100 attempted or marked by minute 110, leaving ten minutes to check. If you fall behind at a checkpoint, skip the longest question types in the next block rather than rushing everything.

Write these four lines on your rough sheet at the start. They are starting points: after two mocks, adjust them by looking at where you most often fall behind.

How do I review an AILET mock test?

Log every wrong answer with its branch, its cause and the time you spent. Tag each as knowledge gap, misread, elimination failure or time pressure, then redo the questions after seven days. Track your attempts, accuracy and the number of blind guesses across mocks, not just the score.

Common AILET mock mistakes

  • Treating AILET like CLAT PG. There are no passages, so reading time is smaller; be quicker on direct questions.
  • Over-attempting early. A 70-question accurate attempt often beats a 95-question hurried one.
  • Ignoring the last ten minutes. OMR errors in the final shading cost marks that no revision can recover.
