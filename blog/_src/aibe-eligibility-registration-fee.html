The All India Bar Examination decides whether a law graduate can practise in court, so eligibility mistakes can cost you a cycle. The details below are as reported by a preparation guide and a news site, and the Bar Council of India's notice for each cycle is the final authority. Read it on the official AIBE website before you pay.

How do you register for AIBE?

  1. Register on the official AIBE website during the open window.
  2. Fill in personal, education and enrolment details.
  3. Upload a photo, signature, State Bar Council enrolment certificate, and category and identity proof.
  4. Pay the fee online.
  5. Choose your exam language and city.
  6. Download the admit card before the exam.

Keep scanned documents ready in the sizes the portal asks for. A mismatch between the name on your enrolment certificate and your ID is a common cause of trouble, so correct it before you apply.

What is the AIBE application fee?

One guide reports about 3,560 rupees for General and OBC candidates and about 2,560 rupees for SC and ST candidates, and gives no figure for candidates with disabilities. Amounts can vary by edition, and payment gateway charges can apply, so treat these as indicative and check the portal.

Is there a limit on attempts?

The sources we checked do not state a limit. A guide reports that provisional enrolment is valid for about two years, and advises clearing the exam within that period. Confirm the validity with your State Bar Council.
