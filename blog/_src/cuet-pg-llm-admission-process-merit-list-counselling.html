Many students think CUET PG ends with the result. It actually begins a second process: separate applications, merit lists and counselling at each university. This guide answers the questions about that stage so that you know what to prepare while you wait for the 2027 result. Shortlist options early with our universities list.

What happens after the CUET PG result?

NTA publishes the scorecard, and then each participating university announces its own application, merit list and counselling. NTA does not allot seats. Apply on each university's portal, usually within weeks of the result, which came on 24 April 2026 last cycle.

How is the CUET PG LLM merit list prepared?

Each university ranks applicants by CUET PG score, applies its reservation policy and eligibility criteria, and publishes its own merit list. Some add weightage or a second stage, so read each university's admission notice. There is no common national merit list.

Tie-breakers vary: some use the LL.B. percentage, some use age. Read the prospectus for the exact order.

What is a good score in CUET PG LLM?

There is no single figure, because cut-offs depend on the university, the category and the number of seats. As a working rule, a score above about 70 percent of 300 keeps most options open, but check each university's previous merit lists.

Published cut-offs you may see for BHU and DU are usually for the LL.B. programme, which uses a different paper. Do not use LL.B. cut-offs to judge an LL.M. score. Ask for the LL.M. closing rank of the previous year from the university's notice board or website.

Is there a separate counselling for CUET PG LLM?

Not at national level. Counselling, seat acceptance and fee payment are done on each university's portal, such as DU's CSAS PG portal or BHU's own portal. Multiple universities can offer you seats, so manage deadlines carefully.

Which documents do I need for CUET PG LLM admission?

Keep the scorecard, LL.B. mark-sheets and degree or provisional certificate, class 10 and 12 certificates, category or EWS or PwBD certificate if claimed, photo ID and recent photographs. Prepare scans in advance; many portals have narrow upload windows.

Can I get admission in more than one university?

You can apply and be offered seats in several, but you can finally join only one, and some universities have non-refundable fee rules. Check withdrawal and refund rules before paying any seat acceptance fee.

Verify before you rely on it

Processes differ between universities and change yearly. The 2027 Information Bulletin is not out yet. Confirm every detail on exams.nta.nic.in/cuet-pg.
