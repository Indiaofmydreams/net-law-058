ONGC, India's largest oil and gas producer, has long been a dream PSU for law students. Recruitment is irregular, but when it comes, the pay scale and the work are attractive. Here is what has been reported.

Does ONGC recruit law officers through CLAT PG?

ONGC has recruited Assistant Legal Advisers and law executives through CLAT PG scores in earlier cycles, as reported. Whether it does so now depends on the current notice, since recruitment has not been regular every year.

Follow the ONGC careers page and the CLAT PG announcements after the result.

What is the pay scale?

Law executives at ONGC have historically been placed in the E-1 scale, around ₹60,000 to ₹1,80,000 basic as reported, plus allowances and perks. Treat this as approximate and verify in the latest notice.

What are the eligibility criteria?

A law degree with a minimum percentage (commonly 60%), an age limit with category relaxation and, in CLAT PG cycles, a valid score. Check experience requirements, as some posts ask for post-qualification experience.

Lateral posts for experienced lawyers may use a different process and a higher entry level.

What does an ONGC legal adviser do?

Works on exploration and production contracts, joint ventures, arbitration, land and environment clearances, labour and service matters, and advises business units. Offshore and project work can bring international contract questions.

Read about production sharing contracts and the Petroleum and Natural Gas framework.

How do I improve my chances?

Build strong contract, arbitration and energy law basics, learn how production sharing contracts work and prepare a clear interview story. Strong writing and drafting skills help in the legal department.
