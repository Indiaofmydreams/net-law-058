Regulators are a top aim for LLM graduates who want a mix of law and finance. These jobs rarely open, but pay and work quality are strong. Here is a guide to SEBI, NABARD and similar regulators.

What is the SEBI Grade A Legal stream?

SEBI recruits Officer Grade A (Assistant Manager) in a Legal stream through a multi-phase exam. Legal officers work on securities regulation, enforcement, adjudication and drafting. Notices are published in some years, so check SEBI's careers page.

Our RBI guide covers a similar route.

What does SEBI pay?

Reports list a starting basic of ₹62,500 in the Grade A scale, with a gross reported at roughly ₹1.4 to ₹1.8 lakh a month, depending on housing and city. These figures are from aggregator pages, so confirm in the SEBI notice.

What about NABARD?

NABARD recruits Grade A posts, including a Legal stream, through a prelims, mains and interview process, based on advertisements seen in recent years. Eligibility and pay are in the notice, so verify them there.

NABARD work links to rural credit and development finance law.

What about IRDAI and other regulators?

IRDAI, PFRDA and similar regulators have recruited legal officers in some cycles, usually through their own exams and interviews. Check each regulator's notice.

Keep an alert on regulator websites, since notices are short-lived.

How should I prepare?

Study securities law, banking law, insurance law, company law and contract law at a working level, plus general awareness, English and quantitative basics. Read recent regulator orders and amendments.

Start with Unit 6 and the IBC post.

  • Link it to: SEBI Act, Insurance Act, Banking Regulation Act and company law.
