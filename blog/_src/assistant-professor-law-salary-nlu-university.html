Teaching salaries are less discussed than law firm packages, which makes it hard to plan. This guide explains how pay is structured and what to ask in each type of institution.

What does a government law assistant professor earn?

Entry basic pay under the pay matrix has been reported around ₹57,700 per month at the entry level, with DA, HRA and other allowances raising the gross. Exact pay depends on the institution and city, so check the notice.

How do NLUs and central universities pay?

Central universities follow the UGC pay matrix. NLUs set pay by their own rules, often close to the matrix with some additions. Reports of total pay vary, so check each NLU's advertisement for the scale and benefits.

NLU roles may also require publications and teaching experience.

What do private universities pay?

Private universities vary widely, from modest salaries to competitive ones at well-funded institutions. Offers often depend on qualifications, publications and experience, and pay may be a consolidated amount without government benefits.

Ask about workload, research support and contract terms in writing.

How does pay grow in teaching?

Promotion follows stages: assistant professor, associate professor and professor, based on years of service and academic performance. Pay rises with level, and research grants, consultancy and administrative roles add income.

See the NET and PhD route.

Is teaching worth it financially?

It is stable and respected but usually pays less than top firms at the start. Many choose it for research, flexibility and impact. Compare the whole package including leave, pension rules and research support.
