Public sector banks are among the larger recruiters of law officers. The jobs are stable and spread across the country. Here is how the route works and what it pays.

What is the IBPS SO Law Officer post?

IBPS Specialist Officer recruitment includes Law Officer in Scale I for participating public sector banks, filled through a prelims, mains and interview, as seen in recent cycles. The post covers recovery law, advice and litigation.

SBI runs its own Specialist Cadre Officer recruitment for legal posts from time to time.

What is the eligibility?

A law degree, with a minimum percentage as per the notice, and an age limit with relaxation, as reported. Some banks ask for enrolment as an advocate. Check the current IBPS notice for the exact rule.

LLM candidates qualify but get no special weight in most cycles.

What is the pay?

Scale I officer pay starts at a basic reported near ₹48,000 plus DA, HRA and other allowances, giving gross pay often reported in the ₹80,000 to ₹1 lakh range. These are estimates, so confirm in the notice.

What does a bank law officer do?

Advises on loan documents, recovery under the SARFAESI Act and the IBC, handles litigation and tribunal matters (DRT, NCLT), and supports compliance. Work is regional, often at a zonal or regional office.

How should I prepare?

Revise banking law, negotiable instruments, SARFAESI, IBC, contract and property law, along with English, reasoning and quantitative aptitude for the prelims. Read about recent banking regulations.

Also see RBI Grade B for a higher-level regulator route.

How can this be asked in UGC NET Law, CLAT PG or AIBE?

  • Remember: Bank SO; SARFAESI; IBC; DRT.
  • Link it to: SARFAESI Act, IBC, Negotiable Instruments Act and DRT procedure.
  • Practise: Unit 6 and previous year questions.
