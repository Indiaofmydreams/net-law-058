NTPC is India's largest power generator and employs law officers across plants, regions and head office. Law recruitment is infrequent, which is why each notice draws a large pool. This guide sets out what has been reported and how to prepare.

Does NTPC recruit law graduates through CLAT PG?

NTPC has advertised law posts, including an Assistant Law Officer notice in January 2026 as reported, and has used CLAT PG scores in some cycles. Whether the route is CLAT PG or its own test differs by year, so read the notice.

Do not assume last year's process applies. Some PSUs switch between CLAT PG and their own exam. Follow the PSU list guide.

What is the pay scale for NTPC law officers?

Entry executive scales at NTPC have been reported in the E-1 or E-2 band, roughly ₹40,000 to ₹1,40,000 basic, with allowances, perks and performance pay. Treat this as approximate and confirm the scale in the current notice.

What is the eligibility?

A law degree with a minimum percentage (commonly 60% for General), a valid score or test result as the notice requires, and an age limit with category relaxation. Read the degree date and percentage rules closely.

Reserved categories get percentage and age relaxation as per rules. A candidate with an LLM may not get extra marks unless the notice says so.

What work does an NTPC law officer do?

Contracts for fuel supply and construction, arbitration, power purchase agreements, land and compensation, labour and service disputes, vigilance and RTI. Work is often at a plant, project site or regional office.

Early years are heavy on drafting and follow-up of cases in tribunals and High Courts.

How should I prepare?

Revise contract, arbitration, electricity and labour law, read about NTPC's business and practise a short interview answer on why you choose a PSU. Keep your degree, category and age documents ready.
