The PSU route looks simple on paper: score well in CLAT PG, then clear an interview. In practice each company runs its own process, and small details in the notice decide who is shortlisted. This guide explains the usual stages and how to prepare for each.

What are the stages of PSU recruitment through CLAT PG?

Typically five stages: online registration with the PSU, shortlisting on CLAT PG score, group discussion or group task, personal interview, then document verification and medical test. Some PSUs skip the group stage and use only the interview.

StageWhat to do
RegistrationApply on the PSU portal with CLAT PG roll number and documents
ShortlistingScore-based; cut-offs depend on vacancies and category
GD or taskPractise topics on law, economy and public sector
InterviewPrepare your LLM subject and the company profile
Documents and medicalKeep originals, degree marks, category and age proof

How much weight does the CLAT PG score carry?

It varies. Some PSUs shortlist purely by score and then weigh interview and group stages; others convert the score into a fixed share of the final merit. The advertisement states the weightage, so read it before you apply.

Because CLAT PG has its own cut-offs for NLUs, a candidate with an average score for an NLU seat may still be shortlisted by a PSU with few applicants. Do not self-reject. Read our guide on CLAT PG scores for what a score means.

What is asked in the group discussion and interview?

Expect current legal and business topics, your LLM subject, contract and arbitration basics, the company's work and your reasons for choosing a PSU. Interviews test clarity and attitude more than long recitation of cases.

Revise the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, the Indian Contract Act, the Industrial Disputes framework, now the labour codes, and the basics of land acquisition and environmental clearances. A PSU law officer handles contracts, vigilance, service matters, litigation and RTI replies.

How is the final merit list prepared?

The PSU adds weighted marks from the CLAT PG score, group discussion and interview as per its notice, then applies category and vacancy rules. Ties are usually resolved by rule such as age or marks in a stated component.

A waiting list may be kept for a limited time. Offers are made by email and the portal, and you must accept within the stated number of days.
