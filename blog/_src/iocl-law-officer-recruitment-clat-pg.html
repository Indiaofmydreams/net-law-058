Indian Oil is India's largest oil company by revenue, and its law department supports refining, pipelines and a vast retail network. Law Officer posts are limited, but the work is varied. Here is what has been reported and how to prepare.

How does IOCL recruit law officers?

IOCL has advertised Law Officer posts, as seen in its 2024 notice, and in some cycles has used CLAT PG scores. The method can change, so read the current notice for whether CLAT PG, its own test or another route applies.

Because the route changes, students should prepare for both a score-based and a test-based process.

What is the pay scale for an IOCL law officer?

Officer posts at IOCL are usually in Grade A, with a pay band reported around ₹50,000 to ₹1,60,000 basic and added allowances, perks and performance pay. Treat figures as approximate and confirm in the notice.

What are the eligibility rules?

A law degree with a minimum percentage (commonly 60% for General, relaxed for reserved categories), an age limit as per the notice and, where required, a valid CLAT PG score. Check the exact qualification date.

Experience is not usually required for entry-level law officer posts but may be asked for lateral posts.

What is the selection process?

Typically shortlisting by score or test, followed by a group task or discussion and a personal interview, then document check and medical. Weightage differs, so follow the notice.

What does an IOCL law officer do?

Handles contracts, dealership and marketing disputes, pipeline and land matters, arbitration, labour and service cases, vigilance and RTI work. Postings can be in refineries, regional offices or the corporate office.

Expect transfers across regions as part of the career.
