  • CUET PG LLM: 75 MCQs, 300 marks, +4/-1, computer-based, central and other universities.
  • CLAT PG: 120 passage-based MCQs, -0.25, NLUs.
  • AILET: 100 MCQs, -0.25, NLU Delhi only.
  • All three: allowed; dates do not clash.

Is CUET PG LLM easier than CLAT PG?

Usually yes in difficulty, though not in breadth. CUET PG LLM questions are direct and subject-based across 30-plus subjects, while CLAT PG is passage-based and tests reading and application. Reports rated the 2025 and 2026 CUET PG LLM papers easy to moderate.

That 'easy' is relative: a wide syllabus and -1 marking punish half-knowledge. CLAT PG's passages are lengthier but the law you need is narrower.

Can I take CUET PG LLM, CLAT PG and AILET together?

Yes. They are separate exams with different dates and no rule stopping you from writing all three. For 2026 to 2027 CLAT PG is on 6 December 2026 and AILET on 13 December 2026; CUET PG starts on 2 March 2027.

Because CLAT PG and AILET come first, treat them as the first checkpoint and use the three months after as a focused push for CUET PG, when the syllabus breadth matters most.

Which exam should I take for an NLU LL.M. seat?

CLAT PG for the Consortium's National Law Universities and AILET for NLU Delhi. CUET PG LLM does not give access to NLU seats. Prepare for CUET PG LLM as an additional route to central and state universities.

Does a high CLAT PG score help in CUET PG LLM?

The two scores are separate, and a CLAT PG score cannot be used in place of a CUET PG score. Preparation overlaps in core subjects, so work for one helps the others, but CUET PG needs wider coverage and CLAT PG needs reading practice.

Which exam is best if I can only prepare for one?

If an NLU is your goal, choose CLAT PG. If you want a central university such as DU or BHU, choose CUET PG LLM. If NLU Delhi is your only target, choose AILET. If you are unsure, CUET PG LLM opens the most universities for the least depth.

How do I split my preparation across the three?

Use a common core of Constitution, Criminal Law, Contract, Torts, Jurisprudence and Family Law, then add passage practice for CLAT PG and breadth for CUET PG. A shared mistake notebook for core subjects saves time.
