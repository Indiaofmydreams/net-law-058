Quick answer
  • No fixed list: PSUs join or drop CLAT PG year by year.
  • Typical posts: Law Officer, Assistant Legal Adviser, Executive Trainee (Law).
  • Selection mixes the CLAT PG score with GD and interview.
  • Notices usually come December to March.

Every year a few thousand candidates sit CLAT PG, and a small group aim past the NLU seat to a public sector legal job. The route is real, but the list of PSUs, the number of vacancies and the selection method are decided by each company, not by CLAT. This guide sets out which PSUs have used the route, what they hire for and how to plan.

Which PSUs recruit through CLAT PG?

PSUs that have used CLAT PG scores in recent cycles include POWERGRID, NTPC, IOCL, ONGC, Oil India, Coal India, BHEL, GAIL and SAIL, among others. The list changes every year, so treat any list as a guide and confirm in the CLAT Consortium's PSU announcement.

Some PSUs also recruit law officers through their own written test or through UGC NET and not through CLAT PG at all. Banks and regulators run separate exams. Our posts on POWERGRID, NTPC, IOCL and ONGC go company by company.

What posts do PSUs fill through CLAT PG?

Mostly entry-level legal executive posts such as Law Officer, Assistant Law Officer, Assistant Legal Adviser, Executive Trainee (Law) or Officer Trainee (Law). Titles differ by company, but all sit in the executive cadre with a fixed pay scale.

Do I need an LLM to apply?

Usually the PSU asks for a law degree with a minimum percentage, often 60% for General, and some ask for LLB or LLM plus the CLAT PG score. Rules differ, so read each advertisement and check the cut-off date for the degree.

Reserved categories get a relaxed percentage and age as per Government rules, and PwBD candidates have their own relaxation. Candidates in the final year of LLM may be allowed to apply if results arrive before a stated date. A candidate with an LLB and a high CLAT PG score may still qualify, which is why many students use CLAT PG as a double route to an NLU seat and a PSU interview.

When do PSU notifications come out?

Notices usually appear after the CLAT PG result, often between December and March, with registration at the PSU's own portal. Dates move every year, so keep checking the PSU career pages and the CLAT PG website.

Since the exam is held in the first week of December in many years, the result and the PSU cycle follow it. Our counselling guide explains how the NLU process runs alongside. Do not wait for a PSU result to decide on your NLU seat if it is time to pay.

Is a PSU job worth it compared with other options?

For stability, pay scale and benefits many candidates say yes, but vacancies are small and some years a PSU skips law recruitment. Keep a second path such as judiciary, UGC NET or a firm role.

Read the bond, the posting policy and the transfer rules before accepting. A PSU may post you to a plant or a project site far from a metro, and the early years can be spent on contracts, land and arbitration matters.
