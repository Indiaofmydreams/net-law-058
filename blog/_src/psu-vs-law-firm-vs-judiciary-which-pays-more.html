After CLAT PG or LLM, the same question comes up: which career? The answer depends on what you value in the first five and ten years. This guide compares the main paths on pay, growth, stability and lifestyle.

Which path pays most at the start?

Top law firms often pay the most to fresh NLU graduates, with reported starting packages in the high single-digit to double-digit lakhs. PSUs, judiciary and banks pay less at entry but add benefits. Figures are estimates and vary.

Which path grows fastest?

Law firms can grow quickly for those who make partner, but attrition is high. PSUs and judiciary grow steadily on fixed scales. Independent practice can grow most or least, depending on clients and years of effort.

Regulators combine a good start with structured promotion and are covered in our posts on RBI and SEBI and NABARD.

Which offers the best stability?

Judicial services and central PSUs offer high job security, pensions or defined benefits (check current rules) and predictable promotion. Law firms and in-house roles depend on market cycles and performance.

See our judiciary page for the exam route.

What about work-life balance?

PSUs and banks usually have regular hours; judiciary has heavy caseloads but fixed hours; top firms often have long hours and deadlines. Individual experience varies by team and posting.

Postings matter: a PSU job at a remote plant is different from a corporate office role.

How do I decide?

List what you need in the first five years: money, stability, prestige or learning. Then pick the path that fits and keep a second option open, since each has an exam or hiring cycle with limited seats.
