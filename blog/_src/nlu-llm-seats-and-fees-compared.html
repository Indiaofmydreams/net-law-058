Quick answer
  • One compilation lists LLM fees from about 1.25 lakh (RMLNLU) to 4.55 lakh (NLSIU) a year.
  • Figures are approximate, per year, and exclude hostel and mess. Some NLUs charge by domicile or category.
  • Seats across NLUs are reported at about 1,500 to 1,750, with sharply different demand by NLU.
  • Always check the NLU's own fee notice for your year.

Before you fill NLU preferences, you should know what each option costs. The numbers below come from a single published compilation of NLU fees, which carries its own warnings: no uniformity, approximate totals, hostel charges separate, and some internal inconsistencies. Treat the table as a way to compare NLUs against each other, not as a quote. The official fee notice of each university for your admission year is what you will actually pay.

Why the numbers do not line up

  • Totals are approximate and mix tuition, one-time and recurring charges.
  • Hostel and mess are listed separately.
  • The page contradicts itself on NLSIU and NLU Delhi in its FAQ, which is why we flag them.
  • Fees change every year, and the compilation does not date each row.

How many LLM seats are there?

Published coverage puts LL.M. seats across the NLUs at roughly 1,500 to 1,750. The Consortium reported 17,335 applicants for the PG programme in the CLAT 2026 cycle, so about nine to ten candidates compete for each seat on average. Demand is far from even: a handful of NLUs attract a huge share of the top ranks. We have no verified per-NLU seat matrix, so read each university's notification. How to choose between NLUs

  1. Rank realism. Check closing ranks for your category for the last few years.
  2. Total cost. Add hostel, mess and living costs to the fee.
  3. Specialisations offered. LL.M. specialisations differ by NLU.
  4. Location and faculty. Consider where you will live and what you want to research.
  5. Preference order. Fill preferences in the order you truly want them, then learn Freeze, Float and exit.
