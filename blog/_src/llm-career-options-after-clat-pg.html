An LLM opens many doors, but not automatically. Each career has its own entry, pay and lifestyle. This guide maps twelve paths and what each needs, with links to detailed posts.

What are the main career paths after LLM?

Judicial services, PSU legal posts, regulator grades such as RBI and SEBI, bank law officers, law firms, in-house counsel, academia, Army JAG, court clerkships, independent practice and study abroad. Each has its own exam or selection route.

Which paths are government jobs?

Judicial services, PSUs, regulators, public sector banks, Army JAG, court posts and university teaching are government or public sector. Each recruits through its own notice, exam or interview, and none depends on an LLM alone.

See the judicial services guide for the exam route.

Which paths need an exam?

Judicial services (state exams), UGC NET for assistant professor, RBI, SEBI, NABARD and IBPS SO for officer posts. PSUs use CLAT PG scores or their own tests. Law firms hire by campus and interview.

Competitive exams have limited seats, so start preparing early.

Does an LLM help in all these paths?

It helps for academia and specialised practice, adds weight for some PSUs and regulators, and is optional for judiciary, bank and firm jobs. In many cases experience counts as much as the degree.

How should I combine paths?

Many candidates prepare UGC NET, judicial services and PSU notices together, because the core law overlaps. Build a calendar of exam dates and keep notes by subject so that one effort serves several goals.
