The CLAT PG result is a starting point, not a verdict. This guide gives a simple way to turn your score into a plan, whatever the number.

How should I use my CLAT PG score?

Use it for three things at once: an NLU seat through counselling, PSU shortlisting where applicable and a base to plan UGC NET or judiciary. A single score can serve more than one goal if you watch the dates.

Start with our counselling guide.

What if I get a top NLU seat?

Choose the specialisation that fits your goal, and plan internships and research from the first term. Decide early whether you want firms, PSUs, regulators or teaching, since your LLM year is short.

What if my score is average?

You may still get a good state or private university seat, and PSUs shortlist on their own cut-offs. Keep applying, and use the year to prepare for UGC NET, judiciary or regulator exams.

How do I build a one-year timeline?

Months 1–2: counselling and joining. Months 3–6: coursework, internships and exam preparation. Months 7–12: PSU, regulator and NET notices, dissertation and applications. Adjust dates to each notice.

Use the UGC NET schedule as one fixed point.

What should I keep as a backup?

Keep at least one exam-based path (UGC NET, judiciary or a regulator) and one practice or corporate path. Track notice dates in a simple sheet, so that no deadline passes unnoticed.
