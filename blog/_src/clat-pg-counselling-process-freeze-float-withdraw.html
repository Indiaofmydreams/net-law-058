Quick answer
  • Steps: register, pay, list NLU preferences, get allotments in rounds, Freeze or Float, verify documents, pay the balance fee.
  • Freeze = keep the seat and stop. Float = keep it for now and try for a better one.
  • 2026 cycle as reported: five lists, with only Freeze in the last.
  • Fees, dates and refund rules change each cycle, so read the current Consortium instructions.

Getting a rank in CLAT PG is not the end. Counselling decides which NLU you actually join, and mistakes here can cost money and a seat. The details below describe the 2026 counselling cycle as reported by Careers360, which links the Consortium's own instructions. Dates, fees and rules are re-issued every year, so use this as a map of the process and confirm the specifics on the Consortium website.

What is the CLAT PG counselling process?

  1. Registration. Log in to the Consortium portal with your mobile number and password, validate your invite, submit NLU preferences, enter refund bank details and pay the fee. For 2026, registration was reported to run from 17 to 27 December 2025. Preference limits are set each year, so check the instructions.
  2. Allotment lists. Seats are allotted in rounds, based on your rank, category and preferences.
  3. Freeze or Float. When you pay the confirmation fee you choose one.
  4. Document verification and balance fee. At the allotted NLU you verify documents and pay the remaining university fee.
  5. Reporting. You report to the university and complete induction.

How many seat allotment rounds are there?

For the 2026 cycle, five lists were reported: 7 January, 22 January, 5 February, 2 May and 15 May 2026. Freeze or Float was offered in the first four rounds, and the fifth listed only the Freeze option. Each round had a confirmation-fee window of about a week. The number of rounds and the gaps between them can differ in other years.

What is the difference between Freeze and Float?

  • Freeze: you accept the seat you were allotted. You are out of the running for higher preferences, and your seat stays safe.
  • Float: you accept the seat for now but stay in the running for a higher preference in the next round. If you get one, your earlier seat is released.

Use Float if there is a clearly better NLU you could realistically get. Use Freeze when you are happy with the seat or the next rounds are late, because a long gap can lock your plans. Read where your rank landed in the cut-off guide before deciding.

What if you exit?

An exit option exists. As reported for 2026, exiting after being allotted a seat forfeits the confirmation fee. Registration-fee refunds depend on timing: a full refund if no seat is allotted after the last round, or if you do not choose Freeze or Float and exit, and a deduction of 5,000 rupees if you exit after a deadline or are found ineligible. Refunds go to the same bank account used for payment.

Documents to keep ready

  • Class 10, Class 12 and degree mark sheets, and the CLAT PG admit card.
  • Transfer and migration certificates.
  • Category, income or domicile certificates, where applicable.

Eligibility problems can cancel an admission after allotment, so check your percentage against the rules in our eligibility guide early.

Verify every cycle

Everything above is the 2026 cycle as reported by Careers360, and amounts and dates change each year. The Consortium's counselling instructions for your year are the authority, so read them before you pay anything.
