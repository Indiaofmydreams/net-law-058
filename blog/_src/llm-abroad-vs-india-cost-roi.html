Quick answer
  • Foreign LLM costs run to tens of lakhs; NLU LLM is far lower.
  • Scholarships are competitive.
  • An LLM does not by itself give practice rights abroad.
  • Go with a clear plan.

Many LLM students dream of Oxford, Harvard or NUS. The degree can help, but the cost is high and the return depends on the plan. Here is how to think about it.

How much does an LLM abroad cost?

Total costs for a one-year LLM in the UK, US or Europe commonly reach tens of lakhs of rupees, including tuition and living, with leading US programmes among the highest. Exact fees change every year, so check each university.

Add visa, insurance, travel and exchange-rate risk.

How does that compare with an Indian LLM?

An NLU LLM usually costs a small fraction of that, with total fees in the lakhs. The key question is whether the foreign degree opens jobs and income that justify the extra cost.

What about scholarships and funding?

Scholarships such as Chevening, Commonwealth, Fulbright and university awards exist, but are competitive. Education loans are common, so check interest, repayment and your expected salary.

Apply early, since deadlines can fall many months before the course.

Can I work or practise abroad after an LLM?

An LLM does not automatically give a right to practise. It depends on country rules, bar exams and visa. Many Indian graduates return to India, and some qualify as solicitors in England and Wales through the SQE.

Check the latest visa and bar rules; they change.

When is it worth it?

It can be worth it for those with a clear plan: international arbitration, a foreign bar, academia or a firm that will sponsor or value the degree. Without a plan, an Indian LLM plus experience may offer better value.
