Quick summary
  • AIBE 22 is on 29 November 2026, offline (pen and paper).
  • 100 MCQs, 1 mark each, no negative marking, reported duration 3 hours 30 minutes.
  • Pass mark: 45 percent (General, OBC) or 40 percent (SC, ST, PwD).
  • Admit cards were reported released on 14 November. Read your instructions on what you may carry.

The All India Bar Examination is the test every law graduate must clear to receive the Certificate of Practice and appear before Indian courts. With about a week to go, two things decide the result: knowing the pattern and keeping your preparation calm and organised. Here is the pattern as reported and a plan for the last eight days.

These details come from reports on the Bar Council of India's schedule. The BCI notice and your admit card are the final word, especially for reporting time and what you may carry. Check allindiabarexamination.com.

What to carry and what not to

  • Printed admit card and the photo ID your instructions name.
  • Permitted writing items, as listed in the instructions.
  • If bare Acts are allowed, take only clean copies. Handwritten notes, commentaries and annotated copies have not been allowed in recent sessions. Treat anything the notice does not clearly permit as not allowed.
  • Do not carry phones or electronic devices into the hall.

The no-negative-marking advantage

With no deduction for wrong answers, leaving a question blank can never help you. Mark an answer for every one of the 100 questions, even a guess. A sensible routine for 3 hours 30 minutes:

  1. Pass 1 (about 100 minutes): answer what you know. Mark doubtful ones lightly.
  2. Pass 2 (about 75 minutes): return to doubtful questions and use the Bare Act where allowed.
  3. Last 15 minutes: make sure every question has an answer and the sheet is filled correctly.

Time per question is generous, so the real risk is panic and mis-marking, not speed.

After the exam

Clearing AIBE earns you the Certificate of Practice, which lets you practise before courts as an enrolled advocate. Keep an eye on the BCI website for the answer key, result and certificate process, and rely on the official notice for those dates.

Frequently asked questions

When is AIBE 22?

AIBE 22 is scheduled for 29 November 2026, as reported from the Bar Council of India's schedule. Your admit card carries your centre and reporting time.

How many questions are there in AIBE and is there negative marking?

The paper has 100 multiple choice questions of 1 mark each. Reports state that there is no negative marking, so attempt every question.

What is the passing mark in AIBE?

The qualifying mark is reported as 45 percent for General and OBC candidates and 40 percent for SC, ST and PwD candidates. That means 45 and 40 marks out of 100.

Can I carry a Bare Act into the AIBE hall?

Reports say bare Acts without notes or annotations have been permitted in recent sessions, but the rule that binds you is in the BCI notice and your admit card. Read it before the exam.

When will the AIBE 22 result be declared?

We have not seen a confirmed result date from the BCI, so we do not state one. Follow allindiabarexamination.com.
