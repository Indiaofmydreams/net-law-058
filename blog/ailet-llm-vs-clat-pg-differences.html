Quick answers
  • Different exams: AILET is run by NLU Delhi, CLAT PG by the Consortium of NLUs.
  • Format: AILET LLM is 100 standalone MCQs in 120 minutes; CLAT PG is 120 passage-based MCQs in 120 minutes.
  • Both have -0.25 negative marking.
  • You can take both. The study overlap is large; the skills trained are not identical.

Every LL.M. aspirant eventually asks whether to prepare for AILET, CLAT PG or both. The two exams share a subject base and a negative-marking rule, but they ask questions differently, admit to different institutions and use separate counselling. This guide lines them up so you can plan, using the official scheme where available and clearly marking what is only reported. Confirm dates and eligibility on NLU Delhi's site and the Consortium's site.

Which is tougher, AILET or CLAT PG?

There is no official ranking, and the answer depends on what you find hard.

  • Seat pressure. Reports for the latest cycle put the regular AILET LL.M. intake at about 70 seats on merit, around 81 with foreign national and OCI/PIO seats. CLAT PG spreads roughly 1,500 to 1,750 seats across many NLUs, but the best-known NLUs within it are just as sought after.
  • Question style. AILET rewards precise knowledge: if you do not know the case, section or thinker, reasoning will not rescue you. CLAT PG rewards reading speed and elimination, because the answer is partly in the passage.
  • Time per question. AILET gives about 72 seconds each. CLAT PG gives about 60 seconds, and part of that goes on the passage.
Do not compare cut-off numbers

AILET is out of 100 and CLAT PG out of 120, with different candidate pools and different ranking lists. A score from one cannot be converted into a chance in the other. Compare ranks within each exam, as in our CLAT PG score guide.

Is a CLAT PG score valid for NLU Delhi?

NLU Delhi uses AILET for its own LL.M. admissions. The Consortium's member list has changed over the years, and the NLU page shows where CLAT PG is accepted. Do not assume that a CLAT PG score gets you into NLU Delhi; read the notification for the current cycle. The reverse also holds: an AILET result does not substitute for CLAT PG at the Consortium's universities.

Should you prepare for both?

Usually yes, if you are aiming at NLUs generally and NLU Delhi in particular. The syllabus overlap is large: constitutional law, jurisprudence, criminal law, contract, torts and the other LLB subjects feed both. What changes is the training:

  1. For CLAT PG: practise passages and eliminate options. Our passage method helps.
  2. For AILET: build a case file per branch and drill direct questions. See how to prepare for AILET LLM.
  3. For both: keep an error log and revisit it weekly.

If you have limited time, pick the exam whose target institution you want most, then use the other as a second attempt rather than splitting your attention in half.

How should you choose between them?

  • Want NLU Delhi specifically: AILET is compulsory.
  • Want a wider NLU choice, or public-sector legal jobs that use CLAT PG scores: CLAT PG.
  • Strong at fast reading, weaker at memorised case law: lean on CLAT PG, but still prepare AILET basics.
  • Strong at recall: AILET is natural, but do not skip passage practice if you also apply to the Consortium.

Frequently asked questions

Is AILET the same as CLAT PG?

No. AILET is conducted by NLU Delhi for its own LL.M. admissions. CLAT PG is conducted by the Consortium of NLUs for its member universities. They are separate applications, separate papers and separate counselling.

Which is tougher, AILET LLM or CLAT PG?

There is no official comparison. AILET LLM is harder to get into by seat count, because only about 70 regular seats are reported against a much larger pool, while CLAT PG is harder on reading speed because of long passages. Which feels tougher depends on whether you are stronger at recall or at reading.

Can I take both AILET and CLAT PG?

Yes. They are separate exams with separate forms and fees, and many aspirants appear for both. Check the notified dates for your cycle so they do not clash.

Does a CLAT PG score count for NLU Delhi LLM admission?

NLU Delhi admits its LL.M. through AILET, so do not assume a CLAT PG score replaces it. Confirm in the current NLU Delhi notification.

Is the AILET LLM syllabus different from CLAT PG?

Both rest on core LLB subjects. AILET names thirteen branches and asks direct questions; CLAT PG tests the same subjects through passages. Preparation overlaps heavily.
