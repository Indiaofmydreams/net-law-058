Quick summary

  • The exam runs on 14, 15, 16, 17, 18 and 21 December 2026, in CBT mode.
  • Two shifts a day: 9:00 am to 12:00 noon and 3:00 pm to 6:00 pm.
  • Your own date and shift depend on your subject. For Law, look up code 058 in the official notice.

The National Testing Agency has released the subject-wise schedule for the UGC NET December 2026 session. If you are preparing for Law (058) and Paper 1, this is the moment to turn "sometime in December" into a fixed countdown. Here is what is confirmed, what is still to be checked, and how to use the next three weeks.

How to find your Law (058) date and shift

The schedule is not one date for everyone. NTA assigns each subject a date and shift, listed in Annexure-I of the public notice titled "Public Notice for Subject-wise schedule of UGC-NET December 2026 Examination". The reports we checked did not state the Law row, so we are not guessing it here.

  1. Open ugcnet.nta.nic.in and go to Public Notices.
  2. Open the subject-wise schedule notice and its Annexure-I PDF.
  3. Find subject code 058 (Law) and note the date and shift.
  4. Confirm it again on your admit card, which carries your final centre, date and reporting time.

Exam pattern at a glance

The pattern has been stable in recent sessions. Check the current Information Bulletin for any change.

Each question carries 2 marks, and recent sessions have had no negative marking. Because Paper 2 carries two-thirds of the marks, most of your remaining time belongs to Law. Paper 1 is where an organised candidate picks up easy marks.

What to do between now and the exam

  • Watch for the city slip. It is reported from 23 November. Check your city and travel plan early, especially if you must book a train or stay.
  • Download your admit card on the first day it appears (reported from 7 December). Check your name, photo, subject and shift, and report any error to NTA at once.
  • Switch to full-length mocks. Sit a 100-question Law mock under a timer at least twice a week. Review every wrong answer, not just the score.
  • Revise, don't restart. At this stage, notes and previous year questions beat new books.
  • Plan exam day. Know your reporting time, carry the permitted documents listed on your admit card, and practise on a computer, since the exam is CBT.

Frequently asked questions

When is the UGC NET December 2026 exam?

NTA has scheduled the exam from 14 to 18 December and on 21 December 2026, in computer-based test (CBT) mode, in two shifts: 9:00 am to 12:00 noon and 3:00 pm to 6:00 pm.

On which date is the Law (058) paper?

Each subject has its own date and shift in Annexure-I of NTA's subject-wise schedule notice. The news reports we checked did not list Law, so open the notice on ugcnet.nta.nic.in and look for subject code 058.

When will the exam city slip and admit card come out?

Reports say the exam city intimation is scheduled from 23 November 2026 and admit cards from 7 December 2026. NTA can change these dates, so keep checking the official website.

Is there negative marking in UGC NET?

Recent sessions have not had negative marking, and each question carries 2 marks. Always confirm in the current Information Bulletin.
