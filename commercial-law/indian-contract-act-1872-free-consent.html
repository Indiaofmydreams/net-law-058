12 practice questions with answers

1Consent under Section 13 means:

  1. Agreement upon the same thing in the same sense
  2. Agreement on similar things
  3. Agreement through an agent
  4. Agreement in writing
Show answer

Answer: A. Agreement upon the same thing in the same sense

Report this question

2Under Section 15, coercion involves an act forbidden by:

  1. The Constitution
  2. The Indian Penal Code
  3. The Code of Civil Procedure
  4. Any contract
Show answer

Answer: B. The Indian Penal Code

Report this question

3In Ranganayakamma v. Alwar Setti, a widow's consent to adoption was obtained by threatening to prevent her husband's cremation. This was held to be:

  1. Fraud
  2. Coercion
  3. Misrepresentation
  4. Mistake
Show answer

Answer: B. Coercion

Report this question

4Under Section 16(3), where a dominant party enters an unconscionable transaction, the burden of proving absence of undue influence lies on:

  1. The weaker party
  2. The court
  3. The dominant party
  4. A third party
Show answer

Answer: C. The dominant party

Report this question

5Undue influence arising from a spiritual relationship (guru and disciple) was illustrated in:

  1. Derry v. Peek
  2. Hyde v. Wrench
  3. Mannu Singh v. Umadat Pande
  4. Couturier v. Hastie
Show answer

Answer: C. Mannu Singh v. Umadat Pande

Report this question

6Which of the following is NOT fraud under Section 17?

  1. Suggesting as a fact what is not true, by one who does not believe it
  2. Mere silence about facts likely to affect willingness to contract, absent a duty to speak
  3. Active concealment of a fact
  4. A promise made without any intention of performing it
Show answer

Answer: B. Mere silence about facts likely to affect willingness to contract, absent a duty to speak

Report this question

7Under the proviso to Section 19, a contract induced by misrepresentation is NOT voidable if:

  1. The party had the means of discovering the truth with ordinary diligence
  2. The misrepresentation was innocent
  3. The contract was in writing
  4. The price was paid
Show answer

Answer: A. The party had the means of discovering the truth with ordinary diligence

Report this question

8Misrepresentation under Section 18 differs from fraud in that:

  1. It is always a crime
  2. It makes the contract void
  3. It requires active concealment
  4. There is no intent to deceive
Show answer

Answer: D. There is no intent to deceive

Report this question

9Under Section 20, where both parties are under a mistake as to a matter of fact essential to the agreement, the agreement is:

  1. Voidable
  2. Valid
  3. Unenforceable only
  4. Void
Show answer

Answer: D. Void

Report this question

10Under Section 21, a contract is NOT voidable because of a mistake as to:

  1. Matter of fact
  2. Identity
  3. Subject-matter
  4. A law in force in India
Show answer

Answer: D. A law in force in India

Report this question

11Under Section 22, a contract is not voidable merely because it was caused by:

  1. Fraud
  2. One party's unilateral mistake as to a matter of fact
  3. Coercion
  4. Undue influence
Show answer

Answer: B. One party's unilateral mistake as to a matter of fact

12Section 19A deals with the voidability of agreements caused by:

  1. Undue influence
  2. Mistake
  3. Fraud
  4. Coercion
