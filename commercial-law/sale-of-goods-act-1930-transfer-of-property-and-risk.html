9 practice questions with answers

1Under Section 18, in a contract for the sale of unascertained goods, property in the goods:

  1. Passes at the time of the contract
  2. Passes on payment
  3. Does not pass until the goods are ascertained
  4. Passes on delivery to the carrier
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Does not pass until the goods are ascertained

Ascertainment comes first, then appropriation under s.23.

Report this question

2Under Section 19, in a contract for specific or ascertained goods, property passes at the time:

  1. The price is paid
  2. The goods are delivered
  3. The contract is registered
  4. The parties intend it to pass
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. The parties intend it to pass

Intention is gathered from terms, conduct and circumstances.

Report this question

3Under Section 20, where there is an unconditional contract for specific goods in a deliverable state, property passes:

  1. On delivery
  2. On payment
  3. On inspection
  4. When the contract is made, even if payment or delivery is postponed
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. When the contract is made, even if payment or delivery is postponed

Strong default rule.

Report this question

4A agrees to sell a specific table to B, and the table needs polishing by A to be put in a deliverable state. Under Section 21, property passes when:

  1. The contract is made
  2. A does the polishing and B has notice of it
  3. The price is paid
  4. B takes delivery
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. A does the polishing and B has notice of it

Section 21 protects the buyer until the seller completes what he must do.

Report this question

5Under Section 22, where the seller must weigh, measure or test specific goods in a deliverable state to ascertain the price, property:

  1. Passes on contract
  2. Does not pass until the act is done and the buyer has notice of it
  3. Passes on payment
  4. Passes only on delivery
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Does not pass until the act is done and the buyer has notice of it

Same reasoning as s.21.

Report this question

6Under Section 23(2), delivery of goods to a carrier for transmission to the buyer, without reserving the right of disposal, is deemed to be:

  1. Unconditional appropriation of the goods to the contract
  2. A conditional appropriation
  3. A mere offer
  4. No appropriation
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Unconditional appropriation of the goods to the contract

Property passes to the buyer on that delivery.

Report this question

7Under Section 24, where goods are delivered on approval or 'on sale or return', property passes to the buyer when he:

  1. Merely receives them
  2. Merely inspects them
  3. Signifies approval or acceptance, does any other act adopting the transaction, or retains the goods beyond the time without giving notice of rejection
  4. Pays half the price
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Signifies approval or acceptance, does any other act adopting the transaction, or retains the goods beyond the time without giving notice of rejection

Kirkham v. Attenborough: pledge by buyer was an act adopting the transaction.

Report this question

8Where goods are shipped and the bill of lading makes them deliverable to the order of the seller or his agent, under Section 25 the seller is:

  1. Deemed to have delivered them
  2. Deemed to have waived his lien
  3. Prima facie deemed to have reserved the right of disposal
  4. Deemed to have lost the property
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Prima facie deemed to have reserved the right of disposal

The seller keeps control until conditions are met.

Report this question

9The maxim res perit domino, reflected in Section 26, means:

  1. Risk prima facie passes with the property
  2. Risk always passes with delivery
  3. Risk always remains with the seller
  4. Risk passes with payment
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Risk prima facie passes with the property

Subject to contrary agreement.
