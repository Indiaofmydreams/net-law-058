14 practice questions with answers

1Under Section 3, an LLP is:

  1. An unincorporated association
  2. A firm under the Partnership Act
  3. A branch of a company
  4. A body corporate with a legal entity separate from its partners
Show answer

Answer: D. A body corporate with a legal entity separate from its partners

Report this question

2The minimum number of partners required in an LLP is:

  1. Two
  2. One
  3. Three
  4. Seven
Show answer

Answer: A. Two

Report this question

3Every LLP must have a minimum of:

  1. One designated partner, resident in India
  2. Two designated partners, who are individuals, at least one of whom is resident in India
  3. Three designated partners, all resident in India
  4. Two designated partners, both resident abroad
Show answer

Answer: B. Two designated partners, who are individuals, at least one of whom is resident in India

Report this question

4For the LLP Act, a person is 'resident in India' if he has stayed in India for a period of not less than:

  1. 90 days during the preceding financial year
  2. 120 days during the financial year
  3. 182 days during the financial year
  4. 365 days
Show answer

Answer: B. 120 days during the financial year

Report this question

5The name of an LLP must end with:

  1. 'Limited Liability Partnership' or 'LLP'
  2. 'Ltd.'
  3. 'Pvt. Ltd.'
  4. '& Co.'
Show answer

Answer: A. 'Limited Liability Partnership' or 'LLP'

Report this question

6Under Section 26, every partner of an LLP is the agent of:

  1. The other partners
  2. The LLP, but not of the other partners
  3. Both the LLP and the other partners
  4. The Registrar
Show answer

Answer: B. The LLP, but not of the other partners

Report this question

7In the absence of an LLP agreement, mutual rights and duties of partners are governed by:

  1. The Indian Partnership Act, 1932
  2. The Companies Act, 2013
  3. The Contract Act, 1872
  4. The First Schedule to the LLP Act (Section 23(4))
Show answer

Answer: D. The First Schedule to the LLP Act (Section 23(4))

Report this question

8Under the First Schedule, in the absence of agreement, the partners of an LLP share capital and profits:

  1. In proportion to contribution
  2. As the designated partners decide
  3. In proportion to age
  4. Equally
Show answer

Answer: D. Equally

Report this question

9The Annual Return of an LLP must be filed with the Registrar within:

  1. 30 days of the close of the financial year
  2. 60 days of the close of the financial year
  3. 90 days of the close of the financial year
  4. 180 days of the close of the financial year
Show answer

Answer: B. 60 days of the close of the financial year

Report this question

10The accounts of an LLP must be audited if the turnover exceeds Rs. 40 lakh or the contribution exceeds:

  1. Rs. 25 lakh
  2. Rs. 10 lakh
  3. Rs. 50 lakh
  4. Rs. 1 crore
Show answer

Answer: A. Rs. 25 lakh

Report this question

11Under the LLP Act, which entity CANNOT be converted into an LLP?

  1. A partnership firm
  2. A private company
  3. A listed public company
  4. An unlisted public company
Show answer

Answer: C. A listed public company

Report this question

12Winding up of an LLP may be:

  1. Voluntary only
  2. Voluntary or by the Tribunal
  3. By the Tribunal only
  4. By the Registrar only
Show answer

Answer: B. Voluntary or by the Tribunal

Report this question

13Under Section 17, the contribution of a partner to an LLP may consist of:

  1. Money only
  2. Tangible or intangible property, or any other benefit, including contracts for services
  3. Tangible property only
  4. Immovable property only
Show answer

Answer: B. Tangible or intangible property, or any other benefit, including contracts for services

Report this question

14Under Section 6(2) of the LLP Act, the provisions of the Indian Partnership Act, 1932:

  1. Apply fully to LLPs
  2. Apply only to LLP agreements
  3. Apply only to foreign LLPs
  4. Do not apply to LLPs
Show answer

Answer: D. Do not apply to LLPs
