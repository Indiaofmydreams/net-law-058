18 practice questions with answers

1Under Section 13, a negotiable instrument means:

  1. A promissory note, bill of exchange or cheque payable either to order or to bearer
  2. Only a cheque
  3. Any written promise to pay
  4. Only a bill of exchange
Show answer

Answer: A. A promissory note, bill of exchange or cheque payable either to order or to bearer

Report this question

2The number of original parties to a promissory note is:

  1. One
  2. Two (maker and payee)
  3. Three
  4. Four
Show answer

Answer: B. Two (maker and payee)

Report this question

3Under Section 6, a cheque is a bill of exchange:

  1. Payable after date
  2. Drawn on any person
  3. Payable only to bearer
  4. Drawn on a specified banker and not expressed to be payable otherwise than on demand
Show answer

Answer: D. Drawn on a specified banker and not expressed to be payable otherwise than on demand

Report this question

4Under Section 9, a holder in due course is a person who becomes the possessor of the instrument:

  1. For consideration, before maturity, without sufficient cause to believe that any defect existed in the title of the transferor
  2. As a gift
  3. After maturity
  4. Without consideration
Show answer

Answer: A. For consideration, before maturity, without sufficient cause to believe that any defect existed in the title of the transferor

Report this question

5Under Section 22, the days of grace allowed for a promissory note or bill payable at a fixed time are:

  1. One day
  2. Three days
  3. Two days
  4. Seven days
Show answer

Answer: B. Three days

Report this question

6Under Section 25, if the day of maturity of a promissory note or bill is a public holiday, it is deemed due on:

  1. The next preceding business day
  2. The next succeeding business day
  3. The same day
  4. The first day of the next month
Show answer

Answer: A. The next preceding business day

Report this question

7Under Section 26, a minor may draw, endorse, deliver and negotiate a negotiable instrument so as to bind:

  1. Himself and all parties
  2. Only himself
  3. All parties except himself
  4. Nobody
Show answer

Answer: B. Only himself

8In Rangappa v. Sri Mohan (2010), the Supreme Court held that the presumption under Section 139 includes the existence of:

  1. A partnership
  2. A legally enforceable debt or liability
  3. A guarantee
  4. An agency
Show answer

Answer: B. A legally enforceable debt or liability

Report this question

9For the purpose of Section 138, a cheque must be presented to the bank within:

  1. Three months of the date on which it is drawn, or within its validity period, whichever is earlier
  2. One month of the date on which it is drawn
  3. Six months of the date on which it is drawn
  4. One year of the date on which it is drawn
Show answer

Answer: A. Three months of the date on which it is drawn, or within its validity period, whichever is earlier

Report this question

10Under Section 138(b), the demand notice to the drawer must be given within:

  1. 7 days of dishonour
  2. 15 days of receiving information of dishonour
  3. 30 days of receiving information from the bank of dishonour
  4. 60 days of dishonour
Show answer

Answer: C. 30 days of receiving information from the bank of dishonour

Report this question

11After receiving the demand notice, the drawer has to make payment within:

  1. 7 days
  2. 15 days
  3. 30 days
  4. 45 days
Show answer

Answer: B. 15 days

12Under Section 142(1)(b), the complaint must be made within:

  1. 15 days from the date the cause of action arises
  2. Three months from the date the cause of action arises
  3. One month from the date the cause of action arises
  4. Six months from the date the cause of action arises
Show answer

Answer: C. One month from the date the cause of action arises

13The maximum punishment under Section 138 is:

  1. Imprisonment up to 6 months, or fine
  2. Imprisonment up to 1 year, or fine up to the cheque amount
  3. Imprisonment up to 2 years, or fine up to twice the cheque amount, or both
  4. Imprisonment up to 5 years
Show answer

Answer: C. Imprisonment up to 2 years, or fine up to twice the cheque amount, or both

14Under Section 143A (inserted in 2018), the court trying a Section 138 offence may order the drawer to pay interim compensation up to:

  1. 5% of the cheque amount
  2. 10% of the cheque amount
  3. 20% of the cheque amount
  4. 50% of the cheque amount
Show answer

Answer: C. 20% of the cheque amount

15Under Section 148, in an appeal by the drawer against conviction, the appellate court may order deposit of a minimum of:

  1. 10% of the fine or compensation awarded
  2. 50% of the fine or compensation awarded
  3. The whole amount
  4. 20% of the fine or compensation awarded
Show answer

Answer: D. 20% of the fine or compensation awarded

16After the 2015 Amendment to Section 142(2), in case of a cheque delivered for collection through an account, the offence is inquired into by the court within whose jurisdiction:

  1. The drawee bank's branch is situated
  2. The drawer resides
  3. The branch of the bank where the payee or holder in due course maintains the account is situated
  4. The cheque was written
Show answer

Answer: C. The branch of the bank where the payee or holder in due course maintains the account is situated

Report this question

17Under Section 123, a cheque is 'generally crossed' when it bears across its face:

  1. The signature of the drawer only
  2. The name of a banker
  3. Two parallel transverse lines, with or without the words 'and Company' or 'not negotiable'
  4. The words 'Account Payee' only
Show answer

Answer: C. Two parallel transverse lines, with or without the words 'and Company' or 'not negotiable'

Report this question

18Under Section 130, a person taking a cheque crossed 'not negotiable':

  1. Acquires a better title than the transferor
  2. Acquires a perfect title
  3. Cannot take it at all
  4. Does not acquire, and cannot give, a better title than that of the person from whom he took it
Show answer

Answer: D. Does not acquire, and cannot give, a better title than that of the person from whom he took it
