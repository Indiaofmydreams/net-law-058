About this question set

This set has 22 multiple-choice questions on Commercial Law from the UGC NET Law (058) syllabus, unit 6, Commercial Law. Try each question first, then tap Show answer to check the key and read the explanation. 18 of the 22 questions carry a written explanation.

1Privity of contract means:

  1. Third parties pay
  2. Only parties can sue on the contract (subject to exceptions)
  3. Courts decide
  4. Anyone can sue
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Only parties can sue on the contract (subject to exceptions)

General rule.

2Quantum meruit means:

  1. No value
  2. Whole value
  3. As much as is earned
  4. As much as is lost
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. As much as is earned

Reasonable remuneration.

3Assertion (A): An agreement without consideration is generally void.
Reason (R): Consideration is an essential element of a valid contract.

  1. Both A and R are true, and R is the correct explanation of A
  2. Both A and R are true, but R is not the correct explanation of A
  3. A is true, but R is false
  4. A is false, but R is true

Answer: A. Both A and R are true, and R is the correct explanation of A

Subject to statutory exceptions.

12A offers to sell a car for ₹5 lakh. B replies offering ₹4 lakh. B's reply is a:

  1. Counter-offer
  2. Acceptance
  3. Revocation
  4. Invitation to offer
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Counter-offer

Counter-offer rejects the original.

Report this question

13A agrees to perform at B's concert but dies before it. The contract is:

  1. Enforceable against estate
  2. Discharged by supervening impossibility
  3. Voidable
  4. Breached by A
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Discharged by supervening impossibility

Personal performance.

14After dishonour of a cheque and notice, the drawer must pay within how many days of receipt of notice?

  1. 45
  2. 30
  3. 15
  4. 7
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. 15

Section 138 NI Act.

15Goods are sold 'as is' and the buyer relies on his own judgment. The principle is:

  1. Quantum meruit
  2. Caveat venditor
  3. Estoppel
  4. Caveat emptor
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Caveat emptor

Let the buyer beware.

Report this question

16Privity of contract was stressed in:

  1. Salomon
  2. Carlill
  3. Hadley
  4. Tweddle v. Atkinson
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Tweddle v. Atkinson

A stranger cannot sue.

17Lalman Shukla v. Gauri Dutt held that acceptance requires:

  1. Writing
  2. Registration
  3. Payment
  4. Knowledge of the offer
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Knowledge of the offer

Performance without knowledge is not acceptance.

Report this question

18Chinnaya v. Ramayya is associated with:

  1. Coercion
  2. Minor's contracts
  3. Consideration may move from a third party in India
  4. Frustration
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Consideration may move from a third party in India

Indian law differs from English privity of consideration.

Report this question

19In Mohori Bibee v. Dharmodas Ghose, the Privy Council held that a minor's agreement is:

  1. Void ab initio
  2. Voidable
  3. Valid
  4. Valid if beneficial
Show answer

Answer: A. Void ab initio

Report this question

20Under the Indian Majority Act, 1875, the age of majority is 21 years where:

  1. The person is married
  2. The person is an orphan
  3. The person is employed
  4. A guardian has been appointed by a court or the person is under the Court of Wards
Show answer

Answer: D. A guardian has been appointed by a court or the person is under the Court of Wards

Report this question

21Under Section 184 of the Contract Act:

  1. A minor cannot be an agent
  2. A minor can be a principal
  3. A minor can be an agent but is not answerable to the principal
  4. A minor can ratify contracts
Show answer

Answer: C. A minor can be an agent but is not answerable to the principal

Report this question

22A minor, on attaining majority, ratifies a contract made during minority. The ratification is:

  1. Valid
  2. Voidable
  3. Invalid, as a void contract cannot be ratified without fresh consideration
  4. Valid if the minor benefited
Show answer

Answer: C. Invalid, as a void contract cannot be ratified without fresh consideration
