About this question set

This set has 20 multiple-choice questions on Torts & Consumer Protection from the UGC NET Law (058) syllabus, unit 5, Law of Torts and Consumer Protection. Try each question first, then tap Show answer to check the key and read the explanation. 20 of the 20 questions carry a written explanation.

For the concepts and case law behind these questions, read the Torts & Consumer Protection notes. Once you are comfortable, test yourself under exam conditions with a full-length mock test.

1The primary aim of tort law is:

  1. Revenge
  2. Compensation
  3. Reform
  4. Punishment
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Compensation

Remedy through unliquidated damages.

2A tort is remedied mainly by:

  1. Fine
  2. Penance
  3. Action for unliquidated damages
  4. Imprisonment
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Action for unliquidated damages

3Trespass differs from nuisance in that trespass is:

  1. A contract
  2. Always indirect
  3. A crime
  4. A direct interference
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. A direct interference

Nuisance is indirect interference with enjoyment.

14A surgical instrument is left inside a patient. The patient relies on:

  1. Remoteness
  2. Damnum sine injuria

Negligence is inferred.
