About this question set

This set has 21 multiple-choice questions on Blood Relations from the UGC NET Paper 1 syllabus, unit 5, Mathematical Reasoning and Aptitude. Try each question first, then tap Show answer to check the key and read the explanation. 21 of the 21 questions carry a written explanation.

For the concepts and case law behind these questions, read the Mathematical Reasoning notes. Once you are comfortable, test yourself under exam conditions with a full-length mock test.

21 practice questions with answers

1Pointing to a woman in a photograph, a man says, "She is the daughter of my grandfather's only son." How is she related to him?

  1. Cousin
  2. Aunt
  3. Mother
  4. Sister
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Sister

The grandfather's only son is the man's father, so the woman is the father's daughter, which makes her his sister.

Report this question

2A is B's sister. C is B's mother. D is C's father. How is A related to D?

  1. Grandmother
  2. Daughter
  3. Granddaughter
  4. Aunt
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Granddaughter

A is C's daughter, and D is C's father, so A is D's granddaughter.

Report this question

3Pointing to a man, a woman says, "His mother is the only daughter of my mother." How is the woman related to the man?

  1. Mother
  2. Grandmother
  3. Aunt
  4. Sister
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Mother

The only daughter of the woman's mother is the woman herself, so she is the man's mother.

Report this question

4A man introduces a boy as "the son of the brother of my mother." The boy is the man's:

  1. Nephew
  2. Cousin
  3. Brother
  4. Uncle
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Cousin

The mother's brother is the maternal uncle, and the uncle's son is the cousin.

Report this question

5Rohan says to Mohan, "Your mother is my mother's only daughter." How is Rohan related to Mohan?

  1. Grandfather
  2. Brother
  3. Father
  4. Maternal uncle
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Maternal uncle

Rohan's mother's only daughter is Rohan's sister, who is Mohan's mother, so Rohan is Mohan's mother's brother.

Report this question

6A is the brother of B. B is the sister of C. C is the father of D. How is A related to D?

  1. Nephew
  2. Brother
  3. Uncle
  4. Father
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Uncle

A and C are siblings, so A is the paternal uncle of D.

Report this question

7A and B are brothers. C is A's daughter. D is B's son. How is C related to D?

  1. Cousin
  2. Sister
  3. Aunt
  4. Niece
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Cousin

The children of two brothers are cousins.

8Pointing to a photograph, Ravi says, "He is the son of the only son of my grandfather." How is the person related to Ravi?

  1. Nephew
  2. Brother
  3. Uncle
  4. Cousin
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Brother

The grandfather's only son is Ravi's father, so his son is Ravi's brother.

Report this question

9A is the mother of B. C is the son of B. D is the father of A. How is D related to C?

  1. Grandfather
  2. Father
  3. Uncle
  4. Great-grandfather
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Great-grandfather

D is A's father, A is B's mother, and B is C's parent, which makes D three generations above C.

10A and B are a married couple. C is the son of A. D is the wife of C. E is the daughter of D. F is the brother of C. How is F related to E?

  1. Brother
  2. Father
  3. Uncle
  4. Cousin
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Uncle

C is E's father, and F is C's brother, so F is E's paternal uncle.

11Pointing to a man on a stage, Meena says, "He is the brother of the daughter of the wife of my husband." How is the man related to Meena?

  1. Son
  2. Husband
  3. Brother
  4. Nephew
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Son

The wife of Meena's husband is Meena, her daughter's brother is Meena's son.

12X is the son of Y, and Y is the son of Z. W is the wife of X. How is W related to Z?

  1. Daughter-in-law
  2. Granddaughter-in-law
  3. Granddaughter
  4. Niece
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Granddaughter-in-law

X is Z's grandson, so X's wife is Z's granddaughter-in-law.

13A is B's brother. C is A's father. D is C's mother. How is D related to B?

  1. Maternal grandmother
  2. Aunt
  3. Mother
  4. Paternal grandmother
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Paternal grandmother

C is the father of A and B, so C's mother D is the paternal grandmother.

14The brother of one's mother is called:

  1. Brother-in-law
  2. Paternal uncle
  3. Maternal uncle
  4. Nephew
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Maternal uncle

A relationship term, where the mother's side gives "maternal".

15One's mother's mother's only son is one's:

  1. Maternal uncle
  2. Brother
  3. Paternal uncle
  4. Father
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Maternal uncle

The mother's mother's son is the mother's brother, who is the maternal uncle.

16A is the sister of B. B is the brother of C. C is the father of D. How is A related to D?

  1. Maternal aunt
  2. Paternal aunt
  3. Mother
  4. Sister
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Paternal aunt

A and C are siblings, and C is D's father, so A is D's father's sister.

Report this question

17Statement I: The son of one's father's brother is called a cousin.
Statement II: The daughter of one's mother's sister is called a cousin.

  1. Both false
  2. I false, II true
  3. Both true
  4. I true, II false
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Both true

"cousin" covers the children of any uncle or aunt.

Report this question

18Assertion (A): Cousins can be either male or female.
Reason (R): "Cousin" denotes the child of one's uncle or aunt without specifying gender.

  1. A false, R true
  2. A true, R false
  3. Both false
  4. Both true, and R is the correct explanation of A
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Both true, and R is the correct explanation of A

The term itself has no gender.

Report this question

19Match List I with List II:
List I: (a) Paternal grandfather (b) Maternal uncle (c) Sister-in-law (d) Nephew
List II: (i) Mother's brother (ii) Brother's son (iii) Father's father (iv) Husband's sister

  1. a-iii, b-i, c-iv, d-ii
  2. a-i, b-iii, c-iv, d-ii
  3. a-iii, b-i, c-ii, d-iv
  4. a-ii, b-i, c-iii, d-iv
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. a-iii, b-i, c-iv, d-ii

Each relationship term is matched with its description.

20Arrange in order of generation, from oldest to youngest:
1. Grandson
2. Father
3. Great-grandfather
4. Grandfather

  1. 2, 1, 4, 3
  2. 3, 4, 2, 1
  3. 4, 3, 2, 1
  4. 3, 4, 1, 2
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. 3, 4, 2, 1

Great-grandfather, grandfather, father, grandson.
