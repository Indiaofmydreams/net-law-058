About this question set

This set has 19 multiple-choice questions on SWAYAMPRABHA: Complete from the UGC NET Paper 1 syllabus, unit 1, Teaching Aptitude. Try each question first, then tap Show answer to check the key and read the explanation. 19 of the 19 questions carry a written explanation.

For the concepts and case law behind these questions, read the Teaching Aptitude notes. Once you are comfortable, test yourself under exam conditions with a full-length mock test.

19 practice questions with answers

1The original number of DTH channels under SWAYAMPRABHA at its launch was:

  1. 20
  2. 32
  3. 50
  4. 10
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. 32

Direct memory: SWAYAMPRABHA launched with 32 DTH channels. This specific number has been asked in UGC NET.

Report this question

2SWAYAMPRABHA channels broadcast content:

  1. Only during examination season
  2. Only on weekdays
  3. Only for 2 hours per day
  4. 24 hours a day, 7 days a week
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. 24 hours a day, 7 days a week

Direct memory: 24×7 broadcasting. Each 4-hour slot is repeated 6 times daily to maximise reach across time zones and learner schedules.

Report this question

3The primary purpose of SWAYAMPRABHA is to:

  1. Train teachers only
  2. Reach students who do not have internet access, by delivering educational content via satellite/DTH television
  3. Provide entertainment television
  4. Broadcast government news
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Reach students who do not have internet access, by delivering educational content via satellite/DTH television

Concept: SWAYAMPRABHA addresses the digital divide specifically. Where internet fails (rural India, hilly terrain, economically poor households), a TV with DTH connection works. Critical equity tool.

Report this question

4SWAYAMPRABHA uses which satellite for broadcasting?

  1. EDUSAT
  2. GSAT-15
  3. INSAT-3C
  4. CARTOSAT
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. GSAT-15

Direct memory: SWAYAMPRABHA uses GSAT-15 satellite for Ku-band transmission. This technical detail has appeared in some NET question banks.

Report this question

5The content broadcast on SWAYAMPRABHA is sourced from:

  1. Bollywood films and entertainment
  2. State government archives
  3. Foreign universities only
  4. Courses developed by IITs, IIMs, central universities, and other SWAYAM content providers
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Courses developed by IITs, IIMs, central universities, and other SWAYAM content providers

Concept: SWAYAMPRABHA = same content as SWAYAM, delivered via DTH. The content ecosystem is shared — SWAYAM (internet) + SWAYAMPRABHA (DTH) = complementary.

Report this question

6SWAYAMPRABHA channels are managed by which organisation?

  1. Private television companies
  2. BCCI
  3. Doordarshan alone
  4. Ministry of Education through CEC (Consortium for Educational Communication) and other national coordinators
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Ministry of Education through CEC (Consortium for Educational Communication) and other national coordinators

Concept: CEC = primary coordinator for SWAYAMPRABHA. CEC has been producing educational video content since the 1990s for Doordarshan.

Report this question

7SWAYAMPRABHA's coverage includes courses for which levels?

  1. School level (9–12), undergraduate, postgraduate, and vocational
  2. Only postgraduate and research
  3. Only Class 1–8
  4. Only higher education (degree level)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. School level (9–12), undergraduate, postgraduate, and vocational

Concept: SWAYAMPRABHA mirrors SWAYAM coverage. Class 9+ to PG. Includes vocational and professional content.

Report this question

8SWAYAMPRABHA was launched in the year:

  1. 2016
  2. 2019
  3. 2015
  4. 2017
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. 2017

Direct memory: SWAYAMPRABHA was launched simultaneously with SWAYAM in 2017. Both launched on July 9, 2017.

9How does SWAYAMPRABHA address the problem of the digital divide more effectively than SWAYAM?

  1. It doesn't — SWAYAM is more effective
  2. By using DTH television which requires only a satellite dish and TV — not internet or smartphone
  3. By reducing course fees
  4. By distributing free laptops
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. By using DTH television which requires only a satellite dish and TV — not internet or smartphone

Concept: DTH penetration in India = much higher than broadband internet in rural areas. A TV set with a DTH dish is sufficient — no data cost, no smartphone needed. This is SWAYAMPRABHA's key advantage.

10The 4-hour content slot on SWAYAMPRABHA is repeated how many times per day?

  1. 2 times
  2. 8 times
  3. 6 times
  4. 4 times
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. 6 times

Direct memory: Each 4-hour content block repeats 6 times in 24 hours = 6 × 4 = 24 hours. Students can watch at any convenient time.

11Which problem of traditional distance education does SWAYAMPRABHA specifically solve?

  1. Poor examination security
  2. Absence of discussion forums
  3. Lack of printed study materials
  4. One-way passive content delivery to students who cannot attend physical classes and lack internet
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. One-way passive content delivery to students who cannot attend physical classes and lack internet

Concept: SWAYAMPRABHA's limitation = it is still one-way (broadcast, no interaction). But it solves the ACCESS problem for the internet-less student.

Report this question

12SWAYAMPRABHA channels are viewable through:

  1. Only DD Free Dish
  2. Regular cable TV only
  3. Free-to-air DTH — viewable with any DTH set-top box without subscription fee
  4. Only paid Tata Sky or Airtel DTH
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Only DD Free Dish

Concept: SWAYAMPRABHA is broadcast on DD Free Dish (Doordarshan's free DTH). No subscription needed. Free access = maximum reach to economically weaker sections.

Report this question

13The SWAYAMPRABHA initiative is most aligned with which constitutional provision?

  1. Right to Property
  2. Right to Religion
  3. Right to Education (Article 21A) and the principle of equal opportunity
  4. Right to Freedom of Speech
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Right to Education (Article 21A) and the principle of equal opportunity

Concept: Article 21A = right to education for all children 6–14. SWAYAMPRABHA extends this spirit to higher education through free, accessible content.

Report this question

14What distinguishes SWAYAMPRABHA from conventional educational television like Doordarshan?

  1. It is broadcast on dedicated educational channels exclusively for curriculum-aligned content from top institutions, available 24×7
  2. It is more entertaining
  3. It shows documentaries
  4. It is in colour
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. It is broadcast on dedicated educational channels exclusively for curriculum-aligned content from top institutions, available 24×7

Concept: Old Doordarshan education = occasional, low quality, not curriculum-aligned. SWAYAMPRABHA = dedicated, 24×7, IIT/IIM-quality, aligned with SWAYAM content.

Report this question

15Can a student earn a certificate by watching SWAYAMPRABHA alone?

  1. No — SWAYAMPRABHA is supplementary; certification requires SWAYAM enrolment and proctored exam
  2. Yes — after 100 hours of viewing
  3. Yes — watching completes the course
  4. Yes — a DTH viewing certificate is issued
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. No — SWAYAMPRABHA is supplementary; certification requires SWAYAM enrolment and proctored exam

Concept: SWAYAMPRABHA = content delivery only. It is not a standalone certification mechanism. Students must enrol on SWAYAM, complete course requirements, and pass the proctored exam.

Report this question

16The target audience that SWAYAMPRABHA is MOST specifically designed for is:

  1. Rural, semi-urban, and economically disadvantaged students without internet access
  2. NRI students abroad
  3. Foreign students studying in India
  4. Urban students with high-speed internet
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Rural, semi-urban, and economically disadvantaged students without internet access

Concept: SWAYAMPRABHA's raison d'être = serve the last mile. Students in aspirational districts, tribal areas, and economically marginalised communities who are excluded from internet-based platforms.

Report this question

17SWAYAMPRABHA's content is primarily in which language(s)?

  1. Hindi only
  2. Only regional languages
  3. English only
  4. Primarily English and Hindi, with growing content in regional languages
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Primarily English and Hindi, with growing content in regional languages

Concept: Most SWAYAM/SWAYAMPRABHA content = English. Hindi content growing. Regional language content being developed — a priority under NEP 2020 for mother tongue instruction.

Report this question

18Which of the following is a major limitation of SWAYAMPRABHA as an educational tool?

  1. It is one-way — no interaction, no doubt-clearing, no feedback possible during broadcast
  2. It lacks content from reputed institutions
  3. It is only available in urban areas
  4. It charges high fees
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. It is one-way — no interaction, no doubt-clearing, no feedback possible during broadcast

Concept: Television = passive medium. Student watches but cannot ask questions. No ZPD, no scaffolding, no social learning during broadcast. Students must go online for interaction.

Report this question

19Which combination MOST comprehensively addresses the educational access problem in India?

  1. SWAYAM (internet-based) + SWAYAMPRABHA (DTH-based) together — covering both connected and unconnected learners
  2. NPTEL alone
  3. SWAYAM alone
  4. SWAYAMPRABHA alone
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. SWAYAM (internet-based) + SWAYAMPRABHA (DTH-based) together — covering both connected and unconnected learners

Concept: The two are designed as complementary. SWAYAM = for those with internet. SWAYAMPRABHA = for those without. Together = near-universal coverage of Indian student population.
