About this question set

This set has 15 multiple-choice questions on Emergency Provisions from the UGC NET Law (058) syllabus, unit 2, Constitutional and Administrative Law. Try each question first, then tap Show answer to check the key and read the explanation. 15 of the 15 questions carry a written explanation.

For the concepts and case law behind these questions, read the Constitutional & Administrative Law notes. Once you are comfortable, test yourself under exam conditions with a full-length mock test.

15 practice questions with answers

1During a National Emergency (war/external aggression), Article 358 suspends Article 19 rights:

  1. On Presidential order specifying which rights
  2. Only in affected border states
  3. Automatically but only as regards laws inconsistent with Article 19 made during Emergency
  4. Automatically and absolutely for the duration
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Automatically but only as regards laws inconsistent with Article 19 made during Emergency

Article 358 operates automatically during war/external aggression emergency but only saves laws MADE DURING the Emergency. Pre-existing laws that violate Article 19 are not automatically saved by Article 358.

2Article 359 differs from Article 358 in that:

  1. Article 359 relates to financial emergency only
  2. Article 359 requires a Presidential Order specifying which FRs (other than Arts. 20 & 21) can be suspended — it is not automatic
  3. Article 359 is permanent
  4. Article 359 applies only to internal emergency
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Article 359 requires a Presidential Order specifying which FRs (other than Arts. 20 & 21) can be suspended — it is not automatic

Article 358 operates automatically (only for Art. 19, only during war/external aggression). Article 359 requires the President to issue an Order specifying which Fundamental Rights (excluding Arts. 20 & 21) shall not be enforceable. It is discretionary and specific.

Report this question

3Which Articles of the Constitution CANNOT be suspended even during National Emergency by Presidential Order under Article 359?

  1. Articles 21 and 22
  2. Articles 19 and 21
  3. Articles 14 and 15
  4. Articles 20 and 21
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Articles 20 and 21

The 44th Amendment, 1978 amended Article 359 to exclude Articles 20 and 21 from the President's power to suspend enforcement of Fundamental Rights during Emergency. Articles 20 (protection from ex post facto laws, double jeopardy, self-incrimination) and 21 (right to life) can NEVER be suspended.

Report this question

4During a National Emergency, Parliament acquires power to legislate on:

  1. Only Union List subjects
  2. Both Union List and State List subjects — Article 250
  3. All three Lists with extra powers
  4. Residuary subjects only
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Both Union List and State List subjects — Article 250

Under Article 250, while a proclamation of National Emergency is in operation, Parliament has power to make laws with respect to any matter in the State List. This extends Parliamentary legislative power significantly during Emergency.

Report this question

5During National Emergency, the President can modify constitutional provisions relating to distribution of revenues under:

  1. Article 354
  2. Article 268
  3. Article 360
  4. Article 280
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Article 354

Article 354 enables the President to direct that all or any provisions of Articles 268–279 (dealing with distribution of revenues between Union and States) shall apply with modifications or exceptions during a National Emergency.

Report this question

6The maximum period for which President's Rule can operate without fresh Parliamentary approval is:

  1. 1 year
  2. 6 months
  3. 3 years
  4. 2 months
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. 6 months

A proclamation under Article 356 must be approved by Parliament within 2 months. Once approved, it operates for 6 months. It can be renewed every 6 months up to a maximum of 3 years (beyond 1 year only with special conditions under Article 356(4) & (5)).

Report this question

7Under Article 356, President's Rule beyond ONE YEAR requires, in addition to Parliamentary approval:

  1. Supreme Court certification
  2. Both (A) or (B) — either condition must be satisfied
  3. A recommendation by the Election Commission that elections cannot be held in that State
  4. A declaration of National Emergency in the whole of India or the concerned State
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Both (A) or (B) — either condition must be satisfied

Article 356(4) and (5) provide that beyond one year, President's Rule can only be extended if either: (a) a National Emergency is in operation in the whole or part of the concerned State; OR (b) the Election Commission certifies that elections to the Legislative Assembly of the State cannot be held.

Report this question

8The absolute maximum period for which President's Rule can be imposed in a State is:

  1. 3 years
  2. 1 year
  3. 2 years
  4. 5 years
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. 3 years

President's Rule under Article 356 can be imposed for a maximum of 3 years. After 3 years, fresh elections must be held. Extension beyond 1 year requires special conditions under Article 356(4) & (5).

Report this question

9In S.R. Bommai v. Union of India (1994), the Supreme Court held that the Proclamation under Article 356 is subject to judicial review on which ground?

  1. President has no discretion in issuing it
  2. Mala fide exercise or based on wholly irrelevant grounds — courts can examine the material
  3. It violates Directive Principles
  4. It is void unless confirmed by Supreme Court
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Mala fide exercise or based on wholly irrelevant grounds — courts can examine the material

S.R. Bommai (1994) — 9-judge bench held: (1) Art. 356 proclamation is justiciable; (2) President cannot dissolve Assembly until Parliament approves (suspended animation); (3) Secularism is basic structure; (4) Floor of the House is the sole test of majority; (5) Courts can examine whether material before President was relevant. Concept note: S.R. Bommai (1994) is one of the most important cases on federalism. It effectively curtailed arbitrary use of Article 356 and upheld federalism as a basic structure of the Constitution.

10'Suspended animation' of State Legislative Assembly during President's Rule means:

  1. The Assembly is dissolved immediately
  2. The Assembly is dissolved after 6 months
  3. The Assembly is neither dissolved nor allowed to function — kept in suspended state pending Parliamentary approval
  4. The Assembly is reconstituted
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. The Assembly is neither dissolved nor allowed to function — kept in suspended state pending Parliamentary approval

S.R. Bommai held that until Parliament approves the proclamation under Article 356, the President cannot dissolve the State Legislative Assembly. It is put in 'suspended animation' — neither dissolved nor operative. If Parliament disapproves, the Assembly revives.

11Financial Emergency under Article 360 can be proclaimed when:

  1. A State refuses to pay taxes
  2. GDP falls below a threshold
  3. There is a balance of payments crisis
  4. The financial stability of India or any part thereof is threatened
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. The financial stability of India or any part thereof is threatened

Article 360 provides that the President may proclaim a Financial Emergency if he is satisfied that the financial stability or credit of India or of any part of the territory thereof is threatened.

Report this question

12During Financial Emergency, the President can direct reduction of salaries and allowances of:

  1. Only Union government employees
  2. All government servants including Supreme Court and High Court judges
  3. Only non-judicial government servants
  4. Only State employees
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. All government servants including Supreme Court and High Court judges

Article 360(4)(b) uniquely allows reduction of salaries and allowances of all persons serving in connection with the affairs of the Union including the judges of the Supreme Court and High Courts during Financial Emergency. Financial Emergency has never been proclaimed in India.

Report this question

13Under Article 356, which of the following powers does the President NOT acquire during President's Rule?

  1. Power to dissolve the High Court of the State
  2. Power to suspend constitutional provisions relating to any body in the State
  3. Power to declare that Parliament can legislate on State List subjects
  4. Power to assume functions of State government
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Power to dissolve the High Court of the State

During President's Rule, the President takes over the executive functions of the State government and Parliament can legislate on State subjects. However, the High Courts continue to function — they are constitutional courts established under Articles 214-231 and are not affected by Article 356.

Report this question

14Assertion (A): National Emergency once proclaimed continues indefinitely unless revoked. Reason (R): Parliamentary approval by special majority is required every six months for continuation.

  1. Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A
  2. Both A and R are true but R is not the correct explanation
  3. A is true but R is false
  4. A is false but R is true
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. A is false but R is true

A is false because National Emergency does NOT continue indefinitely — it requires 6-monthly renewal by special majority. R is true — the mechanism is 6-monthly renewal by special majority under Article 352(6). A is the wrong statement; R is the correct rule.

Report this question

15After the 44th Amendment, 1978, which two Fundamental Rights CANNOT be suspended even during National Emergency?

  1. Articles 14 and 19
  2. Articles 21 and 22
  3. Articles 19 and 21
  4. Articles 20 and 21
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Articles 20 and 21

The 44th Amendment, 1978 amended Article 359 to provide that the President cannot suspend the enforcement of Articles 20 and 21 even during a National Emergency. This was a direct response to the abuse of emergency powers during 1975-77.
