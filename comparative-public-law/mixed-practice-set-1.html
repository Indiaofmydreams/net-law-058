About this question set

This set has 18 multiple-choice questions on Comparative Public Law from the UGC NET Law (058) syllabus, unit 10, Comparative Public Law and Systems of Governance. Try each question first, then tap Show answer to check the key and read the explanation. 18 of the 18 questions carry a written explanation.

For the concepts and case law behind these questions, read the Comparative Public Law notes. Once you are comfortable, test yourself under exam conditions with a full-length mock test.

18 practice questions with answers

1The first modern federation is considered to be:

  1. USA
  2. Canada
  3. Switzerland
  4. India
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. USA

The US Constitution, 1787.

Report this question

2Residuary powers in Canada rest with:

  1. Courts
  2. Federal Parliament
  3. Provinces
  4. Governor General
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Federal Parliament

A strong-centre federation.

Report this question

3Montesquieu's 'Spirit of the Laws' propounded:

  1. Rule of law
  2. Federalism
  3. Separation of powers
  4. Judicial review
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Separation of powers

Published in 1748.

Report this question

4In a parliamentary system the executive is responsible to:

  1. Legislature
  2. President
  3. Judiciary
  4. Voters directly
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Legislature

Collective responsibility to the House.

Report this question

5Dicey's three meanings of the rule of law include:

  1. Administrative courts
  2. Executive discretion
  3. Supremacy of regular law
  4. Natural rights
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Supremacy of regular law

Also equality before law and constitution as result of ordinary law.

Report this question

6The formal version of the rule of law is associated with:

  1. Joseph Raz
  2. Rawls
  3. Fuller only
  4. Dworkin
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Joseph Raz

Raz: The Rule of Law and Its Virtue.

Report this question

7Judicial review in the USA began with:

  1. Brown v. Board
  2. Dred Scott
  3. McCulloch v. Maryland
  4. Marbury v. Madison
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Marbury v. Madison

Decided in 1803.

Report this question

8France's Conseil constitutionnel conducts:

  1. Review by Senate
  2. Only post-enactment review
  3. No review
  4. Pre-promulgation review
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Pre-promulgation review

A priori abstract review.

Report this question

9A US constitutional amendment needs ratification by:

  1. Three-fourths of States
  2. Half of States
  3. Two-thirds of States
  4. Governors
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Three-fourths of States

After proposal by two-thirds of Congress.

Report this question

10The institution of Ombudsman originated in:

  1. UK
  2. USA
  3. Sweden
  4. India
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Sweden

1809.

Report this question

11UK's Parliamentary Commissioner for Administration was created in:

  1. 1967
  2. 1809
  3. 2005
  4. 1950
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. 1967

Parliamentary Commissioner Act, 1967.

Report this question

12India's Lokpal and Lokayuktas Act was enacted in:

  1. 2011
  2. 2013
  3. 1993
  4. 2005
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. 2013

Received assent in 2014 and came in force.

Report this question

13India's RTI Act was enacted in:

  1. 2005
  2. 1972
  3. 2013
  4. 2000
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. 2005

Right to Information Act, 2005.

Report this question

14US Freedom of Information Act was enacted in:

  1. 2005
  2. 1974
  3. 1946
  4. 1966
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. 1966

FOIA, 1966.

15The UK Supreme Court was established under the:

  1. HRA 1998
  2. Magna Carta
  3. Act of Settlement
  4. Constitutional Reform Act, 2005
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Constitutional Reform Act, 2005

It began work in 2009.

Report this question

16PIL jurisprudence was pioneered in India by Justices:

  1. P.N. Bhagwati and V.R. Krishna Iyer
  2. H.R. Khanna and Beg
  3. Chandrachud and Reddy
  4. Sikri and Hegde
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. P.N. Bhagwati and V.R. Krishna Iyer

S.P. Gupta (1981) widened locus standi.

Report this question

17The functional method is associated with comparative law scholars:

  1. Austin and Hart
  2. Kelsen and Pound
  3. Zweigert and Kötz
  4. Dicey and Bagehot
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Zweigert and Kötz

They emphasise comparing legal solutions to common problems.

Report this question

18Germany's 'eternity clause' is in:

  1. Article 79(3) Basic Law
  2. Article 1
  3. Article 93
  4. Article 20
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Article 79(3) Basic Law

It bars amendments affecting basic principles.
