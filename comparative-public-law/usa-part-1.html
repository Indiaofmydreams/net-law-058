About this question set

This set has 21 multiple-choice questions on USA from the UGC NET Law (058) syllabus, unit 10, Comparative Public Law and Systems of Governance. Try each question first, then tap Show answer to check the key. This set is an answer-key drill: check each answer, then revisit the notes for any you miss.

1Judicial review in the USA was established by the Supreme Court in:

  1. Gibbons v. Ogden
  2. McCulloch v. Maryland
  3. Marbury v. Madison
  4. Dred Scott v. Sandford
Show answer

Answer: C. Marbury v. Madison

2The judicial power of the United States is vested in one Supreme Court under:

  1. Article I
  2. Article III
  3. Article II
  4. Article V
Show answer

Answer: B. Article III

3The Commerce Clause of the US Constitution is found in:

  1. Article I, Section 8, Clause 3
  2. Article I, Section 9
  3. Article II, Section 2
  4. Article III, Section 2

  1. Tenth Amendment

  1. Simple majority of the states

16Dred Scott v. Sandford (1857) held that:
