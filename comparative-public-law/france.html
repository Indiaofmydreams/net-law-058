About this question set

This set has 10 multiple-choice questions on France from the UGC NET Law (058) syllabus, unit 10, Comparative Public Law and Systems of Governance. Try each question first, then tap Show answer to check the key. This set is an answer-key drill: check each answer, then revisit the notes for any you miss.

For the concepts and case law behind these questions, read the Comparative Public Law notes. Once you are comfortable, test yourself under exam conditions with a full-length mock test.

10 practice questions with answers

1The Constitution of the Fifth French Republic was adopted in:

  1. 1958
  2. 1946
  3. 1875
  4. 1971
Show answer

Answer: A. 1958

Report this question

2The body exercising constitutional review in France is the:

  1. Cour de Cassation
  2. Conseil d'État
  3. National Assembly
  4. Conseil constitutionnel
Show answer

Answer: D. Conseil constitutionnel

Report this question

3The apex court of French administrative law (droit administratif) is the:

  1. Cour de Cassation
  2. Conseil d'État
  3. Conseil constitutionnel
  4. Tribunal des Conflits
Show answer

Answer: B. Conseil d'État

Report this question

4Emergency powers of the French President are provided in:

  1. Article 5
  2. Article 49
  3. Article 16
  4. Article 89
Show answer

Answer: C. Article 16

Report this question

5Article 49(3) of the French Constitution allows the Government to:

  1. Adopt a bill without a vote unless a censure motion passes
  2. Dissolve Parliament
  3. Declare emergency
  4. Amend the Constitution
Show answer

Answer: A. Adopt a bill without a vote unless a censure motion passes

Report this question

6The 1971 Liberté d'association decision of the Conseil constitutionnel:

  1. Abolished the Senate
  2. Created the Conseil d'État
  3. Introduced presidential elections
  4. Gave constitutional value to the Preamble (bloc de constitutionnalité)
Show answer

Answer: D. Gave constitutional value to the Preamble (bloc de constitutionnalité)

Report this question

7The Question prioritaire de constitutionnalité (QPC) was introduced by the:

  1. 1958 Constitution
  2. 2008 constitutional revision
  3. 1962 referendum
  4. 1946 Constitution
Show answer

Answer: B. 2008 constitutional revision

Report this question

8The Blanco decision (1873) established:

  1. Judicial review of laws
  2. The autonomy of administrative law and state liability
  3. Presidential powers
  4. The Senate's role
Show answer

Answer: B. The autonomy of administrative law and state liability

Report this question

9"Cohabitation" refers to a situation where:

  1. The President and Prime Minister belong to opposing political camps
  2. The Senate and Assembly differ
  3. Two Presidents serve
  4. Parliament is dissolved
Show answer

Answer: A. The President and Prime Minister belong to opposing political camps

Report this question

10Article 61 of the Constitution provides for:

  1. Emergency powers
  2. Referendum
  3. Impeachment
  4. Review of laws by the Conseil constitutionnel before promulgation
Show answer

Answer: D. Review of laws by the Conseil constitutionnel before promulgation
