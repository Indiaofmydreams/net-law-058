About this question set

This set has 20 multiple-choice questions on USA from the UGC NET Law (058) syllabus, unit 10, Comparative Public Law and Systems of Governance. Try each question first, then tap Show answer to check the key. This set is an answer-key drill: check each answer, then revisit the notes for any you miss.

1New York v. United States (1992) held that Congress cannot:

  1. Tax states
  2. Regulate waste
  3. Commandeer state legislatures to enact a federal regulatory programme
  4. Use the Commerce Clause
Show answer

Answer: C. Commandeer state legislatures to enact a federal regulatory programme

2Seminole Tribe v. Florida (1996) held that Congress cannot, under the Indian Commerce Clause:

  1. Regulate tribes
  2. Abrogate state sovereign immunity
  3. Tax tribes
  4. Make treaties

Answer: B. Abrogate state sovereign immunity

3Heart of Atlanta Motel v. United States (1964) upheld Title II of the Civil Rights Act under the:
