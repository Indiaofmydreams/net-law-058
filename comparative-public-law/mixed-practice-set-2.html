About this question set

This set has 18 multiple-choice questions on Comparative Public Law from the UGC NET Law (058) syllabus, unit 10, Comparative Public Law and Systems of Governance. Try each question first, then tap Show answer to check the key and read the explanation. 18 of the 18 questions carry a written explanation.

For the concepts and case law behind these questions, read the Comparative Public Law notes. Once you are comfortable, test yourself under exam conditions with a full-length mock test.

18 practice questions with answers

1Parliamentary sovereignty is a feature of the:

  1. UK
  2. South Africa
  3. USA
  4. Switzerland
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. UK

Dicey's central principle.

Report this question

2Federalism was held part of India's basic structure in:

  1. Kihoto Hollohan
  2. Berubari
  3. S.R. Bommai v. Union of India
  4. Golaknath
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. S.R. Bommai v. Union of India

1994.

Report this question

3Constitutional supremacy differs from parliamentary sovereignty because:

  1. Courts can invalidate laws
  2. No court review
  3. Parliament is supreme
  4. Executive is supreme
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Courts can invalidate laws

Constitution is the higher law.

Report this question

4The essence of federalism is:

  1. Presidential rule
  2. Single legislature
  3. Division of powers between levels
  4. Judicial supremacy
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Division of powers between levels

Two levels with their own spheres.

Report this question

5Judicial activism refers to:

  1. Judicial restraint
  2. A proactive judicial role in rights and policy
  3. Jury trial
  4. Executive control
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. A proactive judicial role in rights and policy

Courts shaping public policy.

Report this question

6Open government means:

  1. Transparency and public access to information
  2. Judicial rule
  3. Secret governance
  4. Presidential rule
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Transparency and public access to information

Basis of RTI and FOI laws.
