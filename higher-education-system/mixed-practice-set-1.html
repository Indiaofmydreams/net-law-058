About this question set

This set has 21 multiple-choice questions on Higher Education System from the UGC NET Paper 1 syllabus, unit 10, Higher Education System. Try each question first, then tap Show answer to check the key and read the explanation. 21 of the 21 questions carry a written explanation.

For the concepts and case law behind these questions, read the Higher Education System notes. Once you are comfortable, test yourself under exam conditions with a full-length mock test.

21 practice questions with answers

1Nalanda, an ancient centre of higher learning, was located in:

  1. Tamil Nadu
  2. Punjab
  3. Gujarat
  4. Bihar
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Bihar

Nalanda Mahavihara, in present-day Bihar.

Report this question

2Takshashila (Taxila) was an ancient centre of learning in present-day:

  1. Nepal
  2. Pakistan
  3. Sri Lanka
  4. Bhutan
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Pakistan

Taxila lies near Rawalpindi.

Report this question

3The UGC Act was passed in:

  1. 1948
  2. 1953
  3. 1956
  4. 1986
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. 1956

UGC became a statutory body in 1956.

4NAAC is responsible for:

  1. Funding schools
  2. Teacher recruitment
  3. Assessment and accreditation of institutions
  4. Conducting NET
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Assessment and accreditation of institutions

National Assessment and Accreditation Council.

5NEP 2020 proposed a single umbrella regulator for higher education called:

  1. HECI
  2. UGC only
  3. NCTE only
  4. AICTE only
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. HECI

Higher Education Commission of India.

6AICTE regulates:

  2. Teacher education only
  3. Technical education

9Consider the statements: 1. NCTE regulates teacher education. 2. The Bar Council of India regulates medical education.

  1. Only statement 1 is correct
  2. Only statement 2 is correct
  3. Both statements are correct
  4. Neither statement is correct
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Only statement 1 is correct

Medical education is regulated by the medical commission.

10Assertion (A): NAAC accreditation promotes quality assurance.
Reason (R): It evaluates institutions against defined criteria.

  1. Both A and R are true, and R is the correct explanation of A
  2. Both A and R are true, but R is not the correct explanation of A
  3. A is true, but R is false
  4. A is false, but R is true
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Both A and R are true, and R is the correct explanation of A

R explains A.
