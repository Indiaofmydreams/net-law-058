15 practice questions with answers

1The defining feature of distance education is:

  1. Physical separation of teacher and learner with mediated communication
  2. Teacher and learner in the same classroom
  3. Compulsory daily attendance
  4. A fixed timetable for all learners
Show answer

Answer: A. Physical separation of teacher and learner with mediated communication

Report this question

2"Openness" in open learning mainly refers to:

  1. Free tuition
  2. Absence of examinations
  3. Removal of barriers related to entry qualification, age, place and pace
  4. Classes held outdoors
Show answer

Answer: C. Removal of barriers related to entry qualification, age, place and pace

3A key advantage of the ODL mode over the regular mode is:

  1. Flexibility of time, place and pace
  2. Limited to young learners
  3. Fixed academic calendar
  4. Compulsory attendance
