8 practice questions with answers

1Before the 42nd Amendment (1976), education was primarily a subject in the:

  1. Residuary list
  2. Union List
  3. Concurrent List
  4. State List
Show answer

Answer: D. State List

Report this question

2In a conflict between a Union law and a State law on a Concurrent List subject, Article 254 generally provides that:

  1. Both are void
  2. The Union law prevails
  3. The State law prevails always
  4. The High Court decides which prevails
Show answer

Answer: B. The Union law prevails

Report this question

3Distribution of legislative powers is provided in Article:

  1. 200
  2. 246
  3. 356
  4. 226
Show answer

Answer: B. 246

Report this question

4Entry 64 of the Union List relates to:

  1. Institutions for scientific or technical education declared by Parliament to be of national importance
  2. Primary schools
  3. Private colleges
  4. State universities
Show answer

Answer: A. Institutions for scientific or technical education declared by Parliament to be of national importance

Report this question

5Entry 65 of the Union List relates to:

  1. Primary education
  2. Religious schools
  3. Libraries of States
  4. Union agencies and institutions for professional, vocational or technical training, including training of police officers
Show answer

Answer: D. Union agencies and institutions for professional, vocational or technical training, including training of police officers

Report this question

6Entry 32 of the State List relates to:

  1. Foreign trade
  2. Atomic energy
  3. Incorporation and regulation of universities, other than those in the Union List
  4. Defence
Show answer

Answer: C. Incorporation and regulation of universities, other than those in the Union List

Report this question

7A consequence of education being a Concurrent subject is that:

  1. Only the States can legislate on it
  2. Only the Union can legislate on it
  3. Both the Union and the States can legislate on it
  4. Only courts can legislate on it
Show answer

Answer: C. Both the Union and the States can legislate on it

Report this question

8Under Article 254(2), a State law on a Concurrent subject can prevail in that State if it:

  1. Is reserved for the President and receives assent
  2. Is approved by UGC
  3. Is approved by the Supreme Court
  4. Is passed unanimously
Show answer

Answer: A. Is reserved for the President and receives assent
