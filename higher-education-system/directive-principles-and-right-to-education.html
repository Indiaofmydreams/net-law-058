This set has 14 multiple-choice questions on Directive Principles and Right to Education from the UGC NET Paper 1 syllabus, unit 10, Higher Education System. 14 practice questions with answers

1Article 41 directs the State to make effective provision for the right to work, education and public assistance within the limits of its:

  1. Population
  2. Economic capacity and development
  3. Literacy rate
  4. Area
Show answer

Answer: B. Economic capacity and development

2The original Article 45 directed free and compulsory education for children up to age 14 within how many years of the Constitution's commencement?

  1. 5
  2. 10
  3. 20
  4. 15
Show answer

Answer: B. 10

3After the 86th Amendment, Article 45 provides for:

  1. Adult education
  2. Higher education
  3. Early childhood care and education for children below six
  4. Vocational education

4Article 46 directs the State to promote the educational and economic interests of:

  1. Only women
  2. Only minorities
  3. SCs, STs and other weaker sections
  4. Only the elderly

  2. Tribal children only
  3. Girls only
  4. Linguistic minority groups

7Directive Principles of State Policy are:

  1. Amendable only by the States
  2. Non-justiciable
  3. Enforceable by courts as fundamental rights
  4. Part of Schedule VII

  2. Develop in a healthy manner and in conditions of freedom and dignity
