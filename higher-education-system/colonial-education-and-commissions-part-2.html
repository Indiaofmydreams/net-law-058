This set has 16 multiple-choice questions on Colonial Education and Commissions from the UGC NET Paper 1 syllabus, unit 10, Higher Education System. 16 practice questions with answers

1The Sadler Commission recommended that the Intermediate stage be:

  1. Replaced by technical courses only
  2. Separated from the university and put under Intermediate colleges
  3. Made a part of primary education
  4. Abolished completely
Show answer

Answer: B. Separated from the university and put under Intermediate colleges

  1. 1921

  3. 1902
