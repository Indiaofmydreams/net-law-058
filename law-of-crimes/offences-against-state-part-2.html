About this question set

This set has 17 multiple-choice questions on Offences Against State from the UGC NET Law (058) syllabus, unit 4, Law of Crimes. Try each question first, then tap Show answer to check the key. This set is an answer-key drill: check each answer, then revisit the notes for any you miss.

17 practice questions with answers

1Under BNS 2023, the terrorist act (Section 113) punishment for causing death is:

  1. Minimum 10 years only
  2. Death or imprisonment for life, and also liable to fine
  3. Rigorous imprisonment of 7 years
  4. Life imprisonment without fine
Show answer

Answer: B. Death or imprisonment for life, and also liable to fine

2The National Security Act 1980 (NSA) permits preventive detention for a maximum of:

  1. 6 months only
  2. 3 years without review
  3. Indefinite detention without review
  4. 12 months at a time (extendable), with Advisory Board reviewing within 7 weeks
Show answer

Answer: D. 12 months at a time (extendable), with Advisory Board reviewing within 7 weeks

8Assertion (A): Sedition as a word has been completely removed from Indian statutory criminal law by BNS 2023.   1. Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A
  2. Both A and R are true but R is NOT the correct explanation of A

  1. Detained person must be informed within 24 hours
  2. Maximum detention is 1 month without review

16The distinction between 'waging war' under Indian law (Section 121 IPC/Section 147 BNS) and English 'treason' is:

  1. India does not use the term 'treason' — Section 121 IPC covers waging war against the Government of India including levying war, attempting to wage war, or abetting such acts
  2. They are identical
  3. Treason requires betrayal to a foreign power; Section 121 only covers internal war
  4. Section 121 IPC is narrower than English treason law
Show answer

Answer: A. India does not use the term 'treason' — Section 121 IPC covers waging war against the Government of India including levying war, attempting to wage war, or abetting such acts
