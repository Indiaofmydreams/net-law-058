This set has 20 multiple-choice questions on Offences Against Property from the UGC NET Law (058) syllabus, unit 4, Law of Crimes. Try each question first, then tap Show answer to check the key. This set is an answer-key drill: check each answer, then revisit the notes for any you miss.

  2. Section 379

3The essential ingredients of theft include:

  1. Dishonest intention + moving of property + without consent
  2. Force + property + consent
  3. Deception + property + gain

  1. 2 or more persons

  2. Mere extortion

9For the offence of theft, moving of property is essential. This means:

  1. The property must be completely removed from the premises
  2. The property must be concealed
  3. Even the slightest movement of the property constitutes the act element of theft
  4. The property must be sold
Show answer

Answer: C. Even the slightest movement of the property constitutes the act element of theft

10Criminal breach of trust under IPC Section 405 requires:

  1. Force + property + dishonest intent
  2. Deception + property
  3. Entrustment of property + dishonest misappropriation or conversion
  4. Property + absence of consent
Show answer

Answer: C. Entrustment of property + dishonest misappropriation or conversion

12The offence of extortion under IPC differs from robbery in that:

  1. They are identical
  2. In extortion, fear is used to make the person deliver property; in robbery, force or fear is used at the time of taking
  3. Robbery requires a weapon; extortion does not
  4. Extortion is a bailable offence; robbery is not
Show answer

Answer: B. In extortion, fear is used to make the person deliver property; in robbery, force or fear is used at the time of taking

15Dishonest misappropriation of property found (Section 403 IPC) requires that:

  1. The person stole the property first
  2. The property must be government property
  3. The person received it through entrustment
  4. The person finds property and has reason to believe it belongs to someone, yet keeps it with dishonest intention
Show answer

Answer: D. The person finds property and has reason to believe it belongs to someone, yet keeps it with dishonest intention

16In dacoity, if one of the five persons is acquitted, the remaining four:

  1. Cannot be convicted of dacoity since the minimum number of 5 persons is no longer established
  2. Can still be convicted of dacoity individually
  3. Are automatically acquitted as well
  4. Can be convicted of dacoity by the court's discretion
Show answer

Answer: A. Cannot be convicted of dacoity since the minimum number of 5 persons is no longer established

17The essential difference between criminal misappropriation (Section 403 IPC) and criminal breach of trust (Section 405 IPC) is:

  1. They are identical offences
  2. Criminal misappropriation does NOT require prior entrustment; criminal breach of trust REQUIRES prior lawful entrustment
  3. Criminal breach of trust does not require dishonesty
  4. Criminal misappropriation requires force
Show answer

Answer: B. Criminal misappropriation does NOT require prior entrustment; criminal breach of trust REQUIRES prior lawful entrustment

18Under BNS 2023, cheating by personation is specifically addressed under:

  1. Section 318 BNS
  2. Section 320 BNS
  3. Section 316 BNS
  4. Section 319 BNS
Show answer

19Under BNS 2023, snatching (Section 304) is defined as:

  1. A form of robbery
  2. A form of dacoity
  3. Identical to theft under Section 303 BNS
  4. Theft of movable property from the person of another by suddenly, quickly, or forcibly grabbing, taking, or removing such property

Answer: D. Theft of movable property from the person of another by suddenly, quickly, or forcibly grabbing, taking, or removing such property

20Under BNS 2023, the maximum punishment for dacoity (Section 310) is:

  1. Death penalty in all cases
  2. Simple imprisonment of 7 years
  3. Rigorous imprisonment for life, or rigorous imprisonment up to 10 years, and also liable to fine
  4. Rigorous imprisonment of 14 years maximum
