4In Virsa Singh v. State of Punjab (1958), the Supreme Court laid down the test for the third clause of Section 300 IPC (murder). The test requires:

  1. The accused must have intended death specifically
  2. The injury must be fatal in all cases without exception
  3. The accused must have used a weapon
  4. A bodily injury was caused; the injury was intentionally inflicted; it was sufficient in the ordinary course of nature to cause death - determined OBJECTIVELY

10Culpable homicide is said to be committed with knowledge (and not intention) when:

  1. The person desires the death of the victim
