About this question set

This set has 16 multiple-choice questions on Drug Trafficking and Counterfeiting from the UGC NET Law (058) syllabus, unit 4, Law of Crimes. Try each question first, then tap Show answer to check the key. This set is an answer-key drill: check each answer, then revisit the notes for any you miss.

16 practice questions with answers

1Under NDPS Act 1985, the minimum punishment for offences involving 'commercial quantity' is:

  1. Simple imprisonment of 5 years
  2. Rigorous imprisonment of 10 years extendable to 20 years with fine not less than Rs. 1 lakh extendable to Rs. 2 lakhs
  3. Rigorous imprisonment of 3 years only
  4. Fine only
Show answer

Answer: B. Rigorous imprisonment of 10 years extendable to 20 years with fine not less than Rs. 1 lakh extendable to Rs. 2 lakhs

Report this question

2Under NDPS Act 1985, 'small quantity' of a narcotic drug is significant because:

  1. Punishment for small quantity is rigorous imprisonment up to 6 months or fine up to Rs. 10,000 or both — far less than commercial quantity offences
  2. Small quantity attracts death penalty
  3. Small quantity offences are non-cognizable
  4. Small quantity is not punishable under NDPS Act
Show answer

Answer: A. Punishment for small quantity is rigorous imprisonment up to 6 months or fine up to Rs. 10,000 or both — far less than commercial quantity offences

Report this question

3Under NDPS Act 1985, Section 42 empowers which authority to enter, search, seize and arrest WITHOUT a warrant?

  1. Only the police
  2. Any officer of a gazetted rank of the departments of central excise, narcotics, customs, revenue intelligence, or any other department notified by the Central Government
  3. Only the NIA
  4. Only a Magistrate
Show answer

Answer: B. Any officer of a gazetted rank of the departments of central excise, narcotics, customs, revenue intelligence, or any other department notified by the Central Government

Report this question

4The Supreme Court in State of Punjab v. Baldev Singh (1999) held regarding NDPS searches that:

  1. Section 50 NDPS is merely directory, not mandatory
  2. Police can search without informing the accused of any rights
  3. The requirement of informing the accused of their right to be searched before a gazetted officer or Magistrate (Section 50) is mandatory and its violation vitiates the conviction
  4. Only female officers can conduct personal searches
Show answer

Answer: C. The requirement of informing the accused of their right to be searched before a gazetted officer or Magistrate (Section 50) is mandatory and its violation vitiates the conviction

Report this question

5Counterfeiting of Government stamps is an offence under IPC. The BNS 2023 equivalent of Section 255 IPC (counterfeiting Government stamp) is approximately at:

  1. Section 178 BNS
  2. Section 200 BNS
  3. Section 336 BNS
  4. Section 229 BNS
Show answer

Answer: D. Section 229 BNS

Report this question

6Under NDPS Act 1985, the Special Court trying commercial quantity cases must complete trial within:

  1. 6 months always
  2. 3 months
  3. One year from the date of filing of the charge sheet, extendable for reasons to be recorded
  4. No time limit prescribed
Show answer

Answer: C. One year from the date of filing of the charge sheet, extendable for reasons to be recorded

Report this question

7Which of the following is NOT covered under the definition of 'narcotic drug' under NDPS Act 1985?

  1. Cocaine
  2. Opium
  3. Cannabis
  4. Alcohol (ethyl alcohol)
Show answer

Answer: D. Alcohol (ethyl alcohol)

Report this question

8The Supreme Court in Tofan Singh v. State of Tamil Nadu (2021) held regarding statements under Section 67 NDPS Act that:

  1. Confessions to NCB officers are fully admissible
  2. Section 67 statements are admissible as they override Evidence Act
  3. Officers under NDPS Act are 'police officers' and therefore confessions made to them are inadmissible under Section 25 of the Indian Evidence Act
  4. Only written confessions under Section 67 are admissible
Show answer

Answer: C. Officers under NDPS Act are 'police officers' and therefore confessions made to them are inadmissible under Section 25 of the Indian Evidence Act

Report this question

9Under BNS 2023, Section 179 deals with:

  1. Making counterfeiting instruments
  2. Using as genuine a forged or counterfeit currency note or bank note — punishable with life imprisonment or up to 10 years RI and fine
  3. Possession of counterfeit currency
  4. Delivering counterfeit currency
Show answer

Answer: B. Using as genuine a forged or counterfeit currency note or bank note — punishable with life imprisonment or up to 10 years RI and fine

Report this question

10The NDPS (Amendment) Act 2014 amended Section 27A NDPS to:

  1. Reduce punishment for first-time offenders
  2. Abolish the death penalty for drug offences
  3. Include harbouring offenders involved in drug trafficking as a separately punishable offence with enhanced punishment
  4. Make all drug offences bailable
Show answer

Answer: C. Include harbouring offenders involved in drug trafficking as a separately punishable offence with enhanced punishment

Report this question

11Which section of NDPS Act 1985 provides for forfeiture of property derived from or used in drug trafficking?

  1. Section 37 NDPS Act
  2. Sections 68A-68Y NDPS Act (Chapter VA) — introduced by 1989 Amendment for forfeiture of illegally acquired property
  3. Section 27A NDPS Act
  4. Section 50 NDPS Act
Show answer

Answer: B. Sections 68A-68Y NDPS Act (Chapter VA) — introduced by 1989 Amendment for forfeiture of illegally acquired property

Report this question

12Under IPC/BNS, 'making a false document' includes which of the following acts?

  1. Only physically writing a document
  2. Dishonestly or fraudulently making, signing, sealing or executing a document; causing a person to sign/seal/execute a document by inducing them to believe it is a different document
  3. Only signing a document
  4. Only using a rubber stamp falsely
Show answer

Answer: B. Dishonestly or fraudulently making, signing, sealing or executing a document; causing a person to sign/seal/execute a document by inducing them to believe it is a different document

Report this question

13The offence of 'possession of instrument or material for counterfeiting' currency under BNS 2023 is covered under:

  1. Section 178 BNS
  2. Section 181 BNS (equivalent to Section 489D IPC)
  3. Section 179 BNS
  4. Section 183 BNS
Show answer

Answer: B. Section 181 BNS (equivalent to Section 489D IPC)

Report this question

14Under NDPS Act, the maximum punishment for producing/manufacturing narcotic drugs in commercial quantity is:

  1. Life imprisonment only
  2. Maximum 7 years
  3. Rigorous imprisonment of 10-20 years with fine of Rs. 1-2 lakhs; death penalty on second conviction for certain offences
  4. Fine only
Show answer

Answer: C. Rigorous imprisonment of 10-20 years with fine of Rs. 1-2 lakhs; death penalty on second conviction for certain offences

Report this question

15Section 50 of NDPS Act 1985 applies to:

  1. All searches under NDPS Act
  2. Only searches of premises
  3. Personal search of a person (not to search of a vehicle or premises) — the right to be taken before a gazetted officer or Magistrate applies only to personal searches
  4. Only searches of vehicles
Show answer

Answer: C. Personal search of a person (not to search of a vehicle or premises) — the right to be taken before a gazetted officer or Magistrate applies only to personal searches

Report this question

16The offence of delivering or transferring counterfeit currency (Section 489C IPC) under BNS 2023 corresponds to:

  1. Section 178 BNS
  2. Section 182 BNS
  3. Section 180 BNS
  4. Section 183 BNS
Show answer

Answer: B. Section 182 BNS
