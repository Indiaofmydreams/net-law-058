1Under BNS 2023, which section specifically deals with gang rape?

  1. Section 63 BNS
  2. Section 64 BNS
  3. Section 66 BNS
  4. Section 70 BNS - when rape is committed by one or more persons constituting a group, each shall be deemed to have committed rape

  1. Offences where minimum sentence is 7+ years but no maximum is specified are NOT 'heinous offences' under JJ Act — the uncertainty makes them 'serious' offences, not heinous

  3. The JJ Act was unconstitutional
  4. Heinous offences include all offences above 3 years

Answer: A. Offences where minimum sentence is 7+ years but no maximum is specified are NOT 'heinous offences' under JJ Act — the uncertainty makes them 'serious' offences, not heinous

22The Supreme Court's landmark judgment in Vishaka v. State of Rajasthan (1997) laid down guidelines on sexual harassment at workplace because:
