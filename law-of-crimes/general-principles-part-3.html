1The 'mens rea in regulatory offences' principle was comprehensively discussed in Gian Chand v. State of Punjab holding that:

  1. Mens rea may be excluded in regulatory statutes if the legislative intent clearly so indicates, especially when the offence is of grave social concern
  2. Mens rea can never be excluded
  3. All regulatory offences are strict liability
  4. Only Parliament can exclude mens rea
Show answer

Answer: A. Mens rea may be excluded in regulatory statutes if the legislative intent clearly so indicates, especially when the offence is of grave social concern

  3. Only in offences against the State

Answer: A. When Parliament uses clear language excluding it in regulatory statutes, or when the statute is of absolute/strict liability nature
