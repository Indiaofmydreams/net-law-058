1A police officer in good faith exceeds his authority while executing a lawful court order and causes hurt. Which exception protects him?

  1. Section 76 IPC
  2. Section 78 IPC (Section 16 BNS) - act done in pursuance of a judgment/order of court, done in good faith
  3. Section 79 IPC
  4. Section 96 IPC
Show answer

Answer: B. Section 78 IPC (Section 16 BNS) - act done in pursuance of a judgment/order of court, done in good faith

2Section 76 and Section 79 IPC are both about mistake of fact but are NOT identical because:

  1. They are completely identical
  2. Section 76 applies only to police officers
  3. Section 79 requires court order
  4. Section 76 covers a person BOUND BY LAW to do an act; Section 79 covers a person JUSTIFIED BY LAW

Answer: D. Section 76 covers a person BOUND BY LAW to do an act; Section 79 covers a person JUSTIFIED BY LAW

14Section 80 IPC/Section 18 BNS (accident) requires that the act must be done:

  1. Only without criminal intention
  2. Lawfully, without criminal intention or knowledge, by lawful means, with proper care and caution — ALL four elements must coexist for the accident defence to succeed

15Sections 76 and 79 IPC are sometimes called 'colour of law' defences. Their common foundational requirement is:

  1. The accused must have acted in GOOD FAITH — honest and reasonable belief based on mistake of FACT (not law) — the defence fails if the mistake is one of law
  2. Both require a court order
  3. Both apply only to public servants
  4. Both are absolute defences with no requirements
Show answer

Answer: A. The accused must have acted in GOOD FAITH — honest and reasonable belief based on mistake of FACT (not law) — the defence fails if the mistake is one of law

17The 'irresistible impulse test' for insanity is NOT recognised under Section 84 IPC because:

  1. Indian courts have never heard of the irresistible impulse test
  2. Section 84 IPC follows the M'Naghten Rules which focus on COGNITIVE incapacity (knowing nature/wrongness of act), not volitional incapacity (ability to control conduct)
  3. The irresistible impulse test is the same as M'Naghten
  4. BNS 2023 introduced the irresistible impulse test
Show answer

Answer: B. Section 84 IPC follows the M'Naghten Rules which focus on COGNITIVE incapacity (knowing nature/wrongness of act), not volitional incapacity (ability to control conduct)

18Section 89 IPC/Section 27 BNS provides for consent by guardian for acts done on a child or insane person. This section requires:

  1. Any consent by parent for any act
  2. Court permission in addition to guardian's consent
  3. The act to be done in good faith for the benefit of the child/insane person AND the act must not be one intended to cause death or grievous hurt

19The right of private defence is a RIGHT, not a duty. This means:

  1. A person must always fight back when attacked
  2. Failure to exercise private defence is itself an offence
