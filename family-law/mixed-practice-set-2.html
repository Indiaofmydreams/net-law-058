About this question set

This set has 18 multiple-choice questions on Family Law from the UGC NET Law (058) syllabus, unit 7, Family Law. Try each question first, then tap Show answer to check the key and read the explanation. 18 of the 18 questions carry a written explanation.

For the concepts and case law behind these questions, read the Family Law notes. Once you are comfortable, test yourself under exam conditions with a full-length mock test.

18 practice questions with answers

1Minimum marriage age for a woman in India under PCMA 2006 is:

  1. 15
  2. 18
  3. 16
  4. 21
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. 18

Eighteen for women, twenty-one for men.

Report this question

2The Parsi Marriage and Divorce Act was enacted in:

  1. 1872
  2. 1936
  3. 1869
  4. 1955
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. 1936

Parsi Marriage and Divorce Act, 1936.

Report this question

3The Supreme Court uses which Article to dissolve a marriage for irretrievable breakdown?

  1. 32
  2. 226
  3. 136
  4. 142
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. 142

Shilpa Sailesh v. Varun Sreenivasan (2023).

Report this question

4Mubarat in Muslim law is:

  1. Divorce by mutual consent
  2. Husband's repudiation
  3. Judicial divorce
  4. Separation without divorce
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Divorce by mutual consent

Both spouses desire dissolution.

Report this question

5Khula is a divorce:

  1. By court only
  2. At the wife's instance with consideration
  3. By husband alone
  4. By panchayat
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. At the wife's instance with consideration

Wife releases dower or other consideration.

Report this question

6The doctrine of pious obligation relates to:

  1. Guardianship
  2. Dower
  3. A Hindu son's liability to pay his father's debts
  4. Adoption
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. A Hindu son's liability to pay his father's debts

Mitakshara concept.

16A Hindu wife has deserted her husband for over two years without cause. He may seek:

  1. Divorce on desertion
  2. Annulment
  3. Judicial separation only
  4. Nothing
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Divorce on desertion

Desertion is a ground.

Report this question

17A Muslim man orally gifts property but never delivers possession. The gift is:

  1. Valid
  2. Voidable
  3. Invalid
  4. Revocable only
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Invalid

Delivery is essential.

Report this question

18A Hindu male dies intestate leaving widow, son and daughter. They inherit as:

  1. Class I heirs, equally
  2. Only the son
  3. Only daughter
  4. Only the widow
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Class I heirs, equally

Hindu Succession Act.
