About this question set

This set has 18 multiple-choice questions on Family Law from the UGC NET Law (058) syllabus, unit 7, Family Law. Try each question first, then tap Show answer to check the key and read the explanation. 18 of the 18 questions carry a written explanation.

For the concepts and case law behind these questions, read the Family Law notes. Once you are comfortable, test yourself under exam conditions with a full-length mock test.

18 practice questions with answers

1The Hindu Marriage Act was enacted in:

  1. 1954
  2. 1961
  3. 1956
  4. 1955
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. 1955

Hindu Marriage Act, 1955.

Report this question

2Instant triple talaq was held invalid in:

  1. Danial Latifi
  2. Shah Bano
  3. Shayara Bano v. Union of India
  4. Mohd. Ahmed Khan
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Shayara Bano v. Union of India

Decided in 2017.

Report this question

3The Muslim Women (Protection of Rights on Marriage) Act, 2019 criminalised:

  1. Polygamy
  2. Instant triple talaq
  3. Nikah halala
  4. Mehr
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Instant triple talaq

Talaq-e-biddat is void and punishable.

Report this question

4Maintenance to a divorced Muslim woman under Section 125 CrPC was upheld in:

  1. Vineeta Sharma
  2. Shayara Bano
  3. Shah Bano
  4. Sarla Mudgal
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Shah Bano

Mohd. Ahmed Khan v. Shah Bano Begum (1985).

Report this question

5Mahr (dower) belongs to:

  1. Hindu law
  2. Muslim law
  3. Parsi law
  4. Christian law
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Muslim law

A sum payable by the husband to the wife on marriage.

Report this question

6Stridhan was considered in:

  1. Kesavananda
  2. Pratibha Rani v. Suraj Kumar
  3. Vishaka
  4. Shah Bano
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Pratibha Rani v. Suraj Kumar

The wife has absolute rights over her stridhan.

Report this question

7Daughters became coparceners by birth by the Hindu Succession (Amendment) Act of:

  1. 2015
  2. 2005
  3. 1956
  4. 1991
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. 2005

Vineeta Sharma v. Rakesh Sharma (2020) clarified it applies irrespective of father's death date.

Report this question

8Adoption available to persons of any religion is under the:

  1. HAMA 1956
  2. Guardians and Wards Act
  3. Special Marriage Act
  4. Juvenile Justice Act, 2015
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Juvenile Justice Act, 2015

Shabnam Hashmi v. Union of India (2014).

Report this question

9A mother was recognised as natural guardian during father's lifetime in:

  1. Shah Bano
  2. Vishaka
  3. Gita Hariharan v. RBI
  4. Danial Latifi
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Gita Hariharan v. RBI

Decided in 1999.

Report this question

10The Special Marriage Act was enacted in:

  1. 1955
  2. 1954
  3. 1872
  4. 1936
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. 1954

It provides civil marriage irrespective of religion.

Report this question

11'Relationship in the nature of marriage' appears in:

  1. Muslim Personal Law Act
  2. Protection of Women from Domestic Violence Act, 2005
  3. Hindu Marriage Act
  4. Special Marriage Act
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Protection of Women from Domestic Violence Act, 2005

Live-in relationships are covered.

Report this question

12Recognition of foreign matrimonial decrees was considered in:

  1. Danial Latifi
  2. Sarla Mudgal
  3. Y. Narasimha Rao v. Y. Venkata Lakshmi
  4. Shah Bano
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Y. Narasimha Rao v. Y. Venkata Lakshmi

Section 13 CPC governs.

Report this question

13The Uniform Civil Code is mentioned in:

  1. Article 21
  2. Article 44
  3. Article 51A
  4. Article 25
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Article 44

A Directive Principle.

Report this question

14A Muslim can bequeath by will without heirs' consent up to:

  1. One-third
  2. One-half
  3. Two-thirds
  4. Entire estate
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. One-third

Beyond one-third requires consent.

Report this question

15Essentials of hiba (gift) in Muslim law include declaration, acceptance and:

  1. Registration
  2. Witnesses
  3. Consideration
  4. Delivery of possession
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Delivery of possession

Delivery of possession completes the gift.

Report this question

16Birth right by birth in the joint family in father's lifetime belongs to:

  1. Mitakshara
  2. Dayabhaga
  3. Both
  4. Neither
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Mitakshara

Dayabhaga gives no right by birth during father's life.

Report this question

17Restitution of conjugal rights was upheld in:

  1. Saroj Rani v. Sudarshan Kumar
  2. Sareetha v. Venkata Subbaiah
  3. Shayara Bano
  4. Hirachand Srinivas
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Saroj Rani v. Sudarshan Kumar

The Supreme Court upheld Section 9 HMA.

Report this question

18The Supreme Court held a divorced Muslim woman can claim maintenance under Section 125 CrPC in:

  1. Shabnam Hashmi
  2. Mohd. Abdul Samad v. State of Telangana
  3. Danial Latifi
  4. Rajesh Sharma
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Mohd. Abdul Samad v. State of Telangana

Decided in 2024.
