About this question set

This set has 22 multiple-choice questions on Public International Law & IHL from the UGC NET Law (058) syllabus, unit 3, Public International Law and IHL. Try each question first, then tap Show answer to check the key and read the explanation. 22 of the 22 questions carry a written explanation.

For the concepts and case law behind these questions, read the Public International Law & IHL notes. Once you are comfortable, test yourself under exam conditions with a full-length mock test.

Topics in this set

  • General concepts (22)

22 practice questions with answers

1Customary international law requires state practice and:

  1. Court judgment
  2. Treaty ratification
  3. Opinio juris
  4. UN resolution
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Opinio juris

Opinio juris is the belief that the practice is legally obligatory.

Report this question

2The Vienna Convention on the Law of Treaties was adopted in:

  1. 1969
  2. 1998
  3. 1949
  4. 1982
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. 1969

The VCLT was adopted in 1969 and entered into force in 1980.

Report this question

3A peremptory norm of general international law is called:

  1. Jus cogens
  2. Pacta sunt servanda
  3. Erga omnes
  4. Soft law
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Jus cogens

Article 53 VCLT: no derogation allowed.

Report this question

4Under the declaratory theory, recognition of a State:

  1. Requires UN approval
  2. Is a legal duty
  3. Creates statehood
  4. Merely acknowledges existing statehood
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Merely acknowledges existing statehood

Statehood exists once the Montevideo criteria are met.

Report this question

5The Montevideo Convention (1933) criteria do NOT include:

  1. Defined territory
  2. Recognition by the UN Security Council
  3. Effective government
  4. Permanent population
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Recognition by the UN Security Council

UN recognition is not a criterion for statehood.

Report this question

6The principle of non-refoulement is found in Article 33 of the:

  1. UDHR
  2. VCLT
  3. Geneva Convention I
  4. 1951 Refugee Convention
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. 1951 Refugee Convention

It bars returning refugees to territories where they face persecution.

Report this question

7India is:

  1. Not a party to the 1951 Refugee Convention
  2. A party to both
  3. A party to its 1967 Protocol only
  4. A party to the 1951 Refugee Convention
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Not a party to the 1951 Refugee Convention

India has not signed either instrument.

Report this question

8The 'genuine link' test for nationality was laid down in:

  1. Nottebohm case
  2. Corfu Channel
  3. Lotus case
  4. Asylum case
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Nottebohm case

ICJ, 1955, Liechtenstein v. Guatemala.

Report this question

9The rule of double criminality in extradition means:

  1. Offence must be crime in both States
  2. Two trials
  3. Two requests
  4. Two treaties
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Offence must be crime in both States

The act must be an offence in the requesting and requested States.

Report this question

10The Asylum case (1950) was between:

  1. Colombia and Peru
  2. Peru and Chile
  3. Norway and UK
  4. Argentina and Brazil
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Colombia and Peru

It concerned diplomatic asylum for Haya de la Torre.

Report this question

11The number of principal organs of the United Nations is:

  1. Five
  2. Six
  3. Seven
  4. Eight
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Six

General Assembly, Security Council, ECOSOC, Trusteeship Council, ICJ, Secretariat.

Report this question

12Veto power in the Security Council under Article 27 belongs to:

  1. All members
  2. General Assembly
  3. Elected members
  4. Five permanent members
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Five permanent members

The P5: China, France, Russia, UK and USA.

Report this question

13Enforcement action for threats to the peace is in which Chapter of the UN Charter?

  1. Chapter XIV
  2. Chapter XI
  3. Chapter VII
  4. Chapter VI
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Chapter VII

Chapter VI covers pacific settlement; Chapter VII covers enforcement.

Report this question

14The WTO came into existence in:

  1. 1995
  2. 2001
  3. 1989
  4. 1947
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. 1995

WTO replaced the GATT framework from 1 January 1995.

Report this question

15The MFN principle under GATT is in:

  1. Article I
  2. Article III
  3. Article XI
  4. Article XX
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Article I

Most-favoured-nation treatment. National treatment is Article III.

Report this question

16How many Geneva Conventions of 1949 exist?

  1. Two
  2. Three
  3. Four
  4. Five
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Four

Wounded and sick on land, at sea, prisoners of war, and civilians.

Report this question

17The Martens Clause is:

  1. A residual protection for civilians and combatants
  2. A treaty on trade
  3. A rule on recognition
  4. A rule on extradition
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. A residual protection for civilians and combatants

It keeps humanity and public conscience as guides where treaty law is silent.

Report this question

18The principle of distinction in IHL requires distinguishing between:

  1. Civilians and combatants
  2. Treaty and custom
  3. Land and sea
  4. States and organisations
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Civilians and combatants

Attacks may be directed only at military objectives and combatants.

Report this question

19The Rome Statute establishing the ICC was adopted in:

  1. 1949
  2. 2002
  3. 1945
  4. 1998
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. 1998

Adopted in 1998 and in force from 2002. India is not a party.

Report this question

20The Lotus case (1927) held that States may act unless:

  1. Authorised
  2. Approved by UN
  3. Reciprocated
  4. Prohibited by international law
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Prohibited by international law

PCIJ: restrictions upon independence of States cannot be presumed.

Report this question

21The dualist theory holds that international and municipal law are:

  1. Identical in source
  2. One system
  3. Separate systems
  4. Subordinate to treaties
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Separate systems

Dualism requires transformation into domestic law.

Report this question

22Obligations erga omnes are owed to:

  1. Only treaty parties
  2. The international community as a whole
  3. The Security Council
  4. Only neighbours
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. The international community as a whole

Barcelona Traction (1970).

Report this question
