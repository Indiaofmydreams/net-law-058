How this section is different

No timer, by design

Nothing on the AILET practice or mock pages counts down. The aim is accuracy and understanding first. When you want exam speed, time yourself off-site: the real paper gives about 72 seconds per question.

Built for the AILET paper

AILET LL.M. is one paper of 100 questions drawn from different branches of law, not passage-based. Practice sets and mocks here follow that shape, with subject tags so your weak branches show up.

Revision is automatic

You never copy a mistake into a notebook. It lands there on its own, along with questions you bookmark or skip in a mock. Your data stays in your browser, with backup and restore.
