AILET LL.M. SYLLABUS

Syllabus and tracker

NLU Delhi lists only the branches of law the LL.M. paper draws from. This page breaks each branch into topics and the cases to know, then lets you tick what you have covered and revised.

How to read this. The thirteen branches named in NLU Delhi's scheme are: Constitutional Law, Jurisprudence, Administrative Law, Law of Contract, Law of Torts, Family Law, Criminal Law, Property Law, Company Law, Public International Law, Tax Law, Environmental Law, and Labour and Industrial Law. The topic breakdown under each is our own, built from standard LL.B. coverage and recent papers, and is not an official list. The tier labels (core, regular, occasional) are our reading of past-paper trends.

1Constitutional LawCORE · 16 topics0%

Foundations

Preamble, nature of the Constitution, sources, Union and its territory, citizenship (Arts 5 to 11; Citizenship Act 1955 including s.6A)
Amendment and Basic Structure: Art 368, Shankari Prasad to Kesavananda Bharati, Minerva Mills, Waman Rao, I.R. Coelho (Ninth Schedule), recent amendments (101st GST, 103rd EWS, 106th women's reservation)

Rights and duties

State and Art 13: meaning of State (Art 12), pre- and post-Constitution laws, eclipse, severability, waiver
Equality (Arts 14 to 18): reasonable classification, arbitrariness (E.P. Royappa), legitimate expectation; reservations (Indra Sawhney, M. Nagaraj, EWS, creamy layer, sub-classification of SCs in Davinder Singh 2024)
Freedoms (Art 19) and reasonable restrictions; speech, assembly, association, movement, profession; Shreya Singhal, Anuradha Bhasin
Arts 20 to 22: ex post facto laws, double jeopardy, self-incrimination, arrest and preventive detention safeguards
Art 21 and its expansion: Maneka Gandhi, privacy (Puttaswamy), dignity, livelihood, health, environment, speedy trial; Art 21A
Religious freedom (Arts 25 to 28): essential religious practices, Sabarimala, Shirur Mutt; Arts 29 and 30 cultural and educational rights
Art 32 and 226: writs and their grounds, PIL, locus standi, Directive Principles, relationship with Fundamental Rights, Fundamental Duties

Government structure

Union Executive: President, Vice-President, Council of Ministers, ordinances (Art 123), pardon (Arts 72 and 161), Governor's powers
Legislature: composition, procedure, money and financial bills, privileges, Tenth Schedule (Kihoto Hollohan), delegated legislation
Judiciary: Supreme Court jurisdictions (original, appellate, advisory, review, curative), High Courts, appointments and the Collegium, NJAC, independence, contempt, judicial review
Federalism: legislative relations and Seventh Schedule (Arts 245 to 254, 246A), administrative and financial relations, GST Council, Finance Commission, inter-State matters (Arts 262, 263)
Emergency and special provisions: Arts 352, 356 (S.R. Bommai), 360; Art 370 and Arts 371 to 371J; Fifth and Sixth Schedules
Constitutional bodies and local government: Election Commission, CAG, UPSC, Panchayats and Municipalities (73rd and 74th Amendments), tribunals (Arts 323A, 323B)

Cases to know cold

A.K. Gopalan, Kesavananda Bharati, Maneka Gandhi, Minerva Mills, S.R. Bommai, Indra Sawhney, Vishaka, Puttaswamy, Navtej Johar, Joseph Shine, Shayara Bano, Kihoto Hollohan, I.R. Coelho, Supriyo v Union of India
Note: Recent developments: abrogation of Art 370 upheld (Dec 2023); electoral bonds struck down (Feb 2024); s.6A Citizenship Act upheld (Oct 2024); sub-classification of Scheduled Castes (Aug 2024); Supriyo (Oct 2023). Verify anything newer on the Supreme Court website.
2JurisprudenceCORE · 11 topics0%

Nature and schools of law

Nature and definition of law; law and morality; law, justice and the rule of law
Natural law: Greek and Stoic roots, Aquinas, Grotius, Fuller's inner morality, Finnis, Dworkin
Analytical positivism: Bentham, Austin's command theory, Hart's rules and the rule of recognition, Kelsen's Pure Theory and Grundnorm, Raz
Historical and sociological schools: Savigny, Maine (status to contract), Ehrlich's living law, Pound's social engineering, Duguit
Realism and later schools: American and Scandinavian realism, Marxist, critical legal studies, feminist jurisprudence, Indian jurisprudence

Legal concepts

Rights and duties: Hohfeld's fundamental legal conceptions, kinds of rights, Salmond and Austin on rights
Legal personality: natural and juristic persons, corporations, idols, animals, rivers (Mohd. Salim), the state
Possession, ownership, property, title; obligations; liability (civil and criminal, strict, vicarious, absolute), mens rea, negligence
Sources of law: legislation, precedent (ratio, obiter, stare decisis, per incuriam, sub silentio), custom (requisites), juristic writings
Theories of justice: Aristotle, Rawls, Nozick, Sen; theories of punishment; administration of justice

Cases and thinkers

Salomon v Salomon, Donoghue v Stevenson (as jurisprudence), Bachan Singh (punishment), Kesavananda (rule of law), Shiv Kumar Sharma, Hart–Fuller debate, Hart–Devlin debate
Note: Jurisprudence questions in AILET are usually match-the-thinker-to-theory or concept-definition questions. Build a one-page table of thinker, school, key idea, key work.
3Administrative LawREGULAR · 8 topics0%

Core principles

Nature, scope and sources; rule of law (Dicey), separation of powers, droit administratif
Delegated legislation: need, limits (excessive delegation, conditional legislation, sub-delegation), parliamentary and judicial control
Natural justice: audi alteram partem, nemo judex in causa sua, reasoned decisions, bias, exceptions; Maneka Gandhi, Ridge v Baldwin
Administrative discretion and judicial review: Wednesbury unreasonableness, proportionality, legitimate expectation, promissory estoppel
Remedies: writs (certiorari, mandamus, prohibition, quo warranto, habeas corpus), statutory appeals

Accountability

Liability of the State: tort (Art 300, sovereign and non-sovereign functions, constitutional torts) and contract (Art 299)
Tribunals and quasi-judicial bodies, administrative tribunals, Lokpal and Lokayuktas, Central Vigilance Commission, Commissions of Inquiry
Right to Information Act 2005, public interest litigation, public trust doctrine
4Law of ContractCORE · 10 topics0%

Indian Contract Act 1872

Formation: proposal, acceptance, communication, revocation, lapse; invitation to treat; Carlill v Carbolic Smoke Ball, Lalman Shukla
Consideration and privity: adequacy, past consideration, exceptions (s.25), Tweddle v Atkinson, Dunlop; capacity (minors: Mohori Bibee), unsound mind
Free consent: coercion, undue influence, fraud, misrepresentation, mistake; Central Inland Water Transport; legality of object and consideration
Void and voidable agreements: restraint of trade, wagering, uncertainty, impossible acts; contingent contracts; quasi-contracts (ss.68 to 72)
Performance and discharge: tender, novation, frustration (s.56, Satyabrata Ghose), anticipatory breach
Breach and remedies: damages and remoteness (Hadley v Baxendale, ss.73 to 75), liquidated damages and penalty, quantum meruit
Special contracts: indemnity and guarantee, bailment and pledge, agency

Allied statutes

Sale of Goods Act 1930: conditions and warranties, caveat emptor, passing of property, rights of the unpaid seller
Specific Relief Act 1963 as amended in 2018: specific performance as a general rule, injunctions, rectification and cancellation
Partnership Act 1932 and the LLP Act 2008; standard form contracts and unfair terms; government contracts
5Law of TortsCORE · 8 topics0%

General principles

Nature of tort, tort versus crime and breach of contract, damnum sine injuria and injuria sine damno, general defences
Negligence: duty, breach and damage, Donoghue v Stevenson, Caparo, res ipsa loquitur, contributory negligence, remoteness (Wagon Mound), professional and medical negligence (Bolam, Jacob Mathew)
Strict and absolute liability: Rylands v Fletcher and its Indian departure in M.C. Mehta and the Bhopal litigation
Vicarious liability: master and servant, course of employment, State liability

Specific torts

Nuisance (public and private), trespass to person, land and goods, false imprisonment, malicious prosecution
Defamation: libel and slander, defences, civil and criminal (Subramanian Swamy 2016), freedom of speech
Constitutional torts: Rudul Sah, Nilabati Behera, compensation for rights violations
Consumer Protection Act 2019: consumer, deficiency in service, unfair trade practice, product liability, CCPA, e-commerce; Motor Vehicles Act no-fault liability
6Family LawREGULAR · 6 topics0%

Hindu law

Hindu Marriage Act 1955: conditions, void and voidable marriages, restitution, judicial separation, divorce (including mutual consent), maintenance and alimony
Hindu Succession Act 1956 and the 2005 amendment (Vineeta Sharma: daughters as coparceners by birth); joint family and coparcenary
Hindu Minority and Guardianship Act, Hindu Adoptions and Maintenance Act

Muslim, Christian, Parsi and general law

Muslim law: sources and schools, nikah and mehr, talaq (Shayara Bano; Muslim Women (Protection of Rights on Marriage) Act 2019), maintenance (Shah Bano, Danial Latifi), inheritance, wills, gift, wakf
Christian and Parsi law: Indian Divorce Act 1869 as amended, Indian Succession Act 1925; Special Marriage Act 1954; Guardians and Wards Act 1890
Protective and constitutional themes: Domestic Violence Act 2005, live-in relationships, maintenance under BNSS s.144 (formerly CrPC s.125), Uniform Civil Code and Art 44
Note: Watch: recent Supreme Court directions on maintenance, the Waqf (Amendment) Act 2025 litigation and State-level UCC legislation (Uttarakhand, 2024).
7Criminal LawCORE · 9 topics0%

Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita 2023 (replaced the IPC from 1 July 2024)

General principles: mens rea, stages of crime, general exceptions (private defence, insanity, intoxication, necessity), punishments including community service
Joint and inchoate liability: common intention (s.3(5)), common object (s.190), abetment, criminal conspiracy, attempt
Offences against the body: culpable homicide and murder (ss.100 to 105), mob lynching (s.103(2)), hurt, kidnapping, sexual offences
New offences: organised crime (s.111), terrorist act (s.113), acts endangering sovereignty (s.152, which replaced sedition), snatching, petty organised crime
Property and other offences: theft, extortion, robbery, dacoity, cheating, criminal breach of trust, forgery, defamation, offences against the State

Procedure and evidence

Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita 2023: zero FIR and e-FIR, arrest (s.35), bail and the rule for undertrials (s.479), search with videography, forensic and time limits, trials in absentia, plea bargaining, victim compensation
Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam 2023: electronic records and the certificate (s.63), admissions and confessions, presumptions, burden of proof, expert and dying declarations

Doctrine and special laws

Capital punishment: Bachan Singh, Machhi Singh, Shatrughan Chauhan; Navtej Johar (s.377), Joseph Shine (adultery)
Special laws: POCSO, NDPS, PMLA, UAPA, SC/ST (Prevention of Atrocities) Act, Dowry Prohibition Act, Juvenile Justice Act 2015, Prevention of Corruption Act 1988
Note: The BNS, BNSS and BSA changed section numbers across the board. Many questions test whether you know the new number, the new offence or the old IPC equivalent. Always check a bare Act before relying on a section number.
8Property LawREGULAR · 5 topics0%

Transfer of Property Act 1882

General principles: transferable and non-transferable property, transfer by operation of law, conditions and restraints, vested and contingent interests
Doctrines: lis pendens, part performance, feeding the grant by estoppel, election, ostensible owner, fraudulent transfer, apportionment
Specific transfers: sale, mortgage (types, rights, redemption, foreclosure), lease (ss.105 to 117), exchange, gift, actionable claims

Allied statutes

Easements Act 1882, Indian Trusts Act 1882, Registration Act 1908 (compulsory registration, s.17)
Benami Transactions (Prohibition) Act 1988 as amended in 2016 (Ganpati Dealcom, 2022); RERA 2016; land acquisition (LARR Act 2013), right to property under Art 300A
9Company LawREGULAR · 5 topics0%

Companies Act 2013

Incorporation and personality: Salomon, lifting the veil, types of company, MoA and AoA, promoters, prospectus
Internal management: directors (duties, independent directors, KMP), meetings and resolutions, doctrines of ultra vires, constructive notice and indoor management (Turquand's rule)
Members and minorities: majority rule, Foss v Harbottle, oppression and mismanagement (ss.241 and 242), class actions, CSR (s.135)
Winding up and mergers: NCLT and NCLAT, compromises and arrangements; securities regulation (SEBI Act, insider trading); LLP Act 2008

Insolvency

Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code 2016: CIRP, moratorium, resolution professional, committee of creditors, Swiss Ribbon, Essar Steel, personal guarantors
10Public International LawREGULAR · 7 topics0%

Foundations

Nature and sources: Art 38 of the ICJ Statute, treaties, custom, general principles, soft law, jus cogens and erga omnes; monism and dualism, Arts 51 and 253 in India
Subjects and statehood: recognition, succession, territory, jurisdiction, immunities, State responsibility
Law of treaties: Vienna Convention 1969, reservations, interpretation, termination

Institutions and regimes

United Nations: organs, Security Council, General Assembly, ICJ (contentious and advisory jurisdiction), regional organisations
Law of the sea (UNCLOS), air and space law, diplomatic and consular relations, extradition and asylum
Human rights and humanitarian law: UDHR, ICCPR, ICESCR, Geneva Conventions, refugees and non-refoulement; international criminal law and the ICC
International environmental and economic law: Stockholm, Rio, Paris Agreement, WTO basics, investment arbitration
11Tax LawOCCASIONAL · 3 topics0%

Direct and indirect tax

Constitutional framework: Arts 265, 246A, 268 to 293, 279A (GST Council), tax versus fee, retrospective taxation (Vodafone and the 2021 amendment)
Income tax: residence, heads of income, exemptions and deductions, clubbing, set-off, assessment, appeals and penalties; avoidance and GAAR (Azadi Bachao Andolan, McDowell)
GST: levy and supply, input tax credit, CGST, SGST and IGST, composition, the 101st Amendment, Mohit Minerals (2022)
Note: Watch: the Income-tax Act 2025 is intended to replace the 1961 Act from 1 April 2026. Check the current position, and the tax-year terminology, before relying on 'assessment year'.
12Environmental LawOCCASIONAL · 4 topics0%

Principles and statutes

Constitutional basis: Arts 21, 48A, 51A(g); principles: sustainable development, precautionary principle, polluter pays, public trust, intergenerational equity
Statutes: Water Act 1974, Air Act 1981, Environment (Protection) Act 1986, Wildlife Protection Act 1972, Forest Conservation Act 1980 as amended in 2023, Biological Diversity Act 2002, NGT Act 2010, Public Liability Insurance Act 1991
Regulation: EIA, CRZ, hazardous waste, environmental clearances
Cases: M.C. Mehta series, Vellore Citizens Welfare Forum, Indian Council for Enviro-Legal Action, T.N. Godavarman, Narmada Bachao Andolan, M.K. Ranjitsinh (2024: right against the adverse effects of climate change)
13Labour and Industrial LawOCCASIONAL · 4 topics0%

Codes and classic statutes

The four Labour Codes: Wages, Industrial Relations, Social Security, and Occupational Safety, Health and Working Conditions; which older laws they absorb
Industrial Disputes Act 1947 (still the source of most case law): industry (Bangalore Water Supply), workman, retrenchment, lay-off, strikes and lockouts, settlement and adjudication
Trade unions, standing orders, contract labour; Factories Act, minimum wages, gratuity, EPF, ESI, Employees' Compensation Act, Maternity Benefit Act
Constitutional provisions: Arts 14, 19(1)(c), 21, 23, 24 and the Directive Principles; POSH Act 2013 (Vishaka); gig and platform workers
Note: Check which provisions and rules of the Labour Codes are currently in force before answering any 'current position' question.
14Allied areas that appear in past papersOCCASIONAL · 8 topics0%

Procedure, evidence and dispute resolution

Civil Procedure Code: jurisdiction, res judicata, res sub judice, pleadings, interim orders, execution, appeals; Limitation Act 1963
Arbitration and Conciliation Act 1996 (2015, 2019, 2021 amendments), interim measures, setting aside awards, enforcement; Mediation Act 2023; Legal Services Authorities Act
Law of Evidence (now the BSA 2023): relevancy, admissions, confessions, burden of proof, estoppel, privilege

Other regular visitors

Intellectual property: patents (s.3(d), Novartis), copyright, trade marks, designs, geographical indications, passing off
Competition Act 2002 (2023 amendment); consumer protection; banking law and negotiable instruments (s.138), SARFAESI
Human rights law (Protection of Human Rights Act 1993), interpretation of statutes (literal, golden, mischief and purposive rules, General Clauses Act)
Information technology and data protection: IT Act 2000, Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, intermediary liability
Current legal developments: major Supreme Court judgments, new Acts and committee reports of the last 12 months
