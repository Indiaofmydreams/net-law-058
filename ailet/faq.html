AILET FAQ

Short answers for LL.M. aspirants. For the full picture, read the exam guide.

What is AILET?

AILET is the All India Law Entrance Test conducted by National Law University Delhi for admission to its B.A. LL.B. (Hons.), one-year LL.M. and Ph.D. programmes. This site covers the LL.M. paper.

What is the AILET LL.M. exam pattern?

The paper has 100 multiple-choice questions of one mark each, all from different branches of law, to be done in 120 minutes. There is negative marking of 0.25 for each wrong answer. This is the scheme published on NLU Delhi's website.

Is there negative marking in AILET LL.M.?

Yes. 0.25 mark is deducted for every wrong answer, so four wrong answers cancel one right one. Unattempted questions carry no penalty.

When is AILET 2027?

The AILET 2027 examination is on Sunday, 13 December 2026, from 2:00 PM to 4:00 PM. Applications opened on 7 August 2026 and were scheduled to close on 10 November 2026; check nationallawuniversitydelhi.in for any extension and for admit card dates.

What is the AILET LL.M. syllabus?

NLU Delhi states only that the 100 questions are from different branches of law. Past papers have drawn on Constitutional Law, Jurisprudence, Administrative Law, Law of Contract, Law of Torts, Family Law, Criminal Law, Property Law, Company Law, Public International Law, Tax Law, Environmental Law, and Labour and Industrial Law, with allied areas such as evidence, procedure, arbitration and intellectual property appearing too. Our syllabus page breaks each branch into topics.

Which subject matters most?

Constitutional Law has been the largest block in recent papers, followed by Jurisprudence, Criminal Law, Contract and Torts. These weightages come from coaching analyses of past papers, not from NLU Delhi, and they change from year to year.

Who is eligible for AILET LL.M.?

As reported for the 2027 cycle, candidates need an LL.B. degree with at least 50% marks (45% for SC, ST and PwD), and final-year LL.B. students have been allowed to apply. Confirm the exact conditions in the official admission notification.

How many seats are there?

Reports for the 2027 cycle put the regular LL.M. intake at roughly 70 seats on merit, plus seats for foreign nationals and OCI/PIO candidates and a supernumerary seat, around 81 in total. Check the notification for the exact number and the reservation scheme.

How is a tie in marks broken?

NLU Delhi decides ties first by senior age and then by a computerised draw of lots.

Is AILET the same as CLAT PG?

No. CLAT PG is conducted by the Consortium of NLUs for its member universities and is passage-based. AILET is conducted by NLU Delhi for its own LL.M. and has 100 standalone questions. Many students attempt both. NLU Delhi admits its LL.M. through AILET, so do not assume a CLAT PG score replaces it.

Why is there no timer on this site's AILET practice and mocks?

So you can focus on accuracy and understanding first. Nothing counts down on the practice or mock pages. When you want to build speed, time yourself off-site: 100 questions in 120 minutes is about 72 seconds per question.

How do the mock tests work without a timer?

You answer at your own pace, can mark questions for review or clear a response, and submit when ready. You then get net marks, accuracy and a subject-wise and difficulty-wise breakdown, plus full explanations. Your answers are saved if you close the tab.

Where do my mistakes go?

Every wrong answer, in practice or in a mock, is saved automatically in My mistakes with the question, your answer and the explanation. You can filter by subject, retry them or print the list.

Are these questions from the real AILET papers?

No. They are original practice questions written for NET Law-Verse. NLU Delhi publishes previous LL.M. papers on its own website, and solving those is a good complement.

Where is my progress stored?

Only in your browser, on your device. Clearing site data erases it, so use Backup on the Progress tab to download a copy and Restore to bring it back or move to another device.
