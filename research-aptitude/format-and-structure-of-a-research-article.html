About this question set

This set has 20 multiple-choice questions on Format and Structure of a Research Article from the UGC NET Paper 1 syllabus, unit 2, Research Aptitude. Try each question first, then tap Show answer to check the key. This set is an answer-key drill: check each answer, then revisit the notes for any you miss.

For the concepts and case law behind these questions, read the Research Aptitude notes. Once you are comfortable, test yourself under exam conditions with a full-length mock test.

20 practice questions with answers

1IMRaD stands for:

  1. Index, Methods, References and Data
  2. Idea, Measurement, Report and Design
  3. Introduction, Meaning, Review and Discussion
  4. Introduction, Methods, Results and Discussion
Show answer

Answer: D. Introduction, Methods, Results and Discussion

Report this question

2The methods section of an article should be written so that:

  1. Nobody can repeat the study
  2. It contains only the results
  3. Other researchers can replicate the study
  4. It contains only opinions
Show answer

Answer: C. Other researchers can replicate the study

Report this question

3The results section mainly:

  1. Reports the findings, usually with tables and figures
  2. Interprets the findings at length
  3. Lists the references
  4. Gives the acknowledgements
Show answer

Answer: A. Reports the findings, usually with tables and figures

Report this question

4The discussion section of an article:

  1. Contains the full questionnaire
  2. Replaces the results
  3. Interprets the findings, compares them with the literature and notes limitations
  4. Contains only the title
Show answer

Answer: C. Interprets the findings, compares them with the literature and notes limitations

Report this question

5Keywords in an article mainly help in:

  1. Increasing the word count
  2. Indexing and retrieval of the article
  3. Replacing the abstract
  4. Acknowledging funders
Show answer

Answer: B. Indexing and retrieval of the article

Report this question

6A structured abstract uses headings such as:

  1. Chapter 1, Chapter 2
  2. Background, Methods, Results and Conclusion
  3. Footnote, Endnote
  4. Index, Glossary
Show answer

Answer: B. Background, Methods, Results and Conclusion

Report this question

7The title of a research article should be:

  1. As long as possible
  2. Unrelated to the content
  3. Concise and informative
  4. Written as a question only
Show answer

Answer: C. Concise and informative

Report this question

8A review article:

  1. Summarises, synthesises and critically evaluates existing research on a topic
  2. Reports original experimental data only
  3. Is a questionnaire
  4. Is an editorial opinion only
Show answer

Answer: A. Summarises, synthesises and critically evaluates existing research on a topic

Report this question

9A short communication (research note) is:

  1. A full-length thesis
  2. A brief report of preliminary or significant findings
  3. A book
  4. A newspaper report
Show answer

Answer: B. A brief report of preliminary or significant findings

Report this question

10An editorial in a journal is generally:

  1. A raw data set
  2. A peer-reviewed experiment
  3. A piece written by the editor on an issue or on the contents of the issue
  4. A bibliography
Show answer

Answer: C. A piece written by the editor on an issue or on the contents of the issue

Report this question

11Peer review is:

  1. Printing of the journal
  2. Evaluation of a manuscript by independent experts before publication
  3. A funding process
  4. Typing of the manuscript
Show answer

Answer: B. Evaluation of a manuscript by independent experts before publication

Report this question

12In double-blind peer review:

  1. Only the reviewer is anonymous
  2. The reader is anonymous
  3. Neither the authors' nor the reviewers' identities are revealed to each other
  4. The editor is anonymous
Show answer

Answer: C. Neither the authors' nor the reviewers' identities are revealed to each other

Report this question

13The journal impact factor is based on:

  1. The number of pages
  2. The average number of citations received in a year by articles published in the previous two years
  3. The number of authors
  4. The price of the journal
Show answer

Answer: B. The average number of citations received in a year by articles published in the previous two years

Report this question

14The h-index, proposed by Hirsch, indicates that a scholar has:

  1. h papers, each cited at least h times
  2. h journals
  3. h co-authors
  4. h pages
Show answer

Answer: A. h papers, each cited at least h times

Report this question

15A DOI is:

  1. A unique, persistent identifier for a digital object such as an article
  2. A degree of originality index
  3. A type of journal ranking
  4. A sampling method
Show answer

Answer: A. A unique, persistent identifier for a digital object such as an article

Report this question

16ORCID provides:

  1. A unique digital identifier for individual researchers
  2. A plagiarism check
  3. A citation style
  4. A journal ranking
Show answer

Answer: A. A unique digital identifier for individual researchers

Report this question

17ISSN is the standard number for:

  1. Books
  2. Serial publications such as journals
  3. Theses only
  4. Conferences only
Show answer

Answer: B. Serial publications such as journals

Report this question

18Predatory journals are characterised by:

  1. Charging fees with little or no genuine peer review
  2. Rigorous peer review
  3. Free access to all editors
  4. Indexing in all major databases
Show answer

Answer: A. Charging fees with little or no genuine peer review

Report this question

19Submitting the same manuscript to several journals at the same time is:

  1. Good research practice
  2. Required by editors
  3. Mandatory
  4. Unethical
Show answer

Answer: D. Unethical

Report this question

20The corresponding author is the person who:

  1. Writes only the references
  2. Funds the study
  3. Reviews the paper
  4. Handles communication with the journal on behalf of all authors
Show answer

Answer: D. Handles communication with the journal on behalf of all authors
