About this question set

This set has 20 multiple-choice questions on Meaning and Characteristics of Research from the UGC NET Paper 1 syllabus, unit 2, Research Aptitude. Try each question first, then tap Show answer to check the key. This set is an answer-key drill: check each answer, then revisit the notes for any you miss.

For the concepts and case law behind these questions, read the Research Aptitude notes. Once you are comfortable, test yourself under exam conditions with a full-length mock test.

20 practice questions with answers

1Research is best described as:

  1. Random collection of information
  2. Systematic and objective inquiry to discover or verify knowledge
  3. Reproducing existing literature
  4. Opinion based on experience
Show answer

Answer: B. Systematic and objective inquiry to discover or verify knowledge

Report this question

2The literal meaning of "research" is:

  1. To copy
  2. To search again
  3. To report
  4. To guess
Show answer

Answer: B. To search again

Report this question

3"Research is a systematized effort to gain new knowledge" is the definition given by:

  1. Redman and Mory
  2. Best
  3. Kerlinger
  4. Travers
Show answer

Answer: A. Redman and Mory

Report this question

4"Systematic, controlled, empirical and critical investigation of hypothetical propositions" is associated with:

  1. Whitney
  2. Good
  3. Kerlinger
  4. Lundberg
Show answer

Answer: C. Kerlinger

Report this question

5Research differs from common sense because it is:

  1. Systematic and empirically tested
  2. Based on tradition
  3. Free of evidence
  4. Free of theory
Show answer

Answer: A. Systematic and empirically tested

Report this question

6Which is NOT a purpose of research?

  1. Discovering new facts
  2. Testing theories
  3. Solving problems
  4. Promoting beliefs without evidence
Show answer

Answer: D. Promoting beliefs without evidence

7Research methodology refers to:

  1. The questionnaire used
  2. The statistical tables in the report
  3. The list of respondents
  4. The logic and rationale behind the choice and use of methods
Show answer

Answer: D. The logic and rationale behind the choice and use of methods

Report this question

8Which of the following is NOT a characteristic of research?

  1. Empirical
  2. Systematic
  3. Verifiable
  4. Subjective bias
Show answer

Answer: D. Subjective bias

Report this question

9Replicability in research means:

  1. The study can be repeated by others with similar results
  2. Results can never be tested again
  3. Only the original researcher can repeat it
  4. Findings apply to all populations
Show answer

Answer: A. The study can be repeated by others with similar results

Report this question

10"Empirical" research is based on:

  1. Observation or experiment
  2. Intuition
  3. Authority
  4. Tradition
Show answer

Answer: A. Observation or experiment

Report this question

11Research is called "cyclical" because:

  1. It is funded every year
  2. It is repeated annually
  3. It has only one step
  4. Findings generate new problems for further research
Show answer

Answer: D. Findings generate new problems for further research

Report this question

12Objectivity in research means:

  1. Personal values drive the findings
  2. Only numerical data are used
  3. Government approval is obtained
  4. Findings are free from the researcher's personal bias
Show answer

Answer: D. Findings are free from the researcher's personal bias

Report this question

13Generalizability refers to:

  1. Using a large questionnaire
  2. Applying findings beyond the sample to the wider population
  3. Using only primary data
  4. Studying one case only
Show answer

Answer: B. Applying findings beyond the sample to the wider population

Report this question

14Consider the following statements:
I. Research is systematic.
II. Research relies on speculation alone.
III. Research is verifiable.

  1. I and II
  2. II and III
  3. I, II and III
  4. I and III
Show answer

Answer: D. I and III

15Assertion (A): Objectivity is essential in research.
Reason (R): It reduces the influence of the researcher's personal bias on findings.

  1. Both A and R are true, and R explains A
  2. Both true, but R does not explain A
  3. A is true, R is false
  4. A is false, R is true

16Assertion (A): Operational definitions improve the replicability of research.
Reason (R): They specify the exact procedures by which concepts are measured.

  1. Both true, and R explains A
  2. Both true, but R does not explain A
  3. A true, R false
  4. A false, R true

17Inductive reasoning moves from:

  1. General to specific

  3. Theory to hypothesis
