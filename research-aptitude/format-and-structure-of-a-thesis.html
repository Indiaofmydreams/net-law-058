25 practice questions with answers

1A thesis is:

  1. A newspaper column
  2. A questionnaire
  3. A systematic written account of research submitted for an academic degree
  4. A short abstract
Show answer

Answer: C. A systematic written account of research submitted for an academic degree

Report this question

2A thesis is conventionally divided into:

  1. Introduction and conclusion only
  2. Tables and figures only
  3. Abstract and appendix only
  4. Preliminary pages, main text and reference (end) matter
Show answer

Answer: D. Preliminary pages, main text and reference (end) matter

Report this question

3A research synopsis is:

  1. The final thesis
  2. The viva-voce report
  3. A brief outline of the proposed research submitted for registration or approval
  4. A questionnaire
Show answer

Answer: C. A brief outline of the proposed research submitted for registration or approval

Report this question

4In the Indian context, the Ph.D. degree is awarded on submission and acceptance of a:

  1. Questionnaire
  2. Thesis
  3. Seminar paper
  4. Abstract only
Show answer

Answer: B. Thesis

Report this question

5Which of the following belongs to the preliminary pages?

  1. Conclusion chapter
  2. Certificate
  3. Appendix
  4. Index
Show answer

Answer: B. Certificate

Report this question

6The declaration in a thesis states that:

  1. The supervisor is the author
  2. The thesis has been published
  3. The work is the researcher's own original work and has not been submitted elsewhere for another degree
  4. The thesis is plagiarised
Show answer

Answer: C. The work is the researcher's own original work and has not been submitted elsewhere for another degree

Report this question

7The certificate in a thesis is generally signed by the:

  1. Librarian
  2. Supervisor (guide)
  3. Respondents
  4. Publisher
Show answer

Answer: B. Supervisor (guide)

Report this question

8The acknowledgements page is meant to:

  1. Thank the persons and institutions who helped the researcher
  2. Summarise the findings
  3. List the references
  4. Define terms
Show answer

Answer: A. Thank the persons and institutions who helped the researcher

Report this question

9In most thesis formats, the preliminary pages are numbered with:

  1. Arabic numerals
  2. Letters only
  3. Roman numerals
  4. No numbering at all
Show answer

Answer: C. Roman numerals

Report this question

10The table of contents:

  1. Lists only the tables
  2. Lists the chapter and section headings with page numbers
  3. Gives the results
  4. Lists the respondents
Show answer

Answer: B. Lists the chapter and section headings with page numbers

Report this question

11The abstract of a thesis:

  1. Is a list of sources
  2. Contains the tools in full
  3. Contains only the title
  4. Is a concise summary of the problem, method, findings and conclusions
Show answer

Answer: D. Is a concise summary of the problem, method, findings and conclusions

Report this question

12Which of the following is NOT usually found on the title page of a thesis?

  1. Detailed results tables
  2. Title of the study
  3. Name of the researcher
  4. Degree and institution
Show answer

Answer: A. Detailed results tables

Report this question

13The introduction chapter usually contains:

  1. Final recommendations only
  2. Raw data
  3. The index
  4. Background, statement of the problem, objectives, hypotheses and significance
Show answer

Answer: D. Background, statement of the problem, objectives, hypotheses and significance

Report this question

14The review of related literature chapter:

  1. Critically examines earlier studies to show what is known and where the gap lies
  2. Reports the researcher's results
  3. Lists the questionnaire items
  4. Gives only the bibliography
Show answer

Answer: A. Critically examines earlier studies to show what is known and where the gap lies

Report this question

15Which of the following is NOT normally part of the methodology chapter?

  1. Sample
  2. Tools of data collection
  3. Final recommendations
  4. Procedure of data collection
Show answer

Answer: C. Final recommendations

Report this question

16The analysis and results chapter presents:

  1. Only the literature
  2. Findings supported by tables and figures
  3. Only the acknowledgements
  4. Only the bibliography
Show answer

Answer: B. Findings supported by tables and figures

Report this question

17The discussion of findings:

  1. Replaces the methodology
  2. Reproduces the review of literature
  3. Contains only the references
  4. Interprets the results in relation to the hypotheses, theory and earlier studies
Show answer

Answer: D. Interprets the results in relation to the hypotheses, theory and earlier studies

Report this question

18The final chapter of a thesis typically includes:

  1. The questionnaire
  2. The title page
  3. The index
  4. Summary, conclusions, implications and suggestions for further research
Show answer

Answer: D. Summary, conclusions, implications and suggestions for further research

Report this question

19Appendices are used to include:

  1. The conclusions
  2. The acknowledgements
  3. The title
  4. Supplementary material such as tools, raw data and permission letters
Show answer

Answer: D. Supplementary material such as tools, raw data and permission letters

Report this question

20The difference between "References" and "Bibliography" is that:

  1. They are always identical
  2. A bibliography lists only the cited works
  3. References list only the works cited in the text, while a bibliography may also include works consulted but not cited
  4. References are placed in the preliminary pages
Show answer

Answer: C. References list only the works cited in the text, while a bibliography may also include works consulted but not cited

Report this question

21An index at the end of a thesis or book:

  1. Contains the conclusions
  2. Is an alphabetical list of topics and names with page numbers
  3. Is the abstract
  4. Lists the authors' addresses
Show answer

Answer: B. Is an alphabetical list of topics and names with page numbers

Report this question

22A glossary:

  1. Explains technical terms and their meanings
  2. Lists the references
  3. Lists the tables
  4. Lists the hypotheses
Show answer

Answer: A. Explains technical terms and their meanings

23Under the UGC regulations, a copy of the Ph.D. thesis is deposited in the repository named:

  1. Shodhganga
  2. Scopus
  3. JSTOR
  4. Web of Science
Show answer

Answer: A. Shodhganga

24Under the UGC (Promotion of Academic Integrity and Prevention of Plagiarism in HEIs) Regulations, 2018, a similarity of up to what percentage falls in Level 0 (no penalty)?

  1. 5%
  2. 10%
  3. 20%
  4. 40%
Show answer

Answer: B. 10%

Report this question

25Which of the following is excluded from the similarity check under the UGC 2018 plagiarism regulations?

  1. References and bibliography
  2. Text copied without acknowledgement
  3. Copied results
  4. Tables copied without credit
Show answer

Answer: A. References and bibliography
