About this question set

This set has 19 multiple-choice questions on Types of Research from the UGC NET Paper 1 syllabus, unit 2, Research Aptitude. Try each question first, then tap Show answer to check the key. This set is an answer-key drill: check each answer, then revisit the notes for any you miss.

For the concepts and case law behind these questions, read the Research Aptitude notes. Once you are comfortable, test yourself under exam conditions with a full-length mock test.

19 practice questions with answers

1Qualitative research mainly deals with:

  1. Numerical data only
  2. Laboratory experiments only
  3. Non-numerical data such as meanings and experiences
  4. Statistical tables
Show answer

Answer: C. Non-numerical data such as meanings and experiences

Report this question

2Quantitative research mainly uses:

  1. Only interviews
  2. Numerical data and statistical analysis
  3. Only observation notes
  4. Only narratives
Show answer

Answer: B. Numerical data and statistical analysis

Report this question

3Mixed-methods research:

  1. Uses only surveys
  2. Combines qualitative and quantitative approaches
  3. Uses only experiments
  4. Avoids data collection
Show answer

Answer: B. Combines qualitative and quantitative approaches

Report this question

4Grounded theory is associated with:

  1. Glaser and Strauss
  2. Kuhn and Popper
  3. Comte and Mill
  4. Lewin and Corey
Show answer

Answer: A. Glaser and Strauss

Report this question

5Ethnography studies:

  1. The culture and behaviour of a group through immersion in its natural setting
  2. Statistical correlations
  3. Laboratory behaviour
  4. The impact of a policy
Show answer

Answer: A. The culture and behaviour of a group through immersion in its natural setting

Report this question

6Phenomenological research focuses on:

  1. Large-scale numerical trends
  2. Lived experiences of individuals
  3. Drug effects
  4. Archival records
Show answer

Answer: B. Lived experiences of individuals

Report this question

7Conceptual research relates to:

  1. Abstract ideas and theory development
  2. Laboratory trials
  3. Field surveys only
  4. Counting objects
Show answer

Answer: A. Abstract ideas and theory development

Report this question

8A case study is:

  1. A study of a large random sample
  2. A laboratory experiment
  3. A literature survey
  4. An in-depth study of a single unit or a few units
Show answer

Answer: D. An in-depth study of a single unit or a few units

Report this question

9Experimental research involves:

  1. No control over variables
  2. Manipulation of the independent variable under controlled conditions
  3. Only document analysis
  4. Only opinion polls
Show answer

Answer: B. Manipulation of the independent variable under controlled conditions

Report this question

10Historical research uses:

  1. Laboratory apparatus
  2. Past events and documents
  3. Random assignment
  4. Control groups
Show answer

Answer: B. Past events and documents

Report this question

11Ex post facto research:

  1. Manipulates variables before the event
  2. Is always laboratory-based
  3. Is purely theoretical
  4. Investigates events after they have occurred, without manipulation
Show answer

Answer: D. Investigates events after they have occurred, without manipulation

Report this question

12Evaluation research is mainly meant to:

  1. Build a grand theory
  2. Assess the effectiveness of a programme or intervention
  3. Record folk traditions
  4. Test only hypotheses
Show answer

Answer: B. Assess the effectiveness of a programme or intervention

Report this question

13Research that integrates methods or concepts from more than one discipline is:

  1. Pure experimental research
  2. Ex post facto research
  3. Replication research
  4. Interdisciplinary research
Show answer

Answer: D. Interdisciplinary research

Report this question

14Content analysis is:

  1. The systematic analysis of the content of documents, media or other communication
  2. A laboratory technique
  3. A drug trial
  4. A random sampling technique
Show answer

Answer: A. The systematic analysis of the content of documents, media or other communication

Report this question

15A census differs from a sample survey in that it:

  1. Covers only 10% of the units
  2. Is a pilot study
  3. Covers every unit of the population
  4. Studies one case
Show answer

Answer: C. Covers every unit of the population

Report this question

16Field research is conducted in:

  1. A controlled laboratory
  2. A library only
  3. A computer simulation only
  4. Real-life, natural settings
Show answer

Answer: D. Real-life, natural settings

Report this question

17A key strength of laboratory research is:

  1. High control over variables, though the setting is artificial
  2. High natural realism
  3. No need for hypotheses
  4. Absence of ethical issues
Show answer

Answer: A. High control over variables, though the setting is artificial

Report this question

18Meta-analysis is:

  1. A single-case study
  2. A sampling error
  3. A statistical technique for combining the results of multiple studies
  4. An interview technique
Show answer

Answer: C. A statistical technique for combining the results of multiple studies

Report this question

19The Delphi technique:

  1. Uses a single expert interview
  2. Gathers expert opinion over iterative rounds to reach consensus
  3. Is a laboratory experiment
  4. Is a type of census
