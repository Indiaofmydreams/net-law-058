UGC NET Paper 1 Unit 8: ICT Notes and MCQs

ICT questions are mostly full forms, definitions and names of government platforms. You do not need to be technical, only familiar with the terms. These notes gather the abbreviations and digital initiatives that appear most often.

What Unit 8 covers

ICT is a factual unit, so a short, regularly revised list of terms and platforms is the best preparation. The official syllabus lists 4 topics for this unit. Each is explained below with the points examiners most often use.

1ICT: general abbreviations and terminology

  • ICT means Information and Communication Technology. It covers computers, networks, software and all tools used to create, store, send and receive information.
  • Hardware is the physical part of a computer, such as the CPU, RAM and keyboard. Software is the set of programs, either system software (operating system) or application software (word processor).
  • A bit is the smallest unit of data. A byte is 8 bits. Memory units rise as KB, MB, GB and TB.
  • RAM is volatile working memory, and ROM is permanent read-only memory. Common file extensions are .docx (Word), .xlsx (Excel), .pptx (PowerPoint) and .pdf.

2Basics of internet, intranet, e-mail and audio/video conferencing

  • The internet is a global network of networks. The World Wide Web (WWW) is the system of linked pages on it, viewed with a browser and found with a search engine.
  • An intranet is a private network within an organisation. An extranet shares part of it with outside partners.
  • E-mail is asynchronous. To sends the message, Cc sends a visible copy, and Bcc sends a blind copy whose recipients are hidden from others.
  • Audio and video conferencing allows real-time meetings and online classes through tools such as Google Meet, Zoom, Microsoft Teams and Webex.

3Digital initiatives in higher education

  • SWAYAM offers MOOCs from school to postgraduate level. SWAYAM Prabha is a group of DTH television channels for educational content.
  • e-PG Pathshala gives e-content for postgraduate courses. Shodhganga is the repository of Indian theses and dissertations, run by INFLIBNET.
  • National Digital Library of India (NDLI), NPTEL (courses from the IITs and IISc), National Academic Depository (NAD) and the Academic Bank of Credits are other key initiatives.
  • Open educational resources (OER) are teaching and learning materials freely available for use and reuse.

4ICT and governance

  • e-governance is the use of ICT to deliver public services and run administration. Digital India was launched in July 2015 with pillars such as broadband highways, e-governance and universal access to mobile connectivity.
  • Examples: DigiLocker for documents, UMANG for services, UPI for payments, and the Aadhaar-enabled services.
  • The Information Technology Act, 2000 gives legal recognition to electronic records and digital signatures and deals with cyber offences.
  • Cyber safety basics: use strong passwords, two-factor authentication and firewalls, and watch for phishing, which is a fraudulent attempt to obtain sensitive information, often by fake e-mail.

How to study this unit

  • Make a one-page list of abbreviations and Indian platforms. Most ICT questions are full-form or match-the-following.
  • Learn what each government platform does in one phrase, for example Shodhganga means theses and SWAYAM means MOOCs.
  • Use the tools yourself: send an e-mail with Cc and Bcc, and join a video call. Hands-on practice makes terms easier to recall.
