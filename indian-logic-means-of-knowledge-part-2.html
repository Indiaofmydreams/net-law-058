About this question set

This set has 25 multiple-choice questions on Indian Logic: Means of Knowledge from the UGC NET Paper 1 syllabus, unit 6, Logical Reasoning. Try each question first, then tap Show answer to check the key. This set is an answer-key drill: check each answer, then revisit the notes for any you miss.

25 practice questions with answers

1Perception of absence (abhava) is explained by:

  1. Samyoga
  2. Jnanalakshana
  3. Visheshana-visheshya-bhava
  4. Samavaya
Show answer

Answer: C. Visheshana-visheshya-bhava

Report this question

2Which of the following is NOT an alaukika pratyaksha?

  1. Jnanalakshana
  2. Yogaja
  3. Samanyalakshana
  4. Samyoga
Show answer

Answer: D. Samyoga

Report this question

3Perception of all members of a class through the universal is called:

  1. Samanyalakshana
  2. Jnanalakshana
  3. Yogaja
  4. Savikalpaka
Show answer

Answer: A. Samanyalakshana

Report this question

4Seeing a piece of sandalwood and perceiving "fragrant sandalwood" is an example of:

  1. Yogaja
  2. Samyoga
  3. Jnanalakshana
  4. Samanyalakshana
Show answer

Answer: C. Jnanalakshana

Report this question

5Extraordinary perception of past, future and distant objects by yogic power is called:

  1. Yogaja
  2. Jnanalakshana
  3. Nirvikalpaka
  4. Samanyalakshana
Show answer

Answer: A. Yogaja

6Which is NOT a type of laukika sannikarsha?

  1. Samyoga
  2. Samyukta-samavaya
  3. Samavaya
  4. Jnanalakshana
Show answer

Answer: D. Jnanalakshana

7The literal meaning of "Anumana" is:

  1. Verbal knowledge
  2. Knowledge that follows other knowledge
  3. Knowledge of similarity
  4. Direct knowledge
Show answer

Answer: B. Knowledge that follows other knowledge

8The conclusion or inferential cognition produced by anumana is called:

  1. Anumiti
  2. Vyapti
  3. Paramarsha
  4. Pratijna

9The knowledge that the hetu, pervaded by the sadhya, is present in the paksha is called:

  1. Upamiti
  2. Paramarsha
  3. Pratijna
  4. Smriti

10Paksha corresponds to which term of the Western syllogism?

  1. Copula
  2. Major term
  3. Minor term

11Sadhya corresponds to the:

  1. Subject
  2. Minor term
  3. Middle term
  4. Major term

12Hetu (or Linga) corresponds to the:

  1. Minor term

  4. Middle term

14A locus where the sadhya is definitely known not to exist is called:

  1. Paksha
  2. Vipaksha
  3. Upadhi
  4. Sapaksha

15Vyapti means:

  1. Similarity between two objects
  2. Verbal testimony
  3. Invariable concomitance between hetu and sadhya
  4. Non-perception

16In Nyaya, vyapti is established through:

  1. Repeated observation of agreement in presence without any contrary instance, aided by tarka
  2. Authority alone
  3. A single observation
  4. Dream

Report this question

17A condition that is co-extensive with the sadhya but not with the hetu is called:

  1. Paksha
  2. Linga
  3. Nigamana
  4. Upadhi

Answer: D. Upadhi

18A vyapti in which hetu and sadhya are of equal extension is called:

  1. Upadhi
  2. Vishamavyapti
  3. Samavyapti
  4. Anvaya

19The three kinds of anumana mentioned in the Nyaya Sutra are:

  1. Svartha, Parartha, Ubhaya

  3. Laukika, Alaukika, Yogaja
  4. Kevalanvayi, Kevalavyatireki, Anvayavyatireki

  1. Sheshavat
