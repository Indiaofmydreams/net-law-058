14 practice questions with answers

1The Stockholm Conference 1972 produced the Stockholm Declaration with how many principles?

  1. 21 principles
  2. 24 principles
  3. 27 principles
  4. 26 principles
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. 26 principles

The Stockholm Declaration on the Human Environment (1972) contains 26 principles and a Preamble with 7 proclamations. The most famous are Principle 1 (human right to environment), Principle 21 (no-harm rule), and Principle 22 (state liability for transboundary harm). Contrast with Rio Declaration which has 27 principles.

2The Rio Declaration 1992 has how many principles?

  1. 25
  2. 26
  3. 27
  4. 30
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. 27

The Rio Declaration on Environment and Development (1992) contains 27 principles - one more than the Stockholm Declaration (26 principles). The additional principle reflects the evolution of international environmental law in the 20 years between the two conferences.

3India's first State Pollution Control Board was established in which state?

  1. Maharashtra
  2. West Bengal
  3. Tamil Nadu
  4. Orissa (now Odisha)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Orissa (now Odisha)

Orissa (now Odisha) established the first State Pollution Control Board in India under the Water (Prevention and Control of Pollution) Act 1974. This is a direct memory question - Odisha's SPCB was the pioneer before other states followed.

4The first session of the Conference of Parties (COP1) to UNFCCC was held in:

  1. Geneva, 1994
  2. Kyoto, 1997
  3. New York, 1992
  4. Berlin, 1995
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Berlin, 1995

COP1 was held in Berlin, Germany in 1995. It produced the 'Berlin Mandate' which called for negotiations on binding emission reduction commitments for developed countries - the process that led to the Kyoto Protocol at COP3 (1997).

5The International Day for the Abolition of Slavery is observed on:

  1. 10 December
  2. 1 August
  3. 2 December
  4. 25 March
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. 2 December

2 December is observed as the International Day for the Abolition of Slavery - the date on which the UN General Assembly adopted the Supplementary Convention on the Abolition of Slavery in 1949. The International Day of Remembrance of the Victims of Slavery and the Transatlantic Slave Trade is on 25 March.

6The 'Beti Bachao, Beti Padhao' scheme in India was launched in response to concerns about:

  1. Female literacy below 50%
  2. Female infanticide and declining child sex ratio
  3. Child marriage of girls
  4. Maternal mortality rate
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Female infanticide and declining child sex ratio

Beti Bachao, Beti Padhao scheme was launched in January 2015 in response to the declining Child Sex Ratio (CSR) - girls per 1000 boys - revealed by Census 2011 (919 girls per 1000 boys). The scheme addresses sex-selective abortion (PCPNDT Act 1994 enforcement) and girl child education.

7India ratified the Paris Agreement on:

  1. 12 December 2015 - same day as adoption
  2. 2 October 2016 - Gandhi Jayanti
  3. 22 April 2016 - Earth Day
  4. 26 January 2017
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. 2 October 2016 - Gandhi Jayanti

India ratified the Paris Agreement on 2 October 2016 - the birth anniversary of Mahatma Gandhi (Gandhi Jayanti). This was a symbolic choice reflecting India's commitment to sustainable development principles aligned with Gandhian philosophy of living in harmony with nature.

8The Persons with Disabilities Act 1995 provided for reservation of how many percent of vacancies in government establishments for persons with disabilities?

  1. 1%
  2. 3%
  3. 4%
  4. 5%
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. 3%

The Persons with Disabilities (Equal Opportunities, Protection of Rights and Full Participation) Act 1995 provided for 3% reservation in government establishments (1% each for visually impaired, hearing impaired, and orthopaedically handicapped). The RPwD Act 2016 increased this to 4% for government posts and 5% for higher education institutions.

9The first COP (Conference of Parties) to the Convention on Biological Diversity was held in:

  1. Geneva - 1995
  2. Buenos Aires - 1994
  3. Montreal - 2002
  4. Nassau, Bahamas - 1994
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Nassau, Bahamas - 1994

The first Conference of Parties (COP1) to the Convention on Biological Diversity was held in Nassau, Bahamas in November-December 1994. Subsequent COPs have been held biennially in various locations. COP10 (2010) in Nagoya produced the Nagoya Protocol on ABS.

10The 'National Action Plan on Climate Change' (NAPCC) has how many National Missions?

  1. 5
  2. 6
  3. 8
  4. 10
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. 8

India's National Action Plan on Climate Change (NAPCC, 2008) has eight national missions: National Solar Mission; National Mission for Enhanced Energy Efficiency; National Mission on Sustainable Habitat; National Water Mission; National Mission for Sustaining the Himalayan Ecosystem; National Mission for a Green India; National Mission for Sustainable Agriculture; National Mission for Strategic Knowledge for Climate Change.

11India ratified the ICCPR on:

  1. 26 January 1950
  2. 10 April 1979
  3. 12 November 1997
  4. 15 August 1980
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. 10 April 1979

India ratified the International Covenant on Civil and Political Rights (ICCPR) on 10 April 1979. India also ratified the ICESCR on the same date. However, India has not ratified the Optional Protocol to the ICCPR (individual complaint mechanism) or the Second Optional Protocol (death penalty abolition).

12The 'Persons with Disabilities Act 1995' was replaced in which year?

  1. 2006
  2. 2012
  3. 2016
  4. 2019
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. 2016

The Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act 2016 replaced the Persons with Disabilities (Equal Opportunities, Protection of Rights and Full Participation) Act 1995. The 2016 Act was enacted to align Indian law with the UN Convention on the Rights of Persons with Disabilities (CRPD) 2006, which India had ratified in 2007.

13The CRPD (Convention on Rights of Persons with Disabilities) was adopted in which year and India ratified it in which year?

  1. Adopted 2006, India ratified 2007
  2. Adopted 2004, India ratified 2006
  3. Adopted 2006, India ratified 2008
  4. Adopted 2008, India ratified 2010
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Adopted 2006, India ratified 2007

CRPD was adopted by UNGA on 13 December 2006 and entered into force in May 2008. India ratified the CRPD (and its Optional Protocol) on 1 October 2007 - making it one of the early ratifying states. This prompted India to eventually enact the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act 2016.

Report this question

14The ICCPR contains how many articles?

  1. 30
  2. 45
  3. 53
  4. 60
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. 53

The International Covenant on Civil and Political Rights (ICCPR) contains 53 articles divided into six parts: Part I (Art 1 - self-determination); Part II (Arts 2-5 - general obligations); Part III (Arts 6-27 - substantive rights); Part IV (Arts 28-45 - Human Rights Committee); Part V (Arts 46-47 - interaction with UN Charter); Part VI (Arts 48-53 - final clauses). This total of 53 articles is a frequently testable memory detail.
