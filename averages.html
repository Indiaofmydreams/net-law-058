About this question set

For the concepts and case law behind these questions, read the Mathematical Reasoning notes. Once you are comfortable, test yourself under exam conditions with a full-length mock test.

21 practice questions with answers

1The average of 5 numbers is 20. If one number, 30, is removed, the average of the remaining numbers is:

  1. 20
  2. 15
  3. 17.5
  4. 18
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. 17.5

The sum is 100, and (100 − 30) ÷ 4 = 17.5.

2The average of the first 10 natural numbers is:

  1. 4.5
  2. 6
  3. 5
  4. 5.5
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. 5.5

The sum is 55, and 55 ÷ 10 = 5.5.

3The average of 12, 15, 18, 21 and 24 is:

  1. 18
  2. 20
  3. 16
  4. 17
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. 18

The sum is 90, and 90 ÷ 5 = 18.

4The average of 6 numbers is 20. If 8 is replaced by 26, the new average is:

  1. 22
  2. 23
  3. 21
  4. 24
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. 23

The sum rises by 18, so the average rises by 18 ÷ 6 = 3.

Report this question

5The average age of 5 persons is 24 years. When a new person joins, the average becomes 25. The new person's age is:

  1. 32
  2. 36
  3. 30
  4. 28
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. 30

6 × 25 − 5 × 24 = 150 − 120 = 30.

6The average of the first 20 natural numbers is:

  1. 11.5
  2. 10
  3. 11
  4. 10.5
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. 10.5

The average of the first n natural numbers is (n + 1)/2 = 10.5.

830 students average 60 marks and 20 students average 70 marks. The overall average is:

  1. 64
  2. 62
  3. 65
  4. 66
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. 64

Weighted average = (1800 + 1400) ÷ 50 = 64.

9A batsman's average after 10 innings is 40. He scores 62 in the 11th innings. His new average is:

  1. 43
  2. 41
  3. 42
  4. 44
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. 42

(400 + 62) ÷ 11 = 42.

10A man travels equal distances at 40 km/h and 60 km/h. His average speed is:

  1. 52 km/h
  2. 45 km/h
  3. 50 km/h
  4. 48 km/h
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. 48 km/h

For equal distances, average speed = 2ab/(a + b) = 4800 ÷ 100 = 48.

11The average of 4 numbers is 15. Three of them are 10, 12 and 20. The fourth is:

  1. 18
  2. 22
  3. 20
  4. 16
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. 18

The total is 60, and 60 − 42 = 18.

12The average of 7 numbers is 15. The average of the first four is 12 and of the last four is 18. The fourth number is:

  1. 18
  2. 15
  3. 12
  4. 14
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. 15

The two groups total 48 + 72 = 120 and count the 4th number twice, so 4th = 120 − 105 = 15.

13The average weight of 8 persons rises by 2 kg when a person weighing 60 kg is replaced. The weight of the new person is:

  1. 68 kg
  2. 72 kg
  3. 80 kg
  4. 76 kg
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. 76 kg

The total rises by 8 × 2 = 16, so the new weight is 60 + 16 = 76.

14The average of the even numbers from 2 to 20 is:

  1. 12
  2. 10.5
  3. 11
  4. 10
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. 11

For an arithmetic series, the average is (first + last) ÷ 2 = 22 ÷ 2 = 11.

15The average marks in 5 subjects is 72. After a 6th subject the average becomes 74. The marks in the 6th subject are:

  1. 86
  2. 84
  3. 80
  4. 90
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. 84

6 × 74 − 5 × 72 = 444 − 360 = 84.

16The average of 11 numbers is 50. The average of the first six is 49 and of the last six is 52. The 6th number is:

  1. 56
  2. 58
  3. 60
  4. 54

Answer: A. 56

The two groups total 294 + 312 = 606 and count the 6th number twice, and 606 − 550 = 56.

17The average of 5 numbers is 27. When one number is removed, the average of the remaining numbers is 25. The number removed is:

  1. 30
  2. 40

  4. 45

5 × 27 − 4 × 25 = 135 − 100 = 35.

18The average age of 30 students is 14 years. When the teacher is included, the average becomes 15. The teacher's age is:

  1. 44
  2. 46
  3. 48
  4. 45

Answer: D. 45

31 × 15 − 30 × 14 = 465 − 420 = 45.
