1A 20% discount on ₹500 gives a price of:

  1. ₹380
  2. ₹400
  3. ₹420
  4. ₹450
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. ₹400

500 − 100 = 400.

13Mathematical induction, used to prove a statement for all natural numbers, is actually a form of:

  1. Abductive guess
  2. Enumerative induction
  3. Deductive proof
  4. Analogical reasoning
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Deductive proof

It uses a base case and an inductive step, and the proof is deductive.

Report this question

14"A number divisible by both 2 and 3 is divisible by 6. 84 is divisible by 2 and 3. Hence 84 is divisible by 6." This reasoning is:

  1. Inductive
  2. Deductive
  3. Analogical
  4. Abductive
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Deductive

A general rule applied to a specific number gives a necessary conclusion.

Report this question

15"If x is even, then x² is even. x² = 49, which is not even. So x is not even." This is:

  1. Denying the antecedent
  2. Affirming the consequent
  3. Modus Ponens
  4. Modus Tollens
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Modus Tollens

Deny the consequent, so the antecedent is denied.

Report this question

16"If n is divisible by 4, then n is even. 18 is even. So 18 is divisible by 4." This reasoning is:

  1. The fallacy of affirming the consequent
  2. Valid Modus Ponens
  3. Valid disjunctive syllogism
  4. Valid Modus Tollens
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. The fallacy of affirming the consequent

From "even" you cannot go back to "divisible by 4" (for example 18 is even but not divisible by 4).

17A father is 3 times as old as his son. The sum of their ages is 48. The son's age is:

  1. 12
  2. 15
  3. 16
  4. 10
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. 12

3x + x = 48, so x = 12.

18A father is 30 years older than his son. After 5 years, the father's age will be 4 times the son's age. The son's present age is:

  1. 10
  2. 6
  3. 5
  4. 8
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. 5

X + 35 = 4(x + 5), so 3x = 15 and x = 5.
