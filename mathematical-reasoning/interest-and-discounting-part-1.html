About this question set

This set has 13 multiple-choice questions on Interest and Discounting from the UGC NET Paper 1 syllabus, unit 5, Mathematical Reasoning and Aptitude. Try each question first, then tap Show answer to check the key and read the explanation. 13 of the 13 questions carry a written explanation.

13 practice questions with answers

1The simple interest on ₹2,000 at 5% per annum for 3 years is:

  1. ₹330
  2. ₹300
  3. ₹200
  4. ₹100
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. ₹300

SI = (P × R × T) ÷ 100 = (2000 × 5 × 3) ÷ 100 = 300.

2The compound interest on ₹1,000 at 10% per annum for 2 years is:

  1. ₹220
  2. ₹100
  3. ₹210
  4. ₹200
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. ₹210

The amount is 1000 × 1.1 × 1.1 = 1210, so CI = 210.

Report this question

3The simple interest on ₹5,000 at 8% per annum for 2 years is:

  1. ₹800
  2. ₹400
  3. ₹840
  4. ₹1,000
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. ₹800

SI = (P × R × T) ÷ 100 = (5000 × 8 × 2) ÷ 100 = 800.

Report this question

4A sum amounts to ₹1,260 in 2 years at 10% simple interest. The principal is:

  1. ₹1,100
  2. ₹1,200
  3. ₹1,000
  4. ₹1,050
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. ₹1,050

P × (1 + 0.2) = 1260, so P = 1050.

Report this question

5The simple interest on ₹4,000 in 3 years is ₹960. The rate of interest is:

  1. 6%
  2. 7%
  3. 8%
  4. 9%
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. 8%

R = (SI × 100) ÷ (P × T) = 96000 ÷ 12000 = 8.

Report this question

6In how many years will ₹2,000 become ₹2,600 at 10% simple interest?

  1. 2
  2. 3
  3. 5
  4. 4
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. 3

SI = 600, so T = (600 × 100) ÷ (2000 × 10) = 3.

Report this question

7The compound interest on ₹8,000 at 5% per annum for 2 years is:

  1. ₹820
  2. ₹800
  3. ₹840
  4. ₹810
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. ₹820

The amount is 8000 × 1.05² = 8820, so CI = 820.

Report this question

8The difference between compound interest and simple interest on ₹5,000 at 10% per annum for 2 years is:

  1. ₹75
  2. ₹100
  3. ₹25
  4. ₹50
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. ₹50

The difference for 2 years is P(R/100)² = 5000 × 0.01 = 50.

Report this question

9₹16,000 is invested at 10% per annum compounded half-yearly for 1 year. The amount is:

  1. ₹17,680
  2. ₹17,640
  3. ₹17,600
  4. ₹17,500
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. ₹17,640

The rate is 5% per half-year for 2 periods, so 16000 × 1.05² = 17640.

Report this question

10A sum doubles itself in 8 years at simple interest. The rate per annum is:

  1. 10%
  2. 8%
  3. 12.5%
  4. 15%
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. 12.5%

SI = P in 8 years, so R = 100 ÷ 8 = 12.5.

Report this question

11The true discount on a bill of ₹1,100 due 1 year hence at 10% p.a. simple interest is:

  1. ₹110
  2. ₹120
  3. ₹90
  4. ₹100
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. ₹100

Present worth = 1100 ÷ 1.1 = 1000, so TD = 1100 − 1000 = 100.

Report this question

12Banker's discount is the simple interest on the:

  1. Face value of the bill
  2. True discount
  3. Present worth
  4. Cash value
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Face value of the bill

BD is charged on the amount of the bill (face value) for the unexpired time.

Report this question

13Banker's gain (BG) equals:

  1. BD × TD
  2. BD − TD
  3. TD − BD
  4. BD + TD
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. BD − TD

BG is the difference between banker's discount and true discount, and it equals the interest on the true discount.
