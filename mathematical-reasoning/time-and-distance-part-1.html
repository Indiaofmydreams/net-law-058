About this question set

This set has 15 multiple-choice questions on Time and Distance from the UGC NET Paper 1 syllabus, unit 5, Mathematical Reasoning and Aptitude. Try each question first, then tap Show answer to check the key and read the explanation. 15 of the 15 questions carry a written explanation.

For the concepts and case law behind these questions, read the Mathematical Reasoning notes. Once you are comfortable, test yourself under exam conditions with a full-length mock test.

15 practice questions with answers

1A car travels at 60 km/h for 2.5 hours. The distance covered is:

  1. 150 km
  2. 160 km
  3. 125 km
  4. 140 km
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. 150 km

Distance = speed × time = 60 × 2.5 = 150.

Report this question

2A train 120 m long crosses a pole in 6 seconds. Its speed is:

  1. 54 km/h
  2. 60 km/h
  3. 72 km/h
  4. 80 km/h
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. 72 km/h

120 ÷ 6 = 20 m/s, and 20 × 18/5 = 72 km/h.

Report this question

3A boat's speed in still water is 12 km/h and the stream flows at 3 km/h. Its downstream speed is:

  1. 9 km/h
  2. 12 km/h
  3. 18 km/h
  4. 15 km/h
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. 15 km/h

Downstream speed = boat speed + stream speed.

472 km/h in m/s is:

  1. 15
  2. 20
  3. 25
  4. 18
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. 20

Multiply km/h by 5/18, so 72 × 5/18 = 20.

525 m/s in km/h is:

  1. 80
  2. 100
  3. 90
  4. 75
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. 90

Multiply m/s by 18/5, so 25 × 18/5 = 90.

6A train 150 m long passes a pole in 10 seconds. Its speed is:

  1. 72 km/h
  2. 48 km/h
  3. 60 km/h
  4. 54 km/h
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. 54 km/h

150 ÷ 10 = 15 m/s, and 15 × 18/5 = 54.

Report this question

7A train 200 m long crosses a 300 m bridge in 25 seconds. Its speed is:

  1. 72 km/h
  2. 80 km/h
  3. 60 km/h
  4. 54 km/h
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. 72 km/h

The distance covered is 500 m, so 500 ÷ 25 = 20 m/s = 72 km/h.

Report this question

8Two trains 100 m and 150 m long run in opposite directions at 36 km/h and 54 km/h. They cross each other in:

  1. 15 s
  2. 10 s
  3. 12 s
  4. 8 s
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. 10 s

The relative speed is 90 km/h = 25 m/s, and 250 ÷ 25 = 10.

Report this question

9A man walks at 5 km/h for 3 hours and then at 4 km/h for 2 hours. His average speed is:

  1. 4.8 km/h
  2. 5 km/h
  3. 4.6 km/h
  4. 4.5 km/h
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. 4.6 km/h

Total distance = 15 + 8 = 23 km in 5 hours, and 23 ÷ 5 = 4.6.

Report this question

10A boat's speed in still water is 10 km/h and the stream flows at 2 km/h. The time to go 24 km upstream is:

  1. 2 h
  2. 4 h
  3. 2.4 h
  4. 3 h
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. 3 h

The upstream speed is 10 − 2 = 8 km/h, so 24 ÷ 8 = 3.

Report this question

11A boat goes 30 km downstream in 2 hours and returns upstream in 3 hours. The speed of the stream is:

  1. 2 km/h
  2. 2.5 km/h
  3. 3 km/h
  4. 5 km/h
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. 2.5 km/h

The speeds are 15 and 10 km/h, so the stream speed is (15 − 10) ÷ 2 = 2.5.

Report this question

12Two cars travel in the same direction at 60 km/h and 40 km/h, with the faster car 100 km behind. How long does it take to catch up?

  1. 5 h
  2. 2 h
  3. 4 h
  4. 10 h
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. 5 h

The relative speed is 20 km/h, so 100 ÷ 20 = 5.

Report this question

13Statement I: The average speed over equal distances at speeds a and b is (a + b)/2.
Statement II: The average speed over equal distances at speeds a and b is 2ab/(a + b).

  1. I true, II false
  2. Both true
  3. I false, II true
  4. Both false
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. I false, II true

For equal distances the harmonic mean applies, and the arithmetic mean applies only for equal times.

Report this question

14The distance covered in 45 minutes at 80 km/h is:

  1. 50 km
  2. 55 km
  3. 64 km
  4. 60 km
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. 60 km

45 minutes = 0.75 h, and 80 × 0.75 = 60.

Report this question

15A train 180 m long passes a man walking in the same direction at 6 km/h in 18 seconds. The train's speed is:

  1. 54 km/h
  2. 42 km/h
  3. 36 km/h
  4. 48 km/h
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. 42 km/h

The relative speed is 180 ÷ 18 = 10 m/s = 36 km/h, so the train's speed is 36 + 6 = 42 km/h.
