About this question set

This set has 16 multiple-choice questions on Profit and Loss from the UGC NET Paper 1 syllabus, unit 5, Mathematical Reasoning and Aptitude. Try each question first, then tap Show answer to check the key and read the explanation. 16 of the 16 questions carry a written explanation.

For the concepts and case law behind these questions, read the Mathematical Reasoning notes. Once you are comfortable, test yourself under exam conditions with a full-length mock test.

16 practice questions with answers

1An article bought for ₹400 is sold for ₹500. The profit percentage is:

  1. 22.5%
  2. 20%
  3. 25%
  4. 12.5%
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. 25%

Profit % = (100 ÷ 400) × 100 = 25%.

Report this question

2An article marked at ₹800 is sold at a 10% discount. The selling price is:

  1. ₹780
  2. ₹700
  3. ₹750
  4. ₹720
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. ₹720

800 × 0.9 = 720.

3Two successive discounts of 10% and 20% are equal to a single discount of:

  1. 30%
  2. 28%
  3. 32%
  4. 25%
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. 28%

0.9 × 0.8 = 0.72, so the discount is 28%.

Report this question

4An article bought for ₹500 is sold for ₹575. The profit percentage is:

  1. 15%
  2. 20%
  3. 12.5%
  4. 17.5%
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. 15%

75 ÷ 500 × 100 = 15.

Report this question

5An article bought for ₹800 is sold for ₹680. The loss percentage is:

  1. 18%
  2. 20%
  3. 12%
  4. 15%
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. 15%

120 ÷ 800 × 100 = 15.

6The selling price of an article with cost price ₹600 and a 20% profit is:

  1. ₹780
  2. ₹720
  3. ₹750
  4. ₹700
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. ₹720

600 × 1.2 = 720.

Report this question

7An article marked at ₹1,000 is sold with successive discounts of 20% and 10%. The selling price is:

  1. ₹720
  2. ₹680
  3. ₹700
  4. ₹750
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. ₹720

1000 × 0.8 × 0.9 = 720.

Report this question

8A trader marks goods 25% above cost price and allows a 20% discount. His result is:

  1. 25% profit
  2. 5% loss
  3. No profit, no loss
  4. 5% profit
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. No profit, no loss

1.25 × 0.8 = 1.00, so the selling price equals the cost price.

Report this question

9When an article is sold at a 10% loss, it fetches ₹120 less than if it were sold at a 5% gain. The cost price is:

  1. ₹1,000
  2. ₹600
  3. ₹800
  4. ₹750
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. ₹800

The difference of 15% of CP is 120, so CP = 800.

Report this question

10Two articles are each sold for ₹990, one at 10% profit and the other at 10% loss. The overall result is:

  1. 2% loss
  2. No loss
  3. 1% gain
  4. 1% loss
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. 1% loss

When the selling prices are equal and the profit and loss percentages are the same (x%), there is a loss of x²/100 %, here 1%.

Report this question

11If the cost price of 10 articles equals the selling price of 8 articles, the profit percentage is:

  1. 25%
  2. 15%
  3. 30%
  4. 20%
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. 25%

SP/CP = 10/8 = 1.25, so the profit is 25%.

Report this question

12A shopkeeper sells at cost price but uses a weight of 900 g instead of 1 kg. His gain percentage is:

  1. 12%
  2. 11 1/9 %
  3. 9%
  4. 10%
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. 11 1/9 %

The gain is 100 ÷ 900 × 100 = 11 1/9 %.

Report this question

13A discount of 15% is given on a marked price of ₹2,000. The selling price is:

  1. ₹1,700
  2. ₹1,800
  3. ₹1,600
  4. ₹1,750
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. ₹1,700

The discount is 300, so SP = 2000 − 300 = 1700.

Report this question

14Two successive discounts of 20% and 25% are equal to a single discount of:

  1. 42.5%
  2. 45%
  3. 35%
  4. 40%
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. 40%

0.8 × 0.75 = 0.6, so the discount is 40%.

Report this question

15Statement I: Profit percentage is calculated on the cost price.
Statement II: Discount percentage is calculated on the marked price.

  1. I false, II true
  2. I true, II false
  3. Both true
  4. Both false
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Both true

These are the standard bases for the two percentages.

Report this question

16Match List I with List II:
List I: (a) Profit % (b) Loss % (c) Discount % (d) Selling price at a profit of p%
List II: (i) (MP − SP)/MP × 100 (ii) (CP − SP)/CP × 100 (iii) (SP − CP)/CP × 100 (iv) CP × (1 + p/100)

  1. a-iii, b-i, c-ii, d-iv
  2. a-iii, b-ii, c-i, d-iv
  3. a-i, b-ii, c-iii, d-iv
  4. a-ii, b-iii, c-i, d-iv
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. a-iii, b-ii, c-i, d-iv

Profit and loss are measured on CP, and discount on the marked price.
