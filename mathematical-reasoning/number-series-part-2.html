1Find the next number: 1, 4, 27, 16, 125, 36, 343, ?

  1. 64
  2. 49
  3. 81
  4. 512
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. 64

Alternate cubes (1³, 3³, 5³, 7³) and squares (2², 4², 6², 8²), so the next is 8² = 64.

13Statement I: In the series 4, 9, 16, 25, 36, every term is a perfect square.
Statement II: The next term of the series is 49.

  1. Both true
  2. I false, II true
  3. Both false
  4. I true, II false
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Both true

The squares of 2 to 6 are followed by 7² = 49.

Report this question

14Match each series with its rule:
List I: (a) 3, 6, 12, 24 (b) 2, 5, 8, 11 (c) 1, 4, 9, 16 (d) 1, 1, 2, 3, 5
List II: (i) Fibonacci (ii) Perfect squares (iii) Arithmetic progression (iv) Geometric progression

  1. a-iv, b-iii, c-i, d-ii
  2. a-iv, b-iii, c-ii, d-i
  3. a-iii, b-iv, c-ii, d-i
  4. a-i, b-ii, c-iii, d-iv
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. a-iv, b-iii, c-ii, d-i

A ratio of 2 means GP, a constant difference means AP, and each term as the sum of the two before it means Fibonacci.
