About this question set

This set has 18 multiple-choice questions on Coding-Decoding from the UGC NET Paper 1 syllabus, unit 5, Mathematical Reasoning and Aptitude. Try each question first, then tap Show answer to check the key and read the explanation. 18 of the 18 questions carry a written explanation.

18 practice questions with answers

1If the letters in each adjacent pair are interchanged, 'PLAN' becomes 'LPNA'. Then 'STAR' becomes:

  1. TSRA
  2. RATS
  3. STRA
  4. TSAR
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. TSRA

Swap letters 1 and 2, then 3 and 4, giving TS and RA.

2If sweet is called bitter, bitter is called sour, sour is called salty and salty is called spicy, which taste would a lemon have in this code?

  1. Bitter
  2. Salty
  3. Spicy
  4. Sour
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Salty

A lemon is sour, and sour is called salty.

Report this question

3In a code, 'P + Q' means P is the father of Q, 'P − Q' means P is the sister of Q and 'P × Q' means P is the mother of Q. Which expression means "A is the grandmother of C"?

  1. A × B − C
  2. A − B × C
  3. A + B × C
  4. A × B + C
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. A × B + C

A is the mother of B, and B is the father of C, so A is C's grandmother.

4Using the same code, which expression means "A is the maternal aunt of C"?

  1. A × B − C
  2. A + B − C
  3. A − B × C
  4. A × B + C
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. A − B × C

A is the sister of B, and B is the mother of C, so A is C's maternal aunt.

5If A = 2, B = 4, C = 6 and so on (each letter is worth twice its position), the value of BAD is:

  1. 14
  2. 16
  3. 18
  4. 12
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. 14

B = 4, A = 2, D = 8, so the total is 14.

6If CODE is written as DQEG, then BOLD is written as:

  1. DQMF
  2. CQMF
  3. CQMG
  4. CPMF
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. CQMF

The shifts alternate +1, +2, +1, +2, so B→C, O→Q, L→M, D→F.

7Match each coding rule with its code for CAT:
List I: (a) +1 shift (b) Mirror alphabet (A = Z) (c) Reversal of the word (d) Position numbers
List II: (i) DBU (ii) XZG (iii) TAC (iv) 3-1-20

  1. a-ii, b-i, c-iii, d-iv
  2. a-i, b-iii, c-ii, d-iv
  3. a-iv, b-ii, c-iii, d-i
  4. a-i, b-ii, c-iii, d-iv
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. a-i, b-ii, c-iii, d-iv

Each rule produces its own code for CAT.

8If MOTHER is written as OQVJGT, then FATHER is written as:

  1. HCVJGT
  2. GCVJGT
  3. HCVJFT
  4. HBVJGT
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. HCVJGT

Each letter moves +2, so F→H, A→C, T→V, H→J, E→G, R→T.

9If TABLE is coded as UZCKF, then CHAIR is coded as:

  1. DGBHR
  2. DHBGS
  3. DGBHS
  4. BGDHS
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. DGBHS

The shifts alternate +1, −1, +1, −1, +1, so C→D, H→G, A→B, I→H, R→S.

10If each letter is replaced by its position in the alphabet, the product of the values for BED is:

  1. 20
  2. 40
  3. 30
  4. 11
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. 40

B = 2, E = 5, D = 4, so 2 × 5 × 4 = 40.

11In a code, 'rim tak' means 'green apple', 'tak sun' means 'apple juice' and 'lo sun' means 'fresh juice'. The code for 'green' is:

  1. sun
  2. lo
  3. tak
  4. rim
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. rim

'tak' is common to the first two statements and means "apple", so the other word in the first statement, 'rim', means "green".

12In a code, 'pa ni ku' means 'we love music', 'ni ra' means 'love songs' and 'ku sa' means 'music class'. The code for 'we' is:

  1. pa
  2. ku
  3. ni
  4. ra
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. pa

'ni' is "love" and 'ku' is "music", so the leftover code in statement 1, 'pa', is "we".

13If '+' means '−', '−' means '×', '×' means '÷' and '÷' means '+', then 20 × 4 + 3 − 2 ÷ 5 = ?

  1. 3
  2. 4
  3. 6
  4. 5
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. 4

Replace the symbols to get 20 ÷ 4 − 3 × 2 + 5 = 5 − 6 + 5 = 4.

14If A means +, B means −, C means × and D means ÷, then 36 D 6 C 3 A 4 B 2 = ?

  1. 24
  2. 18
  3. 20
  4. 22
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. 20

36 ÷ 6 × 3 + 4 − 2 = 18 + 4 − 2 = 20.

15If SOLID is coded as TQOMI, then MOUSE is coded as:

  1. MQXWJ
  2. NPXWJ
  3. NQXVJ
  4. NQXWJ
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. NQXWJ

The shifts are +1, +2, +3, +4, +5, so M→N, O→Q, U→X, S→W, E→J.

16If DELHI is coded as WVOSR, then PUNE is coded as:

  1. KFMV
  2. KFNV
  3. KGMV
  4. JFMV
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. KFMV

This is the mirror alphabet (A↔Z), so P→K, U→F, N→M, E→V.

17If PLAN is coded as NJYL, then FUND is coded as:

  1. ESLB
  2. DTLB
  3. DSLB
  4. DSLC
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. DSLB

Each letter moves −2, so F→D, U→S, N→L, D→B.

18In a code, 'A # B' means A is the brother of B, 'A @ B' means A is the father of B and 'A $ B' means A is the mother of B. Which expression means "P is the paternal uncle of R"?

  1. P $ Q # R
  2. P # Q @ R
  3. P @ Q # R
  4. P # Q $ R
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. P # Q @ R

P is the brother of Q, and Q is the father of R, so P is R's father's brother.
