15 practice questions with answers

1If 3 pencils cost ₹15, what is the cost of 12 pencils?

  1. ₹45
  2. ₹50
  3. ₹75
  4. ₹60
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. ₹60

Direct proportion. 12 is 4 times 3, so 4 × 15 = 60.

2If A : B = 2 : 3 and B : C = 4 : 5, then A : B : C is:

  1. 8 : 12 : 15
  2. 4 : 6 : 5
  3. 6 : 9 : 10
  4. 2 : 3 : 5
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. 8 : 12 : 15

Make B equal in both ratios. 2:3 becomes 8:12 and 4:5 becomes 12:15.

Report this question

3₹720 is divided in the ratio 4 : 5. The larger share is:

  1. ₹320
  2. ₹400
  3. ₹450
  4. ₹360
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. ₹400

One part is 720 ÷ 9 = 80, so the larger share is 5 × 80 = 400.

Report this question

4₹1,200 is divided among A, B and C in the ratio 2 : 3 : 5. B's share is:

  1. ₹400
  2. ₹240
  3. ₹360
  4. ₹600
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. ₹360

One part is 1200 ÷ 10 = 120, so B gets 3 × 120 = 360.

Report this question

5If a : b = 3 : 4 and b : c = 6 : 7, then a : b : c is:

  1. 9 : 12 : 7
  2. 3 : 6 : 7
  3. 3 : 4 : 7
  4. 9 : 12 : 14
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. 9 : 12 : 14

Make b equal in both ratios. 3 : 4 becomes 9 : 12 and 6 : 7 becomes 12 : 14.

6The fourth proportional to 4, 9 and 12 is:

  1. 27
  2. 36
  3. 18
  4. 24
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. 27

4 : 9 = 12 : x, so x = 9 × 12 ÷ 4 = 27.

7The mean proportional between 4 and 16 is:

  1. 10
  2. 8
  3. 12
  4. 6
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. 8

√(4 × 16) = 8.

8The third proportional to 9 and 12 is:

  1. 18
  2. 14
  3. 16
  4. 15
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. 16

9 : 12 = 12 : x, so x = 144 ÷ 9 = 16.

9Two numbers are in the ratio 5 : 7 and their sum is 96. Their difference is:

  1. 16
  2. 18
  3. 12
  4. 14
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. 16

One part is 96 ÷ 12 = 8, and the difference is 2 parts = 16.

1015 men can build a wall in 12 days. How many days will 20 men take?

  1. 16
  2. 10
  3. 8
  4. 9
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. 9

Inverse proportion. Total work is 15 × 12 = 180 man-days, and 180 ÷ 20 = 9.

11A invests ₹6,000 and B invests ₹9,000 for the same period. Out of a profit of ₹3,000, A's share is:

  1. ₹1,500
  2. ₹1,200
  3. ₹1,000
  4. ₹1,800
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. ₹1,200

The ratio is 2 : 3, so A gets 2/5 × 3000 = 1200.

Report this question

12A invests ₹5,000 for 12 months and B invests ₹6,000 for 6 months. Out of a profit of ₹4,000, A gets:

  1. ₹2,000
  2. ₹1,500
  3. ₹2,500
  4. ₹3,000
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. ₹2,500

The ratio is (5000 × 12) : (6000 × 6) = 5 : 3, so A gets 5/8 × 4000 = 2500.

Report this question

13A 40-litre mixture contains milk and water in the ratio 3 : 1. How much water must be added to make the ratio 3 : 2?

  1. 8 L
  2. 15 L
  3. 5 L
  4. 10 L
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. 10 L

Milk = 30 L and water = 10 L. For 3 : 2 the water must be 20 L, so add 10 L.

Report this question

14In what ratio should rice at ₹30/kg be mixed with rice at ₹40/kg to get a mixture worth ₹36/kg (cheaper : dearer)?

  1. 2 : 3
  2. 4 : 3
  3. 1 : 2
  4. 3 : 2
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. 2 : 3

Allegation gives (40 − 36) : (36 − 30) = 4 : 6 = 2 : 3.

Report this question

15If x : y = 3 : 4, then (2x + y) : (x + 3y) = ?

  1. 5 : 7
  2. 2 : 3
  3. 4 : 5
  4. 3 : 4
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. 2 : 3

Take x = 3 and y = 4, so (6 + 4) : (3 + 12) = 10 : 15 = 2 : 3.
