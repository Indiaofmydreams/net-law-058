About this question set

This set has 13 multiple-choice questions on Percentage from the UGC NET Paper 1 syllabus, unit 5, Mathematical Reasoning and Aptitude. Try each question first, then tap Show answer to check the key and read the explanation. 13 of the 13 questions carry a written explanation.

For the concepts and case law behind these questions, read the Mathematical Reasoning notes. Once you are comfortable, test yourself under exam conditions with a full-length mock test.

13 practice questions with answers

1Assertion (A): A 50% increase followed by a 50% decrease results in a net loss of 25%.
Reason (R): 1.5 × 0.5 = 0.75, which is 75% of the original value.

  1. A false, R true
  2. Both true, and R is the correct explanation of A
  3. A true, R false
  4. Both false
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Both true, and R is the correct explanation of A

The final value is 75% of the original, which is a 25% loss.

212.5% of 640 is:

  1. 80
  2. 64
  3. 75
  4. 90
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. 80

12.5% = 1/8, and 640 ÷ 8 = 80.

3If each side of a square is increased by 10%, its area increases by:

  1. 22%
  2. 10%
  3. 21%
  4. 20%
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. 21%

1.1² = 1.21, so the area is up by 21%.

4Candidate A gets 55% of the votes and wins by 2,000 votes. The total number of valid votes is:

  1. 18,000
  2. 15,000
  3. 25,000
  4. 20,000
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. 20,000

The margin is 55% − 45% = 10%, so 10% of the total is 2000 and the total is 20,000.

5A salary is reduced by 20%. By what percentage must it be increased to restore the original?

  1. 20%
  2. 25%
  3. 30%
  4. 15%
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. 25%

From 80 back to 100 is an increase of 20 ÷ 80 × 100 = 25%.

640% of a number is 60 more than 25% of the number. The number is:

  1. 600
  2. 500
  3. 400
  4. 300
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. 400

15% of the number is 60, so the number is 400.

7The pass mark is 35%. A student gets 210 marks and fails by 14 marks. The maximum marks are:

  1. 640
  2. 700
  3. 600
  4. 560
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. 640

The pass mark is 224, so the maximum is 224 ÷ 0.35 = 640.

Report this question

8If 18% of a number is 63, the number is:

  1. 420
  2. 400
  3. 350
  4. 300
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. 350

63 ÷ 0.18 = 350.

Report this question

9A's income is 20% less than B's. B's income is more than A's by:

  1. 20%
  2. 25%
  3. 30%
  4. 22%
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. 25%

With B = 100 and A = 80, the difference is 20 ÷ 80 × 100 = 25%.

Report this question

10In an exam, 80% passed Maths, 70% passed Science and 60% passed both. The percentage who failed both is:

  1. 30%
  2. 40%
  3. 20%
  4. 10%
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. 10%

Passed at least one = 80 + 70 − 60 = 90%, so 10% failed both.

11The length of a rectangle is increased by 25% and its breadth by 20%. The area increases by:

  1. 50%
  2. 45%
  3. 55%
  4. 40%
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. 50%

1.25 × 1.2 = 1.5.

12A seller sells 40% of his apples and is left with 420. He originally had:

  1. 750
  2. 700
  3. 800
  4. 600
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. 700

60% of the total is 420, so the total is 420 ÷ 0.6 = 700.

13A village's population increases by 10% in the first year and decreases by 10% in the second. The net change is:

  1. 1% increase
  2. No change
  3. 1% decrease
  4. 2% decrease
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. 1% decrease

1.1 × 0.9 = 0.99.
