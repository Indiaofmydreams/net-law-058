About this question set

This set has 15 multiple-choice questions on Ratio, Proportion and Partnership from the UGC NET Paper 1 syllabus, unit 5, Mathematical Reasoning and Aptitude. For the concepts and case law behind these questions, read the Mathematical Reasoning notes. Once you are comfortable, test yourself under exam conditions with a full-length mock test.

15 practice questions with answers

1A car uses 8 litres of fuel for 100 km. For 250 km it will use:

  1. 16 L
  2. 18 L
  3. 20 L
  4. 25 L
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. 20 L

Direct proportion, so 8 × 250 ÷ 100 = 20.

2Statement I: If a : b = c : d, then ad = bc.
Statement II: If a : b = c : d, then (a + b) : b = (c + d) : d.

  1. Both false
  2. I true, II false
  3. I false, II true
  4. Both true
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Both true

The cross-product rule and componendo are both valid properties of proportion.

Report this question

3The ratio of boys to girls in a class is 5 : 4 and there are 45 students. The number of girls is:

  1. 25
  2. 24
  3. 20
  4. 18
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. 20

4/9 × 45 = 20.

Report this question

4The ages of A and B are in the ratio 3 : 5. After 6 years the ratio becomes 2 : 3. A's present age is:

  1. 24
  2. 18
  3. 15
  4. 12
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. 18

(3x + 6) : (5x + 6) = 2 : 3 gives 9x + 18 = 10x + 12, so x = 6 and A = 18.

Report this question

5A, B and C invest in the ratio 3 : 4 : 5. Out of a profit of ₹6,000, C's share is:

  1. ₹2,500
  2. ₹1,500
  3. ₹2,000
  4. ₹3,000
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. ₹2,500

C's share is 5/12 × 6000 = 2500.

6A 60-litre mixture of milk and water is in the ratio 2 : 1. How much water must be added to make the ratio 1 : 1?

  1. 15 L
  2. 30 L
  3. 10 L
  4. 20 L
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. 20 L

Milk is 40 L and water is 20 L, so 20 L more water gives 1 : 1.

Report this question

76 men can reap a field in 12 days. How many men are needed to reap it in 8 days?

  1. 9
  2. 8
  3. 10
  4. 12
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. 9

The work is 6 × 12 = 72 man-days, and 72 ÷ 8 = 9.

8If 5 : x = x : 20, then x is:

  1. 15
  2. 10
  3. 8
  4. 5
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. 10

X² = 5 × 20 = 100, so x = 10.

Report this question

9In what ratio should water be mixed with milk costing ₹60 per litre to get a mixture worth ₹48 per litre (water : milk)?

  1. 3 : 1
  2. 1 : 5
  3. 1 : 4
  4. 4 : 1
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. 1 : 4

Allegation gives (60 − 48) : (48 − 0) = 12 : 48 = 1 : 4.

Report this question

10The salaries of A and B are in the ratio 4 : 5. When each gets a ₹2,000 increment, the ratio becomes 5 : 6. A's original salary is:

  1. ₹10,000
  2. ₹12,000
  3. ₹8,000
  4. ₹6,000
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. ₹8,000

(4x + 2000) : (5x + 2000) = 5 : 6 gives x = 2000, so A = 8000.

Report this question

11₹4,200 is divided among A, B and C with A : B = 2 : 3 and B : C = 4 : 5. C's share is:

  1. ₹1,800
  2. ₹2,000
  3. ₹1,500
  4. ₹1,600
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. ₹1,800

A : B : C = 8 : 12 : 15, so C gets 15/35 × 4200 = 1800.

Report this question

12Tea at ₹40/kg and tea at ₹60/kg are mixed in the ratio 3 : 2. The price per kg of the mixture is:

  1. ₹52
  2. ₹48
  3. ₹45
  4. ₹50
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. ₹48

(3 × 40 + 2 × 60) ÷ 5 = 240 ÷ 5 = 48.

Report this question

13If 6 dozen bananas cost ₹288, the cost of 15 bananas is:

  1. ₹54
  2. ₹56
  3. ₹64
  4. ₹60
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. ₹60

6 dozen = 72 bananas, so each costs ₹4 and 15 × 4 = 60.

Report this question

14Two numbers are in the ratio 3 : 5. When 9 is added to each, the ratio becomes 3 : 4. The smaller number is:

  1. 15
  2. 6
  3. 12
  4. 9
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. 9

(3x + 9) : (5x + 9) = 3 : 4 gives 12x + 36 = 15x + 27, so x = 3 and the numbers are 9 and 15.

Report this question

15Statement I: If a : b = c : d, then a : c = b : d.
Statement II: If a : b = c : d, then (a − b) : b = (c − d) : d.

  1. Both true
  2. I false, II true
  3. I true, II false
  4. Both false
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Both true

These are the properties called alternendo and dividendo.
