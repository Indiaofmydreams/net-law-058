About this question set

This set has 14 multiple-choice questions on Time and Distance from the UGC NET Paper 1 syllabus, unit 5, Mathematical Reasoning and Aptitude. Try each question first, then tap Show answer to check the key and read the explanation. 14 of the 14 questions carry a written explanation.

For the concepts and case law behind these questions, read the Mathematical Reasoning notes. Once you are comfortable, test yourself under exam conditions with a full-length mock test.

14 practice questions with answers

1A man rows 12 km upstream in 3 hours and 12 km downstream in 2 hours. His speed in still water is:

  1. 5 km/h
  2. 5.5 km/h
  3. 4 km/h
  4. 6 km/h
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. 5 km/h

The speeds are 4 and 6 km/h, so the still-water speed is (4 + 6) ÷ 2 = 5.

2Two persons start from the same point in opposite directions at 4 km/h and 5 km/h. After 3 hours they are apart by:

  1. 30 km
  2. 9 km
  3. 24 km
  4. 27 km
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. 27 km

The relative speed is 9 km/h, so 9 × 3 = 27.

Report this question

3Walking at 3/4 of his usual speed, a man reaches 20 minutes late. His usual time is:

  1. 40 min
  2. 50 min
  3. 60 min
  4. 80 min
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. 60 min

The new time is 4t/3, so t/3 = 20 and t = 60.

Report this question

4A man goes to a place at 30 km/h and returns at 60 km/h. His average speed is:

  1. 42 km/h
  2. 40 km/h
  3. 45 km/h
  4. 50 km/h
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. 40 km/h

For equal distances, average speed = 2ab/(a + b) = 3600 ÷ 90 = 40.

Report this question

5Two trains of lengths 150 m and 100 m run in the same direction at 72 km/h (faster, behind) and 54 km/h. The faster train overtakes the slower one in:

  1. 50 s
  2. 60 s
  3. 25 s
  4. 40 s
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. 50 s

The relative speed is 18 km/h = 5 m/s, and the distance is 250 m, so 250 ÷ 5 = 50.

Report this question

6A boat's speed in still water is 15 km/h and the stream flows at 5 km/h. The time to go 40 km downstream and return is:

  1. 5 h
  2. 7 h
  3. 8 h
  4. 6 h
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. 6 h

Downstream is 40 ÷ 20 = 2 h and upstream is 40 ÷ 10 = 4 h, so the total is 6 h.

Report this question

7A train running at 90 km/h crosses a 250 m platform in 20 seconds. The length of the train is:

  1. 200 m
  2. 500 m
  3. 250 m
  4. 300 m
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. 250 m

90 km/h = 25 m/s, so it covers 500 m in 20 s, and 500 − 250 = 250.

Report this question

8A man covers a distance in 6 hours at 12 km/h. At 18 km/h he would take:

  1. 4 h
  2. 3 h
  3. 8 h
  4. 5 h
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. 4 h

The distance is 72 km, and 72 ÷ 18 = 4.

9Two trains start from stations 300 km apart towards each other at 50 km/h and 70 km/h. They meet after:

  1. 3 h
  2. 2.5 h
  3. 2 h
  4. 3.5 h
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. 2.5 h

The relative speed is 120 km/h, so 300 ÷ 120 = 2.5.

Report this question

10A man rows downstream at 9 km/h and upstream at 5 km/h. The speed of the stream is:

  1. 3 km/h
  2. 4 km/h
  3. 2 km/h
  4. 1 km/h
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. 2 km/h

(9 − 5) ÷ 2 = 2.

Report this question

11A man covers half the journey at 20 km/h and the other half at 30 km/h. His average speed is:

  1. 25 km/h
  2. 26 km/h
  3. 22.5 km/h
  4. 24 km/h
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. 24 km/h

2ab/(a + b) = 1200 ÷ 50 = 24.

Report this question

12A policeman spots a thief 200 m ahead. The thief runs at 10 km/h and the policeman at 11 km/h. The thief is caught after he has run:

  1. 1 km
  2. 2 km
  3. 1.5 km
  4. 2.5 km
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. 2 km

The relative speed is 1 km/h and the gap is 0.2 km, so the chase takes 0.2 h and the thief runs 10 × 0.2 = 2 km.

Report this question

13A 100 m long train passes a pole in 9 seconds. Its speed is:

  1. 40 km/h
  2. 36 km/h
  3. 45 km/h
  4. 38 km/h
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. 40 km/h

100 ÷ 9 m/s × 18/5 = 40 km/h.

Report this question

14A man walks 3 km east and then 4 km north. His distance from the starting point is:

  1. 6 km
  2. 7 km
  3. 4 km
  4. 5 km
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. 5 km

The two legs are at right angles, so the distance is √(3² + 4²) = 5.
