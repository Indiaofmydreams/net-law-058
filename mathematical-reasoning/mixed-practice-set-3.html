17 practice questions with answers

1The sum of the present ages of A and B is 55. Five years ago, A was twice as old as B. A's present age is:

  1. 30
  2. 40
  3. 25
  4. 35
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. 35

Five years ago the sum was 45, so B was 15 and A was 30. Now A is 35.

Report this question

2If x + 1/x = 5, then x² + 1/x² is:

  1. 25
  2. 23
  3. 27
  4. 21
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. 23

Square both sides, 25 = x² + 2 + 1/x², so x² + 1/x² = 23.

Report this question

3If a + b = 10 and ab = 21, then a² + b² is:

  1. 58
  2. 79
  3. 42
  4. 100
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. 58

(a + b)² − 2ab = 100 − 42 = 58.

Report this question

4A father's age is 4 times his son's age. After 6 years it will be 3 times the son's age. The son's present age is:

  1. 6
  2. 8
  3. 12
  4. 10
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. 12

4x + 6 = 3(x + 6) gives x = 12.

5The area of a circle of radius 7 cm (π = 22/7) is:

  1. 308 cm²
  2. 154 cm²
  3. 49 cm²
  4. 44 cm²
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. 154 cm²

(22/7) × 7 × 7 = 154.

6The perimeter of a rectangle is 36 cm and its length is 10 cm. Its area is:

  1. 80 cm²
  2. 90 cm²
  3. 72 cm²
  4. 64 cm²
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. 80 cm²

2(10 + b) = 36 gives b = 8, so the area is 10 × 8 = 80.

8The circumference of a circle of radius 14 cm (π = 22/7) is:

  1. 44 cm
  2. 176 cm
  3. 154 cm
  4. 88 cm
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. 88 cm

2πr = 2 × 22/7 × 14 = 88.

9The area of a square whose diagonal is 10 cm is:

  1. 100 cm²
  2. 75 cm²
  3. 50 cm²
  4. 25 cm²
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. 50 cm²

Area = (diagonal)² ÷ 2 = 100 ÷ 2 = 50.

10The volume of a cylinder of radius 7 cm and height 10 cm (π = 22/7) is:

  1. 1,640 cm³
