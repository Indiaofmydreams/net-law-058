20 practice questions with answers

15 workers complete a job in 12 days. How many days will 10 workers take, working at the same rate?

  1. 10
  2. 6
  3. 24
  4. 8
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. 6

Inverse proportion. Total work = 5 × 12 = 60 worker-days, and 60 ÷ 10 = 6 days.

2A can do a job in 10 days and B in 15 days. Working together, they finish it in:

  1. 6 days
  2. 8 days
  3. 12 days
  4. 5 days
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. 6 days

1/10 + 1/15 = 1/6 of the work per day.

3A can do a work in 12 days and B in 18 days. Together they finish it in:

  1. 7.5 days
  2. 6 days
  3. 7.2 days
  4. 8 days
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. 7.2 days

1/12 + 1/18 = 5/36 per day, so 36 ÷ 5 = 7.2.

4A can do a work in 20 days, and A and B together can do it in 12 days. B alone takes:

  1. 28 days
  2. 25 days
  3. 32 days
  4. 30 days
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. 30 days

1/12 − 1/20 = 1/30.

510 men can complete a work in 15 days. After 5 days, 5 men leave. The remaining work will take:

  1. 20 days
  2. 25 days
  3. 30 days
  4. 10 days
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. 20 days

The total is 150 man-days and 50 are done, so 100 remain. With 5 men, 100 ÷ 5 = 20.

Report this question

6Pipe A fills a tank in 6 hours and pipe B empties it in 12 hours. If both are opened, the tank fills in:

  1. 18 h
  2. 10 h
  3. 12 h
  4. 8 h
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. 12 h

Net rate = 1/6 − 1/12 = 1/12 per hour.

Report this question

7Pipes A and B can fill a tank in 12 and 24 hours. Together they fill it in:

  1. 6 h
  2. 8 h
  3. 10 h
  4. 9 h
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. 8 h

1/12 + 1/24 = 3/24 = 1/8 per hour.

8A is twice as efficient as B. Together they finish a job in 12 days. A alone would take:

  1. 24 days
  2. 16 days
  3. 20 days
  4. 18 days
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. 18 days

A does 2/3 of the combined daily work (1/12), which is 1/18 per day.

9A completes 1/3 of a job in 5 days. The whole job takes:

  1. 20 days
  2. 15 days
  3. 10 days
  4. 12 days
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. 15 days

5 × 3 = 15.

10A and B can do a work in 8 and 12 days. They work together for 3 days, then A finishes the rest alone in:

  1. 5 days
  2. 2 days
  3. 4 days
  4. 3 days
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. 3 days

The combined rate is 5/24 per day, so 3 days gives 5/8 done. The remaining 3/8 at A's rate of 1/8 takes 3 days.

11Pipes A and B fill a tank in 20 and 30 minutes, and pipe C empties it in 15 minutes. With all three open, the tank fills in:

  1. 75 min
  2. 30 min
  3. 60 min
  4. 45 min
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. 60 min

The net rate is 1/20 + 1/30 − 1/15 = 1/60 per minute.

12A can finish a work in 15 days and B in 10 days. They work together for 4 days, then A leaves. B finishes the rest in:

  1. 3⅓ days
  2. 4 days
  3. 2 days
  4. 3 days
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. 3⅓ days

The combined rate is 1/6, so 4 days gives 2/3 done. The remaining 1/3 at B's rate of 1/10 takes 10/3 = 3⅓ days.

138 men can complete a job in 12 days. How long will 6 men take to complete 3/4 of the job?

  1. 9 days
  2. 16 days
  3. 18 days
  4. 12 days
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. 12 days

The job is 96 man-days, 3/4 of it is 72, and 72 ÷ 6 = 12.

Report this question

14A is three times as efficient as B and takes 10 days less than B. Working together, they finish the work in:

  1. 4 days
  2. 3.75 days
  3. 3 days
  4. 5 days
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. 3.75 days

If A takes t days then B takes 3t, so 2t = 10 and t = 5. Together: 1/5 + 1/15 = 4/15, so 15/4 = 3.75 days.

Report this question

15Statement I: If A completes a work in x days, A's one-day work is 1/x.
Statement II: If A and B work together, their combined one-day work is the sum of their individual one-day works.

  1. I false, II true
  2. Both false
  3. Both true
  4. I true, II false
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Both true

These are the two basic rules behind every time-and-work problem.

16A can do a work in 24 days and B in 8 days. Together they finish it in:

  1. 6 days
  2. 4 days
  3. 8 days
  4. 5 days
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. 6 days

1/24 + 1/8 = 1/6 per day.

Report this question

17A and B together finish a work in 12 days, B and C in 15 days, and C and A in 20 days. Working together, A, B and C finish it in:

  1. 8 days
  2. 15 days
  3. 10 days
  4. 12 days
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. 10 days

Adding the three rates gives 2(A + B + C) = 1/12 + 1/15 + 1/20 = 1/5, so A + B + C = 1/10.

18A tap fills a tank in 8 hours, and a leak empties it in 24 hours. With the leak, the tank fills in:

  1. 16 h
  2. 12 h
  3. 10 h
  4. 14 h
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. 12 h

Net rate = 1/8 − 1/24 = 1/12 per hour.

1912 workers can finish a job in 20 days. After 8 days, how many extra workers are needed to finish the rest in 6 days?

  1. 16
  2. 10
  3. 8
  4. 12
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. 12

The job is 240 worker-days and 96 are done, so 144 remain. In 6 days that needs 24 workers, which is 12 extra.

20A can do 1/3 of a work in 4 days and B can do 1/4 of it in 3 days. Together they finish the work in:

  1. 6 days
  2. 5 days
  3. 8 days
  4. 4 days
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. 6 days

A takes 12 days and B takes 12 days, so together they take 6 days.
