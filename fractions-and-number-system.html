20 practice questions with answers

1Statement I: The sum of two odd numbers is odd.
Statement II: The product of two odd numbers is odd.

  1. I false, II true
  2. Both false
  3. Both true
  4. I true, II false
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. I false, II true

Odd + odd = even, and odd × odd = odd.

2The product of two numbers is 216 and their HCF is 6. Their LCM is:

  1. 18
  2. 36
  3. 42
  4. 30
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. 36

Product = HCF × LCM, so LCM = 216 ÷ 6 = 36.

33/4 + 5/6 = ?

  1. 8/10
  2. 15/24
  3. 11/12
  4. 19/12
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. 19/12

LCM of the denominators is 12, so 9/12 + 10/12 = 19/12.

4Find the value of 2/3 of 3/4 of 24.

  1. 8
  2. 16
  3. 12
  4. 6

Answer: C. 12

3/4 of 24 = 18, and 2/3 of 18 = 12.

5Which is the largest fraction: 3/4, 4/5, 5/6, 2/3?

  1. 5/6
  2. 3/4
  3. 4/5
  4. 2/3
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. 5/6

As decimals they are 0.75, 0.80, 0.833 and 0.667.

6The HCF of 36 and 48 is:

  1. 6
  2. 9
  3. 24
  4. 12
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. 12

36 = 2² × 3² and 48 = 2⁴ × 3, so the HCF is 2² × 3 = 12.

7The LCM of 12, 18 and 24 is:

  1. 48
  2. 144
  3. 72
  4. 36
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. 72

The highest powers are 2³ and 3², so the LCM is 8 × 9 = 72.

8The greatest number that divides 43 and 91 leaving remainder 3 in each case is:

  1. 16
  2. 8
  3. 4
  4. 12
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. 8

HCF of (43 − 3) and (91 − 3), which is HCF(40, 88) = 8.

9If the number 5A3 is divisible by 9, the digit A is:

  1. 1
  2. 2
  3. 3
  4. 4
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. 1

The digit sum 5 + A + 3 must be a multiple of 9, so A = 1 (513 = 9 × 57).

10The smallest 4-digit number divisible by 12 is:

  1. 1002
  2. 1004
  3. 1012
  4. 1008
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. 1008

1000 ÷ 12 leaves remainder 4, so add 8 to get 1008.

11The recurring decimal 0.333… equals:

  1. 33/100
  2. 1/3
  3. 1/30
  4. 3/10
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. 1/3

Let x = 0.333…, then 10x − x = 3, so x = 1/3.

12The remainder when 2¹⁰ is divided by 7 is:

  1. 1
  2. 4
  3. 2
  4. 3
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. 2

2³ = 8 leaves remainder 1, so 2⁹ leaves 1 and 2¹⁰ leaves 2 (1024 = 7 × 146 + 2).

13Simplify: (3/5 ÷ 9/10) × 2/3

  1. 5/9
  2. 2/3
  3. 4/9
  4. 1/3

3/5 × 10/9 = 2/3, and 2/3 × 2/3 = 4/9.

14The sum of the first 50 natural numbers is:

  1. 1300
  2. 1250
  3. 1225
  4. 1275
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. 1275

N(n + 1)/2 = 50 × 51 ÷ 2 = 1275.

15The HCF and LCM of two numbers are 4 and 120. If one number is 24, the other is:

  1. 20
  2. 15
  3. 40
  4. 30
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. 20

Product of the numbers = HCF × LCM, so 480 ÷ 24 = 20.

16The unit digit of 7⁹⁵ is:

  1. 7
  2. 3
  3. 1
  4. 9
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. 3

The unit digits of powers of 7 repeat as 7, 9, 3, 1, and 95 leaves remainder 3 on division by 4, so the digit is 3.

17Which of the following is divisible by 11?

  1. 1011
  2. 3456
  3. 2728
  4. 5439
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. 2728

The alternating digit sum of 2728 is 2 − 7 + 2 − 8 = −11, which is a multiple of 11.

19The sum of the first 15 odd numbers is:

  1. 220
  2. 240
  3. 210
  4. 225
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. 225

The sum of the first n odd numbers is n², so 15² = 225.

20Which of the following is divisible by both 3 and 4?

  1. 5,436
  2. 9,030
  3. 7,342
  4. 8,451

Answer: A. 5,436

5436 has digit sum 18 (divisible by 3), and its last two digits, 36, are divisible by 4.
