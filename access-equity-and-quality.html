About this question set

This set has 12 multiple-choice questions on Access, Equity and Quality from the UGC NET Paper 1 syllabus, unit 10, Higher Education System. Try each question first, then tap Show answer to check the key. This set is an answer-key drill: check each answer, then revisit the notes for any you miss.

For the concepts and case law behind these questions, read the Higher Education System notes. Once you are comfortable, test yourself under exam conditions with a full-length mock test.

12 practice questions with answers

1The Gross Enrolment Ratio (GER) in higher education in India is calculated for the age group:

  1. 21–30
  2. 18–23
  3. 15–19
  4. 25–35
Show answer

Answer: B. 18–23

Report this question

2The Gender Parity Index (GPI) in higher education is the ratio of:

  1. Female enrolment to total enrolment
  2. Male GER to female GER
  3. Female GER to male GER
  4. Female teachers to male teachers
Show answer

Answer: C. Female GER to male GER

Report this question

3The All India Survey on Higher Education (AISHE) is conducted by the:

  1. NAAC
  2. Ministry of Education
  3. NITI Aayog
  4. NTA
Show answer

Answer: B. Ministry of Education

Report this question

4The UGC (Promotion of Equity in Higher Educational Institutions) Regulations, 2012 require HEIs to set up an:

  1. Equal Opportunity Cell
  2. Sports Board
  3. Placement Cell only
  4. Alumni Cell
Show answer

Answer: A. Equal Opportunity Cell

5The NAAC's revised assessment framework is based on how many criteria?

  1. 10
  2. 5
  3. 12
  4. 7

  1. 3.01–3.25
  2. 2.76–3.00
  3. 3.26–3.50
  4. 3.51–4.00

7The NIRF ranks institutions on how many broad parameters?

  1. 3
  2. 7
  3. 4
  4. 5

8Recent NAAC reforms (2024) move towards:

  1. Abolition of accreditation
  2. Binary accreditation (Accredited / Not Accredited)
  3. Only A++ grading
  4. Accreditation of schools only
Show answer

Answer: B. Binary accreditation (Accredited / Not Accredited)

9A major access barrier to online learning is the:

  1. Excess of teachers
  2. Excess of libraries
  3. Free tuition
  4. Digital divide
Show answer

Answer: D. Digital divide

Report this question

10"Equity" in higher education chiefly means:

  1. Only fee waivers
  2. Exclusive focus on elite institutions
  3. Fair opportunities and support for disadvantaged groups
  4. Identical treatment of all learners regardless of need
Show answer

Answer: C. Fair opportunities and support for disadvantaged groups

Report this question

11Which of the following is a major quality challenge in Indian higher education?

  1. Faculty shortages and outdated curricula
  2. Excess of accreditation bodies only
  3. Excess of laboratories
  4. Excess of research funding
Show answer

Answer: A. Faculty shortages and outdated curricula

12Assertion (A): Rapid expansion of enrolment without adequate faculty and infrastructure can compromise quality.
Reason (R): Quality depends on inputs such as faculty, infrastructure and curriculum.

  1. Both A and R are true, and R explains A
  2. Both true, but R does not explain A
  3. A is true, R is false
  4. A is false, R is true
