1Salomon v. Salomon & Co. Ltd. established that:

  1. A company is an agent of its members
  2. Directors are personally liable for the company's debts
  3. A company is a legal person separate from its members
  4. A company cannot own property
Show answer

Answer: C. A company is a legal person separate from its members

2Under Section 3(1), the minimum number of members required is: I. Public company: 7; II. Private company: 2; III. One Person Company: 1.

  1. I only
  2. I and II only
  3. II and III only
  4. I, II and III
Show answer

Answer: D. I, II and III

3Under Section 2(21), in a company limited by guarantee, the liability of members is limited to:

  1. The unpaid amount on their shares
  2. Unlimited liability
  3. The dividends received
  4. The amount they undertook in the memorandum to contribute to the assets in the event of winding up

Answer: D. The amount they undertook in the memorandum to contribute to the assets in the event of winding up

4Which of the following is NOT permitted for a company licensed under Section 8?

  1. Receiving donations

7In a One Person Company, the memorandum must name:

11Under Section 13(4), shifting the registered office from one State to another requires:

  1. A special resolution and approval of the Central Government
  2. An ordinary resolution

12Ashbury Railway Carriage & Iron Co. v. Riche held that an ultra vires act is:

  1. Voidable
  2. Void, and cannot be ratified even by all the members
  3. Valid if the members ratify it
  4. Valid if the directors approve it

13The doctrine of indoor management, laid down in Royal British Bank v. Turquand, protects:

  1. Outsiders dealing in good faith, who may assume that internal procedures have been complied with
  2. Directors against members
  3. Members against directors
  4. The company against outsiders
Show answer

Answer: A. Outsiders dealing in good faith, who may assume that internal procedures have been complied with

14Which of the following is NOT an exception to the doctrine of indoor management?

  1. Knowledge of the irregularity by the outsider
  2. Suspicious circumstances which put the outsider on inquiry
  3. Forgery
  4. The outsider acted in good faith without knowledge of any irregularity

15Eley v. Positive Government Security Life Assurance Co. is authority for the proposition that:

  1. Articles bind the company to outsiders
  2. Articles are a contract between the company and members as members; rights of a member as an outsider (e.g., as solicitor) cannot be enforced through the articles
  3. The memorandum may be altered at will
  4. Directors owe duties to creditors
Show answer

Answer: B. Articles are a contract between the company and members as members; rights of a member as an outsider (e.g., as solicitor) cannot be enforced through the articles

19In Erlanger v. New Sombrero Phosphate Co., the House of Lords held that a promoter:

  1. Stands in a fiduciary position and must disclose any secret profit
  2. Is an agent of the company

20In Kelner v. Baxter, a contract made by promoters on behalf of a company not yet incorporated was held to be:

  2. Binding on the company after ratification
