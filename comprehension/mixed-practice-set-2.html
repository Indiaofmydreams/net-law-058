About this question set

This set has 15 multiple-choice questions on Comprehension from the UGC NET Paper 1 syllabus, unit 3, Comprehension. Try each question first, then tap Show answer to check the key and read the explanation. 15 of the 15 questions carry a written explanation.

For the concepts and case law behind these questions, read the Comprehension notes. Once you are comfortable, test yourself under exam conditions with a full-length mock test.

Topics in this set

  • General concepts (15)

15 practice questions with answers

1'Implicit' means:

  1. Clearly stated
  2. Implied, not directly stated
  3. Written
  4. Wrong
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Implied, not directly stated

Opposite of explicit.

Report this question

2Passage: 'Rivers have shaped civilisations, supplying water, fertile soil and transport.' Which statement is NOT supported?

  1. Rivers influenced civilisations
  2. Rivers provided water
  3. Rivers supported transport
  4. Rivers have no role in agriculture
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Rivers have no role in agriculture

Fertile soil implies a role in agriculture.

Report this question

3A passage that argues for a viewpoint using reasons is:

  1. Purely factual
  2. Argumentative
  3. Descriptive
  4. Narrative
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Argumentative

It aims to persuade.

Report this question

4The 'gist' of a passage is its:

  1. Title only
  2. Main idea in brief
  3. First word
  4. Longest sentence
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Main idea in brief

The essential meaning.

Report this question

5Passage: 'She was reluctant to speak, yet her silence conveyed strong disapproval.' Her silence indicated:

  1. Confusion
  2. Disapproval
  3. Joy
  4. Approval
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Disapproval

The passage states it directly.

Report this question

6The idiom 'to burn the midnight oil' means:

  1. To waste fuel
  2. To sleep early
  3. To cook at night
  4. To work late into the night
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. To work late into the night

Working or studying late.

Report this question

7The idiom 'a piece of cake' means:

  1. A reward
  2. A sweet dish
  3. Something very easy
  4. A gift
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Something very easy

An easy task.

Report this question

8One word for 'a person who loves books' is:

  1. Philanthropist
  2. Bibliophile
  3. Misanthrope
  4. Linguist
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Bibliophile

Biblio means book.

Report this question

9Fill in the blank: 'He insisted ___ going.'

  1. on
  2. to
  3. in
  4. at
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. on

'Insist on' is the correct collocation.

Report this question

10Choose the correct spelling:

  1. Accomodation
  2. Acomodation
  3. Accommodation
  4. Acommodation
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Accommodation

Double c and double m.

Report this question

11The 'author's purpose' in a passage refers to:

  1. The publisher
  2. Why the author wrote it
  3. The font
  4. The page count
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Why the author wrote it

E.g. to inform, persuade or entertain.

Report this question

12'Explicit' means:

  1. Unclear
  2. Hidden
  3. Doubtful
  4. Clearly stated
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Clearly stated

Opposite of implicit.

Report this question

13Consider the statements: 1. Context helps decode unfamiliar words. 2. Every word has only one meaning in all contexts.

  1. Only statement 1 is correct
  2. Only statement 2 is correct
  3. Both statements are correct
  4. Neither statement is correct
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Only statement 1 is correct

Words can have several meanings.

14Assertion (A): Skimming helps to grasp the general idea quickly.
Reason (R): It involves reading selected parts rather than every word.

  1. Both A and R are true, and R is the correct explanation of A
  2. Both A and R are true, but R is not the correct explanation of A
  3. A is true, but R is false
  4. A is false, but R is true
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Both A and R are true, and R is the correct explanation of A

R explains A.
