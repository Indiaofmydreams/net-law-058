About this question set

This set has 15 multiple-choice questions on Comprehension from the UGC NET Paper 1 syllabus, unit 3, Comprehension. Try each question first, then tap Show answer to check the key and read the explanation. 15 of the 15 questions carry a written explanation.

For the concepts and case law behind these questions, read the Comprehension notes. Once you are comfortable, test yourself under exam conditions with a full-length mock test.

Topics in this set

  • General concepts (15)

15 practice questions with answers

1Passage: 'Regular reading improves vocabulary and sharpens critical thinking, though it needs sustained effort.' The central idea is:

  1. Effort is harmful
  2. Vocabulary is useless
  3. Reading has benefits but needs effort
  4. Critical thinking is inborn
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Reading has benefits but needs effort

The passage states benefits and the effort required.

Report this question

2Passage: 'The library was silent; only the clock ticked.' The mood is:

  1. Angry
  2. Calm and quiet
  3. Joyful
  4. Chaotic
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Calm and quiet

Silence and a ticking clock suggest calm.

Report this question

3In comprehension, 'inference' means:

  1. A personal opinion unrelated to the text
  2. The title
  3. A conclusion drawn from given information, though not stated directly
  4. A word-for-word copy
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. A conclusion drawn from given information, though not stated directly

Inferences are supported by the passage.

Report this question

4The word 'terse' most nearly means:

  1. Cheerful
  2. Brief and to the point
  3. Confusing
  4. Lengthy
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Brief and to the point

Terse means concise.

Report this question

5A good strategy for comprehension questions is to:

  1. Skip the passage
  2. Guess
  3. Use outside knowledge only
  4. Base answers on the passage
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Base answers on the passage

Answers must be supported by the text.

Report this question

6The 'tone' of a passage refers to:

  1. The title
  2. The number of paragraphs
  3. The author's attitude
  4. The font
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. The author's attitude

Tone reveals the author's attitude.

Report this question

7Match: A Candid, B Futile, C Prudent with (1) Frank (2) Useless (3) Careful

  1. A-2, B-1, C-3
  2. A-1, B-2, C-3
  3. A-1, B-3, C-2
  4. A-3, B-2, C-1
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. A-1, B-2, C-3

Word meanings.

Report this question

8Consider the statements: 1. The title of a passage often hints at its central theme. 2. Supporting details are irrelevant to the main idea.

  1. Only statement 1 is correct
  2. Only statement 2 is correct
  3. Both statements are correct
  4. Neither statement is correct
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Only statement 1 is correct

Supporting details develop the main idea.

Report this question

9Consider the statements: 1. An inference must be supported by the passage. 2. An assumption is always stated explicitly.

  1. Only statement 1 is correct
  2. Only statement 2 is correct
  3. Both statements are correct
  4. Neither statement is correct
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Only statement 1 is correct

Assumptions are usually unstated.

Report this question

10Assertion (A): Answers to comprehension questions should come from the passage.
Reason (R): Outside knowledge may distort the author's intended meaning.

  1. Both A and R are true, and R is the correct explanation of A
  2. Both A and R are true, but R is not the correct explanation of A
  3. A is true, but R is false
  4. A is false, but R is true
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Both A and R are true, and R is the correct explanation of A

R explains A.

Report this question

11Passage: 'Technology has made information easily accessible, yet the ability to judge its reliability remains rare.' The main point is:

  1. Reliability is unimportant
  2. Judging reliability is rare despite easy access
  3. Technology is harmful
  4. Information is scarce
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Judging reliability is rare despite easy access

The contrast is access versus judgment.

Report this question

12The word 'meticulous' means:

  1. Rude
  2. Lazy
  3. Careful and precise
  4. Careless
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Careful and precise

Meticulous means showing great attention to detail.

Report this question

13The antonym of 'scarce' is:

  1. Rare
  2. Little
  3. Limited
  4. Abundant
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Abundant

Scarce means in short supply.

Report this question

14The word 'ephemeral' means:

  1. Heavy
  2. Short-lived
  3. Eternal
  4. Famous
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Short-lived

Lasting a very short time.

Report this question

15The antonym of 'verbose' is:

  1. Long
  2. Wordy
  3. Concise
  4. Loud
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Concise

Verbose means using too many words.
