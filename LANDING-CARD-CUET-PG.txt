LANDING PAGE (exam chooser) - CUET PG CARD
==========================================
The exam chooser shown in your screenshots ("Your law exams, sorted.") is NOT in the zip you uploaded:
the root index.html in the zip is the blog index. So that file could not be edited here.

To add the card on your live chooser, duplicate the AILET card block and swap these values:

  Badge            AL            ->  CU
  Ribbon           New           ->  New
  Title            AILET         ->  CUET PG
  Subtitle         NLU Delhi LL.M. entrance   ->  Central university LL.M. entrance
  Description      Free CUET PG LL.M. preparation: what the exam is, pattern and +4/-1 marking, a 33-subject syllabus with a
                   progress tracker, paper analysis and trends, universities that use the score, practice questions and a
                   revision notebook that collects every mistake. No timer, no sign-up.
  Chips            Exam guide | Syllabus tracker | Paper analysis | Universities | Practice Arena | Revision notebook
  Stat             75 MCQs in the paper
  Button text      Open CUET PG
  Link             /cuet-pg/
  Accent colour    any unused theme colour (AILET uses pink, AIBE orange; teal/violet are in use by CLAT PG/P1)

Also add "CUET PG" to the chooser's top nav / any exam list, and to nl-welcome-promo.js if it lists exams.
