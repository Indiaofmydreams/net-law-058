About this question set

This set has 22 multiple-choice questions on Verbal Communication from the UGC NET Paper 1 syllabus, unit 4, Communication. Try each question first, then tap Show answer to check the key. This set is an answer-key drill: check each answer, then revisit the notes for any you miss.

For the concepts and case law behind these questions, read the Communication notes. Once you are comfortable, test yourself under exam conditions with a full-length mock test.

22 practice questions with answers

1Which of the following is NOT one of the 7 Cs of effective communication?

  1. Clarity
  2. Courtesy
  3. Concreteness
  4. Competition
Show answer

Answer: D. Competition

Report this question

2The 7 Cs of communication were popularised by:

  1. Cutlip and Center
  2. Shannon and Weaver
  3. Berlo and Schramm
  4. Hall and Hofstede
Show answer

Answer: A. Cutlip and Center

Report this question

3Using simple and specific words so that the message is easily understood reflects:

  1. Courtesy
  2. Completeness
  3. Clarity
  4. Consideration
Show answer

Answer: C. Clarity

Report this question

4Expressing a message in the fewest words without losing its meaning is:

  1. Conciseness
  2. Completeness
  3. Correctness
  4. Concreteness
Show answer

Answer: A. Conciseness

Report this question

5Correctness in communication refers to:

  1. Use of long sentences
  2. Accuracy of facts, grammar and suitable level of language
  3. Use of technical jargon
  4. Speed of delivery
Show answer

Answer: B. Accuracy of facts, grammar and suitable level of language

Report this question

6A message that gives the receiver all the information needed to understand and act on it is:

  1. Concise
  2. Complete
  3. Courteous
  4. Concrete
Show answer

Answer: B. Complete

7Respect, politeness and sincerity in a message reflect:

  1. Clarity
  2. Correctness
  3. Conciseness
  4. Courtesy

Answer: D. Courtesy

  1. Courtesy
  2. Consideration
  3. Conciseness
  4. Concreteness

  1. Conciseness

  1. Absence of a permanent record
  2. Impossibility of revision
  3. Delayed feedback
  4. Inability to reach many people

12A major advantage of oral communication is:

  1. Immediate feedback
  2. Permanent record
  3. Legal evidence

13Hearing differs from listening in that hearing is:

  1. A psychological process involving interpretation
  2. Always intentional
  3. The same as active listening
  4. A physiological process of receiving sound

14Active listening involves:

  1. Hearing without responding
  2. Attention, understanding and responding
  3. Interrupting frequently
  4. Preparing arguments while the speaker talks
Show answer

Answer: B. Attention, understanding and responding

Report this question

15Passive listening is best described as:

  1. Receiving the message without active engagement or feedback
  2. Questioning the speaker
  3. Note-taking and paraphrasing
  4. Evaluating the message critically

Answer: A. Receiving the message without active engagement or feedback

Report this question

16Listening to music or poetry for enjoyment is:

  1. Critical listening
  2. Empathic listening
  3. Appreciative listening
  4. Informational listening
Show answer

Answer: C. Appreciative listening

17Listening to understand the speaker's feelings is:

  1. Critical listening
  2. Discriminative listening
  3. Empathic listening
  4. Appreciative listening
Show answer

Answer: C. Empathic listening

18Evaluating and judging the speaker's arguments is:

  1. Appreciative listening
  2. Critical (evaluative) listening
  3. Empathic listening
  4. Discriminative listening

19Listening to a lecture in order to learn facts is:

  1. Pseudo-listening
  2. Informational (comprehensive) listening
  3. Appreciative listening
  4. Empathic listening
