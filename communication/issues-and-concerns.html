About this question set

This set has 15 multiple-choice questions on Issues and Concerns from the UGC NET Paper 1 syllabus, unit 4, Communication. Try each question first, then tap Show answer to check the key. This set is an answer-key drill: check each answer, then revisit the notes for any you miss.

For the concepts and case law behind these questions, read the Communication notes. Once you are comfortable, test yourself under exam conditions with a full-length mock test.

15 practice questions with answers

1Which of the following is a principle of media ethics?

  1. Sensationalism
  2. Paid propaganda
  3. Rumour-spreading
  4. Accuracy and fairness
Show answer

Answer: D. Accuracy and fairness

2"Paid news" means:

  1. News about payments
  2. Subscription-based news
  3. News or analysis appearing in the media for a price in cash or kind
  4. News about banks
Show answer

Answer: C. News or analysis appearing in the media for a price in cash or kind

3Systematically favouring one side in news coverage is:

  1. Objectivity
  2. Gatekeeping
  3. Cultivation
  4. Media bias

Answer: D. Media bias

4Exaggerating events to attract audiences is called:

  1. Objectivity
  2. Sensationalism
  3. Fact-checking

5Deliberately created false information intended to deceive is:

  1. Misinformation
  2. Disinformation

  1. Disinformation
  2. Propaganda
  3. Misinformation
