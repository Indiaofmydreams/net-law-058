About this question set

This set has 10 multiple-choice questions on Role of Mass Media in Society from the UGC NET Paper 1 syllabus, unit 4, Communication. Try each question first, then tap Show answer to check the key. This set is an answer-key drill: check each answer, then revisit the notes for any you miss.

For the concepts and case law behind these questions, read the Communication notes. Once you are comfortable, test yourself under exam conditions with a full-length mock test.

10 practice questions with answers

1Mass media shape public opinion mainly by:

  1. Making laws
  2. Collecting taxes
  3. Selecting, presenting and interpreting information on issues
  4. Conducting elections
Show answer

Answer: C. Selecting, presenting and interpreting information on issues

Report this question

2The press is called the "Fourth Estate" because it:

  1. Is the fourth military branch
  2. Acts as a watchdog over the three organs of government
  3. Collects revenue
  4. Frames laws
Show answer

Answer: B. Acts as a watchdog over the three organs of government

Report this question

3The phrase "Fourth Estate" is commonly attributed to:

  1. Lasswell
  2. Berlo
  3. Aristotle
  4. Edmund Burke
Show answer

Answer: D. Edmund Burke

Report this question

4In a democracy, the media's key role is:

  1. Replacing the judiciary
  2. Controlling elections
  3. Informing citizens and holding power accountable
  4. Making policy
Show answer

Answer: C. Informing citizens and holding power accountable

Report this question

5Public health campaigns on television illustrate the role of media in:

  1. Censorship
  2. Education and awareness
  3. Gatekeeping only
  4. Selling goods
Show answer

Answer: B. Education and awareness

Report this question

6Portraying social issues like gender inequality can promote:

  1. Social change
  2. Social stagnation
  3. Censorship
  4. Isolation
Show answer

Answer: A. Social change

Report this question

7Passing on values and traditions across generations through media is:

  1. Surveillance
  2. Cultural transmission
  3. Correlation
  4. Gatekeeping
Show answer

Answer: B. Cultural transmission

Report this question

8Watchdog journalism mainly involves:

  1. Exposing corruption and wrongdoing
  2. Reporting only sports
  3. Reporting only weather
  4. Providing only entertainment
Show answer

Answer: A. Exposing corruption and wrongdoing

Report this question

9Highlighting poverty, health and literacy issues to mobilise action reflects the media's role in:

  1. Entertainment only
  2. Advertising only
  3. Cultural isolation
  4. Social change and development
Show answer

Answer: D. Social change and development

Report this question

10Press freedom in India is derived from Article 19(1)(a), which guarantees:

  1. Freedom of speech and expression
  2. Right to equality
  3. Right to property
  4. Right to constitutional remedies
Show answer

Answer: A. Freedom of speech and expression
