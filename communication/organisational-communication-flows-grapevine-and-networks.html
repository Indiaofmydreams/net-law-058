11 practice questions with answers

1Formal communication in an organization follows:

  1. The grapevine
  2. The official chain of command
  3. Rumours
  4. Social networks
Show answer

Answer: B. The official chain of command

Report this question

2Informal communication is communication that:

  1. Follows official hierarchy
  2. Is always written
  3. Does not follow official channels
  4. Is always upward
Show answer

Answer: C. Does not follow official channels

Report this question

3Downward communication flows from:

  1. Subordinates to superiors
  2. Peers to peers
  3. Across departments
  4. Superiors to subordinates
Show answer

Answer: D. Superiors to subordinates

Report this question

4Which of the following is an example of upward communication?

  1. Employee grievance report
  2. Policy statement
  3. Job instructions
  4. Circular to staff
Show answer

Answer: A. Employee grievance report

Report this question

5Horizontal (lateral) communication takes place between:

  1. Superior and subordinate
  2. Persons at the same level
  3. Subordinate and superior
  4. Persons outside the organization
Show answer

Answer: B. Persons at the same level

Report this question

6Diagonal communication takes place between:

  1. Persons at the same level
  2. Boss and secretary only
  3. Two friends outside work
  4. Persons at different levels in different departments
Show answer

Answer: D. Persons at different levels in different departments

7The term "grapevine" originated during the:

  1. Industrial Revolution
  2. American Civil War
  3. Second World War
  4. French Revolution
Show answer

8The most common grapevine pattern identified by Keith Davis is:

  1. Cluster chain
  2. Single strand
  3. Gossip chain
  4. Probability chain

  2. All-channel
