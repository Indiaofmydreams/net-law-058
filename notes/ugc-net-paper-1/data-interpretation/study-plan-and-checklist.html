§Overview#

This unit rewards daily short practice more than long weekend sessions, because speed and accuracy are built through repetition. A one-week plan is below, and it can be stretched to two or three weeks by repeating each block.

11.2How to practise#

In brief

Use previous-year Paper 1 question papers from the official NTA UGC NET website for real data sets; the arithmetic and style of the data are the best guide to what to…

  • Use previous-year Paper 1 question papers from the official NTA UGC NET website for real data sets; the arithmetic and style of the data are the best guide to what to expect.

  • Time every set. Start at 10 minutes for 5 questions and bring it down to 6 or 7.

  • Keep an error log with three columns: the question type, the trap that caught you, and the correct method. Review it each evening.

  • Practise reading the display before reading the question, and say the units aloud once.

  • Attempt a mix of tables, bar charts, line charts and pie charts; the two-pie and stacked-bar cases deserve extra practice.

11.3Exam-day checklist#

In brief

Read the title, units and legend of the data display every time. Check the base of every percentage and the direction of every ratio. Approximate first, and compute exactly…

  • Read the title, units and legend of the data display every time.

  • Check the base of every percentage and the direction of every ratio.

  • Approximate first, and compute exactly only if two options are close.

  • Mark an answer for every question, as there is no negative marking.

  • Keep roughly 8 to 10 minutes for the unit, and do not let one hard question take more than 3 minutes.

  • Leave a spare minute at the end to recheck any answer where you changed the unit or the base.

These notes follow the standard UGC NET Paper 1 syllabus and general subject knowledge; the latest NTA notification was not checked when they were written. The exam pattern, syllabus wording and the status of laws and schemes such as the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 can change. Before the exam, check the current information bulletin on the official NTA UGC NET website. All data sets in Section 8 are invented for practice and are not real statistics.
