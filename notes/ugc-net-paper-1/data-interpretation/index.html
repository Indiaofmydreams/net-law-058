UGC NET PAPER 1 · CODE 00 · UNIT 07

UGC NET Paper 1 Data Interpretation: Detailed Study Notes

Unit 7 carries five questions (ten marks) and is the most predictable unit in Paper 1: the five questions usually come from one table or chart. The notes follow the five official syllabus blocks: sources and classification of data, quantitative and qualitative data, graphical representation, data interpretation and data and governance. Practise reading the title, axis and units first, learn the percentage and ratio formulas, and use approximation to save time.

    How to use these notes

    1. Round 1: LearnStart at Topic 0, then follow the order: data and its types, sources, classification, quantitative and qualitative data, graphs, formulas, the five-step interpretation method and speed tricks, then data and governance.
    2. Round 2: SolveWork every solved illustration in Topic 7 on paper before reading the answer, then time yourself on sets from the MCQ pages.
    3. Last week: RecallUse the rapid-recall sheet and Topic 10 (formula sheet, memory aids, pairs the examiner contrasts), then follow the seven-day plan in Topic 11.
