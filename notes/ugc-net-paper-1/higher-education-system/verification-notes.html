9.2Time-sensitive facts to re-check#

In brief

Covers VBSA Bill, NAAC reforms, Foreign campuses, Statistical figures.

  • VBSA Bill
  • NAAC reforms
  • Foreign campuses
  • Statistical figures

  • VBSA Bill: status after July 2026 (JPC report, passage, any change of council names or scope). Check PRS India and the Ministry of Education.

  • NAAC reforms: the binary accreditation and maturity-based levels have been rolling out since 2024; confirm the current scheme and grade bands.

  • Reservation figures, EWS income criterion, ABC and NCrF details: confirm on UGC and Ministry of Education notices.

  • Foreign campuses: the list of approved campuses grows; confirm which ones are operating.

  • Classical languages, schemes and acronym expansions (ULLAS, PM-SETU, PM-Vidyalaxmi, SWAYAM Plus) can be updated or renamed.

  • Statistical figures (GER, number of universities and colleges, R&D spending) come from AISHE and other annual surveys; use the latest edition.
