Takshashila (Taxila)Near Rawalpindi, Pakistan; Flourished from about 6th century BCE to 5th century CE; Individual teachers, not a single campus; Vedas, 18 shilpas (crafts), medicine, archery, politics. Associated with Panini (grammar), Chanakya (Kautilya), Jivaka (physician), Charaka
Varanasi (Kashi)Uttar Pradesh; Ancient to present; Brahmanical and Sanskrit learning, philosophy
NalandaRajgir, Bihar; Traditionally founded by Kumaragupta I (5th century CE); patronised by Gupta, Harsha, Pala rulers; Buddhist and secular studies; entrance test at the gate; about 10,000 students and 1,500 teachers in Hiuen Tsang's account; nine-storey library Dharmaganj
VikramshilaBhagalpur, Bihar; Founded by Dharmapala (Pala dynasty), 8th to 9th century; Tantric Buddhism, Tibetan links; famous scholar Atisha Dipankara
OdantapuriBihar Sharif, Bihar; Pala period (traditionally Gopala); Buddhist studies; said to have inspired Tibet's Samye monastery
JagaddalaBengal; Ramapala, Pala dynasty; Tantric Buddhism, manuscripts
Somapura Mahavihara (Paharpur)Bangladesh; Dharmapala; Large monastic complex
ValabhiGujarat; Maitraka dynasty; Hinayana Buddhism; rivalled Nalanda; scholars Gunamati and Sthiramati
Kanchi (Kanchipuram)Tamil Nadu; Pallava period; Sanskrit, Buddhism, Jainism, philosophy
Mithila and Nadia (Navadvip)Bihar and West Bengal; Medieval period; Nyaya and Navya-Nyaya logic
UjjainMadhya Pradesh; Gupta and after; Astronomy, mathematics

1953UGC set up as an interim body; Inaugurated by Maulana Abul Kalam Azad; Became statutory by the UGC Act 1956

1986National Policy on Education; Rajiv Gandhi government; "Common core"; Operation Blackboard; Navodaya Vidyalayas; strengthening open universities (IGNOU 1985); rural universities; autonomous colleges; National Council of Higher Education idea

ChancellorTitular, ceremonial head; for State universities usually the Governor; presides over convocations and confers degrees
Vice-Chancellor (VC)Chief academic and executive officer; chairs Executive Council and Academic Council; term usually five years in Central universities; UGC 2018 regulations ask for at least ten years as Professor (or equivalent) for VC
