17.1One-page formula sheet#

In brief

Topic Key formulas and facts Reasoning Deductive: general to particular, certain. Inductive: particular to general, probable. Abductive: best explanation. Valid/invalid for…

Topic Key formulas and facts
Reasoning Deductive: general to particular, certain. Inductive: particular to general, probable. Abductive: best explanation. Valid/invalid for deduction; strong/weak for induction
Series sums n(n+1)/2; n squared for odd numbers; n(n+1) for even numbers; sum of squares n(n+1)(2n+1)/6; sum of cubes [n(n+1)/2] squared
AP and GP AP: a + (n-1)d; sum = n/2 [2a + (n-1)d]. GP: a r^(n-1); sum = a(r^n - 1)/(r - 1)
Alphabet A=1 ... Z=26; opposite letters sum to 27; EJOTY = 5, 10, 15, 20, 25
Divisibility 3 and 9 by digit sum; 11 by alternating sums; 4 by last two digits; 8 by last three digits
HCF and LCM HCF x LCM = product (two numbers only); same remainder r: HCF of (a - r), (b - r); smallest number leaving r: LCM + r
Factors N = p^a q^b ..., number of factors = (a+1)(b+1)...
Unit digits Last-digit cycles: 2 gives 2, 4, 8, 6; 3 gives 3, 9, 7, 1; 7 gives 7, 9, 3, 1; 8 gives 8, 4, 2, 6; 4 gives 4, 6; 9 gives 9, 1
Ratio Share = N x a/(a+b+c); fourth proportional bc/a; mean proportional square root of (ab)
Alligation Cheaper : dearer = (d - m) : (m - c)
Percentage Change % = (new - old)/old x 100; successive: a + b + ab/100; A x% more than B, B is x/(100+x) x 100 % less than A
Profit and loss Profit % = profit/CP x 100; SP = CP(100 + P)/100; discount on MP; successive discount d1 + d2 - d1 d2/100; same-SP gain and loss x%: loss x squared/100 %
Simple interest PRT/100; A = P(1 + RT/100)
Compound interest A = P(1 + R/100)^n; half-yearly: R/2 and 2n; CI - SI (2 years) = P(R/100) squared
Discounting PW = FV x 100/(100 + RT); TD = SI on PW; BD = SI on FV; BG = TD squared/PW
Average Sum = average x n; combined = (n1 a1 + n2 a2)/(n1 + n2); new item = old average + (n+1) x change
Time and work Rate = 1/x; together xy/(x + y); wages in the ratio of work done; pipes: inlet +, outlet -
Speed km/h x 5/18 = m/s; average speed (equal distances) 2ab/(a + b); relative speed: opposite add, same subtract
Trains Pole: L/S; platform: (L + P)/S; two trains: (L1 + L2)/relative speed
Boats Down = u + v; up = u - v; u = (D + U)/2; v = (D - U)/2
Mensuration Circle pi r squared and 2 pi r; cylinder pi r squared h; cone 1/3 pi r squared h; sphere 4/3 pi r cubed
PnC and probability nPr = n!/(n-r)!; nCr = n!/[r!(n-r)!]; P(E) = favourable/total; P(not E) = 1 - P(E)

17.2Ten mistakes that cost the most marks#

In brief

Reading the question too fast and answering a different quantity (CI instead of amount, loss instead of loss percentage). Not converting units (minutes and hours, km/h and…

  1. Reading the question too fast and answering a different quantity (CI instead of amount, loss instead of loss percentage).

  2. Not converting units (minutes and hours, km/h and m/s, months and years).

  3. Using the wrong base in percentage, profit and discount questions.

  4. Adding successive percentages and discounts.

  5. Taking the arithmetic mean of speeds for the average speed.

  6. Skipping the compounding period (half-yearly, quarterly).

  7. Mistaking validity for truth in reasoning questions.

  8. Forgetting wrap-around (Z to A) in letter shifts.

  9. Treating a relationship as certain when the person's gender is not given.

  10. Not checking the answer against the options for reasonableness.

17.4Last-minute checklist#

In brief

  • Recite squares to 30, cubes to 15, the fraction-percent chart and the divisibility rules.

  • Re-read the distractor map in sub-topic 16.6 and the ten mistakes above.

  • Revise the one-page formula sheet once, then stop learning new things.

  • In the exam: read the question fully, circle the units, estimate, calculate, then check the options.

  • Attempt Unit 5 in one block of 8 to 10 minutes, and mark any question that needs more than about 2 minutes for a second pass.

  • Answer every question, because there is no negative marking.
