High-yield facts

Rapid-recall facts
ItemKey fact
ReasoningDeductive: general to particular, certain. Inductive: particular to general, probable. Abductive: best explanation. Valid/invalid for deduction; strong/weak for induction
Series sumsn(n+1)/2; n squared for odd numbers; n(n+1) for even numbers; sum of squares n(n+1)(2n+1)/6; sum of cubes [n(n+1)/2] squared
AP and GPAP: a + (n-1)d; sum = n/2 [2a + (n-1)d]. GP: a r^(n-1); sum = a(r^n - 1)/(r - 1)
AlphabetA=1 ... Z=26; opposite letters sum to 27; EJOTY = 5, 10, 15, 20, 25
Divisibility3 and 9 by digit sum; 11 by alternating sums; 4 by last two digits; 8 by last three digits
HCF and LCMHCF x LCM = product (two numbers only); same remainder r: HCF of (a - r), (b - r); smallest number leaving r: LCM + r
FactorsN = p^a q^b ..., number of factors = (a+1)(b+1)...
Unit digitsLast-digit cycles: 2 gives 2, 4, 8, 6; 3 gives 3, 9, 7, 1; 7 gives 7, 9, 3, 1; 8 gives 8, 4, 2, 6; 4 gives 4, 6; 9 gives 9, 1
RatioShare = N x a/(a+b+c); fourth proportional bc/a; mean proportional square root of (ab)
AlligationCheaper : dearer = (d - m) : (m - c)
PercentageChange % = (new - old)/old x 100; successive: a + b + ab/100; A x% more than B, B is x/(100+x) x 100 % less than A
Profit and lossProfit % = profit/CP x 100; SP = CP(100 + P)/100; discount on MP; successive discount d1 + d2 - d1 d2/100; same-SP gain and loss x%: loss x squared/100 %
Simple interestPRT/100; A = P(1 + RT/100)
Compound interestA = P(1 + R/100)^n; half-yearly: R/2 and 2n; CI - SI (2 years) = P(R/100) squared
DiscountingPW = FV x 100/(100 + RT); TD = SI on PW; BD = SI on FV; BG = TD squared/PW
AverageSum = average x n; combined = (n1 a1 + n2 a2)/(n1 + n2); new item = old average + (n+1) x change
Time and workRate = 1/x; together xy/(x + y); wages in the ratio of work done; pipes: inlet +, outlet -
Speedkm/h x 5/18 = m/s; average speed (equal distances) 2ab/(a + b); relative speed: opposite add, same subtract
TrainsPole: L/S; platform: (L + P)/S; two trains: (L1 + L2)/relative speed
BoatsDown = u + v; up = u - v; u = (D + U)/2; v = (D - U)/2
MensurationCircle pi r squared and 2 pi r; cylinder pi r squared h; cone 1/3 pi r squared h; sphere 4/3 pi r cubed
PnC and probabilitynPr = n!/(n-r)!; nCr = n!/[r!(n-r)!]; P(E) = favourable/total; P(not E) = 1 - P(E)
