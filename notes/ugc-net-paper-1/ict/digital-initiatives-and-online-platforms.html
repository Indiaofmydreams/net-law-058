14.1Quick Table: Who Runs What#

In brief

This is the highest-yield area for UGC NET .

  • 2017
  • class 9 to postgraduate
  • Four quadrants
  • NPTEL

This is the highest-yield area for UGC NET. Memorise who runs it, what it does, and its unique keyword.

Initiative Key facts (keyword)
SWAYAM Study Webs of Active-Learning for Young Aspiring Minds. National MOOC platform, launched 2017, courses from class 9 to postgraduate, free to learn and paid proctored certificate. Four quadrants: video lecture, reading material, self-assessment and discussion forum. National Coordinators: NPTEL (engineering), UGC (non-technical postgraduate), CEC (non-technical undergraduate), NCERT and NIOS (school), AICTE (self-paced and international), IGNOU (out-of-school), IIMB (management) and NITTTR (teacher training). UGC allows up to 40% of a programme's credits to be earned through SWAYAM courses (raised from 20%)
SWAYAM Prabha Group of DTH educational TV channels (34 initially, now more), 24x7, broadcast through GSAT-15 satellite. INFLIBNET is the coordinating and technical partner. Content from IITs, UGC, CEC, IGNOU, NCERT, NIOS and others
NPTEL National Programme on Technology Enhanced Learning, 2003, by IITs and IISc. Web and video courses in engineering, science and now humanities and management
e-PG Pathshala Postgraduate e-content in about 70 subjects, by UGC and INFLIBNET. Four-quadrant model: e-Tutorial, e-Text, Web Resources and Self-Assessment
e-Pathshala Web portal and app of NCERT with e-books and resources for school students and teachers
NDLI National Digital Library of India, single-window search for educational resources, developed at IIT Kharagpur under NMEICT, launched 2016
Shodhganga Repository of Indian electronic theses and dissertations (ETDs), by INFLIBNET, 2010. UGC regulations require submission of PhD theses
Shodhgangotri Repository of research synopses of PhD scholars
Shodhshuddhi Plagiarism detection facility provided by INFLIBNET to academic institutions
INFLIBNET Information and Library Network Centre, an inter-university centre of UGC at Gandhinagar (Gujarat)
e-ShodhSindhu Consortium giving access to e-journals and e-books, formed by merging UGC-INFONET, N-LIST and INDEST-AICTE
Vidwan National database of experts and researchers (profiles)
ONOS One Nation One Subscription, nationwide access to international research journals for government higher education institutions, run through INFLIBNET, launched in January 2025
NAD National Academic Depository, digital storage of academic awards (degrees and certificates), run by NSDL and CDSL, linked to DigiLocker, 2017
DigiLocker Cloud-based digital document wallet (2015), documents are legally valid (IT Act rules)
ABC Academic Bank of Credits, launched July 2021, stores credits earned from multiple institutions, enabling credit transfer, multiple entry and exit (NEP 2020). Students register through DigiLocker
APAAR Automated Permanent Academic Account Registry, One Nation One Student ID, a 12-digit lifelong ID linked to ABC, as per NEP 2020
NMEICT National Mission on Education through ICT, launched 2009 by the Ministry of Education (then MHRD), for content generation and connectivity
Virtual Labs Remote access to simulated experiments, IIT-led project under NMEICT
e-Yantra IIT Bombay, robotics education initiative
Spoken Tutorial IIT Bombay, short audio-video tutorials on open-source software, in many Indian languages
PM e-Vidya Programme (May 2020) combining digital, online and on-air education: DIKSHA, TV channels (One Class One Channel), radio, podcasts
DIKSHA Digital Infrastructure for Knowledge Sharing, school-level platform for teachers and learners, with QR-coded textbooks, based on open-source Sunbird
AISHE All India Survey on Higher Education, annual survey by the Ministry of Education, gives GER, enrolment and institution counts
NIRF National Institutional Ranking Framework, launched 2015, first rankings in 2016. Parameters: Teaching, Learning and Resources (TLR); Research and Professional Practice (RPP); Graduation Outcomes (GO); Outreach and Inclusivity (OI); Perception (PR)
SAMARTH e-governance ERP platform for higher education institutions (admissions, fees, exams, HR)
UDISE+ Unified District Information System for Education, school data

14.2Key Distinctions and Traps#

In brief

Covers SWAYAM, e-PG Pathshala, Shodhganga, NAD, NDLI and more.

  • SWAYAM
  • SWAYAM Prabha
  • e-PG Pathshala
  • e-Pathshala
  • Shodhganga
  • Shodhgangotri
  • Shodhshuddhi
  • NAD

  • SWAYAM (MOOC platform, online courses) versus SWAYAM Prabha (TV channels). A very common trap.

  • e-PG Pathshala (PG, UGC) versus e-Pathshala (school, NCERT).

  • Shodhganga (full theses) versus Shodhgangotri (synopses) versus Shodhshuddhi (plagiarism checker).

  • NAD stores certificates, ABC stores credits, APAAR is the student ID, DigiLocker is the wallet.

  • NDLI is a digital library at IIT Kharagpur, and NPTEL is a course platform by IITs and IISc.

  • Most UGC programmes are coordinated through INFLIBNET (Gandhinagar) and CEC (Consortium for Educational Communication, Delhi).

  • International platforms: Coursera, edX, Udacity, FutureLearn, Khan Academy, Udemy.
