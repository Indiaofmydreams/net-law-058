UGC NET Law (058) Law of Torts and Consumer Protection: Detailed Study Notes

Unit V combines English and Indian case law in torts with three statutes: the Consumer Protection Act, the Motor Vehicles Act and the Competition Act. Topic 0 explains the exam pattern and the repealed-1986-Act trap. Topics 1 to 3 cover the nature of tort, general principles and defences; Topic 4 covers negligence, nuisance, trespass and defamation; Topics 5 to 7 cover remoteness, strict and absolute liability and State liability; Topics 8 to 10 cover the three statutes, and Topic 11 is a rapid revision sheet of case anchors, numbers and concept traps.

    How to use these notes

    1. Round 1: ReadStart with Topic 0, then read Topics 1 to 10 in order. Learn each case with its principle in one line; Topic 4 is the longest, so take Negligence, Nuisance, Trespass and Defamation one at a time.
    2. Round 2: ReviseSwitch on Revision mode on any topic page to keep each section's key terms, lists and tables. For the statutes (Topics 8 to 10) learn the amounts, periods and the latest amendments.
    3. Last week: RecallUse the rapid-recall sheet (numbers and periods, concept traps), then Topic 11 and practice MCQs.
