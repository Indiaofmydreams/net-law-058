25.1Emergency Articles#

In brief

Covers 3-5-6-0, 358 = AUTO, 359 = ORDER.

  • 3-5-6-0
  • 352
  • 353

  • 3-5-6-0: Article 352 (National) → 353 (Effect) → 354 (Revenue) → 355 (Duty) → 356 (State) → 357 (Legislative) → 358 (Art.19 Auto-suspend) → 359 (Presidential Order) → 360 (Financial) → 365 (Non-compliance)

  • 358 = AUTO (automatic suspension of Art. 19, only war/external aggression)

  • 359 = ORDER (Presidential Order needed; NOT Arts. 20/21)

25.8Articles 20, 21: Non-Suspendable: Trick#

In brief

Covers 20 = EDS, 21 = LIFE, NEVER suspended.

  • 20 = EDS
  • 21 = LIFE
  • NEVER suspended

  • 20 = EDS — Ex post facto, Double jeopardy, Self-incrimination

  • 21 = LIFE — Life includes all expanded rights (liberty, education, privacy, environment...)

  • Both 20 and 21 together = NEVER suspended (44th Amendment, 1978)
