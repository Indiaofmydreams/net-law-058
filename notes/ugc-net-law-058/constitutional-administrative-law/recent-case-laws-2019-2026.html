19.1Constitutional Law: Recent Cases#

In brief

Case Year Court Key Holding Anuradha Bhasin v. Union of India 2020 SC (3-judge) Internet shutdown orders must be published; internet access is part of Art. 19(1)(a) and Art.…

Case Year Court Key Holding
Anuradha Bhasin v. Union of India 2020 SC (3-judge) Internet shutdown orders must be published; internet access is part of Art. 19(1)(a) and Art. 19(1)(g); indefinite suspension of internet violates Constitution
Arnab Ranjan Goswami v. Union of India 2020 SC (2-judge) Art. 21 protects liberty of the press; HC cannot refuse bail without reasons where criminal prosecution is an attempt to throttle free speech
Election Commission of India v. M.R. Vijayabhaskar 2021 SC ECI's decisions are open to judicial review; SC can examine conduct of ECI under Art. 324
Arup Bhuyan v. State of Assam (reconsideration) 2023 SC (5-judge) Overruled earlier view; mere membership of a banned organisation is an offence under UAPA; individual acts not required
Supriyo v. Union of India 2023 SC (5-judge Constitution Bench) No fundamental right to same-sex marriage under the Constitution; Parliament/State Legislatures alone can legislate on marriage; Special Marriage Act not read down
Government of NCT of Delhi v. Union of India 2023 SC (5-judge Constitution Bench) Delhi government has legislative and executive power over 'services' (Entry 41, List II); LG acts on Cabinet advice on services; Centre overrode this by Constitution (Amendment) Act 2023 — Constitution (105th Amendment)
Subhash Desai v. Principal Secretary, Governor of Maharashtra 2023 SC (5-judge) Governor cannot act on extraneous political considerations in summoning/dissolving Assembly; Governors must act on CM's advice in summoning; disqualification decisions of Speaker subject to judicial review — referred to larger bench on some issues
Prashant Kumar Mishra v. Bar Council of India 2023 SC Advocates enrolled in one State Bar Council can practise throughout India — Section 30 of Advocates Act, 1961 is self-executory
Centre for Public Interest Litigation v. Union of India (Electoral Bonds) 2024 SC (5-judge Constitution Bench) Electoral bonds scheme struck down as unconstitutional — violates voters' right to information under Art. 19(1)(a); anonymous political funding violates transparency; SBI directed to disclose all details to ECI
Sita Soren v. Union of India 2024 SC (7-judge Constitution Bench) Overruled P.V. Narasimha Rao (1998); MPs/MLAs who take bribes to vote or ask questions in legislature cannot claim protection of Arts. 105(2) and 194(2) respectively; bribery is NOT a legislative act
Ashwini Kumar Upadhyay v. Union of India (Waqf Amendment) 2024–2025 SC Waqf (Amendment) Act, 2025 challenged; SC issued notice; arguments ongoing — raised issues under Arts. 14, 25, 26 and right of Muslim minorities
Re: Article 370 (Dr. Farooq Abdullah Case) 2023 SC (5-judge) Upheld abrogation of Art. 370 by Presidential Order; Art. 370 was a temporary provision; President acting with Parliament's approval had the power; J&K's statehood must be restored — timeline to be fixed
Anoop Baranwal v. Union of India (ECI Appointments) 2023 SC (5-judge Constitution Bench) SC directed that appointment of CEC and ECs shall be by a committee comprising PM, Leader of Opposition, and CJI — till Parliament enacts law; Parliament then enacted CEC Act 2023 removing CJI — Act challenged
In Re: Criminalisation of Politics (ADR v. Union of India) 2023–2024 SC Candidates with pending criminal cases must publish information on their website, social media, and local newspaper; political parties must explain reason for fielding such candidates
Setalvad v. Union of India 2022 SC Upheld CBI investigation into Zakia Jafri complaint on 2002 Gujarat riots; SC cautioned against abuse of process to reopen settled matters
Vivek Narayan Sharma v. Union of India (Demonetisation Case) 2023 SC (5-judge) Demonetisation of Rs. 500/1000 notes (November 2016) upheld by 4:1 majority; RBI recommendation preceded Centre's decision; dissent by Justice B.V. Nagarathna — procedure not followed
Bilkis Bano v. Union of India 2024 SC (2-judge) Gujarat government had no jurisdiction to grant remission in the Bilkis Bano rape case (offence against Central law); remission orders quashed; convicts directed to surrender
Devika Biswas v. Union of India 2016 SC Coercive sterilisation camps violate Art. 21; right to reproductive autonomy is part of Art. 21
Justice K.S. Puttaswamy v. Union of India (Aadhaar-2) 2018 SC (5-judge) Aadhaar upheld as constitutional for government benefits; struck down for private entities, mobile authentication, schools; dissent on Money Bill certification by Justice Chandrachud
Vineeta Sharma v. Rakesh Sharma 2020 SC (3-judge) Daughter's right to be coparcener under Hindu Succession Act (as amended 2005) is applicable even if father died before 2005 — right is by birth, not dependent on father's survival
Manish Kumar v. Union of India 2021 SC Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC) amendments upheld; pre-packaged insolvency resolution process for MSMEs valid
State of Tamil Nadu v. Governor of Tamil Nadu 2023–2024 SC SC issued unprecedented mandamus to Governor of Tamil Nadu to act on Bills pending assent within a fixed time; later Governor referred Bills to President — SC held Governor cannot indefinitely withhold or refer Bills without reason; laid down time limits for gubernatorial action
