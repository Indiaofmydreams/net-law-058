A.K. Gopalan v. State of Madras1950; Narrow interpretation of Art. 21 — any procedure prescribed by law suffices
State of Madras v. Champakam Dorairajan1951; FRs prevail over DPSPs; prompted 1st Amendment adding Art. 15(4)
Shankari Prasad v. Union of India1951; Constitutional amendments are NOT 'law' under Art. 13
In re Delhi Laws Act1951; Excessive delegation is unconstitutional; Parliament cannot abdicate essential legislative function
Bhikhaji Narain Dhakras v. State of MP1955; Doctrine of Eclipse — pre-constitutional law inconsistent with FR becomes dormant, not void
R.M.D. Chamarbaugwala v. Union of India1957; Doctrine of Severability — only unconstitutional part of law struck down
Berubari Union Case1960; Preamble NOT part of Constitution (overruled by Kesavananda Bharati)
State of West Bengal v. Anwar Ali Sarkar1952; Twin tests of reasonable classification under Article 14 laid down
Golak Nath v. State of Punjab1967; Parliament cannot amend Fundamental Rights (overruled by Kesavananda Bharati)
Kesavananda Bharati v. State of Kerala1973; Basic Structure Doctrine; Preamble is part of Constitution; Parliament can amend but not destroy basic structure
Shamsher Singh v. State of Punjab1974; President and Governor are constitutional heads; act on CoM advice in normal matters
E.P. Royappa v. State of Tamil Nadu1974; Arbitrariness violates Art. 14; equality is antithetic to arbitrariness
Indira Nehru Gandhi v. Raj Narain1975; Free and fair elections is basic structure; Rule of Law is basic structure; 39th Amendment struck down
Maneka Gandhi v. Union of India1978; 'Golden triangle' (Arts. 14, 19, 21); procedure under Art. 21 must be just, fair, reasonable
Hussainara Khatoon v. State of Bihar1979; Speedy trial is a component of Art. 21; landmark PIL on undertrial prisoners
Minerva Mills v. Union of India1980; Harmony between Parts III and IV is basic structure; absolute primacy of DPSPs struck down
Olga Tellis v. Bombay Municipal Corp1985; Right to livelihood is part of Art. 21
Bandhua Mukti Morcha v. Union of India1984; SC ordered identification, release, rehabilitation of bonded labourers under Arts. 21 and 23
Indra Sawhney v. Union of India1992; Reservations capped at 50%; creamy layer exclusion; Mandal Commission upheld with limits
S.R. Bommai v. Union of India1994; Art. 356 is justiciable; Assembly in suspended animation; secularism is basic structure
T.M.A. Pai Foundation v. State of Karnataka2002; Rights of minority and non-minority educational institutions; 11-judge bench
I.R. Coelho v. State of Tamil Nadu2007; Laws in 9th Schedule after April 24, 1973 can be challenged if they violate basic structure
K.S. Puttaswamy v. Union of India2017; Right to privacy is a fundamental right under Art. 21; 9-judge bench unanimous
Navtej Singh Johar v. Union of India2018; Section 377 IPC read down — consensual same-sex relations between adults decriminalised
Common Cause v. Union of India2018; Right to die with dignity; passive euthanasia and advance directives (living will) recognised
Janhit Abhiyan v. Union of India2022; 103rd Amendment (10% EWS reservation) upheld by 3:2 majority
Nabam Rebia v. Deputy Speaker, Arunachal Pradesh2016; Governor cannot advance Assembly session contrary to CoM advice
B.P. Singhal v. Union of India2010; Removal of Governor cannot be arbitrary or for political reasons
Rameshwar Prasad v. Union of India2006; Dissolution of Bihar Assembly by Governor struck down as mala fide
Sita Soren v. Union of India2024; MPs taking bribes to vote have NO immunity under Art. 105; overruled P.V. Narasimha Rao
Kihoto Hollohan v. Zachillhu1992; Tenth Schedule (Anti-Defection) upheld; Speaker's decision subject to judicial review
Selvi v. State of Karnataka2010; Narco-analysis, brain mapping, polygraph without consent violate Art. 20(3)
Association for Democratic Reforms v. Union of India2002; Candidates must disclose criminal antecedents, assets, education — right to know under Art. 19(1)(a)
Lily Thomas v. Union of India2013; Convicted MPs/MLAs disqualified from date of conviction — Section 8(4) RPA struck down
Vishaka v. State of Rajasthan1997; Sexual harassment at workplace violates Arts. 14, 19, 21; Vishaka Guidelines with force of law
Shayara Bano v. Union of India2017; Triple talaq struck down as manifestly arbitrary under Art. 14
Dr. Farooq Abdullah v. Union of India2023; Abrogation of Art. 370 and J&K's reorganisation upheld by 5-judge bench
Dimes v. Grand Junction Canal1852; Pecuniary bias automatically disqualifies adjudicator — no proof of actual influence needed
Cooper v. Wandsworth Board of Works1863; Early recognition of audi alteram partem — hearing required even without statutory provision
R v. Sussex Justices, ex parte McCarthy1924; 'Justice must not only be done but must also be seen to be done'
Associated Provincial Picture Houses v. Wednesbury Corporation1948; Wednesbury unreasonableness — decision so unreasonable no reasonable authority could make it
Ridge v. Baldwin1964; Natural justice applies to decisions affecting rights, not just strictly judicial functions — landmark revival
Syed Yakoob v. K.S. Radhakrishnan1964; Error apparent on the face of the record as a distinct ground of certiorari defined
Anisminic v. Foreign Compensation Commission1969; ANY error of law makes a decision a nullity — abolished distinction between jurisdictional and non-jurisdictional errors
A.K. Kraipak v. Union of India1970; Natural justice applies to administrative functions too; rule against bias applies broadly
Gullapalli Nageswara Rao v. A.P. SRTC1959; Investigator cannot be adjudicator; departmental bias recognised
Bhagat Raja v. Union of India1967; Quasi-judicial orders must be reasoned/speaking orders — duty to give reasons
Council of Civil Service Unions v. Minister (GCHQ)1985; Lord Diplock's three grounds: Illegality, Irrationality, Procedural Impropriety
Swadeshi Cotton Mills v. Union of India1981; Natural justice is flexible — content depends on context, statutory framework, consequences
Navjyoti Coop. Group Housing Society v. Union of India1992; First clear recognition of Legitimate Expectation doctrine in India
Union of India v. Hindustan Development Corporation1993; Legitimate Expectation gives procedural right only, NOT substantive right to expected benefit
Motilal Padampat Sugar Mills v. State of UP1979; Promissory estoppel against Government confirmed in India
S.P. Gupta v. Union of India (First Judges Case)1981; Executive primacy in judicial appointments
SC Advocates-on-Record Association v. Union of India (Second Judges Case)1993; Collegium system established; CJI's opinion gets primacy
In re Special Reference (Third Judges Case)1998; Collegium expanded to CJI + 4 senior-most SC judges
SC Advocates-on-Record Association v. Union of India (NJAC Case)2015; 99th Amendment struck down; NJAC unconstitutional; judicial independence is basic structure
L. Chandra Kumar v. Union of India1997; HC's power under Arts. 226/227 is basic structure; CAT decisions subject to HC review
Tata Cellular v. Union of India1994; Judicial review of government contracts: courts apply illegality, irrationality, procedural impropriety
Om Kumar v. Union of India2001; Proportionality doctrine adopted in Indian admin law for review of administrative punishment
D.C. Wadhwa v. State of Bihar1987; Repeated re-promulgation of Ordinances without legislative approval is fraud on Constitution
Pradeep Kumar Biswas v. IICB2002; Instrumentality/agency test for 'State' under Art. 12 — functional test is the key
Ram Jawaya Kapur v. State of Punjab1955; India does not adopt strict separation of powers but has checks and balances
University of Mysore v. C.D. Govinda Rao1964; Quo Warranto is a discretionary writ, not a writ of right
1st Amendment1951; Added Art. 15(4); created 9th Schedule; land reform laws protected; public order added to Art. 19(2)
7th Amendment1956; Reorganisation of States on linguistic basis; Union Territories created; one HC for multiple States
14th Amendment1962; Pondicherry included; Art. 239-A empowers Parliament to create UT Legislatures
24th Amendment1971; Parliament's power to amend ANY provision including FRs (Art. 368 amended); response to Golak Nath
25th Amendment1971; Art. 31C inserted — laws giving effect to Art. 39(b)/(c) cannot be challenged on Arts. 14/19 grounds
26th Amendment1971; Abolished privy purses and special privileges of former rulers of princely States
38th Amendment1975; Presidential proclamation of Emergency made non-justiciable (Emergency era; largely reversed by 44th)
39th Amendment1975; Election of President, VP, PM, Speaker not subject to judicial review — STRUCK DOWN in Indira Nehru Gandhi v. Raj Narain (1975)
42nd Amendment1976; Most sweeping — 'Socialist', 'Secular', 'Integrity' added to Preamble; Fundamental Duties (Art. 51-A); Art. 31C expanded; Administrative Tribunals (Arts. 323-A, 323-B); Education to Concurrent List
44th Amendment1978; Reversed most of 42nd Amendment's excesses; 'Armed rebellion' replaced 'internal disturbance'; Cabinet's written advice required for Emergency; Arts. 20/21 non-suspendable; Right to Property → Art. 300-A
52nd Amendment1985; Anti-Defection Law — 10th Schedule inserted
61st Amendment1988; Voting age reduced from 21 to 18 years (Art. 326)
69th Amendment1991; Delhi given special status as NCT — Legislative Assembly and Council of Ministers
73rd Amendment1992; Panchayati Raj institutions — Part IX added; 11th Schedule (29 subjects for Panchayats)
74th Amendment1992; Municipal bodies — Part IX-A added; 12th Schedule (18 subjects for municipalities)
77th Amendment1995; Art. 16(4-A) — reservation in promotion for SC/ST
81st Amendment2000; Art. 16(4-B) — unfilled SC/ST vacancies carried forward; not subject to 50% ceiling
84th Amendment2001; Delimitation of constituencies freeze extended till 2026
86th Amendment2002; Art. 21-A — free and compulsory education 6–14 years; 11th Fundamental Duty; Art. 45 amended
91st Amendment2003; 10th Schedule amended — 'split' exception removed; only merger of 2/3 recognised; CoM size capped at 15%
93rd Amendment2005; Art. 15(5) — reservation for OBC/SC/ST in private educational institutions
97th Amendment2011; Part IX-B added; right to form cooperative societies under Art. 19(1)(c)
99th Amendment2014; NJAC established — STRUCK DOWN by SC in 2015 as violating basic structure (judicial independence)
100th Amendment2015; Land Boundary Agreement with Bangladesh
101st Amendment2016; Goods and Services Tax (GST) introduced; Art. 246-A, 269-A; GST Council established
102nd Amendment2018; National Commission for Backward Classes given constitutional status (Art. 338-B)
103rd Amendment2019; Art. 15(6) and Art. 16(6) — 10% reservation for EWS; upheld in Janhit Abhiyan (2022)
104th Amendment2020; Abolished nomination of Anglo-Indian members to Lok Sabha and State Assemblies (Arts. 331 & 333)
105th Amendment2021; Restored power of States to identify OBCs for State reservation purposes
B.N. Rau Committee (Constitutional Adviser)1946–49; Drafted key provisions of Constitution; studied constitutions of USA, UK, Ireland, Canada, Australia
Swaran Singh Committee1976; Recommended insertion of Fundamental Duties (42nd Amendment)
Sarkaria Commission1983–1988; Reviewed Centre-State relations; recommended reviving Inter-State Council; greater restraint in Art. 356; independent Governors
Venkatachaliah Commission (National Commission to Review the Constitution)2000–2002; Comprehensive review; recommended strengthening Parliamentary committees, regulating anti-defection
J.S. Verma Committee (Fundamental Duties)1999; Reviewed enforceability of Fundamental Duties; noted they have legal dimensions
Punchhi Commission2007–2010; Second commission on Centre-State relations; recommended stronger Inter-State Council, codifying Governor's discretion, time-limit for assent
P.B. Gajendragadkar Commission1967; Enquired into communal violence; linked to secularism provisions
L.M. Singhvi Committee1986; Recommended 73rd/74th Amendments (Panchayati Raj); coined term 'gram sabha'
Ranganath Misra Commission2007; Recommended reservation for minorities and linguistic minorities; some Fundamental Duties be made justiciable
Administrative Reforms Commission (1st ARC)1966–1970; Recommended Lokpal and Lokayukta; Morarji Desai headed it
2nd Administrative Reforms Commission2005–2009; Thirteen reports on administrative reforms including citizen-centric administration, Ethics in Governance, Public Order
21st Law Commission of India2018; Recommended Uniform Civil Code — India is not ready yet; codification of personal laws should come first
Law Commission Report 2772018; Recommended abolition of sedition law (Section 124-A IPC) — since carried forward; SC stayed its operation in 2022
