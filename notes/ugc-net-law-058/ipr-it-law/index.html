UGC NET Law (058) Intellectual Property Rights and IT Law: Detailed Study Notes

Unit IX is statute-heavy and predictable: section numbers, time periods and landmark cases repeat every cycle. Topic 0 explains the exam pattern and the priority of each topic. Topics 1 to 3 cover the concept, theories and international conventions; Topics 4 to 6 cover copyright, patents and trademarks; Topics 7 and 8 cover geographical indications, biodiversity and traditional knowledge; Topics 9 and 10 cover the IT Act, cyber crimes and adjudication. Topic 11 collects master tables of numbers, institutions, matching pairs, a chronology, common traps and a revision plan.

    How to use these notes

    1. Round 1: ReadStart with Topic 0 and its priority table. Give the most time to Topics 4 to 6 and 9 to 10 (copyright, patents, trademarks, IT and cyber law), which carry the most questions.
    2. Round 2: ReviseSwitch on Revision mode on any topic page to keep each section's key terms, lists and tables. Learn the section numbers with their subjects, and the cases with their principles.
    3. Last week: RecallUse the rapid-recall sheet (numbers, institutions, matching pairs), then the chronology and common traps in Topic 11, and practice MCQs.
