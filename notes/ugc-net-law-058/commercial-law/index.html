UGC NET Law (058) Commercial Law: Detailed Study Notes

Commercial Law is a high-scoring unit built on statutes, section numbers and time limits as much as on cases. The notes follow the syllabus: the Indian Contract Act (formation, breach, quasi-contract, specific contracts), the Sale of Goods Act, Partnership and LLP, the Negotiable Instruments Act, Company Law (incorporation, shares, directors, meetings) and CSR. Topic 0 explains how the unit is tested and Topic 11 collects the priority order, a numbers and time-limits cheat sheet, a landmark-case master list and a revision plan.

    How to use these notes

    1. Round 1: ReadStart with Topic 0, then go through Topics 1 to 10. Company Law (Topics 8 and 9) usually carries the most questions, so give it the most time; learn definitions, numbers and thresholds.
    2. Round 2: ReviseSwitch on Revision mode on any topic page to keep each section's key terms, lists and tables. Use the Exam Pointers at the end of Topic 5 and the priority order in Topic 11.
    3. Last week: RecallUse the rapid-recall sheet (numbers, time limits and the case master list), then the recent amendments in Topic 11 and practice MCQs.
