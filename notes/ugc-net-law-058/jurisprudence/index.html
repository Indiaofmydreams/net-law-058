UGC NET Law (058) Jurisprudence: Detailed Study Notes

Jurisprudence is the most name-heavy unit of Law 058: who wrote which book, which school holds which view, and which case settled which principle. Topic 0 shows how the unit is tested. Topics 1 and 2 cover the nature and sources of law and every school of thought; Topics 3 to 7 cover law and morality, rights and duties, legal personality, property and liability; Topics 8 to 10 are the newer topics (law and poverty, global justice, modernism and post-modernism). Each topic ends with a Memory Capsule, and Topic 11 holds the thinker-work list, the case-law list and ten exam-day strategies.

    How to use these notes

    1. Round 1: ReadStart with Topic 0, then read Topics 1 to 10 in order. For each school build a two-column memory sheet: thinker and book, core idea, one-line criticism.
    2. Round 2: ReviseSwitch on Revision mode on any topic page to keep each section's key terms, lists and tables, plus the Memory Capsule at the end of the topic. Recite the debates: Hart-Austin, Hart-Fuller, Hart-Devlin, Hart-Dworkin.
    3. Last week: RecallUse the rapid-recall sheet (thinkers with their works and years, and the case-law list), then Topic 11's exam-day strategies and practice MCQs.
