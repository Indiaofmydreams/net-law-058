11.5Frequent confusions (MCQ traps in one place)#

In brief

Covers Void vs voidable, Judicial separation vs divorce, Hiba (gift) vs wasiyat (will), Aul vs Radd, Wakf vs trust and more.

  • Void vs voidable
  • Judicial separation vs divorce
  • Hiba (gift) vs wasiyat (will)
  • Aul vs Radd
  • Wakf vs trust

  • Void vs voidable: HMA Sections 11 (void) and 12 (voidable); child is legitimate in both (Section 16).

  • Judicial separation vs divorce: marriage subsists after judicial separation.

  • Mutual consent vs irretrievable breakdown: 13B is statutory; irretrievable breakdown is Article 142 only.

  • Dower vs dowry; mahr vs mutah (gift on divorce) vs muta (temporary marriage).

  • Hizanat (custody) vs wilayat (guardianship): mother has hizanat; father has wilayat.

  • Guardian and Wards Act (secular) vs HMGA (Hindu).

  • Hiba (gift) vs wasiyat (will): hiba effective immediately with delivery; wasiyat after death.

  • Aul vs Radd: Aul reduces shares; Radd returns the surplus.

  • Wakf vs trust: ownership in God; mutawalli is not owner.

  • Section 125 CrPC vs Section 144 BNSS: same subject; do not confuse with Section 144 CrPC (prohibitory orders).

  • Natural guardian of a married Hindu girl: her husband.
