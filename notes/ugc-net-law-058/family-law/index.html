UGC NET Law (058) Family Law: Detailed Study Notes

Family Law mixes codified statutes (Hindu, Special Marriage, Maintenance, Succession) with uncodified personal law and a fast-moving constitutional layer: live-in relationships, foreign decrees and the new State Uniform Civil Codes. Topic 0 gives the exam pattern, recent trends and the top 25 one-liners to memorise first. Topics 1 to 10 follow the ten syllabus topics. Each ends with a Memory Capsule of MCQ traps and an Analysis Angle on the recent trend, and Topic 11 is a revision toolkit of cases, section cheat-sheet, numbers, chronology and confusions.

    How to use these notes

    1. Round 1: ReadStart with Topic 0 and its 25 one-liners, then read Topics 1 to 10 in order. Each topic has the concept, provisions and conditions in lists, and tables for cases and comparisons.
    2. Round 2: ReviseSwitch on Revision mode on any topic page to keep each section's key terms, lists and tables, plus the MCQ traps (Memory Capsule) and the recent trend (Analysis Angle). Then work through the Topic 11 toolkit.
    3. Last week: RecallUse the rapid-recall sheet (master case table, section cheat-sheet, numbers and time limits), then the chronology in Topic 11 and practice MCQs.
