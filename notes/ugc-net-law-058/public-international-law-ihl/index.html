UGC NET Law (058) Public International Law and IHL: Detailed Study Notes

Public International Law and IHL is a scoring unit because the questions are factual and the same cases, treaties and Article numbers repeat. Topic 0 shows how the unit is tested. Topics 1 to 4 cover sources, recognition, nationality, refugees, extradition and asylum; Topics 5 to 7 cover the UN organs, dispute settlement and state responsibility, and the WTO; Topic 8 covers international humanitarian law. Topics 9 and 10 are revision: rapid tables of numbers, years and cases, then frequently confused pairs, mnemonics and a recent-developments radar.

    How to use these notes

    1. Round 1: ReadStart with Topic 0, then read Topics 1 to 8 in order. For each topic learn the definition, the key Articles and years, three to five leading cases, the India angle and the recent development.
    2. Round 2: ReviseSwitch on Revision mode on any topic page to keep each section's key terms, lists and tables. Then work through Topic 9 (numbers, years, case-to-principle) and Topic 10 (confusing pairs and mnemonics).
    3. Last week: RecallUse the rapid-recall sheet (numbers, years, case matches, WTO agreements), then the recent-developments radar in Topic 10, and practise MCQs.
