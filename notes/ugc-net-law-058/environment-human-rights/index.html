UGC NET Law (058) Environment and Human Rights: Detailed Study Notes

Unit VIII is one of the most predictable units of Law 058: ten syllabus topics, mostly factual questions, and the same years, Articles, section numbers, bodies and landmark cases recycled every cycle. Topic 0 gives the exam pattern, trends and a priority map. Topics 1 to 5 cover environment law (concepts, international law, the Indian framework, EIA and hazardous waste, the National Green Tribunal) and Topics 6 to 10 cover human rights (concept, universalism and relativism, the International Bill of Rights, group rights, NHRC and national commissions).

    1. Round 1: ReadStart with Topic 0 and its priority map. Read the topics marked very high first: the NGT (Topic 5), NHRC and commissions (Topic 10), the International Bill of Rights (Topic 8) and the Indian framework (Topic 3).
    2. Round 2: ReviseSwitch on Revision mode on any topic page to keep each section's key lists and tables, plus the Memory Capsule at the end of the topic. Rewrite the conference chronology and the commission comparison from memory.
    3. Last week: RecallUse the rapid-recall sheet (conferences, conventions, statutes, cases, HR timeline) and then practise MCQs topic by topic.
