Try these five before you start. They are part of the paper, so you may prefer to skip the answers until after you have attempted it.

1Virsa Singh v. State of Punjab is associated with:

  1. Conspiracy
  2. Intentional bodily injury sufficient in ordinary course to cause death
  3. Insanity
  4. Provocation
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Intentional bodily injury sufficient in ordinary course to cause death

Test for murder under the injury clause.

  1. Forest conservation
  2. Wildlife trade

  4. Air pollution
Show answer and explanation

5The doctrine of colourable legislation rests on the idea that:

  1. What cannot be done directly cannot be done indirectly
  2. Law operates prospectively
  3. Courts cannot review laws
  4. Hear both sides
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. What cannot be done directly cannot be done indirectly

A legislature cannot transgress its limits by disguise.
