About this question set

This set has 20 multiple-choice questions on Constitutional & Administrative Law from the UGC NET Law (058) syllabus, unit 2, Constitutional and Administrative Law. Try each question first, then tap Show answer to check the key and read the explanation. 20 of the 20 questions carry a written explanation.

For the concepts and case law behind these questions, read the Constitutional & Administrative Law notes. Once you are comfortable, test yourself under exam conditions with a full-length mock test.

20 practice questions with answers

1The Preamble was held to be part of the Constitution in:

  1. Berubari Union case
  2. Kesavananda Bharati
  3. Sajjan Singh
  4. Golaknath
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Kesavananda Bharati

Kesavananda overruled Berubari on this point.

Report this question

2Right to education for children aged 6–14 is under:

  1. Article 30
  2. Article 29
  3. Article 21A
  4. Article 45
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Article 21A

Inserted by the 86th Amendment, 2002.

Report this question

3Fundamental Duties were added by the:

  1. 24th Amendment
  2. 73rd Amendment
  3. 44th Amendment
  4. 42nd Amendment
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. 42nd Amendment

Article 51A was inserted on the Swaran Singh Committee's recommendation.

Report this question

4The 'heart and soul' of the Constitution according to Dr. Ambedkar:

  1. Article 32
  2. Article 226
  3. Article 19
  4. Article 14
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Article 32

Article 32 gives the right to move the Supreme Court for fundamental rights.

Report this question

5Judicial review of a Presidential Proclamation under Article 356 was affirmed in:

  1. Indira Sawhney
  2. Kihoto Hollohan
  3. L. Chandra Kumar
  4. S.R. Bommai v. Union of India
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. S.R. Bommai v. Union of India

Bommai (1994) held such a proclamation is justiciable.

Report this question

6Procedure for amending the Constitution is in:

  1. Article 356
  2. Article 370
  3. Article 368
  4. Article 352
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Article 368

Article 368 provides Parliament's amending power and procedure.

Report this question

7Harmony between Fundamental Rights and DPSPs was held part of the basic structure in:

  1. Golaknath
  2. Waman Rao
  3. Minerva Mills
  4. Shankari Prasad
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Minerva Mills

Minerva Mills (1980) struck down parts of the 42nd Amendment.

Report this question

8Articles 14, 19 and 21 are inter-related, and procedure must be fair, just and reasonable, per:

  1. A.K. Gopalan
  2. Maneka Gandhi
  3. Kharak Singh
  4. ADM Jabalpur
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Maneka Gandhi

Maneka Gandhi (1978) expanded Article 21.

Report this question

9The Election Commission of India derives power from:

  1. Article 329
  2. Article 324
  3. Article 280
  4. Article 326
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Article 324

Article 324 vests superintendence of elections in the Commission.

Report this question

10Residuary legislative power in India rests with:

  1. States
  2. Governor
  3. Concurrent list
  4. Union
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Union

Article 248 and Entry 97 of the Union List give residuary power to Parliament.

Report this question

11'Armed rebellion' replaced 'internal disturbance' as ground for National Emergency by:

  1. 38th Amendment
  2. 52nd Amendment
  3. 44th Amendment
  4. 42nd Amendment
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. 44th Amendment

The 44th Amendment amended Article 352.

Report this question

12The rule 'audi alteram partem' means:

  1. Hear the other side
  2. Reasoned decision
  3. Legitimate expectation
  4. No one be a judge in own cause
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Hear the other side

It is the right to be heard, a core natural justice principle.

Report this question

13'Nemo judex in causa sua' is the rule against:

  1. Delay
  2. Bias
  3. Arbitrariness
  4. Retrospectivity
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Bias

No one should be a judge in their own cause.

Report this question

14Which case blurred the distinction between quasi-judicial and administrative functions for natural justice?

  1. Cooper v. Wandsworth
  2. A.K. Kraipak v. Union of India
  3. Ridge v. Baldwin
  4. Maneka Gandhi
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. A.K. Kraipak v. Union of India

Kraipak applied natural justice to administrative action.

Report this question

15'Wednesbury unreasonableness' comes from:

  1. Associated Provincial Picture Houses v. Wednesbury Corporation
  2. Ridge v. Baldwin
  3. Anisminic
  4. Entick v. Carrington
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Associated Provincial Picture Houses v. Wednesbury Corporation

A decision so unreasonable that no reasonable authority could make it.

Report this question

16Promissory estoppel against the Government was developed in India in:

  1. Navtej Johar
  2. Motilal Padampat Sugar Mills v. State of U.P.
  3. Kesavananda Bharati
  4. Bachan Singh
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Motilal Padampat Sugar Mills v. State of U.P.

Motilal Padampat applied the doctrine to public authorities.

Report this question

17Lord Diplock's three grounds of judicial review are:

  1. Vires, fairness, necessity
  2. Jurisdiction, merit, equity
  3. Illegality, irrationality, procedural impropriety
  4. Bias, delay, malice
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Illegality, irrationality, procedural impropriety

Stated in the GCHQ case (CCSU v. Minister for the Civil Service).

Report this question

18The writ of mandamus means:

  1. To be certified
  2. You may have the body
  3. By what authority
  4. We command
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. We command

It commands a public authority to perform a public duty.

Report this question

19NJAC Act, 2014 was struck down in:

  1. Third Judges Case
  2. Supreme Court Advocates-on-Record Association v. Union of India (2015)
  3. S.P. Gupta
  4. Second Judges Case
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Supreme Court Advocates-on-Record Association v. Union of India (2015)

The Fourth Judges Case restored the collegium system.

Report this question

20The test of 'intelligible differentia' and 'rational nexus' relates to:

  1. Article 14
  2. Article 19
  3. Article 21
  4. Article 25
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Article 14

It is the test for permissible classification under Article 14.
