About this question set

This set has 12 multiple-choice questions on Chronological Arrangement from the UGC NET Law (058) syllabus, unit 2, Constitutional and Administrative Law. Try each question first, then tap Show answer to check the key and read the explanation. 12 of the 12 questions carry a written explanation.

For the concepts and case law behind these questions, read the Constitutional & Administrative Law notes. Once you are comfortable, test yourself under exam conditions with a full-length mock test.
