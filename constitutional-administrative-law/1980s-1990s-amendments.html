About this question set

This set has 19 multiple-choice questions on 1980s-1990s Amendments from the UGC NET Law (058) syllabus, unit 2, Constitutional and Administrative Law. Try each question first, then tap Show answer to check the key and read the explanation. 19 of the 19 questions carry a written explanation.

For the concepts and case law behind these questions, read the Constitutional & Administrative Law notes. Once you are comfortable, test yourself under exam conditions with a full-length mock test.

19 practice questions with answers

1The Anti-Defection Law and the Tenth Schedule were introduced by which Amendment?

  1. Forty-second Amendment, 1976
  2. Sixty-first Amendment, 1989
  3. Ninety-first Amendment, 2003
  4. Fifty-second Amendment, 1985
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Fifty-second Amendment, 1985

Arts. 101, 102, 190 and 191 were amended to include disqualification on defection.

Report this question

2Kihoto Hollohan v. Zachillhu (1992) held that:

  1. The Speaker's decision is final and non-justiciable
  2. The Tenth Schedule is valid but paragraph 7 (ouster of jurisdiction) is unconstitutional for want of ratification
  3. Para 4 (merger) is invalid
  4. The entire Tenth Schedule is unconstitutional
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. The Tenth Schedule is valid but paragraph 7 (ouster of jurisdiction) is unconstitutional for want of ratification

Decided by a 3:2 majority; the Speaker acts as a tribunal and his decisions are subject to judicial review.

Report this question

3Which Amendment removed the exception regarding 'split' in the Tenth Schedule?

  1. Seventy-fourth Amendment
  2. Fifty-fifth Amendment
  3. Fifty-second Amendment
  4. Ninety-first Amendment, 2003
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Ninety-first Amendment, 2003

Also capped the Council of Ministers at 15% of the total strength of the Lok Sabha (Art. 75(1A)) and inserted Art. 361B.

Report this question

4The minimum voting age was reduced from 21 to 18 years by:

  1. Forty-second Amendment
  2. Seventy-third Amendment
  3. Eighty-fourth Amendment
  4. Sixty-first Amendment (1988)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Sixty-first Amendment (1988)

Art. 326 was amended; came into force on 28 March 1989.

Report this question

5Special provisions for Mizoram (Art. 371G) were inserted by the:

  1. Fifty-sixth Amendment, 1987
  2. Thirteenth Amendment, 1962
  3. Fifty-fifth Amendment, 1986
  4. Fifty-third Amendment, 1986
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Fifty-third Amendment, 1986

55th → Arunachal Pradesh (Art. 371H); 56th → Goa (Art. 371I).

Report this question

6Authoritative text of the Constitution in Hindi was provided under Art. 394A inserted by the:

  1. Sixtieth Amendment
  2. Fifty-seventh Amendment
  3. Sixty-first Amendment
  4. Fifty-eighth Amendment, 1987
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Fifty-eighth Amendment, 1987

President may publish authoritative Hindi translation.

Report this question

7The Sixtieth Amendment (1988) increased the ceiling of profession tax from:

  1. ₹250 to ₹2,500 per annum
  2. ₹2,500 to ₹25,000
  3. ₹500 to ₹5,000
  4. ₹100 to ₹1,000
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. ₹250 to ₹2,500 per annum

Amended Art. 276(2).

Report this question

8Delhi was given the status of 'National Capital Territory' by which Amendment?

  1. Sixty-ninth Amendment, 1991
  2. Seventy-first Amendment
  3. Fifty-fifth Amendment
  4. Seventy-third Amendment
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Sixty-ninth Amendment, 1991

Inserted Arts. 239AA and 239AB. Government of NCT of Delhi v. Union of India (2018) interpreted these provisions.

Report this question

9The Seventy-third Amendment (1992) added which Part and Schedule?

  1. Part IXA and Twelfth Schedule
  2. Part IXB and Eleventh Schedule
  3. Part IX and Eleventh Schedule
  4. Part XIVA and Ninth Schedule
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Part IX and Eleventh Schedule

Arts. 243 to 243-O; the Eleventh Schedule has 29 subjects.

Report this question

10The Seventy-fourth Amendment (1992) inserted:

  1. Part IXA (Municipalities) and Twelfth Schedule (18 subjects)
  2. Part IXB (Co-operatives)
  3. Part IX (Panchayats) and Eleventh Schedule
  4. Part XVIII
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Part IXA (Municipalities) and Twelfth Schedule (18 subjects)

Arts. 243-P to 243-ZG.

Report this question

11Art. 243-D requires reservation of not less than ____ of total seats for women in Panchayats.

  1. One-half
  2. One-third
  3. One-fourth
  4. Twenty percent
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. One-third

Raised to one-half by several States by State legislation.

Report this question

12When did the 73rd and 74th Amendments come into force respectively?

  1. 26 January 1993 and 1 April 1993
  2. 24 April 1992 and 1 June 1993
  3. 24 April 1993 and 1 June 1993
  4. 20 April 1992 and 1 June 1992
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. 24 April 1993 and 1 June 1993

Passed in December 1992 and ratified in 1993; 24 April is celebrated as National Panchayati Raj Day.

Report this question

13The Seventy-second Amendment (1992) relates to:

  1. Goa
  2. Reservation of seats for Scheduled Tribes in Tripura Legislative Assembly
  3. Sikkim
  4. Mizoram
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Reservation of seats for Scheduled Tribes in Tripura Legislative Assembly

Minor but frequently asked.

Report this question

14Which of the following Amendments dealt with Art. 16(4A) — reservation in promotions for SC/STs?

  1. Eighty-second Amendment
  2. Eighty-first Amendment
  3. Eighty-fifth Amendment
  4. Seventy-seventh Amendment, 1995
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Seventy-seventh Amendment, 1995

Followed Indra Sawhney v. Union of India (1992), which held that reservation in promotions was not permitted.

Report this question

15The Seventy-sixth Amendment (1994) placed which State law in the Ninth Schedule?

  1. Bihar Land Reforms Act
  2. Tamil Nadu Backward Classes, SC and ST (Reservation of Seats) Act, 1993 providing 69% reservation
  3. Maharashtra Maratha Reservation Act
  4. Karnataka Reservation Act
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Tamil Nadu Backward Classes, SC and ST (Reservation of Seats) Act, 1993 providing 69% reservation

Challenged in I.R. Coelho and in the Indra Sawhney context.

Report this question

16Indra Sawhney v. Union of India (1992) was decided by a bench of:

  1. Eleven judges
  2. Nine judges, upholding 27% OBC reservation but capping total reservation at 50%
  3. Five judges
  4. Seven judges
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Nine judges, upholding 27% OBC reservation but capping total reservation at 50%

Also introduced the 'creamy layer' exclusion and held Art. 16(4) does not permit reservation in promotions.

Report this question

17S.R. Bommai v. Union of India (1994) is related to:

  1. Imposition of President's Rule under Art. 356 and secularism
  2. Right to education
  3. Delimitation of constituencies
  4. Freedom of press
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Imposition of President's Rule under Art. 356 and secularism

Held that Art. 356 is subject to judicial review and secularism is part of the basic structure.

Report this question

18The Fifty-ninth Amendment (1988), later repealed, extended the power to impose Emergency in which State?

  1. Assam
  2. Punjab (Art. 356(5))
  3. Nagaland
  4. Jammu and Kashmir
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Punjab (Art. 356(5))

Repealed by the 63rd Amendment (1989).

Report this question

19The Sixty-fifth Amendment (1990) provided for:

  1. National Commission for OBCs
  2. National Commission for SCs and STs (Art. 338)
  3. National Human Rights Commission
  4. National Judicial Commission
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. National Commission for SCs and STs (Art. 338)

Replaced by two separate commissions by the 89th Amendment (2003).
