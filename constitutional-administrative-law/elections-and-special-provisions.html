9 practice questions with answers

1N.P. Ponnuswami v. Returning Officer, Namakkal (1952) laid down that:

  1. Art. 329(b) bars courts from interfering in the election process once it has begun, relief being by election petition after the election
  2. Courts may interfere with any stage of the election process
  3. Election disputes lie under Art. 32 only
  4. Rejection of nomination can always be challenged by writ
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Art. 329(b) bars courts from interfering in the election process once it has begun, relief being by election petition after the election

Art. 329(b) is a non obstante bar.

2Mohinder Singh Gill v. Chief Election Commissioner (1978) explained that Art. 324:

  1. Is a plenary residuary power which can fill gaps where law is silent, but cannot override law and must be exercised fairly
  2. Is subordinate to the Representation of the People Act
  3. Gives the Commission legislative power
  4. Applies only to Parliament elections
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Is a plenary residuary power which can fill gaps where law is silent, but cannot override law and must be exercised fairly

Art. 324 is supplementary to enacted law.

3Lily Thomas v. Union of India (2013) is notable for:

  1. Upholding s.8(4) of the Representation of the People Act
  2. Introducing NOTA
  3. Striking down s.8(4) RPA, so a convicted legislator sentenced to two or more years is disqualified immediately
  4. Mandating disclosure of assets
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Striking down s.8(4) RPA, so a convicted legislator sentenced to two or more years is disqualified immediately

The protection of three months/appeal for sitting members was invalid.

4In Samatha v. State of Andhra Pradesh (1997), the Supreme Court held that:

  1. Leases of tribal land to private parties are valid
  2. The Governor cannot regulate land transfer
  3. The Fifth Schedule applies to Assam
  4. Transfer or lease of land in Scheduled Areas to non-tribals and private companies is void under the Fifth Schedule
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Transfer or lease of land in Scheduled Areas to non-tribals and private companies is void under the Fifth Schedule

The State is a 'person' for the purpose of para 5(2) of the Fifth Schedule.

5In Re: Article 370 (2023), the Constitution Bench held that:

  1. Art. 370 was a temporary provision, and the President could issue the 2019 notification under Art. 370(3) even after the Constituent Assembly of J&K ceased to exist
  2. Art. 370 was a permanent provision
  3. The J&K Reorganisation Act was invalid
  4. Ladakh could not be made a Union Territory
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Art. 370 was a temporary provision, and the President could issue the 2019 notification under Art. 370(3) even after the Constituent Assembly of J&K ceased to exist

The recommendation of the Constituent Assembly was not binding on the President.

6Under Art. 348(2), the Governor, with the previous consent of the President, may authorise the use of Hindi or another State official language in:

  1. Proceedings of the High Court, though not its judgments, decrees or orders
  2. Judgments, decrees and orders of the High Court
  3. Proceedings of the Supreme Court
  4. Parliament
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Proceedings of the High Court, though not its judgments, decrees or orders

The proviso excludes judgments, decrees and orders.

7Art. 340 enables the President to appoint a Commission to investigate the conditions of backward classes. The first such Commission was:

  1. Mandal Commission (1979)
  2. Swaran Singh Commission
  3. Sarkaria Commission
  4. Kaka Kalelkar Commission (1953)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Kaka Kalelkar Commission (1953)

The second was the B.P. Mandal Commission (1979).

8Under Art. 371A, an Act of Parliament affecting which of the following does not apply to Nagaland unless its Legislative Assembly so resolves?

  1. Citizenship
  2. Defence of India
  3. Taxation
  4. Naga customary law and procedure, and ownership and transfer of land and resources
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Naga customary law and procedure, and ownership and transfer of land and resources

Art. 371A(1)(a) also covers religious or social practices of the Nagas.

9In In Re: Section 6A of the Citizenship Act, 1955 (2024), the Constitution Bench by a 4:1 majority:

  1. Struck down s.6A
  2. Held it violated Art. 29
  3. Upheld s.6A, which grants citizenship to migrants from Bangladesh who entered Assam before 25 March 1971
  4. Fixed the cut-off at 1951
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Upheld s.6A, which grants citizenship to migrants from Bangladesh who entered Assam before 25 March 1971

Pardiwala J dissented.
