About this question set

This set has 25 multiple-choice questions on 1970s Amendments & Emergency Era from the UGC NET Law (058) syllabus, unit 2, Constitutional and Administrative Law. Try each question first, then tap Show answer to check the key and read the explanation. 25 of the 25 questions carry a written explanation.

For the concepts and case law behind these questions, read the Constitutional & Administrative Law notes. Once you are comfortable, test yourself under exam conditions with a full-length mock test.

25 practice questions with answers

1The Twenty-fifth Amendment replaced the word 'compensation' by 'amount' in Art. 31(2). This followed which case?

  1. Kesavananda Bharati
  2. R.C. Cooper v. Union of India (Bank Nationalisation case, 1970)
  3. Golak Nath
  4. Indira Gandhi v. Raj Narain
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. R.C. Cooper v. Union of India (Bank Nationalisation case, 1970)

The Court had held that compensation must be the 'equivalent' of the property acquired.

Report this question

2Abolition of Privy Purses and insertion of Art. 363A was done by:

  1. Twenty-ninth Amendment, 1972
  2. Twenty-fourth Amendment, 1971
  3. Twenty-fifth Amendment, 1971
  4. Twenty-sixth Amendment, 1971
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Twenty-sixth Amendment, 1971

Followed Madhav Rao Scindia v. Union of India (1970-71), which struck down the Presidential Order derecognising Rulers.

Report this question

3The Thirty-first Amendment (1973) increased the strength of the Lok Sabha from:

  1. 525 to 545
  2. 545 to 552
  3. 500 to 525
  4. 530 to 550
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. 525 to 545

Currently the maximum is 550 (530 States + 20 UTs) with the Anglo-Indian nomination ended by the 104th Amendment.

Report this question

4Telangana Regional Committee and Arts. 371D and 371E were inserted by:

  1. Fifty-fifth Amendment, 1986
  2. Thirty-third Amendment, 1974
  3. Thirty-second Amendment, 1973
  4. Ninety-eighth Amendment, 2012
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Thirty-second Amendment, 1973

Art. 371D relates to equitable opportunities in public employment and education in Andhra Pradesh/Telangana.

Report this question

5Which Amendment removed the monetary valuation limit for civil appeals to the Supreme Court?

  1. Fifteenth Amendment, 1963
  2. Sixty-first Amendment, 1989
  3. Thirtieth Amendment, 1972
  4. Forty-second Amendment, 1976
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Thirtieth Amendment, 1972

Appeals now lie on a 'substantial question of law of general importance'.

Report this question

6The Twenty-ninth Amendment (1972) placed which State land reform Acts in the Ninth Schedule?

  1. Bihar Land Reforms Acts
  2. Zamindari Abolition Acts of UP
  3. Kerala Land Reforms (Amendment) Acts
  4. Madras Land Reforms Acts
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Kerala Land Reforms (Amendment) Acts

These were challenged in Kesavananda Bharati.

Report this question

7Sikkim became an Associate State by the ___ Amendment and a full State by the ___ Amendment.

  1. 34th (1974); 35th (1975)
  2. 31st (1973); 32nd (1973)
  3. 35th (1974); 36th (1975)
  4. 36th (1975); 37th (1975)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. 35th (1974); 36th (1975)

Sikkim became the 22nd State; Art. 371F inserted. The old Tenth Schedule for Sikkim was introduced by the 35th and omitted by the 36th.

Report this question

8The Thirty-third Amendment (1974) relates to:

  1. Resignation of Members of Parliament/State Legislatures — Presiding officer must be satisfied that resignation is voluntary
  2. Extension of Lok Sabha term
  3. Disqualification on ground of defection
  4. Creation of Union Territories
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Resignation of Members of Parliament/State Legislatures — Presiding officer must be satisfied that resignation is voluntary

Amended Arts. 101(3)(b) and 190(3)(b).

Report this question

9Thirty-eighth Amendment (1975) made which of the following non-justiciable?

  1. Parliamentary privileges
  2. Election of the Prime Minister
  3. Appointment of Governors
  4. President's satisfaction in declaring Emergency (Art. 352), and in promulgating ordinances
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. President's satisfaction in declaring Emergency (Art. 352), and in promulgating ordinances

Later reversed by the 44th Amendment.

Report this question

10The Thirty-ninth Amendment (1975) placed the election of which functionaries beyond judicial scrutiny through the new Art. 329A?

  1. President, Vice-President, Prime Minister and Speaker
  2. Governor and Chief Ministers
  3. Judges of High Courts
  4. Members of Rajya Sabha
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. President, Vice-President, Prime Minister and Speaker

Clause (4) of Art. 329A was struck down in Indira Nehru Gandhi v. Raj Narain (1975).

Report this question

11In Indira Nehru Gandhi v. Raj Narain (1975) the Supreme Court struck down which part of the 39th Amendment?

  1. Clause (4) of Art. 329A, as violating free and fair elections, democracy and rule of law (basic structure)
  2. Ninth Schedule additions
  3. Art. 71
  4. Entire Art. 329A
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Clause (4) of Art. 329A, as violating free and fair elections, democracy and rule of law (basic structure)

The Court applied the basic structure doctrine for the first time to strike down a constitutional amendment.

Report this question

12The Fortieth Amendment (1976) dealt with:

  1. Territorial waters, continental shelf, EEZ and maritime zones (Art. 297)
  2. Fundamental Duties
  3. Lok Sabha tenure
  4. Anti-defection
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Territorial waters, continental shelf, EEZ and maritime zones (Art. 297)

Also added more laws to the Ninth Schedule.

Report this question

13Which Committee's recommendations led to the Forty-second Amendment (1976)?

  1. Ashok Mehta Committee
  2. Venkatachaliah Commission
  3. Swaran Singh Committee
  4. Sarkaria Commission
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Swaran Singh Committee

Often called a 'Mini Constitution'.

Report this question

14Which of the following was NOT done by the Forty-second Amendment?

  1. Added Part XIVA (Tribunals)
  2. Added Part IVA (Fundamental Duties)
  3. Inserted Art. 21A (Right to Education)
  4. Added 'Socialist', 'Secular' and 'Integrity' to the Preamble
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Inserted Art. 21A (Right to Education)

Art. 21A came by the 86th Amendment. The 42nd also added Arts. 39A, 43A, 48A and 31D.

Report this question

15Part XIVA (Arts. 323A and 323B) on Tribunals was inserted by which Amendment?

  1. Seventy-third Amendment, 1992
  2. Fifty-second Amendment, 1985
  3. Forty-second Amendment, 1976
  4. Forty-fourth Amendment, 1978
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Forty-second Amendment, 1976

L. Chandra Kumar v. Union of India (1997) held clauses excluding High Court jurisdiction unconstitutional.

Report this question

16The Fundamental Duties were originally ____ in number, and the 11th duty was added by the ____ Amendment.

  1. 10; Eighty-sixth Amendment, 2002
  2. 10; Sixty-first Amendment
  3. 12; Forty-second Amendment
  4. 11; Forty-fourth Amendment
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. 10; Eighty-sixth Amendment, 2002

Art. 51A(k): duty of parent/guardian to provide education to child aged 6-14.

Report this question

17The term of Lok Sabha was extended from five to six years by the ____ Amendment and restored to five years by the ____ Amendment.

  1. 39th; 44th
  2. 38th; 42nd
  3. 42nd; 52nd
  4. 42nd; 44th
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. 42nd; 44th

The extension was to remain valid during Emergency; 44th also restored Art. 83(2) and 172(1).

Report this question

18Which Amendment deleted Art. 19(1)(f) and Art. 31 and made property a legal right under Art. 300A?

  1. Forty-fourth Amendment, 1978
  2. Forty-second Amendment, 1976
  3. Fourth Amendment, 1955
  4. Twenty-fifth Amendment, 1971
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Forty-fourth Amendment, 1978

Property ceased to be a Fundamental Right; it is now a constitutional right and a human right (Jilubhai Nanbhai Khachar v. State of Gujarat, 1995).

Report this question

19The Forty-fourth Amendment replaced 'internal disturbance' in Art. 352 with:

  1. 'Breakdown of constitutional machinery'
  2. 'Armed rebellion'
  3. 'Public emergency'
  4. 'External aggression'
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. 'Armed rebellion'

Emergency must now be approved by Parliament within one month by special majority, and Arts. 20 and 21 cannot be suspended.

Report this question

20Under the Forty-fourth Amendment, which Fundamental Rights cannot be suspended during Emergency (Art. 359)?

  1. Articles 20 and 21
  2. Articles 14 and 19
  3. Articles 25 and 26
  4. Articles 32 and 226
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Articles 20 and 21

Overcame ADM Jabalpur v. Shivkant Shukla (1976) which had held Art. 21 could be suspended.

Report this question

21ADM Jabalpur v. Shivkant Shukla (1976) was effectively superseded by which Amendment?

  1. Forty-fourth Amendment
  2. Thirty-ninth Amendment
  3. Forty-second Amendment
  4. Fifty-second Amendment
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Forty-fourth Amendment

Habeas Corpus case; the 44th Amendment ensured that Arts. 20-21 remain enforceable during Emergency.

Report this question

22The Forty-third Amendment (1977) is notable for:

  1. Introducing Part IXA
  2. Introducing the GST
  3. Introducing Art. 21A
  4. Repealing Arts. 31D and 32A inserted by the 42nd Amendment, restoring judicial review powers
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Repealing Arts. 31D and 32A inserted by the 42nd Amendment, restoring judicial review powers

It restored the jurisdiction of the Supreme Court and High Courts curtailed by the 42nd.

Report this question

23Assertion (A): The 44th Amendment removed the Right to Property from Part III.
Reason (R): It was inserted as a legal right under Art. 300A in Part XII.

  1. Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A
  2. Both A and R are true but R is not the correct explanation of A
  3. A is true but R is false
  4. A is false but R is true
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A

Property is now a constitutional right and not a Fundamental Right.

Report this question

24Assertion (A): The 42nd Amendment is often referred to as the 'Mini Constitution'.
Reason (R): It made wide-ranging changes to the Preamble, Directive Principles, Fundamental Duties and judiciary.

  1. Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A
  2. Both A and R are true but R is not the correct explanation of A
  3. A is true but R is false
  4. A is false but R is true
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A

The 44th Amendment undid many of its changes.

Report this question

25I. Art. 31C was inserted by the 25th Amendment and extended by the 42nd Amendment to all Directive Principles.
II. The extended Art. 31C was struck down in Minerva Mills (1980).

  1. Both I and II are correct
  2. Only I is correct
  3. Only II is correct
  4. Neither I nor II is correct
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Both I and II are correct

Both true.
