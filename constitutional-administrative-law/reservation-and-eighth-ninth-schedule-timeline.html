About this question set

This set has 14 multiple-choice questions on Reservation & Eighth/Ninth Schedule Timeline from the UGC NET Law (058) syllabus, unit 2, Constitutional and Administrative Law. Try each question first, then tap Show answer to check the key and read the explanation. 14 of the 14 questions carry a written explanation.

For the concepts and case law behind these questions, read the Constitutional & Administrative Law notes. Once you are comfortable, test yourself under exam conditions with a full-length mock test.

14 practice questions with answers

1Which Amendment first extended reservation for SC/ST in legislatures (originally to 1960) up to 1970?

  1. Twenty-third Amendment, 1969
  2. Sixty-second Amendment, 1989
  3. Forty-fifth Amendment, 1980
  4. Eighth Amendment, 1960
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Eighth Amendment, 1960

23rd → 1980; 45th → 1990; 62nd → 2000; 79th → 2010; 95th → 2020; 104th → 2030.

Report this question

2The Seventy-ninth Amendment (1999) extended reservation for SC/ST in legislatures up to:

  1. 2020
  2. 2030
  3. 2010
  4. 2000
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. 2010

Subsequent extensions: 95th (2020) and 104th (2030).

Report this question

3Which Amendment inserted Art. 16(4B) (carry-forward of unfilled vacancies for SC/ST)?

  1. Eighty-second Amendment
  2. Seventy-seventh Amendment
  3. Eighty-fifth Amendment
  4. Eighty-first Amendment, 2000
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Eighty-first Amendment, 2000

It treats carried-forward vacancies as a separate class, outside the 50% cap.

Report this question

4Which Amendment provides that nothing in Art. 335 prevents relaxation in qualifying marks for reservation in promotion for SC/ST?

  1. Eighty-first Amendment
  2. Seventy-seventh Amendment
  3. Eighty-fifth Amendment
  4. Eighty-second Amendment, 2000
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Eighty-second Amendment, 2000

Proviso added to Art. 335.

Report this question

5The Eighth Schedule originally had 14 languages. Which Amendment added Sindhi?

  1. Seventy-first Amendment, 1992
  2. Twenty-first Amendment, 1967
  3. Fifty-third Amendment, 1986
  4. Ninety-second Amendment, 2003
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Twenty-first Amendment, 1967

Languages currently: 22.

6Which Amendment inserted Art. 371F for Sikkim?

  1. Thirty-sixth Amendment, 1975
  2. Fifty-third Amendment, 1986
  3. Thirty-fifth Amendment, 1974
  4. Thirty-eighth Amendment, 1975
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Thirty-sixth Amendment, 1975

Sikkim became the 22nd State of India.

7Article 371I (Goa) was inserted by the:

  1. Fifty-sixth Amendment, 1987
  2. Fifty-eighth Amendment, 1987
  3. Fifty-third Amendment, 1986
  4. Fifty-fifth Amendment, 1986
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Fifty-sixth Amendment, 1987

Goa became the 25th State in 1987.

Report this question

8Which Amendment inserted Art. 21A in the Constitution?

  1. Ninety-third Amendment
  2. Eighty-sixth Amendment, 2002
  3. Ninety-seventh Amendment
  4. Forty-fourth Amendment
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Eighty-sixth Amendment, 2002

Also substituted Art. 45 and inserted Art. 51A(k).

Report this question

9Which of these cases led to the First Amendment's insertion of Art. 31B and the Ninth Schedule?

  1. Sajjan Singh
  2. Golak Nath
  3. Kameshwar Singh v. State of Bihar (Patna HC, 1950)
  4. Kesavananda
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Kameshwar Singh v. State of Bihar (Patna HC, 1950)

The Patna HC struck down the Bihar Land Reforms Act, 1950 as violating Art. 14; the 1st Amendment protected zamindari abolition laws.

Report this question

10Which Amendment first altered Art. 3 by adding an Explanation that 'State' includes Union Territory in clause (a) of Art. 3?

  1. Seventh Amendment
  2. Eighteenth Amendment, 1966
  3. Fifth Amendment
  4. Ninth Amendment
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Eighteenth Amendment, 1966

Facilitated reorganisation of Punjab and creation of Haryana.

Report this question

11Which Amendment inserted Art. 239AA, 239AB for Delhi?

  1. Fourteenth Amendment
  2. Sixty-ninth Amendment
  3. Seventy-first Amendment
  4. Seventh Amendment
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Sixty-ninth Amendment

Delhi has a Legislative Assembly and Council of Ministers headed by a Chief Minister.

Report this question

12Which Amendment gave constitutional status to NCBC and inserted Art. 342A?

  1. One Hundred and Second
  2. Ninety-ninth
  3. One Hundred and Third
  4. One Hundred and Fifth
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. One Hundred and Second

105th restored State power in Art. 342A(3).

Report this question

13The Ninth Schedule's purpose is to:

  1. Provide anti-defection law
  2. List subjects of the Concurrent List
  3. List 22 languages
  4. Protect certain laws from judicial review on grounds of violation of Fundamental Rights
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Protect certain laws from judicial review on grounds of violation of Fundamental Rights

Art. 31B; subject to I.R. Coelho (2007).

14Which Article was inserted by the 99th Amendment as 'Judicial Appointments Commission'?

  1. Article 217
  2. Article 124A
  3. Article 233
  4. Article 124
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Article 124A

Arts. 124A-124C.
