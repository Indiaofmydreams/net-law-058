22 practice questions with answers

1Which Article provides for Inter-State Council for coordination between States and the Union?

  1. Article 267
  2. Article 265
  3. Article 261
  4. Article 263
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Article 263

Article 263 empowers the President to establish an Inter-State Council if it appears to him that the public interests would be served by the establishment of such a council.

2The 'Doctrine of Occupied Field' in legislative relations means:

  1. Only Union can occupy Concurrent List
  2. When Parliament intends to cover the entire field on a Concurrent List subject, there is no scope for State legislation
  3. States cannot legislate at all on Concurrent subjects
  4. When Parliament legislates on a Concurrent List subject, the State law is automatically repealed
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. When Parliament intends to cover the entire field on a Concurrent List subject, there is no scope for State legislation

The Doctrine of Occupied Field operates under Article 254. When Parliament's law is intended to be exhaustive and covers the entire field of a Concurrent List subject, a State law on the same subject is repugnant to the extent it encroaches on the occupied field.

3Under Article 262, Parliament may by law provide that neither the Supreme Court nor any other court shall exercise jurisdiction in respect of:

  1. Maritime boundary disputes
  2. Inter-State water disputes
  3. Interstate electricity disputes
  4. River water pollution disputes
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Inter-State water disputes

Article 262 enables Parliament to provide by law for the adjudication of disputes relating to the waters of inter-State rivers and river valleys, and to exclude the jurisdiction of the Supreme Court. The Inter-State Water Disputes Act, 1956 creates specific tribunals for this purpose.

Report this question

4The Sarkaria Commission (1988) on Centre-State relations recommended:

  1. Abolition of Inter-State Council
  2. The Governor's role should be increased
  3. Finance Commission recommendations should be made binding
  4. Inter-State Council should be revived and made a permanent body for Union-State coordination
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Inter-State Council should be revived and made a permanent body for Union-State coordination

The Sarkaria Commission (1988) made comprehensive recommendations on Centre-State relations including: revival of Inter-State Council; restraint in use of Article 356; appointment of persons with standing in State as Governor; Governor to follow conventions. The Punchhi Commission (2010) also made similar recommendations.

Report this question

5Under Article 365, if any State fails to comply with Union directions, the President may treat it as:

  1. Governor's Rule
  2. Financial Emergency
  3. A breakdown of constitutional machinery — triggering Article 356
  4. Suspension of State Legislature
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. A breakdown of constitutional machinery — triggering Article 356

Article 365 provides that where any State has failed to comply with or give effect to any direction given in the exercise of the executive power of the Union, it shall be lawful for the President to hold that a situation has arisen in which the government of the State cannot be carried on in accordance with the Constitution — thus enabling Art. 356.

Report this question

6The Finance Commission is constituted under which Article?

  1. Article 279
  2. Article 280
  3. Article 282
  4. Article 281
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Article 280

Article 280 provides that the President shall, within two years from the commencement of the Constitution and thereafter at the expiration of every fifth year or at such earlier time as the President considers necessary, constitute a Finance Commission to recommend distribution of tax revenues.

Report this question

7Under Article 256, the executive power of every State shall be so exercised as to ensure:

  1. Compliance with Union laws made by Parliament
  2. Compliance with Supreme Court orders
  3. Compliance with Finance Commission recommendations
  4. Compliance with Governor's instructions
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Compliance with Union laws made by Parliament

Article 256 imposes a mandatory obligation on States to exercise their executive power so as to ensure compliance with the laws made by Parliament and any existing laws that apply in the State. The Union may give necessary directions to the State for this purpose.

Report this question

8Under Article 344, the President shall constitute an Official Languages Commission within how many years of commencement of Constitution?

  1. 5 years of commencement
  2. 10 years of commencement
  3. 15 years
  4. On Parliament's direction only
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. 5 years of commencement

Article 344 requires the President to constitute a Commission at the expiration of 5 years from commencement of the Constitution, and again at the expiration of 10 years, to report on the progressive use of Hindi as official language and restrictions on the use of English.

Report this question

9The Punchhi Commission (2010) was set up to review:

  1. Centre-State financial relations only
  2. Centre-State relations broadly — constitutional federalism, Governor's role, Article 356, inter-State water disputes
  3. Judicial appointments
  4. Electoral reforms
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Centre-State relations broadly — constitutional federalism, Governor's role, Article 356, inter-State water disputes

The Punchhi Commission (headed by former CJI Madan Mohan Punchhi, 2010) was constituted to review the working of the constitutional scheme of Centre-State relations. It recommended: restraint in use of Art. 356; Governors to be constitutional heads; delimitation of Governor's discretionary powers; strengthening Inter-State Council.

10Match the Article with the circumstance in which Parliament may legislate on a State List subject.
List I:
1. Art. 249
2. Art. 250
3. Art. 252
4. Art. 253
List II:
a. Rajya Sabha resolution (2/3 present and voting) in national interest
b. Proclamation of Emergency in operation
c. Resolution of legislatures of two or more States
d. Implementation of treaties and international agreements

  1. 1-b, 2-a, 3-c, 4-d
  2. 1-a, 2-b, 3-c, 4-d
  3. 1-a, 2-b, 3-d, 4-c
  4. 1-c, 2-b, 3-a, 4-d
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. 1-a, 2-b, 3-c, 4-d

A Rajya Sabha resolution under Art. 249 lasts one year and is renewable.

11In Union of India v. H.S. Dhillon (1971), the Supreme Court held that:

  1. Parliament's residuary power under Art. 248 and Entry 97 of List I extends to taxation of matters not mentioned in List II or III
  2. Parliament has no residuary power of taxation
  3. Taxes are always concurrent
  4. Entry 97 is limited to non-fiscal laws
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Parliament's residuary power under Art. 248 and Entry 97 of List I extends to taxation of matters not mentioned in List II or III

The wealth tax on agricultural land value was upheld as a matter outside List II.

Report this question

12Under Art. 365, the President may hold that the constitutional machinery in a State has failed where the State:

  1. Fails to implement central schemes
  2. Fails to comply with or give effect to directions given by the Union in exercise of its executive power
  3. Delays assent to Bills
  4. Violates fiscal discipline
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Fails to comply with or give effect to directions given by the Union in exercise of its executive power

Art. 365 operates alongside Art. 356.

Report this question

13Which of the following owes its constitutional basis to a specific Article (Art. 263)?

  1. Zonal Councils
  2. Inter-State Council
  3. NITI Aayog
  4. National Development Council
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Inter-State Council

Zonal Councils were created by the States Reorganisation Act, 1956; the Inter-State Council was set up in 1990 under Art. 263.

Report this question

14Under Art. 280, the President constitutes a Finance Commission at the expiration of every fifth year or earlier, consisting of:

  1. A Chairman and two other members
  2. A Chairman and four other members
  3. A Chairman and six other members
  4. The Finance Minister and four members
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. A Chairman and four other members

The qualifications of members are prescribed by Parliament.

15In Union of India v. Mohit Minerals Pvt. Ltd. (2022), the Supreme Court held that GST Council recommendations are:

  1. Binding on Union and States
  2. Binding on the Union only
  3. Only persuasive, as Parliament and State legislatures have simultaneous power under Art. 246A
  4. Mandatory for State legislatures
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Only persuasive, as Parliament and State legislatures have simultaneous power under Art. 246A

The Court emphasised cooperative federalism.

Report this question

16Under Art. 289(2), the Union may tax the trade or business of a State only if:

  1. The State consents
  2. The Finance Commission recommends
  3. The President so directs
  4. Parliament by law so provides
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Parliament by law so provides

Art. 289(1) otherwise exempts State property and income from Union taxation.

17In Vidya Devi v. State of Himachal Pradesh (2020), the Supreme Court held that:

  1. Right to property has no constitutional basis after the 44th Amendment
  2. The State can acquire property without law
  3. The right to property is a constitutional and human right under Art. 300A, and the State cannot deprive a person of property without authority of law or claim title by adverse possession
  4. Art. 300A requires full market compensation
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. The right to property is a constitutional and human right under Art. 300A, and the State cannot deprive a person of property without authority of law or claim title by adverse possession

A welfare State cannot invoke adverse possession against a citizen.

18Automobile Transport (Rajasthan) Ltd. v. State of Rajasthan (1962) is associated with:

  1. Prohibition on all taxes under Art. 301
  2. Cession of territory
  3. Direct prohibition of State monopolies
  4. The concept of compensatory taxes not hampering freedom of trade under Art. 301
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. The concept of compensatory taxes not hampering freedom of trade under Art. 301

Taxes charged for use of facilities are not restrictions on trade.

19Under Art. 304(b), a Bill imposing reasonable restrictions on trade in a State in the public interest:

  1. Requires the Governor's recommendation
  2. Can be introduced only with the previous sanction of the President
  3. Needs Rajya Sabha approval
  4. Can be introduced freely
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Can be introduced only with the previous sanction of the President

The proviso to Art. 304(b).

20In GVK Industries Ltd. v. ITO (2011), the Supreme Court held that Parliament's power to make extra-territorial laws under Art. 245(2):

  1. Exists but requires a nexus with India (territorial nexus)
  2. Is unlimited
  3. Is limited to treaties
  4. Is available only to States
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Exists but requires a nexus with India (territorial nexus)

State legislatures do not have extra-territorial power but may legislate with nexus.

Report this question

21Under Art. 246A(2), the power to make laws on GST where supply of goods or services occurs in the course of inter-State trade or commerce lies with:

  1. The State legislatures exclusively
  2. Both concurrently
  3. Parliament exclusively
  4. The GST Council
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Parliament exclusively

Art. 246A(1) gives simultaneous power otherwise.

Report this question

22Under Art. 271, a surcharge levied on taxes referred to in Arts. 269 and 270:

  1. Is shared with the States
  2. Is wholly retained by the Union
  3. Is assigned to Panchayats
  4. Is referred to the Finance Commission
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Is wholly retained by the Union

Cesses and surcharges are not part of the divisible pool.
