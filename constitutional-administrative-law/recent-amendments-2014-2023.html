About this question set

This set has 15 multiple-choice questions on Recent Amendments (2014-2023) from the UGC NET Law (058) syllabus, unit 2, Constitutional and Administrative Law. Try each question first, then tap Show answer to check the key and read the explanation. 15 of the 15 questions carry a written explanation.

For the concepts and case law behind these questions, read the Constitutional & Administrative Law notes. Once you are comfortable, test yourself under exam conditions with a full-length mock test.

15 practice questions with answers

1The National Judicial Appointments Commission (NJAC) was created by the:

  1. Ninety-seventh Amendment
  2. One Hundred and Second Amendment
  3. Sixty-ninth Amendment
  4. Ninety-ninth Amendment, 2014
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Ninety-ninth Amendment, 2014

Inserted Arts. 124A, 124B, 124C. Struck down in Supreme Court Advocates-on-Record Association v. Union of India (2015) by 4:1.

Report this question

2Which case is known as the 'Fourth Judges Case'?

  1. Supreme Court Advocates-on-Record Association (1993)
  2. Supreme Court Advocates-on-Record Association v. Union of India (2015)
  3. In re Special Reference No. 1 of 1998
  4. S.P. Gupta v. Union of India (1981)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Supreme Court Advocates-on-Record Association v. Union of India (2015)

First Judges Case: S.P. Gupta (1981); Second: SCAORA (1993); Third: Special Reference (1998).

Report this question

3The Hundredth Amendment (2015) is related to:

  1. Women reservation
  2. NJAC
  3. Land Boundary Agreement between India and Bangladesh — exchange of enclaves
  4. Goods and Services Tax
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Land Boundary Agreement between India and Bangladesh — exchange of enclaves

Altered the First Schedule (territories of Assam, West Bengal, Tripura, Meghalaya).

Report this question

4The GST was introduced by the ____ Amendment, which inserted Arts. 246A, 269A and 279A.

  1. One Hundredth Amendment
  2. One Hundred and First Amendment, 2016
  3. Ninety-ninth Amendment
  4. One Hundred and Second Amendment
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. One Hundred and First Amendment, 2016

GST came into effect on 1 July 2017; the GST Council is under Art. 279A.

Report this question

5The National Commission for Backward Classes (NCBC) was given constitutional status by the:

  1. One Hundred and Second Amendment, 2018
  2. Ninety-third Amendment
  3. One Hundred and Third Amendment
  4. One Hundred and Fifth Amendment
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. One Hundred and Second Amendment, 2018

Inserted Arts. 338B and 342A.

Report this question

6The One Hundred and Fifth Amendment (2021) clarified that:

  1. NCBC has no powers
  2. 50% ceiling was abolished
  3. States and UTs may prepare their own lists of Socially and Educationally Backward Classes (Art. 342A(3))
  4. Only Parliament can identify SEBCs
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. States and UTs may prepare their own lists of Socially and Educationally Backward Classes (Art. 342A(3))

Overrode the effect of Jaishri Laxmanrao Patil v. Chief Minister of Maharashtra (2021) which struck down the Maratha reservation.

Report this question

7Dr. Jaishri Laxmanrao Patil v. Chief Minister (2021) is also known as the:

  1. Maratha Reservation Case
  2. Mandal Case
  3. Mysore Reservation Case
  4. EWS Case
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Maratha Reservation Case

Held that after the 102nd Amendment only the President/Parliament can identify SEBCs, and that the 50% ceiling from Indra Sawhney remained intact.

Report this question

8Ten percent reservation for Economically Weaker Sections was introduced by the:

  1. One Hundred and Fifth Amendment
  2. One Hundred and Third Amendment, 2019
  3. One Hundred and Second Amendment
  4. One Hundred and Fourth Amendment
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. One Hundred and Third Amendment, 2019

Inserted Arts. 15(6) and 16(6).

Report this question

9In Janhit Abhiyan v. Union of India (2022), the Supreme Court upheld the 103rd Amendment by a majority of:

  1. 5:0
  2. 4:3
  3. 4:1
  4. 3:2
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. 3:2

CJI U.U. Lalit and Justice Ravindra Bhat dissented.

Report this question

10Which Amendment discontinued nomination of Anglo-Indian members to the Lok Sabha and State Assemblies?

  1. One Hundred and Third Amendment
  2. Ninety-fifth Amendment
  3. One Hundred and Sixth Amendment
  4. One Hundred and Fourth Amendment, 2019
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. One Hundred and Fourth Amendment, 2019

Omitted Arts. 331 and 333 and extended SC/ST seat reservation to 2030.

Report this question

11The Nari Shakti Vandan Adhiniyam (2023) is the ____ Amendment Act.

  1. One Hundred and Fourth
  2. One Hundred and Sixth Amendment
  3. One Hundred and Seventh
  4. One Hundred and Fifth
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. One Hundred and Sixth Amendment

Provides one-third reservation for women in the Lok Sabha, State Assemblies and Delhi Assembly (Arts. 330A, 332A, 239AA); effective after delimitation following the first census after commencement.

Report this question

12The Constitution (106th Amendment) Act, 2023 reserves one-third of seats for women, valid for:

  1. 25 years
  2. 20 years
  3. 10 years
  4. 15 years from commencement (extendable by Parliament)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. 15 years from commencement (extendable by Parliament)

Includes sub-reservation for SC/ST women.

Report this question

13Assertion (A): The 99th Amendment creating the NJAC was declared unconstitutional.
Reason (R): It compromised the independence of the judiciary — a basic feature.

  1. Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A
  2. Both A and R are true but R is not the correct explanation of A
  3. A is true but R is false
  4. A is false but R is true
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A

Collegium system revived in the Fourth Judges Case.

Report this question

14Assertion (A): The 103rd Amendment, providing EWS reservation, was upheld by the Supreme Court.
Reason (R): The 50% ceiling is part of the basic structure and cannot be exceeded under any circumstances.

  1. Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A
  2. Both A and R are true but R is not the correct explanation of A
  3. A is true but R is false
  4. A is false but R is true
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. A is true but R is false

The majority held the 50% ceiling is not inviolable and EWS reservation does not violate basic structure; hence R is false.

Report this question

15I. The 101st Amendment introduced Art. 279A (GST Council).
II. Art. 268A (service tax) was omitted by the 101st Amendment.

  1. Both I and II are correct
  2. Only I is correct
  3. Only II is correct
  4. Neither I nor II is correct
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Both I and II are correct

Both true.
