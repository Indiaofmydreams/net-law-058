About this question set

This set has 25 multiple-choice questions on Additional Concept Questions: Logical Reasoning from the UGC NET Paper 1 syllabus, unit 6, Logical Reasoning. 1The argument form "If P then Q; P; therefore Q" is called:

  1. Modus tollens
  2. Reductio ad absurdum
  3. Modus ponens
  4. Disjunctive syllogism
Show answer

Answer: C. Modus ponens

Report this question

2The argument form "If P then Q; not Q; therefore not P" is called:

  1. Constructive dilemma
  2. Modus ponens
  3. Hypothetical syllogism
  4. Modus tollens
Show answer

Answer: D. Modus tollens

Report this question

3"If P then Q; Q; therefore P" commits the fallacy of:

  1. Undistributed middle
  2. Affirming the consequent
  3. Illicit major
  4. Denying the antecedent
Show answer

Answer: B. Affirming the consequent

4"If P then Q; not P; therefore not Q" commits the fallacy of:

5"If P then Q; if Q then R; therefore if P then R" is a:

  4. Disjunctive syllogism

6"Either P or Q; not P; therefore Q" is a:

  1. Sorites

  4. Disjunctive syllogism

7In a dilemma, the minor premise is:

  1. A conjunctive proposition

  4. A singular proposition

8Which is the form of a constructive dilemma?

  1. (P→Q) and (R→S); P or R; therefore Q or S
  2. P or Q; not P; therefore Q
  3. If P then Q; P; therefore Q
  4. (P→Q) and (R→S); not Q or not S; therefore not P or not R
Show answer

Answer: A. (P→Q) and (R→S); P or R; therefore Q or S

Report this question

9"Escaping between the horns" of a dilemma means:

  1. Denying one of the conditional premises
  2. Constructing a counter-dilemma
  3. Rejecting the disjunctive premise by showing other alternatives exist
  4. Accepting both conclusions
Show answer

Answer: C. Rejecting the disjunctive premise by showing other alternatives exist
