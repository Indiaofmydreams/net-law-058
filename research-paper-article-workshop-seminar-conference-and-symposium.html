About this question set

This set has 25 multiple-choice questions on Research Paper, Article, Workshop, Seminar, Conference and Symposium from the UGC NET Paper 1 syllabus, unit 2, Research Aptitude. Try each question first, then tap Show answer to check the key. This set is an answer-key drill: check each answer, then revisit the notes for any you miss.

For the concepts and case law behind these questions, read the Research Aptitude notes. Once you are comfortable, test yourself under exam conditions with a full-length mock test.

25 practice questions with answers

1A seminar is best described as:

  1. A hands-on skill training session only
  2. A solo reading exercise
  3. A group meeting where participants present papers on a specific topic and discuss them under the guidance of a chair or expert
  4. A newspaper interview
Show answer

Answer: C. A group meeting where participants present papers on a specific topic and discuss them under the guidance of a chair or expert

Report this question

2A workshop is mainly:

  1. A passive lecture only
  2. A practical, hands-on session aimed at developing particular skills
  3. A published journal
  4. A funding agency
Show answer

Answer: B. A practical, hands-on session aimed at developing particular skills

Report this question

3A symposium is a meeting in which:

  1. Several experts present views on different aspects of one theme
  2. A single speaker reads a thesis
  3. Participants only practise skills
  4. Only posters are displayed
Show answer

Answer: A. Several experts present views on different aspects of one theme

Report this question

4Which of the following is the most practice-oriented?

  1. Keynote address
  2. Plenary lecture
  3. Workshop
  4. Symposium
Show answer

Answer: C. Workshop

Report this question

5A colloquium is generally:

  1. A funding scheme
  2. An academic meeting for discussion among scholars, usually less formal than a conference
  3. A statistical method
  4. A citation style
Show answer

Answer: B. An academic meeting for discussion among scholars, usually less formal than a conference

Report this question

6Match the following:
List I: (i) Workshop, (ii) Seminar, (iii) Symposium, (iv) Conference
List II: (P) several experts on different aspects of one theme, (Q) large-scale meeting with many sessions, (R) hands-on skill development, (S) paper presentation and discussion on a topic

  1. i-S, ii-R, iii-Q, iv-P
  2. i-P, ii-Q, iii-R, iv-S
  3. i-Q, ii-P, iii-S, iv-R
  4. i-R, ii-S, iii-P, iv-Q
Show answer

Answer: D. i-R, ii-S, iii-P, iv-Q

Report this question

7Assertion (A): Workshops are preferred for developing research skills such as data analysis.
Reason (R): Participants practise the skills under expert guidance.

  1. Both A and R are true, and R explains A
  2. Both are true, but R does not explain A
  3. A is true, R is false
  4. A is false, R is true
Show answer

Answer: A. Both A and R are true, and R explains A

Report this question

8Consider the following statements:
I. A seminar involves paper presentation and discussion.
II. A workshop emphasises practical, participatory learning.
III. A conference is always attended by only one participant.

  1. II and III only
  2. I and III only
  3. I and II only
  4. I, II and III
Show answer

Answer: C. I and II only

Report this question

9A conference is typically:

  1. A meeting of two persons
  2. A private tutorial
  3. A large gathering of researchers for presenting and discussing papers in multiple sessions
  4. An online quiz
Show answer

Answer: C. A large gathering of researchers for presenting and discussing papers in multiple sessions

Report this question

10A keynote address is:

  1. The closing vote of thanks
  2. A questionnaire
  3. A main speech by an eminent speaker that sets the theme or tone of the meeting
  4. A poster
Show answer

Answer: C. A main speech by an eminent speaker that sets the theme or tone of the meeting

Report this question

11A plenary session is one that:

  1. Only a few participants may attend
  2. All participants attend together
  3. Is held in secret
  4. Has no speakers
Show answer

Answer: B. All participants attend together

Report this question

12Technical (parallel) sessions in a conference are:

  1. Held only once at the end
  2. Several sessions held at the same time in different halls on different themes
  3. Held without papers
  4. Held only for organisers
Show answer

Answer: B. Several sessions held at the same time in different halls on different themes

13Conference proceedings are:

  1. The tea-time schedule
  2. The list of organisers
  3. A published collection of the papers or abstracts presented at the conference
  4. The registration receipts
Show answer

14A "call for papers" is:

  1. An invitation to submit abstracts or papers for a conference or journal
  2. A funding grant
  3. A certificate
  4. A thesis defence

15A poster presentation:

  1. Is a long spoken lecture
  2. Displays a visual summary of the research on a board for viewing and discussion
  3. Is a written thesis
  4. Is a funding proposal

16A conference abstract is:

  1. The final thesis
  2. A reference list
  3. A short summary of the proposed paper, submitted for acceptance by the organisers
  4. A certificate

17A session chair:

  1. Moderates the session and manages the time of the presenters
  2. Only prints certificates
  3. Only registers participants
  4. Pays the fees
Show answer

18A discussant in a conference session:

  1. Registers the participants
  2. Gives critical comments on a paper presented
  3. Prints the proceedings
  4. Funds the meeting

19A rapporteur is the person who:

  1. Funds the conference
  2. Chairs the inaugural session
  3. Records and summarises the proceedings of a session
  4. Designs the posters
Show answer

20A panel discussion is:

  1. A written examination
  2. A laboratory experiment
  3. A thesis viva
  4. A discussion among a group of experts on a topic, before an audience, usually led by a moderator
Show answer

Answer: D. A discussion among a group of experts on a topic, before an audience, usually led by a moderator

Report this question

21A webinar is:

  1. A seminar conducted over the internet
  2. A printed journal
  3. A workshop held only in a library
  4. A survey
Show answer

Answer: A. A seminar conducted over the internet

22A research paper differs from a general opinion article in that it:

  1. Contains only opinions
  2. Needs no references
  3. Contains no data
  4. Reports original research, with a stated method and evidence-based findings
Show answer

Answer: D. Reports original research, with a stated method and evidence-based findings

23Papers presented at a conference are often later published in:

  1. A telephone directory
  2. A novel
  3. The conference proceedings or an edited volume
  4. A dictionary

24A monograph is:

  1. A newspaper column
  2. A detailed scholarly work on a single subject or a narrow topic
  3. A conference schedule
  4. A short abstract

Answer: B. A detailed scholarly work on a single subject or a narrow topic

25A book review:

  1. Reproduces the whole book
  2. Is a questionnaire
