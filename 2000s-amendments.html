About this question set

This set has 20 multiple-choice questions on 2000s Amendments from the UGC NET Law (058) syllabus, unit 2, Constitutional and Administrative Law. Try each question first, then tap Show answer to check the key and read the explanation. 20 of the 20 questions carry a written explanation.

For the concepts and case law behind these questions, read the Constitutional & Administrative Law notes. Once you are comfortable, test yourself under exam conditions with a full-length mock test.

20 practice questions with answers

1The Eighty-fourth Amendment (2001) froze delimitation of constituencies until:

  1. 2011
  2. 2031
  3. The first census taken after the year 2026
  4. 2001
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. The first census taken after the year 2026

Based on 1991 census originally; the 87th Amendment (2003) changed this to 2001 census for readjustment.

Report this question

2Which Amendment shifted the base of delimitation from 1991 census to 2001 census?

  1. Eighty-fifth Amendment, 2001
  2. Ninety-first Amendment, 2003
  3. Eighty-seventh Amendment, 2003
  4. Eighty-fourth Amendment, 2001
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Eighty-seventh Amendment, 2003

Delimitation was done under the Delimitation Act, 2002.

Report this question

3The Eighty-fifth Amendment (2001) provided for:

  1. Carry-forward of unfilled vacancies
  2. Consequential seniority in promotion for SC/ST government servants (Art. 16(4A))
  3. Creamy layer exclusion
  4. Relaxation of qualifying marks
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Consequential seniority in promotion for SC/ST government servants (Art. 16(4A))

The 81st (2000) inserted Art. 16(4B) for carry-forward vacancies; the 82nd (2000) added the proviso to Art. 335.

Report this question

4M. Nagaraj v. Union of India (2006) upheld the validity of the 77th, 81st, 82nd and 85th Amendments subject to which conditions?

  1. Prior approval of the President
  2. Mandatory creamy layer for SCs only
  3. Only 50% ceiling
  4. Quantifiable data on backwardness and inadequacy of representation, and maintaining administrative efficiency
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Quantifiable data on backwardness and inadequacy of representation, and maintaining administrative efficiency

Jarnail Singh v. Lachhmi Narain Gupta (2018) clarified that quantifiable data on backwardness is not needed for SC/ST but creamy layer applies.

Report this question

5Right to Education (Art. 21A) was inserted by the:

  1. Seventy-third Amendment, 1992
  2. Ninety-third Amendment, 2005
  3. Eighty-sixth Amendment, 2002
  4. Forty-second Amendment, 1976
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Eighty-sixth Amendment, 2002

Free and compulsory education for children aged 6-14; Right to Education Act enacted in 2009 and enforced on 1 April 2010.

Report this question

6Which case recognised education as a Fundamental Right up to the age of 14 years before the 86th Amendment?

  1. Unni Krishnan J.P. v. State of A.P. (1993)
  2. Mohini Jain v. State of Karnataka (1992)
  3. T.M.A. Pai Foundation (2002)
  4. Pramati Educational Trust (2014)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Unni Krishnan J.P. v. State of A.P. (1993)

Mohini Jain (1992) first recognised right to education under Art. 21; Unni Krishnan limited it to age 14.

Report this question

7The Eighty-ninth Amendment (2003) provided for:

  1. Creation of National Commission for BCs
  2. GST
  3. Division of the National Commission for SC/ST into separate commissions (Arts. 338 and 338A)
  4. Anti-defection
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Division of the National Commission for SC/ST into separate commissions (Arts. 338 and 338A)

Separate National Commission for Scheduled Tribes under Art. 338A.

Report this question

8Council of Ministers cannot exceed ____ of the total strength of the Lok Sabha/Legislative Assembly under the 91st Amendment.

  1. 12%
  2. 10%
  3. 20%
  4. 15%
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. 15%

In small States, minimum 12 Ministers; Art. 164(1A).

Report this question

9Service tax was brought under Art. 268A by the:

  1. Ninety-seventh Amendment, 2011
  2. Eighty-eighth Amendment, 2003
  3. One Hundred and First Amendment, 2016
  4. Eightieth Amendment, 2000
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Eighty-eighth Amendment, 2003

Art. 268A was omitted by the 101st Amendment.

Report this question

10Eightieth Amendment (2000) implemented recommendations of which Finance Commission regarding tax devolution?

  1. Twelfth Finance Commission
  2. Tenth Finance Commission — alternative scheme of devolution under Art. 270
  3. Eleventh Finance Commission
  4. Fourteenth Finance Commission
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Tenth Finance Commission — alternative scheme of devolution under Art. 270

Altered Arts. 268-272; share of Union taxes to States.

Report this question

11Ninety-fourth Amendment (2006) removed the requirement of a tribal welfare Minister in which State?

  1. Bihar (Art. 164(1) proviso)
  2. Gujarat
  3. Maharashtra
  4. Kerala
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Bihar (Art. 164(1) proviso)

Retained for Madhya Pradesh, Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand and Odisha.

Report this question

12Ninety-third Amendment (2005) inserted which Clause?

  1. Art. 15(6)
  2. Art. 21A
  3. Art. 16(6)
  4. Art. 15(5) — reservation in educational institutions including private unaided institutions (except minority institutions)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Art. 15(5) — reservation in educational institutions including private unaided institutions (except minority institutions)

It followed the T.M.A. Pai Foundation and Inamdar rulings on private unaided institutions.

Report this question

13Ashoka Kumar Thakur v. Union of India (2008) upheld:

  1. 103rd Amendment
  2. Central Educational Institutions (Reservation in Admission) Act, 2006 and the 93rd Amendment, excluding the creamy layer among OBCs
  3. 99th Amendment
  4. Mandal Commission Report in toto
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Central Educational Institutions (Reservation in Admission) Act, 2006 and the 93rd Amendment, excluding the creamy layer among OBCs

Constitution Bench decision by 5 judges (Justice Dalveer Bhandari dissenting in part).

Report this question

14Pramati Educational & Cultural Trust v. Union of India (2014) held that Art. 15(5) is constitutionally valid:

  1. Only for government institutions
  2. For all private institutions including minority
  3. Insofar as it applies to private unaided non-minority institutions; minority institutions are exempt
  4. Only for aided institutions
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Insofar as it applies to private unaided non-minority institutions; minority institutions are exempt

The Court did not apply Art. 15(5) to minority institutions protected by Art. 30(1).

Report this question

15Ninety-fifth Amendment (2009) extended reservation of seats for SC/ST in the Lok Sabha and State Assemblies up to:

  1. 2025
  2. 2010
  3. 2020
  4. 2030
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. 2020

Extended again by the 104th Amendment to 2030.

Report this question

16Ninety-sixth Amendment (2011) replaced the word 'Oriya' in the Eighth Schedule with:

  1. 'Utkali'
  2. 'Odia'
  3. 'Odiya'
  4. 'Kalinga'
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. 'Odia'

Also changed the name of the State from Orissa to Odisha by a separate Act.

Report this question

17Which Amendment (2011) inserted Part IXB on Co-operative Societies?

  1. Ninety-ninth Amendment
  2. One Hundred and First Amendment
  3. Ninety-seventh Amendment
  4. Ninety-fifth Amendment
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Ninety-seventh Amendment

Added 'co-operative societies' to Art. 19(1)(c), Art. 43B (DPSP), and Part IXB (Arts. 243ZH-243ZT).

Report this question

18Special provisions for Hyderabad-Karnataka region (now Kalyana-Karnataka) are under Art. 371J, inserted by the:

  1. Ninety-eighth Amendment, 2012
  2. One Hundred Amendment
  3. Ninety-fifth Amendment
  4. Ninety-seventh Amendment
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Ninety-eighth Amendment, 2012

Provides for a regional development board and reservation in educational institutions and jobs.

Report this question

19The Ninetieth Amendment (2003) relates to:

  1. Anti-defection
  2. Sikkim's status
  3. Representation of Bodoland Territorial Areas District (Art. 332(6))
  4. Creation of Jharkhand
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Representation of Bodoland Territorial Areas District (Art. 332(6))

Retains non-tribal representation in Assam's Bodoland Territorial Council.

Report this question

20I. The 92nd Amendment added Bodo, Dogri, Maithili and Santhali to the Eighth Schedule.
II. The 71st Amendment added Konkani, Manipuri and Nepali to the Eighth Schedule.

  1. Both I and II are correct
  2. Only I is correct
  3. Only II is correct
  4. Neither I nor II is correct
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Both I and II are correct

Both true. The Eighth Schedule currently has 22 languages.
