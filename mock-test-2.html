Sample questions from this test

Try these five before you start. They are part of the paper, so you may prefer to skip the answers until after you have attempted it.

1Corpus and animus are the two elements of:

  1. Ownership
  2. Possession
  3. Easement
  4. Trust
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Possession

Possession requires physical control (corpus) and intention to hold (animus).

2Cultural relativism holds that human rights are:

  1. Natural only
  2. Universal
  3. Absolute
  4. Culture-dependent
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Culture-dependent

It contrasts with universalism.

3Recognition of foreign matrimonial decrees was considered in:

  1. Danial Latifi
  2. Sarla Mudgal
  3. Y. Narasimha Rao v. Y. Venkata Lakshmi
  4. Shah Bano
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Y. Narasimha Rao v. Y. Venkata Lakshmi

Section 13 CPC governs.

4Lord Diplock's three grounds of judicial review are:

  1. Vires, fairness, necessity
  2. Jurisdiction, merit, equity
  3. Illegality, irrationality, procedural impropriety
  4. Bias, delay, malice
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Illegality, irrationality, procedural impropriety

Stated in the GCHQ case (CCSU v. Minister for the Civil Service).

5The MFN principle under GATT is in:

  1. Article I
  2. Article III
  3. Article XI
  4. Article XX
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Article I

Most-favoured-nation treatment. National treatment is Article III.
