About this question set

This set has 17 multiple-choice questions on SWAYAM: Comprehensive from the UGC NET Paper 1 syllabus, unit 1, Teaching Aptitude. Try each question first, then tap Show answer to check the key and read the explanation. 17 of the 17 questions carry a written explanation.

For the concepts and case law behind these questions, read the Teaching Aptitude notes. Once you are comfortable, test yourself under exam conditions with a full-length mock test.

17 practice questions with answers

1The self-assessment component of SWAYAM (Quadrant 3) primarily serves which educational purpose?

  1. Final grading
  2. Peer evaluation
  3. Formative evaluation — helping students monitor their own learning progress
  4. Teacher evaluation
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Formative evaluation — helping students monitor their own learning progress

Concept: Self-assessment quizzes = formative = immediate feedback = learner adjusts understanding. Not for final certification. Promotes metacognition.

2How does SWAYAM address the problem of quality variation across colleges in India?

  1. By ensuring every student, regardless of their college's quality, has access to the same high-quality content from top institutions
  2. By reducing the curriculum
  3. By standardising the number of classes per week
  4. By replacing all college teachers
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. By ensuring every student, regardless of their college's quality, has access to the same high-quality content from top institutions

Concept: A student in a small college in rural Chhattisgarh can now access an IIT professor's lectures. SWAYAM equalises quality access — this is its most transformative contribution.

Report this question

3SWAYAM is aligned with which major Indian educational policy?

  1. RTE Act 2009
  2. National Education Policy 2020
  3. NPE 1986
  4. Kothari Commission (1966)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. National Education Policy 2020

Concept: NEP 2020 explicitly mentions SWAYAM and online education as central to achieving its goals of multidisciplinary, flexible, accessible education. SWAYAM + ABC (Academic Bank of Credits) = NEP 2020 implementation tools.

4Which of the following is a UNIQUE feature of SWAYAM compared to global MOOC platforms like Coursera?

  1. SWAYAM offers video lectures
  2. SWAYAM courses are specifically designed for Indian curricula and credit transfer within Indian universities
  3. SWAYAM uses online quizzes
  4. SWAYAM has discussion forums
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. SWAYAM courses are specifically designed for Indian curricula and credit transfer within Indian universities

Concept: SWAYAM = designed for INDIAN educational system. Credit transfer, Indian curriculum alignment, Indian language options = uniquely Indian features absent in Coursera/edX.

Report this question

5The proctored examination for SWAYAM certification is conducted by:

  1. At designated examination centres across India
  2. By peer evaluators
  3. Online from home only
  4. At the learner's college campus without supervision
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. At designated examination centres across India

Concept: Proctored = supervised in-person. Ensures authenticity of certification. NTA (National Testing Agency) or designated centres conduct these exams.

Report this question

6SWAYAM's impact on the "digital divide" in India is best described as:

  1. SWAYAM has worsened the digital divide by making education device-dependent
  2. SWAYAM partially bridges the divide by providing free, high-quality content but is limited by internet access requirements
  3. SWAYAM has no impact on the digital divide
  4. SWAYAM has completely eliminated the digital divide
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. SWAYAM partially bridges the divide by providing free, high-quality content but is limited by internet access requirements

Concept: SWAYAM helps but requires internet/device. Students without connectivity still cannot access it — hence SWAYAMPRABHA (DTH) was added to reach those without internet.

7According to UGC, universities are encouraged to recognise SWAYAM courses for which purpose?

  1. Sports scholarships
  2. Student credit transfer toward their formal degree programmes
  3. Teacher promotions only
  4. Hostel allotment
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Student credit transfer toward their formal degree programmes

Concept: UGC's credit transfer framework = official integration of SWAYAM into the formal degree system. This gives SWAYAM academic legitimacy beyond just informal upskilling.

8The ministry that provides financial support for SWAYAM is:

  1. Ministry of Finance
  2. Ministry of Commerce
  3. Ministry of Electronics and IT
  4. Ministry of Education
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Ministry of Education

Direct memory: MoE funds and oversees SWAYAM as part of its national digital education infrastructure.

9Which of the following subjects is NOT typically offered on SWAYAM?

  1. Indian history
  2. Engineering mathematics
  3. Indian literature
  4. Physical fitness training (hands-on sport)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Physical fitness training (hands-on sport)

Concept: SWAYAM = theoretical and knowledge-based courses. Physical/psychomotor skills requiring physical presence cannot be taught on SWAYAM. Same limitation as all MOOCs.

10SWAYAM PRABHA courses are made available on SWAYAM platform as:

  1. Only for school students
  2. Supplementary video content alongside SWAYAM courses
  3. Replacement for SWAYAM courses
  4. Separate paid content
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Supplementary video content alongside SWAYAM courses

Concept: SWAYAMPRABHA content complements SWAYAM. The same video lectures broadcast on DTH are also available on the SWAYAM platform — two delivery channels, same content.

11AICTE coordinates SWAYAM courses in which domain?

  1. Medical education
  2. Technical education — engineering and technology
  3. Humanities and social sciences
  4. Agricultural sciences
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Technical education — engineering and technology

Concept: AICTE (All India Council for Technical Education) = national coordinator for technical courses on SWAYAM. Coordinates with engineering colleges.

  2. Postgraduate distance education

  1. 9
  2. 7
  3. 12
  4. 5

15SWAYAM primarily targets which category of learners in India?

  1. Only students from IITs and IIMs
  2. Students in conventional colleges, especially those with limited access to quality teaching
  3. Only distance education learners
  4. Students in premier institutes who need advanced content
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Students in conventional colleges, especially those with limited access to quality teaching

Concept: The primary target = students in thousands of ordinary colleges across India who have poor quality teaching, limited library resources, and geographic disadvantages.

Report this question

16The Academic Bank of Credits (ABC) under NEP 2020 works in conjunction with SWAYAM by:

  1. Storing credits earned through SWAYAM courses in a digital account that students can use toward their degrees
  2. Replacing SWAYAM entirely
  3. Conducting SWAYAM examinations
  4. Providing scholarships for SWAYAM learners
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Storing credits earned through SWAYAM courses in a digital account that students can use toward their degrees

Concept: ABC = digital credit bank. SWAYAM credits → deposited in ABC → counted toward degree. Together they create a flexible, credit-based education ecosystem.

17SWAYAM has been most impactful during which period and why?

  1. During 2014–15, because it was new
  2. During examination seasons only
  3. During COVID-19 pandemic (2020–21), because it provided continuity of education when physical institutions closed
  4. During 2010–11, before its official launch
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. During COVID-19 pandemic (2020–21), because it provided continuity of education when physical institutions closed

Concept: COVID-19 = SWAYAM's greatest test and validation. Enrolment surged dramatically. Proved the value of digital infrastructure. Post-COVID, SWAYAM became a permanent fixture.
