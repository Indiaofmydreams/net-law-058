About this question set

This set has 21 multiple-choice questions on Higher Education System from the UGC NET Paper 1 syllabus, unit 10, Higher Education System. Try each question first, then tap Show answer to check the key and read the explanation. 9 of the 21 questions carry a written explanation.

For the concepts and case law behind these questions, read the Higher Education System notes. Once you are comfortable, test yourself under exam conditions with a full-length mock test.
