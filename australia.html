About this question set

This set has 9 multiple-choice questions on Australia from the UGC NET Law (058) syllabus, unit 10, Comparative Public Law and Systems of Governance. Try each question first, then tap Show answer to check the key. This set is an answer-key drill: check each answer, then revisit the notes for any you miss.

For the concepts and case law behind these questions, read the Comparative Public Law notes. Once you are comfortable, test yourself under exam conditions with a full-length mock test.

9 practice questions with answers

1Constitutional amendment in Australia under Section 128 requires:

  1. Two-thirds of Parliament
  2. A parliamentary majority only
  3. A double majority at a referendum
  4. Consent of the Governor-General alone
Show answer

Answer: C. A double majority at a referendum

Report this question

2Which Australian case rejected the doctrine of reserved state powers and implied immunities?

  1. The Engineers' Case (1920)
  2. Cole v. Whitfield
  3. Mabo
  4. Tasmanian Dam Case
Show answer

Answer: A. The Engineers' Case (1920)

Report this question

3The implied freedom of political communication in Australia was recognised in:

  1. Mabo v. Queensland
  2. Wik Peoples
  3. Dietrich v. The Queen
  4. Australian Capital Television v. Commonwealth (1992)
Show answer

Answer: D. Australian Capital Television v. Commonwealth (1992)

Report this question

4Mabo v. Queensland (No. 2) (1992) rejected the doctrine of:

  1. Federalism
  2. Separation of powers
  3. Terra nullius
  4. Responsible government
Show answer

Answer: C. Terra nullius

Report this question

5The Boilermakers' Case (1956) is associated with:

  1. Strict separation of federal judicial power
  2. Free trade among states
  3. Native title
  4. Implied rights
Show answer

Answer: A. Strict separation of federal judicial power

Report this question

6The Commonwealth's enumerated legislative powers are principally in:

  1. Section 51 of the Constitution
  2. Section 128
  3. Section 109
  4. Section 92
Show answer

Answer: A. Section 51 of the Constitution

Report this question

7Section 109 of the Constitution provides that, in case of inconsistency:

  1. State law prevails
  2. The High Court decides case by case
  3. Commonwealth law prevails to the extent of the inconsistency
  4. Both are void
Show answer

Answer: C. Commonwealth law prevails to the extent of the inconsistency

Report this question

8Australian Communist Party v. Commonwealth (1951) struck down:

  1. The Communist Party Dissolution Act
  2. The Banking Act
  3. The Immigration Act
  4. The Racial Discrimination Act
Show answer

Answer: A. The Communist Party Dissolution Act

9Lange v. ABC (1997) modified the implied freedom of:

  1. Religion
  2. Political communication
  3. Movement
  4. Association only
