8 practice questions with answers

1Under Section 2(30), 'debenture' includes:

  1. Only secured bonds
  2. Only convertible instruments
  3. Only instruments issued to the public
  4. Debenture stock, bonds or any other instrument evidencing a debt, whether or not constituting a charge on the company's assets
Show answer

Answer: D. Debenture stock, bonds or any other instrument evidencing a debt, whether or not constituting a charge on the company's assets

Report this question

2Under Section 71(1), a company:

  1. May issue debentures carrying voting rights
  2. May issue debentures carrying voting rights with Tribunal approval
  3. May not issue debentures carrying any voting rights
  4. May issue them only to directors
Show answer

Answer: C. May not issue debentures carrying any voting rights

Report this question

3Debentures convertible into shares, wholly or partly, may be issued under Section 71(1) only after approval by:

  1. A board resolution
  2. An ordinary resolution
  3. Unanimous consent of members
  4. A special resolution
Show answer

Answer: D. A special resolution

Report this question

4Under Section 71(5), a debenture trustee must be appointed before issuing a prospectus or making an offer of debentures to the public or to members exceeding:

  1. 50
  2. 200
  3. 500
  4. 1,000
Show answer

Answer: C. 500

Report this question

5Under Section 77, a charge created by a company must be registered with the Registrar within ___ days of creation, extendable by the Registrar by a further ___ days on payment of additional fee:

  1. 15; 30
  2. 30; 300
  3. 60; 60
  4. 90; 180
Show answer

Answer: B. 30; 300

Report this question

6Which of the following is NOT a characteristic of a floating charge (Re Yorkshire Woolcombers Association)?

  1. It attaches specifically and immediately to identified assets
  2. It is a charge on a class of assets present and future
  3. The class of assets changes from time to time
  4. The company can carry on business in the ordinary way until the charge crystallises
Show answer

Answer: A. It attaches specifically and immediately to identified assets

Report this question

7Consider the following: I. A debenture holder is a creditor; a shareholder is a member. II. Interest on debentures is payable irrespective of profits; dividend depends on profits. III. A debenture holder has no voting right in general meetings.

  1. I only
  2. I and II only
  3. II and III only
  4. I, II and III
Show answer

Answer: D. I, II and III

Report this question

8A floating charge crystallises (becomes fixed) upon:

  1. Payment of interest
  2. A change of directors
  3. The appointment of a receiver or the commencement of winding up
  4. Increase in share capital
Show answer

Answer: C. The appointment of a receiver or the commencement of winding up
