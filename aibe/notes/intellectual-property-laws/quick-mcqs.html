Answer B. Sec 57.

Answer B. Rejected 'sweat of the brow'.

Answer C. Sec 53.

Answer A. Glivec; enhanced therapeutic efficacy needed.

Answer C. Sec 84.

Answer A. Nexavar, 2012.

Answer C. Sec 25.
