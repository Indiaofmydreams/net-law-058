Quick MCQs

46 recall questions to close each study session. Tap an option to see the answer and the reason. There is no timer and no negative marking.

0/ 46 correct

1The minimum number of members for a public company is:

Answer C. Seven (s.3(1)(a)); private company minimum two; OPC one.

2Separate legal personality of a company was established in:

Answer B. Salomon v Salomon & Co (1897).

3A Section 8 company is formed for:

Answer B. Promoting commerce, art, science, charity etc. with income applied only to those objects.

4The rule of indoor management arises from:

Answer B. Turquand's case (1856).

5An ultra vires act of a company is:

Answer B. Beyond the objects clause; void ab initio.

6Which case dealt with the removal of Cyrus Mistry?

Answer A. Supreme Court (2021) restored Tata Sons' position.

7Minimum default amount for initiating CIRP under IBC is:

Answer C. Raised to Rs 1 crore by notification of 24 March 2020.

8A special resolution under the Companies Act needs:

Answer C. Section 114(2): votes in favour must be at least three times the votes against.

9Maximum gap between two Board meetings is:

Answer C. Section 173(1): not more than 120 days.

10Fraud under the Companies Act is punished under:

Answer C. Section 447: 6 months to 10 years and fine up to 3 times the amount involved.

11Debenture holders are:

Answer B. Debentures are debt instruments; holders are creditors.

12Preference shares cannot be issued for redemption beyond:

Answer C. Section 55: maximum 20 years (with exception for infrastructure projects).

13A charge created by a company must be registered within:

Answer B. 30 days of creation, extendable with additional fee (s.77).

14A director's duty under s.166(2) includes acting in the best interests of:

Answer C. Section 166(2) codifies a stakeholder-sensitive duty of good faith.

15Borrowing beyond paid-up capital and free reserves requires:

Answer C. Section 180(1)(c) requires a special resolution.

16Independent directors are NOT entitled to:

Answer B. Section 149(9): no stock options for independent directors.

17Appeal from NCLT to NCLAT under the Companies Act must ordinarily be filed within:

Answer B. Section 421(3): 45 days, extendable by up to 45 days.

18Winding up on 'just and equitable' grounds for quasi-partnership relied on:

Answer B. Ebrahimi (1973).

19Majority required for a scheme under ss.230-232 is:

Answer B. Majority in number representing three-fourths in value of those present and voting.

20Resolution plan under IBC needs approval of CoC by:

Answer B. 66% of the voting share after the 2019 amendment.

21Withdrawal of CIRP application after admission under s.12A needs approval of:

Answer D. Section 12A: ninety per cent of the voting share of the CoC.

22Which case held that the Limitation Act applies to IBC applications?

Answer B. B.K. Educational Services (2018).

23Moratorium under IBC is provided in:

Answer C. Section 14.

24The LLP Act was enacted in:

Answer C. Limited Liability Partnership Act, 2008.

25An LLP is:

Answer B. Section 3 of the LLP Act: body corporate and legal entity separate from partners.

26Appeals from SEBI orders lie to:

Answer B. Securities Appellate Tribunal (s.15T of the SEBI Act).

27Under the Companies Act, 2013 the minimum number of directors in a public company is:

Answer B. Section 149(1).

28The maximum number of directors in a company without a special resolution is:

Answer B. Fifteen; more with a special resolution.

29AGM must be held within how many months of the close of the financial year?

Answer B. Six months; gap between two AGMs not more than fifteen months.

30Maximum gap between two consecutive Board meetings is:

Answer C. Section 173(1).

31A private company can have a maximum of how many members (excluding employees)?

Answer C. Section 2(68): 200.

32CSR obligation is 2% of:

Answer C. Section 135(5).

33Rule in Royal British Bank v Turquand protects:

Answer B. Indoor management.

34Salomon v Salomon & Co established:

Answer B. Corporate personality.

35An auditor of a company (other than a firm) is appointed for a term of:

Answer C. Section 139: one term of five years; second term of five years allowed for individuals; two terms of five years each for firms.

36Fraud under the Companies Act s.447 is punishable with a fine of:

Answer B. Fine not less than the amount involved in fraud, extendable to three times.

37The National Company Law Tribunal is constituted under section:

Answer B. s.408 NCLT; s.410 NCLAT.

38The IBC moratorium comes into effect on:

Answer B. Section 14: on the insolvency commencement date (admission).

39A special resolution under the Companies Act needs votes in favour that are:

Answer B. Section 114(2).

40An independent director may serve at most:

Answer B. Section 149(10)-(11), followed by a cooling-off of 3 years.

41The minimum number of members to form a public company is:

Answer C. Seven.

42Which of these is a Section 8 company?

Answer A. Licensed company with charitable objects; no dividend.

43Unclaimed dividend is transferred to the IEPF after:

Answer C. Seven years from the date of transfer to the Unpaid Dividend Account.

44An unregistered charge is void against:

Answer B. Section 77-78 principle.

45Which case dealt with Cyrus Mistry's removal from Tata Sons?

Answer B. Supreme Court (2021).

46The doctrine in Foss v Harbottle holds that:

Answer B. Proper plaintiff rule.
