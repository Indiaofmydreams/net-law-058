%Where the marks come from

Company Law carries about 2 of 100 questions. Only about 2 questions come from Company Law, almost always basic and concept-based. Master the doctrines (Salomon, lifting the veil, ultra vires, constructive notice, indoor management, Foss v Harbottle), types of companies, directors, meetings and resolutions, oppression and mismanagement, NCLT, and the IBC trigger numbers. Do not over-invest.
