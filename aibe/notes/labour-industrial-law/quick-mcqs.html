Labour and Industrial Law

Quick MCQs

37 recall questions to close each study session. Tap an option to see the answer and the reason. There is no timer and no negative marking.

0/ 37 correct

1The four Labour Codes were brought into force on:

Answer B. Notified with effect from 21 November 2025; Central Rules came on 8-9 May 2026.

2How many Central labour laws were subsumed into the four Labour Codes?

Answer C. 4 + 3 + 9 + 13 = 29.

3The Industrial Relations Code 2020 replaces which of the following?

Answer B. It subsumes the Trade Unions Act, Standing Orders Act and Industrial Disputes Act.

4Which Code replaced the Payment of Bonus Act, 1965?

Answer B. Wages Code subsumes four Acts including the Bonus Act.

5The 'triple test' for 'industry' was laid down in:

Answer B. Bangalore Water Supply (1978, seven judges).

6In State of U.P. v. Jai Bir Singh (2026), the Supreme Court dealt with the meaning of:

Answer B. A nine-judge Bench decided on 20 Aug 2026.

7Under the IR Code, supervisory employees are 'workers' only if their wages do not exceed:

Answer C. Rs 18,000 per month (or as notified).

8A Grievance Redressal Committee is mandatory in an industrial establishment with at least:

Answer B. 20 or more workers; Works Committee for 100+.

9The sole negotiating union must have the support of at least what percentage of workers?

Answer C. 51% of the workers on the muster roll.

10Standing orders under the IR Code apply to establishments with at least:

Answer D. 300 or more (earlier 100).

11The strike notice period under the IR Code is:

Answer D. Notice of 60 days before striking; the strike may not take place within 14 days of notice.

12Under the IR Code, concerted casual leave by what proportion of workers is deemed a strike?

Answer C. 50% or more of workers on a given day.

13Which test is used to decide whether a person is a 'workman'?

Answer A. Burmah Shell (1970) and later cases.

14Retrenchment compensation is:

Answer B. Section 25F ID Act / s.70 IR Code.

15Lay-off compensation is:

Answer B. 50% of basic wages and dearness allowance.

16Prior government permission for lay-off, retrenchment and closure is required in establishments with at least:

Answer D. 300 workers (IR Code Chapter X).

17The Worker Re-skilling Fund receives how many days' wages for each retrenched worker?

Answer B. 15 days' last drawn wages.

18Which Constitution Bench held that regularisation of irregular appointments cannot be ordered ordinarily?

Answer B. Umadevi (2006).

19The 'right to strike' is:

Answer C. T.K. Rangarajan (2003).

20Under the Code on Wages, deductions from wages cannot exceed:

Answer C. 50% of wages.

21Overtime under the Code on Wages is payable at not less than:

Answer B. Twice the normal rate of wages.

22The minimum bonus under the Code on Wages is:

Answer B. 8.33% (maximum 20%).

23Under the Code on Wages, on removal or resignation, wages must be paid within:

Answer B. Within two working days.

24The 'floor wage' is fixed by:

Answer B. Code on Wages s.9 - Central Government; States cannot go below it.

25Gratuity is payable on completion of how many years of continuous service (normal employees)?

Answer C. 5 years; not required for death or disablement; fixed-term employees after 1 year.

26Maternity benefit for a woman with fewer than two surviving children is:

Answer C. 26 weeks.

27A crèche is mandatory in establishments with at least:

Answer C. 50 or more employees.

28The EPF Scheme applies to establishments with at least:

Answer B. 20 or more employees (statutory).

29The ESI Scheme applies to establishments with at least:

Answer A. 10 or more employees; wage limit Rs 21,000.

30Aggregators must contribute what percentage of annual turnover for gig workers (capped at 5% of payouts)?

Answer B. 1% to 2% under the Social Security Code.

31A 'factory' under the OSH Code means premises with at least:

Answer B. 20 workers with power or 40 without power.

32The OSH Code applies to contract labour where the number of contract workers is at least:

Answer C. 50 or more.

33Which case evolved the principle of absolute liability?

Answer B. M.C. Mehta (1987).

34Vishaka v. State of Rajasthan related to:

Answer B. Guidelines against sexual harassment, now POSH Act 2013.

35Which Article prohibits employment of children below 14 in factories and hazardous work?

Answer C. Art 24.

36Which entry of the Concurrent List deals with welfare of labour and provident funds?

Answer C. Entry 24 List III (22: unions and disputes; 23: social security).

37Which Article directs workers' participation in management?

Answer D. Art 43A (42nd Amendment).
