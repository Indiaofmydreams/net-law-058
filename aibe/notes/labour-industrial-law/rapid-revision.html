⚡One-line case revision

  • Bangalore Water Supply (1978) - triple test for 'industry'; Jai Bir Singh (2026) - nine judges refine.
  • Hospital Mazdoor Sabha (1960) - hospitals can be industries.
  • Burmah Shell (1970) - dominant nature of duties decides 'workman'.
  • Punjab Land Development (1990) - broad meaning of 'retrenchment'.
  • Santosh Gupta (1980) - s.25F non-compliance voids retrenchment.
  • Meenakshi Mills (1992) - Chapter VB valid.
  • Rangarajan (2003) - no fundamental right to strike.
  • All India Bank Employees (1962) - Art 19(1)(c) does not guarantee strike or recognition.
  • Umadevi (2006) - no back-door regularisation; Jaggo (2024) - long-serving workers.
  • Randhir Singh (1982); Jagjit Singh (2016) - equal pay for equal work.
  • PUDR (Asiad, 1982) - below minimum wage = forced labour.
  • Vishaka (1997) - POSH guidelines.
  • Oleum Gas (1987) - absolute liability.
  • Steel Authority v. National Union Water Front Workers (2001) - no automatic absorption.

✦Mnemonics

  • 29 = 4 + 3 + 9 + 13 (Wages, IR, Social Security, OSH).
  • Thresholds: 300 (SO & prior permission), 100 (WC), 20 (GRC, EPF), 10 (ESI, gratuity), 50 (contract labour; crèche).
