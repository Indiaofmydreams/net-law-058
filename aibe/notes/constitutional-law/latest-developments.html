Latest developments

Recent judgments, amendments and institutional changes that are favourite current-affairs questions. Notes cover developments up to 16 Nov 2026; anything tagged Verify should be checked against the official source.

Presidential Reference on Governor/President assent to Bills (Arts 200, 201, 143)

Opinion delivered 20 Nov 2025 (five-judge Constitution Bench headed by CJI B.R. Gavai); Special Reference No. 1 of 2025

In response to the President's 14 questions (13 May 2025), the Court held that no judicially fixed timelines can be imposed on Governors/Presidents for acting on Bills, and 'deemed assent' through Art 142 is not permissible because the Court cannot substitute a constitutional functionary. Decisions under Arts 200/201 are not justiciable on merits before a Bill becomes law; limited judicial intervention is possible for prolonged and unexplained inaction. The President need not seek an Art 143 reference on every reserved Bill. The opinion effectively disapproved the 8 April 2025 judgment in State of Tamil Nadu v. Governor of Tamil Nadu.

Why it matters: A direct, high-probability topic: Art 200 options, Art 201, Art 143 binding nature, Art 142 limits, Art 361.

Likely question: Which Article of the Constitution was invoked in the 2025 Presidential Reference on assent to Bills?

Answer: Art 143

Sabarimala reference - Arts 25, 26 and 'essential religious practices'

Nine-judge Bench led by CJI Surya Kant heard Apr-May 2026; judgment reserved 14 May 2026; reports in Oct 2026 say drafting is under way Verify · if pronounced

Seven questions examine the scope of religious freedom, interplay of Art 25 and Art 26 with Art 14 and other FR, whether the essential religious practices (ERP) test is textually grounded, the meaning of 'sections of Hindus' in Art 25(2)(b), and the permissible extent of judicial review and PILs in religious matters. The reference arises from the 2018 Sabarimala judgment and the 2019 review (Kantaru Rajeevaru).

Why it matters: Whichever way it is decided, the verdict could rewrite the ERP doctrine (Shirur Mutt, Durgah Committee, Ismail Faruqui). Learn the 2018 verdict (4:1) and the 2019 review referral (3:2).

Likely question: Which bench strength is hearing the Sabarimala reference in 2026?

Answer: Nine-judge

Defeat of the Constitution (131st Amendment) Bill, 2026 - delimitation/women's reservation

April 2026 (special session)

The Government's package (131st Amendment Bill + Delimitation Bill + UT Laws Bill) failed in the Lok Sabha because the Constitution Amendment Bill did not obtain the two-thirds special majority; the companion Bills were withdrawn. Women's reservation (106th Amendment) takes effect only after delimitation following a census Verify · details.

Why it matters: Tests Art 368 special majority; Arts 82, 81, 330A, 332A.

Likely question: What majority is required to pass a Constitution Amendment Bill under Art 368?

One Nation One Election (129th Amendment Bill) and 130th Amendment Bill (removal of PM/CM/Ministers after 30 days' custody)

JPCs ongoing; JPC on 130th Bill deferred adoption of report on 17 July 2026; JPC on ONOE has time till the last week of Monsoon Session 2026 Verify

129th Amendment (introduced Dec 2024) proposes new Art 82A and amendments to Arts 83, 172, 327 to synchronise Lok Sabha and Assembly polls. 130th Amendment (introduced Aug 2025) proposes Art 75/164/239AA amendments for automatic removal of ministers detained for 30 consecutive days on charges carrying 5+ years.

Why it matters: Possible conceptual questions on Arts 83, 172, 75, 164, 368 and whether State ratification is required.

Likely question: The Kovind Committee reported on which subject?

Answer: Simultaneous elections, March 2024

State of U.P. v. Jai Bir Singh (nine-judge Bench) - meaning of 'industry'

20 Aug 2026

By a 5:4 majority the Bench refined the 1978 Bangalore Water Supply 'triple test'; pending disputes under the repealed Industrial Disputes Act 1947 continue to be governed by the original test; the ruling does not govern the definition under the Industrial Relations Code 2020. Cross-reference to Labour Law.

Why it matters: Larger-bench decisions are favourite 'current affairs' items.

Likely question: How many judges decided the 2026 reference on 'industry'?

Answer: Nine

SIR of electoral rolls (Bihar 2025) and Election Commission's powers

Interim orders Aug-Sept 2025; final decision 2026 Verify

SC upheld ECI's power under Art 324 to conduct a Special Intensive Revision, while holding that Art 326 voting rights and Aadhaar (as an accepted identity document, 12th document) are to be respected.

Why it matters: Art 324, 326, 329; Art 21 and Art 14 challenges.

Likely question: Which Article empowers ECI to superintend elections?

Answer: Art 324

Property Owners Association v. State of Maharashtra and Art 31C

5 Nov 2024 (9 judges)

Art 39(b) does not mandate that every privately owned resource is a 'material resource of the community'; Justice Krishna Iyer's wider view in Ranganatha Reddy was rejected; Art 31C as upheld in Kesavananda survives after Minerva Mills.

Why it matters: DPSP, Art 31C, Art 300A.

Likely question: Which DPSP clause was interpreted in the 2024 nine-judge ruling?

Answer: Art 39(b)

Sita Soren v. Union of India (7 judges)

4 Mar 2024

No parliamentary privilege (Arts 105(2), 194(2)) against prosecution for bribery connected with a vote or speech; overruled P.V. Narasimha Rao (1998).

Why it matters: Privileges; corruption.

Likely question: Which Articles grant parliamentary privileges to MPs/MLAs?

Answer: Arts 105/194

Electoral Bonds struck down (ADR v. Union of India)

15 Feb 2024 (5 judges)

Why it matters: Art 19(1)(a), 14.

Supriyo Chakraborty v. Union of India (marriage equality)

17 Oct 2023 (5 judges)

No fundamental right to marry; the Court declined to read down the Special Marriage Act; Union to form a committee on rights of queer couples.

Delegated legislation and tax: Viraj Impex (2026) and 2026 INSC 1
