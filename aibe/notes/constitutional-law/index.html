%Where the marks come from

Constitutional Law carries about 10 of 100 questions. AIBE is a 100-question multiple-choice, open-book test conducted by the Bar Council of India; qualifying marks are 45% (General/OBC) and 40% (SC/ST/PwD) per BCI rules. Question patterns

  • Direct recall: 'Which Article deals with ...?' / 'Which Amendment inserted ...?'
  • Case-to-principle matching: 'Doctrine of basic structure was propounded in ...'
  • Statement-based: 'Which of the following statements is/are correct?' (1, 2, 3 combinations)
  • Negative framing: 'Which of the following is NOT a fundamental right?'
  • Numbers and periods: 14 days, 6 months, 2/3 majority, 15% ministers, 1/10 quorum, 3 months detention, 24 hours production
  • Current-affairs tie-in: recent Constitution Bench ruling or recent amendment bill
  • Short fact-based scenarios (Governor/Speaker/President action - is it valid?)

High-yield topics

  • Art 21 and its expanded content (privacy, livelihood, dignity, speedy trial, legal aid, health, environment)
  • Art 14 reasonable classification and arbitrariness test
  • Art 19(1) freedoms with 19(2)-(6) restrictions
  • Art 32 vs 226 writs and their scope
  • Basic structure and Art 368 (cases + dates)
  • Art 12 'State' definition and cases
  • President/Governor: election, removal, ordinance, pardon, discretion
  • Anti-defection law (Tenth Schedule)
  • Emergency (Art 352/356/360) and Bommai
  • DPSP vs FR (Minerva Mills) and Art 44 UCC
  • Reservation law (Art 15(4)-(6), 16(4)-(6), Indra Sawhney, 103rd Amendment)
  • Centre-State: Seventh Schedule, Art 254, Art 249, GST Council

Time strategy

  • Target about 90 seconds per Constitutional Law question; these are mostly recall questions, so bank time here for the lengthy CPC/CrPC/Evidence problems.
  • Because AIBE is open-book, mark the Constitution Bare Act with Part-wise flags and keep a one-page index of case names with years.
  • Do not spend time reading long Article text for a question you can answer from memory; use the book only to confirm numbers, periods and clauses.

Common traps

  • Art 21A (education 6-14) vs Art 45 (early childhood care under 6) vs Art 51A(k) (parent's duty).
  • Right to property is NOT a fundamental right since the 44th Amendment (Art 300A is a constitutional right).
  • Right to vote and right to contest are statutory/constitutional rights, not fundamental rights.
  • Right to strike, right to bandh, right to be heard in every case are not fundamental rights.
  • Art 19 is available only to citizens; Art 14, 20, 21, 22 (partly), 25 are available to non-citizens too.
  • Art 20 and 21 cannot be suspended even during Emergency (44th Amendment); Art 19 is suspended only under war/external aggression emergency (Art 358).
  • The Preamble was amended only once (42nd Amendment, 1976).
  • Berubari (1960) said Preamble is not part of the Constitution; Kesavananda (1973) said it is part.
  • Governor cannot pardon a death sentence, but can suspend, remit or commute it (Art 161).
  • Money Bill cannot be introduced in Rajya Sabha; joint sitting under Art 108 does not apply to Money Bills or Constitution Amendment Bills.
