iOverview

Short but scoring: 73rd/74th Amendments, Eleventh and Twelfth Schedules, 97th Amendment and the list of Schedules.

★Must know

  1. 73rd Amendment (1992, effective 24 April 1993 - National Panchayati Raj Day): Part IX (Arts 243-243O) and Eleventh Schedule (29 subjects). Three-tier system (village, intermediate, district; intermediate tier optional for States with population below 20 lakh); Gram Sabha (Art 243A); direct elections; fixed 5-year term; elections within 6 months of dissolution (Art 243E); one-third reservation for women (243D), SC/ST in proportion to population, reservation for chairpersons; State Election Commission (243K) conducts elections; State Finance Commission every 5 years (243I). PESA 1996 extends to Scheduled Areas. Exempt: Nagaland, Meghalaya, Mizoram, hill areas of Manipur, Darjeeling Gorkha Hill Council.
  2. 74th Amendment (1992): Part IXA (Arts 243P-243ZG), Twelfth Schedule (18 subjects); municipalities: Nagar Panchayat, Municipal Council, Municipal Corporation; Wards Committees for population 3 lakh or more; metropolitan planning committee (243ZE) and district planning committee (243ZD).
  3. 97th Amendment (2011): Part IXB (Arts 243ZH-243ZT); cooperative societies made a fundamental right under Art 19(1)(c) and DPSP Art 43B; Rajendra Shah (2021).
  4. Union Territories (Part VIII): Art 239 - administered by President through Administrator; Art 239A - Puducherry; Art 239AA - Delhi (69th Amendment); Art 240 - President can make regulations for Andaman & Nicobar, Lakshadweep, Dadra & Nagar Haveli and Daman & Diu, Puducherry (when Assembly dissolved); Art 241 - High Courts for UTs. J&K reorganised as UT with legislature in 2019; Ladakh UT without legislature.
  5. Schedules (12): 1 - States and UTs; 2 - emoluments of President, Governors, Speakers, judges, CAG; 3 - oaths and affirmations; 4 - allocation of Rajya Sabha seats; 5 - Scheduled Areas and Scheduled Tribes; 6 - tribal areas in Assam, Meghalaya, Tripura, Mizoram; 7 - Union, State, Concurrent Lists; 8 - languages (22); 9 - laws protected from judicial challenge (1st Amendment); 10 - anti-defection (52nd Amendment); 11 - Panchayat powers (29); 12 - Municipality powers (18).
  6. Parts of the Constitution (selected): I Union and its territory; II Citizenship; III Fundamental Rights; IV DPSP; IVA Fundamental Duties; V The Union; VI The States; VIII Union Territories; IX Panchayats; IXA Municipalities; IXB Co-operative Societies; X Scheduled and Tribal Areas; XI Centre-State relations; XII Finance, property, contracts; XIII Trade, commerce; XIV Services; XIVA Tribunals; XV Elections; XVI Special provisions for certain classes; XVII Official language; XVIII Emergency; XIX Miscellaneous; XX Amendment; XXI Temporary, transitional and special provisions; XXII Short title, commencement, Hindi text, repeals. Part VII (States in Part B) repealed by 7th Amendment.

⚖Landmark cases

Union of India v. Rajendra N. Shah

2021

97th Amendment Part IXB invalid for State cooperative societies.

Kishansing Tomar v. Municipal Corporation of Ahmedabad

2006

Municipal elections should not be delayed; Arts 243E/243U.

Suresh Mahajan v. State of M.P.

2022

Triple test for OBC reservation in local bodies must be followed (K. Krishna Murthy 2010; Vikas Kishanrao Gawali 2021).
