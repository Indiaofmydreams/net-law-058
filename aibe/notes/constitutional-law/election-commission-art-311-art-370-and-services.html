iOverview

Art 311 protection of civil servants, Election Commission and the 2023 Act, CAG, UPSC, language provisions and special-status Articles are common.

★Must know

  1. Art 309-311 (Part XIV): Art 309 - recruitment and conditions by law; Art 310 - doctrine of pleasure (civil servants hold office at the pleasure of the President/Governor, subject to Art 311); Art 311(1) - no dismissal or removal by an authority subordinate to the appointing authority; Art 311(2) - no dismissal, removal or reduction in rank except after an inquiry with reasonable opportunity to be heard; exceptions (second proviso): conviction on criminal charge; where inquiry is impracticable; security of the State. Union of India v. Tulsiram Patel (1985); Shamsher Singh (1974).
  2. Art 312 All-India Services - Parliament may create by RS resolution (2/3 of present and voting): Indian Administrative Service (IAS), Indian Police Service (IPS), Indian Forest Service. Art 315-323 Public Service Commissions: UPSC - chairman and members appointed by President, term 6 years or 65 years; State PSC - appointed by Governor, 6 years or 62 years; removal only by the President after SC inquiry on grounds of misbehaviour (Art 317) or by order for insolvency, outside employment, or unfitness. Art 320 consultation functions; Art 323 reports.
  3. Art 324: Election Commission - superintendence, direction and control of elections to Parliament, State legislatures, offices of President and Vice-President. Article 324(5): CEC removal only in same manner and on same grounds as SC judge; other ECs removed only on CEC's recommendation. Anoop Baranwal v. Union of India (March 2023, 5 judges): until Parliament legislates, CEC/EC appointment by committee of PM, Leader of Opposition and CJI. The Chief Election Commissioner and Other Election Commissioners (Appointment, Conditions of Service and Term of Office) Act 2023 replaced the CJI with a Union Cabinet minister nominated by PM - challenge pending Verify. Mohinder Singh Gill v. CEC (1978): Art 324 has residuary powers subject to law; Art 329(b): no court interference in electoral matters once notified (election petition is the only remedy). Art 326: universal adult suffrage (18 years - 61st Amendment, 1988). Art 327-328 laws on elections; Art 243K/243ZA local body elections by SEC.
  4. Special Intensive Revision (SIR) of electoral rolls: Bihar SIR (June 2025) challenged before the SC (Association for Democratic Reforms v. ECI); the Court allowed Aadhaar as a 12th document by an interim order in Sept 2025; the final decision in 2026 upheld ECI's power to purify electoral rolls but stressed constitutional rights (right to vote under Art 326 is statutory-constitutional) Verify · final holding. SIR was extended to other States from late 2025.
  5. Art 148-151: CAG - appointed by President; removal like SC judge; term 6 years or 65; salary charged to Consolidated Fund; audits Union and State accounts (Art 149); reports to President/Governor placed before Parliament/Legislature (Art 151); 'friend, philosopher and guide' of PAC. Art 165 - Advocate General of State.
  6. Art 330-342 Special provisions for SC/ST/Anglo-Indians: Art 330/332 - reservation of seats in LS/Assemblies for SC/ST; 104th Amendment (2019) extended SC/ST reservation to 2030 and discontinued Anglo-Indian nomination; Art 338 NCSC (65th Amendment); 338A NCST (89th Amendment); 338B NCBC (102nd Amendment, 2018); Art 339 - control of Union over administration of Scheduled Areas; Art 340 - Commission for backward classes (Kaka Kalelkar 1953; Mandal 1979); Art 341 - SC list by President (Parliament can modify); Art 342 - ST; Art 342A - President's list of SEBCs (102nd); Art 366(26C) SEBC definition; 105th Amendment restored State list power (2021). Art 335 - SC/ST claims to service consistent with efficiency.
  7. Languages: Art 343 - Hindi in Devanagari script is the official language of the Union; English continued for 15 years (Art 343(2)-(3), Official Languages Act 1963 - English continues indefinitely until legislature decides otherwise; Art 344 Commission); Art 345 - State official language; Art 346-347; Art 348 - language of SC/HC proceedings and authoritative texts of laws - English; Art 349-350; Art 350A - mother tongue instruction at primary level; Art 350B - Special Officer for Linguistic Minorities; Art 351 - directive to develop Hindi. Eighth Schedule - 22 languages (Konkani, Manipuri, Nepali - 71st Amendment; Bodo, Dogri, Maithili, Santhali - 92nd Amendment; Sindhi - 21st; Odia spelling 96th). Classical language status (Marathi, Pali, Prakrit, Assamese and Bengali added in Oct 2024) is administrative, not constitutional.
  8. Special provisions for States: Art 370 (J&K) - abrogation of special status on 5 Aug 2019 upheld in In re: Article 370 of the Constitution (11 Dec 2023, 5 judges, unanimous): Art 370 was a temporary provision; President had power to issue Constitutional Order 273 declaring Art 370 inoperative; J&K Reorganisation Act 2019 valid insofar as it created UT of Ladakh; statehood to be restored as soon as possible; elections to the Assembly were to be held by 30 Sept 2024. Art 371 (Maharashtra, Gujarat), 371A (Nagaland - 13th Amendment), 371B (Assam), 371C (Manipur), 371D and 371E (Andhra Pradesh/Telangana), 371F (Sikkim - 36th Amendment), 371G (Mizoram - 53rd), 371H (Arunachal - 55th), 371I (Goa - 56th), 371J (Karnataka - Hyderabad-Karnataka - 98th Amendment).
  9. Art 244, Fifth Schedule (administration and control of Scheduled Areas and Scheduled Tribes in States other than Assam, Meghalaya, Tripura, Mizoram - Governor's powers), Sixth Schedule (tribal areas in Assam, Meghalaya, Tripura, Mizoram - autonomous district and regional councils); Art 244A (autonomous State in Assam). Samatha v. State of A.P. (1997): tribal land transfers; Orissa Mining Corp. v. MoEF (Niyamgiri, 2013): Gram Sabha consent.
  10. Art 361: immunity of President and Governors - not answerable to any court for exercise and performance of official powers and duties; no criminal proceedings during term; civil proceedings after 2 months' notice. Art 300: suits against Government; Art 361A: protection of publication of legislative proceedings; Art 363-363A: privy purses abolished (26th Amendment 1971). Art 366 definitions; Art 367 interpretation; Art 368 amendment; Arts 369-392 temporary and transitional provisions.
  11. Citizenship and other cross-links: Art 51A, Art 243. Art 50 separation of judiciary from executive.

⚖Landmark cases

Union of India v. Tulsiram Patel

1985

Art 311(2) exceptions valid; pleasure doctrine; limited judicial review.

Mohinder Singh Gill v. Chief Election Commissioner

1978

Art 324 plenary power - subject to law; need for natural justice.

Anoop Baranwal v. Union of India

2023

Independent committee for EC appointments until law is made.

PUCL v. Union of India

2003

Right to know candidates' antecedents under Art 19(1)(a); NOTA in 2013.

Association for Democratic Reforms v. Union of India

2002

Candidates must disclose criminal, financial, educational details.

In re: Article 370 of the Constitution

2023

Abrogation upheld; statehood restoration directed.

Kuldip Nayar v. Union of India

2006

Right to vote and contest elections are statutory, not fundamental.

Union of India v. Rajendra N. Shah

2021

97th Amendment Part IXB invalid for State cooperative societies (not ratified), valid for multi-State societies.

✎Memory aids

  • Art 311: 'No inferior, enquire first - except 3: Conviction, Impracticable, Security'.

!Exam traps

  • TrapCEC is removed like an SC judge; other Election Commissioners only on CEC's recommendation.
  • TrapArt 311 does not apply to employees of statutory bodies/corporations unless extended.
  • TrapHindi in Devanagari script = official language, but English is still allowed under the Official Languages Act 1963.
  • TrapRight to vote is not a FR.
