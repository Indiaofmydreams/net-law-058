#Numbers to remember

  • Preamble amended once - 42nd Amendment (1976).
  • President: 35 years; VP: 35; RS: 30; LS: 25; Governor: 35; voting age 18 (61st Am.).
  • Impeachment of President: 14 days' notice + 1/4 members + 2/3 of total membership of each House.
  • Ordinance - 6 weeks after reassembly; gap between sessions - max 6 months.
  • Council of Ministers - 15% of the House strength (91st Am.).
  • President's Rule - 2 months approval; 6 months; max 3 years.
  • National Emergency - 1 month approval; 6 months renewable.
  • Preventive detention - 3 months before Advisory Board; arrest - 24 hours.
  • Quorum - 1/10 of total membership.
  • LS max 550 (present 543); RS max 250 (present 245); SC judges 34 (as per 2019 Act; 38 reported in 2026 Verify).
  • Retirement: SC 65, HC 62, CAG 65 or 6 yrs, UPSC 65 or 6 yrs, SPSC 62 or 6 yrs.
  • Constitution: 395 Articles / 22 Parts / 8 Schedules at start; now ~448 / 25 / 12.
  • Panchayat - Eleventh Schedule 29 subjects; Municipality - Twelfth Schedule 18 subjects.
  • Eighth Schedule - 22 languages.
  • Joint sittings under Art 108 - 3 (1961, 1978, 2002).
  • Legislative Councils exist in 6 States (AP, Telangana, Karnataka, Maharashtra, Bihar, UP).

⚖Case law in one line

  • Kesavananda Bharati (1973) - basic structure.
  • Golak Nath (1967) - Parliament cannot amend FR (overruled).
  • Minerva Mills (1980) - FR-DPSP harmony; limited amending power.
  • Maneka Gandhi (1978) - Art 21 procedure must be fair, just, reasonable.
  • Puttaswamy (2017) - privacy FR.
  • Indra Sawhney (1992) - 50% cap, creamy layer.
  • S.R. Bommai (1994) - Art 356 reviewable; floor test.
  • Shreya Singhal (2015) - s.66A struck.
  • Navtej Johar (2018) - s.377 partially struck.
  • Shayara Bano (2017) - triple talaq void.
  • Vishaka (1997) - sexual harassment guidelines.
  • Hussainara Khatoon (1979) - speedy trial.
  • Olga Tellis (1985) - livelihood.
  • Kihoto Hollohan (1992) - Tenth Schedule valid.
  • L. Chandra Kumar (1997) - tribunals subject to HC.
  • TMA Pai (2002) - minority institutions.
  • I.R. Coelho (2007) - Ninth Schedule review.
  • Lily Thomas (2013) - immediate disqualification.
  • Sita Soren (2024) - no bribery immunity.
  • Janhit Abhiyan (2022) - EWS upheld.
  • Davinder Singh (2024) - SC/ST sub-classification.
