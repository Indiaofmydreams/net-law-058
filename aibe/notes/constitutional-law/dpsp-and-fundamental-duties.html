Directive Principles of State Policy (Part IV) and Fundamental Duties (Part IV-A)

iOverview

Know classification, key Articles (39, 39A, 40, 41, 43, 44, 45, 46, 47, 48, 48A, 50), relationship with FR, and the list of 11 fundamental duties.

★Must know

  1. Art 36: 'State' same as Part III. Art 37: DPSP are not enforceable by any court but are fundamental in the governance of the country; State has a duty to apply them in making laws.
  2. Classification: Socialistic (Arts 38, 39, 39A, 41, 42, 43, 43A, 47); Gandhian (Arts 40, 43, 43B, 46, 47, 48); Liberal-intellectual (Arts 44, 45, 48, 48A, 49, 50, 51).
  3. Art 38: social order for promotion of welfare (38(2) minimise inequalities - 44th Amendment). Art 39: (a) adequate means of livelihood; (b) distribution of material resources to subserve common good; (c) prevent concentration of wealth; (d) equal pay for equal work for men and women; (e) health and strength of workers, no abuse of children; (f) opportunities for children to develop in healthy manner (42nd Amendment substituted).
  4. Art 39A (42nd Amendment): equal justice and free legal aid. Art 40: village panchayats. Art 41: right to work, education and public assistance (within economic capacity). Art 42: just and humane conditions of work and maternity relief. Art 43: living wage; Art 43A (42nd): workers' participation in management; Art 43B (97th): cooperative societies.
  5. Art 44: Uniform Civil Code for citizens throughout India. Cases: Shah Bano (1985); Sarla Mudgal v. Union of India (1995); John Vallamattom v. Union of India (2003); Shayara Bano (2017). Goa has a UCC (Portuguese Civil Code); Uttarakhand enacted a UCC in 2024 Verify · current status of rules/implementation.
  6. Art 45 (86th Amendment, 2002): early childhood care and education for children below 6. Art 46: promote educational and economic interests of SC/ST and weaker sections. Art 47: raise level of nutrition and standard of living; improve public health; prohibit intoxicating drinks and drugs. Art 48: organise agriculture and animal husbandry; prohibit slaughter of cows and other milch/draught cattle (State of Gujarat v. Mirzapur Moti Kureshi Kassab Jamat, 2005 upheld total ban). Art 48A (42nd): protect and improve environment, safeguard forests and wildlife. Art 49: protect monuments of national importance. Art 50: separate judiciary from executive. Art 51: promote international peace and security.
  7. Relation with FR: Champakam Dorairajan (1951) - FR prevail over DPSP; Kesavananda (1973) and Minerva Mills (1980) - harmony and balance between FR and DPSP is basic structure; neither has absolute primacy. DPSP can be used to interpret and expand FR (e.g., Art 21 through Art 39A, 41, 42, 47, 48A). Art 31C protects laws implementing Art 39(b)-(c).
  8. Fundamental Duties (Art 51A, inserted by the 42nd Amendment, 1976, on the recommendation of the Swaran Singh Committee). Originally 10; the 11th ((k) - parent/guardian to provide opportunities for education to child between 6 and 14) was added by the 86th Amendment (2002). Duties include: abide by the Constitution and respect its ideals, national flag and anthem; cherish ideals of freedom struggle; uphold sovereignty, unity and integrity; defend the country; promote harmony and brotherhood; preserve rich heritage of composite culture; protect and improve natural environment (g); develop scientific temper, humanism; safeguard public property and abjure violence (i); strive for excellence (j); (k) education duty.
  9. Duties are non-justiciable but are relevant in interpreting statutes and in testing reasonableness of restrictions (Rural Litigation and Entitlement Kendra v. State of U.P., 1986; AIIMS Students' Union v. AIIMS, 2001; M.C. Mehta cases on environment).

⚖Landmark cases

State of Madras v. Champakam Dorairajan

1951

DPSP subordinate to FR.

Minerva Mills v. Union of India

1980

Harmony between Part III and IV is basic structure.

Unni Krishnan v. State of A.P.

1993

FR to education read with Art 41/45.

Mohini Jain v. State of Karnataka

1992

Right to education from Art 21 and Art 41; capitation fee invalid.

State of Gujarat v. Mirzapur Moti Kureshi Kassab Jamat

2005

Total ban on cow slaughter upheld citing Art 48.

Sarla Mudgal v. Union of India

1995

Conversion to Islam for bigamy is a fraud; UCC need highlighted.

Randhir Singh v. Union of India

1982

Equal pay for equal work from Arts 14, 16, 39(d).

✎Memory aids

  • DPSP Articles 36-51 (16 Articles). 'Fundamental Duties: 51A(a) to (k)'.
  • '44 = UCC, 45 = Under-6 education, 48A = Environment, 50 = Judiciary separation'.

!Exam traps

  • TrapArt 45 now concerns children below 6 (post-86th Amendment).
  • TrapThe right to education is Art 21A (FR), not DPSP.
  • TrapArt 39A was inserted by the 42nd Amendment (not the 44th).
  • TrapFundamental Duties were not in the original Constitution and are not enforceable by writ.
