Emergency Provisions (Part XVIII: Arts 352-360)

iOverview

Three types of emergencies; conditions of declaration and approval; effect on FR; 44th Amendment safeguards; Bommai on Art 356.

★Must know

  1. National Emergency (Art 352): on grounds of war, external aggression or armed rebellion ('internal disturbance' replaced by 'armed rebellion' - 44th Amendment 1978). President can proclaim on the written recommendation of the Cabinet (Art 352(3)). Must be approved by both Houses within one month by a special majority (majority of total membership + 2/3 of members present and voting); lasts 6 months, renewable indefinitely with approval every six months by simple majority. Revoked by President or by Lok Sabha resolution; one-tenth of LS members may requisition a special sitting to disapprove (Art 352(8)). Judicial review: limited, on grounds of mala fides/extraneous reasons (Minerva Mills 1980).
  2. Effects: Centre can give directions to States on any matter (Art 353); Parliament can legislate on State List (Art 250); LS term extendable by a year at a time (Art 83(2)); Art 354 - revenue distribution may be modified; Art 358 - Art 19 is automatically suspended only on Emergency grounds of war/external aggression (not armed rebellion); Art 359 - President may suspend the right to move court for enforcement of specified FR; Arts 20 and 21 cannot be suspended (44th Amendment). Emergency has been declared in 1962 (China war), 1971 (Pakistan war) and 1975 (internal disturbance - 25 June 1975 to 21 March 1977). The Union notified 25 June as 'Samvidhan Hatya Diwas' in 2024.
  3. ADM Jabalpur v. Shivkant Shukla (1976): during Emergency, right to habeas corpus is suspended - widely condemned; overruled in K.S. Puttaswamy (2017). Justice H.R. Khanna's dissent is celebrated.
  4. State Emergency/President's Rule (Art 356): see Section 1.13 and Bommai. Art 355: duty of Union to protect States against external aggression and internal disturbance and ensure State government is according to the Constitution. Art 365: failure to comply with Union directions can trigger Art 356.
  5. Financial Emergency (Art 360): when financial stability or credit of India or any part is threatened; approval by Parliament within 2 months by simple majority; lasts until revoked; President can direct States to observe financial principles, reduce salaries of all persons in government service (including judges of SC and HC). Never declared yet.
  6. Art 356 usage: Duration - 6 months, extendable to 3 years max; in practice over 130 invocations; S.R. Bommai (1994) curbed misuse. Art 356 imposed on Manipur from 13 Feb 2025 and extended Verify · current status.

✎Memory aids

  • Emergency approval 'ONE month' (352), 'TWO months' (356, 360).
  • 352 = War/External aggression/Armed rebellion.

!Exam traps

  • TrapArmed rebellion (not 'internal disturbance') is the ground after the 44th Amendment.
  • TrapArt 358 applies automatically; Art 359 requires an order of the President.
  • TrapNational Emergency approval - 1 month; President's Rule approval - 2 months.
  • TrapFinancial Emergency can reduce salaries of judges too.
