Constitutional Law

Quick MCQs

54 recall questions to close each study session. Tap an option to see the answer and the reason. There is no timer and no negative marking.

0/ 54 correct

1The doctrine of basic structure was propounded in:

Answer B. Kesavananda Bharati (1973), 13 judges, 7:6.

2Which amendment added the words 'Socialist' and 'Secular' to the Preamble?

Answer B. 42nd Amendment, 1976.

3The right to privacy was held to be a fundamental right in:

Answer C. Puttaswamy (2017), nine judges.

4Article 21A (right to education) was inserted by the:

Answer B. 86th Amendment, 2002; age group 6-14.

5Within how many hours must an arrested person be produced before a Magistrate under Art 22(2)?

Answer B. 24 hours excluding journey time.

6The writ of habeas corpus means:

Answer D. Habeas corpus = produce the body.

7Who described Article 32 as the 'heart and soul' of the Constitution?

Answer B. Dr B.R. Ambedkar.

8The minimum age for membership of the Rajya Sabha is:

Answer C. Art 84: 30 years for RS; 25 for LS.

9Impeachment of the President under Art 61 can be initiated on the ground of:

Answer C. Only 'violation of the Constitution'.

10Which Article abolishes untouchability?

Answer C. Art 17.

11In which case was Art 21 'procedure established by law' read as fair, just and reasonable?

Answer B. Maneka Gandhi (1978).

12Ninth Schedule was added by the:

Answer A. 1st Amendment 1951 - Art 31B.

13Money Bill is defined in which Article?

Answer B. Art 110.

14Anti-defection law is contained in the:

Answer C. Tenth Schedule - 52nd Amendment 1985.

15Panchayati Raj institutions were given constitutional status by the:

Answer C. 73rd Amendment 1992; Part IX.

16Doctrine of eclipse was explained in:

Answer A. Bhikaji Narain Dhakras (1955).

17The Governor cannot grant pardon in respect of:

Answer B. He can suspend, remit or commute but not pardon a death sentence; Art 161.

18National Emergency can be declared on the ground of:

Answer B. Art 352 (post 44th Amendment).

19The 10% EWS reservation was introduced by which Amendment?

Answer C. 103rd (2019); Arts 15(6), 16(6).

20The 50% ceiling on reservation was laid down in:

Answer A. M.R. Balaji (1962); Indra Sawhney (1992).

21Right to property is presently:

Answer C. 44th Amendment (1978).

22Which of the following is NOT a ground for restricting free speech under Art 19(2)?

Answer D. Public health is not listed in 19(2).

23S.66A of IT Act was struck down in:

Answer B. Shreya Singhal (2015).

24Disqualification of an MP on conviction with 2+ years' imprisonment is immediate after which case?

Answer A. Lily Thomas (2013) struck s.8(4) RP Act.

25Which Article provides for the Uniform Civil Code?

Answer B. Art 44.

26GST Council is established under:

Answer C. Art 279A (101st Amendment).

27The residuary power of legislation vests in:

Answer B. Art 248 and Entry 97 List I.

28Art 311 protection applies to:

Answer A. Art 311(1).

29Which body is removed in the same manner as a Supreme Court judge?

Answer B. Art 148(1) CAG; UPSC members removed by President after SC inquiry (Art 317).

30Which of the following requires ratification by at least half of the State legislatures?

Answer B. Art 368(2) proviso covers Arts 54, 55.

31Which Article gives power to the Supreme Court to do 'complete justice'?

Answer C. Art 142.

32Which case struck down the NJAC?

Answer B. Fourth Judges case, 2015.

33Who is the ex officio Chairman of Rajya Sabha?

Answer B. Art 64.

34How many Fundamental Duties were originally inserted by the 42nd Amendment?

Answer B. 10; the 11th added by 86th Amendment (2002).

35Fundamental Duties were added on the recommendation of:

Answer B. Swaran Singh Committee (1976).

36Kihoto Hollohan held:

Answer C. Kihoto Hollohan (1992).

37Which Article empowers Parliament to legislate on a State List subject by a Rajya Sabha resolution?

Answer A. Art 249 - national interest.

38Maximum strength of Lok Sabha under the Constitution is:

Answer C. Art 81 - 550 (530 + 20).

39A Constitution Bench in 2023 held that there is no fundamental right to:

Answer B. Supriyo Chakraborty (Oct 2023).

40Right to vote in India is:

Answer B. Kuldip Nayar (2006); PUCL.

41Art 31C was extended to all DPSP by the 42nd Amendment, which was struck down in:

Answer B. Minerva Mills 1980.

42Under the Nov 2025 Presidential Reference opinion, courts may:

Answer C. No judicially imposed timelines; no deemed assent.

43Contempt of court is a ground for restriction under:

Answer A. Art 19(2).

44Sita Soren (2024) overruled:

Answer B. No bribery immunity.

45Which Article gives power to the President to consult the Supreme Court?

Answer C. Art 143.

46Which of the following is correct regarding Art 20(3)?

Answer B. Applies to accused persons (Kathi Kalu Oghad).

47A Constitution (Amendment) Bill requires:

Answer B. Art 368(2).

48The Preamble was held part of the Constitution in:

Answer B. Kesavananda (1973) departed from Berubari.

49Which Article provides for the Election Commission?

Answer B. Art 324.

50Which case recognised 'third gender' status for transgender persons?

Answer A. NALSA (2014).

51Which writ is issued to prevent a lower court from exceeding jurisdiction?

Answer B. Prohibition - issued before the final order.

52The Tenth Schedule exception on 'merger' requires:

Answer B. Para 4.

53A person can be a minister without being a legislator for at most:

Answer B. Art 75(5), 164(4).

54Which Article provides for an Advocate-General of a State?

Answer C. Art 165.
