%Where the marks come from

Taxation carries about 4 of 100 questions. AIBE is a 100-question multiple-choice, open-book test; qualifying marks 45% (General/OBC) and 40% (SC/ST/PwD). Confirm format and languages in the AIBE-XXI notification Verify.

Question patterns

  • Percentages and periods: bonus 8.33%-20%, retrenchment compensation 15 days per year, strike notice 60 days, gratuity 5 years, maternity 26 weeks, advance tax instalments
  • Definitions: industry (triple test), worker/workman, wages, retrenchment, lay-off, supply, taxable person
  • Case-to-principle matching: Bangalore Water Supply, Uma Devi, Randhir Singh, Vodafone, McDowell, Safari Retreats
  • What replaced what: Income-tax Act 1961 -> 2025; ID Act 1947 -> IR Code; GST rate slabs 2025
  • Constitutional tax questions: who can levy which tax; Art 265; Art 246A; Art 276

High-yield topics

  • Income Tax: residential status, heads of income, new regime slabs, TDS, assessment, appeals (30/60/120 days)
  • GST: supply, composition, ITC (s.16, s.17(5)), registration thresholds, GST 2.0 slabs, appeals, GSTAT
  • Tax and Constitution: Arts 265, 246A, 279A, 276; tax v. fee

Time strategy

  • For tax numbers, think about which law and which year the question is set in.

Common traps

  • Both the Codes (21 Nov 2025) and Income-tax Act 2025 (1 April 2026) are in force, but proceedings for earlier periods continue under the old statutes.
  • A tax is a compulsory exaction without quid pro quo; a fee requires a service (Shirur Mutt).
  • Under GST, alcohol for human consumption is outside GST; petroleum products are outside until notified.
  • Income-tax 'Previous year / Assessment year' is replaced by a single 'Tax Year' only from Tax Year 2026-27.
  • Appeal limits: CIT(A) 30 days, ITAT 60 days, High Court (s.260A) 120 days; GST first appeal 3 months (+1).
