iOverview

A short, scoring block: customs levy and valuation, appeals, stamp duty and the 2023 Constitution Bench on unstamped agreements.

★Must know

  1. Customs Act 1962: levy of duty on import into and export from India (s.12); duties - basic customs duty (BCD) under the Customs Tariff Act 1975, IGST on imports under s.3(7) of the Customs Tariff Act, Social Welfare Surcharge (10% of BCD), Agriculture Infrastructure and Development Cess (AIDC), anti-dumping duty, safeguard duty, countervailing duty; classification by the Harmonised System of Nomenclature (HSN); valuation (s.14) - transaction value as per the Customs Valuation (Determination of Value of Imported Goods) Rules 2007; bill of entry (s.46) and shipping bill (s.50); Customs Act empowers search, seizure and arrest (ss.100, 104), confiscation (s.111 - improperly imported goods; s.113 - export), penalties (ss.112, 114); demand (s.28 - within 2 years; 5 years in cases of fraud/ collusion), refund (s.27 - within one year); drawback (s.74-75); baggage rules.
  2. Customs appeals: Commissioner (Appeals) under s.128 within 60 days; Customs, Excise and Service Tax Appellate Tribunal (CESTAT) under s.129A (within 3 months); High Court under s.130 (substantial question of law); Supreme Court. Customs Authority for Advance Rulings; Settlement Commission replaced by Interim Board.
  3. Cases: Commissioner of Customs v. Dilip Kumar & Co. (2018 - exemption notifications interpreted strictly).
  4. Stamp duty: duty on instruments (documents) - Indian Stamp Act 1899 (Central law applicable to bills of exchange, cheques, etc. under Entry 91 List I) and State Stamp Acts (Entry 63 List II); instrument not duly stamped is inadmissible in evidence (s.35) until duty and penalty are paid; registration (Registration Act 1908) is separate. N.N. Global Mercantile Pvt. Ltd. v. Indo Unique Flame Ltd. (2023, five judges) held that an unstamped arbitration agreement is void; the seven-judge Bench in In Re: Interplay between Arbitration Agreements under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act 1996 and the Indian Stamp Act 1899 (13 Dec 2023) overruled it: an unstamped or inadequately stamped instrument is inadmissible but not void; it is a curable defect and the Arbitral Tribunal decides on stamping (cross-ref ADR).
  5. Securities Transaction Tax (STT) under Chapter VII of the Finance (No.2) Act 2004; Commodities Transaction Tax; Banking Cash Transaction Tax (withdrawn). Professional tax by States (cap Rs 2,500 - Art 276); Property tax by municipalities (Entry 49 List II); Entertainment tax - subsumed in GST except local bodies; Motor vehicle tax (Entry 57 List II); Toll (Entry 59); Wealth tax and Estate duty abolished; gifts are taxed as income under s.56(2)(x); Equalisation Levy withdrawn; Digital taxation; Carbon tax debate.
  6. Black Money Act 2015: 30% tax plus 90% penalty on undisclosed foreign income/assets; Benami Transactions (Prohibition) Act: Ganpati Dealcom (2022); Prevention of Money Laundering Act 2002: tax crimes as scheduled offences; Fugitive Economic Offenders Act 2018 (cross-ref BNSS ss.84-86 proclaimed persons); Smuggling and COFEPOSA - detention; Customs offences: s.135 (evasion of duty or prohibitions) - imprisonment up to 7 years; Customs offences are cognizable (for fiscal offences above specified amount) and bailable depending on value.
  7. Tax and constitutional limitations: Art 265; Art 14 (reasonable classification); Art 19(1)(g) (tax cannot be confiscatory); Art 20(1); Art 300A; Art 301-304 (compensatory taxes do not violate freedom of trade - Automobile Transport (Rajasthan) v. State of Rajasthan, 1962); Entry 54 List II; Art 286; Art 366(29A) (deemed sale - works contract); Union of India v. Bombay Tyre International (1983) (valuation).

⚖Landmark cases

In Re: Interplay between Arbitration Agreements and the Indian Stamp Act, 1899

2023

Seven-judge Bench: unstamped agreement inadmissible but not void; curable; overruled N.N. Global.

Commissioner of Customs v. Dilip Kumar & Co.

2018

Exemption notifications strictly interpreted.

Union of India v. Ganpati Dealcom

1962

Compensatory taxes do not violate Art 301.

✎Memory aids

  • Customs appeals: 60 days (Commissioner Appeals) - CESTAT - HC - SC.

!Exam traps

  • TrapStamp duty on bills of exchange etc. is a Union subject (Entry 91 List I); stamp duty on other documents is a State subject (Entry 63 List II).
  • TrapUnstamped agreement is inadmissible, not void (2023, seven judges).

?Likely questions

  1. Which Act governs customs duty? (Customs Act 1962)
  2. Is an unstamped arbitration agreement void? (No - inadmissible but curable)
