Latest developments

Recent judgments, amendments and institutional changes that are favourite current-affairs questions. Notes cover developments up to 18 Nov 2026; anything tagged Verify should be checked against the official source.

Income-tax Act 2025 comes into force

1 April 2026

The new Act replaces the 1961 Act, introducing the 'Tax Year' and a leaner structure. The substance of the heads of income, residence, TDS, assessment and appeals is retained. The new tax regime remains default; basic exemption Rs 4 lakh and s.87A rebate for income up to Rs 12 lakh (FY 2025-26). Returns for FY 2025-26 (AY 2026-27) are filed under the 1961 Act; Tax Year 2026-27 is the first under the 2025 Act Verify · Finance Act 2026 changes.

Why it matters: Statute replacement questions are common in AIBE.

Likely question: The Income-tax Act 2025 is effective from?

Answer: 1 April 2026

GST 2.0 - rate rationalisation, cess withdrawal and GSTAT

56th GST Council 3 Sept 2025; effective 22 Sept 2025; GSTAT operational 2025-26

Two principal slabs (5% and 18%) plus a 40% demerit rate; compensation cess phased out; individual life and health insurance premium exempted; trade-facilitation measures (registration, refunds); the GST Appellate Tribunal began functioning with a window for pending appeals up to 31 Dec 2026 Verify.

Why it matters: Rates and forum questions.

Likely question: How many principal GST slabs exist after Sept 2025?

Answer: Two - 5% and 18%, plus 40% special

Supreme Court on GST arrests and ITC

2024-2026

Radhika Agarwal (2025) - arrest powers under GST upheld with safeguards; Safari Retreats (2024) functionality test and the retrospective 2025 amendment; 2026 rulings on ITC where supplier defaults and extended limitation under s.74 Verify.

Why it matters: Case-law MCQs under GST.

Likely question: Which test determines 'plant' in s.17(5)(d)?

Answer: Functionality test

Reassessment under the Income-tax Act and Finance Act 2026

2022-2026

Ashish Agarwal (2022) and Rajeev Bansal (2024) fixed the transition for notices issued after 1 April 2021; in 2026 the Finance Act's retrospective s.147A route was challenged and a High Court decision striking it down was stayed by the Supreme Court in Sept 2026 Verify.

Why it matters: Reassessment time limits (3 years / 10 years with Rs 50 lakh).

Likely question: Up to how many years can notice under s.148 be issued if escaped income is Rs 50 lakh or more?

Answer: 10

Stamp duty on arbitration agreements

13 Dec 2023 (seven judges)

Unstamped instruments are inadmissible but not void; the defect is curable. This resolves the N.N. Global dilemma.

Why it matters: Cross-over with ADR.

Likely question: Is an unstamped arbitration agreement void?

Answer: No

Mineral Area Development Authority v. SAIL (9 judges)

25 July 2024

State legislatures may tax mineral rights/lands bearing minerals; royalty is not a tax. Relevant for Centre-State taxation.

Why it matters: Entry 50 List II, Entry 54 List I.

Likely question: Is royalty a tax?

Answer: No

Tax procedure technology and faceless regimes

2020-2026

Faceless assessment (s.144B), faceless appeals, e-assessment; GST e-invoicing for turnover above Rs 5 crore; both being reframed under the 2025 Act.

Why it matters: Procedure questions.

Likely question: Which section provides for faceless assessment?

Answer: s.144B
