⚡One-line case revision

  • Mohit Minerals (2022) - GST Council recommendations not binding.
  • McDowell (1985) vs Azadi Bachao (2003) - avoidance vs planning.
  • Vodafone (2012) - indirect transfer not taxable; retrospective law later withdrawn.
  • Dilip Kumar (2018) - exemption notifications strictly construed.
  • Safari Retreats (2024) - functionality test for 'plant'.
  • Ashish Agarwal (2022); Rajeev Bansal (2024) - reassessment transition.
  • Binoy Viswam (2017) - PAN-Aadhaar linking.
  • Ganpati Dealcom (2022) - Benami Act s.3(2) unconstitutional.

✦Mnemonics

  • Tax five heads: S-H-B-C-O. Residence: 182 / 60+365.
  • GST: 'CGST+SGST / IGST'; slabs 5-18-40.
