Quick MCQs

31 recall questions to close each study session. Tap an option to see the answer and the reason. There is no timer and no negative marking.

0/ 31 correct

1Under which Article can no tax be levied or collected except by authority of law?

Answer B. Art 265.

2Which Article establishes the GST Council?

Answer C. Art 279A.

3Taxes on income other than agricultural income fall in:

Answer A. Union subject; agricultural income tax is a State subject (Entry 46).

4The maximum professional tax under Art 276 is:

Answer B. Rs 2,500 per person per year.

5GST Council recommendations are:

Answer B. Union of India v. Mohit Minerals (2022).

6The Income-tax Act 2025 came into force on:

Answer C. 1 April 2026.

7Under the Income-tax Act 1961, how many heads of income are there?

Answer C. Salaries; House property; PGBP; Capital gains; Other sources.

8An individual is resident if he stays in India for at least:

Answer C. 182 days in the previous year (or 60 days + 365 days in preceding four years).

9Agricultural income is:

Answer B. Exempt from Union income-tax.

10Advance tax is payable if the estimated liability is at least:

Answer C. Rs 10,000.

11An appeal to the ITAT must be filed within:

Answer B. 60 days of receipt of the CIT(A) order.

12An appeal to the High Court under s.260A of the Income-tax Act lies on:

Answer B. Substantial question of law within 120 days.

13The CIT(A) appeal must be filed within:

Answer B. 30 days of service of the order/ demand.

14Notice under s.148 (reassessment) can go up to ten years only if escaped income is at least:

Answer C. Rs 50 lakh or more (s.149).

15The scrutiny notice under s.143(2) must be served within:

Answer B. 3 months from the end of the financial year of filing.

16Wilful tax evasion under s.276C: if tax evaded exceeds Rs 25 lakh, imprisonment is:

Answer C. 6 months to 7 years.

17In which case was the 'look at' approach to tax avoidance applied to indirect transfer of shares?

Answer C. Vodafone (2012).

18Which case upheld linking of PAN with Aadhaar subject to a limitation?

Answer B. Binoy Viswam (2017).

19GST was effective from:

Answer B. 1 July 2017.

20'Supply' under GST is defined in:

Answer B. CGST s.7.

21GST registration threshold for suppliers of goods (normal States) is:

Answer C. Rs 40 lakh (goods); Rs 20 lakh (services).

22Which of the following is outside GST?

Answer B. Alcohol for human consumption is outside GST.

23ITC on motor vehicles is blocked if seating capacity is not more than:

Answer C. Vehicles for transporting persons with seating capacity of not more than 13 (with exceptions) - s.17(5).

24GST slabs after 22 September 2025 are:

Answer B. Two principal slabs and a 40% rate for sin/luxury goods.

25The first appeal under GST (s.107) must be filed within:

Answer C. 3 months from communication (plus one month condonation).

26GST offences are non-bailable where tax evaded exceeds:

Answer C. Rs 5 crore.

27A refund under GST s.54 must be claimed within:

Answer C. 2 years from the relevant date.

28The test applied in Safari Retreats (2024) to interpret 'plant' in s.17(5)(d) is the:

Answer A. Safari Retreats - functionality test.

29An unstamped arbitration agreement is, according to the 2023 seven-judge Bench:

Answer B. In Re: Interplay between Arbitration Act and Stamp Act (13 Dec 2023).

30Stamp duty on bills of exchange and cheques is a:

Answer B. Entry 91 List I; other documents - Entry 63 List II.

31Which of the following is a ground for refusing a refund under GST?

Answer A. Refund incidence passed to consumers is credited to the Consumer Welfare Fund.
