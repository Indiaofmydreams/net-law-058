Cyber Law

Quick MCQs

25 recall questions to close each study session. Tap an option to see the answer and the reason. There is no timer and no negative marking.

0/ 25 correct

1The Information Technology Act 2000 is based on the Model Law on Electronic Commerce adopted by:

Answer B. UNCITRAL Model Law on Electronic Commerce, 1996 (General Assembly Resolution 51/162).

2Which of the following is NOT excluded from the IT Act by the First Schedule?

Answer A. Negotiable instruments other than a cheque are excluded; a cheque is not.

3Which section of the IT Act was struck down in Shreya Singhal v Union of India (2015)?

Answer D. S.66A violated Art 19(1)(a) and was not saved by Art 19(2).

4Identity theft is punishable under which section of the IT Act?

Answer C. S.66C: up to 3 years and fine up to Rs 1 lakh. S.66D is cheating by personation.

5Cyber terrorism under S.66F is punishable with:

Answer B. S.66F provides imprisonment which may extend to imprisonment for life.

6Punishment for child pornography under S.67B (first conviction) is imprisonment up to:

Answer A. 7 years on subsequent conviction.

7S.66E of the IT Act punishes:

Answer D. Up to 3 years or fine up to Rs 2 lakh or both.

8The power to direct blocking of public access to information is contained in:

Answer C. S.69A and the 2009 Blocking Rules; upheld in Shreya Singhal.

9Under S.79, an intermediary loses safe harbour if it:

Answer B. Shreya Singhal read down S.79(3)(b).

10Under the IT Rules 2021, a Significant Social Media Intermediary is one with at least:

Answer A. 5 million registered users in India.

11The Adjudicating Officer under S.46 IT Act can decide claims not exceeding:

Answer D. Claims above Rs 5 crore lie before the competent civil court.

12Appeals from the Adjudicating Officer under the IT Act now lie to:

Answer C. Finance Act 2017 merged the CAT with TDSAT; then High Court within 60 days (S.62).

13Offences under the IT Act punishable with imprisonment of three years are:

Answer B. S.77B: three years and above are cognizable; offences punishable with three years are bailable.

14An investigation of an offence under the IT Act can be carried out by a police officer not below the rank of:

Answer A. S.78 IT Act.

15Puttaswamy v Union of India (2017) held that the right to privacy is:

Answer D. Nine-judge bench; test of legality, legitimate aim and proportionality.

16Under the DPDP Act 2023, a 'child' is a person below the age of:

Answer C. 18 years; verifiable parental consent and no tracking or targeted advertising.

17The maximum monetary penalty under the DPDP Act 2023 (Schedule) is:

Answer B. For failure to take reasonable security safeguards.

18Appeal against an order of the Data Protection Board of India lies to:

Answer A. S.29 DPDP Act.

19The DPDP Rules 2025 were notified on:

Answer D. Phased commencement over about 18 months.

20In Sharat Babu Digumarti v Government of NCT of Delhi (2016), the Supreme Court held that:

Answer C. Special law prevails over general law.

21In Anuradha Bhasin v Union of India (2020) the Supreme Court held that:

Answer B. Orders must be proportionate, published and reviewable.

22Under the 2026 amendment to the IT Rules, unlawful content must be taken down within how many hours of a court order or government notice?

Answer A. Earlier 36 hours; 2 hours for high-risk content such as NCII.

23In the First Schedule, which of the following is excluded from the IT Act?

Answer D. Immovable property contracts are excluded.

24Which section makes e-contracts enforceable despite being formed electronically?

Answer C. S.10A inserted in 2008.

25The national agency for cyber security incident response under S.70B is:

Answer B. NCIIPC (S.70A) protects critical information infrastructure.
