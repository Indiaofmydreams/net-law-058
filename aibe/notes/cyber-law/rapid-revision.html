iBackground and transition

Summary
Cyber Law for AIBE is essentially the Information Technology Act 2000 (as amended in 2008) plus its Rules, supplemented by constitutional privacy law (Puttaswamy), the DPDP Act 2023 and Rules 2025, and the cyber-relevant provisions of the BNS, BNSS and BSA. With only 2 questions, the dependable marks are S.66A/Shreya Singhal, the offence-and-punishment table (S.65-67B, 66C-66F), S.79 safe harbour, the First Schedule exclusions, definitions and digital signatures; the current-affairs mark is the DPDP Act/Rules or the IT Rules amendments.
Approach
Memorise the offence table in 6B-2 first (it covers most past questions), then S.79 and Shreya Singhal, then the DPDP penalty/child/Board facts, then definitions and authorities.

⚡Rapid-fire one-liners

  • IT Act 2000 in force 17 Oct 2000; 2008 amendment in force 27 Oct 2009; based on UNCITRAL Model Law (1996).
  • First Schedule exclusions: negotiable instrument (not cheque), power of attorney, trust, will, immovable property contract.
  • S.65 tampering with source documents: 3 years/Rs 2 lakh. S.66 hacking: 3 years/Rs 5 lakh.
  • S.66A struck down in Shreya Singhal (24 March 2015).
  • S.66B stolen resource; S.66C identity theft; S.66D cheating by personation; S.66E privacy violation; S.66F cyber terrorism (life).
  • S.67 obscenity: 3 yrs/Rs 5 lakh then 5 yrs/Rs 10 lakh. S.67A and 67B: 5 yrs/Rs 10 lakh then 7 yrs/Rs 10 lakh.
  • S.69 interception; S.69A blocking (upheld); S.69B traffic data.
  • S.70 protected system (10 years); S.70A NCIIPC; S.70B CERT-In.
  • S.72 breach of confidentiality 2 yrs; S.72A breach of contract 3 yrs/Rs 5 lakh.
  • S.77A compounding; S.77B offences of 3 years bailable; Inspector investigates (S.78).
  • S.79 safe harbour; actual knowledge = court order/government notification (Shreya Singhal).
  • S.46 Adjudicating Officer up to Rs 5 crore; appeal to TDSAT (earlier CAT); then High Court (S.62).
  • S.43A compensation for failure to protect sensitive personal data; SPDI Rules 2011.
  • Digital signature S.3 (asymmetric crypto + hash); e-signature S.3A; S.10A e-contracts valid.
  • Puttaswamy (2017): privacy is a fundamental right under Art 21; three-fold test.
  • DPDP Act 2023: assent 11 Aug 2023; Rules 13 Nov 2025; child under 18; max penalty Rs 250 crore; Board + TDSAT.
  • DPDP Data Principal rights: access, correction/erasure, grievance, nominate.
  • IT Rules 2021: SSMI = 5 million users; CCO + Nodal Contact + Grievance Officer.
  • 2026 Amendment: SGI labelling; 3-hour takedown; effective 20 Feb 2026.
  • Anuradha Bhasin (2020): internet shutdowns reviewable; internet speech/trade protected.
  • Digumarti (2016): special law (IT Act) prevails over IPC for electronic obscenity.
  • CERT-In 2022 Directions: report incidents in 6 hours; logs 180 days.
