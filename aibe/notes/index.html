AIBE notes: common questions

Are the AIBE notes free?

Yes. Every note, highlight sheet and MCQ on NET Law-Verse is free and needs no sign-up or login.

How are the AIBE notes organised?

Under the 19 subjects of the Bar Council of India AIBE syllabus, in syllabus order. Each subject is split into chapters, and each chapter has an overview, must-know points, key provisions, landmark cases, memory aids, exam traps and likely questions.

Which subjects are available now?

Notes are being added subject by subject. 10 of 19 subjects are live: Constitutional Law, Indian Penal Code / Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, Criminal Procedure Code / Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, Evidence Act / Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, Family Law, Company Law, Environmental Law, Cyber Law, Contract, Specific Relief, Property and Negotiable Instruments, Land Acquisition. The rest are marked Coming soon on the notes page.

Can I highlight the notes and save my highlights?

Yes. Select any text in a chapter and choose a colour. Highlights are saved in your browser and collected chapter by chapter on the My highlights page, where you can print or copy them.

How do I ask a doubt?

Select the text you are unsure about, or use the ? button next to any point, and choose Ask a doubt. Your doubt is saved on your device and you can send it to NET Law-Verse by email in one tap.

Are these notes enough for AIBE?

They are a revision aid, not a substitute for the Bare Acts. AIBE has been reported as an open-book test with unannotated Bare Acts, so use the notes to know what to look up and where. Confirm the rules for your edition in the Bar Council of India notification.

How current are the notes?

Each page shows the date it was last updated. Facts that rest on press or secondary reports are tagged Verify so you can check them against the official source.
