≠Pairs the examiner likes

Delegated vs conditional legislation
Delegated: executive makes subsidiary law; conditional: law complete, executive decides when/ where it applies.
Legitimate expectation vs promissory estoppel
LE: ground of review, no promise needed; PE: requires promise and altered position.
Prohibition vs certiorari
Prohibition prevents; certiorari quashes.

✓Final revision checklist

  • Can I explain the difference between strict and absolute liability with the cases?
  • Do I remember the Sec 140 / Sec 161 / PM-RAHAT amounts and Pranay Sethi percentages?
  • Can I state the consumer pecuniary limits, limitation, appeal periods and deposit?
  • Can I list the grounds of judicial review and the key case for each (CCSU, Wednesbury, Om Kumar, Maneka Gandhi, Kraipak)?
  • Do I know the RTI timelines and Lokpal composition?
  • Can I give the term of copyright, patent, trade mark, design and GI without looking?
  • Have I tabbed the bare Acts for the open-book look-up?
