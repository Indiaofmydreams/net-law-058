iOverview

Use these as 'current affairs' hooks for the three Administrative Law questions.

★Must know

  1. BULLDOZER JUSTICE (13 November 2024): In Re: Directions in the matter of demolition of structures - pan-India due process guidelines (15-day notice, personal hearing, reasoned order, digital portal, videography, personal liability of officials). Violation attracts contempt; subsequent contempt petitions were referred to the High Courts in 2026 (verify).
  2. TRIBUNAL REFORMS: Supreme Court struck down key provisions of the Tribunals Reforms Act 2021 in Madras Bar Association v Union of India (November 2025) and directed the Union to set up a National Tribunals Commission (verify current status).
  3. DPDP Act and RTI: amendment of Sec 8(1)(j) brought into force with the DPDP Rules 2025; constitutional challenge pending.
  4. LEGITIMATE EXPECTATION AND POLICY CHANGE: 2026 judgments reiterate that legitimate expectation is not an absolute right; policy change in public interest, applied non-arbitrarily, can defeat it, but fairness in transition/ notice is required.
  5. PROPORTIONALITY: increasing use in review of administrative restrictions (Anuradha Bhasin (2020), internet shutdowns; State action affecting livelihood).
  6. LOKPAL AND HIGH COURT JUDGES (2025): the Supreme Court stayed Lokpal's order asserting jurisdiction over a sitting High Court Judge; the jurisdictional question remains to be decided.
  7. SECTION 17A, PREVENTION OF CORRUPTION ACT: constitutional validity referred to a larger Bench after a split verdict (2023).
  8. BNS/BNSS and administrative actions: references to IPC/ CrPC in administrative statutes read as BNS/ BNSS.

!Exam traps

  • TrapCheck the date and the court (Supreme Court vs High Court) for recent-development questions.

?Likely questions

  1. Which 2024 judgment framed guidelines on demolitions? In Re: Directions in the matter of demolition of structures.
  2. National Tribunals Commission: suggested by the Supreme Court.
