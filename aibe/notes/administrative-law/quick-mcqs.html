Quick MCQs

11 recall questions to close each study session. Tap an option to see the answer and the reason. There is no timer and no negative marking.

0/ 11 correct

1The Supreme Court held that natural justice forms part of Art 21 in:

Answer B. Maneka Gandhi v Union of India (1978).

2'Essential legislative function' cannot be delegated - leading case:

Answer A. In re Delhi Laws Act (1951).

3Wednesbury unreasonableness relates to which ground of review?

Answer B. GCHQ classification.

4Proportionality as a standard in India was developed in:

Answer A. Om Kumar (2001); Ranjit Thakur earlier.

5Tribunals' jurisdiction under Arts 226/227 cannot be excluded - case:

Answer B. L. Chandra Kumar (1997).

6The writ for quashing an order of a quasi-judicial authority is:

Answer B. Certiorari.

7Notice before suing the Government under Sec 80 CPC:

Answer C. Two months.

8RTI: the Public Information Officer must reply within:

Answer B. 30 days; 48 hours for life or liberty.

9Doctrine of necessity was applied in:

Answer A. A biased authority may act when no other authority is competent.

10In the 'bulldozer justice' judgment (2024), prior notice before demolition must be at least:

Answer B. Fifteen days, with a personal hearing and reasoned order.

11Legitimate expectation was recognised in:

Answer A. Navjyoti (1992).
