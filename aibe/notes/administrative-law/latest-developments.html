Administrative Law

Latest developments

Recent judgments, amendments and institutional changes that are favourite current-affairs questions. Notes cover developments up to 18 Nov 2026; anything tagged Verify should be checked against the official source.

Bulldozer justice guidelines

13 Nov 2024; contempt matters 2026

In Re: Directions in the matter of demolition of structures: 15-day notice, hearing, reasoned order, digital portal, videography, personal liability for officials. 2026: contempt petitions alleging violations were sent to the High Courts (verify).

Why it matters: Natural justice, Art 21, procedural due process.

Tribunal independence

Nov 2025

In Madras Bar Association v Union of India the Supreme Court struck down key provisions of the Tribunals Reforms Act 2021 and directed a National Tribunals Commission (verify status).

Why it matters: L. Chandra Kumar; Arts 323A/323B.

RTI and the DPDP Act

Nov 2025

Sec 44(3) DPDP Act 2023 amended Sec 8(1)(j) RTI Act (personal information exemption) - in force with the DPDP Rules 2025; challenges pending.

Why it matters: Sec 8(1)(j) RTI.
