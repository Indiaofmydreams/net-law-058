Contract, Specific Relief, Property and Negotiable Instruments

Quick MCQs

40 recall questions to close each study session. Tap an option to see the answer and the reason. There is no timer and no negative marking.

0/ 40 correct

1Under Sec 2(d) of the Indian Contract Act, consideration may move from:

Answer B. Indian law allows consideration from the promisee or any other person; the stranger still cannot sue (privity).

2A minor's agreement in India is:

Answer C. Mohori Bibee v Dharmodas Ghose. Sec 68 gives a claim only against the minor's property for necessaries.

3Communication of acceptance is complete as against the proposer when:

Answer C. Sec 4: postal rule against the proposer.

4A threat to commit suicide to obtain consent amounts to:

Answer B. Chikkam Ammiraju v Seshamma. Sec 15 covers acts forbidden by IPC/BNS.

5Which of the following is an exception to Sec 27 (restraint of trade)?

Answer A. The only exception in the Contract Act is sale of goodwill; restraint during employment is outside Sec 27 by case law.

6The doctrine of frustration is codified in:

Answer B. Sec 56 (supervening impossibility); Satyabrata Ghose.

7Under Sec 74, a party complaining of breach is entitled to:

Answer B. Fateh Chand v Balkishan Das.

8The liability of a surety is:

Answer B. Sec 128. Bank of Bihar v Damodar Prasad.

9General lien under Sec 171 is available to all EXCEPT:

Answer D. Only bankers, factors, wharfingers, attorneys and policy-brokers have a general lien.

10A person who lawfully does something for another, not intending to act gratuitously, and the other enjoys the benefit, is covered by:

Answer C. Sec 70; State of West Bengal v B.K. Mondal.

11No consideration is necessary to create:

Answer B. Sec 185.

12A promise in writing and signed to pay a time-barred debt is:

Answer B. Sec 25(3).

13A suit under Sec 6 of the Specific Relief Act must be filed within:

Answer B. Sec 6(2)(a). No appeal or review lies (Sec 6(3)).

14Which is NOT a ground in Sec 14 SRA (as amended in 2018) on which specific performance cannot be enforced?

Answer D. The 'compensation adequate' ground was deleted in 2018.

15Before getting a contract performed through a third party under Sec 20 SRA, the aggrieved party must give notice of at least:

Answer C. Sec 20(2) as substituted in 2018.

16Readiness and willingness under Sec 16(c) means respectively:

Answer B. Recent Supreme Court judgments, e.g., Mohammed Khaleel v Jayamma (2026).

17Injunction cannot be granted to prevent breach of a contract which:

Answer B. Sec 41(e); Sec 42 is the negative covenant exception.

18In suits relating to infrastructure project contracts, suits must ordinarily be disposed of within:

Answer B. Sec 20C.

19A declaratory decree under Sec 34 is:

Answer B. Sec 34 and proviso; Sec 35 limits binding effect.

20Which of the following can be validly transferred under the TPA?

Answer C. Sec 6 bars the others; actionable claims are transferable under Sec 130.

21A sale of tangible immovable property worth Rs 5,000 can be made only by:

Answer C. Sec 54.

22Foreclosure is available in:

Answer C. Sec 67 with Sec 58(c).

23A mortgage by deposit of title-deeds is valid in:

Answer B. Sec 58(f).

24In the absence of contract, a lease of immovable property for manufacturing purposes is deemed to be a lease:

Answer B. Sec 106.

25Notice to terminate a month-to-month lease (non-agricultural) requires at least:

Answer B. Sec 106 as amended in 2002.

26A gift of immovable property must be made by:

Answer B. Sec 123.

27The doctrine of lis pendens is embodied in:

Answer C. Sec 52 TPA.

28Section 53A of the TPA protects:

Answer B. Shield not sword; contract must be registered for contracts since 2001.

29The right of easement can be acquired by prescription after enjoyment for:

Answer C. Sec 15 Easements Act (30 years if against Government property).

30The time for presenting a document for registration is:

Answer B. Registration Act Sec 23.

31Under Sec 138 NI Act, the demand notice must be sent within:

Answer B. Sec 138(b).

32The drawer must pay within ___ days of receipt of the notice:

Answer B. Sec 138(c).

33Maximum imprisonment for an offence under Sec 138 is:

Answer C. Raised to 2 years by the 2018 Amendment.

34Interim compensation under Sec 143A shall not exceed:

Answer C. Sec 143A(2).

35Under Sec 148, the appellate court may order the appellant to deposit a minimum of:

Answer B. Sec 148 (2018).

36Post-2015, the court having jurisdiction where the cheque is delivered for collection through an account is where:

Answer B. Sec 142(2)(a).

37The presumption under Sec 139 is:

Answer B. Rangappa v Sri Mohan; Bir Singh v Mukesh Kumar.

38Under Sec 141, in a prosecution of a company's directors, it is essential that:

Answer B. Aneeta Hada v Godfather Travels.

39Under Sec 25 NI Act, if the day of maturity is a public holiday the instrument is due on:

Answer B. Sec 25.

40Offences under the NI Act are:

Answer B. Sec 147.
