Torts, Motor Vehicles Act and Consumer Protection

Latest developments

Recent judgments, amendments and institutional changes that are favourite current-affairs questions. Notes cover developments up to 18 Nov 2026; anything tagged Verify should be checked against the official source.

PM-RAHAT cashless treatment of road accident victims

May 2025 - May 2026

The Cashless Treatment of Road Accident Victims Scheme 2025 (notified 5 May 2025; up to Rs 1.5 lakh per victim for seven days) was renamed PM-RAHAT by a February 2026 notification. In SaveLife Foundation v Union of India (26 May 2026) the Supreme Court issued directions for full operationalisation within three months, linked to Sec 162 MV Act (golden hour).

Why it matters: Sec 162; Rs 1.5 lakh; seven days; golden hour.

Interim relief scheme for road accident victims

2026

A Compensation to Victims (Interim Relief) Scheme 2026 was framed under Sec 164A. In August 2026 the Supreme Court expressed surprise at the 'quiet' rollout and directed publicity; the scheme operates through insurers for insured vehicles.

Why it matters: Sec 164A; hit-and-run compensation Rs 2 lakh/ Rs 50,000 under Sec 161.

LMV licence and light transport vehicles

Nov 2024

A Constitution Bench reaffirmed Mukund Dewangan: holders of LMV licences may drive light transport vehicles up to 7,500 kg GVW; insurers cannot deny third-party liability on that ground.

Why it matters: Insurance defences under Sec 149(2); pay and recover.

Consumer commissions: pecuniary jurisdiction

Apr 2025 - Aug 2026

Rutu Mihir Panchal v Union of India (29 April 2025) upheld consideration-based jurisdiction (District up to Rs 50 lakh; State Rs 50 lakh to Rs 2 crore; National above Rs 2 crore). In 2026 the Court flagged anomalies and sought the Centre's response. Advocates' services: Bar of Indian Lawyers v D.K. Gandhi (2024) decision stayed/ referred to a larger Bench.

Why it matters: Pecuniary limits and basis; Sec 2(42) service.

Dark patterns and e-commerce regulation

2020-2024

E-Commerce Rules 2020; Guidelines for Prevention and Regulation of Dark Patterns 2023 (13 patterns); Misleading Advertisement Guidelines 2022; Greenwashing Guidelines 2024. CCPA can recall goods and penalise misleading ads up to Rs 10 lakh (Rs 50 lakh subsequent).

Why it matters: CCPA powers: Secs 18-22.

Bhopal curative petition

Mar 2023

Supreme Court dismissed the curative petition seeking additional compensation for the Bhopal gas disaster victims.

Why it matters: Absolute liability; M.C. Mehta; Union Carbide.
