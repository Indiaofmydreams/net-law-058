Torts, Motor Vehicles Act and Consumer Protection

Quick MCQs

31 recall questions to close each study session. Tap an option to see the answer and the reason. There is no timer and no negative marking.

0/ 31 correct

1Ashby v White is an authority on:

Answer B. Violation of a legal right is actionable even without actual loss.

2The 'neighbour principle' was laid down in:

Answer B. Lord Atkin, 1932.

3The rule of absolute liability was laid down in:

Answer B. Oleum gas leak case; no exceptions; hazardous industries.

4Which of the following is NOT an essential of Rylands v Fletcher liability?

Answer D. Negligence need not be proved in strict liability.

5Remoteness of damage is governed by:

Answer B. The Wagon Mound (No 1) replaced Re Polemis.

6Res ipsa loquitur means:

Answer A. It raises an inference of negligence shifting the evidential burden.

7In Kasturi Lal Ralia Ram Jain v State of UP (1965) the Supreme Court held:

Answer B. Sovereign immunity applied; later cases (Nilabati Behera) diluted it.

8Compensation for violation of Art 21 as a public law remedy was first awarded in:

Answer A. Rudul Sah (1983).

9Publication to a third person is an essential of:

Answer B. Publication to a third person is essential.

10Which is NOT an ingredient of malicious prosecution?

Answer D. Termination must be in the plaintiff's favour.

11A rescuer injured while saving someone is:

Answer B. Haynes v Harwood.

12The Supreme Court held that criminal negligence of doctors requires gross negligence in:

Answer B. Jacob Mathew (2005).

13Good Samaritan protection is provided in:

Answer B. Sec 134A (inserted 2019).

14Compulsory insurance against third-party risk is under:

Answer B. Sec 146.

15No-fault liability for death under Sec 140 (as amended in 2019) is:

Answer B. Rs 50,000 for death; Rs 25,000 for permanent disablement.

16Compensation in hit-and-run cases involving death (from 1 April 2022) is:

Answer D. Sec 161: Rs 2 lakh death; Rs 50,000 grievous hurt.

17An appeal against an award of the Motor Accident Claims Tribunal lies to the High Court within:

Answer C. Sec 173.

18The 'pay and recover' doctrine was laid down in:

Answer B. National Insurance Co v Swaran Singh (2004).

19Future prospects for a deceased with a permanent job aged 38 (Pranay Sethi):

Answer D. 50% below 40; 30% for 40-50; 15% for 50-60.

20Which section bars Civil Courts from entertaining motor accident claims?

Answer D. Sec 175.

21Blood alcohol content beyond which driving is an offence under Sec 185:

Answer B. 30 mg per 100 ml of blood.

22A claimant who opts for Sec 163A:

Answer C. Deepal Girishbhai Soni: election between Sec 163A and Sec 166.

23The Consumer Protection Act 2019 came into force in:

Answer B. 20 July 2020.

24Pecuniary jurisdiction of the District Commission (2021 Rules) is up to:

Answer B. District up to Rs 50 lakh; State Rs 50 lakh-Rs 2 crore; National above Rs 2 crore.

25Pecuniary jurisdiction under the 2019 Act is determined by:

Answer B. Upheld in Rutu Mihir Panchal (2025).

26Limitation for filing a consumer complaint is:

Answer B. Sec 69.

27An appeal from the District Commission to the State Commission requires a deposit of:

Answer C. 50% of the amount ordered (Sec 41).

28Number of consumer rights listed in Sec 2(9):

Answer C. Six rights.

29CCPA can penalise a manufacturer for misleading advertisement up to:

Answer C. Rs 10 lakh (first); up to Rs 50 lakh subsequent (Sec 21).

30Product liability under the CPA 2019 is covered in:

Answer C. Secs 82-87.

31Which is NOT within 'service' under Sec 2(42)?

Answer C. Free services and contracts of personal service are excluded.
