What the examiner tests

  • Torts and Administrative Law are case-name driven: expect 'which case laid down...?' questions (Donoghue v Stevenson, M.C. Mehta, Wednesbury, Maneka Gandhi, A.K. Kraipak, L. Chandra Kumar).
  • MV Act, Consumer Protection and IP are number-driven: limits, periods, fines, terms of protection. Make the cheat sheet your first revision tool.
  • Recent developments are fair game: PM-RAHAT cashless treatment scheme, hit-and-run compensation, consumer pecuniary jurisdiction (Rs 50 lakh/ Rs 2 crore), dark patterns guidelines, RTI-DPDP amendment, bulldozer guidelines, ANI v OpenAI.
  • In the open-book setting, tab: CPA Secs 2, 34-41, 69, 71-72; MV Act Secs 134A, 140, 146-149, 161-166, 173, 175; Copyright Secs 13-17, 19, 22, 52, 57, 63; Patents Secs 3, 25, 53, 84; TM Secs 9, 11, 25, 29, 47; RTI Secs 6-8, 19-20.
  • The IPC/CrPC to BNS/BNSS transition appears in torts (defamation - BNS Sec 356; negligence - BNS Sec 106; nuisance - BNS Sec 270) and MV Act (BNS Sec 106(2), 281).
