≠Pairs the examiner likes

Strict liability vs absolute liability
Strict (Rylands): escape, non-natural use, exceptions available. Absolute (M.C. Mehta): hazardous industry, no exceptions, deep-pocket compensation.
Injuria sine damno vs damnum sine injuria
First: right violated, no loss - actionable. Second: loss without legal wrong - not actionable.
Volenti non fit injuria vs contributory negligence
Volenti is a complete defence; contributory negligence reduces damages.
Libel vs slander
Libel: permanent form, actionable per se; slander: transient, needs special damage (exceptions).
Section 140 vs Section 163A MV Act
Sec 140 no-fault fixed sums; Sec 163A structured formula (Second Schedule); claimant elects between 163A and 166.
Defect vs deficiency (CPA)
Defect relates to goods (2(10)); deficiency relates to services (2(11)).

✓Final revision checklist

  • Can I explain the difference between strict and absolute liability with the cases?
  • Do I remember the Sec 140 / Sec 161 / PM-RAHAT amounts and Pranay Sethi percentages?
  • Can I state the consumer pecuniary limits, limitation, appeal periods and deposit?
  • Can I list the grounds of judicial review and the key case for each (CCSU, Wednesbury, Om Kumar, Maneka Gandhi, Kraipak)?
  • Do I know the RTI timelines and Lokpal composition?
  • Can I give the term of copyright, patent, trade mark, design and GI without looking?
  • Have I tabbed the bare Acts for the open-book look-up?

☰Seven-day study plan

  • Day 1: Torts: nature, defences, negligence, strict and absolute liability
  • Day 2: Torts: vicarious and State liability, nuisance, trespass, defamation, remedies; revise maxims and cases
  • Day 3: Motor Vehicles Act: insurance, no-fault, hit-and-run, claims tribunals, compensation formulas, penalties
  • Day 4: Consumer Protection Act 2019: definitions, CCPA, commissions, jurisdiction, appeals, product liability
  • Day 5: Administrative law I: delegated legislation, judicial review, discretion, natural justice
  • Day 6: Administrative law II: tribunals, writs, public servants, Lokpal, RTI; then IP: copyright and patents
  • Day 7: IP: trade marks, designs, GI; cheat sheet, comparison tables and the practice MCQs
