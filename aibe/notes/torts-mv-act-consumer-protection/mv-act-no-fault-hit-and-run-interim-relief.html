Motor Vehicles Act: Liability without Fault, Hit-and-Run, Structured Formula and Interim Relief (Secs 140-144, 161-164B)

iOverview

No-fault liability (Sec 140), structured formula (Sec 163A), hit-and-run (Sec 161), and the newest schemes (Secs 162, 164A, 164B).

★Must know

  1. Sec 140 LIABILITY TO PAY COMPENSATION IN CERTAIN CASES ON THE PRINCIPLE OF NO FAULT: where death or permanent disablement of any person has resulted from an accident arising out of the use of a motor vehicle or motor vehicles, the owner of the vehicle shall, jointly and severally, be liable to pay compensation in respect of such death or disablement in accordance with the provisions of this section. The amount: Rs 50,000 in respect of the death of any person, and Rs 25,000 in respect of the permanent disablement of any person (as increased by the 2019 Amendment; earlier Rs 25,000/ Rs 12,000). The claimant does not need to plead or establish that the death or permanent disablement was due to any wrongful act, neglect or default of the owner or of any other person (Sec 140(3)). The compensation under Sec 140 is in addition to any other compensation (the amount paid is deducted from the final award - Sec 141). Sec 142: permanent disablement: loss of sight, loss of limbs/ permanent loss of use; Sec 143: applicability to transport vehicles; Sec 144: overriding effect.
  2. Sec 163A SPECIAL PROVISIONS AS TO PAYMENT OF COMPENSATION ON STRUCTURED FORMULA BASIS: notwithstanding anything contained in this Act or any other law, the owner of the motor vehicle or the authorised insurer shall be liable to pay in the case of death or permanent disablement due to accident arising out of the use of motor vehicle, compensation, as indicated in the Second Schedule, to the legal heirs or the victim, as the case may be. It is a no-fault claim: the claimant is not required to plead or establish that the death or disablement was due to any wrongful act or negligence of the owner or driver. Deepal Girishbhai Soni v United India Insurance (2004): Sec 163A is a special provision that overrides Sec 166; a claimant who elects Sec 163A cannot claim Sec 166 (and vice versa: the claimant must elect); the structured formula is a final compensation; in Sec 163A claims, negligence/ contributory negligence is irrelevant. The structured formula is in the Second Schedule (originally for victims with annual income up to Rs 40,000 - verify the current position). In Sec 163A proceedings the defence of contributory negligence is not available.
  3. Sec 161 SPECIAL PROVISIONS AS TO COMPENSATION IN CASE OF HIT AND RUN MOTOR ACCIDENTS: 'hit and run motor accident' means an accident arising out of the use of a motor vehicle or motor vehicles the identity whereof cannot be ascertained in spite of reasonable efforts for the purpose. The Central Government/ Solatium Fund (now Motor Vehicle Accident Fund under Sec 164B) pays: Rs 2,00,000 (2 lakh) in the case of death and Rs 50,000 in the case of grievous hurt (the amounts under the Motor Vehicles (Amendment) Act 2019, brought into force on 1 April 2022 by the Central Motor Vehicles (Compensation of Victims of Hit and Run Motor Accidents) Scheme 2022). Previously Rs 25,000 and Rs 12,500 (Solatium Scheme 1989). Compensation under Sec 161 is not payable if the claim is covered by other schemes.
  4. Sec 162: INSURERS' DUTY TO PROVIDE CASHLESS TREATMENT during the GOLDEN HOUR (the time period lasting one hour from the time of traumatic injury, during which prompt medical treatment has the highest likelihood of preventing death - the definition in the Explanation); the Central Government shall make a scheme for the cashless treatment of victims of road accidents during the golden hour. After the Supreme Court's repeated directions (S. Rajaseekaran v Union of India; January 2025 order of Justices Abhay S. Oka and Ujjal Bhuyan), the Central Government notified the Cashless Treatment of Road Accident Victims Scheme, 2025 on 5 May 2025 (treatment up to Rs 1.5 lakh per victim for a maximum of seven days from the date of the accident). By a notification of 19 February 2026 (S.O. 951(E)) the scheme has been renamed the 'Prime Minister - Road Accident Victims' Hospitalisation and Assured Treatment (PM-RAHAT) Scheme'. In SaveLife Foundation v Union of India (26 May 2026) the Supreme Court directed the full operationalisation of PM-RAHAT within three months along with other emergency care directions.
  5. Sec 164A: SCHEME FOR INTERIM RELIEF to victims: the Central Government may make a scheme for interim relief to the victims of road accidents (pending final compensation). Sec 164A came into force on 1 April 2022 but the scheme was not framed for a long time (the Supreme Court criticised the delay). The Central Government has now framed the Compensation to Victims (Interim Relief) Scheme, 2026; in August 2026 the Supreme Court expressed surprise at its 'quiet' rollout (called it an 'unannounced law') and directed wide publicity; the amicus curiae pointed out that the scheme applies only to insured vehicles where the insurance company pays. Sec 164B: MOTOR VEHICLE ACCIDENT FUND - constituted by the Central Government (replacing the Solatium Fund of Sec 161(3) scheme); utilised for (a) treatment of persons injured in road accidents in accordance with the Sec 162 scheme, (b) compensation to representatives of deceased persons in hit-and-run cases, (c) compensation to grievously hurt persons in hit-and-run cases, (d) compensation to victims of accidents where the Sec 140 conditions apply/ for interim relief, (e) other purposes as prescribed. Central Motor Vehicles (Motor Vehicle Accident Fund) Rules 2022; the Fund is financed by a cess/ contributions (including from insurers and a percentage of premium) and Government grants.

!Exam traps

  • TrapNo-fault liability (Sec 140): Rs 50,000 (death), Rs 25,000 (permanent disablement) - NOT for simple injuries.
  • TrapHit-and-run: Rs 2 lakh (death), Rs 50,000 (grievous hurt) from 1 April 2022.
  • TrapSec 163A claimants cannot also claim under Sec 166.
  • TrapCashless treatment: Rs 1.5 lakh for seven days (PM-RAHAT).

?Likely questions

  1. Compensation for death in hit-and-run cases: Rs 2 lakh.
  2. No-fault liability: Sec 140 - Rs 50,000 on death.
  3. Golden hour cashless treatment: Sec 162.
  4. Motor Vehicle Accident Fund: Sec 164B.
