Central Consumer Protection Authority (CCPA), Misleading Advertisements and Penalties

  3. Exam traps

iOverview

New institution under the 2019 Act: class action regulator with powers to recall goods, order refunds and penalise misleading advertisements.

★Must know

  1. Sec 10: the Central Government shall establish the CENTRAL CONSUMER PROTECTION AUTHORITY (CCPA) to regulate matters relating to violation of rights of consumers, unfair trade practices and false or misleading advertisements which are prejudicial to the interests of public and consumers, and to promote, protect and enforce the rights of consumers as a class. Composition: Chief Commissioner and other Commissioners; investigation wing headed by a Director-General (Sec 15). Sec 18: powers and functions - inquire or cause to be inquired or investigated into violations of consumer rights; file complaints before the appropriate Commission; intervene; review matters relating to safety; issue safety notices; advise on consumer welfare; issue guidelines for prevention of unfair trade practices. Sec 19: power to refer matter for investigation to Director-General; Sec 20: POWER TO RECALL goods and withdraw services, reimburse the prices to purchasers, and discontinue practices which are unfair and prejudicial to consumers' interest; Sec 21: POWER TO ISSUE DIRECTIONS AND PENALTIES AGAINST FALSE OR MISLEADING ADVERTISEMENTS - the CCPA may direct the trader/ manufacturer/ endorser/ advertiser/ publisher to discontinue or modify the advertisement; impose a penalty on a manufacturer or an endorser of up to Rs 10 lakh, and for subsequent contravention up to Rs 50 lakh; prohibit the endorser from making endorsement of any product or service for a period of up to one year, and for subsequent contravention up to three years. Sec 21(4)-(5): an endorser shall not be liable if he exercised due diligence to verify the veracity of the claims made in the advertisement. Sec 22: search and seizure; Sec 24: appeal against CCPA orders to the National Commission within 30 days. Sec 17: complaints to the CCPA.
  2. Sec 88: PENALTY for non-compliance of directions of the Central Authority - imprisonment up to six months or fine up to Rs 20 lakh or both. Sec 89: PUNISHMENT FOR FALSE OR MISLEADING ADVERTISEMENT - imprisonment up to two years and fine up to Rs 10 lakh; for every subsequent offence - imprisonment up to five years and fine up to Rs 50 lakh. Sec 90: punishment for manufacturing for sale or storing, selling or distributing or importing products containing adulterant - imprisonment up to six months and fine up to Rs 1 lakh (where no injury); injury - up to one year and Rs 3 lakh; grievous hurt - up to seven years and Rs 5 lakh; death - not less than 7 years extendable to life and fine not less than Rs 10 lakh. Sec 91: punishment for manufacturing for sale or storing, selling or distributing or importing spurious goods - similar graded punishment. (Figures are indicative - verify the bare Act.) Sec 92: cognizance of offences by courts (Magistrate).
  3. SUBORDINATE INSTRUMENTS (HOT): Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules 2020 - apply to all goods and services bought or sold over digital or electronic network, including digital products; to all models of e-commerce (marketplace e-commerce entity and inventory e-commerce entity), to e-commerce retail, and to all forms of unfair trade practices across all models; duties: appoint a grievance officer (acknowledge within 48 hours; redress within one month), no unfair prices (cannot manipulate price), no discrimination, display of seller details, country of origin, return/ refund/ warranty information; no cancellation charges unless similar charges are borne by the e-commerce entity; no flash sales that manipulate the market (Rule 5). Consumer Protection (Direct Selling) Rules 2021. Guidelines for Prevention and Regulation of Dark Patterns 2023 (notified 30 November 2023; 13 specified dark patterns: false urgency, basket sneaking, confirm shaming, forced action, subscription trap, interface interference, bait and switch, drip pricing, disguised advertisements, nagging, trick question, SaaS billing, rogue malwares). Guidelines for Prevention of Misleading Advertisements and Endorsements for Misleading Advertisements 2022 (9 June 2022; conditions for valid advertisements; due diligence; duties of manufacturers/ service providers, advertisers and advertising agencies; surrogate and bait advertisements; free claims; endorsements by celebrities require due diligence and disclosures). Guidelines for Prevention and Regulation of Greenwashing or Misleading Environmental Claims 2024.

!Exam traps

  • TrapSec 21 penalty: Rs 10 lakh (first), Rs 50 lakh (subsequent); endorser ban 1 year (first) / 3 years (subsequent).
  • TrapSec 89 imprisonment: 2 years (first), 5 years (subsequent).
  • TrapCCPA can recall goods and order refunds (Sec 20) but doesn't adjudicate individual compensation claims (that is for Commissions).

?Likely questions

  1. CCPA is established under Sec 10.
  2. Penalty for misleading advertisement by manufacturer/ endorser: up to Rs 10 lakh.
  3. Number of dark patterns specified in the 2023 Guidelines: 13.
