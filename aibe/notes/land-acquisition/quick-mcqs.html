Quick MCQs

9 recall questions to close each study session. Tap an option to see the answer and the reason. There is no timer and no negative marking.

0/ 9 correct

1Right to property is a:

Answer C. 44th Amendment.

2The RFCTLARR Act 2013 came into force on:

Answer B. Enacted 2013, effective 1 January 2014.

3For acquisition of land for private companies, consent of at least ___ of affected families is required:

Answer C. Sec 2(2); 70% for PPP projects.

4Under the 2013 Act, solatium is:

Answer C. Sec 30.

5Under Sec 24(2) of the 2013 Act, lapse of acquisition occurs where:

Answer B. Indore Development Authority v Manoharlal (2020).

6Land acquired under the 2013 Act and unutilised for ___ years must be returned:

Answer C. Sec 101.

7Under the 1894 Act, objections were heard under:

Answer B. Sec 5A.

8Union of India v Tarsem Singh (2019) held that:

Answer B. Sec 3J of the NH Act was struck down to that extent.

9In Kolkata Municipal Corporation v Bimal Kumar Shaw (2024), the Supreme Court identified how many sub-rights under Art 300A?

Answer C. Notice, hearing, reasoned decision, public purpose, compensation, efficiency, conclusion.
