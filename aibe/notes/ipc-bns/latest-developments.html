Indian Penal Code / Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita

Latest developments

Recent judgments, amendments and institutional changes that are favourite current-affairs questions. Notes cover developments up to 19 Nov 2026; anything tagged Verify should be checked against the official source.

BNS, BNSS and BSA came into force on 1 July 2024

2024

BNS, BNSS and BSA came into force on 1 July 2024; offences before that date remain governed by the IPC.

Why it matters: Temporal application; mapping of sections.

Hit-and-run provision (BNS 106(2)) triggered nationwide transporter protests in January 2024

2024

Hit-and-run provision (BNS 106(2)) triggered nationwide transporter protests in January 2024; the Government said it would be implemented only after consultation, so its operation was held in abeyance.

Why it matters: Statement-based question on s.106(2) and its punishment of up to 10 years.

Just Rights for Children Alliance v S. Harish (Supreme Court)

2024

Just Rights for Children Alliance v S. Harish (Supreme Court): storing or viewing child pornography is an offence under POCSO s.15 and IT Act s.67B; the Court recommended replacing 'child pornography' with 'child sexual exploitative and abuse material (CSEAM)'.

Why it matters: POCSO scope; terminology.

Delhi Race Club (1940) Ltd v State of UP

2024

Supreme Court on the distinction between cheating and criminal breach of trust and against criminalising civil disputes.

Why it matters: CBT vs cheating.

Supreme Court in Dara Lakshmi Narayana v State of Telangana and similar cases cautioned against omnibus allega

2024

Supreme Court in Dara Lakshmi Narayana v State of Telangana and similar cases cautioned against omnibus allegations in dowry and cruelty complaints against the husband's relatives.

Why it matters: 498A/BNS 85 misuse; quashing standards.

Mohd. Abdul Samad v State of Telangana: a divorced Muslim woman can claim maintenance under s.125 CrPC (BNSS s

2024

Mohd. Abdul Samad v State of Telangana: a divorced Muslim woman can claim maintenance under s.125 CrPC (BNSS s.144) in addition to the 1986 Act remedy.

Why it matters: Maintenance law overlaps with family law part.

Supreme Court on demolition of properties of accused persons ('bulldozer justice')

2024

guidelines issued in November 2024 requiring notice and an opportunity to respond before demolition.

Why it matters: Rule of law, Art. 21 and criminal process.

Supriyo v Union of India

2023

no fundamental right to same-sex marriage; the Court recorded the Government's commitment to a committee to examine the rights of queer couples.

Why it matters: Marriage law, Navtej Johar background.

Competition (Amendment) Act, 2023

2023

deal value threshold, settlement and commitments.

Why it matters: Company law add-on.

Imran Pratapgadhi v State of Gujarat (Supreme Court)

2025

the Court quashed an FIR over a poem posted on social media and reiterated that speech must be judged by the standard of a reasonable, strong-minded person; free speech and the application of ss.196/197/299 BNS.

Why it matters: Hate speech, Art. 19(1)(a), Amish Devgan test Verify.

Pending: constitutional challenge to the marital rape exception

2025

Pending: constitutional challenge to the marital rape exception; challenges to BNS s.152; challenge on the validity of BNS provisions on organised crime and mob lynching (various High Court petitions).

Why it matters: Which of the following is pending before the Supreme Court? Verify.

Tehseen Poonawalla guidelines on mob lynching, the basis for BNS 103(2)

2018

Tehseen Poonawalla guidelines on mob lynching, the basis for BNS 103(2).

Why it matters: Origin of mob lynching offence.

Manoj v State of MP on mitigation material in death penalty sentencing

2022

Manoj v State of MP on mitigation material in death penalty sentencing; S.G. Vombatkere v UoI on keeping IPC 124A in abeyance.

Why it matters: Death penalty and sedition.
