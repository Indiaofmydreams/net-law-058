iOverview

Territorial operation: BNS s.1(4)-(5) (IPC ss.2-4)

★Must know

  1. Territorial operation: BNS s.1(4)-(5) (IPC ss.2-4): every person is liable for acts or omissions in India; extra-territorial reach covers Indian citizens abroad, offences on ships or aircraft registered in India, and offences targeting a computer resource located in India.
  2. Check the exact wording of BNS s.1(4)-(5) in the bare act for the extra-territorial provisions Verify.
  3. Crime = Actus reus + Mens rea + Injury (harm) + Human being. Maxim: actus non facit reum nisi mens sit rea. Statutory offences may impose strict liability (State of Maharashtra v MH George, 1965).
  4. Four stages: Intention, Preparation, Attempt, Accomplishment. Preparation is generally not punishable, except for specific offences (waging war, dacoity preparation, counterfeiting, possession of counterfeit instruments).
  5. Definitions (BNS s.2): 'child' means under 18 years; 'gender' includes male, female and transgender; 'dishonestly' = intention of causing wrongful gain or wrongful loss; 'fraudulently' = intent to defraud; 'reason to believe'; 'voluntarily'; 'document' now expressly includes electronic and digital records; 'movable property'; 'valuable security'; 'counterfeit'.
  6. Wrongful gain/loss means gain/loss by unlawful means of property to which the gainer is not legally entitled (retaining or keeping out).
  7. Act (s.2) includes illegal omission; 'Illegal' covers everything that is an offence or prohibited by law or gives ground for civil action.
  8. General explanations (BNS s.3): s.3(5) common intention (IPC 34); s.3(6) criminal act by several persons; s.3(7) co-operation by doing one of several acts; s.3(8) effect caused partly by act and partly by omission; s.3(9) act done by several persons.
  9. Sentences and Chapter II: s.4 lists punishments: death, life imprisonment, imprisonment (rigorous or simple), forfeiture of property, fine and community service (new). s.5 commutation (death to life or not less than 7 years; life to not less than 7 years). s.6 life imprisonment treated as 20 years for fractions of terms. Solitary confinement limits in ss.11-12 (not exceeding 14 days at a time, not more than 3 months overall in the scale).
  10. Community service is a punishment in only six provisions: ss.202 (public servant unlawfully engaging in trade), 209 (non-attendance in obedience to summons), 226 (attempt to commit suicide to compel or restrain public servant), 303(2) (petty theft, first offender, value under Rs 5,000, property returned), 355 (drunken misconduct in public), 356(2) (defamation).

⚖Landmark cases

State of Maharashtra v M.H. George

1965

Mens rea is not required where a statute (FERA/RBI notification) imposes strict liability; but mens rea is presumed unless excluded by clear words.

Nathulal v State of MP

1966

Mens rea is the essence of a crime; for storing foodgrains without licence, honest belief in compliance mattered.

Aher Raja Khima v State of Saurashtra

1955

Even where mens rea is a requirement, 'knowledge' includes the capacity to foresee consequences.

Abhayanand Mishra v State of Bihar

1961

Preparation ends and attempt begins when the accused commits the first overt act towards the offence (false representation in an application).

!Exam traps

  • TrapPreparation is not punishable unless the Code says so; the first positive step towards the offence is attempt.
  • TrapA child is anyone under 18 under BNS, but the doli incapax age for criminal responsibility is still 7 (and 7-12 with immaturity).
  • Trap'Community service' appears only six times: do not select it as a punishment for ordinary theft or hurt.
