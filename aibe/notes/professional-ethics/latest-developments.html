Professional Ethics and Professional Misconduct

Latest developments

Recent judgments, amendments and institutional changes that are favourite current-affairs questions. Notes cover developments up to 19 Nov 2026; anything tagged Verify should be checked against the official source.

Advocate-client privilege: In re Summoning Advocates

31 Oct 2025 (2025 INSC 1275)

No summons to advocates except within the s.132 BSA exceptions; prior approval of a superior officer; digital devices only through court; in-house counsel not covered by s.132 Verify · details.

Why it matters: Likely ethics question; links BSA, BNSS.

Likely question: Which section of the BSA protects advocate-client communications?

Answer: s.132

Advocates not 'service providers' under Consumer Protection law

May 2024 (Bar of Indian Lawyers v. D.K. Gandhi PS Rao)

Supreme Court held that advocates' services are not covered by the Consumer Protection Act Verify.

Why it matters: Remedy against advocates.

Likely question: Can a client file a consumer complaint against an advocate?

Answer: No

Enrolment fee cap and AIBE

Gaurav Kumar v. Union of India (30 Jul 2024); AIBE XXI syllabus dated 2 Mar 2026

State Bar Councils cannot charge enrolment fees above the statutory fee; the AIBE remains a mandatory post-enrolment test; the BCI notified the AIBE-XXI syllabus on 2 March 2026.

Why it matters: Directly relevant to aspirants.

Likely question: Which Act fixes the enrolment fee?

Answer: Advocates Act s.24

Foreign lawyers and Advocates (Amendment) Bill

BCI Rules 2022 (amended 2025); draft Bill Feb 2025 withdrawn Verify

Foreign lawyers/ firms may register to practise foreign law and international arbitration on a reciprocal basis, not litigation; the draft Advocates (Amendment) Bill 2025 was withdrawn after protests.

Why it matters: Practice rules.

Likely question: Can a foreign law firm practise litigation in India?

Answer: No
