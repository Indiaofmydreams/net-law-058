Professional Ethics and Professional Misconduct

Quick MCQs

29 recall questions to close each study session. Tap an option to see the answer and the reason. There is no timer and no negative marking.

0/ 29 correct

1The Advocates Act, 1961 recognises:

Answer B. s.29.

2Senior advocates are designated under:

Answer A. s.16 Advocates Act.

3Right of pre-audience is under:

Answer B. s.23 - AG first.

4Who has the first right of pre-audience?

Answer B. Attorney-General of India.

5The right to practise throughout India is conferred by:

Answer B. s.30.

6Professional or other misconduct is punished under:

Answer B. s.35.

7Punishments under s.35 include all EXCEPT:

Answer D. Imprisonment is not a s.35 penalty (but see s.45 for illegal practice).

8An appeal from the State Bar Council Disciplinary Committee lies to the BCI within:

Answer B. s.37.

9A further appeal lies to the Supreme Court within:

Answer B. s.38.

10A State Bar Council's Disciplinary Committee must dispose of a complaint within:

Answer C. s.36B; else transferred to the BCI.

11Which Rule bars an advocate from soliciting work or advertising?

Answer C. Rule 36.

12An advocate stipulating for a fee contingent on the result of litigation is guilty of:

Answer B. Rule 20 prohibits contingent fee (champerty).

13An advocate may appear before a judge who is his close relative:

Answer B. BCI Rule 6.

14An advocate has a lien over the client's papers for unpaid fees:

Answer B. No lien; withholding papers is misconduct.

15A full-time salaried employee who continues in that employment:

Answer B. Rule 49.

16An advocate can be a company director but not its:

Answer B. Rule 48.

17Lawyers' right to strike was rejected in:

Answer A. Harish Uppal (2002).

18The High Court cannot, in contempt proceedings, debar an advocate from practice - held in:

Answer A. Only the Bar Council can; the court may refer.

19Maximum punishment for contempt under the Contempt of Courts Act 1971:

Answer B. s.12.

20Limitation to initiate contempt proceedings is:

Answer B. s.20 Contempt of Courts Act.

21Truth as a defence in contempt was introduced in:

Answer C. s.13(b) by the 2006 Amendment.

22Advocate-client privilege under the BSA is in:

Answer A. s.132 BSA (s.126 Evidence Act).

23Under In re Summoning Advocates (2025), investigating agencies may summon an advocate for client communications only:

Answer B. 2025 INSC 1275.

24Which case concerned foreign law firms practising in India?

Answer A. A.K. Balaji (2018).

25Which case held that State Bar Councils cannot charge enrolment fees beyond the statutory fee?

Answer A. 30 July 2024.

26The BCI Rules on standards of professional conduct are framed under:

Answer B. s.49(1)(c) Advocates Act.

27A senior advocate may:

Answer C. BCI Rules Chapter I restrict seniors.

28The State Bar Council with an electorate above 10,000 advocates has how many elected members (besides the Advocate-General)?

Answer C. 15/20/25 by size.

29Which service is outside the Consumer Protection Act per the Supreme Court (2024)?

Answer A. Bar of Indian Lawyers v. D.K. Gandhi PS Rao (2024) Verify.
