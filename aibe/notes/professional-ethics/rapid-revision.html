⚡One-line case revision

  • Harish Uppal (2002) - no right of lawyers to strike.
  • Pravin C. Shah (2001) - HC cannot debar an advocate; Bar Council can.
  • R.D. Saxena (2000) - no advocate's lien.
  • R.K. Anand (2009) - senior advocate suspended.
  • In re Summoning Advocates (2025) - privilege vs investigating agencies.
  • A.K. Balaji (2018) - foreign law firms.

✦Mnemonics

  • Ethics duties: Court-Client-Opponent-Colleagues; no contingent fee (20), no advertising (36), no business (47).
