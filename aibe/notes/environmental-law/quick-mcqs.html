Quick MCQs

11 recall questions to close each study session. Tap an option to see the answer and the reason. There is no timer and no negative marking.

0/ 11 correct

1Articles 48A and 51A(g) were inserted into the Constitution by the:

Answer B. The 42nd Amendment (1976) also moved forests and wild animals/birds to the Concurrent List.

2The rule of absolute liability for hazardous industries was laid down in:

Answer B. M.C. Mehta (1987) - no Rylands v. Fletcher exceptions.

3The public trust doctrine was applied in India in:

Answer A. Kamal Nath (1997) - Span Motel.

4Precautionary principle and polluter pays were treated as part of Indian environmental law in:

Answer A. Vellore (1996).

5Which Act is NOT within Schedule I of the NGT Act, 2010?

Answer C. Wildlife (Protection) Act 1972, Indian Forest Act 1927 and Forest Rights Act 2006 are outside Schedule I.

6An appeal from an order of the NGT lies to the Supreme Court within:

Answer C. S.22 NGT Act.

7Which Article of the Constitution enables Parliament to enact legislation to implement international agreements?

Answer C. Art 253 - basis of EPA 1986.

8As amended in 2022, the Wild Life (Protection) Act, 1972 has how many schedules?

Answer C. Reduced from six to four; vermin schedule removed.

9The Forest (Conservation) Act, 1980 was renamed in 2023 as:

Answer B. By the 2023 amendment.

10The Supreme Court in M.K. Ranjitsinh v. Union of India (2024) recognised the right against adverse effects of climate change under:

Answer A. Great Indian Bustard case.

11The Montreal Protocol deals with:

Answer A. Montreal Protocol (1987). Basel - wastes; Minamata - mercury; Ramsar - wetlands.
