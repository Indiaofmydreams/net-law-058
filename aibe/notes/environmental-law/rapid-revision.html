iBackground and transition

Summary
With only 2 questions, Environmental Law is a high-yield, low-volume section. Examiners lean on (i) constitutional hooks (Art 21, 48A, 51A(g), 253), (ii) judicially evolved principles with landmark cases, (iii) the NGT Act, EPA 1986, Water Act 1974 and Air Act 1981 and (iv) recent headline judgments. A candidate who masters the principle-to-case matching table below can secure both marks.
Constitutional map
Art 48A (DPSP: State shall protect and improve the environment and safeguard forests and wildlife) and Art 51A(g) (fundamental duty to protect and improve the natural environment) were inserted by the 42nd Amendment (1976), which also moved forests and wild animals/birds to the Concurrent List (Entries 17A and 17B). Art 253 empowers Parliament to legislate to implement international agreements - the basis of EPA 1986, Water Act and Air Act (Stockholm 1972). Art 21 includes the right to a clean and healthy environment; Art 14 and 21 together include the right against the adverse effects of climate change (M.K. Ranjitsinh, 2024). Art 32/226 are the writ routes for PIL; Art 142 is used by the Supreme Court to issue continuing mandamus.

⚡Rapid-fire one-liners

  • Art 48A (DPSP) and Art 51A(g) (Fundamental Duty) - inserted by 42nd Amendment, 1976.
  • Art 253 - basis of EPA 1986; Stockholm 1972 inspired it.
  • Oleum Gas (1987) - absolute liability; Vellore (1996) - precaution + polluter pays + sustainable development; Kamal Nath (1997) - public trust; Bichhri (1996) - polluter pays.
  • Doon Valley (1985) - first ecological case; Subhash Kumar (1991) - clean air/water under Art 21.
  • NGT: Principal Bench Delhi; Bhopal, Pune, Kolkata, Chennai; appeal to Supreme Court within 90 days; 6 months to file (+60 days).
  • NGT Schedule I: 7 Acts; Wildlife Act NOT included.
  • Water Act S.25: consent to establish; Air Act S.21: consent to establish.
  • Air Act: noise is an air pollutant.
  • EIA 2006: Category A (Centre) and B (SEIAA).
  • Wildlife Act 2022: four schedules; vermin schedule removed.
  • FCA 1980 renamed Van (Sanrakshan Evam Samvardhan) Adhiniyam in 2023.
  • National Biodiversity Authority is at Chennai.
  • Paris Agreement: 1.5 degrees C; India: net zero by 2070.
  • Kyoto: binding targets; Montreal: ozone; Basel: hazardous waste.
  • M.K. Ranjitsinh (2024): right against adverse effects of climate change.

✓Sources and verification

  • Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023 (bare Act text) - verify section numbers against the official Gazette copy.
  • Pune Bar Association v. Union of India, W.P.(C) 599 of 2026, decided 22 May 2026 (Supreme Court) - as reported by LiveLaw, Verdictum and JSA Prism (June 2026).
  • AIBE XXI pattern and date (7 June 2026): Bar Council of India / allindiabarexamination.com and Careers360 reports (2026).
  • Vanashakti litigation: Supreme Court judgment of 29 July 2026 as reported by Down To Earth and others - read the judgment text before relying on details.
  • M.K. Ranjitsinh v. Union of India (2024), Vellore Citizens (1996), M.C. Mehta (1987, 1997) - reported judgments.
