iOverview

Four statutes and one tribal-rights law dominate. Recent amendments have made this a current-affairs hotspot.

★Must know

  1. Indian Forest Act, 1927: classifies reserved forest (S.20), village forest (S.28) and protected forest (S.29).
  2. Forest (Conservation) Act, 1980 - renamed Van (Sanrakshan Evam Samvardhan) Adhiniyam, 1980 by the 2023 amendment: prior approval of the Central Government is required for de-reservation of forest, use of forest land for non-forest purposes, assignment by lease to private persons, and clearing of naturally grown trees for reafforestation (S.2). The 2023 amendment limits the Act to land declared/notified as forest or recorded as forest on or after 25 October 1980, and exempts certain categories: forest land along a rail line or public road up to 0.10 hectare, land within 100 km of international borders/LoC/LAC for strategic linear projects up to 10 hectares, security-related infrastructure up to 5 hectares in LWE areas, and eco-tourism, zoos and safaris. The Supreme Court (Godavarman line, interim directions in Ashok Kumar Sharma v. Union of India, 2024) directed that the broad dictionary meaning of 'forest' continues to apply until further orders.
  3. Wild Life (Protection) Act, 1972 (as amended in 2022): reduces schedules from six to four (Schedule I and II - animals with highest/high protection; Schedule III - plants; Schedule IV - species listed in CITES Appendices); removes the 'vermin' schedule and allows the Centre to declare vermin; creates a Standing Committee of the State Board; implements CITES; regulates invasive alien species; allows voluntary surrender of captive animals. Protected areas: national parks (S.35), sanctuaries (S.18), conservation reserves (S.36A) and community reserves (S.36C). Tiger reserves under Chapter IVB (NTCA). Hunting prohibited by S.9.
  4. Biological Diversity Act, 2002: implements the CBD (1992) and Nagoya Protocol (2010); three-tier structure: National Biodiversity Authority (Chennai), State Biodiversity Boards, Biodiversity Management Committees (local bodies); regulates access to biological resources by foreign nationals, NRIs and foreign companies; benefit sharing; People's Biodiversity Registers. Biological Diversity (Amendment) Act 2023 decriminalised offences and exempted AYUSH practitioners and codified traditional knowledge from certain requirements.
  5. Scheduled Tribes and Other Traditional Forest Dwellers (Recognition of Forest Rights) Act, 2006: recognises individual and community forest rights; Gram Sabha is the initiating authority; right to protect, regenerate and manage community forest resources; free, prior and informed consent in forest diversion (Niyamgiri).
  6. Compensatory Afforestation Fund Act, 2016 (CAMPA): funds collected for forest diversion are used for afforestation.
  7. Project Tiger (1973), Project Elephant (1992), National Tiger Conservation Authority (NTCA, 2006), Wildlife Crime Control Bureau (2006).

⚖Landmark cases

T.N. Godavarman Thirumulpad v. Union of India

1996 onwards

Broad dictionary meaning of 'forest'; continuing mandamus; all felling banned without Central approval.

Orissa Mining Corporation v. Ministry of Environment and Forests (Niyamgiri)

2013

Gram Sabhas must decide on religious/cultural claims affected by mining diversion.

Centre for Environmental Law, WWF-I v. Union of India

2013

Protection of Asiatic lions; translocation (Kuno) approved.

M.K. Ranjitsinh v. Union of India

2024

Right against adverse effects of climate change; balance of conservation of Great Indian Bustard with renewable energy.

✎Memory aids

  • WPA 2022: 6 -> 4 schedules; vermin out; CITES in.

!Exam traps

  • TrapAfter the 2022 amendment the Wildlife Act has four schedules, not six.
  • TrapNBA is headquartered at Chennai.

?Likely questions

  1. Which law renamed in 2023 as Van (Sanrakshan Evam Samvardhan) Adhiniyam?
  2. How many schedules in the Wildlife Act after 2022?
  3. Three-tier structure under the Biological Diversity Act.
  4. Who decides forest rights claims? (Gram Sabha as initiating authority)
