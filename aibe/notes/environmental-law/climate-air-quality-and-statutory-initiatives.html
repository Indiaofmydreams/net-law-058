iOverview

Newer legal instruments tested as current affairs.

★Must know

  1. Commission for Air Quality Management in National Capital Region and Adjoining Areas Act, 2021 (CAQM): statutory body that replaced EPCA; powers to issue directions on air pollution in the NCR; GRAP (Graded Response Action Plan) is implemented under it.
  2. National Clean Air Programme (NCAP), 2019: target to reduce PM2.5 and PM10 concentration in 131 non-attainment cities.
  3. Energy Conservation (Amendment) Act, 2022: enables a carbon credit trading scheme; Carbon Credit Trading Scheme notified in 2023; Bureau of Energy Efficiency as administrator.
  4. Green Credit Programme, 2023: tradeable green credits for environmentally positive actions (tree plantation, water conservation).
  5. Public Liability Insurance Act, 1991: no-fault liability of owners handling hazardous substances; mandatory insurance; Environmental Relief Fund.
  6. Mission LiFE (Lifestyle for Environment) launched in 2022.

!Exam traps

  • TrapCAQM replaced EPCA (Environment Pollution (Prevention and Control) Authority).
  • TrapPLI Act creates no-fault liability, not fault-based.

?Likely questions

  1. Which statute created the CAQM?
  2. No-fault liability under which statute?
