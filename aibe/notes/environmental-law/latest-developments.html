Environmental Law

Latest developments

Recent judgments, amendments and institutional changes that are favourite current-affairs questions. Notes cover developments up to 17 Nov 2026; anything tagged Verify should be checked against the official source.

Vanashakti line of cases on ex post facto environmental clearance

29 July 2026

the Supreme Court (reported as Vanashakti III) struck down the Centre's 2021 Office Memorandum permitting retrospective environmental clearance for projects that began without prior EC and reiterated that the right to a clean environment is part of Art 21 and that prior EC is the rule. Earlier phases of the litigation date back to 2025. Verify the holding on the relief given to existing projects before relying on this in an answer.

Why it matters: High-probability current-affairs MCQ: 'prior environmental clearance', 'ex post facto EC', 'Art 21'.

M.K. Ranjitsinh v. Union of India: Supreme Court recognised a right against the adverse effects of climate cha

21 March 2024

M.K. Ranjitsinh v. Union of India: Supreme Court recognised a right against the adverse effects of climate change as part of Arts 14 and 21.

Why it matters: Most-cited climate-rights case; likely MCQ.

Jan Vishwas (Amendment of Provisions) Act, 2023 and the Water (Prevention and Control of Pollution) Amendment

2023-2024

Jan Vishwas (Amendment of Provisions) Act, 2023 and the Water (Prevention and Control of Pollution) Amendment Act, 2024 decriminalised many environmental offences (replacement of imprisonment with penalties through adjudicating officers).

Why it matters: Law-reform MCQ on 'decriminalisation'.

Van (Sanrakshan Evam Samvardhan) Adhiniyam - Forest (Conservation) Amendment Act, 2023

2023

Van (Sanrakshan Evam Samvardhan) Adhiniyam - Forest (Conservation) Amendment Act, 2023; Biological Diversity (Amendment) Act, 2023; Constitution Bench in Animal Welfare Board v. Union of India upheld Jallikattu amendments (May 2023).

Why it matters: Statutory renaming and exemptions are favourite factual questions.

ICJ climate advisory opinion (23 July 2025)

July 2025 / January 2026

ICJ climate advisory opinion (23 July 2025); BBNJ (High Seas Treaty) in force 17 January 2026.

Why it matters: International environmental law current affairs.

November 2026

COP31 (Antalya, Turkiye) is under way; check the final outcome before the exam.
