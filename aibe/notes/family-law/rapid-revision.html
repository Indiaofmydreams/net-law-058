iBackground and transition

Summary
Family Law in India is personal-law based. Hindus (including Buddhists, Jains, Sikhs) are governed by codified statutes of 1955-56 (HMA, HSA, HMGA, HAMA); Muslims by uncodified Shariat plus the Shariat Application Act 1937, DMMA 1939, the 1986 Act and the 2019 Act; Christians by the Indian Christian Marriage Act 1872, Divorce Act 1869 and Indian Succession Act 1925; Parsis by the 1936 Act; inter-faith couples by the Special Marriage Act 1954. Secular overlays include S.144 BNSS (maintenance), DV Act 2005, Dowry Prohibition Act 1961, PCMA 2006, JJ Act 2015 and the Family Courts Act 1984. The 2023-2026 trend is towards gender-just reading (Shayara Bano, Abdul Samad, X v Y, Vineeta Sharma), personal-law reform (state UCCs, Waqf amendment) and procedural reform (Shilpa Sailesh, Rajnesh v Neha).
Approach
For each personal law, learn (1) the governing Act, (2) marriage conditions, (3) divorce modes and periods, (4) maintenance, (5) guardianship/adoption, (6) succession. Then compare across laws using the personal_law_comparison table.

#Numbers cheat sheet

Marriage age
Hindu/SMA/PCMA: groom 21, bride 18. Muslim: puberty (15 presumed) under classical law, but PCMA 2006 prohibits child marriage (conflict in courts).
Hma divorce periods
Desertion 2 years; not heard of 7 years; no divorce petition within 1 year of marriage (S.14); mutual consent: 1 year separation before petition, 6-month wait (waivable), maximum 18 months; wife's puberty repudiation: married before 15, repudiate before 18.
Sapinda
3 generations on mother's side, 5 on father's side (S.3(f) HMA).
Hama adoption
Adoptee under 15 unless custom; 21-year gap for opposite sex; adopter must not have Hindu son/son's son/son's son's son (for son) or daughter/son's daughter (for daughter).
Hmga
Minor = under 18; custody of child under 5 ordinarily with mother; guardian's lease beyond 5 years needs court permission.
Muslim
Iddat: 3 menstrual courses/3 months (divorce), 4 months 10 days (widow), till delivery if pregnant. Will: 1/3 without heirs' consent. Hanafi minimum mahr: 10 dirhams. Pre-emption: 3 demands.
Dmma 1939 s2
Whereabouts unknown 4 years; no maintenance 2 years; husband's imprisonment 7 years; failure of marital obligations 3 years; insanity 2 years; puberty option: married before 15, repudiate before 18 (not consummated).
Dowry
S.3 Dowry Act: min 5 years + fine min Rs 15,000 or value of dowry; S.4 demand: 6 months-2 years; dowry death: 7 years to life; S.498A/BNS 85: up to 3 years + fine; dowry to be handed to woman within 3 months (S.6).
Dv act
Disposal in 60 days; breach of protection order: up to 1 year or fine up to Rs 20,000 or both; appeal to Sessions Court in 30 days.
Pcma 2006
Annulment petition within 2 years of attaining majority; adult male marrying child: up to 2 years RI or fine up to Rs 1 lakh; offences cognizable and non-bailable.
Sma 1954
30-day public notice; 30 days for objections; marry within 3 months of notice; mutual consent divorce after 1 year separation.
It act
IT Act in force 17 Oct 2000; 2008 amendment in force 27 Oct 2009; S.66A struck down 24 Mar 2015; S.67: 3 years + Rs 5 lakh (first), 5 years + Rs 10 lakh (repeat); S.67A/67B: 5 years + Rs 10 lakh (first), 7 years + Rs 10 lakh (repeat); S.66F: life; S.69/69A: up to 7 years + fine; S.70: up to 10 years; S.72: 2 years/Rs 1 lakh; S.72A: 3 years/Rs 5 lakh.
Dpdp
Act assented 11 Aug 2023; Rules notified 13 Nov 2025; child = under 18; maximum penalty Rs 250 crore; Board plus appeal to TDSAT; full obligations phased to about 12 May 2027.

⚡Rapid-fire one-liners

  • HMA S.5: groom 21, bride 18; void = S.5(i), (iv), (v); voidable under S.12.
  • Divorce: desertion 2 years; not heard of 7 years; leprosy omitted 2019.
  • Wife-only grounds: S.13(2) - pre-1955 bigamy, rape/sodomy/bestiality, maintenance non-resumption (1 year), puberty option (before 15; repudiate before 18).
  • S.13B: 1 year separation; 6-18 months; cooling period directory (Amardeep Singh 2017); Art 142 (Shilpa Sailesh 2023).
  • S.9 RCR upheld: Saroj Rani 1984 (T. Sareetha overruled).
  • S.14 HMA: no petition within 1 year of marriage unless exceptional hardship.
  • Saptapadi: where part of rites, complete on the seventh step. Registration is not validity.
  • S.16 HMA: children of void/voidable marriages legitimate; inherit only from parents.
  • S.25 HMA: permanent alimony available even in void marriage (X v Y 2025).
  • HAMA S.7 male adoption with wife's consent; S.8 female unmarried/ex-married; adoptee under 15 unless custom; age gap 21 years for opposite sex.
  • Shabnam Hashmi 2014: JJ Act adoption for all religions.
  • HMGA: natural guardian - father, then mother; child under 5 with mother; welfare paramount.
  • Gita Hariharan 1999: mother can be natural guardian in father's lifetime if he is absent/indifferent.
  • HSA S.6 2005: daughter coparcener by birth (Vineeta Sharma 2020).
  • Class I: mother yes; father is Class II no.1.
  • S.14 HSA: female's property is absolute.
  • S.15 HSA: female intestate - children and husband, then husband's heirs, then parents; inherited-from-parents property goes back to father's heirs.
  • S.8-inherited property is separate property (Yudhishter, Darubai 2026).
  • Mahr is an obligation, not consideration; Hanafi minimum 10 dirhams.
  • Muslim will: 1/3 limit; heirs' consent for bequests to heirs.
  • Hiba: declaration, acceptance, delivery of possession.
  • Instant triple talaq void: Shayara Bano (2017, 3:2); 2019 Act punishes up to 3 years.
  • DMMA S.2: 4-2-7-3-2 and puberty option 15/18.
  • Shah Bano 1985 -> 1986 Act -> Danial Latifi 2001 -> Abdul Samad 2024.
  • Iddat: widow 4 months 10 days; divorced 3 courses/3 months.
  • Christian widow takes 1/3 (S.33 ISA); Mary Roy 1986.
  • SMA: 30-day notice, 30-day objection, 3 months to marry.
  • DV Act: 60-day disposal; Harsora 2016; Ahuja 2020.
  • Dowry Act S.3: min 5 years + Rs 15,000/value; dowry death 7 years to life.
  • PCMA: voidable at option of child; annulment within 2 years of majority; offences cognizable and non-bailable.
  • S.144 BNSS: no cap; divorced wife who has not remarried; disqualified if in adultery/refuses without reason/separate by consent.
  • Supriyo 2023: no fundamental right to marry.
  • Uttarakhand UCC in force 27 January 2025; Gujarat and Assam UCCs passed 2026.

☰One week plan

  • Day 1: Hindu Marriage Act - validity, void/voidable, divorce grounds, S.13B, S.24-25; learn the numbers cheat sheet.
  • Day 2: Maintenance across laws + Hindu adoption + guardianship; 15 MCQs.
  • Day 3: Hindu Succession Act and joint family; then Muslim law I (marriage, mahr).
  • Day 4: Muslim law II-III (divorce, 2019 Act, DMMA, gifts, wills, waqf, pre-emption, inheritance).
  • Day 5: Christian, Parsi, SMA, DV Act, Dowry Act, PCMA, UCC.
  • Day 6: Cyber Law - IT Act offences table, S.79, Shreya Singhal, DPDP Act/Rules, IT Rules 2021 and 2026.
  • Day 7: Rapid-fire lists, all MCQs, personal_law_comparison; tab the bare Acts.
