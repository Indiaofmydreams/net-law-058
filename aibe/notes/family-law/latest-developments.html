Latest developments

Recent judgments, amendments and institutional changes that are favourite current-affairs questions. Notes cover developments up to 17 Nov 2026; anything tagged Verify should be checked against the official source.

Talaq-e-hasan litigation

Nov 2025 - Oct 2026

the Supreme Court sent one dispute to mediation (Justice Kurian Joseph) and on 16 October 2026 asked the Centre to respond to a plea for uniform safeguards for all forms of talaq, clubbing it with the main petition (CJI Surya Kant and Bagchi J).

Why it matters: Current-affairs MCQ on talaq-e-hasan versus talaq-e-biddat; also 2019 Act.

Darubai v Kamalabai (2026 INSC 613)

June 2026

property inherited under S.8 HSA is not coparcenary; no karta power over co-heirs' shares.

Why it matters: Likely HSA/joint family MCQ.

April-May 2026

Gujarat passed its UCC (April 2026; assent reported) and Assam passed its UCC Bill (last week of May 2026), after Uttarakhand (in force 27 January 2025).

Why it matters: UCC and live-in registration questions.

Supreme Court's interim order on the Waqf (Amendment) Act 2025

15 Sept 2025

selective stay (5-year practising-Muslim clause; part of S.3C); cap on non-Muslim board members; Act not stayed as a whole.

Why it matters: Waqf current-affairs MCQ.

X v Y (2025 INSC 197)

Feb 2025

spouse in a void marriage can claim permanent alimony under S.25 HMA; 'illegitimate wife'/'faithful mistress' language is misogynistic.

Why it matters: Maintenance and void marriage MCQ.

Mohd Abdul Samad v State of Telangana

10 July 2024

divorced Muslim women can claim maintenance under S.125 CrPC (S.144 BNSS).

Why it matters: Frequent Muslim maintenance MCQ.

Dolly Rani v Manish Kumar Chanchal

2024

a Hindu marriage requires valid ceremonies; registration certificate is not enough.

Why it matters: HMA S.7 and S.8 MCQ.

Shilpa Sailesh v Varun Sreenivasan (Constitution Bench)

1 May 2023

Art 142 divorce on irretrievable breakdown; cooling period waivable.

Why it matters: Most-tested divorce case.

Supriyo v Union of India

17 Oct 2023 / Jan 2025

no fundamental right to marry; same-sex marriage not recognised under SMA; review dismissed (reported Jan 2025).

Why it matters: SMA and equality MCQ.

BNS/BNSS/BSA in force

1 July 2024

S.85-86 BNS (498A), S.80 BNS (dowry death), S.82 BNS (bigamy), S.144 BNSS (maintenance); adultery not retained in BNS.

Why it matters: Dual numbering in MCQ stems.
