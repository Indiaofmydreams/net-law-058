Quick MCQs

43 recall questions to close each study session. Tap an option to see the answer and the reason. There is no timer and no negative marking.

0/ 43 correct

1Under S.5(iii) of the Hindu Marriage Act, the minimum ages of the bridegroom and the bride are:

Answer B. Groom 21, bride 18 (S.5(iii) HMA).

2A marriage in contravention of S.5(i) (bigamy) of the HMA is:

Answer A. S.11: breach of S.5(i), (iv) or (v) renders the marriage void.

3Which of the following makes a Hindu marriage voidable under S.12 HMA?

Answer D. S.12(1)(d). The other options are void under S.11.

4Under the HMA alone, contravention of the age condition in S.5(iii) makes the marriage:

Answer C. S.18 punishes it; (the PCMA 2006 separately makes child marriage voidable at the child's option).

5Desertion as a ground of divorce under S.13(1)(ib) HMA requires a continuous period of at least:

Answer B. Two years immediately preceding the petition.

6Which divorce ground is available only to a wife under S.13(2) HMA?

Answer A. S.13(2)(ii) is a wife-only ground.

7Under S.13B(2) HMA the court may grant a mutual consent divorce after a minimum waiting period of:

Answer D. 6 months and not later than 18 months from the petition; Amardeep Singh held the 6 months directory.

8In Amardeep Singh v Harveen Kaur (2017) the Supreme Court held that the 6-month period in S.13B(2):

Answer C. Directory; waivable if no chance of reconciliation.

9In Shilpa Sailesh v Varun Sreenivasan (2023), the Constitution Bench held that:

Answer B. Art 142 power, discretionary, with guidelines; not a statutory ground.

10The constitutional validity of restitution of conjugal rights (S.9 HMA) was upheld in:

Answer A. Saroj Rani (1984) upheld S.9 and overruled T. Sareetha.

11Which case laid down illustrative instances of mental cruelty?

Answer D. Samar Ghosh (2007).

12In X v Y (2025 INSC 197) the Supreme Court held that a spouse in a void marriage:

Answer C. Section 25 relief is available to either spouse, depending on facts and conduct.

13Under S.7 HAMA, a married Hindu male who wishes to adopt needs:

Answer B. S.7 proviso.

14Where a male Hindu adopts a daughter, the adopter must be at least how many years older than the adoptee?

Answer A. S.11(iii) HAMA: 21 years where the adopter and adoptee are of opposite sex.

15Shabnam Hashmi v Union of India (2014) held that:

Answer D. The JJ Act is an enabling secular law.

16As per Gita Hariharan v RBI (1999), 'after' in S.6(a) HMGA means:

Answer C. The mother can act as natural guardian when the father is absent or indifferent.

17A natural guardian's sale of a minor's immovable property without the court's permission is:

Answer B. S.8(3) HMGA.

18In Vineeta Sharma v Rakesh Sharma (2020), the Supreme Court held that:

Answer A. Overruling Prakash v Phulavati on this point.

19Which of the following is a Class I heir of a Hindu male under the HSA?

Answer D. Mother is a Class I heir; father is the first entry in Class II.

20Under S.14(1) HSA, property possessed by a Hindu female is held by her as:

Answer C. Absolute ownership; S.14(2) is a narrow exception.

21Under Darubai v Kamalabai (2026 INSC 613), property inherited under S.8 HSA:

Answer B. Following Yudhishter v Ashok Kumar.

22Under Muslim law, mahr (dower) is:

Answer A. Abdul Kadir v Salima (1886).

23In Shayara Bano v Union of India (2017) the Supreme Court:

Answer D. 3:2 majority; talaq-e-hasan is a separate, pending challenge.

24Under the Muslim Women (Protection of Rights on Marriage) Act 2019, pronouncing instant triple talaq is punishable with imprisonment up to:

Answer C. Three years and fine.

25Under S.2 DMMA 1939, a wife can seek dissolution if the husband has failed to provide maintenance for:

Answer B. Two years.

26Danial Latifi v Union of India (2001) held that the Muslim Women (Protection of Rights on Divorce) Act 1986:

Answer A. The Act was upheld but read down in line with Art 14 and 21.

27In Mohd Abdul Samad v State of Telangana (2024) the Supreme Court held that a divorced Muslim woman:

Answer D. S.125 is secular; the 1986 Act does not override it.

28Without the consent of heirs, a Sunni Muslim can bequeath by will not more than:

Answer C. One-third of the net estate.

29The essentials of a valid Muslim gift (hiba) are:

Answer B. Registration is not required (S.129 TPA).

30How many demands must a pre-emptor make under Muslim law?

Answer A. Talab-i-mowasibat, talab-i-ishhad and talab-i-khusumat.

31In its interim order of 15 September 2025 on the Waqf (Amendment) Act 2025, the Supreme Court:

Answer D. Selective stay; non-Muslim members capped at 4 of 22 and 3 of 11.

32Under the Indian Succession Act 1925, where a Christian dies intestate leaving a widow and lineal descendants, the widow takes:

Answer C. S.33(a) - widow 1/3, descendants 2/3.

33Mary Roy v State of Kerala (1986) is associated with:

Answer B. Travancore Christian Succession Act 1916 held superseded.

34Under the Special Marriage Act, the notice of intended marriage is published for:

Answer A. 30 days; objections within that period; marry within 3 months.

35The marriage of a Hindu member of an undivided family under the Special Marriage Act has the effect of:

Answer D. S.19 SMA.

36Hiral P. Harsora v Kusum Narottamdas Harsora (2016) struck down the words 'adult male' in the DV Act so that:

Answer C. S.2(q) DV Act.

37Under the Dowry Prohibition Act, the minimum punishment for giving or taking dowry is:

Answer B. S.3 Dowry Prohibition Act as amended in 1986.

38Punishment for dowry death (S.80 BNS / S.304B IPC) is:

Answer A. Minimum seven years.

39Under the Prohibition of Child Marriage Act 2006, a petition to annul a child marriage must be filed within:

Answer D. S.3(3) - before the petitioner completes two years after attaining majority.

40Supriyo v Union of India (2023) held that:

Answer C. Constitution Bench, 17 October 2023; matter left to Parliament.

41Under S.125 CrPC (S.144 BNSS), a divorced wife is entitled to maintenance until she:

Answer B. A divorced wife who has not remarried is a 'wife' for maintenance.

42Which state was the first to implement a Uniform Civil Code (in force 27 January 2025)?

Answer A. Uttarakhand UCC Act 2024 operationalised 27 January 2025; Goa has the older Portuguese Civil Code.

43Under S.16 HMA, children of a void marriage are:

Answer D. S.16(3) HMA.
