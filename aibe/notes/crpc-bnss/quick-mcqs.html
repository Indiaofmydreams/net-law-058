Criminal Procedure Code / Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita

Quick MCQs

68 recall questions to close each study session. Tap an option to see the answer and the reason. There is no timer and no negative marking.

0/ 68 correct

1The Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita came into force on:

Answer C. BNSS, BNS and BSA came into force on 1 July 2024.

2The BNSS has how many sections?

Answer C. 531 sections (CrPC had 484).

3Repeal and savings under the BNSS is contained in:

Answer C. s.531 repeals the CrPC.

4Which BNSS section corresponds to s.41 CrPC (arrest without warrant by police)?

Answer A. s.41 CrPC -> s.35 BNSS.

5Notice of appearance (old s.41A CrPC) is now in:

Answer A. BNSS s.35(3)-(6).

6An arrested person must be produced before a Magistrate within:

Answer B. Art 22(2); s.58 BNSS (s.57 CrPC).

7Under BNSS, arrest of an infirm or 60+ person for an offence below 3 years requires permission of an officer not below:

Answer C. s.35(7) BNSS.

8Handcuffing of arrested persons is expressly provided in:

Answer A. s.43(3) BNSS - new.

9The FIR provision of the BNSS is:

Answer B. s.173 BNSS (s.154 CrPC).

10An e-FIR must be signed by the informant within:

Answer B. s.173(1) BNSS.

11Preliminary enquiry before FIR under BNSS is allowed for offences punishable with:

Answer B. s.173(3) BNSS; within 14 days with DSP-level permission.

12'Zero FIR' means:

Answer B. Zero FIR - s.173 BNSS; Satvinder Kaur.

13Mandatory visit of a forensic expert to the crime scene applies to offences punishable with:

Answer C. s.176(3) BNSS.

14Maximum police custody allowed by a Magistrate under s.187 BNSS (s.167 CrPC) is:

Answer C. 15 days in the whole.

15Default bail becomes available after how many days for offences punishable with death, life or not less than ten years?

Answer B. 90 days; 60 days for others.

16Which case held that 'not less than ten years' means a minimum of ten years for the 90-day default bail rule?

Answer A. Rakesh Kumar Paul (2017).

17Statements to police during investigation are covered by:

Answer B. s.181 BNSS (s.162 CrPC) - not signed; use limited to contradict.

18Confessions and statements are recorded by a Magistrate under:

Answer C. s.183 BNSS (s.164 CrPC).

19Police report on completion of investigation is filed under:

Answer D. s.193 BNSS (s.173 CrPC).

20Cognizance of offences by Magistrates is dealt with in:

Answer B. s.210 BNSS (s.190 CrPC).

21Cognizance is taken of:

Answer B. Raghubans Dubey v. State of Bihar (1967).

22Under BNSS s.223, a Magistrate taking cognizance on a complaint must now also:

Answer B. New proviso to s.223.

23Prosecution of public servants for acts in official duty requires sanction under:

Answer C. s.218 BNSS (s.197 CrPC).

24Documents must be supplied to the accused within:

Answer B. s.230 BNSS.

25An application for discharge in a Sessions case must be made within how many days of commitment?

Answer C. s.250 BNSS.

26The charge in a Sessions case must be framed within how many days of the first hearing on charge?

Answer C. s.251 BNSS.

27Alteration of charge is permitted:

Answer B. s.239 BNSS (s.216 CrPC).

28A warrant-case is one relating to an offence punishable with death, life or imprisonment exceeding:

Answer C. Definition in s.2 BNSS.

29Under BNSS, summary trial is available for offences punishable up to:

Answer C. s.283 BNSS (CrPC: 2 years).

30Maximum sentence in a summary trial is:

Answer B. Not exceeding 3 months.

31Plea bargaining application must be filed within how many days of the framing of charge?

Answer B. s.289 BNSS.

32Plea bargaining is NOT available for offences punishable with imprisonment exceeding:

Answer C. Excludes offences above 7 years and death/life.

33Trial in absentia of a proclaimed offender is provided in:

Answer C. s.356 BNSS - new.

34Power to proceed against other persons appearing to be guilty is in:

Answer A. Hardeep Singh v. State of Punjab (2014).

35Examination of the accused under s.351 BNSS is:

Answer B. Not on oath; no adverse inference from silence.

36Double jeopardy is dealt with in:

Answer B. s.337 BNSS (s.300 CrPC); Art 20(2).

37Compounding of offences is under:

Answer B. s.359 BNSS (s.320 CrPC).

38Withdrawal from prosecution under BNSS requires the court to also hear:

Answer B. s.360 BNSS - victim must be heard.

39The Witness Protection Scheme is provided under:

Answer C. s.398 BNSS - new.

40Victim compensation scheme (old s.357A CrPC) is in:

Answer B. s.396 BNSS.

41A Sessions Court's death sentence must be confirmed by the High Court under:

Answer B. s.407 BNSS (s.366 CrPC).

42The victim's right to appeal appears as:

Answer A. Proviso to s.413 (s.372 CrPC).

43Appeal against acquittal is provided in:

Answer C. s.419 BNSS (s.378 CrPC).

44Revision is provided in:

Answer B. s.438 (calling for records) and s.442 (HC powers).

45No revision lies against:

Answer B. s.438(2) BNSS (s.397(2) CrPC).

46Transfer of a case from one High Court to another can be ordered by:

Answer C. s.447 BNSS (s.406 CrPC).

47Maintenance of wives, children and parents is under:

Answer B. s.144 BNSS (s.125 CrPC).

48The old s.144 CrPC (urgent cases of nuisance or apprehended danger) is now:

Answer B. s.163 BNSS.

49An order under s.163 BNSS can remain in force for at most:

Answer B. 2 months, extendable to 6.

50Inherent powers of the High Court are saved by:

Answer B. s.528 BNSS (s.482 CrPC).

51Trial and proceedings in electronic mode are authorised by:

Answer C. s.530 BNSS.

52Limitation for cognizance of offences punishable with imprisonment up to one year is:

Answer B. s.514 BNSS (s.468 CrPC).

53There is no limitation for taking cognizance of offences punishable with imprisonment exceeding:

Answer C. Above 3 years - no bar.

54Anticipatory bail is dealt with in:

Answer C. s.482 BNSS (s.438 CrPC).

55Anticipatory bail is not available under BNSS s.482(4) for offences under BNS:

Answer B. Aggravated rape of minors and gang rape of minors Verify · mapping.

56Which Constitution Bench held that anticipatory bail is not limited in time?

Answer A. Sibbia (1980); Sushila Aggarwal (2020).

57Release of an undertrial under s.479 BNSS: a first-time offender (never convicted) is entitled after serving up to:

Answer B. One-third for first-time offenders; one-half for others.

58Cancellation of bail by the High Court or Court of Session is under:

Answer C. s.483(3) BNSS (s.439(2) CrPC).

59Which case laid down categories A to D for bail guidelines?

Answer A. Antil (2022).

60The 'seven categories' for quashing an FIR were laid down in:

Answer B. State of Haryana v. Bhajan Lal (1992).

61Limitation for taking cognizance is counted from the date of:

Answer B. Sarah Mathew (2014, Constitution Bench).

62Metropolitan Magistrates:

Answer B. BNSS omits the Metropolitan Magistrate machinery.

63A Judicial Magistrate of the First Class can impose a fine of up to:

Answer C. s.23 BNSS.

64Mercy petition by a death convict must first go to the Governor within:

Answer B. s.472 BNSS Verify.

65In Bilkis Yakub Rasool (2024), the 'appropriate Government' for remission was held to be the government of the State where:

Answer B. Maharashtra was the appropriate Government.

66Audio-video recording of search and seizure is mandatory under:

Answer C. s.105 BNSS - new.

67Attachment or forfeiture of proceeds of crime by the Magistrate is provided in:

Answer B. s.107 BNSS - new.

68In Mihir Rajesh Shah v. State of Maharashtra (2025) the Supreme Court held:

Answer B. Applies to every arrest under every statute.
