Evidence Act / Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam

Quick MCQs

31 recall questions to close each study session. Tap an option to see the answer and the reason. There is no timer and no negative marking.

0/ 31 correct

1The Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023 came into force on:

Answer B. BSA, BNS and BNSS came into force on 1 July 2024 (assent 25 Dec 2023).

2Which provision of the BSA corresponds to Section 65B of the Indian Evidence Act?

Answer C. S.63 BSA replaced S.65B IEA and added the Schedule certificate with hash value.

3Under Section 63(4) BSA, the certificate must be signed by:

Answer C. Part A is signed by the person in charge; Part B by an expert. The Supreme Court (Pune Bar Association, 2026) held an expert is not confined to a S.79A IT Act Examiner.

4Which additional particular must the Section 63(4) certificate disclose, which Section 65B did not require?

Answer A. The BSA requires disclosure of the hash value.

5Which decision overruled State (NCT of Delhi) v. Navjot Sandhu on electronic evidence?

Answer B. Anvar P.V. (2014) held the S.65B certificate is mandatory.

6In Arjun Panditrao Khotkar (2020), the Supreme Court:

Answer C. Certificate is mandatory for secondary evidence; not needed when the original device is produced.

7A confession made to a police officer:

Answer B. S.23(1) BSA (S.25 IEA).

8Under the proviso to S.23(2) BSA (S.27 IEA), what portion of information given by an accused in custody can be proved?

Answer C. Pulukuri Kottaya - the information distinctly relating to the discovered fact.

9A confession of a co-accused is:

Answer B. Kashmira Singh; Haricharan Kurmi.

10Which of the following is NOT required for a valid dying declaration to be relied on?

Answer B. Laxman v. State of Maharashtra (2002) - Magistrate's presence/medical certificate are not mandatory.

11Under the Indian law of evidence, a dying declaration is relevant:

Answer B. S.26(1)(a) BSA / S.32(1) IEA.

12In a prosecution for dowry death, the Court:

Answer B. S.118 BSA (S.113B IEA) - 'shall presume' once the foundation of cruelty soon before death for dowry is shown.

13Under S.117 BSA (S.113A IEA), where a woman commits suicide within seven years of marriage, the Court:

Answer B. 'May presume' - discretionary.

14Birth during a valid marriage or within how many days after its dissolution, the mother remaining unmarried, is conclusive proof of legitimacy?

Answer C. S.116 BSA / S.112 IEA.

15The burden of proving that a case falls within a General Exception lies on:

Answer B. S.108 BSA (S.105 IEA); standard is preponderance of probability.

16S.109 BSA (S.106 IEA) - burden of proving a fact especially within knowledge:

Answer B. Shambhu Nath Mehra v. State of Ajmer.

17A person who has not been heard of for seven years by those who would naturally have heard of him:

Answer B. S.111 BSA / S.108 IEA.

18Which of the following can be proved despite a written contract under the provisos to S.92 IEA?

Answer B. Proviso (1) to S.92.

19Secondary evidence is admissible when:

Answer A. S.60 BSA (S.65 IEA).

20Which of the following is a primary evidence?

Answer B. S.57 BSA (S.62 IEA).

21A Will:

Answer B. S.68 IEA with S.63 Indian Succession Act - Janki Narayan Bhoir.

22The Evidence Act does NOT apply to:

Answer C. S.1 - applies to judicial proceedings in or before any Court; not to affidavits or proceedings before an arbitrator.

23An accomplice is:

Answer B. S.133 IEA read with S.114 illustration (b); Bhiva Doulu Patil.

24Leading questions in examination-in-chief:

Answer B. IEA S.141-143.

25The evidence of a hostile witness:

Answer B. Bhagwan Singh; Khujji; Sat Paul.

26Test Identification Parade is:

Answer B. Matru v. State of U.P.

27Under S.24 BSA (S.30 IEA), a trial in the absence of an absconding accused is:

Answer B. New Explanation in the BSA.

28In Tofan Singh v. State of T.N. (2020), the Supreme Court held that:

Answer C. Key authority on S.25 IEA / S.23 BSA in special statutes.

29The BSA recognises the opinion of which expert under S.39(2)?

Answer B. S.39(2) BSA.

30Which of the following judgments is conclusive proof?

Answer B. S.41 IEA (S.35 BSA).

31The privilege against disclosure of advocate-client communications is contained in:

Answer B. S.126 IEA / S.132 BSA.
