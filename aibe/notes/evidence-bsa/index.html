%Where the marks come from

Evidence Act / Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam carries about 8 of 100 questions. About 100 questions, 1 mark each; no negative marking; 3 hours 30 minutes to finish; offline, open book (bci permits specified reference material - read the current notification for what is allowed).

What the examiner tests

  • The paper tests recognition of the rule, the section and the leading case - not long reasoning. Most questions are single-line.
  • Since 1 July 2024 the Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023 (BSA) has replaced the Indian Evidence Act, 1872 (IEA). Expect the question stem to use EITHER IEA or BSA numbering, so learn both. Treat the BSA as the primary text from here on.
  • Roughly half of the 8 Evidence questions come from four zones: (1) confessions and admissions, (2) burden of proof and presumptions, (3) documentary/electronic evidence, (4) witnesses and examination. Dying declaration and S.27-type discovery are perennial favourites.
  • Electronic evidence (S.63 BSA) is the single hottest zone: it is new, it has fresh case law (Pune Bar Association, May 2026) and it is easy to frame as an MCQ.
  • The 2 Environmental Law questions almost always come from: constitutional provisions (Art 21, 48A, 51A(g)), principles (polluter pays, precautionary, absolute liability, public trust, sustainable development), NGT Act, EPA 1986, Water/Air Acts, and a landmark case-to-principle match.

Study in this order

  • 1. Electronic evidence - S.61-63 BSA, Schedule certificate, hash value, Anvar/Arjun Panditrao/Pune Bar
  • 2. Confessions and discovery - S.22-24 BSA (IEA 24-30), S.27 IEA / S.23(2) BSA
  • 3. Burden of proof and presumptions - S.104-120 BSA (IEA 101-114A)
  • 4. Dying declaration and other statements of persons who cannot be called - S.26 BSA (IEA 32)
  • 5. Documentary evidence - primary/secondary, proof of execution, oral v documentary
  • 6. Witnesses, privileges and examination - competence, hostile witness, leading questions, S.132 BSA
  • 7. Admissions, relevancy (res gestae, conspiracy), expert opinion, judgments, judicial notice, estoppel

Exam tips

  • Never leave a question blank. Eliminate two options and guess.
  • Use the book only for exact section numbers and wording. Do not look up concepts - there is no time. Tag the bare Act with index tabs for: S.2, S.22-24, S.26, S.39, S.57-63, S.104-120, S.132 and the Schedule to the BSA.

The latest paper

  • AIBE XXI was held on 7 June 2026 (offline, OMR). BCI reported no change in syllabus or pattern for AIBE XXI compared with the previous year.
