What the examiner tests

  • CPC questions are overwhelmingly BARE-ACT questions: 'under which section/ order/ rule?', 'within how many days?', 'which of the following is correct?'. Open-book helps only if you know where to look - tab Secs 2, 9-25, 80, 89, 91-92, 96-115, 144-153; Orders 1, 2, 6-9, 14, 17, 18, 21 (Rules 22, 41, 54, 66, 68, 84-92, 97-103), 22, 23, 32, 33, 37-41, 43, 47.
  • Frequently repeated numbers: 30/90/120 days (written statement), 3 adjournments, 30/60 days (judgment), 15 days (decree), 90 days (substitution), 60 days (abatement), 30 days (ex parte, review), 90 days (appeals/ revision), 15/7 days (sale), 25% (deposit), 60 days (setting aside sale), 2 months (Sec 80 notice), 2 years (Rule 22 notice), 3 months/ 6 weeks (detention).
  • Distinction pairs are common: decree vs order, Sec 10 vs Sec 11, return vs rejection of plaint, set-off vs counter-claim, necessary vs proper party, review vs revision vs appeal, Sec 47 vs Order 21 Rule 97-103.
  • Recent Supreme Court rulings are used as 'updating' hooks: Periyammal (execution in six months), B.S. Lalitha (interlocutory res judicata), Order 7 Rule 11 trends, commercial court time limits.
  • Tag the 2002 amendments (in force 1 July 2002): Order 5 service by courier/ e-mail, Order 6 Rule 17 proviso, Order 17 three adjournments, Order 18 Rule 4 affidavit evidence, Order 21 Rule 41(2), Sec 100A, Sec 102 (Rs 25,000), Sec 39(4).
