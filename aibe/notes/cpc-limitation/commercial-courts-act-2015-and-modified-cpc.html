Commercial Courts Act 2015 (as amended 2018): Modified CPC for Commercial Disputes

iOverview

The Commercial Courts, Commercial Division and Commercial Appellate Division of High Courts Act 2015 amends and overrides the CPC for 'commercial disputes' of 'specified value'. Several features are frequently asked.

★Must know

  1. Coverage: 'commercial dispute' (Sec 2(1)(c)) includes disputes relating to mercantile documents, export/ import, carriage, franchising, distribution, joint ventures, shareholders' agreements, intellectual property rights, construction/ infrastructure, mortgages, insurance and reinsurance, agricultural produce, etc. SPECIFIED VALUE (Sec 2(1)(i)): reduced by the 2018 Amendment to THREE LAKH rupees (earlier one crore). Commercial Courts at district level (for value of Rs 3 lakh up to the pecuniary limit of the district court), Commercial Divisions in High Courts, and Commercial Appellate Divisions/ Courts. Sec 3A: designation of Commercial Appellate Courts at district level. Sec 12A: pre-institution mediation (mandatory unless urgent interim relief is contemplated - Patil Automation, 2022). Sec 13: appeals - from decrees/ orders of Commercial Courts to Commercial Appellate Court/ Division within SIXTY days; only orders specified in Order 43 CPC and Sec 13(1A) are appealable.
  2. MODIFICATIONS TO THE CPC (Sec 16 and the Schedule): (1) summons and written statement - Order V Rule 1: summons must require the defendant to file the written statement within 30 days, and the court may extend by 90 days at the most - i.e., 120 days from service; right to file the written statement is forfeited after 120 days (SCG Contracts, 2019); (2) Order VIII Rule 1 (counter-claim and set-off must also be in the same period; Rule 1A: documents); (3) ORDER XI (disclosure, discovery and inspection of documents) substituted: each party must make a statement of truth, disclose and file all documents in its power, possession, control or custody on affidavit; admission/ denial of documents is mandatory (Order XI Rule 4); (4) ORDER XV-A (case management hearing): the court holds a case management hearing - frames issues, fixes time for filing affidavits of evidence, and dates for oral arguments; the court may impose costs for non-compliance; (5) ORDER XIII-A: summary judgment (where no real prospect of success); (6) evidence by affidavit, time limits for oral arguments, and judgment within 90 days of conclusion of arguments (verify the modified Order XX in the Schedule); (7) Sec 35 COSTS - 'loser pays' regime: the court shall order costs to follow the event; costs include fees and expenses of witnesses, legal fees, other expenses; the court may order interest on costs; (8) Sec 21: the Act has overriding effect over other laws; (9) Sec 15: transfer of pending suits to the Commercial Division; (10) Sec 10: jurisdiction in arbitration matters (applications under the Arbitration Act in commercial disputes lie to Commercial Courts/ Divisions); (11) Sec 12: determination of specified value (the value of subject-matter, including interest). Time-bound trials: the Act emphasises early disposal. Interim relief: Order 39 applies; Sec 12A exempts when urgent relief is sought.
  3. ARBITRATION INTERFACE: Sec 8 Arbitration and Conciliation Act 1996 - a judicial authority before which an action is brought in a matter which is the subject of an arbitration agreement shall, if a party so applies not later than the date of submitting his first statement on the substance of the dispute, refer the parties to arbitration (the 2015/ 2019 amendments - 'notwithstanding any judgment... unless prima facie no valid arbitration agreement exists'); Sec 9: interim measures by court (must be followed by arbitration commencement within 90 days - Sec 9(3), (4)); Sec 34: setting aside awards; Sec 36: enforcement as a decree under CPC; Sec 37: appeals. The CPC applies to execution of awards (Sec 36 - award enforceable as if it were a decree).

!Exam traps

  • TrapSpecified value: Rs 3 lakh (since 2018).
  • TrapWritten statement in commercial suits: 120 days mandatory outer limit.
  • TrapAppeal under Sec 13 within 60 days.
  • TrapPre-institution mediation (Sec 12A): mandatory unless urgent relief contemplated.
  • TrapCommercial courts follow 'costs follow the event' with actual costs.

?Likely questions

  1. Specified value of a commercial dispute: Rs 3 lakh.
  2. Sec 12A: pre-institution mediation.
  3. Appeal from a commercial court decree: within 60 days to the Commercial Appellate Court/ Division.
  4. Order XIII-A: summary judgment.
