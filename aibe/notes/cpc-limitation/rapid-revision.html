≠Pairs the examiner likes

Decree vs Order
Decree: conclusive determination in a suit; may be preliminary/ final; appealable. Order: any other decision; never preliminary; appealable only if listed.
Sec 10 (res sub judice) vs Sec 11 (res judicata)
Sec 10: earlier suit pending - stay of later trial. Sec 11: earlier suit decided - bar on later suit/ issue.
Return vs rejection of plaint
Return (Order 7 Rule 10): lack of jurisdiction; plaint can be re-presented. Rejection (Rule 11): defect such as no cause of action/ bar of law; a decree; fresh plaint allowed (Rule 13).
Set-off vs counter-claim
Set-off: ascertained money debt as a defence (Rule 6). Counter-claim: cross-suit for any claim (Rule 6A), survives dismissal of suit.
Necessary vs proper party
Necessary: no effective decree without him. Proper: presence helps complete adjudication.
Order 2 Rule 2 vs Sec 11
Order 2 Rule 2: omission to claim whole relief on same cause of action. Sec 11: matter actually decided.
Order 9 Rule 3 vs Rule 8
Rule 3: neither party appears - fresh suit allowed. Rule 8: plaintiff absent, defendant present - fresh suit barred (Rule 9).
Order 9 Rule 13 vs appeal under Sec 96(2)
Concurrent remedies against ex parte decree; Rule 13 lost if the appeal is decided on merits (Explanation).
Sec 47 vs Order 21 Rules 97-101
Sec 47: questions between parties/ representatives. Rules 97-101: obstruction/ third parties in possession decrees; title decided in execution.
Attachment before judgment vs temporary injunction
Order 38: security/ attachment against defendant's intent to defeat decree. Order 39: restraining acts in relation to property in dispute.
Review vs Revision vs Appeal
Review: same court, limited grounds, 30 days. Revision: High Court, jurisdictional error, 90 days. Appeal: higher court; statutory right.
First vs second appeal
First: facts and law (Sec 96). Second: only substantial question of law (Sec 100).
Sec 91 vs Sec 92
Sec 91: public nuisance - Advocate-General or 2 persons with leave. Sec 92: public charitable/ religious trusts - same, for listed reliefs.

✓Final revision checklist

  • Can I state, without looking, the numbers: 30/90/120 days, 3 adjournments, 30/60 days, 90/60 days, 15/7 days, 25%, 60 days, 2 months?
  • Can I distinguish decree vs order, Sec 10 vs Sec 11, return vs rejection of plaint, set-off vs counter-claim?
  • Do I know the conditions of res judicata and Explanation IV and VIII?
  • Can I list the grounds of Order 7 Rule 11 and the Supreme Court's boundaries on them?
  • Do I know Order 9 Rules 3, 4, 6, 8, 9, 13 and their time limits?
  • Can I narrate execution: Secs 36-64, Order 21 Rules 22, 41, 54, 66, 68, 84-92 and 97-103?
  • Can I distinguish appeal, second appeal, review, revision and reference with limitation periods?
  • Have I tabbed the bare Code and attempted the practice MCQs?

☰Seven-day study plan

  • Day 1: Definitions (Sec 2), jurisdiction (Secs 9-25), Sec 10, Sec 11 and foreign judgments
  • Day 2: Suits against Government, plaint, return/ rejection, parties, Order 2, summons, written statement, set-off and counter-claim
  • Day 3: Pleadings, Order 9, Orders 10-20 (trial stages and judgment), costs and interest
  • Day 4: Interim reliefs (Orders 38, 39, 40, 26), Order 37, Orders 22-23, 32-34, Secs 91-92, ADR
  • Day 5: Execution I: Secs 36-64 and Order 21 Rules 1-41
  • Day 6: Execution II: attachment, sale, Rules 97-106; appeals, second appeals, orders, review, revision
  • Day 7: Revision of cheat sheet, comparison tables, inherent powers/ caveat/ restitution, practice MCQs
