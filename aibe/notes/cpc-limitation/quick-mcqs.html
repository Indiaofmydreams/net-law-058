Quick MCQs

79 recall questions to close each study session. Tap an option to see the answer and the reason. There is no timer and no negative marking.

0/ 79 correct

1Which of the following is a decree?

Answer B. Sec 2(2) deems rejection of a plaint a decree; dismissal for default is excluded.

2Mesne profits are defined in:

Answer B. Sec 2(12); Sec 2(9) judgment; Sec 2(14) order.

3An order can be:

Answer B. Only decrees can be preliminary; orders are never preliminary.

4Principles on exclusion of the jurisdiction of civil courts were summarised in:

Answer B. Dhulabhai (1968).

5A decree passed by a court lacking inherent (subject-matter) jurisdiction is:

Answer B. Kiran Singh: jurisdiction cannot be conferred by consent.

6Suits for partition of immovable property are to be instituted:

Answer B. Sec 16(b).

7Objection as to the place of suing must be taken:

Answer B. Sec 21.

8Transfer of a suit from a court in one State to a court in another State can be ordered by:

Answer C. Sec 25.

9Section 10 CPC bars:

Answer B. Sec 10 stays trial of the subsequent suit.

10Constructive res judicata is contained in:

Answer B. Matters that might and ought to have been made a ground of defence or attack.

11The maxim 'nemo debet bis vexari pro una et eadem causa' underlies:

Answer C. Res judicata.

12The 2026 Supreme Court ruling in B.S. Lalitha v Bhuvanesh dealt with:

Answer B. 2026 INSC 499.

13A foreign judgment is NOT conclusive when it:

Answer B. Sec 13(e).

14A decree of a superior court of which country can be executed directly under Sec 44A?

Answer B. The UK is a notified reciprocating territory.

15Notice before instituting a suit against the Government must be given at least:

Answer C. Sec 80(1).

16In a suit by or against the Central Government the party named is:

Answer B. Sec 79.

17A plaint is rejected under:

Answer B. Order 7 Rule 11.

18Partial rejection of a plaint under Order 7 Rule 11 is:

Answer B. Sejal Glass v Navilan Merchants.

19The court looks only at ___ while deciding an Order 7 Rule 11 application:

Answer B. Saleem Bhai v State of Maharashtra.

20The rule that a suit must include the whole claim arising from a cause of action is in:

Answer B. Order 2 Rule 2.

21Objections for non-joinder/ misjoinder must be taken:

Answer B. Order 1 Rule 13.

22Representative suits are provided in:

Answer A. Order 1 Rule 8 (permission and notice).

23The defendant must file his written statement within ___ days, extendable up to ___ days in ordinary suits:

Answer B. Order 8 Rule 1.

24In commercial disputes the outer limit for filing the written statement is:

Answer C. SCG Contracts (2019); right forfeited beyond 120 days.

25A counter-claim is dealt with in:

Answer B. Order 8 Rule 6A.

26Substituted service is provided in:

Answer C. Order 5 Rule 20.

27After the commencement of trial, an amendment of pleadings is allowed only if:

Answer B. Proviso to Order 6 Rule 17 (2002).

28An ex parte decree is set aside under:

Answer C. Order 9 Rule 13; limitation 30 days.

29Where a suit is dismissed under Order 9 Rule 8 (plaintiff absent, defendant present), the plaintiff:

Answer B. Order 9 Rule 9.

30Not more than ___ adjournments may be granted to a party during the hearing of a suit:

Answer B. Order 17 Rule 1.

31Examination-in-chief of a witness is on:

Answer B. Order 18 Rule 4.

32Judgment on admissions is under:

Answer A. Order 12 Rule 6.

33A preliminary issue can be tried first under Order 14 Rule 2 if it relates to:

Answer B. Order 14 Rule 2(2).

34The triple test for temporary injunction (Dalpat Kumar) consists of:

Answer A. Dalpat Kumar v Prahlad Singh (1992).

35Disobedience of a temporary injunction can lead to detention in civil prison for up to:

Answer B. Order 39 Rule 2A.

36Attachment before judgment is provided in:

Answer A. Order 38 Rule 5.

37Compensation for obtaining arrest, attachment or injunction on insufficient grounds is under:

Answer B. Sec 95 - not exceeding Rs 50,000.

38In a summary suit the defendant must enter appearance within:

Answer B. Order 37 Rule 3(1).

39Unconditional leave to defend is granted where the defendant:

Answer A. IDBI Trusteeship v Hubtown (2017).

40Limitation for bringing legal representatives on record is:

Answer C. Art 120 Limitation Act.

41An application to set aside abatement must be made within:

Answer B. Art 121 Limitation Act.

42Withdrawal of a suit with liberty to file a fresh suit is under:

Answer A. Order 23 Rule 1(3).

43A suit to set aside a compromise decree on the ground that the compromise was unlawful is:

Answer B. Order 23 Rule 3A; Pushpa Devi Bhagat (2006).

44A compromise by a next friend on behalf of a minor without the leave of the court is:

Answer B. Order 32 Rule 7.

45An application to sue as an indigent person is rejected if:

Answer B. Order 33 Rule 5.

46A suit relating to a public charitable trust for the removal of a trustee may be instituted by:

Answer B. Sec 92.

47How many modes of settlement does Sec 89(1) mention?

Answer C. Arbitration, conciliation, judicial settlement including Lok Adalat, mediation.

48In Afcons Infrastructure v Cherian Varkey the Supreme Court held that consent of parties is necessary for referral to:

Answer D. Consent is required only for arbitration.

49Questions relating to execution, discharge or satisfaction of a decree between parties are determined by:

Answer B. Sec 47.

50An executing court cannot:

Answer B. Vasudev Dhanjibhai Modi.

51A precept under Sec 46 can attach property for up to:

Answer B. Unless extended.

52A woman cannot be arrested or detained in execution of a decree for:

Answer B. Sec 56.

53Maximum detention for a money decree exceeding Rs 5,000 is:

Answer B. Sec 58.

54Which of the following is exempt from attachment under Sec 60?

Answer B. Sec 60(1)(d).

55Notice to show cause under Order 21 Rule 22 is required when execution is applied for after:

Answer B. Rule 22(1)(a).

56No sale of immovable property in execution can take place without JD's consent before the expiry of at least ___ days from affixing the proclamation:

Answer B. Order 21 Rule 68 (7 days for movable).

57The purchaser at an execution sale of immovable property must deposit ___ immediately:

Answer C. Rule 84; balance within 15 days (Rule 85).

58An application to set aside a sale on depositing 5% and the decretal amount must be made within:

Answer B. Rule 89.

59Order 21 Rule 90 requires, for setting aside a sale:

Answer B. Rule 90(2).

60Resistance or obstruction to possession of immovable property is dealt with in:

Answer B. Rules 97-103.

61Orders under Order 21 Rules 98 and 100 are treated as:

Answer B. Rule 103.

62In Periyammal v V. Rajamani (2025) the Supreme Court directed execution petitions to be disposed of within:

Answer B. Six months.

63No appeal lies from a decree passed:

Answer B. Sec 96(3).

64Limitation for a first appeal to the High Court from a decree is:

Answer C. Art 116 (30 days to other courts).

65A second appeal lies to the High Court only on:

Answer B. Sec 100.

66The test of a substantial question of law was laid down in:

Answer A. 1962.

67Cross-objection under Order 41 Rule 22 must be filed within:

Answer B. From service of notice of the hearing.

68Sec 100A bars:

Answer B. Inserted 2002.

69No second appeal lies in a money suit where the value does not exceed:

Answer C. Sec 102 (2002).

70Limitation for an application for review is:

Answer A. Art 124.

71Grounds of review under Order 47 Rule 1 include:

Answer A. Subsequent reversal of law is not a ground.

72Revision under Sec 115 lies to:

Answer B. Where no appeal lies; jurisdictional error.

73A reference to the High Court on a question of law is made under:

Answer A. Sec 113; Order 46.

74Future interest on a decretal amount in non-commercial cases cannot exceed:

Answer B. Sec 34(1).

75Compensatory costs for false or vexatious claims cannot exceed:

Answer B. Sec 35A(2).

76A caveat lodged under Sec 148A remains in force for:

Answer C. Sec 148A(5).

77Restitution of the parties on reversal of a decree is provided in:

Answer A. Sec 144; actus curiae neminem gravabit.

78Inherent powers of the court are saved by:

Answer B. Sec 151.

79The 'specified value' for a commercial dispute under the Commercial Courts Act (as amended in 2018) is:

Answer B. Rs 3 lakh.
