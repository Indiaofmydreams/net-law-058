Public Interest Litigation

Rapid revision

The last-day sheets. AIBE is open book, so knowing where a provision sits is as valuable as memorising it: use these tables to tab your Bare Act.

Jump toOne-line case revisionMnemonicsLinks to other subjects

⚡One-line case revision

  • Hussainara Khatoon (1979) - first PIL.
  • S.P. Gupta (1981) - locus standi liberalised.
  • Bandhua Mukti Morcha (1984) - epistolary jurisdiction.
  • Vishaka (1997) - workplace sexual harassment guidelines.
  • Vineet Narain (1998) - continuing mandamus; CBI.
  • Vellore Citizens (1996) - precautionary and polluter-pays principles.
  • Balwant Singh Chaufal (2010) - guidelines to curb PIL abuse.

✦Mnemonics

  • PIL not entertained: Landlord-tenant, Service, Government complaints, Admissions, Early hearing.
