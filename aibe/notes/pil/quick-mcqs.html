Public Interest Litigation

Quick MCQs

14 recall questions to close each study session. Tap an option to see the answer and the reason. There is no timer and no negative marking.

0/ 14 correct

1The first reported PIL in India was:

Answer B. Hussainara Khatoon (1979).

2Locus standi was liberalised in:

Answer A. S.P. Gupta (1981) - Judges' Transfer case.

3Epistolary jurisdiction refers to:

Answer B. Letters addressed to the court may be treated as PIL.

4PIL can be filed in the High Court under:

Answer B. Art 226; Art 32 for the Supreme Court.

5Which of the following is NOT ordinarily entertained as PIL?

Answer B. 1988 guidelines exclude landlord-tenant, service, pension, admissions, early hearing.

6PIL in service matters was held not maintainable in:

Answer A. Duryodhan Sahu (1998).

7Guidelines to curb abuse of PIL were laid down in:

Answer A. Balwant Singh Chaufal (2010).

8Absolute liability was evolved in:

Answer B. M.C. Mehta (1987).

9Vishaka guidelines relate to:

Answer B. Vishaka (1997) -> POSH Act 2013.

10The precautionary principle and polluter-pays were adopted in:

Answer A. 1996.

11Police reforms directives were issued in:

Answer A. Prakash Singh (2006).

12Continuing mandamus was employed prominently in:

Answer A. Vineet Narain; Vishaka; Godavarman.

13In 2026 the Supreme Court described PIL misuse as:

Answer A. Remarks by the Bench (May 2026).

14The Aravalli Hills definition ruling of Nov 2025 was stayed by the Supreme Court on:

Answer A. Stayed suo motu and an expert committee ordered.
