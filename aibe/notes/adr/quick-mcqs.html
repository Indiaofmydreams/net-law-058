Quick MCQs

31 recall questions to close each study session. Tap an option to see the answer and the reason. There is no timer and no negative marking.

0/ 31 correct

1The Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996 is based on the:

Answer A. Based on the UNCITRAL Model Law (1985) and Conciliation Rules (1980).

2Part III of the Arbitration and Conciliation Act 1996 deals with:

Answer B. Part III (ss.61-81) - conciliation.

3An arbitration agreement under s.7 must be:

Answer B. s.7(3): in writing; record by exchange of communications suffices.

4Under s.8, an application to refer parties to arbitration must be made:

Answer B. s.8(1) as amended in 2015.

5If the court grants interim relief under s.9 before arbitration, arbitral proceedings must commence within:

Answer C. s.9(2) - 90 days.

6In the appointment of arbitrators under s.11(4), the default period for a party to appoint is:

Answer B. 30 days from receipt of request.

7The application under s.11 shall be disposed of expeditiously, and an endeavour shall be made to dispose of it within:

Answer C. s.11(13) - 60 days.

8The Schedule listing persons ineligible to be arbitrators is the:

Answer D. Seventh Schedule; Fifth gives grounds of justifiable doubts.

9The tribunal's power to rule on its own jurisdiction is under:

Answer B. s.16 - competence-competence.

10A challenge to an arbitrator under s.13 must be made within:

Answer B. 15 days of becoming aware of the circumstances.

11Award must be made within how many months from completion of pleadings (domestic arbitration)?

Answer C. s.29A(1) - 12 months; extendable by 6 months by consent.

12Post-award interest under s.31(7)(b), unless the award directs otherwise, is:

Answer B. 2% above the current rate of interest.

13An application to set aside an arbitral award must be made within:

Answer C. s.34(3); s.5 Limitation Act excluded.

14'Patent illegality' as a ground for setting aside an award is available in:

Answer B. s.34(2A).

15Which case held that Part I of the Act applies only to arbitrations seated in India?

Answer B. BALCO (2012), Constitution Bench.

16The 'group of companies doctrine' was recognised by the Constitution Bench in:

Answer A. 6 December 2023.

17Courts' limited power to modify arbitral awards was recognised in:

Answer B. Constitution Bench (30 April 2025), 4:1.

18An unstamped arbitration agreement is, per the 2023 seven-judge Bench:

Answer B. In re Interplay (13 Dec 2023).

19Which section provides for appeals from orders under the Act?

Answer C. s.37 - appealable orders.

20Is a second appeal available under s.37?

Answer B. s.37(3).

21A 'foreign award' is defined in:

Answer B. s.44 (Part II, Chapter I).

22Grounds for refusing enforcement of a foreign award are in:

Answer C. s.48.

23An appeal under Part II lies against an order:

Answer B. s.50 - appeal against refusals.

24A conciliation settlement agreement has the status of:

Answer B. s.74.

25Conciliation proceedings terminate on all of the following EXCEPT:

Answer D. s.76.

26Section 89 of the CPC provides for settlement of disputes through:

Answer A. s.89 CPC.

27An award of a Lok Adalat is:

Answer B. s.21 LSA Act.

28The Permanent Lok Adalat has jurisdiction over:

Answer B. ss.22A-22E LSA Act.

29The Legal Services Authorities Act 1987 gives effect to:

Answer C. Art 39A - equal justice and free legal aid.

30Pre-institution mediation for commercial suits is under:

Answer A. Mandatory per Patil Automation (2022) unless urgent interim relief is contemplated.

31Which body regulates mediators under the Mediation Act 2023?

Answer B. Mediation Council of India (set up 2026).
