Alternative Dispute Resolution

Latest developments

Recent judgments, amendments and institutional changes that are favourite current-affairs questions. Notes cover developments up to 19 Nov 2026; anything tagged Verify should be checked against the official source.

Gayatri Balasamy v. ISG Novasoft Technologies - courts may modify awards in limited ways

30 Apr 2025 (Constitution Bench, 4:1)

Held that courts under ss.34/37 have a limited power to modify awards - severance, correction of clerical/computational errors, post-award interest - and that the Supreme Court may modify awards under Art 142 in rare cases. Justice Viswanathan dissented.

Why it matters: Most important arbitration ruling of 2025; tests s.34 finality.

Likely question: Which Constitution Bench ruled on modification of awards?

Answer: Gayatri Balasamy

Unilateral appointment of arbitrators - CORE decision

Nov 2024 (Constitution Bench)

Clauses allowing a party to appoint a sole arbitrator or nominate from a panel it controls offend the principle of equal treatment; applied prospectively Verify.

Why it matters: Perkins Eastman / TRF line.

Likely question: Is unilateral appointment of a sole arbitrator valid?

Answer: No

Group of companies doctrine and stamping of arbitration agreements

Cox and Kings - 6 Dec 2023; In re Interplay - 13 Dec 2023

Non-signatories may be bound by arbitration agreements on the basis of consent and conduct; unstamped agreements are inadmissible but not void; tribunal decides.

Why it matters: Settles s.8/s.11 questions.

Likely question: Is an unstamped arbitration agreement void?

Answer: No

Mediation Council of India established

27 Aug 2026 Verify

Almost three years after the Mediation Act 2023 received assent, the Central Government notified the Mediation Council of India, the statutory regulator of mediators and service providers.

Why it matters: Statutory body questions.

Likely question: Which body regulates mediation under the Mediation Act 2023?

Answer: Mediation Council of India

Draft Arbitration and Conciliation (Amendment) Bill 2024

Draft circulated Oct 2024; not enacted as of Nov 2026 Verify

Proposals from the Expert Committee (Feb 2024): emergency arbitrators, an appellate arbitral tribunal, seat/venue clarity, narrower court intervention, institutional timelines. The Arbitration Council of India has not been constituted.

Why it matters: Reform trend.

Likely question: Which Part of the Act provides for the Arbitration Council of India?

Answer: Part IA
