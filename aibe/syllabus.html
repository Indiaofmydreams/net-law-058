AIBE SYLLABUS

Syllabus and tracker

AIBE covers 19 subjects. This page breaks each one into topic groups, with the provisions and cases to know, then lets you tick what you have covered and revised.

How to read this. The 19 subjects and their usual question counts are as reported for recent editions; the Bar Council of India publishes the syllabus for each edition and can change it. The topic breakdown under each subject is our own, built from the standard LL.B. coverage of the relevant Acts. Marks shown are approximate and total 100.

1Constitutional LawAbout 10 questions · 7 topic groups0%

Rights and duties

Preamble, features, Union and territory, citizenship (Arts 5 to 11); Part III in full: Art 12 and 13, equality (Arts 14 to 18), freedoms (Art 19), Arts 20 to 22, Art 21 and its expansion, religious freedom (Arts 25 to 28), cultural and educational rights (Arts 29, 30)
Writs and remedies: Arts 32 and 226, the five writs, grounds and limits; Directive Principles and Fundamental Duties and how they relate to Part III

Structure of government

Executive: President, Vice-President, Council of Ministers, Governor, ordinances, pardon; Parliament and State legislatures: composition, procedure, money bills, privileges, anti-defection (Tenth Schedule)
Judiciary: Supreme Court and High Court jurisdictions, appointments and the Collegium, judicial review, contempt; subordinate courts (Arts 233 to 237); tribunals
Federalism: legislative, administrative and financial relations, Seventh Schedule, GST Council, Finance Commission; Emergency provisions (Arts 352, 356, 360)
Amendment and Basic Structure (Art 368, Kesavananda Bharati, Minerva Mills); recent amendments; elections and the Election Commission, services under the Union and States (Arts 308 to 323), CAG, UPSC, Panchayats and Municipalities

Cases to know

Kesavananda Bharati, Maneka Gandhi, Minerva Mills, S.R. Bommai, Indra Sawhney, Vishaka, Puttaswamy, Navtej Johar, Shayara Bano, Kihoto Hollohan
Note: Recent developments are fair game: the 106th Amendment on women's reservation, the Article 370 and electoral bonds judgments, and sub-classification of Scheduled Castes.
2Indian Penal Code / Bharatiya Nyaya SanhitaAbout 8 questions · 6 topic groups0%

General part

Definitions and general principles: mens rea, stages of crime, territorial extent; general exceptions (mistake, accident, infancy, insanity, intoxication, private defence)
Joint and inchoate liability: common intention, common object, abetment, criminal conspiracy, attempt; punishments including community service

Offences

Against the State and public tranquillity: waging war, acts endangering sovereignty (which replaced sedition), unlawful assembly, rioting, affray
Against the human body: culpable homicide and murder, hurt and grievous hurt, wrongful restraint and confinement, kidnapping and abduction, sexual offences, cruelty by husband or relatives, dowry death, mob lynching
Against property: theft, extortion, robbery, dacoity, criminal misappropriation and breach of trust, cheating, mischief, criminal trespass, house-breaking; forgery, defamation, offences relating to marriage
New offences: organised crime, petty organised crime, terrorist acts, snatching, and the old IPC section each new provision replaced
Note: Questions often ask for the new number or the old IPC equivalent. Keep both in view and check a bare Act before relying on a section number.
3Criminal Procedure Code / Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha SanhitaAbout 10 questions · 5 topic groups0%

Investigation and arrest

Classification of offences (cognizable, non-cognizable, bailable, non-bailable); FIR, zero FIR and e-FIR; powers and duties of police; arrest, rights of the arrested person and notice of appearance
Search, seizure and summons, use of audio-video recording, forensic investigation for serious offences; time limits on investigation and the final report

Bail, trial and after

Bail: regular, anticipatory and default bail, bail for undertrial prisoners; security for peace and good behaviour; maintenance of wives, children and parents
Courts and trial: jurisdiction, cognizance, committal, charge, sessions, warrant and summons trials, summary trials, trial in absentia, plea bargaining, compounding
Judgment and review: sentencing and compensation to victims, appeals, reference, revision, inherent powers of the High Court, limitation for taking cognizance, transitional provisions
Note: As with the BNS, section numbers changed. Know the procedure first, then the new numbering.
4Code of Civil Procedure and LimitationAbout 10 questions · 6 topic groups0%

Suits

Jurisdiction and institution: pecuniary, territorial and subject-matter jurisdiction, place of suing, parties, joinder and misjoinder, representative suits
Pleadings: plaint, written statement, set-off and counterclaim, amendment, rejection of plaint; summons and service; appearance and ex parte orders
Res judicata and res sub judice, stay of suits, foreign judgments (ss.13 and 14), suits by or against the Government, suits by indigent persons, summary suits (Order 37)

Interim relief, appeals and execution

Interim orders: temporary injunctions, interim attachment, arrest before judgment, appointment of receiver, commissions, inherent powers (s.151)
Judgment and decree, costs, interest; appeals (first, second, appeals from orders), reference, review, revision; execution of decrees, modes and stay
Limitation Act 1963: periods for common suits and applications, when limitation begins, exclusion and acknowledgment, condonation of delay, continuing wrongs
5Evidence Act / Bharatiya Sakshya AdhiniyamAbout 8 questions · 5 topic groups0%

Relevancy and proof

Relevancy of facts: res gestae, motive, conduct, opinion of experts, character; admissions and confessions and their evidentiary value, dying declarations
Documentary and electronic evidence: primary and secondary evidence, the certificate for electronic records, public and private documents, presumptions about documents
Burden and standard of proof, presumptions, estoppel; judicial notice

Witnesses

Competence and compellability, examination-in-chief, cross-examination and re-examination, leading questions, hostile witnesses, refreshing memory
Privileged communications (including advocate-client privilege), accomplice evidence, improper admission or rejection of evidence
Note: The BSA replaced the Indian Evidence Act 1872. Learn the principles once, then map them to the new section numbers.
6Alternative Dispute ResolutionAbout 4 questions · 3 topic groups0%

Arbitration, conciliation and mediation

Arbitration and Conciliation Act 1996: arbitration agreement, appointment and jurisdiction of the tribunal, interim measures (ss.9 and 17), conduct of proceedings, the award, setting aside (s.34), appeals (s.37), enforcement
Foreign awards (Part II: New York and Geneva Conventions) and the 2015, 2019 and 2021 amendments; conciliation
Mediation Act 2023, Lok Adalats and the Legal Services Authorities Act, s.89 CPC settlement, legal aid
7Family LawAbout 8 questions · 4 topic groups0%

Personal laws

Hindu law: marriage and divorce under the Hindu Marriage Act, maintenance, adoption, guardianship, succession under the Hindu Succession Act including the 2005 amendment, joint family and coparcenary
Muslim law: sources, marriage and mehr, talaq and its forms, the 2019 Act on triple talaq, maintenance, inheritance, gift, wills, wakf
Christian and Parsi law: Indian Divorce Act, Indian Succession Act, Parsi marriage and divorce

General and protective laws

Special Marriage Act 1954, Guardians and Wards Act, Protection of Women from Domestic Violence Act 2005, Dowry Prohibition Act, maintenance across personal laws, live-in relationships
8Public Interest LitigationAbout 4 questions · 2 topic groups0%

Concept and cases

Meaning and development: relaxation of locus standi, epistolary jurisdiction, Arts 32 and 226, amicus curiae, continuing mandamus, court-appointed commissions
Leading PILs: Hussainara Khatoon, Bandhua Mukti Morcha, Vishaka, M.C. Mehta, Olga Tellis; abuse of PIL, costs and guidelines for genuine petitions
9Administrative LawAbout 3 questions · 2 topic groups0%

Principles

Delegated legislation and its control; natural justice (audi alteram partem, bias, reasoned orders); judicial review of administrative action, Wednesbury reasonableness and proportionality
Liability of the State, promissory estoppel and legitimate expectation, tribunals, Lokpal and Lokayuktas, Right to Information Act 2005
10Professional Ethics and Professional MisconductAbout 4 questions · 4 topic groups0%

Advocates Act and the Bar Council Rules

Advocates Act 1961: State Bar Councils and the Bar Council of India, enrolment and disqualification, rights of advocates, the right to practise
Standards of professional conduct (Bar Council of India Rules): duties to the court, to the client, to the opponent and to colleagues; restrictions on other employment; advertising and solicitation
Professional misconduct and the disciplinary procedure (ss.35 and 36 and appeals), punishments; strikes by lawyers (Harish Uppal); contempt of court as it affects advocates; advocate-client privilege

Cases

In re Vinay Chandra Mishra, Harish Uppal v Union of India, R.D. Saxena v Balram Prasad Sharma, Bar Council of India v A.K. Balaji
Note: These are among the most predictable marks in the paper. The Rules are short and the questions are close to the text.
11Company LawAbout 2 questions · 1 topic groups0%

Companies Act 2013

Incorporation and types: corporate personality, MoA and AoA, promoters, prospectus, shares and share capital; directors, meetings and resolutions; winding up, NCLT and NCLAT, oppression and mismanagement
12Environmental LawAbout 2 questions · 1 topic groups0%

Statutes and principles

Environment (Protection) Act 1986, Water Act 1974, Air Act 1981, Wildlife Protection Act 1972, Forest laws, National Green Tribunal; principles: polluter pays, precautionary principle, sustainable development, public trust
13Cyber LawAbout 2 questions · 1 topic groups0%

Information Technology and data

Information Technology Act 2000: electronic records and signatures, offences, intermediary liability and blocking powers, Shreya Singhal and s.66A; Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023
14Labour and Industrial LawAbout 4 questions · 2 topic groups0%

Classic statutes and the Labour Codes

Industrial Disputes Act 1947: industry, workman, retrenchment, lay-off, strikes and lockouts, settlement; Trade Unions Act; Factories Act, Minimum Wages, Payment of Wages, Gratuity, EPF, ESI, Employees' Compensation, Maternity Benefit
The four Labour Codes (Wages, Industrial Relations, Social Security, Occupational Safety) and the laws they absorb
Note: Check which provisions and rules of the Labour Codes are currently in force before answering a 'current position' question.
15Torts, Motor Vehicles Act and Consumer ProtectionAbout 5 questions · 3 topic groups0%

Torts

General principles, negligence, strict and absolute liability, nuisance, trespass, defamation, vicarious liability, defences, remedies

Statutes

Motor Vehicles Act 1988: licensing, third-party insurance, no-fault liability, Motor Accident Claims Tribunals, compensation
Consumer Protection Act 2019: consumer, deficiency, unfair trade practice, product liability, the District, State and National Commissions and their jurisdiction, CCPA
16TaxationAbout 4 questions · 1 topic groups0%

Direct and indirect tax

Income tax: residence, heads of income, exemptions and deductions, assessment, appeals and penalties; GST: supply, input tax credit, the GST Council, the CGST and IGST Acts
Note: Watch: the Income-tax Act 2025 is intended to replace the 1961 Act. Check the current position and terminology.
17Contract, Specific Relief, Property and Negotiable InstrumentsAbout 8 questions · 4 topic groups0%

Contract and specific relief

Indian Contract Act 1872: offer and acceptance, consideration, capacity, free consent, void and voidable agreements, performance, discharge, frustration, quasi-contracts, breach and damages, indemnity, guarantee, bailment, agency
Sale of Goods Act 1930, Partnership Act 1932; Specific Relief Act 1963 as amended in 2018: specific performance, injunctions, rectification, cancellation

Property and negotiable instruments

Transfer of Property Act 1882: sale, mortgage, lease, gift, exchange, actionable claims, doctrines (lis pendens, part performance, feeding the grant, election); Easements Act; Registration Act
Negotiable Instruments Act 1881: promissory note, bill of exchange and cheque, holder in due course, dishonour and notice, the offence under s.138 and its procedure
18Land AcquisitionAbout 2 questions · 1 topic groups0%

LARR Act 2013

Right to Fair Compensation and Transparency in Land Acquisition, Rehabilitation and Resettlement Act 2013: public purpose, social impact assessment, consent, compensation (market value, multiplier, solatium), rehabilitation and resettlement, procedure and lapse of acquisition; the 1894 Act in the background

Statutes

Patents (patentability, s.3(d), term), copyright (authorship, term, fair dealing, moral rights), trade marks (registration, infringement, passing off), designs, geographical indications, remedies
