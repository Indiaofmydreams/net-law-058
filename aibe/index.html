How this section is different

No timer, by design

Nothing on the AIBE practice or mock pages counts down. Build coverage and accuracy first. When you want exam pace, time yourself off-site: the real paper gives about two minutes per question.

Built for a licence exam

AIBE is a pass-or-fail qualifying test with no negative marking, so every subject counts and no question should be left blank. Mock results show your score against the pass mark and where marks leaked, subject by subject.

Revision is automatic

You never copy a mistake into a notebook. It lands there on its own, along with questions you bookmark. Your data stays in your browser, with backup and restore.
