About this mock test

AIBE Mock Test 5 is a free, timed practice paper in the AIBE pattern: 100 standalone questions in 210 minutes (3 hours 30 minutes), one mark each, with no negative marking. A harder paper with finer distinctions, closer-reading questions and more case law. Attempt it once you are scoring comfortably above the pass mark. Subjects covered: Administrative Law; Alternative Dispute Resolution; Civil Procedure (CPC); Company Law; Constitutional Law; Contract, Specific Relief & Sale of Goods; Criminal Law (BNS); Criminal Procedure (BNSS); Cyber Law; Environmental Law; Evidence (BSA); Family Law; Intellectual Property Law; Interpretation of Statutes; Jurisprudence; Labour Law; Land Acquisition Law; Limitation Law; Professional Ethics & Advocates Act; Property Law; Public International Law; Taxation; Tort & Consumer Law. Your answers and result stay in your browser; there is no sign-up.
