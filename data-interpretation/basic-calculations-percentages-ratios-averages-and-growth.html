9Two successive increases of 10% each are equivalent to a single increase of:

  1. 20%
  2. 21%
  3. 22%
  4. 10%
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. 21%

1.10 × 1.10 = 1.21, so the net increase is 21%.

18The average of five numbers is 24. If one number, 30, is removed, the average of the remaining four is:

  1. 23
  2. 24
  3. 21.5
  4. 22.5
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. 22.5

Total = 5 × 24 = 120. Remaining sum = 120 − 30 = 90. Average = 90 ÷ 4 = 22.5.
