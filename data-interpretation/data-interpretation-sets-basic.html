About this question set

This set has 15 multiple-choice questions on Data Interpretation Sets (Basic) from the UGC NET Paper 1 syllabus, unit 7, Data Interpretation. Try each question first, then tap Show answer to check the key and read the explanation. 4 of the 15 questions carry a written explanation.

For the concepts and case law behind these questions, read the Data Interpretation notes. Once you are comfortable, test yourself under exam conditions with a full-length mock test.

1Study the following data and answer the question.
Rice production (in lakh tonnes)
Year | 2019 | 2020 | 2021 | 2022 | 2023
Production | 120 | 132 | 150 | 165 | 180

The percentage increase in production from 2019 to 2021 is:

  1. 20%
  2. 30%
  3. 15%
  4. 25%
Show answer

Answer: D. 25%

2Study the following data and answer the question.
Rice production (in lakh tonnes)
Year | 2019 | 2020 | 2021 | 2022 | 2023
Production | 120 | 132 | 150 | 165 | 180

In which year was the percentage increase over the previous year the highest?

  1. 2020
  2. 2022
  3. 2023
  4. 2021
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. 2021

Yearly increases are 10% (2020), 13.64% (2021), 10% (2022) and 9.09% (2023), so 2021 is highest.

3Study the following data and answer the question.

The percentage increase in production from 2020 to 2023 is approximately:

Answer: B. 36.36%

(180 − 132) ÷ 132 = 36.36%.
