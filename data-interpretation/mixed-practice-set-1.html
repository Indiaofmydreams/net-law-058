About this question set

This set has 15 multiple-choice questions on Data Interpretation from the UGC NET Paper 1 syllabus, unit 7, Data Interpretation. Try each question first, then tap Show answer to check the key and read the explanation. 15 of the 15 questions carry a written explanation.

For the concepts and case law behind these questions, read the Data Interpretation notes. Once you are comfortable, test yourself under exam conditions with a full-length mock test.

Topics in this set

  • General concepts (15)

15 practice questions with answers

1Data collected first-hand by the researcher is:

  1. Historical data
  2. Secondary data
  3. Derived data
  4. Primary data
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Primary data

Primary data is original.

Report this question

2A histogram is used to represent:

  1. Qualitative categories only
  2. Time zones
  3. Maps
  4. Continuous frequency distribution
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Continuous frequency distribution

Bars touch for continuous data.

Report this question

3A pie chart shows data as:

  1. Proportions of a whole
  2. Trends over time
  3. Correlation
  4. Raw lists
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Proportions of a whole

Sectors represent parts of 100%.

Report this question

4Data that can be counted or measured numerically is:

  1. Narrative
  2. Quantitative
  3. Qualitative
  4. Descriptive
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Quantitative

Numerical data.

Report this question

5The mean of 5, 10 and 15 is:

  1. 5
  2. 10
  3. 15
  4. 20
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. 10

30 ÷ 3 = 10.

Report this question

6Sales rose from 100 in 2021 to 120 in 2022. The percentage increase is:

  1. 10%
  2. 15%
  3. 20%
  4. 25%
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. 20%

Increase 20 on 100 = 20%.

Report this question

7A pie-chart sector of 90° represents:

  1. 10%
  2. 20%
  3. 25%
  4. 30%
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. 25%

90/360 = 25%.

Report this question

8The best graph for showing change over time is a:

  1. Scatter of names
  2. Pie chart
  3. Venn diagram
  4. Line chart
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Line chart

Line charts show trends.

Report this question

9Consider the statements: 1. Qualitative data is descriptive. 2. Gender is quantitative data.

  1. Only statement 1 is correct
  2. Only statement 2 is correct
  3. Both statements are correct
  4. Neither statement is correct
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Only statement 1 is correct

Gender is categorical (qualitative).

Report this question

10Assertion (A): Data interpretation requires careful reading of scales and units.
Reason (R): Misreading units can lead to wrong conclusions.

  1. Both A and R are true, and R is the correct explanation of A
  2. Both A and R are true, but R is not the correct explanation of A
  3. A is true, but R is false
  4. A is false, but R is true
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Both A and R are true, and R is the correct explanation of A

R explains A.

Report this question

11The median of 40, 50, 60, 70, 80 is:

  1. 50
  2. 55
  3. 60
  4. 65
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. 60

Middle value.

Report this question

12The mode of 2, 3, 3, 4, 5 is:

  1. 2
  2. 3
  3. 4
  4. 5
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. 3

Most frequent.

Report this question

13The range of 12, 18, 25, 30 is:

  1. 12
  2. 16
  3. 18
  4. 20
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. 18

30 − 12.

1445 out of 180 is what percent?

  1. 20%
  2. 25%
  3. 30%
  4. 45%
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. 25%

45/180 = 25%.

15Population grew from 2,000 to 2,500. Percentage growth is:

  1. 20%
  2. 25%
  3. 30%
  4. 50%
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. 25%

500/2000.
