This set has 25 multiple-choice questions on Table-Based Data Interpretation Sets from the UGC NET Paper 1 syllabus, unit 7, Data Interpretation. Try each question first, then tap Show answer to check the key and read the explanation. 25 of the 25 questions carry a written explanation.
