Sample questions from this test

Try these five before you start. They are part of the paper, so you may prefer to skip the answers until after you have attempted it.

1The remoteness rule in contract damages comes from:

  1. Hadley v. Baxendale
  2. Donoghue
  3. Salomon
  4. Wagon Mound
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Hadley v. Baxendale

Reflected in Section 73 of the Contract Act.

Report this question

2The M'Naghten rules relate to:

  1. Duress
  2. Necessity
  3. Intoxication
  4. Insanity
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Insanity

Laid down in 1843 as the test of insanity.

Report this question

3Stridhan was considered in:

  1. Kesavananda
  2. Pratibha Rani v. Suraj Kumar
  3. Vishaka
  4. Shah Bano
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Pratibha Rani v. Suraj Kumar

The wife has absolute rights over her stridhan.

Report this question

4The 'rule of recognition' is a concept of:

  1. Holmes
  2. Austin
  3. Hart
  4. Pound
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Hart

It is the secondary rule that identifies valid rules of a legal system.

Report this question

5Customary international law requires state practice and:

  1. Court judgment
  2. Treaty ratification
  3. Opinio juris
  4. UN resolution
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Opinio juris

Opinio juris is the belief that the practice is legally obligatory.
