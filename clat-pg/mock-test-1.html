About this mock test

CLAT PG Mock Test 1 is a free, timed practice paper in the CLAT PG pattern: 120 passage-based questions in 120 minutes, one mark each with 0.25 deducted for each wrong answer. A balanced full-length paper across the core CLAT PG subjects: constitutional law, jurisprudence, criminal law, contract, torts, property, IPR, family law and more. Subjects covered: Administrative Law, Arbitration Law, Constitutional Law, Criminal Law, Criminal Procedure, Environmental Law, Family Law, Insolvency and Company Law, Intellectual Property Law, Jurisprudence, Labour Law, Law of Contract, Law of Evidence, Law of Torts, Property Law, Public International Law. Your answers and result stay in your browser; there is no sign-up.
