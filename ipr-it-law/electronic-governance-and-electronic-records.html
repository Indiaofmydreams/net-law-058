8 practice questions with answers

1The Information Technology Act, 2000 is primarily based on the:

  1. EU Directive on E-Commerce (2000)
  2. US Electronic Signatures in Global and National Commerce Act (2000)
  3. Berne Convention
  4. UNCITRAL Model Law on Electronic Commerce (1996)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. UNCITRAL Model Law on Electronic Commerce (1996)

The IT Act, 2000 is modelled substantially on the UNCITRAL Model Law on Electronic Commerce (1996), adopted by the UN General Assembly.

Report this question

2The IT Act, 2000 received the assent of the President of India on:

  1. 9 June 2000
  2. 9 May 2000
  3. 17 October 2000
  4. 1 January 2001
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. 9 June 2000

The Information Technology Act, 2000 received Presidential assent on 9 June 2000.

Report this question

3The IT Act, 2000 came into force on:

  1. 9 June 2000
  2. 17 October 2000
  3. 1 April 2001
  4. 9 May 2000
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. 17 October 2000

The Information Technology Act, 2000 came into force on 17 October 2000.

Report this question

4Section 4 of the IT Act, 2000 deals with:

  1. Legal recognition of digital signatures
  2. Appointment of the Controller of Certifying Authorities
  3. Establishment of Cyber Appellate Tribunal
  4. Legal recognition of electronic records
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Legal recognition of electronic records

Section 4 provides that where any law requires that information or any other matter shall be in writing or in typewritten or printed form, such requirement shall be deemed to have been satisfied if such information or matter is rendered or made available in an electronic form.

Report this question

5Section 6 of the IT Act, 2000 deals with:

  1. Legal recognition of electronic signatures
  2. Retention of electronic records
  3. Publication of rules in the Official Gazette
  4. Use of electronic records and electronic signatures in Government and its agencies
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Use of electronic records and electronic signatures in Government and its agencies

Section 6 enables the government and its agencies to accept the filing, creation, retention and issue of any form or document in electronic form and the use of electronic signatures for such purposes.

Report this question

6India became the ________ country in the world to enact a cyber law:

  1. 5th
  2. 8th
  3. 12th
  4. 15th
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. 12th

India became the 12th nation in the world to enact a comprehensive cyber law with the passage of the Information Technology Act, 2000.

Report this question

7The UNCITRAL Model Law on Electronic Commerce was adopted in:

  1. 1994
  2. 1996
  3. 1998
  4. 2000
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. 1996

The UNCITRAL Model Law on Electronic Commerce was adopted by the United Nations Commission on International Trade Law (UNCITRAL) in 1996.

Report this question

8Section 10A, inserted by the IT Amendment Act 2008, deals with:

  1. Regulation of Certifying Authorities
  2. Validity of contracts formed through electronic communication
  3. Cyber terrorism
  4. Protection of sensitive personal data
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Validity of contracts formed through electronic communication

Section 10A, introduced by the 2008 amendment, provides that where in a contract formation, the communication of proposals, acceptance of proposals, the revocation of proposals and acceptances as the case may be, are expressed in electronic form, such contract shall not be deemed to be unenforceable solely on the ground that such electronic form was used.
