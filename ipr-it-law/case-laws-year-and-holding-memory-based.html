This set has 9 multiple-choice questions on Case Laws: Year and Holding (Memory-Based) from the UGC NET Law (058) syllabus, unit 9, Intellectual Property Rights and Information Technology Law. 9 practice questions with answers

1In which year did the Supreme Court of India decide Novartis AG v. Union of India (Section 3(d) case)?

  1. 2011
  2. 2012
  3. 2013
  4. 2014
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. 2013

The Supreme Court of India decided Novartis AG v. Union of India on 1 April 2013, upholding Section 3(d) of the Patents Act and refusing the patent for the beta-crystalline form of Imatinib Mesylate (Gleevec).

2Shreya Singhal v. Union of India, striking down Section 66A of the IT Act, was decided in:

  1. 2013
  2. 2014
  3. 2015
  4. 2016
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. 2015

Shreya Singhal v. Union of India was decided by the Supreme Court on 24 March 2015.

3The Supreme Court's landmark ruling in Eastern Book Company v. D.B. Modak, rejecting the 'sweat of the brow' doctrine, was given in:

  1. 2005
  2. 2006
  3. 2007
  4. 2008
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. 2008

Eastern Book Company v. D.B. Modak was decided by the Supreme Court of India in 2008.

Report this question

4The Delhi High Court recognised and enforced moral rights of a sculptor under Section 57 of the Copyright Act in the case of Amar Nath Sehgal v. Union of India in:

  1. 2002
  2. 2003
  3. 2004
  4. 2005
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. 2005

Amar Nath Sehgal v. Union of India was decided by the Delhi High Court in 2005. The court awarded damages for destruction of the sculptor's mural and recognised the right of integrity under Section 57.

Report this question

5The Supreme Court decided N.R. Dongre v. Whirlpool Corporation (transborder reputation case) in:

  1. 1994
  2. 1995
  3. 1996
  4. 1997
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. 1996

N.R. Dongre v. Whirlpool Corporation was decided by the Supreme Court in 1996, establishing that a mark with transborder reputation can be protected in India even without prior use in India.

Report this question

6The Delhi High Court decided Yahoo Inc. v. Akash Arora (India's first cybersquatting case) in:

  1. 1997
  2. 1998
  3. 1999
  4. 2000
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. 1999

Yahoo Inc. v. Akash Arora was decided by the Delhi High Court in 1999. It was India's first major domain name/cybersquatting dispute.

Report this question

7The R.G. Anand v. Deluxe Films case on idea-expression dichotomy was decided by the Supreme Court in:

  1. 1975
  2. 1978
  3. 1980
  4. 1982
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. 1978

R.G. Anand v. M/s Deluxe Films & Ors. was decided by the Supreme Court in 1978. It remains the leading Indian authority on the idea-expression dichotomy in copyright law.

Report this question

8The US Supreme Court decided Diamond v. Chakrabarty (living organisms as patentable subject matter) in:

  1. 1978
  2. 1979
  3. 1980
  4. 1981
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. 1980

Diamond v. Chakrabarty was decided by the US Supreme Court in 1980. The court held (5-4) that a live, human-made microorganism is patentable subject matter.

9India's first compulsory licence (Natco v. Bayer, for Nexavar/Sorafenib) was granted by the Patent Controller in:

  1. 2011
  2. 2012
  3. 2013
  4. 2014
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. 2012

The Patent Controller granted India's first compulsory licence to Natco Pharma Ltd. for Sorafenib Tosylate (Nexavar) on 9 March 2012.
