About this question set

This set has 15 multiple-choice questions on International Conventions Pertaining to Intellectual Property from the UGC NET Law (058) syllabus, unit 9, Intellectual Property Rights and Information Technology Law. Try each question first, then tap Show answer to check the key and read the explanation. 15 of the 15 questions carry a written explanation.

For the concepts and case law behind these questions, read the IPR & IT Law notes. Once you are comfortable, test yourself under exam conditions with a full-length mock test.

15 practice questions with answers

1The Paris Convention (1883) deals primarily with:

  1. Industrial property (patents, trademarks, GI)
  2. Copyright protection
  3. Performers' rights
  4. Plant variety protection
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Industrial property (patents, trademarks, GI)

The Paris Convention (1883) is the foundational international treaty for industrial property including patents, trademarks, industrial designs, and geographical indications.

Report this question

2The 'right of priority' under the Paris Convention for patents is:

  1. 6 months
  2. 12 months
  3. 18 months
  4. 24 months
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. 12 months

Under Article 4 of the Paris Convention, the right of priority for patents is 12 months from the date of first filing.

Report this question

3The 'right of priority' under the Paris Convention for trademarks and industrial designs is:

  1. 12 months
  2. 3 months
  3. 18 months
  4. 6 months
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. 6 months

The right of priority for trademarks and industrial designs under the Paris Convention is 6 months.

Report this question

4The Berne Convention (1886) is administered by:

  1. WIPO
  2. WTO
  3. UNESCO
  4. UNCTAD
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. WIPO

The Berne Convention is administered by the World Intellectual Property Organization (WIPO), headquartered in Geneva, Switzerland.

Report this question

5Under the Berne Convention, copyright protection:

  1. Arises automatically upon creation of the work
  2. Requires prior registration
  3. Requires payment of fees
  4. Arises only after publication
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Arises automatically upon creation of the work

The Berne Convention establishes the principle of automatic protection — no formalities such as registration or deposit are required.

Report this question

6The minimum term of copyright protection under the Berne Convention is:

  1. 50 years flat
  2. Life of author + 25 years
  3. Life of author + 50 years
  4. Life of author + 70 years
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Life of author + 50 years

The Berne Convention requires a minimum term of copyright protection of the author's lifetime plus 50 years.

Report this question

7TRIPS Agreement was signed in the year:

  1. 1992
  2. 1993
  3. 1994
  4. 1995
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. 1994

The TRIPS Agreement was concluded as part of the Uruguay Round Multilateral Trade Negotiations and signed at Marrakesh on 15 April 1994.

Report this question

8TRIPS Agreement is Annex to which Agreement?

  1. Paris Convention
  2. Berne Convention
  3. Agreement Establishing the WTO
  4. WIPO Convention
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Agreement Establishing the WTO

TRIPS (Agreement on Trade-Related Aspects of Intellectual Property Rights) is Annex 1C to the Agreement Establishing the World Trade Organization.

Report this question

9The Patent Cooperation Treaty (PCT) facilitates:

  1. Filing of a single international patent application effective in multiple countries
  2. Harmonisation of patent laws
  3. Automatic grant of patent in all member states
  4. Registration of industrial designs internationally
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Filing of a single international patent application effective in multiple countries

The PCT (1970) allows an inventor to file a single international patent application that can be pursued in multiple member countries.

Report this question

10The Budapest Treaty (1977) is relevant for:

  1. Trademark registration
  2. Deposit of microorganisms for patent purposes
  3. Protection of plant varieties
  4. Copyright in computer programs
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Deposit of microorganisms for patent purposes

The Budapest Treaty allows a single deposit of a microorganism at an International Depositary Authority (IDA) to satisfy the disclosure requirement for patent applications in multiple member states.

Report this question

11WIPO was formally established by the WIPO Convention of:

  1. 1961
  2. 1967
  3. 1970
  4. 1974
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. 1967

The Convention Establishing the World Intellectual Property Organization was signed in Stockholm on 14 July 1967.

Report this question

12WIPO became a specialised agency of the United Nations in:

  1. 1967
  2. 1970
  3. 1974
  4. 1980
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. 1974

WIPO became a specialized agency of the United Nations in 1974.

Report this question

13The WIPO Copyright Treaty (WCT) 1996 primarily addresses:

  1. Protection of copyright in the digital environment
  2. Performers' rights
  3. International patent applications
  4. Plant variety protection
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Protection of copyright in the digital environment

The WCT (1996) is a special agreement under the Berne Convention that addresses copyright protection in the digital environment.

Report this question

14The Marrakesh Treaty (2013) is aimed at facilitating access to published works for:

  1. Developing countries
  2. Indigenous communities
  3. Persons with print disabilities / visually impaired
  4. Broadcasting organizations
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Persons with print disabilities / visually impaired

The Marrakesh VIP Treaty (2013) facilitates access to published works for persons who are blind, visually impaired, or otherwise print disabled.

Report this question

15Under TRIPS Agreement, what is the minimum period of patent protection?

  1. 15 years
  2. 25 years
  3. 20 years
  4. 10 years
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. 20 years

TRIPS Article 33 requires that the term of patent protection shall not end before the expiration of 20 years counted from the filing date.
