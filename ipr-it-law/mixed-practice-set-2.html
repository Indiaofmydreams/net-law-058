1Copyright subsists in:

  1. Methods
  2. Facts
  3. Original literary, dramatic, musical and artistic works

Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Original literary, dramatic, musical and artistic works

Expression, not ideas.

  1. Basmati Rice
  2. Mysore Silk

  4. Kanchipuram Silk

21Under the TRIPS Agreement, geographical indications for wines and spirits receive:

  1. Higher/absolute protection (Art. 23)
  2. No special protection
  3. Ordinary protection (Art. 22)
  4. Protection only in WTO member states
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Higher/absolute protection (Art. 23)

TRIPS Article 23 provides additional protection for GIs for wines and spirits — they are protected absolutely against use for wines/spirits not originating from the indicated place, even if there is no confusion or if the true origin is indicated.

Report this question

22The 'Basmati Rice' dispute between India and USA involved:

  1. Trademark registration
  2. Copyright claim
  3. Compulsory licensing
  4. Patent grant to an American company for Basmati rice varieties
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Patent grant to an American company for Basmati rice varieties

RiceTec Inc. of the USA obtained a US patent on certain Basmati rice lines and grains. India challenged this as biopiracy of traditional Indian agricultural heritage, and several patent claims were subsequently cancelled.
