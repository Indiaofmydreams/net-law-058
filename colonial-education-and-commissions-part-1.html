About this question set

This set has 16 multiple-choice questions on Colonial Education and Commissions from the UGC NET Paper 1 syllabus, unit 10, Higher Education System. 1The Charter Act of 1813 earmarked an annual sum for education of:

  1. Rs 10 lakh
