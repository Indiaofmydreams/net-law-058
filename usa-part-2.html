About this question set

This set has 21 multiple-choice questions on USA from the UGC NET Law (058) syllabus, unit 10, Comparative Public Law and Systems of Governance. Try each question first, then tap Show answer to check the key. This set is an answer-key drill: check each answer, then revisit the notes for any you miss.

For the concepts and case law behind these questions, read the Comparative Public Law notes. Once you are comfortable, test yourself under exam conditions with a full-length mock test.

21 practice questions with answers

1The "actual malice" standard for defamation of public officials originated in:

  1. Gertz v. Welch
  2. Near v. Minnesota
  3. New York Times v. Sullivan
  4. Hustler v. Falwell
Show answer

Answer: C. New York Times v. Sullivan

2District of Columbia v. Heller (2008) recognised:

  1. An individual right to keep and bear arms
  2. A collective militia-based right only
  3. A right to abortion
  4. A right to privacy in homes
Show answer

Answer: A. An individual right to keep and bear arms

3Obergefell v. Hodges (2015) recognised same-sex marriage under:

  1. The Ninth Amendment alone
  2. The Due Process and Equal Protection Clauses
  3. The Commerce Clause
  4. The First Amendment
Show answer

Answer: B. The Due Process and Equal Protection Clauses

4The right to state-appointed counsel for indigent felony defendants was established in:

  1. Escobedo v. Illinois
  2. Terry v. Ohio

  4. Gideon v. Wainwright

5The exclusionary rule was made applicable to the states in:

  1. Weeks v. US
  2. Katz v. US
  3. Mapp v. Ohio
  4. Olmstead v. US

6In Trump v. United States (2024), the Supreme Court held that a former President has:

  1. Immunity for official acts within core constitutional powers, with no immunity for unofficial acts
  2. Absolute immunity for all acts
  3. No immunity of any kind
  4. Immunity only while in office
Show answer

Answer: A. Immunity for official acts within core constitutional powers, with no immunity for unofficial acts
