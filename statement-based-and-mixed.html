About this question set

This set has 15 multiple-choice questions on Statement-Based & Mixed from the UGC NET Law (058) syllabus, unit 2, Constitutional and Administrative Law. Try each question first, then tap Show answer to check the key and read the explanation. 15 of the 15 questions carry a written explanation.

For the concepts and case law behind these questions, read the Constitutional & Administrative Law notes. Once you are comfortable, test yourself under exam conditions with a full-length mock test.

15 practice questions with answers

1I. The 73rd Amendment introduced Part IX and Eleventh Schedule.
II. The 74th Amendment introduced Part IXA and Twelfth Schedule.

  1. Both I and II are correct
  2. Only I is correct
  3. Only II is correct
  4. Neither I nor II is correct
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Both I and II are correct

Both statements are correct.

  1. Both I and II are correct
  2. Only I is correct
  3. Only II is correct
  4. Neither I nor II is correct

3I. The Tenth Schedule was inserted by the 52nd Amendment.
II. The Tenth Schedule was struck down in its entirety in Kihoto Hollohan.

  1. Both I and II are correct
  2. Only I is correct
  3. Only II is correct
  4. Neither I nor II is correct
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Only I is correct

Only para 7 was struck down; the Schedule was upheld.

4I. The right to property is a Fundamental Right under Art. 31.
II. The 44th Amendment deleted Art. 31 and Art. 19(1)(f).

  1. Both I and II are correct
  2. Only I is correct
  3. Only II is correct
  4. Neither I nor II is correct
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Only II is correct

Right to property ceased to be a Fundamental Right in 1978; Art. 300A now guarantees a legal right.

  3. Only II is correct
  4. Neither I nor II is correct

Answer: C. Only II is correct

Art. 31C was inserted by the 25th and extended by the 42nd.

10I. The 97th Amendment added the right to form co-operative societies to Art. 19(1)(c).
II. Part IXB was struck down in its entirety in Rajendra N. Shah.

  1. Both I and II are correct
  2. Only I is correct
  3. Only II is correct
  4. Neither I nor II is correct
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Only I is correct

Struck down only for State co-operative societies for want of ratification; remains for multi-State societies.

11Article 368 as it originally stood did not contain the term 'Constitutional Amendment' in its marginal note. Which Amendment added the words 'and procedure therefor'?

  1. Twenty-fourth Amendment
  2. Forty-fourth Amendment
  3. Thirty-ninth Amendment
  4. Forty-second Amendment
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Twenty-fourth Amendment

Marginal note changed to 'Power of Parliament to amend the Constitution and procedure therefor'.

Report this question

12Which Amendment introduced the Preamble words 'Socialist', 'Secular' and 'Integrity'?

  1. Fifty-second Amendment, 1985
  2. Forty-second Amendment, 1976
  3. Forty-fourth Amendment, 1978
  4. Twenty-fourth Amendment, 1971
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Forty-second Amendment, 1976

Challenged unsuccessfully in S.R. Bommai/other cases; Balram Singh v. Union of India (2024) upheld the 42nd Amendment insertions.

Report this question

13Which Amendment introduced Article 51A on Fundamental Duties?

  1. Sixty-first Amendment
  2. Eighty-sixth Amendment
  3. Forty-second Amendment
  4. Forty-fourth Amendment
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Forty-second Amendment

On the recommendation of the Swaran Singh Committee.

14Which Amendment removed 'right to property' from the list of Fundamental Rights?

  1. Twenty-fifth Amendment
  2. Forty-fourth Amendment
  3. Fourth Amendment
  4. Forty-second Amendment
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Forty-fourth Amendment

Art. 300A.

15The Thirty-eighth Amendment, relating to Art. 352, was subsequently modified by the:

  1. Fifty-second Amendment
  2. Forty-second Amendment
  3. Forty-fourth Amendment
  4. Forty-third Amendment
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Forty-fourth Amendment

The 44th made Emergency proclamation justiciable on grounds of malafide/absence of material (Minerva Mills).
