CUET PG LLM SYLLABUS · COQP14

Syllabus and tracker

The Law (LL.M.) paper draws on 33 subject areas of the LL.B. curriculum. This page breaks each into topics with the cases and sections to know, then lets you tick what you have covered and revised.

How to read this. NTA publishes the subject list for the paper but no topic weightage. The subject list follows the COQP14 coverage reported for recent cycles (REPORTED); the topic breakdown and the core, regular and occasional tiers are our own editorial reading of the LL.B. curriculum and recent papers (EDITORIAL). The typical questions figure is an estimate over a 75-question paper, not an official number. Confirm the syllabus in the 2027 Information Bulletin.

1Constitution of IndiaCORE · 13 topics · about 8 to 12 questions0%

Foundations

Preamble and nature of the Constitution: sources, federal features, secularism, socialism, Preamble as part of the Constitution (Kesavananda Bharati; Berubari)
Citizenship (Arts 5 to 11) and the Citizenship Act 1955; State under Art 12 and Art 13 (eclipse, severability, waiver, laws inconsistent with fundamental rights)

Fundamental rights and duties

Equality (Arts 14 to 18): reasonable classification, arbitrariness (E.P. Royappa), reservations (Indra Sawhney, M. Nagaraj, EWS 103rd Amendment, sub-classification in Davinder Singh 2024), untouchability
Freedoms (Art 19) and reasonable restrictions; Arts 20 to 22: ex post facto, double jeopardy, self-incrimination, arrest and preventive detention
Art 21 and its expansion: Maneka Gandhi, privacy (K.S. Puttaswamy), right to education Art 21A, dignity, euthanasia (Common Cause); Arts 23 to 30 (exploitation, religion, minorities, educational rights)
Constitutional remedies (Art 32) and writs: habeas corpus, mandamus, certiorari, prohibition, quo warranto; Art 226 and its wider scope; Fundamental duties (Art 51A)
Directive Principles (Part IV): classification, relation with fundamental rights (Champakam Dorairajan to Minerva Mills), uniform civil code (Art 44)

Government structure

Union executive: President (election, impeachment, ordinance power Art 123, pardon Art 72), Vice-President, Council of Ministers, Attorney General
Parliament: composition, sessions, money and financial bills, privileges, anti-defection (Tenth Schedule; Kihoto Hollohan), Speaker's role; State executive and legislature: Governor's discretion (Nabam Rebia, S.R. Bommai)
Judiciary: Supreme Court jurisdiction (original, appellate, advisory, review), appointment of judges (Second and Third Judges Cases, NJAC), judicial review, PIL, contempt of court
Federalism: legislative, administrative and financial relations, Seventh Schedule, doctrine of pith and substance, repugnancy (Art 254), inter-State water disputes, GST Council

Amendment and emergency

Amendment (Art 368) and the Basic Structure doctrine: Shankari Prasad, Golak Nath, Kesavananda Bharati, Indira Gandhi v Raj Narain, Minerva Mills, I.R. Coelho; key amendments (42nd, 44th, 73rd, 74th, 101st, 103rd, 106th)
Emergency provisions (Arts 352, 356, 360): grounds, judicial review, S.R. Bommai; special provisions for States and Union territories; Art 370 and the 2019 reorganisation
2Contract: General PrinciplesCORE · 5 topics · about 4 to 6 questions0%

Formation

Agreement and contract: offer, acceptance, communication and revocation, invitation to treat, tenders, standard-form contracts (Carlill; Harvey v Facey; Lalman Shukla)
Consideration (s.2(d), s.25): privity, past consideration, exceptions; capacity to contract: minors (Mohori Bibee), persons of unsound mind

Validity and performance

Free consent: coercion, undue influence, fraud, misrepresentation, mistake (ss.13 to 22); legality of object, void agreements (ss.23 to 30), wagering, restraint of trade
Contingent contracts, performance and discharge by agreement, performance, breach, frustration (s.56: Taylor v Caldwell; Satyabrata Ghose), quasi-contracts (ss.68 to 72)
Remedies: damages and remoteness (Hadley v Baxendale; s.73), liquidated damages and penalty (s.74), Specific Relief Act 1963 as amended in 2018, government contracts (Art 299), electronic contracts (IT Act)
3Law of Torts and Consumer ProtectionCORE · 5 topics · about 3 to 5 questions0%

Principles

Nature and general defences: tort versus crime and contract, damnum sine injuria and injuria sine damno, volenti non fit injuria, necessity, private defence, inevitable accident
Negligence: duty of care (Donoghue v Stevenson), breach, damage, res ipsa loquitur, contributory negligence; nuisance; trespass; defamation (libel, slander, defences)
Strict and absolute liability: Rylands v Fletcher, M.C. Mehta (Oleum gas), Bhopal gas leak; vicarious liability, state liability (Kasturilal; Nilabati Behera)

Statutes

Consumer Protection Act 2019: definitions, consumer rights, three-tier commissions and pecuniary jurisdiction, product liability, unfair trade practices, Central Consumer Protection Authority
Motor Vehicles Act 1988 (no-fault liability, compensation) and the Public Liability Insurance Act 1991
4Family Law I: Hindu LawCORE · 4 topics · about 2 to 4 questions0%

Marriage and the family

Sources and schools: Mitakshara and Dayabhaga, Hindu Undivided Family, karta, coparcenary, partition; Hindu Succession (Amendment) Act 2005 and Vineeta Sharma (2020)
Hindu Marriage Act 1955: conditions, void and voidable marriages, restitution of conjugal rights, judicial separation, divorce grounds, mutual consent, irretrievable breakdown (Shilpa Sailesh 2023), alimony

Succession, adoption, maintenance

Hindu Succession Act 1956: intestate succession of males and females, Class I and II heirs, stridhan, disqualifications; wills
Hindu Adoptions and Maintenance Act 1956, Hindu Minority and Guardianship Act 1956, maintenance of wife, children and parents; welfare of the child
5Criminal Law I: Substantive (IPC and BNS)CORE · 5 topics · about 4 to 6 questions0%

General principles

Crime and criminal liability: actus reus, mens rea, stages of a crime, general exceptions (IPC ss.76 to 106; BNS ss.14 to 44), insanity, intoxication, private defence
Group liability and inchoate offences: common intention (s.34, BNS s.3(5)), common object (s.149, BNS s.190), abetment, criminal conspiracy, attempt

Offences

Offences against the State and public tranquillity: sedition replaced by BNS s.152, unlawful assembly, rioting; offences against the human body: culpable homicide versus murder (IPC ss.299, 300; BNS ss.100, 101), hurt, grievous hurt
Sexual offences, kidnapping and abduction; property offences: theft, extortion, robbery, dacoity, criminal breach of trust, cheating; defamation, criminal intimidation, offences against marriage (adultery struck down in Joseph Shine)
Know both numbering systems: recent papers still use IPC terms, but the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita 2023 replaced the IPC from 1 July 2024; learn each key section with its BNS equivalent
6Contract II: Specific ContractsREGULAR · 4 topics · about 2 to 4 questions0%

Indian Contract Act 1872

Indemnity and guarantee (ss.124 to 147): continuing guarantee, rights and discharge of surety; bailment and pledge (ss.148 to 181): duties, lien, rights of pawnee
Agency (ss.182 to 238): creation, authority, ratification, sub-agent, personal liability, termination

Allied statutes

Sale of Goods Act 1930: conditions and warranties, passing of property, unpaid seller's rights, caveat emptor
Partnership Act 1932 (nature, rights, dissolution, registration) and the LLP Act 2008
7Company LawREGULAR · 3 topics · about 2 to 3 questions0%

Companies Act 2013

Incorporation and kinds of companies: corporate personality (Salomon), lifting the veil, memorandum and articles, promoters, one-person and small companies, doctrine of ultra vires and constructive notice
Shares, debentures and charges: prospectus, allotment, share capital, transfer; directors (appointment, duties, independent directors), meetings and resolutions

Regulation

Oppression and mismanagement, winding up, NCLT and NCLAT; SEBI Act 1992 and the securities market; FEMA 1999 basics; insolvency under the IBC 2016
8Property LawREGULAR · 3 topics · about 2 to 4 questions0%

Transfer of Property Act 1882

Concept of property, transfer and its operation, doctrines: lis pendens, part performance, election, feeding the grant by estoppel, benefit and burden, apportionment
Sale, mortgage, lease, gift, exchange: kinds of mortgage, rights of mortgagor and mortgagee, redemption, foreclosure, actionable claims

Allied

Indian Trusts Act 1882 (kinds of trust, trustee duties) and the Easements Act 1882; registration (Registration Act 1908)
9Administrative LawREGULAR · 3 topics · about 2 to 4 questions0%

Principles

Nature, scope and rule of law (Dicey), separation of powers, droit administratif; delegated legislation: need, limits, excessive delegation, control, sub-delegation
Natural justice: audi alteram partem, nemo judex, reasoned decisions, bias, exceptions (Ridge v Baldwin; Maneka Gandhi); administrative discretion and its judicial control, Wednesbury unreasonableness, proportionality, legitimate expectation

Remedies and bodies

Judicial review and writs; tribunals (Art 323A/B, L. Chandra Kumar, Tribunals Reforms Act 2021); liability of government, promissory estoppel, Ombudsman, Lokpal and Lokayuktas, CVC
10Family Law II: Muslim Law and SuccessionREGULAR · 4 topics · about 2 to 3 questions0%

Muslim law

Sources and schools: Quran, Sunna, ijma, qiyas; Hanafi, Shafi'i, Maliki, Hanbali, Shia; marriage (nikah: kinds, mehr or dower, guardianship), divorce (talaq forms, khula, mubarat, Dissolution of Muslim Marriages Act 1939)
Triple talaq: Shayara Bano (2017), Muslim Women (Protection of Rights on Marriage) Act 2019; maintenance: Shah Bano, Danial Latifi, Mohd Ahmed Khan; gifts (hiba), wills (wasiyat), wakf, pre-emption
Muslim inheritance: Quranic sharers, residuaries, distant kindred, Shia versus Sunni rules, guardianship and legitimacy

Succession generally

Indian Succession Act 1925: testamentary and intestate succession, probate, letters of administration; Parsi and Christian succession; Special Marriage Act 1954
11Environmental LawREGULAR · 3 topics · about 2 to 3 questions0%

Framework

Constitutional basis: Arts 21, 48A, 51A(g); principles: polluter pays, precautionary principle, sustainable development, public trust (M.C. Mehta; Vellore Citizens)
Statutes: Environment (Protection) Act 1986, Water Act 1974, Air Act 1981, Wildlife Protection Act 1972, Forest (Conservation) Act 1980, Biological Diversity Act 2002, NGT Act 2010
International: Stockholm 1972, Rio 1992, Kyoto, Paris Agreement 2015, Basel, CITES, Ramsar; EIA and Coastal Regulation Zone; Public Liability Insurance Act
12Labour LawOCCASIONAL · 2 topics · about 1 to 3 questions0%

Industrial relations

Industrial Disputes Act 1947: industry, workman, strike, lock-out, lay-off, retrenchment, closure; Trade Unions Act 1926; Industrial Employment (Standing Orders) Act
Wages and welfare: Minimum Wages, Payment of Wages, Bonus, Gratuity, Employees' Compensation, ESI, EPF; the four Labour Codes (Wages, Industrial Relations, Social Security, Occupational Safety), which consolidate many of these Acts: check their current commencement status
13Criminal Law II: Procedure (CrPC and BNSS)REGULAR · 3 topics · about 3 to 5 questions0%

Process

Structure of criminal courts, arrest and bail (bailable and non-bailable, anticipatory bail s.438 CrPC / s.482 BNSS, default bail), search and seizure, FIR (s.154; BNSS s.173 with zero FIR and e-FIR)
Investigation and cognizance, charge, kinds of trial (sessions, warrant, summons, summary), plea bargaining, compounding, appeals, reference, revision, inherent powers (s.482 CrPC / s.528 BNSS)
Maintenance under s.125 CrPC / s.144 BNSS; limitation for taking cognizance; Juvenile Justice (Care and Protection of Children) Act 2015; Probation of Offenders Act 1958
14JurisprudenceCORE · 5 topics · about 4 to 6 questions0%

Schools and theories

Natural law (Aquinas, Grotius, Fuller, Finnis), analytical positivism (Bentham, Austin: command theory, Kelsen: pure theory, Hart: rules and the rule of recognition)
Historical school (Savigny, Maine), sociological school (Ehrlich, Duguit, Pound: social engineering), legal realism (American and Scandinavian), Critical Legal Studies, feminist jurisprudence
Indian jurisprudence: dharma, Manu, Kautilya, Gandhi, Ambedkar; the Constitution as a social document

Concepts

Sources of law: custom, legislation, precedent (stare decisis, ratio and obiter, per incuriam), juristic writings; nature of law, justice (Rawls, Nozick, Sen, Bentham's utility)
Rights and duties (Hohfeld's jural correlatives), legal personality (corporations, idols, rivers: Mohd Salim; animals), ownership, possession, title, liability, obligations
15Professional EthicsOCCASIONAL · 2 topics · about 1 to 2 questions0%

Advocates Act 1961

Bar Council of India and State Bar Councils, enrolment, right to practise, professional misconduct, disciplinary committees, appeal to the BCI and Supreme Court (ss.35 to 38)
Bar Council of India Rules (duties to court, client, opponent, colleagues), contempt of court (Contempt of Courts Act 1971), accountancy for lawyers; leading cases: Re Vinay Chandra Mishra, Mahipal Singh Rana, Bar Council of Maharashtra v M.V. Dabholkar
16Public International LawREGULAR · 4 topics · about 2 to 4 questions0%

Foundations

Nature and sources (Art 38 ICJ Statute): treaties, custom, general principles, judicial decisions, jus cogens; relationship with municipal law (monism and dualism)
Subjects: states, recognition, state succession, individuals, international organisations; jurisdiction, immunities, extradition and asylum

Practice

Law of treaties (Vienna Convention 1969), law of the sea (UNCLOS: territorial sea, EEZ, continental shelf), United Nations organs, ICJ jurisdiction and leading judgments, use of force and self-defence
International humanitarian law: Geneva Conventions, war crimes, ICC and the Rome Statute; diplomatic and consular relations
17Human Rights LawREGULAR · 2 topics · about 2 to 3 questions0%

International and Indian

UDHR 1948, ICCPR and ICESCR, generations of rights, Optional Protocols; conventions on women (CEDAW), children (CRC), torture (CAT), racial discrimination (CERD), disabilities (CRPD)
Protection of Human Rights Act 1993 (as amended 2019): NHRC, SHRCs, Human Rights Courts; vulnerable groups: women, children, minorities, refugees, persons with disabilities (RPwD Act 2016), transgender persons (NALSA 2014)
18Public Interest LitigationOCCASIONAL · 1 topics · about 1 to 2 questions0%

Concept and practice

Origin and evolution: relaxation of locus standi (S.P. Gupta, Hussainara Khatoon, Bandhua Mukti Morcha), epistolary jurisdiction, guidelines and misuse, costs, continuing mandamus, amicus curiae
19Right to InformationOCCASIONAL · 2 topics · about 1 to 2 questions0%

RTI Act 2005

Right and obligations: public authority, PIO and appellate authority, time limits, exemptions (s.8) and second schedule agencies, third-party information
Information Commissions: CIC and SICs, appointment and removal, penalties (s.20), amendments of 2019 and the DPDP Act 2023 changes to s.8(1)(j); key cases: Raj Narain, S.P. Gupta, Namit Sharma, CBSE v Aditya Bandopadhyay
20Banking LawOCCASIONAL · 2 topics · about 1 to 2 questions0%

Banking and negotiable instruments

Banker and customer: relationship, duties, Banking Regulation Act 1949, RBI Act 1934, SARFAESI Act 2002, DRT and recovery
Negotiable Instruments Act 1881: promissory note, bill, cheque, holder in due course, dishonour (s.138), crossing and endorsement
21Insurance LawOCCASIONAL · 2 topics · about 1 to 2 questions0%

Contracts of insurance

Principles: insurable interest, utmost good faith, indemnity, subrogation, contribution, proximate cause; life, fire, marine and motor insurance
Insurance Act 1938, IRDA Act 1999 and the IRDAI; Marine Insurance Act 1963; claims and the insurance ombudsman
22Alternative Dispute ResolutionOCCASIONAL · 2 topics · about 1 to 2 questions0%

Arbitration and others

Arbitration and Conciliation Act 1996: arbitration agreement, tribunal, interim measures (s.9), challenge to awards (s.34), enforcement, foreign awards (New York and Geneva Conventions), 2015, 2019 and 2021 amendments
Conciliation, mediation and negotiation, Mediation Act 2023, Lok Adalats (Legal Services Authorities Act 1987), Section 89 CPC, family courts
23Civil Procedure CodeREGULAR · 2 topics · about 2 to 3 questions0%

Suits and appeals

Jurisdiction (subject-matter, pecuniary, territorial), res sub judice (s.10), res judicata (s.11), place of suing, institution of suits, parties, pleadings, summons, written statement, set-off, counter-claim
Interim orders (injunction, receiver, attachment before judgment), execution, appeal, review, revision, reference, inherent powers (s.151), commercial courts and summary suits (Order 37), limitation basics
24Intellectual Property Rights I: Patents, Trademarks, GIREGULAR · 2 topics · about 2 to 3 questions0%

Patents, trademarks, GI

Patents Act 1970: patentable subject matter, s.3(d), novelty, inventive step, compulsory licensing, revocation (Novartis, Bayer, Natco); Trade Marks Act 1999: registration, well-known marks, passing off, infringement
Geographical Indications Act 1999, Designs Act 2000, cyber-IPR issues: domain names, database protection, IT Act overlap
25Intellectual Property Rights II: Copyright and TreatiesOCCASIONAL · 2 topics · about 1 to 2 questions0%

Copyright and international regime

Copyright Act 1957: works, authorship and ownership, assignment and licensing, moral rights, fair dealing, infringement, 2012 amendment; Indian Performing Right Society cases
Biodiversity and plant varieties: PPV&FR Act 2001, Biological Diversity Act 2002; treaties: TRIPS, Paris, Berne, PCT, WIPO, Rome Convention, Budapest Treaty
26Penology and VictimologyOCCASIONAL · 2 topics · about 0 to 2 questions0%

Theories and practice

Theories of punishment (deterrent, retributive, preventive, reformative), capital punishment (Bachan Singh, rarest of rare), prison reforms, parole, probation, open prisons
Victimology: types and rights of victims, compensation (s.357 CrPC; s.395 BNSS), victim impact; the 2015 Juvenile Justice Act perspective
27Interpretation of StatutesREGULAR · 2 topics · about 1 to 3 questions0%

Rules and aids

Literal, golden, mischief and purposive rules (Heydon's case; Maxwell, Craies), harmonious construction, ejusdem generis, noscitur a sociis, expressio unius, presumptions (e.g. against retrospective operation)
Internal and external aids: preamble, headings, marginal notes, provisos, explanations, Statement of Objects and Reasons, parliamentary debates; General Clauses Act 1897
28Competition LawOCCASIONAL · 1 topics · about 1 to 2 questions0%

Competition Act 2002

Anti-competitive agreements (s.3: horizontal, vertical, cartels), abuse of dominance (s.4), combinations (ss.5 and 6), CCI powers and duties, appeal to NCLAT; the 2023 amendment (deal-value threshold, leniency plus, settlements)
29Law of Evidence (IEA and BSA)REGULAR · 2 topics · about 2 to 3 questions0%

Core rules

Relevancy and admissibility: facts in issue, res gestae, admissions, confessions (ss.24 to 30 IEA; BSA 2023 ss.22 to 24), dying declaration, expert opinion, electronic records (s.65B IEA; BSA s.63)
Burden and standard of proof, presumptions, estoppel, witnesses: competence, privilege, examination in chief, cross, re-examination, hostile witness, documentary evidence; the Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam 2023 in force from 1 July 2024
30TaxationOCCASIONAL · 1 topics · about 1 to 2 questions0%

Direct and indirect

Income-tax Act 1961: previous year and assessment year, residence, heads of income, exemptions, GST (CGST, SGST, IGST: supply, input tax credit, GST Council), customs and central excise basics; the Income-tax Act 2025 comes into force from 1 April 2026
31White Collar CrimesOCCASIONAL · 2 topics · about 0 to 2 questions0%

Concepts and statutes

Concept (Sutherland), causes and classification: corporate crime, bribery, tax evasion, money laundering, cyber and financial fraud
Prevention of Corruption Act 1988 (2018 amendment), PMLA 2002, Companies Act fraud provisions, SEBI insider trading, Lokpal; Santhanam Committee
32Women and Criminal LawOCCASIONAL · 2 topics · about 0 to 2 questions0%

Protection laws

Sexual offences and the Criminal Law (Amendment) Acts of 2013 and 2018 (Nirbhaya, Kathua), BNS chapter on offences against women; dowry (ss.304B, 498A IPC; BNS ss.80, 85), Protection of Women from Domestic Violence Act 2005
POSH Act 2013 (Vishaka), POCSO Act 2012, Immoral Traffic (Prevention) Act, Maternity Benefit Act, Equal Remuneration; social legislation affecting women
33International Trade LawOCCASIONAL · 2 topics · about 0 to 2 questions0%

WTO and trade

GATT and the WTO: MFN, national treatment, tariffs, dispute settlement understanding, anti-dumping, subsidies, safeguards, TRIPS and GATS
International contracts (CISG, Incoterms, UNCITRAL), foreign investment (FDI policy, BITs), Foreign Trade (Development and Regulation) Act 1992
