About this mock test

CUET PG LL.M. Mock Test 2 is a free, timed practice paper in the CUET PG Law pattern: 75 multiple-choice questions in 90 minutes, +4 for each correct answer and −1 for each wrong answer, for 300 marks. A full-length paper in the CUET PG LL.M. pattern with 11 easy, 43 medium and 21 hard questions across constitutional law, jurisprudence, contract, torts, criminal and procedural law, evidence, international law and more. Fresh questions, same marking scheme. Your answers and result stay in your browser; there is no sign-up.
