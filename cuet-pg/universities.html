Universities using CUET PG

Institutions reported to admit to LL.M. through the CUET PG Law score, with the exams that are not CUET PG. Participation changes every year, so use this as a starting shortlist and confirm on each university's own admission page.

How to use this list. No official, complete list exists in one place: NTA publishes the list of participating universities in each cycle, and each university decides whether its LL.M. uses CUET PG. Entries marked Widely reported were named in several 2026 admission lists; Reported means at least one list names it. Careers360 counted about 142 participating universities in 2026, of which about 54 offer LL.M. programmes (REPORTED).

What to check before you shortlist

  1. Does this year's LL.M. use CUET PG? Open the university's admission page and the NTA participating-university list.
  2. Eligibility: minimum LL.B. percentage, final-year rule, and any age or specialisation condition.
  3. Seats and reservation: LL.M. intake is small; confirm category-wise seats.
  4. Programme: one-year or two-year LL.M., specialisations, and fee for the whole course.
  5. Process dates: each university has its own form, merit list and rounds after the result in April.
  6. Backup list: apply to a mix of central, state and private options.
