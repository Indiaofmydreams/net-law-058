What is CUET PG LLM?

CUET (PG) is the Common University Entrance Test for postgraduate admission, conducted by the National Testing Agency. This guide covers the Law (LL.M.) paper: pattern, marking, dates, eligibility, fee, how it differs from CLAT PG and AILET, and how admission works.

Read this first. NTA issues a new Information Bulletin every cycle and the 2027 one is not out yet. Facts marked OFFICIAL come from NTA's 2026 bulletin and FAQs; facts marked REPORTED come from admission-news coverage and 2026 papers; confirm them in the 2027 bulletin before you rely on them.

Exam pattern: CUET PG LLM

Questions75 multiple-choice questions, all compulsory REPORTED
SectionsNone. No General Test, language or aptitude part in the LL.M. paper
Marks300 (4 marks per question) REPORTED
Negative marking−1 per wrong answer; unattempted carries nothing REPORTED
Duration90 minutes in the 2026 papers, about 72 seconds per question. Some older articles say 105 minutes REPORTED
DifficultyEasy to moderate in 2025 and 2026 per candidate feedback; basics such as Constitution, Contract, Torts and IPC dominated
A worked example. You attempt 60 questions and get 48 right and 12 wrong: 48 × 4 − 12 × 1 = 180 out of 300. Had you skipped the 6 you were least sure about, and 3 of them were right, you would have lost 3 × 4 − 3 × 1 = 9 marks. Skipping pays only when you would have been right less than a fifth of the time. See the score planner.

Eligibility, fee and application

  • Qualification: a bachelor's degree or equivalent; candidates in the final year in 2026 were eligible OFFICIAL. For LL.M. the university will require an LL.B. (three-year or five-year integrated), usually with a minimum percentage (often 50 percent, relaxed for reserved categories) REPORTED. The percentage is set by each university, not by NTA.
  • Age: no age limit for appearing in CUET (PG) OFFICIAL; a university may have its own condition.
  • Fee (2026 cycle, up to two papers): Rs 1,400 General; Rs 1,200 Gen-EWS and OBC-NCL; Rs 1,100 SC, ST and Third Gender; Rs 1,000 PwBD; additional papers Rs 600 to Rs 700 each; outside India Rs 7,000 OFFICIAL. The 2027 fee will be in the new bulletin.
  • How to apply: register on the NTA portal, fill the form, choose the Law (LL.M.) paper code, upload documents, pay the fee online, then check the correction window. Then apply to each university separately after the result.

From score to seat: how admission works

  1. NTA publishes the scorecard with your marks and percentile-style details. This is the only thing NTA does after the exam.
  2. Each university announces its own process: an application form, the seats, the eligibility percentage and the date of the merit list.
  3. You apply separately to every university you want. Delhi University uses its CSAS PG portal; BHU runs its own portal.
  4. Merit lists and rounds follow, with reservation as per each university. Many have document verification and fee payment windows.
  5. Keep a backup: apply to more universities than you think you need, because cut-offs depend on the number of test-takers and seats.

How to prepare for this paper

  1. Lock in the core subjects first. Constitution, Criminal Law (with BNS, BNSS and BSA), Contract, Torts, Jurisprudence and Family Law.
  2. Cover the wide middle of Property, Company, Administrative, Environmental, International, Human Rights, CPC, Evidence and IPR with bare acts and short notes.
  3. Keep a case file and a section map (old and new criminal-law numbers).
  4. Practise untimed, then add pace: 75 questions in 90 minutes is 72 seconds each. The timed mock tests train exactly that.
  5. Review every wrong answer in your revision notebook weekly.
  6. Revise current legal developments last: new Acts and recent landmark judgments.
