About this question set

12 practice questions with answers

1Bailment differs from sale in that, in bailment:

  1. Goods are delivered for a price
  2. The same goods, or goods as directed, must be returned, and property does not pass
  3. Ownership of goods passes to the bailee
  4. No delivery is needed
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. The same goods, or goods as directed, must be returned, and property does not pass

The bailee gets possession only.

Report this question

2Under Section 149, delivery to the bailee may be made by:

  1. Physical handing over only
  2. Doing anything which has the effect of putting the goods in the possession of the bailee or of any person authorised to hold them on his behalf
  3. Written deed only
  4. Registered notice only
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Doing anything which has the effect of putting the goods in the possession of the bailee or of any person authorised to hold them on his behalf

Covers constructive delivery.

Report this question

3Under Section 155, where the bailee mixes the bailor's goods with his own with the bailor's consent:

  1. The bailor loses ownership
  2. The bailee becomes sole owner
  3. The bailor and bailee have an interest in the mixture in proportion to their respective shares
  4. The bailment is void
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. The bailor and bailee have an interest in the mixture in proportion to their respective shares

Consent makes the mixture a co-ownership.

Report this question

4Under Section 156, where the bailee mixes the goods without consent and the goods can be separated:

  1. The bailor may reject the goods and claim their value
  2. The bailor bears the cost of separation
  3. The bailor retains his property, and the bailee must bear the expense of separation and any damage arising from the mixture
  4. The goods are forfeited
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. The bailor retains his property, and the bailee must bear the expense of separation and any damage arising from the mixture

Section 157 applies where separation is impossible.

Report this question

5Under Section 158, where the goods are to be kept or carried by the bailee without remuneration, the bailor must:

  1. Pay nothing
  2. Pay double the expenses
  3. Repay the necessary expenses incurred by the bailee for the bailment
  4. Pay only on demand
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Repay the necessary expenses incurred by the bailee for the bailment

Section 158 covers gratuitous bailment.

Report this question

6Under Section 159, a lender of goods gratuitously may demand their return at any time, but where the loan was for a specified time or purpose and early return causes the borrower a loss exceeding the benefit actually received:

  1. The lender need not pay anything
  2. The lender must indemnify the borrower for the excess
  3. The borrower must return the goods with interest
  4. The loan becomes a sale
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. The lender must indemnify the borrower for the excess

Lender may recall but must make good the excess loss.

Report this question

7Under Section 160, the bailee must return the goods bailed:

  1. Only after demand by the bailor
  2. Only through the court
  3. Within one year
  4. Without demand, as soon as the time for which they were bailed has expired or the purpose has been accomplished
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Without demand, as soon as the time for which they were bailed has expired or the purpose has been accomplished

Failure leads to liability under s.161.

Report this question

8Under Section 162, a gratuitous bailment is, in the absence of a contract to the contrary, terminated by:

  1. Insolvency of the bailor only
  2. Passage of one month
  3. Death of the bailor or the bailee
  4. The court's order only
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Death of the bailor or the bailee

Bailment for reward is not terminated by death in this way.

Report this question

9Under Section 163, a cow bailed for a year gives birth to a calf during the bailment. Absent a contrary contract, the calf belongs to:

  1. The bailee
  2. The calf's seller
  3. The state
  4. The bailor
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. The bailor

The bailee must deliver the increase or profit to the bailor.

Report this question

10Under Section 164, the bailor is responsible to the bailee for any loss sustained because:

  1. The goods were old
  2. The bailor was late
  3. The bailor was not entitled to make the bailment, to receive the goods back, or to give directions about them
  4. The goods were insured
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. The bailor was not entitled to make the bailment, to receive the goods back, or to give directions about them

Protects the bailee against defects in the bailor's title.

Report this question

11Under Section 166, a bailee who in good faith delivers the goods back to the bailor, who had no title, is:

  1. Liable to the true owner
  2. Not responsible to the owner for such delivery
  3. Liable for double value
  4. Liable only for costs
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Not responsible to the owner for such delivery

Section 167 lets the true owner apply to court to stop the delivery.

Report this question

12In Morris v. C.W. Martin & Sons Ltd., a fur was stolen by an employee of the cleaning company to whom it had been sub-bailed. The court held that:

  1. The bailee was not liable for a servant's crime
  2. Only the thief was liable
  3. The owner bore the loss
  4. The bailee was liable, as it had delegated custody to the employee who stole
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. The bailee was liable, as it had delegated custody to the employee who stole

A bailee remains responsible for those to whom he entrusts the goods.
