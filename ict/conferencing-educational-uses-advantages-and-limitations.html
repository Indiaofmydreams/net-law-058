This set has 14 multiple-choice questions on Conferencing: Educational Uses, Advantages and Limitations from the UGC NET Paper 1 syllabus, unit 8, Information and Communication Technology (ICT). 14 practice questions with answers

1A virtual classroom is:

  1. A printed textbook
  2. An online learning environment with live interaction
  3. A physical room
  4. A library
Show answer

Answer: B. An online learning environment with live interaction

Report this question

2In education, webinars are commonly used for:

  1. Expert talks to a large audience
  2. Marking attendance only
  3. Personal chatting
  4. Cooking demonstrations only
Show answer

Answer: A. Expert talks to a large audience

3Remote teaching through video conferencing:

  1. Is not possible
  2. Is possible only in laboratories
  3. Works only through e-mail
  4. Allows teaching students at different locations and was widely used during the COVID-19 lockdowns
Show answer

Answer: D. Allows teaching students at different locations and was widely used during the COVID-19 lockdowns

4Video conferencing enables:

  1. Guest lectures by experts from anywhere
  2. No guest lectures
  3. Only local guest lectures
  4. Only printed lectures
Show answer

Answer: A. Guest lectures by experts from anywhere

5Recorded conferencing sessions help students to:

  1. Revise the lesson later
  2. Delete lectures
  3. Avoid learning
  4. Never revise

Answer: A. Revise the lesson later

6Conferencing tools also support:

  1. Only printing
  2. Group projects, meetings and mentoring across locations
  3. Only text messages
  4. Nothing but entertainment

7A cost advantage of video conferencing is that it:

  2. Increases travel expenses
  3. Requires flights
  4. Reduces travel and accommodation costs

8The "reach" advantage of video conferencing means it:

  1. Works only in one city
  2. Limits participation
  3. Connects participants across geographies
  4. Restricts access

9The "flexibility" advantage means participants can:

  1. Must always travel
  2. Attend only at a fixed place

10A limitation related to connectivity is that:

  1. The Internet is always stable
  2. Poor connectivity can disrupt sessions
  3. It improves audio quality
  4. It is never a problem

  1. Everyone has equal access
  2. All learners own laptops
  3. Unequal access to devices and the Internet restricts participation
  4. It has no effect

12A limitation in online sessions concerning engagement is that:
