1In "Because it has smoke," the member of the syllogism is:

  1. Upanaya
  2. Pratijna
  3. Nigamana
  4. Hetu
Show answer

Answer: D. Hetu

  1. Udaharana
  2. Pratijna
  3. Upanaya
  4. Nigamana
