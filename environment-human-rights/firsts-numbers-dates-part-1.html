15 practice questions with answers

1The first Earth Day was observed on:

  1. 5 June 1970
  2. 22 April 1970
  3. 5 June 1972
  4. 22 April 1972
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. 22 April 1970

The first Earth Day was observed on 22 April 1970 in the United States, organized by Senator Gaylord Nelson. It mobilized 20 million Americans and led to the creation of the US Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) and the enactment of the US Clean Air Act. April 22 is now observed as Earth Day globally.

2World Environment Day is observed every year on:

  1. 22 April
  2. 16 September
  3. 21 March
  4. 5 June
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. 5 June

World Environment Day is observed every year on 5 June - the date on which the Stockholm Conference on Human Environment opened in 1972. It was established by UNGA in 1972 and is the UN's principal vehicle for encouraging awareness and action for protection of the environment.

Report this question

3The International Day of Persons with Disabilities is observed on:

  1. 10 December
  2. 20 November
  3. 18 December
  4. 3 December
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. 3 December

The International Day of Persons with Disabilities is observed on 3 December every year, established by UNGA Resolution 47/3 (1992). It promotes an understanding of disability issues and mobilizes support for the dignity, rights and well-being of persons with disabilities.

Report this question

4Human Rights Day is observed on:

  1. 26 June
  2. 20 November
  3. 8 March
  4. 10 December
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. 10 December

Human Rights Day is observed every year on 10 December - the date on which the UN General Assembly adopted the Universal Declaration of Human Rights in 1948. It is one of the most widely observed international days worldwide.

5The UDHR has how many articles in total?

  1. 25
  2. 28
  3. 30
  4. 33
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. 30

The UDHR has 30 articles covering: Articles 1-2 (foundational principles of dignity and non-discrimination); Articles 3-21 (civil and political rights); Articles 22-27 (economic, social and cultural rights); Articles 28-30 (collective rights and duties).

6The Convention on the Rights of the Child has how many articles?

  1. 41
  2. 45
  3. 54
  4. 60
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. 54

The CRC has 54 articles divided into three parts: Part I (Articles 1-41) - substantive rights; Part II (Articles 42-45) - implementation and monitoring; Part III (Articles 46-54) - final clauses on ratification, entry into force etc. This number (54) is a frequently tested fact.

Report this question

7The ICCPR was opened for signature in which year?

  1. 1948
  2. 1960
  3. 1966
  4. 1976
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. 1966

The ICCPR was adopted by the UN General Assembly on 16 December 1966 (the same day as the ICESCR) and opened for signature. It entered into force on 23 March 1976 after receiving the required 35 ratifications. India ratified the ICCPR on 10 April 1979.

8The Protection of Human Rights Act 1993 received Presidential assent on:

  1. 12 October 1993
  2. 8 January 1994
  3. 26 September 1993
  4. 1 April 1994
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. 8 January 1994

The Protection of Human Rights Act 1993 received the assent of the President on 8 January 1994. However, the NHRC was actually constituted earlier on 12 October 1993 through an ordinance/notification. Both dates are testable - know the distinction.

9The NGT has how many zonal benches apart from the Principal Bench?

  1. 3
  2. 4
  3. 5
  4. 6
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. 4

The NGT has 4 zonal benches apart from the Principal Bench at New Delhi: Central Zone bench at Bhopal; Western Zone bench at Pune; Eastern Zone bench at Kolkata; Southern Zone bench at Chennai. Total = 1 Principal Bench + 4 Zonal Benches = 5 locations.

10The Ozone Layer is found in which layer of the Earth's atmosphere?

  1. Troposphere
  2. Mesosphere
  3. Thermosphere
  4. Stratosphere
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Stratosphere

The ozone layer (ozone-sphere) is located in the stratosphere, approximately 15-35 km above the Earth's surface. It absorbs 97-99% of the Sun's medium-frequency ultraviolet light. Depletion of the ozone layer leads to increased UV-B radiation causing skin cancer, cataracts, and damage to marine ecosystems.

11The Montreal Protocol has been ratified by how many countries - making it:

  1. Ratified by 150 countries
  2. The first UN treaty to achieve universal ratification (197 parties)
  3. Ratified by 180 countries
  4. Ratified by 165 countries
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. The first UN treaty to achieve universal ratification (197 parties)

The Montreal Protocol on Substances that Deplete the Ozone Layer is the first - and so far only - UN treaty to achieve universal ratification, with all 197 UN member states as parties. This is why it is considered the most successful multilateral environmental agreement in history.

12The number of Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) adopted in 2015 is:

  1. 8
  2. 12
  3. 15
  4. 17
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. 17

The 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development has 17 Sustainable Development Goals and 169 targets. The 17 SDGs replaced the 8 Millennium Development Goals (MDGs). Key SDGs: SDG 6 (clean water), SDG 13 (climate action), SDG 14 (oceans), SDG 15 (life on land), SDG 16 (peace, justice, institutions).

13The Kyoto Protocol was adopted in which year and entered into force in which year?

  1. Adopted 1995, in force 1998
  2. Adopted 1997, in force 2002
  3. Adopted 2000, in force 2005
  4. Adopted 1997, in force 2005
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Adopted 1997, in force 2005

The Kyoto Protocol was adopted on 11 December 1997 at COP3 in Kyoto, Japan. It entered into force on 16 February 2005 after Russia's ratification. India ratified in 2002. The 8-year gap between adoption and entry into force is a testable detail.

Report this question

14The Paris Agreement was adopted at COP21 on which date?

  1. 10 November 2015
  2. 12 December 2015
  3. 25 September 2015
  4. 30 November 2015
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. 12 December 2015

The Paris Agreement was adopted at COP21 on 12 December 2015 in Le Bourget, Paris, France. It was opened for signature on 22 April 2016 (Earth Day). India ratified it on 2 October 2016 - the birth anniversary of Mahatma Gandhi.

Report this question

15The number of core UN human rights treaties as of 2024 is:

  1. 6
  2. 7
  3. 9
  4. 11
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. 9

There are 9 core international human rights treaties: ICERD (1965), ICCPR (1966), ICESCR (1966), CEDAW (1979), CAT (1984), CRC (1989), ICMW (1990 - migrant workers), CRPD (2006), ICPPED (2006 - enforced disappearance). Each has a corresponding treaty monitoring committee.
