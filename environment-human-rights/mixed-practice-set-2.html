About this question set

This set has 18 multiple-choice questions on Environment & Human Rights from the UGC NET Law (058) syllabus, unit 8, Environment and Human Rights Law. Try each question first, then tap Show answer to check the key and read the explanation. 18 of the 18 questions carry a written explanation.

For the concepts and case law behind these questions, read the Environment & Human Rights notes. Once you are comfortable, test yourself under exam conditions with a full-length mock test.

18 practice questions with answers

1The Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016 recognises how many disabilities?

  1. 7
  2. 14
  3. 21
  4. 25
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. 21

It replaced the 1995 Act.

Report this question

2Sexual harassment guidelines at workplace were laid down in:

  1. Maneka Gandhi
  2. Vishaka v. State of Rajasthan
  3. Shah Bano
  4. Nilabati Behera
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Vishaka v. State of Rajasthan

1997, later followed by POSH Act 2013.

Report this question

3Inter-generational equity is part of:

  1. Federalism
  2. Rule of law
  3. Sustainable development
  4. Strict liability
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Sustainable development

Present generations hold the environment in trust.

Report this question

4The Environment (Protection) Act 1986 was enacted to implement:

  1. Paris Agreement
  2. Kyoto Protocol
  3. Rio Declaration
  4. Stockholm Conference decisions
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Stockholm Conference decisions

Under Article 253.

5Common but differentiated responsibilities means:

  1. Equal burdens
  2. Burden only on developed
  3. No duty
  4. All States share burdens differently
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. All States share burdens differently

Rio Principle 7.

6Environmental PIL expanded in India through:

  1. Relaxed locus standi
  2. Strict standing
  3. Arbitration
  4. Legislation only
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Relaxed locus standi

Courts relaxed standing rules.

7Assertion (A): Environmental clearance is required for specified projects.
Reason (R): EIA assesses environmental impact before approval.

  1. Both A and R are true, and R is the correct explanation of A
  2. Both A and R are true, but R is not the correct explanation of A
