About this question set

This set has 18 multiple-choice questions on Environment & Human Rights from the UGC NET Law (058) syllabus, unit 8, Environment and Human Rights Law. Try each question first, then tap Show answer to check the key and read the explanation. 18 of the 18 questions carry a written explanation.

For the concepts and case law behind these questions, read the Environment & Human Rights notes. Once you are comfortable, test yourself under exam conditions with a full-length mock test.

18 practice questions with answers

1The Stockholm Declaration was adopted in:

  1. 2002
  2. 1987
  3. 1972
  4. 1992
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. 1972

UN Conference on the Human Environment, 1972.

Report this question

2The precautionary principle is stated in Rio Declaration Principle:

  1. 21
  2. 10
  3. 1
  4. 15
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. 15

Adopted at the 1992 Earth Summit.

Report this question

3The polluter pays principle was applied in:

  1. Kesavananda
  2. Vishaka
  3. Shah Bano
  4. Indian Council for Enviro-Legal Action v. Union of India
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Indian Council for Enviro-Legal Action v. Union of India

Decided in 1996.

Report this question

4The Vellore Citizens' Welfare Forum case adopted:

  1. Precautionary principle and sustainable development
  2. Only strict liability
  3. Public trust only
  4. None
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Precautionary principle and sustainable development

Decided in 1996.

Report this question

5Public trust doctrine in India was applied in:

  1. Shah Bano
  2. M.C. Mehta v. Kamal Nath
  3. Maneka Gandhi
  4. Vishaka
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. M.C. Mehta v. Kamal Nath

Decided in 1997.

Report this question

6Duty to protect the environment as a fundamental duty is in:

  1. Article 14
  2. Article 48A
  3. Article 51A(g)
  4. Article 21
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Article 51A(g)

Article 48A is a Directive Principle.

Report this question

7The Environment (Protection) Act was passed in:

  1. 1986
  2. 1974
  3. 2010
  4. 1981
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. 1986

It followed the Bhopal Gas tragedy.

Report this question

8The National Green Tribunal was established in:

  1. 1986
  2. 2010
  3. 2015
  4. 2002
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. 2010

National Green Tribunal Act, 2010.

Report this question

9The EIA Notification currently followed was issued under EPA in:

  1. 2011
  2. 2020 only
  3. 2006
  4. 1994
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. 2006

EIA Notification, 2006.

Report this question

10The Basel Convention relates to:

  1. Climate change
  2. Biodiversity
  3. Ozone layer
  4. Transboundary movement of hazardous wastes
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Transboundary movement of hazardous wastes

Adopted in 1989.

Report this question

11The Montreal Protocol concerns:

  1. Wetlands
  2. Greenhouse gases
  3. Ozone-depleting substances
  4. Whaling
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Ozone-depleting substances

Adopted in 1987.

Report this question

12The Paris Agreement was adopted in:

  1. 2015
  2. 1997
  3. 2009
  4. 2022
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. 2015

Adopted at COP21.

Report this question

13Right to a pollution-free environment under Article 21 was held in:

  1. ADM Jabalpur
  2. Subhash Kumar v. State of Bihar
  3. Shah Bano
  4. Kesavananda
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Subhash Kumar v. State of Bihar

Decided in 1991.

Report this question

14'Our Common Future' (1987) is the report of the:

  1. Brundtland Commission
  2. Club of Rome
  3. Kyoto Conference
  4. Rio Conference
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Brundtland Commission

It popularised sustainable development.

Report this question

15The UDHR was adopted on:

  1. 24 October 1945
  2. 10 December 1948
  3. 16 December 1966
  4. 26 January 1950
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. 10 December 1948

Celebrated as Human Rights Day.

Report this question

16The International Bill of Human Rights comprises UDHR, ICCPR and:

  1. CRC
  2. CAT
  3. ICESCR
  4. CEDAW
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. ICESCR

Adopted in 1966, in force 1976.

Report this question

17Cultural relativism holds that human rights are:

  1. Natural only
  2. Universal
  3. Absolute
  4. Culture-dependent
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Culture-dependent

It contrasts with universalism.

Report this question

18The Protection of Human Rights Act was passed in:

  1. 1986
  2. 2005
  3. 2013
  4. 1993
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. 1993

It created the NHRC.
