20 practice questions with answers

1The primary objective of teaching at the higher education level according to the National Education Policy 2020 is:

  1. Restricting learning to a single discipline
  2. Preparing students only for employment
  3. Ensuring maximum marks in board examinations
  4. Developing holistic, multidisciplinary, critical, and creative individuals
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Developing holistic, multidisciplinary, critical, and creative individuals

Concept: NEP 2020 explicitly emphasises holistic development — not just job readiness. This framing appears in recent NET papers.

Report this question

2Instructional objectives that describe observable and measurable learner behaviour are called:

  1. Vague objectives
  2. Behavioural / Performance objectives
  3. Hidden objectives
  4. Affective objectives
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Behavioural / Performance objectives

Concept: Behavioural objectives (Mager, 1962) = specific, measurable, observable. They say what the learner WILL DO, not what the teacher will do.

Report this question

3Robert Mager's format for writing a behavioural objective includes which three components?

  1. Performance, Condition, Criterion
  2. Objective, Content, Evaluation
  3. Aim, Goal, Purpose
  4. Topic, Method, Assessment
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Performance, Condition, Criterion

Concept: Mager's ABC of objectives:
A = Audience (who), B = Behaviour (observable verb), C = Condition (under what circumstances), D = Degree/Criterion (how well).
The three core = Performance + Condition + Criterion.

Report this question

4Which of the following is a correctly written behavioural objective?

  1. The student will be able to list five causes of World War I without referring to notes
  2. The student will appreciate classical music
  3. The student will know about the French Revolution
  4. The student will understand photosynthesis
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. The student will be able to list five causes of World War I without referring to notes

Concept: "List" = observable verb + "five causes" = criterion + "without notes" = condition. Options A, B, D use vague verbs — understand, know, appreciate — not measurable.

Report this question

5The objective "The student will develop a love for literature" belongs to which domain?

  1. Psychomotor
  2. Metacognitive
  3. Affective
  4. Cognitive
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Affective

Concept: "Love," "appreciate," "value" = affective domain. Deals with emotions, attitudes, dispositions.

Report this question

6General aims of education are derived from:

  1. Teacher preferences
  2. Classroom observation
  3. Philosophical and societal values
  4. Examination results
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Philosophical and societal values

Concept: Aims = broad, long-term, philosophical. Objectives = specific, short-term, measurable. Aims flow from society's vision of an ideal human being.

Report this question

7Which of the following correctly distinguishes between "aims" and "objectives" of teaching?

  1. Aims are specific; objectives are broad
  2. There is no difference between the two
  3. Aims are broad and long-term; objectives are specific and short-term
  4. Aims are immediate; objectives are long-term
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Aims are broad and long-term; objectives are specific and short-term

Concept: Aim = "develop good citizens." Objective = "student will explain three rights of a citizen." Hierarchically: Aim → Goal → Objective → Learning outcome.

Report this question

8The objective "Student will be able to compose an original essay arguing for renewable energy" represents which level of Bloom's revised taxonomy?

  1. Remember
  2. Apply
  3. Evaluate
  4. Create
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Create

Concept: Compose + original = Create level. Any production of a new product = Create.

9According to Tyler's Rationale, the sources of educational objectives are:

  1. Textbooks, curriculum guides, examinations
  2. Government, school administration, parents
  3. Learner, contemporary life, subject specialists — filtered through philosophy and psychology
  4. Teacher, student, society
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Learner, contemporary life, subject specialists — filtered through philosophy and psychology

Concept: Tyler's (1949) three sources = learner needs + society's demands + subject matter. Then filtered through philosophical and psychological screens.

Report this question

10"The student will demonstrate correct operation of a microscope" is an objective from which domain?

  1. Cognitive
  2. Metacognitive
  3. Psychomotor
  4. Affective
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Psychomotor

Concept: "Demonstrate correct operation" = physical skill = psychomotor. Any hands-on manipulation of equipment = psychomotor.

Report this question

11The concept of "Instructional Objectives" in education was systematised by:

  1. Ralph Tyler
  2. Robert Mager
  3. Benjamin Bloom
  4. All three contributed at different stages
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. All three contributed at different stages

Concept: Tyler = curriculum rationale (1949). Bloom = taxonomy for classifying objectives (1956). Mager = how to write precise behavioural objectives (1962). All three built on each other.

Report this question

12Which of the following objectives best represents the "Application" level of Bloom's revised taxonomy?

  1. Explain why objects fall at the same rate in a vacuum
  2. Define the law of gravitation
  3. Use the law of gravitation to calculate the force between two objects
  4. Evaluate the limitations of Newton's model of gravity
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Use the law of gravitation to calculate the force between two objects

Concept: Use + calculate = Apply. Define = Remember. Explain = Understand. Evaluate limitations = Evaluate.

Report this question

13Immediate objectives of teaching are also called:

  1. Instructional objectives / Lesson objectives
  2. Aims
  3. Social goals
  4. Terminal behaviours
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Instructional objectives / Lesson objectives

Concept: Immediate = lesson-level objectives. These are the daily, specific, observable objectives tied to a single class or unit.

Report this question

14Which of the following is NOT a characteristic of a well-stated instructional objective?

  1. It specifies the expected learner behaviour
  2. It is observable
  3. It is measurable
  4. It describes the teacher's activity
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. It describes the teacher's activity

Concept: Objectives describe what the LEARNER will do, not the teacher. "The teacher will explain..." is a teaching activity, not a learning objective.

Report this question

15The objective "Given a passage, the student will identify the main idea with 80% accuracy" illustrates:

  1. A poorly written objective
  2. An affective objective
  3. A well-written behavioural objective with condition and criterion
  4. An aim
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. A well-written behavioural objective with condition and criterion

Concept: "Given a passage" = condition. "Identify the main idea" = behaviour. "80% accuracy" = criterion. Mager's complete format.

Report this question

16According to Dewey, the central objective of education is:

  1. Preparing students to pass competitive examinations
  2. Instilling national values
  3. Transmitting cultural heritage unchanged
  4. Growth itself — education is growth with no fixed external end
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Growth itself — education is growth with no fixed external end

Concept: Dewey's famous statement: "Education is not preparation for life; education is life itself." Growth = continuous reconstruction of experience.

Report this question

17The objective "Student will value the importance of environmental conservation" is best assessed through:

  1. Mathematical problem-solving tasks
  2. Short answer exam
  3. Multiple choice test
  4. Observation of student behaviour, attitude surveys, and reflective journals
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Observation of student behaviour, attitude surveys, and reflective journals

Concept: Affective objectives cannot be measured by written tests alone. They require observation of actual behaviour and attitudes over time.

Report this question

18Terminal behaviour in instructional objectives refers to:

  1. Teacher's end-of-class behaviour
  2. The final examination grade
  3. Behaviour shown at the beginning of learning
  4. The specific behaviour the learner should exhibit at the END of instruction
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. The specific behaviour the learner should exhibit at the END of instruction

Concept: Terminal = end-point. Mager's framework — terminal behaviour = what learner CAN DO after the lesson that they couldn't before.

Report this question

19Which of the following is the CORRECT hierarchy from broadest to most specific?

  1. Aim → Goal → Objective → Learning Outcome
  2. Objective → Goal → Aim
  3. Goal → Aim → Learning Outcome → Objective
  4. Learning Outcome → Objective → Goal → Aim
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Aim → Goal → Objective → Learning Outcome

Concept: Hierarchical narrowing: Aim (broadest, philosophical) → Goal (program-level) → Objective (lesson-level) → Learning Outcome (specific, measurable result).

Report this question

20"By the end of this course, students will be able to critically analyse research articles in their field" is an example of:

  1. A vague classroom objective
  2. A memory-level instructional objective
  3. A psychomotor domain aim
  4. A programme-level learning outcome
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. A programme-level learning outcome

Concept: "By end of course" = programme-level. "Critically analyse" = HOTS (Analyse level). This is the kind of outcome OBE and NAAC frameworks require.
