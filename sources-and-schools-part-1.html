About this question set

This set has 13 multiple-choice questions on Sources and Schools from the UGC NET Law (058) syllabus, unit 7, Family Law. Try each question first, then tap Show answer to check the key and read the explanation. 13 of the 13 questions carry a written explanation.

For the concepts and case law behind these questions, read the Family Law notes. Once you are comfortable, test yourself under exam conditions with a full-length mock test.

13 practice questions with answers

1The primary source of Hindu Personal Law is:

  1. Smriti
  2. Judicial decisions
  3. Shruti
  4. Custom
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Shruti

Shruti (what is heard) refers to the Vedas — the primary revealed source of Hindu Law. Smriti (what is remembered) is secondary.

Report this question

2Which of the following is NOT a school of Hindu Law?

  1. Mithila
  2. Hanafi
  3. Mitakshara
  4. Dayabhaga
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Hanafi

Hanafi is a school of Muslim Law. Mitakshara, Dayabhaga, and Mithila are schools of Hindu Law.

Report this question

3The Dayabhaga School of Hindu Law is followed mainly in:

  1. Bengal and Assam
  2. Punjab
  3. Mithila
  4. Maharashtra
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Bengal and Assam

The Dayabhaga School, authored by Jimutavahana, is followed mainly in Bengal and Assam.

Report this question

4Which school of Muslim Law is predominantly followed in India?

  1. Shafi
  2. Maliki
  3. Hanbali
  4. Hanafi
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Hanafi

The Hanafi school (founded by Imam Abu Hanifa) is the predominant school of Muslim Law followed in India.

Report this question

5The Muslim Personal Law (Shariat) Application Act was enacted in:

  1. 1939
  2. 1937
  3. 1955
  4. 1956
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. 1937

The Muslim Personal Law (Shariat) Application Act, 1937 applies Muslim personal law to specified matters for Muslims in India.

Report this question

6Under Mitakshara School, which of the following is correct regarding joint family property?

  1. Sons acquire rights by birth
  2. Sons acquire rights only on father's death
  3. Women have equal coparcenary rights since ancient times
  4. Only the Karta has rights
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Sons acquire rights by birth

Under the Mitakshara School, sons (and now daughters after 2005 amendment) acquire interest in joint family property by birth — this is the cardinal principle of unobstructed heritage.

Report this question

7The Jimavallee is a sub-school of:

  1. Dravidian
  2. Mithila
  3. Dayabhaga
  4. Mitakshara
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Mitakshara

Jimavallee (also spelled Jimutavahana-based) — the Mitakshara School has several sub-schools including Benaras, Mithila, Maharashtra (Bombay), Madras (Dravida), and Punjab.

Report this question

8Which of the following is the correct sequence of Hindu Law sources in order of authority?

  1. Digest → Custom → Smriti → Shruti
  2. Shruti → Smriti → Custom → Digest
  3. Custom → Shruti → Smriti → Digest
  4. Smriti → Shruti → Custom → Digest
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Shruti → Smriti → Custom → Digest

The hierarchy is: Shruti (Vedas) → Smriti (Manusmriti, Yajnavalkya Smriti etc.) → Custom → Digests/Commentaries.

Report this question

9Arrange the following Hindu Code Bills in correct chronological order of enactment: I. Hindu Marriage Act, II. Hindu Adoptions and Maintenance Act, III. Hindu Minority and Guardianship Act, IV. Hindu Succession Act

  1. I, IV, II, III
  2. IV, I, III, II
  3. I, II, III, IV
  4. I, IV, III, II
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. I, IV, III, II

Hindu Marriage Act (1955) → Hindu Succession Act (1956) → Hindu Minority and Guardianship Act (1956) → Hindu Adoptions and Maintenance Act (1956).

Report this question

10What does 'Sapinda relationship' mean under the HMA, 1955?

  1. Within 7 generations on father's side
  2. Within 2 generations on mother's side
  3. A person is sapinda of another if within 3 generations on the mother's side or within 5 generations on the father's side
  4. Relationship within 3 degrees on mother's side and 5 on father's side
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. A person is sapinda of another if within 3 generations on the mother's side or within 5 generations on the father's side

Section 3(f) HMA defines sapinda relationship: a person is sapinda of another if they are within 3 degrees of lineal ascent on the mother's side and within 5 degrees on the father's side.

Report this question

11The Hindu Code Bills were largely drafted by:

  1. Rajendra Prasad
  2. B.R. Ambedkar
  3. Sardar Patel
  4. Jawaharlal Nehru
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. B.R. Ambedkar

Dr. B.R. Ambedkar, as Law Minister, was the primary architect of the Hindu Code Bills, though he resigned over the dilution of his draft.

Report this question

12The Mitakshara School of Hindu Law is a commentary on which foundational text?

  1. Parashara Smriti
  2. Narada Smriti
  3. Manusmriti
  4. Yajnavalkya Smriti
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Yajnavalkya Smriti

Both the Mitakshara (by Vijnaneswara) and Dayabhaga (by Jimutavahana) are commentaries on the Yajnavalkya Smriti.

Report this question

13The Mitakshara School was authored by:

  1. Manu
  2. Jimutavahana
  3. Vijnaneswara
  4. Yajnavalkya
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Vijnaneswara

The Mitakshara was authored by Vijnaneswara (also spelled Vigneshwara), a minister at the court of King Vikramaditya of the Chalukya dynasty.
